title: The Spiritual Garden of God
scripture: Matthew 13:25 AMP
verse: But while his men were sleeping, his enemy came and sowed weeds (resembling wheat) among the wheat and went away.
body:
  - The phrase the spiritual garden of God is not commonly used in our time. However, it paints a powerful picture of what your heart represents. As written in Genesis 3:8 NIV,Then the man and his wife heard the sound of the LORD God as he was walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and they hid from the LORD God among the trees of the garden.
  - From the beginning, God had a place where He fellowshipped with man. That place is no longer Eden, it is now in your heart. God desires to dwell and walk in the garden of your heart. He desires fellowship, intimacy, and a platform where seeds of His word can be nurtured to bear fruit.
  - Your heart is God's garden. Seeds are planted there every time you hear the Word or receive divine inspiration. These seeds are meant to be meditated upon until they take root and begin to influence your actions. Only then can you bear fruits that please God. As contained in Timothy 4:15 NKJV, Meditate on these things; give yourself entirely to them, that your progress may be evident to all.
  - The Bible says in Proverbs 4:23 NLT, Guard your heart above all else, for it determines the course of your life. While God is interested in your heart, the enemy is too. Just like in the parable of the sower, the devil comes when men are not watchful to sow weeds among the wheat. He introduces wrong thoughts that oppose God's Word. The fall of man began when Eve permitted the wrong seed planted by Satan to stay in her heart.
  - Guard your heart diligently. Allow only the seeds sown by God to grow. If a thought contradicts God's Word, simply reject and resist it.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for making my heart your garden. I will give myself completely to your Word and to the inspirations you drop in my heart. As I meditate on your Word, it changes me, and I bear fruits to the glory of your name. In Jesus' name, amen.
