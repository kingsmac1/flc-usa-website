date: '2025-08-25'
title: Be a Faithful Witness
scripture: Isaiah 43:10 KJV
verse: Ye are my witnesses, saith the LORD, and my servant whom I have chosen...
body:
  - You are chosen by God to witness for Him, and He needs you to be faithful in carrying out this assignment. The Lord needs you to testify of His power, goodness, and faithfulness to others. Since to witness means to testify, you can testify about God not only verbally but also through your actions.
  - As Timothy 4:12 AMP rightly puts, ...but be an example and set a pattern for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, and in moral purity. Someone who witnesses for the Lord should show these qualities in their life.
  - Being a witness for the Lord also involves the responsibility of proclaiming the truth even when it is resisted. And the gospel is the truth. Through the truth of the gospel and your testimony which you are commanded to share with others, people will get saved and lives will be changed.
  - If you preach the gospel and live differently from what you preach, you are not being faithful. The Lord expects better from
  - 'you. Acts 17:30 KJV says, And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth all men everywhere to repent. Moreover, 1 Corinthians 9:27 KJV says, But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway. The word "castaway" is the same as "disqualified" and it comes from the Greek word adokimos. In this context, it means failing to meet a standard or being rejected after failing a test.'
  - As a witness for the Lord, be faithful by genuinely living out the scriptures. We should all strive not to fall short of God's standard for those He will reward. I encourage you to remain truthful, courageous, and persevere despite the challenges you may face. Live holy and let the love of Christ continue to be your motivation.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for trusting me by choosing me to witness for you. I affirm I will always be faithful in witnessing for you. I lay aside every weight and everything that pulls me down. I am strengthened by your Spirit to walk closely with you. In Jesus' name, amen.
