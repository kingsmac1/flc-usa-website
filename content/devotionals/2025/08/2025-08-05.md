title: You are God’s Kind
scripture: Genesis 1:26 KJV
verse: 'And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion...'
body:
  - To say you are God's kind means you are an associate of the God kind. This may sound too lofty for some people, while others may even consider it blasphemy. Regardless of how it sounds, this is a truth clearly revealed in Scripture.
  - 'From our opening Scripture in Genesis 1:26, And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion, we see that God made man in His own image and likeness. Out of all of God''s creations, man was the only being made in this unique way. This means there is a special relationship between God and man.'
  - The phrase Let us make man shows that the Trinity was involved in the creation of man, and that man was fashioned to share in certain divine qualities. These include righteousness, morality, and the capacity for fellowship with God (1 John 1:3 KJV).
  - God exercises dominion, and He enabled man to exercise dominion too. One of the reasons He made us in His image and
  - likeness is that He wanted man to have the ability to function like Him and to enjoy deep fellowship with Him. Animals cannot fellowship with God. So do not let anyone call you a higher animal. God did not make you an animal, and He never called you one. You were made with the qualities and functionalities of God.
  - Even more, He gave you His Spirit to dwell in you. Isn't that wonderful? You are truly unique. As He is, so are we in this world (1 John 4:17). Live out this truth in your daily life. You are God's kind, not Satan's kind. Believe this truth. Know your worth and dignity in Christ.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for making me in your image and likeness. I testify today that I am God's kind, I am God's specie, I have the nature and life of God. As He is, so am I in this world. I live daily expressing the multifaceted glory of God in this world. In Jesus’ name, amen.
