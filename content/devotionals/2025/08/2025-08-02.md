title: God Can Use you Too
scripture: John 6:12-13 ESV
verse: And when they had eaten their fill, he told his disciples, "Gather up the leftover fragments, that nothing may be lost." So they gathered them up and filled twelve baskets...
body:
  - In John 6, we find the account of Jesus feeding five thousand people with just five loaves of bread and two fishes. At first, Philip, one of His disciples, doubted, but Jesus used the two fishes and five loaves of bread to feed the entire multitude. And when they were all satisfied, there was leftover food that filled twelve baskets.
  - There are many lessons to be learned from this miracle. Firstly, have you ever imagined that the resources of the young boy (five loaves and two fishes) could be useful to Jesus in accomplishing a divine assignment? It seemed small and insignificant, but Jesus used them. This teaches us that anything is possible if you believe. No matter what stage you are in your life, you are not too small or too unqualified to be used by God to do mighty things. The Lord qualifies those who seem unqualified.
  - The second lesson from this miracle is from how Jesus instructed His disciples to gather the leftover food, and they filled twelve baskets. Some of this leftover food may have fallen
  - on the ground because the multitude was satisfied and did not need them anymore. Some of the food may have even been trampled underfoot. In biblical numerology, the number twelve signifies wholeness and completion.
  - Have you made mistakes? Do you feel worthless? Has your family or society made you feel that you are good for nothing or that you are existing for no reason? I want you to know that Jesus can make you whole and complete again.
  - The first step is to accept Him as your Lord and Saviour. Then, make yourself available to Him, and He will amaze you with how greatly He can use you and reveal Himself in your life.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you because anything is possible to him that believes. I believe in you with all my heart. I make myself available to you. Please use me for your glory and express yourself greatly in and through me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
