title: Serving the Lord with a Perfect Heart
scripture: 2 Chronicles 19:9 KJV
verse: And he charged them, saying, Thus shall ye do in the fear of the Lord, faithfully, and with a perfect heart.
body:
  - God's love and mercy are not dependent on human perfection. God is not seeking perfect people. Rather, He seeks those who are willing and obedient. Isaiah 1:19 KJV says, "If ye be willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land." The good of the land refers to God's abundance, favour, prosperity, and fruitfulness in the place where you dwell, but it is only reserved for those who are willing and obedient.
  - To be willing is a heart posture that is eager and ready to do the will and desires of the Lord. To be obedient is to be compliant and submissive to the will of God. Though they are similar, there is a difference. Someone can be obedient out of compulsion, ambition, or even for selfish gain. But God is more interested in the state of your heart than the outward acts of your service.
  - Many people may appear to be doing the right things, but their hearts are not aligned with God. This proves that the state of a person's heart is more important than what they present to others. As Proverbs 16:2 NLT rightly says, "People may be
  - pure in their own eyes, but the Lord examines their motives". Jeremiah 17:10 NLT buttresses this when it says "But I, the Lord, search all hearts and examine secret motives. I give all people their due rewards, according to what their actions deserve".
  - Your heart is a determining factor in your walk and relationship with God, and it also affects how fast you grow with Him. Your availability for God is not just about your time, but more importantly about your heart. When you serve the Lord with an imperfect heart, your results may not carry weight or be pleasing to Him. God sees your heart, not just your outcome. 1 Samuel 15:22 says that God delights in obedience more than sacrifice.
  - In your service to the Lord, it is important to perform all you do with a perfect heart. Regularly examine your heart to see if you have drifted from the mindset of righteousness. Through the scriptures and fellowship with the Holy Spirit, the Lord will align your heart to His ways, cause you to flourish, and make you a partaker in the blessings of service.
prayer: I declare, Lord, purge my heart of any resistance or deviation from your desires. My mind is stayed on your word to guide my heart right. I am willing and obedient. I serve you with my whole heart and in reverence, in Jesus' name. Amen.
