title: You are not of this World
scripture: John 17:16 AMP
verse: They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world
body:
  - The term the world, as used in several parts of the Bible, can mean different things. It could refer to the physical earth, mankind, animals, plants, and the inhabitants and elements of the earth. It could also refer to a negative condition of living outside of God's standard. In such context, it means being apart from God and following ways that do not align with God's will.
  - In John 17:16, the world, as used here, refers to a system of values, beliefs, and practices that are opposed to God. These values fight against the principles found in God's word. That is why Romans 12:2 TPT says, Stop imitating the ideals and opinions of the culture around you, but be inwardly transformed by the Holy Spirit through a total reformation of how you think. This will empower you to discern God's will as you live a beautiful life, satisfying and perfect in his eyes.
  - As followers of Christ, we must place God's will above cultural and societal traditions. Do not envy or try to be like worldly people. They should not be your role models. Let your attention be fixed on Christ. Let your life be an example of the believer in both your words and your actions. 2 Timothy 4:12 encourages
  - us in this. Do not be drawn to worldly patterns of speech, dressing, or lifestyle.
  - Those who live by the ways of this world are called children of disobedience. They follow sinful patterns. In 2 Corinthians 4:4, Satan is referred to as the god of this world. He blinds people's minds so they do not receive the truth of the gospel. This is why some people dislike hearing about leaving worldly ways.
  - You are a child of God and a child of light. Let your light shine so brightly that those who dwell in darkness may see the light of God through you.
prayer: I affirm that I am not of this world. I refuse to be drawn to worldly things. My desire is to do the will of God. I will not conform to the cultures and traditions of this age. Rather, I allow God to transform me from within.
furtherStudies: 1 John 2:15-17 KJV, Ephesians 2:2 KJV
