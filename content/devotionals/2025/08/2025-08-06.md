title: The Fellowship of the Holyspirit
scripture: Genesis 3:8 KJV
verse: 'And they heard the voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day: and Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God amongst the trees of the garden.'
body:
  - The phrase the cool of the day in our opening scripture implies a specific time when God came into Eden for fellowship with man. It likely refers to the early evening, possibly when the heat of the day had subsided and the gentle breeze of God began to blow. It suggests a time of calmness, when the tension of the day had faded, creating space for reflection and quietness. This is a time free from distractions.
  - 'It is important to observe that God''s manifested presence appeared in the cool of the day. This reveals a pattern: God desires undistracted fellowship with His people. While His abiding presence is always with us (John 14:16), His manifested presence is often experienced during intentional, undisturbed times of communion with Him.'
  - Until you create such moments, you may not fully experience the richness of His presence. From today onward, create a special time each day to fellowship with the Lord. Although it
  - does not have to be in the evening, it should be a time free from distractions. It should be a special moment to study the scriptures, worship, meditate on His Word, and reflect on His works.
  - 'I pray for you today: may the Lord cause you to develop a deep and intimate relationship with Him. May He continually draw you into the secret place. May the love and glory of His presence fill your heart always, in Jesus'' name. Amen.'
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your word to me today. I declare that I will always create special and quality time to fellowship with you. Thank you for the richness of your presence that I will experience every time I pray, study, and meditate on your Word. In Jesus' name, amen.
