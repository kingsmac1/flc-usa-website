date: '2025-08-17'
title: God Enjoys your Fellowship
scripture: Genesis 3:8 KJV
verse: 'And they heard the voice of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day: and Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the Lord God amongst the trees of the garden.'
body:
  - One of the reasons God made man in His image and likeness is because He wanted man to carry His attributes and function in His capacity here on earth as His representative. Beyond that, God made us after His own kind because He desired to fellowship with us.
  - Fellowship is a communion. It is a state of companionship and a friendly association with people who share the same interest and experiences. From our opening Bible verse in Genesis 3:8, God always came to the garden to fellowship with man because He enjoyed that companionship.
  - Through Jesus Christ, the promise of the Holy Spirit has been fulfilled in us, so that anyone who believes in Jesus becomes a habitation of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19) and a partaker of the divine nature of God and the shared experience with Him. Hallelujah!
  - In the New Covenant, God now dwells in us. He does not need
  - to come in the cool of the day as He did with Adam. He now lives in you always because He enjoys the fellowship and does not want to miss a second of it. Our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son Jesus Christ (1 John 1:3 KJV).
  - Now that you know God enjoys fellowship with you, begin to do things that will enhance your relationship with Him, so that He will be pleased and glorified through your life.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for finding me fit to be your habitation. I am glad you enjoy my communion with you. Teach me, Lord, to always have that intimate relationship and fellowship with you so that all of me will bring glory to your name.
furtherStudies: John 17:21-23 KJV, 1 Corinthians 1:9 KJV, Colossians 1:27 KJV
