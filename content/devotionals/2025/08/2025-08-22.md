date: '2025-08-22'
title: Proclaim the Gospel
scripture: Matthew 28:19 KJV
verse: Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.
body:
  - The gospel is the good news of the saving power of Christ. It is about Jesus, His death, resurrection, and how He offers salvation to all who believe in Him. Romans 1:16 AMP says, I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes.
  - The gospel offers hope and transformation to all who accept this truth. But how will people hear this gospel if there is no one to preach it to them? How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher? (Romans 10:14 KJV). The only way others can hear and believe in Jesus is for someone to preach or disseminate the gospel to them.
  - Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me (Isaiah 6:8 KJV). Here, Isaiah immediately responded to God's need for a person to be sent to tell the people what God had in mind. Today, God is still seeking for someone who will step out of their
  - comfort zone to preach the gospel. Will you reply God, Here am I, send me?
  - Again Jesus said, Peace be with you! As the Father has sent me, I am sending you (John 20:21 NIV). Now, Jesus has sent you to proclaim the message of the gospel. Do not delay in acting on this divine assignment. Go and tell everyone about Jesus.
prayer: I declare, I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ. Therefore, I will proclaim it everywhere I find myself. Lord, send me around the world with the good news of your saving power. Thank you, Lord, for granting me boldness and utterance. In Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: Psalm 67:2 KJV, John 17:18-19 KJV, Isaiah 61:1 KJV
