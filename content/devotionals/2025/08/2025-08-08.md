title: Enhancing the Fellowship of the Holyspirit
scripture: 2 Corinthians 13:14 NKJV
verse: The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen.
body:
  - The Holy Spirit assures us of our salvation and inheritance in Christ (Ephesians 1:13-14 KJV). The Holy Spirit is not a monument; He is active and alive in our daily walk as believers. He indwells, guides, and empowers. He is not passive. He desires our attention and delights in constant communion with us.
  - Your body is the temple and dwelling place of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19). Therefore, glorify God with your body. A temple is a sacred place dedicated to the worship of God. Dedicate your body to the Lord and avoid any defilement or ungodly practices. The Lord expects you to stay away from carnal activities that do not glorify Him. The Holy Spirit has a distinct personality. He can be pleased or grieved. Do only what glorifies Him.
  - The extent to which the Holy Spirit operates in your life is dependent on you. This is determined by how much of the Word of God you have in you. The process begins with the renewal of your mind. Change your way of thinking and let your thoughts
  - align with scripture. As rightly said in Romans 12:2 NLT, Do not copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.
  - Psalm 42:1 NLT says, As the deer longs for streams of water, so I long for you, O God. This is a strong expression of desire for God. Develop a deep hunger to fellowship with the Lord through prayer, Bible study, meditation, and fellowship with other believers. As you do this consistently, you will build a rich and meaningful walk with the Holy Spirit.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank you for the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, your love, and the sweet fellowship of the Holy Spirit that will always be my experience. I thank you for your Spirit that you have poured out abundantly in my life. I will continue to love and glorify you, in Jesus' name, amen.
