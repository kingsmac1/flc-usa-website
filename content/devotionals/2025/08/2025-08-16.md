date: '2025-08-16'
title: Unequal Yoking
scripture: 1 Corinthians 10:21 KJV
verse: 'Ye cannot drink the cup of the Lord, and the cup of devils: ye cannot be partakers of the Lord''s table, and of the table of devils.'
body:
  - Have you ever seen believers who try to blend Christian practices with pagan or ungodly practices? Many times, some believers copy worldly ways and bring them into the church. That is not acceptable. Examples include Christians singing secular music in church or imitating worldly dance steps. These are practices inspired by the god of this world, and they are classified as idolatry too.
  - Unequal yoking refers to the incompatibility between a believer and an unbeliever, or between godly values and ungodly ones. Your devotion and commitment must be to Christ. You cannot compromise. You cannot be lukewarm. Do not participate in practices that do not glorify God. No matter who tries to persuade you, do not be a man-pleaser.
  - When I hear someone say a believer followed friends to a nightclub to celebrate a birthday, I wonder what the believer is looking for in such a place. Jesus is not there. Therefore, stay away from such environments.
  - 'In 1 Kings 11:4 KJV, For it came to pass, when Solomon was old, that his wives turned away his heart after other gods: and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of David his father. This is one of the reasons God was against marriage between the Jews (God''s people) and unbelievers. Such associations have creeping effects. They can influence you to accept ungodly ways, leading to spiritual compromise.'
  - 2 Corinthians 6:14 AMP says, Do not be unequally bound together with unbelievers (do not make mismatched alliances with them, inconsistent with your faith). For what partnership can righteousness have with lawlessness? Or what fellowship can light have with darkness? Even in your effort to win unbelievers, be mindful. Use discernment and allow the Holy Spirit to guide you.
prayer: The wisdom of God is working in me and the Holy Spirit guides me to deal wisely with everyone I meet. I am the light to those in darkness. I remain upright in Christ. I shine brightly, leading others to Christ. I maintain godly values and integrity always, in Jesus' name. Amen.
