---
date: '2025-08-01'
title: Speak Up for Jesus
scripture: John 7:13 NLT
verse: But no one had the courage to speak favorably about him in public, for they were afraid of getting in trouble with the Jewish leaders
body:
  - Not speaking up for Jesus and all He has done is a sign of ungratefulness, unfaithfulness, and cowardice. In John 7:13, the people knew Jesus, but they remained silent when it was time to speak up for Him in public. They were all afraid and no one said a word because they feared the scribes and the Pharisees.
  - Today, some people feel they are intellectuals, well informed, influential, or wealthy. These kinds of people often try to regulate or oppose your worship and expression about God. They are the Pharisees of our time. These modern Pharisees tend to despise the passion believers have for God. They find ways to make you feel that you are fanatical or overly zealous. All of these are subtle attempts to weaken your fire for God and make you lose your boldness to speak up for the Lord.
  - Timothy was almost a victim of such people, but Paul, being knowledgeable, encouraged him. That is why he wrote in 1 Timothy 4:12, Let no one despise your youth.
  - Many people talk about Jesus in private or in church, but when it
  - comes to making a public statement or affirmation of their faith, they feel embarrassed or remain silent. They do not want to be seen as different, lose friends, or forfeit benefits. This only reveals that they are not proud of Jesus and are ashamed to be identified with Him.
  - Many people are excited to take pictures and be associated with celebrities and the elites in society. But when it comes to standing for godly standards, the church, or anything related to God, they shy away. Everyone who acknowledges me publicly here on earth, I will also acknowledge before my Father in heaven (Matthew 10:32 NLT).
  - Be bold. Speak up for Jesus and all He has done for you. Defend His righteous cause. Speak up for godly standards and morals. Speak up to defend the Church. Do not be afraid of anything. The fear of man is a snare, but whoever trusts in the Lord is set securely on high (Proverbs 29:25).
prayer: I affirm that Jesus is my Lord! Thank you, Lord, for the public shame you went through and for being crucified so that I could be saved. Thank you for such great love. I will forever be grateful. I will always stand tall, speak up for you, and defend your name. In Jesus' name. Amen.
