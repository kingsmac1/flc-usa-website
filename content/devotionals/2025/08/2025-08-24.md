date: '2025-08-24'
title: Make Disciples For the Lord
scripture: Matthew 28:20 KJV
verse: Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you.
body:
  - The great commission is about soul winning, and soul winning is not complete until the person who got saved starts bringing others to the Lord. This involves discipling the soul you won. That is why when souls are won during a crusade, efforts are made to follow them up and ensure they find a Bible believing church near them.
  - Discipleship involves teaching the newborn souls the word of God and the way of life. This teaching encompasses moral, ethical, and spiritual aspects of life. This will help them to be established in Christ. You equip them for Christian service until they reach a unity of faith and knowledge of Christ to become mature (Ephesians 4:12 to 15).
  - In as much as these new believers are saved, you do not just leave them without raising and training them. Similar to how you care for a new born biological child, you patiently nurture, teach, encourage, intercede for them, get them involved in the church, and have oversight of what is going on with them. This is because the devil will still try to get them back into the world, distract them, and toss them around with different winds of
  - doctrine.
  - As someone who disciples others for the Lord, let your life be an example of a firm believer in words and manner (1 Timothy 4:12). Be a good example for others to emulate because this is the best approach to leadership.
  - John 15:16 KJV says, Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit should remain. God has chosen and ordained you for the great commission. Win souls, and the Lord will enable you to retain them so they do not fall back to their old lives of sin.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for making me an able minister of reconciliation. I will not be ashamed to share the gospel with others. As I talk to them about you, they get saved, and I will disciple them for you. I will also be fruitful unto you, in Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: Matthew 28:19 KJV, Colossians 1:10 KJV, Ephesians 4:10-16
