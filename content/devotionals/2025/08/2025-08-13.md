title: Living by Faith
scripture: Habakkuk 2:4 KJV
verse: 'Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by faith.'
body:
  - Are there times you rely on your own abilities, wealth, eloquence, beauty, fame, family, or other personal advantages? In Habakkuk 2:4, the phrase his soul which is lifted up refers to the prideful. The prideful person depends on their own strength and wisdom. They are self- reliant and arrogant.
  - Take for example, a student who has studied and prepared well for an examination. If the student goes in with full confidence that good grades will come simply because of personal effort, that is relying on self. This does not take away the place of hard work. God knows you have what it takes to execute a particular project, task, or assignment, but He still wants you to depend on Him and not on yourself. This is because your life is meant to give glory to God.
  - When you rely on yourself, you eventually stop giving glory to God. Then pride creeps in, and your heart becomes lifted. You begin to take credit for things that only God deserves praise for. This was the case of the Pharisees. They depended on self- righteousness and the Law, and their hearts became proud.
  - From our opening scripture, living by faith is set in contrast with being prideful. As a believer, you got saved by faith, and you must continue to live by faith in God and His word. You may have everything figured out, but God still requires you to trust Him (See Romans 1:17).
  - 'Maybe you are wondering, How do I live by faith? How do I rely on God when I can handle things by myself? Or I have tried living by faith before, and it didn''t work. The simple answer is this: as you open your heart to listen to, study, and meditate on God''s word, your faith will grow. You will begin to see things differently, and your faith will overcome anything that comes your way.'
  - As Romans 10:17 KJV rightly says, So then, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.
prayer: I declare that my faith is alive, and my faith prevails over every circumstance of life. I live daily by faith and not by depending on my strength and abilities. I depend solely on God's strength, which gives me the advantage.
