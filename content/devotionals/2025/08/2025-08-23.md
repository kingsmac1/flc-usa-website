title: Greater Works
scripture: Matthew 28:19–20 KJV
verse: 'Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you…'
body:
  - The scripture above is commonly known as the Great Commission. John 14:12 AMP says, I assure you and most solemnly say to you, anyone who believes in Me as Saviour will also do the things that I do; and he will do even greater things than these in extent and outreach, because I am going back to the Father. Jesus was a soul winner. He discipled people to continue in His ways. He was able to carry out His earthly ministry successfully because of the presence and power of the Holy Spirit.
  - 'As detailed in Acts 10:38 KJV, How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with Him. The Holy Spirit brings power for effective ministry. As a believer, the same Holy Spirit that was upon Jesus is now at work in you. He enables you to do greater works.'
  - Jesus has empowered you to do greater works than He did. This does not mean you will be more anointed than Jesus or that you will perform more miracles than He did. Rather, He was referring to the reach and impact of your ministry.
  - Jesus could not physically evangelize in London, China, or the United States while He was on earth, but you can reach these places now. He did not gather one hundred thousand people at once, yet in our time there have been crusades where millions were in attendance. You can minister to people in places and on platforms that were impossible in His earthly ministry.
  - 'Acts 1:8 KJV says, But ye shall receive power after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me… The Holy Ghost has brought divine enablement and miracle-working power to your life and ministry. Step out in faith and reach more people for Jesus.'
