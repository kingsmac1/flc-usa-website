title: Total Reliance on the Holy Spirit
scripture: Romans 8:14 KJV
verse: For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.
body:
  - Reliance on the Holy Spirit is very important in your Christian living. When you got saved, it was the Holy Spirit that baptized you into Christ. Therefore, you need to depend on Him for guidance, spiritual growth, and fulfilment of God's purposes in and through you at different stages of life.
  - The Holy Spirit is your teacher, advocate, helper, standby, comforter, counselor, intercessor, and strengthener. These are the roles the Holy Spirit performs in the life of a believer, and you need them. This implies that without the Holy Spirit, life is hopeless and full of errors. You cannot live a successful Christian life without Him. That is why you need total reliance on Him.
  - The Holy Spirit empowers you to live holy, resist temptation, and produce the fruit of the recreated human spirit. Galatians 5:22- 23 KJV says, But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance. These are outward signs of the life of a Christian. Yet many believers do not have them. Rather, they exhibit fruits
  - of the flesh, which is very wrong.
  - If only you depend on the Holy Spirit, He will help you live a life that pleases God. There are many heights you cannot attain in life and many things God cannot do through you until you totally depend on the Holy Spirit. From Romans 8:14 KJV, For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. For you to be led, you have to submit first.
  - I pray for you today, as you submit to the Holy Spirit, He will guide you into your inheritance in Christ, in Jesus' name. Amen.
prayer: Thank you, precious Holy Spirit, for your ministry in my life. I submit totally to you to take control of everything about my life. May you use me for your glory and express the multifaceted dimensions and expressions in and through me, in Jesus' name. Amen.
