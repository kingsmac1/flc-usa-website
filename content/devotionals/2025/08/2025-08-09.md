title: Swift Spiritual Response
scripture: Job 33:14 KJV
verse: Although God speaks again and again, no one pays attention to what He says.
body:
  - In the military, there is a special unit known as the quick response force. It is trained for rapid deployment to handle emergencies or fast-developing situations. Likewise, in hospitals, there are medical teams trained to act swiftly in emergencies. In the realm of the spirit, there is also a need for swift spiritual responses to certain circumstances.
  - There are two kinds of spiritual swift responses. One is from God. This refers to how God responds to our prayers and needs in a timely manner. As seen in Isaiah 65:24 NLT, I will answer them before they even call to me. While they are still talking about their needs, I will go ahead and answer their prayers. The other kind is from us. This involves our ability to respond immediately to the promptings and instructions of the Holy Spirit.
  - He sends his command to the earth; his word runs swiftly (Psalm 147:15 NIV). This shows that God's word is full of power and goes forth to accomplish His purpose without delay. So why should man delay in responding to the promptings of the Holy Spirit? There are moments when a swift response is crucial
  - because lives and destinies may depend on it. At such times, any delay may be dangerous.
  - There are times when the Lord prompts you not to enter a place or to avoid a particular situation. Sometimes, He places a sudden urgency in your heart to begin to pray. When this happens, do not ignore it. Leave all other things and go to pray. That could be a divine moment to quench a spiritual fire or open a door of breakthrough.
  - Psalm 62:11 KJV says, God has spoken once; twice have I heard this. When God speaks, value it. Act immediately. His instructions are final and do not need to be repeated for confirmation. Train yourself to practice the presence of God and to respond quickly to His promptings. Obedience is the key. As you study God's word and stay in constant prayer, His voice and communications will become clearer to you.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your presence that always ministers to me. I declare that I will be swift in responding to your voice. When you speak, I will trust you and act on your words and promptings, in Jesus' name. Amen.
