date: '2025-08-14'
title: The Saving Power of the Gospel
scripture: Romans 1:16 KJV
verse: For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God unto salvation to everyone that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.
body:
  - The gospel is the good news of Jesus Christ's death and resurrection which offers salvation to all who believe. It is not merely a historical account. The gospel reveals the righteousness of God and His plan to reconcile mankind to Himself through Jesus Christ.
  - The gospel is the power of God that leads to salvation. When the gospel is preached, God's power becomes available to save everyone who believes it. The gospel carries divine authority and brings complete change. It saves from sin, from the consequences of sin, from the influence of Satan, and from eternal condemnation.
  - The more you share the gospel, the more it gains victory and dominance over lies, darkness, and opposing forces of the devil. As the gospel prevails, people and entire communities come into the light and begin to live for the glory of God. Have confidence in the gospel. It is our hope. Be active in sharing the gospel despite cultural and societal pressures. The world will
  - become a better place when more people know about Jesus.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for the saving power of the gospel. As I preach the gospel, people are liberated from darkness, falsehood, sin, and the grip of Satan. Grant me more opportunities to reach the lost and use me to reveal your counsel in Jesus' name. Amen!
