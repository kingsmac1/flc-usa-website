title: Trust in God’s Provision
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17 KJV
verse: 'Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.'
body:
  - Romans 10:9 KJV states, That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. This is the process of being saved, which is the same as being born again. The person is now born into Christ. That is why 2 Corinthians 5:17 uses the phrase if any man be in Christ.
  - 'When anyone makes the public declaration of the Lordship of Jesus and believes in their heart that God raised Him from the dead, such a person is baptized into Christ by the Holy Spirit because of their faith in Christ. That person becomes a child of God. If you are a believer in Jesus Christ, you are a child of God. The implication of being a child of God is this: the way your biological father cares for, provides for, plans for, and protects his little child, God will not only care for, provide for, and protect you more than your parents, He will also cause you to enjoy His great plans for you. You will enjoy His providence.'
  - 'As a believer, since God is your Father, you do not have any reason to worry about anything: how to raise the bills, the future,'
  - your health, and many other things. The key is to rest in His love. He loved you and cared for you even before you were born, and He still loves you. He loves you more than you can love and care for yourself.
  - Matthew 6:26 NIV says, Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they? God is actively involved in your affairs.
  - When the scripture says in Romans 12:2 TPT, Stop imitating the ideals and opinions of the culture around you, but be inwardly transformed by the Holy Spirit through a total reformation of how you think, it means no matter where you live, do not be influenced by the way people around you worry or chase after things. Rather, begin to think according to Matthew 6:26. God is your Father and He cares, provides, and protects you.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for your love towards me. Thank you for being so gracious, loving, and kind to me. I will bask in your love, knowing that you are actively involved in all my affairs. Glory to God!
