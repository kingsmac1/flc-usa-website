title: The Principle of Spiritual Fusion
scripture: Matthew 18:20 KJV
verse: For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.
body:
  - In science, nuclear fusion is the coming together of two or more atomic nuclei to produce a massive amount of energy. The energy generated by one nucleus cannot compare to the energy released when several nuclei are fused together. In Christianity, the principle of spiritual fusion can be applied to the coming together of two or more people in fellowship in the name of Jesus. This is also referred to as corporate fellowship.
  - Instances of spiritual fusion in the Bible include the union of marriage (Genesis 2:24), the body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12:12 to 13), and unity in the Spirit (Ephesians 4:3 to 4). All these examples underscore the importance of being united in purpose, spirit, and in love.
  - Mattew 18:20 KJV says, For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them. This reveals that when two or more believers gather for the purpose of Jesus and His Word, it reflects unity of purpose and confirms that God is there.
  - Does this mean God is not with you when you pray by yourself? Certainly not. God's presence is always with you (Matthew 28:20). But in the scripture above, Jesus was referring to corporate anointing. God can answer your prayer when you pray alone, but you will not enjoy the benefit of corporate anointing without being in the midst of other believers.
  - Do not be deceived by people who say the church is in their heart and that they can stay at home to pray and God will hear them. There is a unique spiritual empowerment that manifests only when believers gather in unity. This kind of unity permits the Spirit of God to move in a way that impacts lives. One person may be blessed because of the spiritual gifts that are transferred. Many more can receive healing, direction, and answers in such an atmosphere.
  - Therefore, it is paramount to not miss corporate gatherings of believers, especially church services anymore (Hebrews 10:25 ESV).
prayer: Thank you Lord for your word today. I receive these truths and I apply them in my life. Thank you for the corporate anointing that is always available when I fellowship with other believers. I will be committed and consistent in participating in the fellowship of believers. Amen!
