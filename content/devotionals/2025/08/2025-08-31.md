date: '2025-08-31'
title: Send me, Lord!
scripture: Isaiah 6:8 KJV
verse: Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me.
body:
  - In Matthew 28:20, our Lord Jesus Christ gave us a divine mandate to preach the gospel and make disciples of nations. This involves nurturing and teaching new believers the ways of Christ to ensure they become established in Him.
  - Souls are perishing and because of the short time left to reach the unsaved and the unchurched, the Lord is in need of people whom He will send as messengers to share His saving grace. Whenever you have needs, you ask God and He answers you. Now, God has a need. He needs you to witness for Him. Are you going to turn Him down or will you say, “Send me Lord?”
  - Psalm 67:2 TPT says, Send us out all over the world so that everyone, everywhere will discover your ways and know who you are and see your power to save. This was the prayer of David. Say this same prayer and ask God to send you as His oracle and mouthpiece.
  - You are the light of the world. You cannot be hidden and quiet anymore. Sit up and shine. Reflect the glory of the Lord by availing yourself to be used by Him. Isaiah 52:7 NLT says, How
  - beautiful on the mountain are the feet of the messenger who brings good news, the good news of peace and salvation, the news that the God of Israel reigns!
  - When you received the Holy Spirit, you received divine enablement to proclaim the gospel to everyone around you. Therefore, go out in the fullness of this power of the Holy Spirit and be that messenger for the Lord.
prayer: I affirm that I am God's messenger to the world. I go about proclaiming the gospel and the saving power of Jesus. I am bold and courageous for the Lord. I preach this gospel in and out of season by the enablement of the Holy Spirit. Thank you, Lord, for you are with me always. Amen!
furtherStudies: 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, Romans 1:16
readingPlan: Ps 90-96; 1 Co 7
author: Apostle Chuks
