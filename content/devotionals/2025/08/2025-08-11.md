title: The Breath of God
scripture: 2 Timothy 3:16 KJV
verse: All scripture is given by the inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness
body:
  - The authority and inerrancy of Scripture simply mean that the Scriptures are infallible and serve as the ultimate authority on matters of faith, Christian living, and the issues of life. I love the New International Version (NIV) rendition of 2 Timothy 3:16. It states, All scripture is God breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness. This reveals that all scripture (not some scripture) is divinely infused with the breath of God. They are not merely human writings.
  - Just like God breathed life into Adam and he became a living soul (Genesis 2:7), and in Ezekiel 37:5, how the breath of God brought life to the dead bones in the valley, so does the word of God bring life.
  - The word breath in Hebrew is ruach. It means spirit. This is also portrayed in Acts chapter 2, where the Holy Spirit came as a rushing mighty wind. The breath of God is the Holy Spirit, and He brings renewal of spirit, soul, and body, leading to restoration.
  - God's word is free from error. It is reliable, trustworthy, and
  - inspired by God. It is supreme. Through the Scriptures, we understand the mind of God and interpret the world around us, our experiences, and receive direction for all areas of life.
  - When you open up your heart to God's word, it changes you into what it talks about. This is because the Holy Spirit, which is the breath of God, is working on you. It also means the Holy Spirit overshadows you the same way He overshadowed Mary and she conceived. I pray for you today, may the breath of God fill you up right now and make His refreshing and tangible presence real to you in Jesus name. Amen.
