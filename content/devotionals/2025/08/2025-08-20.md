title: You are Divinely Enabled
scripture: Philippians 4:13 KJV
verse: I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.
body:
  - You are divinely enabled through Christ. Christ lives in you in the person of the Holy Spirit who forever abides in you. Because of His divine presence in your life, He gives you the strength you need every hour of the day to carry out any task or assignment.
  - There is an inexhaustible supply of strength and ability in you through the Holy Spirit. This is not a one-time supply of strength; rather, it is an unending stream that continually flows through your life. Because of this, impossibilities no longer exist for you. All you need to do is trust in the ability of the Holy Spirit in you.
  - This enablement of the Holy Spirit covers spiritual strength, physical strength, emotional strength, and moral strength. He strengthens you to achieve all good things and fulfill God's purpose for your life. Isaiah 40:31 AMP says, But those who wait for the LORD who expect, look for, and hope in Him will gain new strength and renew their strength... If you ever feel weary or exhausted, do not worry. God has already made provision for your strength to be renewed, and that renewal comes by waiting upon the Lord.
  - Waiting upon the Lord is an active way of patiently trusting Him with full expectation. It involves engaging in prayers, studying His Word, obeying Him, and looking forward to His intervention.
  - As you carry out your daily activities at work, school, ministry, or whatever responsibilities you are faced with, always have the mindset that you can achieve the desired result and even exceed it. Do not try to measure yourself by your natural abilities or mental capacity. Instead, depend on the Holy Spirit who knows how and the best way to divinely enable you.
prayer: I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me. I am divinely enabled, filled with divine skills, energy, strength, and abilities. I can accomplish whatever I set my heart to do. When I show up, solutions show up because the Holy Spirit enables me. Hallelujah!
