date: '2025-08-21'
title: Prevailing in the Mission Field
scripture: 2 Corinthians 4:4 KJV
verse: In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.
body:
  - Our opening Bible verse reveals that spiritual blindness is not the inability to see with the eyes. Rather, it is the inability to understand spiritual truth. It is a state of spiritual ignorance and resistance to truth from God's word.
  - Satan, who is called the god of this world, is the one responsible for spiritual blindness. He blocks the minds of people from understanding and accepting the gospel. He makes them resistant, stubborn and deaf to God's word. The devil bends minds through manipulation. He twists people's thoughts, causing them to argue blindly. This is why some individuals keep hearing the gospel and even hear about Jesus often, yet they turn back to their old ways.
  - Some time ago, I pioneered a church in a particular city. It was obvious that much effort had gone into evangelism, outreach and prayers. Many people were excited. Some got saved and several promised to come to church, but on service days they were nowhere to be found. One night, the Lord showed me a vision. In the vision, I saw a veil covering the mind of someone I had invited to church. After that vision, I was glad because God
  - had shown me the real problem. Then I began a strategic spiritual warfare against the satanic forces that had taken hold of people's minds in that city.
  - Have you ever wondered why it seems like some places are known for specific kinds of evil? There could be a particular town, city or state where prostitution, crime, accidents, violence, divorce, poverty and theft are very common. Satanic forces have made such places their home.
  - For things to improve in families, economies, homes and ministries in such areas, someone needs to rise up and pray. They need to fight in the spirit like Daniel did in Daniel 10:13 to 11:1. Through your prayers, God will open the minds of people to receive the gospel. They will be saved, and the word of the Lord will shine light into their hearts and deliver them from darkness and spiritual blindness.
prayer: I declare in the name of Jesus, I bind every satanic power of darkness that has blinded the minds of people in my city, state and nation. I command it to lose its grip. I pray that light will come to them through the gospel. I declare the souls of men and women are reclaimed for the Lord. The works of the Lord will prevail in Jesus' name. Amen.
