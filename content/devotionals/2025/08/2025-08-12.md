title: Crucifying the Flesh
scripture: Galatians 2:20 NIV
verse: I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.
body:
  - Crucifying the flesh does not mean killing yourself. Although that may sound strange, it is closely related. Here, you are not dealing with the physical body, but with the ungodly desires that your body wants to express itself through.
  - Every believer, everyone in Christ, died with Christ. This means that the old self with its sinful desires and nature is dead. Every believer also resurrected with Christ, meaning that just as Christ rose up with a glorified and renewed body, we also rose with a new identity and renewed nature. This new nature is called the life and nature of God. It is now our responsibility to live out this new nature. Just as it is the nature of dogs to bark, it is our nature as believers to act like Christ.
  - Romans 6:4 AMP says We have therefore been buried with Him through baptism into death, so that just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory and power of the Father, we too might walk habitually in newness of life, abandoning our old ways. Abandoning our old ways means forsaking the former
  - lifestyle and desires.
  - Ephesians 4:22 to 23 NLT says Throw off your old sinful nature and your former way of life, which is corrupted by lust and deception. Instead, let the Spirit renew your thoughts and attitudes. Crucifying the flesh and renewing your thoughts and attitudes is the transformation work of the Holy Spirit. It only happens if you permit Him. This is not a one-time event. It is a continuous process. You replace your ways of thinking with God's word. Doing this will give you new ways of thinking, communication, and action, all to the glory of God.
prayer: I am a new creation in Christ Jesus. My old sinful nature and desires are gone. I have a new nature now. I have new desires, new ways of thinking, new ways of communication, and a new lifestyle as Christ. I produce works of righteousness in Jesus' name. Amen.
