date: '2025-08-03'
title: Your Decisions Have External Consequences
scripture: Joshua 24:14 KJV
verse: 'Therefore fear the Lord, and serve him in sincerity and in truth: and put away the gods which your fathers served on the other side of the flood, and in Egypt; and serve ye the Lord.'
body:
  - The choices you make in life, whether small or big, good or bad, can have lasting impact not only on your current physical and spiritual life, but also on your eternal destiny. Joshua 24:14, as seen in the opening scripture, is a call to reverence and respect God's authority and holiness. It is a call to serve Him wholeheartedly and to reject syncretism. Syncretism means the mixing of beliefs and practices that do not glorify God.
  - Sadly, there are many believers who still believe in astral projection, zodiac signs, yoga, communication with the dead, and other unscriptural practices. Some still keep in their possession ungodly mystical books, items such as talismans, amulets, magic pendants, and inherited ornaments with strange designs and symbols. God is not pleased with your decision to continue holding on to or believing in such things. Forfeit all of them and be faithful to God.
  - Matthew 7:14 ESV says, For the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life, and those who find it are few. The path
  - that leads to eternal life with the Father is not wide or popular. It is narrow, requiring discipline, sacrifice, and commitment to the teachings of God's Word. On the other hand, the wide path is a life of moral laxity, worldly indulgence, and rejection of Christ's values. Its consequences include eternal separation from God. It is a place no one should end up.
  - Your decision to accept Christ, live for Him, and bear fruit as Christ did is the best decision you can ever make. You will be rewarded by the Father when you stand before Him.
prayer: I declare that I have made the decision to follow and live for Jesus Christ all the days of my life. I declare Jesus is Lord over my heart. I am strengthened by the Lord and will always bear godly fruit and make godly decisions. In Jesus' name, Amen.
