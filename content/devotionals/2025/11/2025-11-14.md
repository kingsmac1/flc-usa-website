date: '2025-11-14'
title: All Things Work Together For Your Good
scripture: Romans 8:28 (NIV)
verse: And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.
body:
  - Life doesn't always make sense in the moment. Sometimes we go through seasons of loss, confusion, or disappointment, wondering if God sees us at all. Yet Scripture reminds us that even when life feels chaotic, God is still at work, quietly weaving every detail into a greater purpose. Romans 8:28 assures us that all things, not just the good or pleasant, work together for the benefit of those who love God and are called by Him.
  - It's natural to ask, “Why did this happen to me?” But a deeper response of faith says, “Lord, I don't understand, but I trust You.” Your pain is not wasted. God doesn't cause evil, but He does redeem it. In Genesis 50:20, Joseph told his brothers, “You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good...” Joseph's story reminds us that betrayal, injustice, and hardship are no match for God's purpose. What the enemy meant for destruction, God can flip for destiny.
  - At the cross, Jesus endured the greatest injustice in human history. Yet out of His suffering came the greatest salvation. 2 Corinthians 4:17 reminds us, “For our light and momentary
  - troubles are achieving for us an eternal glory that far outweighs them all.” God's perspective is eternal. He sees the end from the beginning, and He's building something beautiful, even through your brokenness.
  - There are seasons where it feels like your life is falling apart, when in reality, it's falling into place. You may not see the full picture now, but God does. He's not just the God of good days, He's also God during dark nights, delays, detours, and divine surprises.
  - 'You may not understand everything you''re walking through today, but you can be confident of this: God is working behind the scenes for your good. His plans are better, bigger, and more beautiful than you can imagine. He knows how to bring meaning out of your troubles, healing out of your hurt, and purpose out of your pain. What situation in your life currently feels confusing, painful, or unfair? Surrender it to God today. Let go of needing all the answers and, instead, trust in the Author of your story. He''s not finished yet.'
prayer: Dear Heavenly Father, I Thank you for all things are working together for my good. Even when things get complex, I know you are in control. I will not be shaken, because I know you write beautiful endings. I walk by faith and not by sight, knowing that greater things are ahead. Amen!
