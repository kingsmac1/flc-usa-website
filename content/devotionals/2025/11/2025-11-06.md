title: Be Temperate in All Things
scripture: 1 Corinthians 9:25 (KJV)
verse: And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible.
body:
  - 'To be temperate is to walk in self-governance under the leadership of the Holy Spirit. It simply means exercising restraint, balance, and discipline in every aspect of life: our habits, emotions, appetites, and responses. The apostle Paul compares the Christian life to that of an athlete in training. Just as runners who aim to win must resist distractions and impulses to keep their focus, so must believers live intentionally to receive the eternal prize that God promises.'
  - Temperance is often misunderstood as rigid restriction, but in truth, it is a gateway to freedom. A temperate person is not bound by cravings, emotional outbursts, or constant overindulgence. Instead, they operate with quiet strength, making decisions not out of impulse but out of wisdom. They know when to speak and when to stay silent, when to work and when to rest, when to give and when to withhold. This balance is not natural; it is cultivated through spiritual maturity and daily submission to God's guidance. God calls us to be temperate in all things, not just in visible areas like eating or spending, but in our thoughts, activities, relationships, ambitions, and even in how we handle success or adversity. A temperate believer does not allow highs to make them proud or lows to make them bitter. They remain steady, anchored in the truth of God's Word. This steadiness is a mark of those who are growing in grace and walking by the Spirit.
  - 'It''s important to note that this kind of self-control isn''t something we muster on our own. It is part of the fruit of the Spirit: something that grows in us as we yield daily to Christ. We are not striving for mastery by mere willpower; we are being transformed into His likeness, empowered to say "no" to what hinders us and "yes" to what pleases Him. The temperate life is not just more peaceful; it is more powerful.'
  - 'In a culture that promotes excess, temperance is a testimony. When others are driven by greed, lust, and ego, the believer who walks in self-control reflects the character of Christ. This is how we shine: not by being louder, but by being grounded. And in due time, those who have walked this path will receive the crown that never fades.'
