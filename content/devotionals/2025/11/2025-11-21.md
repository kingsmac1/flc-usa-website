title: Be Selfless
scripture: Philippians 2:3 (NIV)
verse: Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility, value others above yourselves.
body:
  - 'Selflessness is the act of putting others'' needs, interests, or well-being ahead of your own, without expecting anything in return. It is a key virtue in the Christian life and a reflection of God''s love at work in us. Your existence is not just about yourself. Selflessness goes against the grain of human nature. From a young age, we are naturally inclined to look after ourselves first. But the gospel calls us to a radical shift: to consider others before ourselves. This doesn''t mean we neglect our own needs, but that we willingly prioritize love and service. At the heart of selflessness is humility: a heart posture that says, “I don''t always have to come first.”'
  - 'One of the most powerful examples of selflessness is found in the life of Jesus. In Mark 10:45, He said, “For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.” Jesus had every right to demand honor and recognition, yet He chose a life of service. His ultimate act of selflessness was laying down His life on the cross. This is the standard He sets for us: not comfort, but compassion.'
  - Another inspiring example is the Good Samaritan in Luke 10:33–35. When others passed by a wounded man, it was the Samaritan (considered an outsider) who stopped, showed compassion, and went the extra mile. He didn't just offer temporary help; he took full responsibility for the man's care. His actions remind us that selflessness often shows up in practical, inconvenient moments. Real love interrupts our schedules and costs us something.
  - Paul also modeled and taught selflessness. In 1 Corinthians 10:24, he wrote, “No one should seek their own good, but the good of others.” This principle shaped his ministry. He lived not for applause or personal comfort but to build others up. Whether preaching, traveling, or writing letters, Paul gave his life to serve others and glorify Christ. Selflessness requires intentionality. It doesn't happen by accident.
  - Living selflessly doesn't mean you become a doormat. It means you choose love over pride, service over selfishness, and giving over gaining. When you live this way, you're not only blessing others; you're building eternal impact. Ask the Holy Spirit to show you where you've been overly focused on yourself, and He should reshape your heart.
prayer: My life is sold out to the LORD. I will not live for myself. Rather, I will live a selfless life. I will follow the example of Jesus and serve others with love and humility. I will not seek my own gain, but the good of those around me. I trust that as I pour out, God will always pour into me. My life is not my own; it belongs to Christ and His purpose.
