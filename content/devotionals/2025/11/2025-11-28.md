date: '2025-11-28'
title: You Are a Product of Grace
scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:10 (KJV)
verse: 'But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain...'
body:
  - Your life is living proof of what God's grace can accomplish. Everything you are, everything you have overcome, and everything you continue to grow into is because of His divine grace. You are not merely a result of effort or determination; grace has carried you, sustained you, and positioned you for purpose. Where strength failed, grace stepped in. Where you lacked direction, grace gave you alignment.
  - 'Without Christ, we were lost. We were caught in sin, separated from God, and unable to make ourselves right before Him. The Scriptures describe humanity as dead in trespasses, alienated from God, and deserving of judgment. Yet, even in that broken state, God extended His grace. He gave us what we didn''t deserve: forgiveness, restoration, and adoption into His family. Grace is not a reward for good behavior; it''s a gift for the undeserving, a divine invitation into new life.'
  - Grace doesn't stop at salvation; it is active in our daily walk. It strengthens us in weakness, gives wisdom for decisions, and empowers us to grow in Christlikeness. The more we depend on God's grace, the more we realize it is His ability working in and through us. Grace changes how we view our past, how we handle today, and how we hope for tomorrow. It is God's presence enabling us to become who He's called us to be.
  - This truth should lead to a posture of humility. You are not self- made; you are grace-made. When you see others struggling, remember it is grace, and not your strength, that made the difference. Let this reality deepen your compassion and ignite your gratitude. God's grace is still working in you, shaping your future and aligning you with His purpose.
  - Keep yielding to God's grace. Let it refine you, strengthen you, and keep you anchored. You don't have to strive to prove your worth because grace already affirms it. Walk confidently, not because of who you are in the natural, but because of who God is in you. You are not just surviving. You are growing, thriving, and becoming all that grace empowers you to be.
prayer: I am who I am because of the grace of God. God's favor surrounds me, His strength empowers me, and His love sustains me. I do not depend on my ability, but on His grace working in me daily. I walk in freedom, purpose, and peace, knowing that His grace is more than enough. Amen!
