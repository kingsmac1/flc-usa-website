title: Keep Your Heart Pure Before God
scripture: Matthew 5:8 (NKJV)
verse: Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.
body:
  - Your heart is the wellspring of your life. Everything you say, do, and pursue flows from the condition of your heart. In Scripture, purity of heart isn't just about innocence; it's about singleness of purpose, genuine love for God, and a life that seeks to honor Him. Jesus taught that the pure in heart will see God, not just in eternity, but even now, in their daily lives and decisions.
  - The lives of Samuel and Eli's sons provide a great contrast. Eli's sons, though raised in the tabernacle and given spiritual responsibility, lived corrupt lives. 1 Samuel 2:12 says, “Eli's sons were scoundrels; they had no regard for the Lord.” They were greedy, immoral, and disrespectful of God's laws. Their hearts were impure, and eventually, their actions brought judgment upon themselves and their family.
  - In contrast, young Samuel served faithfully in the house of God from a young age. He listened to God's voice, obeyed His word, and honored His presence. 1 Samuel 2:26 tells us, “And the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favor with the Lord and with people.” Samuel's life was marked by reverence, humility, and purity. As he grew, God entrusted him with prophetic insight, national leadership, and priestly duties, all because his heart was postured rightly before the Lord.
  - Proverbs 4:23 commands, “Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.” What you allow into your heart will shape your thoughts, relationships, and spiritual life. A pure heart is not a perfect heart, but a heart that consistently seeks God, repents quickly, and resists compromise. Like Samuel, we must choose daily to walk in honor, truth, and reverence.
  - Keeping your heart pure means resisting the spirit of this age (greed, selfish ambition, lust, and pride) and instead, cultivating holiness, gratitude, and integrity. It's not just about doing right things; it's about being right with God in the secret place. When your heart is clean before God, you become a vessel fit for honor. Like Samuel, choose to be set apart not just in action, but in the unseen depths of your heart.
prayer: My heart is sanctified and I guard my heart pure before God. I will not be swayed by the world's ways or compromise my integrity. My heart is devoted to the Lord, and I walk in truth, humility, and reverence. Like Samuel, I will grow in wisdom and favor as I stay faithful to God's Word in Jesus' name. Amen! FURTHER
