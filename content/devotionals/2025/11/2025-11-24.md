title: Be Generous with Kindness
scripture: Colossians 3:12 TPT
verse: ...Be merciful as you endeavor to understand others, and be compassionate, showing kindness toward all. Be gentle and humble, unoffendable in your patience with others.
body:
  - Kindness is more than a polite gesture; it is a fruit of the Spirit and a reflection of God's character. In a world often marked by self-interest and indifference, generosity in kindness stands out as a powerful witness to Christ's love.
  - The account of Naomi and Ruth beautifully illustrates this. After losing her husband and sons, Naomi was left bitter and broken. Yet Ruth, her Moabite daughter-in-law, chose to stay by her side. Ruth's famous words, “Where you go, I will go; where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God” (Ruth 1:16) reflect a heart full of loyalty, love, and kindness. This was not a convenient decision for Ruth. Staying with Naomi meant choosing uncertainty, poverty, and likely rejection in a foreign land. But her kindness wasn't dependent on circumstances. It flowed from a deep, sacrificial love.
  - '1 Corinthians 13:4, says “Love is patient, love is kind...” Ruth''s love was just that; patient and kind, even when it cost her something. Later, Naomi became a vessel of kindness herself. In Ruth 3, we see her guiding Ruth with wisdom and care: “My'
  - 'daughter, should I not try to find a home for you, where you will be well provided for?” (Ruth 3:1, NIV). Even in her grief, Naomi thought of Ruth''s well-being, teaching us that kindness doesn''t wait for everything in life to be perfect. Philippians 2:4 encourages this mindset: “Let each of you look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others.”'
  - Boaz, too, showed extravagant kindness when he noticed Ruth gleaning in his fields. In Ruth 2:12, he says, “May the Lord repay you for what you have done. May you be richly rewarded by the Lord, the God of Israel, under whose wings you have come to take refuge.” He protected her, provided for her, and eventually became her redeemer.
  - 'In your everyday life, ask yourself: "Where can I be more generous with kindness?" It may be a listening ear to a friend, a gentle response to someone difficult, or a helping hand to someone in need. Like Ruth, our acts of kindness might seem small, but in God''s hands, they become part of something much bigger.'
prayer: I am a vessel of God's kindness. I choose to love generously, speak gently, and act selflessly. Just like Ruth, I will walk in kindness even when it costs me something. My kindness will reflect the heart of Christ in every situation in Jesus' name, Amen.
