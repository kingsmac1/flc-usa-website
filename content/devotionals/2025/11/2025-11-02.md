title: 'Offence: The Bait of the Enemy'
scripture: Psalm 119:165 (KJV)
verse: 'Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them.'
body:
  - 'Offence is one of the most subtle and effective tools the enemy uses to derail believers from their divine course. It starts small: a word spoken out of turn, a gesture misread, a moment overlooked. And if not checked, it festers into resentment, bitterness, and spiritual stagnation. Jesus warned in Luke 17:1 that offences will surely come, but our response determines whether we grow or stumble. The enemy doesn''t need to destroy you directly; he just needs you to take the bait of offence and you''ll sabotage yourself.'
  - When a believer harbors offence, their heart becomes a breeding ground for unforgiveness. It clouds discernment, chokes joy, and stifles spiritual growth. The danger lies in how justifiable it can feel. “They hurt me,” “They should've known better,” or “They don't respect me”. These thoughts are baited hooks. Yet, the kingdom of God operates differently. Jesus, though wrongfully accused, beaten, and crucified, declared, “Father, forgive them.” He modeled a higher way, calling us to respond not from the flesh, but by the Spirit.
  - True freedom begins where offence ends. Releasing offence doesn't mean what was done was right; it simply means you're choosing not to let it control your spirit. The enemy's goal is always division between friends, spouses, churches, and even you and God. When you carry offence, it distorts your view of people and often of God Himself. But when you reject the bait, you disarm the enemy. You choose peace over poison, growth over grudge, and maturity over misery. The cure for offence is “love”. The Psalmist declares that those who “love” God's law have great peace and are not easily offended. This peace is not circumstantial; it's supernatural. It is the result of a heart anchored in truth and shielded by grace. When you value God's perspective over your emotions, you become unshakable. Offences may come, but they won't find a resting place in you.
  - Refuse to take the bait. Lay down offence, pick up forgiveness, and move forward in grace. You were not called to be easily offended; you were called to walk in love, patience, and power. Let your heart remain tender before God, but tough against the schemes of the enemy.
prayer: Father, I refuse to take the bait of offence. I release every hurt, every word, and every act done against me. I choose love over bitterness and peace over pride. I declare that my heart is guarded by Your Word, and nothing shall offend me. I walk in freedom, healing, and maturity through Christ Jesus. Amen.
