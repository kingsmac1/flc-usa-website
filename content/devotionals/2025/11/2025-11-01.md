date: '2025-11-01'
title: Be a Person of Knowledge
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:3 (KJV)
verse: 'Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.'
body:
  - God is a God of knowledge. He weighs not just what we do, but why we do it. This means knowledge is not optional for the believer; it's essential. To walk with God effectively, we must pursue knowledge with intentionality and humility. Ignorance is dangerous, especially when cloaked in pride. As Hosea 4:6 warns, "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge." Notice it says “My people” and not the world. This shows that even God's own can perish if they choose ignorance over understanding.
  - Zeal is not a substitute for knowledge. Many are passionate but misinformed, sincere yet sincerely wrong. Romans 10:2 speaks of those who have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge. This kind of zeal can completely lead to error, misrepresentation of truth, or even spiritual burnout. Passion must be grounded in truth. When knowledge is absent, even good intentions can cause harm. But when zeal is rooted in revelation, it becomes a powerful force for change and impact.
  - 'The Word of God paints a clear contrast between the wise and the foolish. Proverbs repeatedly tells us that fools despise wisdom and instruction. A foolish person ignores correction and avoids learning. But the wise person seeks understanding and values counsel. To grow in knowledge is to grow in stature: spiritually, emotionally, and even materially. Proverbs 24:5 says, “A wise man is strong; yea, a man of knowledge increaseth strength.”'
  - 'Knowledge not only strengthens but distinguishes. Those who apply knowledge become problem solvers, solution providers, and leaders in their generation. The Bible says in Proverbs 8:18, “Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.” This speaks of wisdom personified. When you value knowledge and apply it rightly, honor and lasting success will follow. Ignorance will always limit, but knowledge will always elevate. Make it your daily pursuit to grow in knowledge: the knowledge of God, of His Word, and of the world around you. Study, ask questions, read widely, and remain teachable. Knowledge is a key that opens doors. When you walk in knowledge, you don''t just survive, you lead, you influence, and you reign. Don''t just be zealous; be knowledgeable. In a world filled with noise, wisdom will set you apart. Seek knowledge, apply it, and let your life reflect the wisdom of God'
prayer: Lord, I thank You that You are a God of knowledge. I reject ignorance and embrace wisdom. The Holy spirit fills me with insight, understanding, and discernment. I confidently walk in the knowledge of God's word always and refuse all forms of mediocrity in Jesus' name, Amen!
