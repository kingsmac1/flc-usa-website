date: '2025-11-10'
title: Let God Rule in Your Life
scripture: Romans 12:2 (NKJV)
verse: Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God.
body:
  - True transformation doesn't begin with changing your habits, image, or environment; it begins in your heart and mind. Many people try to fix their lives from the outside, but lasting change only comes when you allow God to take His rightful place within. Letting God rule in your life means surrendering not just your actions, but your motives, thoughts, desires, and decisions.
  - Romans 12:2 tells us to “be transformed by the renewing of your mind”, not just your behavior. When God rules in your life, His Word reshapes how you think and see the world. This transformation isn't surface-level; it's deep, spiritual, and progressive. The Holy Spirit begins to align your thoughts with God's truth, your values with His kingdom, and your goals with His purpose.
  - 'In Ezekiel 36:26, God makes a beautiful promise: “I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you…” When you invite God to rule in your life, He doesn''t just improve your behavior; He gives you a new heart. That means your earthly desires change, your convictions deepen, and your love for righteousness grows. It''s not about religious performance; it''s about a surrendered relationship.'
  - The lordship of Jesus is not partial; it's total. Galatians 2:20 says, “It is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me…” When He rules in your life, He governs your priorities, guides your steps, and guards your peace. It's not a forced submission but a joyful yielding. As you walk with Him daily, His character begins to be formed in you. That's what it means to be transformed.
  - 'God doesn''t just want to visit your life on Sundays; He wants to rule daily. And when He does, everything begins to shift. You begin to reflect Christ not just in what you do, but in who you are. Transformation starts within, but its fruit is seen outwardly: in how you love, lead, speak, and live. Is God ruling every area of your life or are there parts you''re still managing on your own? Let this be a moment of surrender. Ask the Lord to renew your mind, purify your heart, and lead you in making decisions. Transformation is a journey, but it begins with a “yes.” Say yes to His rule, and trust Him to do a deep, lasting work from the inside out.'
