date: '2025-11-15'
title: Be a Person of Admirable Qualities
scripture: 1 Peter 3:3–4 (NKJV)
verse: Do not let your adornment be merely outward... rather let it be the hidden person of the heart, with the incorruptible beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is very precious in the sight of God.
body:
  - True beauty has nothing to do with outer appearance; it flows from the heart. Character is the fragrance of a person's inner life. Good character is like a sweet- smelling aroma; it draws others in, leaves a lasting impression, and honors God. In contrast, bad character repels, even if hidden behind charisma. As believers, we are called to deliberately develop admirable qualities that reflect Christ in our daily conduct.
  - In Galatians 5:22–23, Paul describes the fruit of the Spirit as love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. These are not just moral ideals; they are spiritual qualities produced in us by the Holy Spirit as we walk closely with God. When someone possesses these traits, their life becomes refreshing and admirable, much like a beautiful fragrance in a room full of noise and chaos.
  - In Proverbs 22:1, it says, “A good name is more desirable than great riches; to be esteemed is better than silver or gold.” Your reputation flows directly from your character. A person of integrity, humility, and honesty may not always be the most noticed, but they are always the most respected. Your life should speak louder than your words. Character is not built overnight; it's built by consistent choices that align with God's truth.
  - Jesus, our ultimate example, was admired not just for His power, but for His purity, humility, and love. He was approachable, trustworthy, compassionate, and sincere. People followed Him not just for miracles, but because of His heart. Philippians 2:5 urges us, “Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus.” To be Christlike is to live with admirable qualities not for applause, but because it honors the One who lives in us. You don't stumble into good character; you develop it intentionally. It takes self-awareness, repentance, humility, and the willingness to grow. If your attitude, words, or habits have been less than admirable, don't make excuses. Let the Holy Spirit refine you. Ask Him to help you become someone whose life releases the sweet aroma of Christ wherever you go. Don't just pray to be used by God; ask to be shaped by Him.
prayer: I am a person of admirable character. The fragrance of Christ flows from my life. I reject bad attitudes, selfishness, and pride. By the help of the Holy Spirit, I am being transformed day by day into the image of Christ. My life will leave a Godly legacy in Jesus' name. Amen!' name, Amen!
