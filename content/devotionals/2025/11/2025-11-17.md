date: '2025-11-17'
title: Take Initiative
scripture: James 2:17 (NIV)
verse: In the same way, faith by itself, if it is not accompanied by action, is dead.
body:
  - God has given every believer the ability to think, act, and move in alignment with His purpose. While prayer is foundational to our walk with God, it was never meant to be a replacement for action. Many believers are waiting for a miracle, but God is often waiting for them to take initiative. Faith is not passive; it is active. It listens, believes, and moves. A prayerful life must also be a productive one.
  - In James 2:17, the Word makes it clear that “faith without works is dead.” This means that belief alone, without corresponding action, produces no results. For example, you can believe God will provide you with a job, but if you never apply, update your skills, or seek opportunities, your faith becomes empty. God partners with your actions to bring His will to life. You pray, but you also move.
  - Take the example of Nehemiah, who didn't just weep over Jerusalem's broken walls; he did something about it. After praying and fasting, he went to the king, made a request, gathered resources, organized workers, and rebuilt the wall. Nehemiah's story in Nehemiah 2:4–5 shows a clear balance of spiritual devotion and practical steps. He prayed, but he also planned. That's initiative, and that's faith in motion.
  - Even in the New Testament, we see initiative in the life of the paralytic's friends. In Mark 2:4, they didn't wait for the crowd to clear or for someone to invite them forward; they tore open the roof to lower their friend to Jesus. That's not just faith; that's determined, creative action. And Jesus responded! Taking initiative often requires courage, creativity, and perseverance, but it gets heaven's attention.
  - Of course, we must always remain sensitive to the Holy Spirit's leading. There are moments when God may instruct us to “be still,” to wait, or to hold off. But when there is no such restraint, we must act. Don't fold your hands waiting for change; be the vessel God uses to create it. Ask for wisdom, take a step, solve problems, and move forward. Miracles often meet us in motion. What area of your life have you been waiting on God, when in truth, God is waiting on you? Whether it's starting a business, restoring a relationship, applying for school, or helping someone in need, don't delay!. Pray, plan, and pursue. Faith- filled action honors God. Take the first step and trust that He will guide the rest.
prayer: MY FAITH is alive. I take initiative and refuse to fold my hands and wait for what God has already empowered me to pursue. I am led by the Spirit, and I will take action aligned with God's will for my life. I am proactive and filled with divine ideas. I will execute them and have amazing results. Glory to GOD!
