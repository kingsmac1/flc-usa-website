date: '2025-11-25'
title: Be a Person of Integrity
scripture: Proverbs 11:3 (NIV)
verse: The integrity of the upright guides them, but the unfaithful are destroyed by their duplicity.
body:
  - Integrity is the inner compass that guides your life when no one else is watching. It's choosing honesty over convenience, righteousness over compromise, and truth over deception. In today's world, where moral lines are often blurred, integrity stands out like a bright light in darkness. God is not just looking for gifted people; He's looking for those whose hearts and actions reflect His character. He values integrity deeply, and He blesses those who live by it.
  - Proverbs 10:9 says, “Whoever walks in integrity walks securely, but whoever takes crooked paths will be found out.” This scripture reminds us that integrity brings a deep sense of peace and security. When your actions are aligned with truth, you don't have to worry about being exposed or caught in a lie. There is a freedom in living transparently. On the other hand, dishonesty sets traps that eventually ensnare the one who set them. God's desire is that we walk on a straight path; one that brings stability and safety to our lives.
  - David asked in Psalm 15:1–2, “Lord, who may dwell in your sacred tent? Who may live on your holy mountain?” The answer? “The one whose walk is blameless, who does what is righteous, who speaks the truth from their heart.” Integrity is more than doing the right thing; it's about being the right kind of
  - person. It's about having truth rooted in your heart, not just in your words. God delights in those who reflect His truth in both speech and action. Daniel is a model of integrity in the Old Testament. Daniel 6:4 says, “They could find no corruption in him, because he was trustworthy and neither corrupt nor negligent.” Even when surrounded by enemies, Daniel's character could not be questioned. He was faithful, diligent, and unwavering in his commitment to God and his responsibilities. His integrity didn't just protect him; it positioned him for promotion. Integrity often brings trials, but it also attracts the favor of God.
  - God is calling His people to rise above the standards of the world. Integrity might not always be popular or easy, but it is always right. Whether in business, family, ministry, or personal life, be the same person behind closed doors as you are in public. Don't cut corners, don't cover up sin, and don't compromise truth. Let your yes be yes and your no be no. In a world where there is noise and deception, your integrity will be your loudest sermon.
prayer: I am a person of integrity. My character reflects Christ, and my life is guided by honesty and righteousness. I choose integrity over compromise, and I will stand for truth in every season of life In Jesus name, Amen!
