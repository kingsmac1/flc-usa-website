date: '2025-11-13'
title: Endeavour to Finish Well
scripture: 2 Timothy 4:7 (NIV)
verse: I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.
body:
  - Starting strong is important, but finishing well is essential. Many people begin a journey, a relationship, a calling, or an assignment with enthusiasm and great hope. But as time passes, distractions, challenges, pride, or compromise begin to chip away at their consistency. The truth is, how you end matters more than how you begin. Scripture is filled with both inspiring and sobering examples that serve as lessons for our own walk.
  - We read of people like King Saul, who was chosen by God and anointed as Israel's first king. He started with humility but later lost his way through disobedience and insecurity. In 1 Samuel 15:11, God said, “I regret that I have made Saul king, because he has turned away from me.” Saul's story warns us that spiritual failure often begins when we stop obeying God's voice. A great start does not guarantee a great finish without continued surrender.
  - 'Samson is another example. Gifted with supernatural strength and destined for greatness, he allowed compromise, pride, and relationships to pull him off track. Though God still used him in his final moment, his life was marked by inconsistency. Similarly, Solomon, the wisest man who ever lived, allowed foreign influences and idolatry to corrupt his later years. 1 Kings 11:4 says, “His heart was not fully devoted to the Lord his God, as the heart of David his father had been.” These stories remind us to stay alert and intentional about our spiritual walk. In contrast, the Apostle Paul stands as a powerful example of someone who finished well. Despite intense persecution, hardship, and opposition, Paul remained faithful to his call. In 2 Timothy 4:7, near the end of his life, he wrote: “I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.” Finishing well isn''t about being perfect; it''s about remaining faithful, obedient, and committed to God''s purpose until the very end.'
  - 'Wherever you are today, let this be your prayer: “Lord, help me finish well.” It''s not enough to begin with zeal; you need God''s grace, wisdom, and strength to endure and complete what He has entrusted to you. Are there areas in your life where you started strong but lost momentum? Ask God to reignite your passion and refocus your heart. The same God who helped you begin well is faithful to help you finish well, if you keep walking with Him.'
prayer: I am divinely enabled by God not only to begin well but to finish strong. By God's grace, I will stay faithful to my assignment, my relationships, and my purpose. I refuse to be distracted, discouraged, or derailed. I fix my eyes on Jesus, and with His help, I will complete my race with joy and honour. Amen!
