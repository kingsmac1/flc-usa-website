title: You Reside in Christ
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV)
verse: 'Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!'
body:
  - 'Where do you live? Most people respond to that question with a physical location: a city, a neighborhood, a house. But as a believer, your truest, most permanent residence is in Christ. You don''t just follow Him from afar; you live in Him, and He lives in you. This reality changes everything. It means you are never alone, never uncovered, and never disconnected from the source of life.'
  - In 2 Corinthians 5:17, Paul writes that anyone who is in Christ is a new creation. This is not just a spiritual upgrade; it's a complete relocation. Your spiritual address is no longer in sin, shame, or separation. You've been moved into grace, righteousness, and intimacy with God. Being "in Christ" means that His identity becomes your identity. His victory becomes your victory. His righteousness becomes your covering.
  - Paul also wrote in Acts 17:28, “For in Him we live and move and have our being.” That means your entire existence is wrapped up in Him. Every step you take, every breath you breathe, every purpose you pursue all flows from the life of Christ within you. You are not trying to get closer to God; if you are in Christ, you are as close as you can be. He is your environment, your dwelling place, and your spiritual home.
  - 'Another powerful truth is found in Colossians 3:3: “For you died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God.” This verse is a fortress of security. Your life is not exposed to the attacks of the enemy; it is hidden in Christ. When the world tries to define you by your past, your pain, or your failures, you can boldly say, “That person is dead. My real life is hidden in Christ, and only what is true of Him is now true of me.” So, the next time someone casually asks, “Where do you live?” Answer with boldness: “I live in Christ!” That''s not just a spiritual slogan; it''s your eternal reality. The more you become aware of your position in Christ, the less this world can shake you. You don''t live in fear. You don''t live in lack. You don''t live in condemnation. You live in the presence, power, and provision of Jesus.'
  - Take a moment today to meditate on your true identity and residence. Begin to confess, “I live in Christ”. Do this not just with your words, but with your mindset and actions. You are not trying to reach God; you're already in Him. Live from that place of confidence and rest.
prayer: I live in Christ! He is my home, my covering, my strength, and my identity. I am a new creation. My life is hidden in Him, and in Him, I live, move, and have my being. I am not defined by this world. I am defined by the One in whom I dwell. Hallelujah!
