date: '2025-11-19'
title: Christ Has Qualified You
scripture: Colossians 1:12 (NIV)
verse: Giving thanks to the Father, who has qualified you to share in the inheritance of his holy people in the kingdom of light.
body:
  - 'Have you ever felt unqualified? Perhaps you''ve been disqualified by others because of your age, race, background, or education. Maybe you''ve disqualified yourself because of past mistakes or insecurities. But the truth of the gospel is this: you are qualified not by your résumé, but by Christ''s righteousness. Through His finished work on the cross, He has made you worthy and capable of fulfilling His purpose in your life.'
  - The Bible tells us in 2 Corinthians 3:5–6, “Not that we are competent in ourselves to claim anything for ourselves, but our competence comes from God. He has made us competent as ministers of a new covenant...” This means our sufficiency comes from Him. You may not feel equipped, but if God has called you, He has also empowered you. What He qualifies, He also prepares and strengthens.
  - 'Philippians 4:13 reinforces this truth: “I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.” The key is not just “I can do all things”; it''s through Christ. He is your strength, your wisdom, your access, and your identity. You don''t need to'
  - measure up to worldly standards. When Christ qualifies you, no opinion, rejection, or limitation can stand in the way of what He wants to do through you. Let's also remember 1 Corinthians 1:27–29, where Paul writes that God chooses the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and the weak to shame the strong. Why? So that no one can boast before Him. God delights in using those the world overlooks. Your past doesn't disqualify you; God can use it to display His grace. Your weakness isn't a setback; it's the platform for His strength.
  - 'When your mind begins to doubt and compare, speak the truth: “Christ is my qualifier.” Let that shape your thinking. Stop shrinking back because of what others say you lack. Stand tall, not in pride, but in faith, knowing that your identity is rooted in Christ. He didn''t choose you because you were perfect; He qualified you because of His perfect love. Take a moment to identify where you''ve believed the lie that you''re not enough. Is it in ministry? Career? Relationships? Education? Replace those lies with truth: Christ in me is more than enough.'
prayer: I am qualified by Christ. I do not rely on my background, education, or status. My confidence comes from the finished work of Jesus. I declare that I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me. I am more than enough. I'm qualified, and I live out a beautiful, healthy and successful life in Jesus' name, Amen!
