title: Christ Has Set You Free
scripture: Galatians 5:1 (KJV)
verse: Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.
body:
  - 'Freedom in Christ is not an abstract idea or a future promise. Rather, it is a present, living reality for every believer. Through His death and resurrection, Jesus broke the power of sin, guilt, fear, and every spiritual bondage that once held us captive. He didn''t just loosen the chains; He destroyed them. Now, the call is simple yet profound: Stand fast in that liberty. In other words, don''t drift back into the mindset or habits of slavery when you''ve been given the right to live free.'
  - 'This freedom isn''t permission to live carelessly; it''s the empowerment to live fully. You are no longer bound to earn God''s approval, strive for identity, or carry the shame of your past. In Christ, you are forgiven, made righteous, and accepted. Romans 8:2 puts it this way: “For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.” That''s not a metaphor; it''s a truth to live by.'
  - Picture a prisoner whose sentence has been canceled and whose door is wide open, yet he stays inside, unsure whether he can really step out. Many believers live like that. Though
  - 'Christ has set them free, they still carry the weight of yesterday: old habits, fearful thoughts, or unworthiness. But true freedom begins when you agree with what Christ has already done. Freedom starts with a renewed mind that says, “I''m no longer a slave. I am a child of God.”'
  - This freedom must be guarded. Paul warns not to be entangled again with a yoke of bondage. That can be subtle, trying to earn God's love, falling back into guilt, or comparing yourself with others. But when you stay rooted in His Word, walk in step with His Spirit, and declare the truth of your new identity, freedom becomes your daily experience. As Jesus said in John 8:36, “If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed”.
  - So, walk boldly today, not as someone hoping to be free, but as someone Christ has already freed. Let your words, thoughts, and actions reflect the liberty He paid for. Freedom is not just your gift. It's your calling!
prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You for setting me free. I declare that I will no longer live as though I'm still bound. I embrace the liberty You've given me and choose to walk in it daily with joy, confidence, and purpose. In You, I am truly free. Amen.
