date: '2025-11-27'
title: You Have Immeasurable Riches in Christ
scripture: Ephesians 2:7 (KJV)
verse: That in the ages to come, he might show the exceeding riches of his grace in his kindness toward us through Christ Jesus.
body:
  - 'When you came into Christ, you didn''t just receive forgiveness; you were ushered into an inheritance far greater than silver or gold. Scripture speaks of “the exceeding riches of His grace”, a divine storehouse that cannot be depleted. These riches are not measured by material possessions but by spiritual abundance: peace that surpasses understanding, joy unspeakable, the indwelling Holy Spirit, and the power to live victoriously.'
  - 'The world chases riches that fade, but in Christ, we have riches that endure. Proverbs 10:22 reminds us that “The blessing of the LORD, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it.” These are the kind of riches that don''t deteriorate with time or come with hidden sorrow. God''s grace has placed eternal treasures in your spirit: wisdom, righteousness, favor, and strength to do His will. You are not spiritually bankrupt; you are divinely loaded.'
  - You are seated with Christ in heavenly places (Ephesians 2:6), and that positioning gives you access to all spiritual blessings. You don't have to strive for what God has already made
  - available. Instead, learn to receive the realities of your inheritance by faith. When you understand what you carry, you stop living below your potential. Every promise in Christ is yours. You are rich in mercy, rich in love, and rich in purpose.
  - Many believers live as though they are in spiritual poverty. They are ignorant of what has been freely given to them. But God wants your eyes to be opened (Ephesians 1:18) so that you'll stop begging for what is already yours. You are not an orphan; you're an heir. You don't just hope for victory; you've been positioned to walk in it. Grace has given you access to the deep wells of God's abundance. Draw from them daily.
  - Walk with the awareness that you carry something heaven recognizes. The immeasurable riches of Christ are not for someday; they are available for now. Live boldly, pray confidently, and serve faithfully, knowing that grace has made you rich beyond measure.
prayer: I am abundantly supplied in Christ. I have access to spiritual riches that cannot be counted or corrupted. I walk in wisdom, favor, strength, and peace. I am not lacking; I am overflowing. Every day, I grow in the awareness and experience of the riches of God's grace in my life.
