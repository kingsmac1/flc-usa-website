title: There is a Reward for Diligence
scripture: Proverbs 22:29 (ESV)
verse: Do you see a man skilled in his work? He will stand before kings; he will not stand before obscure men.
body:
  - God honors hard work. Hard work doesn't necessarily mean struggles. Rather, it refers to consistent effort, dedication and persistence towards achieving a goal. This simply mean to be diligent. From the very beginning, mankind was created to be productive. In Genesis, God placed Adam in the garden to work it and take care of it. Work was not a result of the fall; it was part of God's design for a meaningful life. As believers, we are not called to laziness or mediocrity but to discipline, diligence, and excellence; not just for personal gain but for God's glory.
  - 'Proverbs 22:29 reveals a powerful principle: Diligence brings elevation. It says that a man skilled and diligent in his work will stand before kings. This is a spiritual law, and it works whether the diligent person is a believer or not. That''s why many unbelievers who apply discipline and hard work rise to places of influence and success. God''s principles are universal in application. However, the believer has something more: a divine edge.'
  - That divine advantage is the grace of God. While others rely
  - solely on effort, we have the help of the Holy Spirit, who empowers us beyond our human capacity. 1 Corinthians 15:10 says, “But by the grace of God I am what I am... yet not I, but the grace of God that was with me.” Paul acknowledged that he worked harder than many, but it was God's grace that made the difference. As a believer, you must put in the work, but never forget that God's hand on your life multiplies the results.
  - Hard work involves more than physical effort. It requires consistency, focus, and inner discipline. Proverbs 13:4 says, “The soul of the sluggard craves and gets nothing, while the soul of the diligent is richly supplied.” Wanting success is not enough; working for it is what produces fruit. When your efforts align with God's purpose, and when you apply both discipline and dependence on Him, your life becomes a powerful testimony of grace and grit working together.
  - So, don't settle for excuses or spiritualize laziness. Prayer is essential, but it is not a substitute for preparation and persistence. As you combine hard work with God's grace, you'll not only succeed; you'll stand before kings.
prayer: I am diligent, disciplined, and empowered by the Holy Spirit. I do not make excuses or embrace laziness. I work with excellence, knowing that my efforts are blessed by God. I will stand before kings, not obscure men, because the hand of the Lord is on my life. Amen!
