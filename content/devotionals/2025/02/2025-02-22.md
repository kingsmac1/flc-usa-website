title: His Love is Unconditional, Unlimited, and Complete
scripture: 1 John 4:16 (ESV)
verse: So we have come to know and to believe the love that God has for us. God is love, and whoever abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him.
body:
  - God's love is unconditional, unlimited, and complete. "We have come to know and believe the love that God has for us. God is love" (1 John 4:16 ESV). There are several kinds of love, but none of them compare to God's love. God's love is the highest form of love, known as Agape in Greek.
  - 'Agape is a profound, sacrificial love that surpasses circumstances. It is unconditional and has no limitations. In both good times and challenging times, His love remains the same. Whether you are saved or not yet saved, His love for you is constant. You might wonder why God extends His love to sinners and unbelievers. The answer is in Romans 2:4 (NLT): "Don''t you see how wonderfully kind, tolerant, and patient God is with you? Does this mean nothing to you? Can''t you see that His kindness is intended to turn you from your sin?" God''s love is an act of kindness, and His intention is to turn hearts to Him.'
  - God's love is unlimited. This means His love has no boundaries; it is eternal and unending. "I have loved you with an everlasting
  - love" (Jeremiah 31:3 KJV). His love is everlasting.
  - God's love is complete; it is perfect and whole. There is nothing missing in His love for us, and there will never be any love greater than His. Therefore, rest in His love.
prayer: Precious Father, thank You for Your great love towards me, shown in the sacrifice of Your Son Jesus so that I might be saved. Thank You for such endless, unlimited, unconditional, and complete love. I am eternally grateful to You. In Jesus' name, amen!
