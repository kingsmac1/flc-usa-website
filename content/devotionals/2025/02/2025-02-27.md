date: '2025-02-27'
title: Bearing Good Fruits
scripture: Matthew 12:33 (NLT)
verse: A tree is identified by its fruits. If a tree is good, its fruits will be good. If a tree is bad, its fruits will be bad.
body:
  - Atree is identified by its fruits. If a tree is good, it will produce good fruits. If a tree is bad, it will produce bad fruits. Your words and actions are your fruits. As Matthew 7:20 says, “So then, by their fruits you will recognize them.” Through your actions and words, one can tell who you are and what you are made of. You cannot fake it. Even if you try, time and light will reveal your true identity.
  - Galatians 5:19-20 points out the fruits of the flesh, and these fruits are very unpleasant to God. Anyone who bears such fruits will not inherit the kingdom of God. As Galatians 5:16 (NLT) says, “So I say, let the Holy Spirit guide your lives. Then you won't be doing what your sinful nature craves.” When you yield to the Holy Spirit, you can bring forth good fruits (Matthew 3:8- 10).
  - 'Galatians 5:22-23 (NLT) says, “But the Holy Spirit produces this kind of fruit in our lives: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.” By these fruits, it will be clear that you are a true believer and are yielding to the Holy Spirit. These fruits are different from the signs that'
  - follow believers (Mark 16:17).
  - As John 15:4 (ESV) says, “Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in me.” God is calling you to yield yourself to His word and His Spirit. When you do so, His manifested presence will abide in you, and you will produce good fruits.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for Your Spirit that dwells in me, causing me to exhibit Your nature and bear fruits of righteousness. Through Your Spirit, I bring forth good fruits to the glory and praise of Your name. Amen!
furtherStudies: John 8:31 (NLT), Galatians 5:16-26 (NLT), Luke 6:43 (NLT)
