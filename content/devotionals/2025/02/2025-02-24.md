title: 'Bitterness: The Poison of the Soul'
scripture: Hebrews 12:15 NLT
verse: Look after each other so that none of you fails to receive the grace of God. Watch out that no poisonous root of bitterness grows up to trouble you, corrupting many.
body:
  - Offense refers to a state of being hurt, upset, or angry. When a person is offended, they develop a strong negative emotional reaction to whatever triggered the situation. If you get offended easily, you tend to become angry very quickly. Prolonged anger leads to hatred, and hatred leads to bitterness. It does not just end there; bitterness is cancerous.
  - “Catch all the little foxes, those little foxes, before they ruin the vineyard of love, for the grapevines are blossoming!” – Song of Solomon 2:15 NLT. Bitterness is one of the little foxes that destroy lives. It troubles you and causes you to sow seeds of bitterness into the lives of those around you, corrupting many others (Deuteronomy 29:18).
  - In Jeremiah 20:9, Prophet Jeremiah describes his bitterness as fire in his bones. Bitterness spreads like fire and works like cancer. It robs you of peace and affects your health. Proverbs 14:30 AMP says, “A calm and peaceful and tranquil heart is life and health to the body, but bitterness and envy are like
  - rottenness to the bones.” Choose to have a calm, peaceful, and tranquil heart so that you can live a healthy life.
prayer: I choose joy, peace, meekness, and happiness. My heart is full of joy. I rebuke the spirit of bitterness and anger, and I command you to get your hands off of me. I do not give room for unforgiveness, malice, anger, or bitterness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
