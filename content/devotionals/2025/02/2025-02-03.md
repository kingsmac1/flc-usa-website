date: '2025-02-03'
title: The Lord Keeps you in Perfect Peace
scripture: Isaiah 26:3 (KJV)
verse: 'Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.'
body:
  - 't he prophet Isaiah shares the benefits of trusting the Lord: Everlasting Strength. When you trust in the Lord, you find strength that does not end. It is an unshakable, uninterrupted strength that you can rely on. Do you need strength to run your business, manage your family, or navigate your life? The strength you need is found in trusting in the Lord. Many people feel like they cannot continue on a project, journey, or ministry, but I bring you good news. If today, you trust in the Lord with all your heart, you will receive the strength to keep going.'
  - Proverbs 3:5 says, "Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding." Do not depend on your strategies, your background, money, or connections. Trust in the Lord and His strength will show up. When you trust in the Lord, He fills you with perfect peace. From our opening scripture, we see that the Lord gives peace to those whose confidence is in Him. This peace is all-round and complete, bringing calm even when everything around you is chaotic. Your physical abilities and resources can fail you, but the strength and peace from the Lord are never-ending.
  - This peace does not mean that challenges or storms won't come. Jeremiah 17:5-9 makes it clear that relying on your own strength will leave you disappointed. But the Lord promises that even in the midst of difficulties, He will keep you in peace. Isaiah 43:2 (NLT) says, "When you go through deep waters, I will be with you. When you go through rivers of difficulty, you will not drown. When you walk through the fire of oppression, you will not be burned up; the flames will not consume you."
  - The Lord gave Daniel peace in the lion's den, and He gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego peace in the fiery furnace. You are not an exception. His peace will keep you today, just as He has done for others.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for Your word that has strengthened my faith. I declare that I will trust You at all times. I experience the peace You give, and Your peace fills my heart now and forever. In Jesus' name, Amen.
