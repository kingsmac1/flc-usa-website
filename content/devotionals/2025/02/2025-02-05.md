title: Protect Your Faith
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7 (KJV)
verse: For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
body:
  - 'f rom the verse above, four kinds of spirits are mentioned: the spirit of fear, the spirit of power, the spirit of love, and the spirit of a sound mind. The spirit of fear does not come from God. Its purpose is to cause fear and weaken your faith. This is why God tells us, "Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father''s good pleasure to give you the kingdom." – Luke 12:32 (KJV)'
  - Fear is a direct enemy of faith. When the spirit of fear takes hold of you, it attacks your faith, making you vulnerable to the enemy's schemes. Consider Peter in Matthew 14:30 (KJV). He began to sink when fear overwhelmed him.
  - Your faith is your shield and defense. Ephesians 6:16 (KJV) says, "Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked." The enemy knows this and targets your faith through fear. Without faith, you cannot please God, as stated in Hebrews 11:6 (KJV), and receiving from God becomes impossible.
  - Living a victorious and holy life requires protecting your faith.
  - Fear often comes through various sources, especially the media. Be mindful of what you allow into your heart. Feed on God's Word continuously, as Romans 10:17 (KJV) reminds us, "So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." Protect your heart diligently, for it is the source of life, as noted in Proverbs 4:23 (KJV).
prayer: Lord, I thank You for Your Word that stirs my faith and strengthens my spirit. I reject every trace of fear and doubt in my heart. Thank You for giving me the Spirit of love, power, and a sound mind. I choose to live in faith and victory every day, in Jesus' name. Amen.
furtherStudies: Ephesians 6:16, Romans 10:17, Proverbs 4:23
