title: You Have Received Grace upon Grace
scripture: John 1:16 (NASB)
verse: For of His fullness we have all received, and grace upon grace.
body:
  - ut of God's fullness, He has given you an overflow of grace - grace piled upon grace. This signifies abundance and sufficiency. No wonder "My grace is sufficient for you." (2 Corinthians 12:9 ESV) reminds us that His grace is enough. It is not just provided, but it is sufficient for every circumstance. Your responsibility is to activate and utilize this grace.
  - When God calls you for an assignment, He supplies all the grace you need to fulfill it. Similarly, when He promotes you, He provides the grace needed to sustain you at that level. This does not mean challenges will not arise, but the grace to overcome them has already been made available. As "Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in the heavenly realms with every spiritual blessing in Christ" (Ephesians 1:3 NIV) affirms, your blessings and grace are stored in the heavenly places.
  - To see these spiritual blessings manifest in your daily life, certain spiritual disciplines are necessary. Prayer, studying the Word of God, fasting, and making faith declarations help
  - release these blessings into the physical realm when you need them.
  - You see, you have grace heaped up for you. Think of it like water nearby when soap gets into your eyes while bathing. You do not need to panic or scream for help. Instead, you simply rinse your eyes with the water. Likewise, believers should not wander around looking for solutions to their challenges when God's grace is already available. His grace is sufficient for every situation you face.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for lavishing Your grace upon me. I am grateful for the abundance of grace You have made available. I choose to utilize this grace to impact my world and change circumstances around me.
furtherStudies: 2 Timothy 2:1 KJV, 2 Corinthians 12:9 KJV, Titus 2:11 KJV
