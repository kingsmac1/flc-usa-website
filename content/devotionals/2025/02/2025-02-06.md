title: You Have Received the Spirit of Love
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7 (KJV)
verse: For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
body:
  - t he Holy Spirit is the Spirit of love. This Spirit makes it possible for you to live a life of love and obedience to God. As Jesus said in John 14:15 (KJV), "If ye love me, keep my commandments." The Spirit of love helps you not just to hear God's Word but also to obey it, as instructed in James 1:22 (KJV), "But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves." This Spirit inspires you to walk in the fear of the Lord, compelling you to live a life that pleases God and turn away from sin. Sin loses its dominion over anyone who lives by the Spirit of love.
  - The Spirit of love also compels you to do good and share the gospel with others. As Apostle Paul said in 2 Corinthians 5:14 (NIV), "For Christ's love compels us, because we are convinced that one died for all, and therefore all died." His motivation for preaching the gospel was the love of Christ, which drove him to ensure others experienced salvation.
  - This Spirit pours God's love into our hearts, as described in Romans 5:5 (KJV), "And hope maketh not ashamed; because
  - the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us." With this love in you, you can treat others with kindness, pray for them, and preach to them so they may not perish.
prayer: I declare that I have not received the spirit of fear but the Spirit of love, power, and a sound mind. I walk in righteousness and love everyone around me. The Holy Spirit compels me to share the gospel and bring others into the knowledge of Christ. Amen.
furtherStudies: Romans 5:5, John 10:7-18, 2 Corinthians 5:11-15
