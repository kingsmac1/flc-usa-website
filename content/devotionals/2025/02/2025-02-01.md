date: '2025-02-01'
title: Your Choices Are Very Important
scripture: Proverbs 3:5-6 (NLT)
verse: Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take
body:
  - Common sense is not as common as it seems. In fact, many people make decisions that defy sound reasoning. While a decision may appear logical, it does not always align with God's will. Proverbs 3:5 reminds us to "trust in the Lord with all your heart and do not depend on your own understanding." We are called to let the Lord guide our decision making, even when His path doesn't immediately make sense to us.
  - Esau's life is a sobering example of the consequences of careless choices. He often acted impulsively, focusing only on immediate gratification without considering the long term impact. One of the most glaring instances was when he traded his birthright, a blessing of immense value, for a mere bowl of soup (Genesis 25:29-34). This decision revealed his disregard for what truly mattered and exposed his lack of foresight. Esau's poor choices didn't stop there. Against God's will and his parents' counsel, he married women from the Hittites, a pagan group forbidden by God (Genesis 26:34-35). These decisions
  - brought bitterness to his family and ultimately distanced him further from God's plan for his life.
  - What about you? What choices are you making today? Are you trading away your faith, integrity, or relationships to satisfy temporary desires? Are you prioritizing fleeting needs over eternal values?
  - God wants to guide you in making wise and godly decisions. If you seek His guidance, He will lead you along the best path. Take a moment to pause, pray, and consider the consequences of your actions before making a decision.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for the Holy Spirit who teaches me all things and guides me in the path I should follow. I ask for Your wisdom to make the right choices in every situation. From today onwards, I yield myself to the leading and guidance of the Holy Spirit. In Jesus' name, Amen!
