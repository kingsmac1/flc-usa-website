title: Christ Gives you Rest
scripture: Matthew 11:28 (KJV)
verse: Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.
body:
  - In Matthew 11:28, Jesus extends an open invitation to everyone. He says, “Come unto me, all of you who labour and are carrying heavy burdens, and I promise to give you rest.” The labour referred to here is not the work of your daily job or endeavors but the toil of living in vanity, serving sin, and being enslaved to Satan.
  - For every labour, there is a reward (Proverbs 31:31, Proverbs 12:14). When you labour or render service to Satan, his reward comes in the form of affliction, troubles, and eternal condemnation unless you repent and accept salvation. Similarly, labouring for Jesus involves taking up His yoke, which is light. His reward is salvation, which brings the blessings of divine providence on earth and prepares you to spend eternity with God the Father in heaven. In heaven, you will receive your full reward for serving the Lord.
  - Here, Jesus calls out to everyone in trouble, distress, affliction, or enslaved by sin. He offers to make things right for you and give you rest. In His rest, you will experience peace. As Hebrews 4:10 (NLT) says, “For all who have entered into God's
  - rest have rested from their labours.” This rest is the assurance Jesus gives when you receive Him into your heart as your Lord and Savior.
  - For more information on how to be saved or to rededicate your life to the Lord, kindly check the last few pages of this devotional.
