title: All Things Work Together for Good
scripture: Romans 8:28 KJV
verse: And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
body:
  - 'There are a few questions you need to ask yourself: (1) Do you love God? (2) Are you called according to God''s purpose? If your answer is "yes," then certainly all things will work together for your good. This means you cannot be disadvantaged, regardless of what challenges you may be facing. God can turn your story around and bring you out of that situation, even when you feel bad.'
  - 'In Genesis 45:4-8, Joseph recognized how God allowed everything to happen the way it did for a better and bigger plan. I like what Joseph said in verse 8: "So it was God who sent me here, not you! And he is the one who made me an adviser to Pharaoh - the manager of his entire palace and the governor of all Egypt" (ALT).'
  - Although Joseph's brothers had wanted to get rid of him, God used their evil actions to accomplish His ultimate plan. He sent Joseph ahead of time to preserve their lives, save Egypt from famine, and prepare the path for the nation of Israel to evolve.
  - God is supreme, and His purposes cannot be restricted by human actions. When others plan evil against you, God will turn their plans into instruments and channels that will favor you. I pray for you today that all the devil planned for evil will be turned around for your good. Your miracle will become a testimony in Jesus' name. Amen.
prayer: I declare that all things are working together for my good, my joy, and my peace. I cannot be disadvantaged. Everything I face in life brings me closer to God's ultimate plan for my life. In Jesus' name, Amen!
