title: You Have an Unction from God
scripture: 1 John 2:20 (KJV)
verse: But you have an unction from the Holy One, and ye know all things.
body:
  - 't he Amplified Bible explains the above verse in detail: “But you have an anointing from the Holy One [you have been set apart, specially prepared by the Holy Spirit], and all of you know [the truth because He teaches us, illuminates our minds, and guards us from error].” The word "anointing" translates to "chrisma," the same term used for "unction" in 1 John 2:20 and 1 John 2:27. It refers to the Holy Spirit''s work in the believer''s life.'
  - The pronoun "ye" in 1 John 2:20 emphasizes the difference between true believers and those who do not believe that Jesus is the Son of God. This experience is exclusive to believers. When you received the Holy Spirit, you received the anointing. This anointing equips you to live a victorious Christian life.
  - This anointing of the Holy Spirit brings light to your heart and clarity to your mind, helping you discern truth. It reveals divine insights and enables you to know things that no one else can teach you. This "knowing" comes from the Spirit within, offering guidance and protection from deception.
  - The unction of the Holy Spirit helps you navigate life, making it difficult for anyone to mislead you. It is a gift that transforms your spiritual walk, but you must remain aware of it. God does not want you to be ignorant of this unction. Instead, He desires that you grow in it by yielding to the Holy Spirit and engaging in activities that foster spiritual development, such as prayer, fasting, and studying the Bible. As you do, you will move from one level of glory to another.
