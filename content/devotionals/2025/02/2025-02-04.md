date: '2025-02-04'
title: You Are Never Alone
scripture: Isaiah 41:10 (ESV)
verse: Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.
body:
  - a s a believer in Christ Jesus, you are never alone. God is with you, and not only is He with you, but He is also in you. This truth from Scripture should fill your heart with confidence. God says, "Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God." – Isaiah 41:10 (ESV). Even when you are alone in your room, God is right there with you. Let this truth become a constant awareness in your heart.
  - 'From our opening verse, we see God intervening in difficult situations. Are you facing a dilemma? Are you battling sickness in your body? Are you dealing with a financial crisis? Does it seem like everything around you is falling apart? God''s message to you is clear: “Fear not, do not be dismayed!” He reassures you with the promise that He will strengthen you, help you, and bring you out of whatever situation you are in.'
  - God's word is unfailing, and what He has done for others, He is ready to do for you. God was with men like Gideon, Moses, Jacob, Abraham, Joshua, and many others. He is the same God who will not leave you to face life's challenges alone. Deuteronomy 31:8 (ESV) says, "It is the Lord who goes before
  - you. He will be with you; He will not leave you or forsake you. Do not fear or be dismayed."
  - God has already gone ahead of you to ensure that no challenge can overwhelm you. He will not abandon you. Therefore, do not fear and do not be discouraged. He has prepared a way of rescue and will deliver you with His mighty hand.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You for being ever-present in my life. You have planned my victory ahead of time, and for this, I am grateful. I declare that I will never be discouraged. I am fearless, courageous, strengthened, and victorious in Jesus' name. Amen.
furtherStudies: Deuteronomy 31:6, Joshua 1:5, Isaiah 43:1-3, Matthew 28:20
