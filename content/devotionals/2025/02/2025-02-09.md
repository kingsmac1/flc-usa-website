title: Offense is Dangerous
scripture: Mark 11:25 (NLT)
verse: But when you are praying, first forgive anyone you are holding a grudge against, so that your Father in heaven will forgive your sins too.
body:
  - f or a while, the Lord has been pressing it upon my heart to write on the subject of offense. Often, believers find themselves weighed down because they are holding onto offenses caused by others. Offense arises from a perceived insult, disrespect, or disappointment. Other words associated with offense include anger, resentment, indignation, wrath, and vexation. These emotions can harm your spirit, soul, and body.
  - A few years ago, I met a Christian brother who was easily angered. When I asked him why he behaved this way, he said it was “holy anger.” However, whether anger seems holy or unholy, it is important to understand that it does not align with the recreated human spirit. God may lead you to feel displeasure toward unrighteousness or evil, but it is vital to act under the Spirit's guidance and not from the flesh.
  - The Bible reminds us, "Human anger does not produce the righteousness that God desires" (James 1:20 NIV). Decisions made in anger often lead to destruction. Many have ruined their homes, marriages, relationships, and opportunities because of
  - uncontrolled anger. Self-control helps you stay calm during pressure and unfavorable situations.
  - Proverbs 25:28 (NLT) says "A person without self-control is like a city with broken-down walls." When the walls of a city are broken, it becomes vulnerable to attacks. Similarly, when you lack self-control, you are easily hurt by people's words and actions. Offense does not harm those who offend you; it only hurts you. Holding onto offense can lead to unforgiveness, which hinders your prayers (Mark 11:25 KJV) and leaves you spiritually vulnerable, especially during spiritual battles. I pray that the Lord will give you the grace to let go of every hurt and resentment. May He strengthen your heart to forgive and release every burden, in Jesus' name.
prayer: Dear Lord, I ask for Your forgiveness for the hurts and resentment I have held in my heart. Help me to forgive those who have wronged me and to walk in self-control. Teach me to live free from offense and to remain calm in every situation, in Jesus' name.
furtherStudies: Proverbs 25:28 TPT, Proverbs 16:32 KJV, 1 Samuel 20:30 KJV, Proverbs 22:24
