title: Seek His Kingdom First
scripture: Matthew 6:33 (KJV)
verse: But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you.
body:
  - t o seek the Kingdom of God simply means to live a devoted life to God, prioritizing His ways in every area of your life. It also means allowing your desires and pursuits to be focused on God and His kingdom. When you prioritize God in this manner, He will prioritize you and guide you in righteousness.
  - Many people try to live life without God. They put God and all that concerns Him in a corner or a box. Instead, they struggle and chase wealth, fame, their careers, and everything else that defines success according to human standards. True success, however, is a life of fulfillment, and it can only be found and accomplished in God. Anything apart from that is vanity.
  - If you really want to achieve your ambitions, commit them to God. He will guide you and direct your path and efforts toward success, especially according to His definition of success. As detailed in Proverbs 16:3 (AMP), "Commit your works to the Lord [submit and trust them to Him], and your plans will succeed [if you respond to His will and guidance]." Today, God is saying to you, "Seek my kingdom first," and in all your ways,
  - acknowledge Him. "He will make your path straight" (Proverbs 3:6 NIV).
prayer: Heavenly Father, I will continue to seek You and Your kingdom. The work of Your kingdom prospers in my hands. Thank You, Lord, for You lead me in the path of life and bring all my plans to fruition. In Jesus' name, amen!
