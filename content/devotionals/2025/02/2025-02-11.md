title: Hold on to His Word
scripture: Genesis 30:3 (NLT)
verse: Then Rachel told him, 'Take my maid, Bilhah, and sleep with her. She will bear children for me, and through her, I can have a family too.
body:
  - t he stories of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob show how each patriarch faced challenges when their wives experienced delays in conceiving. Their responses to these difficulties varied. Abraham followed Sarah's advice to have a child with Hagar, which led to complications and a bitter experience.
  - Isaac, however, prayed to the Lord for his wife, Rebekah, and God answered his prayer, enabling her to conceive (Genesis 25:21 NIV). Jacob repeated a mistake similar to Abraham's by accepting Rachel's suggestion to have children through her maid, Bilhah, which also brought challenges (Genesis 30:3 (NLT)).
  - Despite these struggles, God's promise to Abraham to make him a father of many nations and to bless his descendants remained unshaken. However, the devil actively challenged these promises, demonstrating that spiritual opposition often comes after God gives a word or promotion. This reminds us to stay spiritually alert and hold firmly to God's word during times of testing.
  - Abraham's response to pressure is a model for us. As stated in Romans 4:19-21, "He did not waver at the promise of God through unbelief, but was strengthened in faith, giving glory to God." Abraham trusted God's word, despite his circumstances. When it seems like God's promises are delayed, remain steadfast. Act like Abraham by holding on to God's word until His promises are fulfilled in your life. As Jesus taught in Luke 8:15 (KJV), "But that on the good ground are they, which in an honest and good heart, having heard the word, keep it, and bring forth fruit with patience."
prayer: My faith is alive in God. I am not moved by what I see or hear, but only by the word of God. I hold on to His promises until they are fulfilled in my life. In Jesus' name, Amen! FURTHER
