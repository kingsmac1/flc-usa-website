title: Establishing God’s Will on Earth
scripture: Jeremiah 29:11 (NIV)
verse: For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the Lord, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.
body:
  - g od's will refers to His desires, plans, and intentions. When we pray, saying “Your will be done,” we are asking God to accomplish His desires on earth (Matthew 6:10 NIV). During creation, God was purposeful; He had a clear desire and will for everything, and He still seeks for His purposes to be fulfilled on earth.
  - In Matthew 26:39, Jesus prayed, “Let Your will be done,” in the Garden of Gethsemane before His crucifixion. Though He had a choice, He submitted to God's desire, proving His dedication to seeing God's will accomplished.
  - Scriptures like Ephesians 4:15 (“Speak the truth in love”), Ephesians 5:18 (“Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to debauchery”), and 1 Corinthians 6:18 (“Flee from sexual immorality”) reflect God's will for His creation. Praying “Your will be done” means asking God to increase righteousness on earth, turning hearts away from sin and toward a deeper knowledge of Him.
  - Jeremiah 29:11 and 3 John 1:2 reveal that God desires for you to prosper, to remain healthy, and to enjoy a beautiful future. If you are not yet experiencing these blessings, it is time to pray fervently for God's will to be done. Such prayers will cause God's plans and desires to manifest in your life.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for Your great desires for my life and for the earth. I submit to Your Lordship. Let Your will be done in my life and not mine. Let Your righteousness increase, and may Your kingdom reign on earth as it is in heaven. In Jesus' name, amen!
