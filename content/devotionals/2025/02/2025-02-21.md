date: '2025-02-21'
title: Let your Light Shine
scripture: Matthew 5:16 (KJV)
verse: Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.
body:
  - m atthew 5:14 (ESV) says, "You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden." As a believer, you are the light of the world, a city on the mountaintop that cannot be hidden. You are light, so stop trying to hide yourself.
  - To hide simply means not manifesting. There are so many graces and abilities God has bestowed upon you. Many believers are not impacting their world with these blessings of God. They are either ignorant of this truth, do not know how to manifest, or something is stopping them from manifesting.
  - Firstly, I want you to understand that God is light, and when you got saved, you received the light of life. "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness but will have the light of life" (John 8:12 ESV). Because the light of life resides in you, it makes you a light. Secondly, God expects you to transform the lives of others.
  - To manifest means to shine. Since God's expectation is for you to shine, how do you do this? "Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path" (Psalm 119:105 KJV). God's word is the
  - key. As you study God's word, it brings clarity to your life and shows you which areas of your life He wants you to manifest. It will produce good works that will be obvious, and people will begin to glorify God.
  - From Matthew 5:16, your shining might be thoughts and inspirations that God places in your heart. Feel free to share them with others. It could be ideas - go ahead and document them as books and share them with others. As you do this, you are letting your light shine.
  - There are many other ways to manifest; just ensure they are benevolent to all, just as Jesus went about doing good. "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and power, and He went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil" (Acts 10:38 NKJV).
  - I pray for you today, may the Lord cause your light to shine brightly, and may anything hindering your manifestation be broken in Jesus' name. Amen!
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for making me the light of the world and a city set upon a hill that cannot be hidden. I pray that from today henceforth, my hunger for your word will increase, and your light will illuminate my path and shine through me. In Jesus' name, amen!
