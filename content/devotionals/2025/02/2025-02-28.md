date: '2025-02-28'
title: Don’t Be Lukewarm
scripture: Matthew 12:30 (NLT)
verse: Anyone who isn't with me opposes me, and anyone who isn't working with me is actually working against me.
body:
  - a re you truly for God or against Him? Are you laboring with Him or against Him? There is no middle ground. You have to choose where you stand with God. You do not necessarily need to make a bold declaration to be against Him, but your words and actions will reveal on whose side you are. As Matthew 7:16 says, “By their fruits we know if they are with Christ or not. From our opening scripture, the phrase “actually working against me” implies not laboring for Him. Laboring for the devil produces fruits of unrighteousness and the flesh.
  - A lukewarm Christian is unstable and unenthusiastic about their faith in God. Jesus warned against lukewarm behaviors. As a true believer, you have no business living like someone who is not saved. It is sad to see believers still going to ungodly clubs, engaging in inappropriate activities in the name of fun or cooling off, or even consulting diviners or psychic readers. God does not condone such things, and He wants you to change your ways (Acts 17:30-31).
  - It is impossible to be neutral with God. Any person who is not
  - actively following Jesus is working against Him. As Matthew 6:24 (NKJV) says, “No one can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other.” Make a decision to serve the Lord, be enthusiastic about your faith in God, and live to please Him.
prayer: I am burning, active, and aglow for the Lord. My love for the Lord will not wax cold. I am consistent, and I boldly stand firm in Christ Jesus. Amen.
