title: All Things Were Created for His Glory
scripture: Romans 11:36 (LB)
verse: For everything comes from God alone. Everything lives by his power, and everything is for his glory. To him be glory evermore.
body:
  - p salm 24:1 (NIV) says "The earth is the Lord's, and everything in it, the world, and all who live in it." The entire world and all creation belong to the Lord, for He made everything for Himself. The ultimate purpose of the universe is to reveal the glory of God.
  - The glory of God is who He is; it is the essence of His nature and the radiance of His splendor. It is the atmosphere of His presence, reflecting His goodness and all His eternal, intrinsic characteristics.
  - Everything God created reveals His glory in one way or another. You can literally see God's glory in all creation - through the air, the wind, the land, and both animate and inanimate things. "The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands" (Psalm 19:1 NIV).
  - Your desire should be to live to glorify God. Ensure that your thoughts, words, actions, the places you go, and the things you do glorify Him. This fulfills the purpose for which you were
  - created. "So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God" (1 Corinthians 10:31 NIV).
prayer: Precious Father, I rejoice and thank You for creating me in Your glory and for Your glory. I thank You for the understanding and knowledge I have received from Your Word. From today onward, I will live my life to glorify You. Let my light shine brightly so others can glorify You too. In Jesus' name, amen.
