title: The Greatest Achievement in Life
scripture: Isaiah 43:7 (NAB)
verse: All who are called by my name I created for my glory, whom I formed and made.
body:
  - Isaiah 64:8 (NKJV) says "But now, O Lord, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You our potter; and all we are the work of Your hand." God is the potter, and we are the clay. He molds and shapes us into whatever fulfills His purpose, all for His glory. His satisfaction lies in creation serving its purpose and bringing Him glory.
  - God gave human beings the power of choice - the ability to choose between good and evil. You have the choice to live for His glory or not. The Creator is delighted and satisfied when He sees His creation fulfilling its purpose as a reflection of His glory.
  - What use is a vessel if it does not serve its purpose? Living for God's glory is the greatest achievement any person can accomplish. It is the reason He created you. "Everyone who is called by my name, whom I have created for my glory, I have formed him, yes, I have made him" (Isaiah 43:7 NKJV).
  - Proverbs 16:4 says, "The Lord has made all for Himself." Therefore, let your utmost desire be to glorify God. When you live to satisfy the purpose for which you were created, He will be
  - pleased with you.
prayer: I am the glory of God. I reflect the glory of God in all my ways. I was created in God's image for His glory. I have been bought with a price, and I will always live to please and satisfy the reason God made me.
furtherStudies: Psalm 8:1 (KJV), Romans 11:36 (KJV), Hebrews 2:10 (KJV), Revelation 4:11 (KJV)
