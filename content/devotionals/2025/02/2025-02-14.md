title: His Love is Unfailing
scripture: Psalm 100:5 (NIV)
verse: For the LORD is good and his love endures forever; his faithfulness continues through all generations.
body:
  - 'Ilove the way our opening verse is rendered in the New Living Translation: "For the Lord is good. His unfailing love continues forever, and his faithfulness continues to each generation." God''s love is truly unfailing. Unlike human love, which is often fickle and can change depending on loyalty or interests, God''s love remains constant.'
  - There are times when I have seen believers feeling depressed and deeply worried because they thought their mistakes were so grievous that God would have difficulty forgiving them. At times, they even feel they have exhausted God's love and mercy. However, God's love for you endures forever. This means His love has endurance, is never exhausted, and is everlasting. “I have loved you with an everlasting love; I have drawn you with unfailing kindness” (Jeremiah 31:3).
  - In Romans 8:35, 38-39, Paul asks, “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall trouble or hardship or persecution or famine or nakedness or danger or sword? For I am convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither the present nor the future, nor any powers, neither height nor
  - depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.” God's love for you is eternal, and nothing can separate you from the love of God in Christ Jesus. His love for you endures forever and continues through all generations.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for loving me with an everlasting love. Your faithfulness reassures me that nothing can separate me from Your love. Help me to extend Your love to others, regardless of how they treat me. I walk confidently in the knowledge of Your unfailing love. In Jesus' name, Amen.
