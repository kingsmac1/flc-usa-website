date: '2025-02-07'
title: Always Ask God
scripture: Genesis 25:21 (NLT)
verse: Isaac pleaded with the Lord on behalf of his wife, because she was unable to have children. The Lord answered Isaac's prayer, and Rebekah became pregnant with twins.
body:
  - g od is waiting for you to ask Him. He desires that you bring your needs to Him. He is always willing and ready to answer your prayers. As Jesus said in John 16:24 (NKJV), "Until now you have asked nothing in My name. Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be full." God wants you to ask so that He can grant your request, and your joy will be complete.
  - Do not simply wish for God to intervene. Wishes remain unfulfilled if no action is taken. While they may bring temporary comfort, they cannot replace the act of asking in faith. As a believer, you have the right in Christ to make your requests known to God and to receive answers. In John 14:14 (NKJV), Jesus promises, "If you ask anything in My name, I will do it."
  - From our opening verse, Isaac "pleaded" with the Lord. This does not mean he begged; instead, it means he earnestly prayed and made his request. God responded by blessing him and Rebekah with twins. If your situation feels impossible,
  - whether it is related to health, finances, or any other challenge, simply ask God. He is always ready and willing to meet your needs and fulfill your requests.
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for the privilege to approach You in prayer. I ask boldly and confidently in the name of Jesus, knowing my requests will be granted. My joy is made complete because You always hear and answer me. Amen.
furtherStudies: Mark 11:24, Matthew 21:22, James 5:16
