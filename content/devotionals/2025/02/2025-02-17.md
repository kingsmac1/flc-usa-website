date: '2025-02-17'
title: Dealing with Hurt
scripture: Colossians 3:13 NLT
verse: Make allowance for each other's faults, and forgive anyone who offends you. Remember, the Lord forgave you, so you must forgive others
body:
  - w e all have feelings and emotions. There are things that could affect your feelings and make you feel hurt when ill-treated. There are certain circumstances and people that will always try to make you feel hurt, but it is important to deal with the situation wisely, according to the principles of the Word of God.
  - God wants your joy to be full. "These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full" (John 15:11 KJV). God will not bring you any hurt or harm. People get hurt when offended. Offense is a trap that can lead you into a rabbit hole of resentment. "Do not give the devil a foothold" (Ephesians 4:27 NLT).
  - To deal with hurt, the first thing to do is to surrender the right to seek revenge. "Do not seek revenge or bear a grudge against anyone" (Leviticus 19:18). Rather, allow God to justify the situation. "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28). Jesus understands that the situation can burden and wear you out, so He is asking you to pray and commit the situation to Him. He will give you His
  - peace and freedom, taking away the burden of hurt from you.
  - One of the amazing things about committing the situation to God is that He will heal you, take away the bitterness, and clean up the bad memories, leading you to wholeness. Since you have committed it to God, you have to let go because God is handling the situation. It is just like after a car collision; the insurance company gets involved in settling the claims and compensating the parties. You no longer have to bother yourself or try to justify who was right or wrong.
  - Lastly, repent of any sinful anger you may have harbored in your heart before committing the situation to God.
prayer: Dear Father, this day, I repent of every anger I have harbored in my heart. I forgive everyone who has hurt me. Heal me of every hurt, and help me to let go of all offense and bitterness. I release them all right now. In Jesus' name, amen.
