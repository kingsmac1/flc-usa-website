title: Be a Person of Value
scripture: Matthew 5:16 (NLT)
verse: In the same way, let your good deeds shine out for all to see, so that everyone will praise your heavenly Father.
body:
  - d ecide today that you will bring value wherever you go, no matter the situation or how small the impact might seem. People of value make things better than they found them. They are the ones who bring about change and improvement. They do not sit back and wait for things to happen; they make things happen.
  - People of value are initiators. They see a need and take action. They are the spark that ignites progress. In the Bible, we see many examples of people who lived with this kind of character. One such person was Rebekah. In Genesis 24:17-18 (KJV), when Rebekah saw a need, she didn't hesitate. It was common to offer a drink to a stranger, but Rebekah went above and beyond by fetching water for ten thirsty camels. This act of service caught the attention of Eliezer, the servant of Abraham, who was sent to find a wife for Isaac. Rebekah's willingness to help others, even when it seemed like a small act, made a big impact. This is why she became the chosen bride for Isaac.
  - David is another example of a person of value. In 2 Samuel 7:1-
  - 15, David saw a need for a place to worship God and decided to build a temple for Him, even though God had not asked him to. Ruth, too, showed great value in her commitment to Naomi, choosing to stay with her even when it would have been easier to go her own way. Ruth's determination and loyalty made a difference in Naomi's life (Ruth 1:16 -19).
  - People of value are always looking for ways to help and improve the lives of others. They act with purpose and are always ready to serve. In the end, there is always a reward for people of such great character.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for making me a person of value. I am committed to bringing change and offering solutions to the needs of my generation. I am a goal setter and will make the world around me better than the way I found it.
furtherStudies: Genesis 24:17-21, Ruth 2:1-6, John 3:16
