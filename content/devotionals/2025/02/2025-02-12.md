title: You Have Been Ordained to Receive Answers to your Prayers
scripture: Mark 11:24 (NLT)
verse: I tell you, you can pray for anything, and if you believe that you have received it, it will be yours.
body:
  - m any believers hesitate when called to pray because they feel God no longer answers prayers or doubt the effectiveness of prayer. However, Jesus clearly commanded in Mark 11:24 that we should pray for anything, believe we have received it, and it will be ours.
  - The main challenge lies in not truly believing in the answers to our prayers. This lack of faith can make prayer seem uninteresting and unfruitful. Yet, as children of God, we are ordained to receive answers to our prayers. The one who answers prayers lives in us, expecting us to come to Him in faith. God finds joy in hearing and answering our prayers because it is an opportunity for fellowship with Him (1 John 1:3).
  - Jesus emphasized this in Matthew 21:22 (NIV), saying, "If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer." Similarly, Matthew 7:11 reminds us of God's willingness to give good gifts to those who ask Him. God is waiting for you to pray.
  - When you believe and ask in faith, you will receive answers that bring joy and fulfillment to your heart.
prayer: Precious Father, I thank You for the privilege of prayer and the assurance of receiving answers. I am confident in Your word and will always believe in Your power to grant all of my desires when I pray. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Philippians 4:6, James 5:16, Mark 11:24
