date: '2025-02-20'
title: Be Slow to Anger
scripture: James 1:19 (NIV)
verse: 'My dear brothers and sisters, take note of this: Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to become angry.'
body:
  - a nger is not good for you, and because of the disasters that accompany it, God is calling you to order. "He whose spirit is without restraint is like a city broken down and without walls" (Proverbs 25:28 ASV).
  - You are the temple of God, and His Spirit lives in you. As 1 Corinthians 6:19 (NKJV) says, "Do you not know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you?" There is a hedge around you that provides protection. Many people break this hedge through anger, making themselves vulnerable to the enemy's attacks. "Whoever breaks through a wall may be bitten by a snake" (Ecclesiastes 10:8 KJV).
  - The snake refers to the devil, and he can only attack when the walls are broken down. By default, a Christian should not be victimized or defeated. "No weapon formed against you shall prosper" (Isaiah 54:17 NKJV). However, many times the reason the devil succeeds in his attacks is that people have broken their walls of defense through anger, resentment, and unforgiveness.
  - '"Do not be quickly provoked in your spirit, for anger resides in the lap of fools" (Ecclesiastes 7:9 NIV). James admonished the brethren to be slow to anger. Anger reveals a person''s foolishness. The more offended or angry one becomes, the more foolishness takes over their life. God has filled us with the spirit of wisdom; therefore, do not give room for foolishness like anger.'
prayer: I declare that the spirit of wisdom is at work in me. I do not accommodate foolishness. I am slow to anger. I have self- control, and my walls of defense are well-fortified, protecting me from the enemy.
furtherStudies: Nehemiah 9:17 (KJV), Proverbs 15:18 (KJV), Proverbs 14:17 (KJV), Proverbs 14:29 (KJV)
