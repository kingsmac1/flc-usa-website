title: Being Born Again
scripture: 1 Peter 1:23 (KJV)
verse: Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of Incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.
body:
  - In John 3:3-7, we see a conversation between Jesus and Nicodemus, a religious leader who wanted to know how to be saved. Jesus told him, "Unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God." To be "born again" means a change of heart, a spiritual renewal that only God can give.
  - 'Being born again is an act of God where eternal life is given to those who believe in Jesus. It is often referred to as being saved. Romans 3:23 (ESV) says, "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." Sin brings punishment, but God sent His Son, Jesus, to take your place and bear the consequences of sin. When you believe in Jesus, God promises salvation as stated in Romans 10:9: "If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved."'
  - From that moment, you are a new person. 2 Corinthians 5:17 (NLT) declares, "This means that anyone who belongs to Christ has become a new person. The old life is gone; a new life has begun." God sees you without a past, and He calls you to make a conscious effort to live for Him. This includes joining a Bible-
  - believing church to help guide you on your journey with God.
prayer: Precious Lord, thank You for the gift of Your Son, Jesus, who has brought salvation to my soul. I am a new creation in Christ, and I will continue to walk in the realities of who I am in Him. I choose to be a vessel of righteousness, bringing glory to Your name. Amen.
