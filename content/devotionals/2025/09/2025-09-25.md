title: Rendering Diligent Services
scripture: Proverbs 22:29 KJV
verse: Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.
body:
  - 'In the Bible, diligence is a virtue that means to work hard, persevere, and pay close attention to one''s duties. To be diligent basically involves being intentional. Diligence is characterized by careful attention to detail, a commitment to excellence, completeness, and persistence. It is a quality that connects with wisdom and prosperity. Proverbs 10:4 KJV says, He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.'
  - From our opening scripture, we see that a life of diligence is expected from every believer in everything they do, including the workplace. Diligence and skill were highly regarded both in Bible days and in our present times. In Exodus 31:1-6, during the construction of the Tabernacle, God specifically chose skilled and diligent artisans. The Hebrew word for skilled refers to expertise and proficiency, showing that God values excellence in your profession.
  - People who are highly diligent in whatever they do often find themselves and their services required by royalty and people of influence. God usually rewards diligence with opportunities for
  - leadership and impact. In Genesis 41:38-42, Joseph rose to prominence in Egypt because of his diligence. In Daniel 1:19- 20, Daniel's wisdom and ability to interpret dreams elevated him before the king of Babylon. David moved from being a shepherd boy to a king. Nehemiah rose from a cupbearer to a governor.
  - Honor and recognition come with excellence. Obscure men are those without influence or recognition, but God elevates those who are faithful, skilled, and diligent. Do not be the one who is committed in church but performs poorly in school or at work. You have an excellent spirit. Therefore, strive to be diligent, for the Lord will give you opportunities for greater service and impact. Ecclesiastes 9:10 KJV says, Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might.
  - 'You can also be diligent in your thoughts, words, and actions. Joshua 1:8 KJV says, This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success. This verse emphasizes the importance of intentionally keeping God''s Word in your mouth, speaking it consistently and purposefully. It also calls for guarding your heart and mind by meditating on His Word day and night so that your actions align with His will.'
  - Such consistency in speech, thought, and conduct requires diligence. Therefore, remain diligent in all things, continually aligning your life with God's truth, and you will walk in prosperity and good success. Finally, in your service to God, be diligent in carrying out all He has planned for you.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for the gift of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit has brought wisdom and excellence into my life and causes me to produce excellence in all things. I declare that I will always be diligent and never lazy or slothful in business. There is no mediocrity in my life. I make all things beautiful and excellent, hallelujah.
