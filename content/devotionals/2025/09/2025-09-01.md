title: Your Confidence is in Christ
scripture: Proverbs 3:26 KJV
verse: For the Lord shall be thy confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being taken.
body:
  - The Oxford Dictionary defines confidence as the feeling or belief that one can rely on something or someone. Many people only place their confidence in material things, wealth, connections, or even in other people. But until you face a situation where those things and individuals fail you, you may not truly understand what it means to trust in the Lord alone.
  - At times, God allows you to come to a point in life where the things you once relied on fail, so that you can run to Him and trust Him fully. Everything about your life should give glory to God (Isaiah 43:7). If all things always go smoothly for you, you may develop a sense of self-reliance. But when faced with situations beyond your ability, it becomes easier to depend on God and glorify Him in the end.
  - This is not a call to low self-esteem or cowardice. As believers, our confidence is rooted in our trust and assurance in the person and finished work of Christ. His redemptive work on the cross and His continual intercession for all who believe in Him (Romans 8:34) give us boldness. Knowing all Christ has done for us, He expects us to allow His finished work to change the way we think, speak, and act.
  - Philippians 4:13 KJV says, I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me. This should be the way we speak, because we have full confidence that Christ living in us strengthens us and gives us the ability to face any task. This confidence in Christ gives you a positive picture of hope and encouragement.
  - In all things and circumstances, always know that God's faithfulness is certain. He started a good work in you and will complete it. I am convinced and confident of this very thing, that He who has begun a good work in you will continue to perfect and complete it (Philippians 1:6 AMP).
prayer: Thank you, Jesus, for your sacrificial and finished work that has given me boldness to come before your throne of grace. Thank you for the confidence we have in you, knowing that you will finish every good work you have started in me. This I pray in Jesus' name, Amen.
