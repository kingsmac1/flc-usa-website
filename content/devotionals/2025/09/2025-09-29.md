title: The Lord is with you
scripture: Deuteronomy 31:8 NLT
verse: Do not be afraid or discouraged, for the LORD will personally go ahead of you. He will be with you; He will neither fail you nor abandon you.
body:
  - Discouragement and fear are natural ways humans respond to challenges or uncertainties. As believers, the reverse is the case. Because of the abiding presence of the Holy Spirit in our lives, we have an advantage. The Holy Spirit counters fear and discouragement by giving us divine assurance.
  - In 1 Samuel 17, we find the account of Goliath and David. Goliath was a skilled and experienced giant warrior. He used divination and worshipped Dagon, and even had to invoke his god when he cursed David (1 Samuel 17:43). On the other hand, David was a young shepherd with no military experience. He could not even use the armor. But he confronted Goliath, defeated him, and the rest of the Philistines ran away.
  - David was not afraid, and he was not discouraged because his confidence was in God. He did not try to quantify or evaluate the opposition. The more you evaluate the challenge, the more it occupies your mind and magnifies fear. At that point, faith dwindles and you will miss out on the grace and victory God has
  - made available to you (Jonah 2:8 KJV).
  - Anytime fear arises within you, just know it is not from God. 2 Timothy 1:7 says, For God has not given you the spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind. God knows that there are things that will show up to discourage and plant fear in your path of destiny. That is why He assures you of His presence with you, with His unfailing faithfulness and commitment never to forsake you. Be assured that God is with you.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank You for Your presence in me and with me at all times. I am grateful that You go ahead of me to make every crooked way straight. I declare that I glide through a smooth path in life. Every challenge bows before me because the Lord is with me. I am fearless and courageous always, in Jesus' name. Amen.
