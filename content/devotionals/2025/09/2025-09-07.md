title: The Outpouring of the spirit
scripture: Ezekiel 36:27 ESV
verse: And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules
body:
  - The book of Joel 2:28 KJV says, And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions. This is God's abundant release of His Spirit upon all people.
  - In the book of Acts chapter 2, on the day of Pentecost, the promise of the Holy Spirit was fulfilled. Acts 2:2-4 says, And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting. And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them. And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.
  - From the day of Pentecost till now, the Holy Spirit has been poured out upon all believers. The Holy Spirit brings divine empowerment and enables Christian living. The outpouring of the Holy Spirit has no cultural, social, gender, or ethnic
  - restriction.
  - 'The Holy Spirit is your teacher, advocate, comforter, counselor, helper, and standby. He has also made your body His abode (1 Corinthians 3:16). The Holy Spirit is the gift of the Father to humanity. When His presence is in your life, you are never alone. Perhaps you have been desiring that God should fill you with the Holy Spirit and enable you to benefit from all the blessings the Spirit offers, I would like to pray for you. Kindly repeat after me:'
  - Thank you, Lord, for the gift of the Holy Spirit which you freely give to all who ask and believe in you. I ask you Lord to fill me with the Holy Spirit and enable me to speak in tongues right now in Jesus' name. Amen.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for the outpouring of your Spirit in my life. I testify that I am full of the Holy Spirit. Lord, have your way and full expression in my life in Jesus' name. Amen.
