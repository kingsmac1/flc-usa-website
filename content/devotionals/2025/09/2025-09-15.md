date: '2025-09-15'
title: Renew Your Mind
scripture: Romans 12:2 KJV
verse: 'And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect will of God.'
body:
  - Abeliever should not imitate the fleeting patterns and lifestyles of this present world, but instead be wholly transformed by the renewing of the mind. Your mind is the seat of your reasoning, thoughts, and understanding.
  - When you came into the world as a baby, your mind was neutral. Over time, it absorbed information from your environment; some good, some evil. When the mind is filled with worldly principles, it becomes an instrument for evil. But when it is informed and guided by the Spirit, it becomes an instrument for good and for God's works.
  - That is why God needs you to renew your mind. Through education and experiences, the mind learns to distinguish between good and evil. But true renewal comes by replacing all the old, corrupt, and negative information with God's Word.
  - 'Think of it like a computer: when you delete corrupt files and install new ones, the system runs better. In the same way, when you replace negative thoughts and worldly patterns with the Word of God, your life takes on a new outlook. This new outlook is called transformation.'
  - Whatever kind of change you want to see in your life begins from your mind. When you feed your mind with God's Word, it will influence your life from the inside, and eventually, it will show on the outside.
  - Ephesians 4:23 NLT says, Instead, let the Spirit renew your thoughts and attitudes. Renewal is not a one-time thing; it is a continuous process. Each time God's Word comes to you, don't resist it or argue with it. Receive it with meekness, allow it to shape your thought process, and the Holy Spirit will cause true transformation. Over time, you will see this change reflected in your words, actions, and lifestyle.
prayer: God's Word is alive in me. It is transforming my life daily. I do not resist the Word of God; I receive it with meekness and gladness. I yield myself to the Spirit of God and His work of transformation in me. The Holy Spirit is renewing my thoughts, shaping my attitude, and causing my life to reflect God's glory. In Jesus' name, Amen!
