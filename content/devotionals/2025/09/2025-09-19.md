title: Where is your Faith?
scripture: Luke 8:25 KJV
verse: And He said unto them, Where is your faith? And they being afraid wondered, saying one to another, What manner of man is this! for He commandeth even the winds and water, and they obey Him.
body:
  - Where is your faith? Is your faith placed in the wrong things? Is your confidence rooted in riches because of what they can acquire for you? Is your faith in yourself? Your beauty, intelligence, eloquence, skills, and hard work or is your faith in God?
  - The disciples of Jesus, as seen in Luke 8:25, did not exercise faith in God despite all the wonders and miracles they had seen Jesus perform. Some of them were fishermen and could swim; perhaps they had always relied on their swimming skills. But the moment they faced a storm beyond their abilities, they became afraid. If their faith had been anchored in God, no matter the occasion, they would not have been shaken.
  - The winds and the waters obeyed the command of Jesus. This is not only applicable to Him; the winds and the waters can obey you too when you have faith and do not doubt. Doubt is the enemy of your miracle. The winds and waters represent the storms and turbulence of life. You can speak to any storm or challenge you are facing, and it will cease. Mark 11:23 NIV says, Truly I tell you, if anyone says to this mountain, Go, throw
  - yourself into the sea,' and does not doubt in their heart but believes that what they say will happen, it will be done for them.
  - Now, use the mighty name of Jesus to speak to that seemingly insurmountable challenge or obstacle before you. Do not doubt in your heart, and God will cause it to be removed from your way.
prayer: My faith is alive in God. I am strong in the Lord and in the power of His might. My faith is steadfast and unmovable. Therefore, I speak peace into my home, my finances, my health, and all that concerns me. I declare that every storm ceases right now, in Jesus' name. Amen.
