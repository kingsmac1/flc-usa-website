title: He Keeps you in Perfect Peace
scripture: Isaiah 26:3 KJV
verse: 'Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.'
body:
  - It is God's responsibility to cause you to experience peace. The world cannot give you true peace. Perfect peace is from God, and it surpasses human understanding. In Hebrew, the phrase perfect peace is translated as shalom shalom, a repetition that signifies all-round peace.
  - 2 Thessalonians 3:16 KJV says, Now the Lord of peace himself give you peace always by all means. God, who is the Lord of peace, can give you peace in your spirit, soul, and body. Peace in your body means health, wholeness, and a life free from infirmity. The Holy Spirit, who dwells in you, vitalizes your mortal body, ensuring that every cell, organ, hormone, and system functions perfectly (Romans 8:11).
  - 3 John 1:2 NIV says, Dear friend, I pray that you may enjoy good health and that all may go well with you, even as your soul is getting along well. God desires that you live in perfect health. This is available to those whose minds are fixed on Him. Do not allow sickness to dominate your thoughts or shift your attention. God requires undivided focus on His Word.
  - When Peter walked on water in Matthew 14:22-23, he began to sink only when he allowed the wind to distract him. You become whatever captures your attention. Keep your focus on God's Word concerning your health. Meditate on it, confess it, and speak it boldly to your body. The result will be perfect peace in your health. Glory to God!
prayer: Lord, I trust You with all my heart, and I keep my mind stayed on Your Word. I experience perfect peace in my body. I am healthy and totally sound. The life of God flows in my blood and sustains me in divine health, in Jesus' name. Amen.
