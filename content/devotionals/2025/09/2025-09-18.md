title: His Word Heals
scripture: Psalm 107:20 KJV
verse: He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.
body:
  - The Good News Translation renders Psalm 107:20 as, He sent his word, and healed them; he rescued them from the grave. The Word of God is not just a spoken language; it is a totally dynamic force that accomplishes God's purpose. There is great power and authority in God's Word.
  - 'Romans 1:16 KJV says, For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to everyone that believeth… God''s Word is very powerful. Anyone who believes God''s Word will experience the same power contained in it.'
  - From the beginning of creation, as recorded in Genesis, God spoke His Word, and everything He said manifested physically. When the Scripture says, He sent His word, it means God spoke His Word. The effect of that spoken Word was healing and deliverance.
  - Sickness and disease are incipient forms of death. Every sickness is an early stage designed to lead to death. So, when Scripture declares, He delivered them from the grave, it means
  - God's Word rescued them from sicknesses that were meant to take them to the grave. God's Word crystallizes into what was spoken. As declared in John 1:14, The Word became flesh.
  - You may be in dire need of healing right now. The key is to hold on to God's Word concerning your health and believe strongly in what He has said. 1 Peter 2:24 says, By his stripes you were healed. Do you believe what God's Word says about you? If your answer is Yes, then no matter what is happening in your body, declare to yourself, I am healed, and thank you, Father, for healing me in Jesus' name. Amen.
  - 'I pray for you: May the healing anointing of the Holy Spirit come upon you right now. I proclaim that you are healed of every disease, sickness, and infirmity. May you receive wholeness in your body in Jesus'' name. Amen.'
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for healing me from every disease. I affirm that I am healed and whole in Jesus' name. I thank You, Lord, because whom You set free is free indeed. I declare that I am free from every disease, every virus, and every malfunctioning in my body.
