body:
  - 'Healing is the bread of God''s children. In Matthew 15:26, Jesus reveals that healing belongs to those who are His. Bread is essential for survival; in the same way, healing is essential for life. Sickness and infirmities come in many forms: diseases, afflictions, and even torments caused by demonic influence.'
  - 'Jesus, moved by compassion, healed all manner of diseases and infirmities. His mercy endures forever, and that same mercy is available to you today. Hebrews 13:8 NIV declares: Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever. This means He has not changed. The same Jesus who healed in Bible days is still healing today.'
  - From Matthew 15:26, healing is the bread of God's children. Just as bread is necessary for survival, so is healing. God desires to give you healing. Even as you read this devotional, the healing power of God is available for you. All He requires is your faith.
  - Lift your right hand as I pray for you. In the name of Jesus Christ, the Son of the Living God, I declare the healing power of God rests upon you right now. I command every demon of infirmity and affliction to depart from you. I command every symptom of sickness to disappear now. Be healed in the name of Jesus! Amen. Congratulations, you are healed. Go ahead and thank God.
prayer: Lord, thank You for healing me. I affirm that I am free from every pain, sickness, and infirmity. The Holy Spirit lives in me. He quickens and vitalizes my mortal body daily. I am impregnable to diseases and sickness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
