title: The Test of your Obedience
scripture: 2 Corinthians 10:6 NIV
verse: And we will be ready to punish every act of disobedience, once your obedience is complete.
body:
  - Every believer will encounter moments where obedience is tested. These are times that demand alignment with God's will, even when it totally feels uncomfortable, inconvenient, or unpopular. These tests reveal more than just outward behavior; they uncover the posture of your heart.
  - All through Scripture, we see that God allows His people to pass through seasons that prove their loyalty and devotion, whether they will walk in His ways or yield to the desires of the flesh. These tests are never meant to destroy, but to refine and strengthen faith. As James 1:3 teaches, The testing of your faith produces perseverance.
  - Obedience is not mere compliance; it is worship. It is declaring, Lord, not my will, but Yours be done. Each step of obedience is a seed that produces growth, maturity, and favor with God. It positions you for divine promotion.
  - On the other hand, disobedience creates cracks in your spiritual wall. It opens the door for the enemy to creep in. Ecclesiastes 10:8 KJV says, He that breaketh the hedge, the serpent shall bite. Persistent rebellion absolutely weakens your defenses, while obedience fortifies them. God has declared, I will make you a wall to this people, a fortified wall of bronze; they will fight against you but will not overcome you, for I am with you to rescue and save you (Jeremiah 15:20).
  - Remember, the enemy who tempts you is the same one who accuses you before God. Don't let him trap you in cycles of guilt and defeat. Victory is possible through Christ. You can resist temptation. You can obey. You are not a child of disobedience. You are a child of God. Let this truth guide your choices daily.
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for the grace to walk in obedience. By Your Spirit, I choose Your will over mine and honor You in my thoughts, words, and actions. I am a child of God. I completely walk in obedience. My walls are fortified. The enemy has no hold on me, in Jesus' name, Amen!
