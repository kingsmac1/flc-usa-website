title: Working Out your Salvation
scripture: Philippians 2:12 KJV
verse: Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.
body:
  - 'The Scripture above does not suggest that salvation is achieved through numerous human efforts. Salvation is a gift received through faith in Jesus Christ, as revealed in Ephesians 2:8 KJV: For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God. A gift is something freely given.'
  - One receives salvation by confessing Jesus as Lord (Romans 10:9). After that, God expects us to work out our salvation. This means pursuing spiritual maturity and living in accordance with the salvation we have received. The good news is that God works in us, giving us both the desire and the ability to do what pleases Him (Philippians 2:13).
  - Working out your salvation is not a passive experience. It requires deliberate effort and must be done with reverence and awe towards God. While salvation is received once, it must be nurtured, maintained, and expressed in fruitful living. Jesus Himself warned that unfruitfulness leads to rejection (Matthew 7:21). Salvation is not once saved, always saved. A life of
  - constant sin without repentance shows a person is not truly working out their salvation.
  - The ultimate goal of salvation is eternity with the Father in heaven. Working out your salvation through faith, obedience, Bible study, prayer, service, and spiritual growth makes you fruitful to God and keeps you aligned with His purpose.
prayer: Thank You, my Heavenly Father, for saving me. Thank You for always working in me both to will and to do Your good pleasure. I declare that I will work out my salvation with reverence and godly fear. My life will bear fruits unto You, and I will live for the praise of Your name, in Jesus' name. Amen.
