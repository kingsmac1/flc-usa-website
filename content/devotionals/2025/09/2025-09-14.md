title: Your Confession Brings the Manifestation
scripture: Romans 10:10 KJV
verse: For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.
body:
  - The Word of God becomes most effective in your life not only when you believe it, but when you confess it. That is why, at salvation, it was not enough to believe in Jesus as the Son of God; you also had to open your mouth and declare it.
  - The Greek word for confession is homologeo, which means to express the same thing, to agree with, or to acknowledge. Your confession is the outward expression of your inward belief; it is the release of your faith.
  - Jesus said, All things are possible to him that believeth (Mark 9:23). Yet many believe without seeing results. Why? This is because, often times, the confession is missing. Faith is both believing and speaking.
  - When you confess, you are affirming what God has already said concerning you. This is why Joshua 1:8 instructs, This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth… You would observe
  - that it says mouth, not just heart.
  - Your life will always move in the direction of your words. The circumstances you experience today are largely the result of the words you spoke in the past. Words are nothing but seeds. When you confess God's Word, you are sowing seeds that must germinate and produce fruits that look exactly like your words (Galatians 6:7; Hebrews 4:12).
  - Paul said in 2 Corinthians 4:13 KJV, We having the same spirit of faith, according as it is written, I believed, and therefore have I spoken; we also believe, and therefore speak. This is a strong reminder that faith speaks. Begin to boldly declare what you have seen and believed in Scripture. As you do, those words will shape your reality.
prayer: The Word of God is alive in me. My words are full of power. I confess that I am who God says I am. I am not moved by what I see; I am only moved by the Word of God. The Word is gaining ascendancy over my mind, my body, my words, and everything I do. I grow daily in the knowledge of God's Word. Glory to God!
