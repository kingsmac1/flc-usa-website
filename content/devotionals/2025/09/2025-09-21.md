date: '2025-09-21'
title: Advancing His Kingdom through Prayers
scripture: Psalm 110:3 KJV
verse: 'Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth'
body:
  - The Amplified version of Psalm 110:3 says, Your people will offer themselves willingly [to participate in Your battle] in the day of Your power… Every effort the body of Christ puts in for the work of the Lord is an opposition to the kingdom of darkness. Evangelism, prayer, Bible study, visitation, giving to the needy, loving one another, encouraging others, and many more are all efforts to cause the kingdom of God to prosper. Yet Satan and his kingdom have always opposed the advancement of God's work. In the day of His power, God's people will be willing to put in efforts towards the expansion of His kingdom.
  - Matthew 11:12 KJV says, And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force. The kingdom of heaven is God's sovereign rule, which Jesus came to establish on earth. Everyone who receives salvation has been ushered into God's kingdom. But the kingdom of heaven on earth has always faced opposition and persecution. John the Baptist, Jesus, and His disciples encountered violence, and this opposition continues today. These attacks may come from individuals or groups,
  - including religious leaders and political authorities, but they are ultimately engineered from the kingdom of darkness.
  - This clearly shows the ongoing spiritual warfare between God's kingdom and the forces of evil. The obvious violent reaction to the advancement of God's kingdom was evident in the hostility toward Jesus and His message, the martyrdom of His disciples, the present-day killings of Christians, and the attacks against the church. The enemy seeks to silence the gospel and distract God's people from their inheritance and their responsibility to advance His kingdom.
  - From the scripture above, the violent refers to men whose minds are made up, men who are ready to employ spiritual force to advance God's kingdom. They take it by force, grasping it as decisively as bandits snatch their prey. Our Lord Jesus was pointing to the energy and determination required to press into God's kingdom, urging us to show the same if salvation is to be obtained and worked out (Philippians 2:12).
  - You may speak to people all day without making an impact, but when you minister in the place of prayer, the Spirit of God compels them to listen and receive the message of salvation. Prayer is what sets people on fire for the Lord. Prayer ignites revival. Through prayer, the Holy Spirit convicts unbelievers of sin, stirs believers to greater devotion, and quickens the church and the nations to turn to God. This is how the kingdom of God prospers and advances in our time.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for the work of your Spirit in my life. Thank you for the light and revelation your word has brought to me today. I declare that I am a burning and shining light. As I pray, men and women are set on fire for you, turning to repentance. The nations are saved, and your will is done on earth in Jesus' name. Amen.
