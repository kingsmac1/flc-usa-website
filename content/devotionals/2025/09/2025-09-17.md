title: Your Faith Moves God
scripture: James 1:6 NKJV
verse: But let him ask in faith, with no doubting…
body:
  - To doubt means to have a divided mind; to not be fully convinced. It means you are not completely sure it will happen. Many people often doubt because they feel unworthy, or they feel their sin disqualifies them, or maybe they think they haven't prayed enough. Some even believe they are not God's favorites, or that their challenge is too big for Him to answer.
  - The good news is, faith is not about how you feel. It is about what God's Word has said. God wants you to simply believe. Don't try to figure out how it will happen. Just trust what His Word has declared concerning your situation.
  - Mark 11:23 ISV says, I tell all of you with certainty, if anyone says to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,' if he doesn't doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will happen, it will be done for him. There is so much audacity, assurance, and confidence in the words of Jesus in the scripture above. That means it is not a probability. It is a law.
  - Faith is that law. Speaking to a challenge you are facing, without
  - doubting in your heart, ensures your words will come to pass. Afflictions and challenges do not move God. It is your faith that moves Him. Faith is a catalyst; it activates God's intervention.
  - Hebrews 11:2 KJV says, By faith the elders obtained a good report. The great people of old were commended for their faith. By faith, they achieved very great things, overcame obstacles, defeated enemies, and obtained victories. You too can apply your faith, achieve great things, and have testimonies.
  - 'Zechariah 4:7 boldy declares: What are you, O great mountain, before Zerubbabel? You shall become a plain… What is that great obstacle, affliction, sickness, or challenge before you? Speak to it, and the hand of God will take it away, in Jesus'' name, Amen!'
prayer: In the name of Jesus, I speak to every obstacle against my health, my family, my ministry, and my finances. Be removed completely and made into a plain ground for me to thrive and advance. I am making progress in every area of my life in the name of Jesus. Amen.
