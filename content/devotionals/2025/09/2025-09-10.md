title: 'Love: A Proof of a True Disciple'
scripture: John 13:35 KJV
verse: By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.
body:
  - In His teaching to the disciples, Jesus emphasized that the love they show toward one another is the evidence to the world that they belong to Him. Love is the proof of true discipleship. The love Jesus referred to is the God-kind of love. It is unconditional, selfless, and sacrificial. This is called Agape love.
  - 'Sadly, in today''s church, we often see pastors against pastors, believers competing with one another, and churches filled with jealousy, malice, and quarrels. Such behavior does not reflect the life of true disciples. 1 John 4:7 NIV says: Dear friends, let us love one another, for love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. Verse 8 continues: Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love.'
  - Love is not merely a profession with words; it is revealed in our actions, patience, and kindness towards one another. As 1 Corinthians 13:4-8 teaches, Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs…
  - 'Paul also wrote in 2 Corinthians 13:5: Examine yourselves to see if your faith is genuine. Test yourselves. Surely you know that Jesus Christ is among you; if not, you have failed the test of genuine faith. If love is absent in your life, your faith is questionable. Moreover, Irrespective of the offense, past hurt, or misunderstanding you may have had with another believer, remember that even while you were lost in sin, God still loved you. He continues to love you unconditionally. Therefore, let go of bitterness and allow the love of God to flow through you by loving others.'
prayer: Dear Lord, I affirm that love is my nature, for Your love is shed abroad in my heart by the Holy Spirit. I choose to walk in love always. In my heart, there is no space for hatred, bitterness, or unforgiveness. The love of God flows through me continually. In Jesus' name, Amen.
