title: Stop the Accusation
scripture: Isaiah 43:25 NIV
verse: I, even I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake, and remembers your sins no more.
body:
  - 'God alone has the power to blot out sin, and He did so fully through the sacrifice of Jesus on the cross. But notice the second part of that verse: remembers your sins no more. This is not only God''s posture toward us; it should also be our posture toward others and ourselves.'
  - To remember someone's wrongs means to keep a record of them, to rehearse them continuously in our minds, or to use them as a weapon when hurt lingers. But true forgiveness means letting go of hurt completely. It signifies restoration of relationship and freedom from accusation.
  - 'The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 5:19 NIV: ...not counting people''s sins against them... To count someone''s sins against them is to accuse them, and when we do, we are not reflecting the nature of God. Forgiveness does not wait for apologies; it flows from the love of God within us.'
  - The one whose nature is to accuse vehemently is the devil.
  - 'Revelation 12:10 calls him the accuser of God''s people. Satan constantly accuses believers before God, but at the same time, Jesus is interceding and advocating for us. Colossians 2:14 NLT tells us: He canceled the record of the charges against us and took it away by nailing it to the cross.'
  - Beloved, Jesus has already erased the record of your sins. That means you must first forgive yourself. Stop replaying your past failures. Then, extend that same forgiveness to others. Don't remind them of their wrongs. Don't wait for them to apologize before you release them in your heart.
  - 'I pray for you: May the Lord purge your heart of every trace of self-accusation, bitterness, pain, and unforgiveness, in Jesus'' name.'
prayer: The Holy Spirit is working on my mind right now. He is flushing away every file of resentment, offense, trauma, unforgiveness, and condemnation. I let them go. I experience the love of Christ lavished upon my heart. My heart is healed, free, and at peace in Jesus' name. Amen!
