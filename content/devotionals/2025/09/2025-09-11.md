title: God’s Word Has a Curative Ability
scripture: Proverbs 4:21–22 NKJV
verse: …for they are life to those who find them and health to one's whole body.
body:
  - Psalm 107:20 reveals that God sent His Word and healed the people, delivering them from destruction. God's Word brings healing and deliverance only to those who embrace it. He will not force His Word on anyone, but the moment you accept it wholeheartedly and without doubt, your healing and deliverance will manifest.
  - The key is God's Word. It doesn't matter how many times or how many people have prayed for you; if you do not receive the Word with faith, it will not produce healing in you. Before the aspect of receiving God's Word comes into play, you must first hear the Word or study it for yourself. That is why Proverbs 4:22 says, God's words are life to those who find them and health to their whole body. You have to find it by making yourself available to it.
  - The word health in Proverbs 4:22 is from the Hebrew word Marpe, which means healing or medicine. Just like medicine has curative ability when taken properly, God's Word also has
  - spiritual and physical ailments, completely bringing restoration and wholeness.
prayer: The Holy Spirit lives in me; therefore, I am not susceptible to infirmity. The life of God in me keeps me in perfect health. Every day of my life is filled with God's glory, beauty, excellence, and grace. I exercise dominion over sickness and death. My body is infused with divine health and strength, in Jesus' name. Amen!
