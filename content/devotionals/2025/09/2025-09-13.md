date: '2025-09-13'
title: Don’t Give in to Satan’s Deception
scripture: John 13:27 NIV
verse: As soon as Judas took the bread, Satan entered into him. So Jesus told him, 'What you are about to do, do quickly.
body:
  - Before Satan can use anyone to execute his agenda, he usually begins by giving that person a prompting. These promptings come as lies and deceptions. If he succeeds in gaining control of your mind, then it becomes easy for him to use you.
  - From John 13:27, the phrase when Judas took the bread means Judas had finally made up his mind to betray Jesus. The enemy had been prompting him repeatedly until he fully accepted the idea. Once he yielded, Satan entered into him.
  - In the same way, I have heard people say they heard voices telling them to do wrong things, or that their minds told them to commit suicide. These are promptings of Satan; they are direct attacks on the mind. Things like unforgiveness, hate, envy, jealousy, lies, and depression are also his promptings. Once they are accommodated in the heart, they crystallize into real actions that harm both you and others, eventually leading to regret, just as Judas experienced.
  - We see the same in the case of Ananias. Acts 5:3 NLT says,
  - Then Peter said, 'Ananias, why have you let Satan fill your heart? You lied to the Holy Spirit, and you kept some of the money for yourself.' Satan prompted him, and he yielded to the thought. That deception eventually led to his death. The enemy is always trying to use his manipulative skills to push believers against the will of God. This is why discernment is vital.
  - As a believer, you have the responsibility to resist these attacks and lies against your mind. Proverbs 4:23 CEV says, Carefully guard your thoughts because they are the source of true life. Be deliberate about rejecting thoughts that are not aligned with God's will. If you notice your mind being flooded with ungodly or unpleasant thoughts, rebuke them immediately and they will cease. In this regard, James 4:7 KJV stresses, Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.
prayer: I declare that my mind is anointed. The Holy Spirit broods over me and regulates my thoughts. I do not accommodate evil or unwholesome ideas. I have the mind of Christ. I walk in love, power, and a sound mind in Jesus' name. Amen.
