date: '2025-09-04'
title: There is no condemnation Against you
scripture: John 3:18 KJV
verse: 'He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.'
body:
  - 'The key to being free from condemnation is to believe in Jesus Christ as the Son of God and Saviour. Your justification is not based on your works but on your faith in Christ Jesus. John 3:18 NIV declares: Whoever believes in him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe stands condemned already because they have not believed in the name of God''s one and only Son.'
  - In simple terms, the one who believes in Jesus is justified. He is acquitted. But the one who refuses to believe in Him is already condemned. Unbelief results in separation from God.
  - According to Romans 8:1 KJV, There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit. Condemnation means judgment and penalty for sin. However, because you are in Christ Jesus (because you are born again), every charge and accusation against you has been blotted out by the blood of Jesus, making you free and justified. Colossians 2:14 KJV
  - 'says: Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross.'
  - 'The devil, described as the accuser of the brethren (Revelation 12:10), always seeks to remind people of their past mistakes and shortcomings. He tries to make them feel that their sins are too big and unforgivable, leading to regret, depression, and self- condemnation. But always remind yourself that Jesus has already paid for all your sins. Therefore, boldly declare: Jesus has permanently set me free; I am free from guilt, and there is no condemnation against me because I am in Christ Jesus.'
prayer: I am a child of God. Jesus has set me free from my past, every guilt, and every accusation. My mind is sound. The weapons of the enemy will not prosper against me. The blood of Jesus speaks on my behalf always. The devil has no hold on me. In Jesus' name, Amen!
