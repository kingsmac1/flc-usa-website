title: You are Healed
scripture: Isaiah 53:5 KJV
verse: 'But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed.'
body:
  - 'The physical suffering of Jesus during His crucifixion brought both spiritual and physical healing to every believer. Isaiah 53:4 NET says, But he lifted up our illness, he carried our pain; even though we thought he was being punished, attacked by God, and afflicted for something he had done. Our focus here is: He lifted up our illness, He carried our pain. This is not a future promise; it has already been done.'
  - During the crucifixion, there was a divine exchange. Jesus took your place. He carried your sicknesses, pains, afflictions, sorrows, burdens, and weaknesses. He did not want anyone who believes in Him to suffer sickness and affliction, so He took them away.
  - The punishment He endured was for your sake; so that you could have peace, wholeness, and reconciliation with God. By His stripes (the wounds from His scourging), you were healed. Because of this, you have no reason to remain in sickness. Hallelujah!
  - God does not see you as sick; He sees you as healed. The one for whom Jesus bore sickness and sin. The moment you gave your life to Christ, that exchange became your reality. So, what next?
  - Meditate on this truth until it renews your mind. Declare it boldly to yourself daily, even if symptoms linger. Continue this way until you experience the physical evidence of your healing. Glory to God!
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for taking away my sins, sicknesses, pain, diseases, afflictions, sorrows, and suffering. I declare that I am healed spiritually, mentally, and physically. The stripes of Jesus have healed me. I am totally whole, in Jesus' name. Amen!
