title: You Have Received a Royal Calling
scripture: 1 Peter 2:9 KJV
verse: But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light.
body:
  - In the Old Testament, the tribe of Levi was set apart as priests to serve Israel. This was known as the Levitical priesthood, and their duties included offering sacrifices, maintaining the tabernacle, and acting as mediators between God and the people.
  - In Genesis 14:18–21, we are introduced to Melchizedek, king of Salem and priest of the Most High God. He blessed Abram, showing us a foreshadow of a priesthood that was both royal and spiritual. This pointed forward to Christ.
  - 'The New Testament clearly confirms this in Hebrews 7:17 KJV: For he testifieth, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec. Christ''s death and resurrection brought a new covenant, establishing Him as our eternal High Priest. Because of Him, all believers now share in this royal priesthood, a reality often called the priesthood of all believers.'
  - 'This means you are no longer ordinary. In Christ, you are both a king and a priest. You have direct access to God''s presence and carry spiritual authority. You don’t need a human mediator at all; Jesus has already made the way open. As part of this royal calling:'
  - · Your prayers, worship, and service are powerful and meaningful. · You represent God on earth, offering spiritual sacrifices such as thanksgiving, praise, fervent worship, obedience, and the joyous sharing of His Word. · Your life is to reflect God's character, goodness, and glory daily.
  - Don't let feelings of insignificance hold you back. You have been set apart by God Himself. Walk in the dignity and responsibility of your calling, serving Him with boldness and devotion
prayer: Father, thank You for calling me into Your royal priesthood. Thank You for making me a competent minister of Your name. Today I declare that I will continually offer to You my sacrifice of praise, my body, and my substance. Use me as a vessel to reflect Your light in my world, in Jesus' name. Amen.
