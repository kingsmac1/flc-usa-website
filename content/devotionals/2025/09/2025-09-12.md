date: '2025-09-12'
title: He Vitalizes your Body
scripture: Romans 8:11 KJV
verse: But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you. -
body:
  - There is an influence of alcohol on the consumer. In that same way, the Holy Spirit has an influence not only on your spirit but also on your body. It is important to know your body is the dwelling place of the Holy Spirit.
  - 1 Corinthians 6:19 NLT says, Don't you realize that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit, who lives in you and was given to you by God? God wants you to be fully aware and understand clearly that His Spirit in you is not dormant. He is actively working and influencing all areas of your life, including your body. Until you accept this truth, you might not experience His full impact.
  - The word quicken as used in Romans 8:11 KJV means to make alive, to vitalize, to strengthen. This is the active and ongoing role of the Holy Spirit in you, ensuring that you are energized, revitalized, and strengthened at all times. Hallelujah!
  - Normally, no one can put his or her finger into a moving fan blade without being cut off by its active rotation. In the same manner, because of the active influence of the Holy Spirit in you, every germ, virus, or disease that tries to attack your body is cut off. Glory to God!
  - Not only are you supposed to think this way, but you must also make faith declarations of divine health over your body always.
prayer: I am a well-watered garden, and streams of healing waters flow from me. I don't contract or transmit diseases. Divine health is working in me. The presence of the Holy Spirit in me quickens, strengthens, and replenishes my body. I am always healthy and sound in Jesus' name. Amen.
