title: Enablement of Divine Skills
scripture: Daniel 9:21-22 KJV
verse: Yea, whiles I was speaking in prayer, even the man Gabriel, whom had seen in the vision at the beginning, being caused to fly swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening oblation. And he informed me, and talked with me, and said, O Daniel, I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding
body:
  - The skill in the context of our opening Bible verse refers to wisdom. This skill comes with discernment and insight into the complexities of life. It is an enablement to see beyond the surface level. The involvement of an angel from God shows that this wisdom is divine and not intellectual.
  - 'Proverbs 4:7 KJV clearly teaches us: Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore, get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding. Wisdom is a divine attribute that reveals God''s character. The Hebrew word for wisdom is chokmah, which entails not just knowledge but also the skillful application of knowledge. Wisdom is supreme. It is of greater value than riches and earthly power. This is why it is important to prioritize wisdom above all else.'
  - The scripture tells us to acquire wisdom, not fame or riches. With wisdom, riches and other necessities of life will follow. Wisdom is not automatic; it must be diligently pursued. This
  - pursuit is likened to a treasure hunt, as Proverbs 2:4 describes wisdom in relation to silver and hidden treasures. King Solomon understood this principle, which is why he asked God for wisdom.
  - In the Book of Daniel, divine intervention went beyond the impartation of wisdom and skill. When Daniel set his heart to seek God through prayer and fasting, his request was heard immediately. God dispatched the angel Gabriel to deliver the answer, but Gabriel was resisted and delayed for twenty-one days by the spiritual force of the Persian kingdom. Because Daniel remained steadfast, God sent Michael, a higher-ranked angel, to assist Gabriel and ensure the message was delivered. This shows the reality of spiritual warfare behind answered prayer and the layered nature of divine intervention. Daniel did not only receive wisdom; he also received angelic help, revealing that God's response matched both the resistance faced and the persistence shown (Daniel 10:12-13 KJV).
  - Are there areas in your life where you need divine wisdom, understanding, solutions, or intervention? Do you wonder why certain situations have lingered? Often, unseen forces are at work behind the scenes. What you truly need is divine skill. Divine skill brings wisdom, progress, breakthrough, direction, and deliverance. Make time to seek the Lord earnestly through prayer and fasting, and He will intervene.
