title: Be Spiritually Minded
scripture: Romans 8:6 KJV
verse: For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace
body:
  - The sinful nature of humanity has its consequences. The flesh is in opposition to the Spirit, and the flesh also has its influence on life. To be carnally minded means to have a mindset that is dominated by self and fleshly desires.
  - To be spiritually minded refers to developing a mindset that is transformed by the Spirit of God. This also means having a mindset that is dominated by God's will and purpose. Genesis 2:17 KJV says, But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it, for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die. Death here is not just physical but also spiritual, which is separation from God. This means being carnally minded leads to separation from God.
  - Separation from God as a result of carnal mindedness leads to fleshly living and bearing fruits of the flesh. It is enmity with God (Romans 8:7). This separation hinders your ability to partake in the divine nature of God.
  - To be spiritually minded is life and peace. This life and peace begin here on earth. Life refers to eternal life, which is the life
  - and nature of God. Through the Holy Spirit, you are connected to your source, enabled to bear fruits according to the desires of God. You also experience the peace of God that comes with spiritual mindedness. That is why Isaiah 26:3 KJV says, You will keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee.
  - Finally, open your heart to learn, study, and meditate on the word of God. God's word will fill your heart, regulate your thought process, and shift you from being carnally minded to being spiritually minded. Then you will experience the life and peace of God in abundance.
prayer: I have the life of God. My mind is regulated by the Holy Spirit and the word of God. God's word renews my mind. I refuse to conform to the systems of this world; rather, God's word transforms me always. I have the mind of Christ.
