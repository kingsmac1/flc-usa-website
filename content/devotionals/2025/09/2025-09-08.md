date: '2025-09-08'
title: Don’t Underestimate God’s Abilities
scripture: Jeremiah 32:27
verse: Behold, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh; is there anything too hard for me?
body:
  - Not underestimating God means trusting that He can do far more than you can imagine. It means refusing to limit your expectations of Him to your own understanding. God's love and power are limitless, and His ability goes beyond human reasoning.
  - In Matthew 14:13–21, when Jesus asked Philip where they could buy bread to feed the 5,000, Philip immediately started calculating the cost. That's what many of us do; we analyze situations with human logic and forget that God is not bound by our limitations.
  - Jesus went on to feed over five thousand people (excluding women and children) with only five loaves of bread and two fish. Not only were they all satisfied, but twelve baskets of leftovers remained. That miracle defied every human calculation.
  - When a situation looks too big, too costly, or beyond human effort and science, remember that God is sovereign. Just because you don't understand how it will happen doesn't mean
  - it's impossible with Him. Don't put God in a box. As Mark 10:27 teaches, With man, it is impossible, but not with God. For all things are possible with God.
  - 'Proverbs 3:5 reminds us: Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding. Even when you don''t see the process, God wants you to trust His ability to bring about the miraculous in your life.'
prayer: Dear Lord, thank You for Your supremacy. Thank You because nothing is impossible with You. I am grateful for Your power to do exceedingly, abundantly above all I could ever ask or imagine. I choose to trust in You completely. In Jesus' name, Amen.
