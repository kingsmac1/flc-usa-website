title: The Law of the Spirit of Life
scripture: Galatians 5:1 AMP
verse: It was for this freedom that Christ set us free (completely liberating us); therefore keep standing firm and do not be subject again to a yoke of slavery (which you once removed).
body:
  - Romans 8:2 AMP says, For the law of the Spirit of life [which is] in Christ Jesus [the law of our new being] has set you free from the law of sin and death. The word law as used in the scripture above refers to a principle or power. The Spirit of life refers to the Holy Spirit, who imparts divine life into a believer.
  - The Holy Spirit is the life-giving Spirit. He brings life into dead and dry situations. He makes whole, quickens, restores, and even brings healing to the sick body. This Spirit of life gives eternal life to everyone who receives Christ, making them a new creation in Him.
  - On the other hand, the law of sin and death refers to the principle that leads to death. This came as a result of Adam's disobedience (Romans 5:19). Because of this principle, mankind could not attain righteousness by their own efforts. Even the Law of Moses could not make people righteous before
  - God; instead, it revealed sin and placed humanity under bondage.
  - But glory to God! Romans 3:25 NIV says, God presented Christ as a sacrifice of atonement, through the shedding of His blood – to be received by faith. Jesus became the perfect sacrifice. Now, through faith, anyone can receive the benefits of His atoning sacrifice. The moment we accept Christ, the Holy Spirit comes into our lives and breaks the power of sin and death, making us free.
  - Now, in Christ, you have become the righteousness of God. Sin and death no longer have dominion over you (Romans 6:9). Hallelujah!
prayer: Thank You, Jesus, for being the perfect sacrifice for my sins. Thank You for making me the righteousness of God in You. I testify that the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has set me free from the law of sin and death. Therefore, sin and death have no dominion over me. Hallelujah!
