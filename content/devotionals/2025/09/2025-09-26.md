date: '2025-09-26'
title: Revival in the Church
scripture: Psalm 110:3 KJV
verse: 'Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth'
body:
  - In the day of His power, God's people will be willing. Willing to do what? Willing to serve God in Spirit and in truth, to be committed and consistent, to be discipled and actively involved in His work. The willingness of the people reveals a readiness to serve and support the agenda of the Lord. This is an indication of a transformation of hearts and minds leading to revival.
  - I have heard and seen cases where brethren do not want to get committed in church or support the work of the Lord. As a shepherd or leader of such people, you do not have to get frustrated or compel them to be actively involved in the vision. Simply learn to win behind closed doors. I mean, you should win not behind the pulpit but on your knees in prayer. Except you pray, you might struggle, complain, and even get discouraged. Their willingness is not dependent on your eloquence or human effort but by the Spirit.
  - In the church today, our major opponent is not human beings. We are not battling with man but against spiritual forces of darkness that veil and bend the minds of people. 2 Corinthians
  - 4:4 KJV says, In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them. This blinding of minds is an intense spiritual blindness that prevents people from understanding and accepting the truth of the gospel.
  - Satan does not blind the minds of unbelievers only. He tries to stop them from believing the gospel and also distracts believers to ensure the gospel does not take root in them. His plan is to make them unfruitful and unestablished in God. That is why some new converts find themselves drifting back to worldliness. We wage this war and obtain victory through prayer.
  - Galatians 4:19 KJV says, My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you. To travail in prayer until Christ is formed in others implies a deep, often painful form of intercessory prayer, where you intensely pray for others until they reveal the character and nature of Christ. It is a spiritual labor compared to childbirth, reflecting the intense effort and longing for spiritual growth in those being prayed for.
  - This is an ongoing process, revealing that spiritual maturity is not instantaneous but requires time and effort. The labor continues until Christ is completely and permanently formed in the hearts of the brethren. During this process, the Lord will put His word in their minds and inscribe it on their hearts (Jeremiah 31:33). Then they will become willing to accept the counsel of God and be vitally involved in the Master's agenda.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for the light of your word that has shined upon my heart today. I thank you for the divine awakening of my heart by your Spirit. I will remain on fire for you and continue to intercede for others. I declare that the influence of the devil over my mind, family, and nation is broken. May the light of your word shine brightly upon their hearts, leading to a great revival in Jesus' name. Amen.
