title: Love for the Lord
scripture: 1 Corinthians 2:9 NIV
verse: 'However, as it is written: What no eye has seen, what no ear has heard, and what no human mind has conceived — the things God has prepared for those who love him'
body:
  - J ohn 14:15 AMP says, If you really love me, you will obey my commandments. Jesus is saying that obeying His commandments is the proof of anyone's love for Him. Love for Christ is not merely an emotional feeling. Rather, it is an action that involves your commitment to the Lord. How committed are you to God?
  - 'God wants you to keep His commandments, not out of legalistic obligation as it was under the law of Moses, but by living out the principles of His word today. Now, His laws are written on your heart: I will put my laws in their minds and inscribe them on their hearts (Hebrews 8:10). When you live according to God''s principles, it shows that you are faithful and that you love the Lord.'
  - There are many blessings and rewards made available for all those who love the Lord (Hebrews 11:6). From 1 Corinthians 2:9, we see that human eyes, ears, and minds are limited and cannot fully comprehend or measure the great things God has planned for those who love Him. It is such a glorious future that
  - awaits all faithful believers. These blessings are not just for the future but also for this present life.
  - Romans 8:28 NLT says, And we know that God causes everything to work together for the good of those who love God and are called according to His purpose for them. To all those who love the Lord and are aligned with His will, God is constantly ensuring that everything on earth aligns for their good. Then you can confidently say that you cannot be disadvantaged because God is at work concerning your life.
prayer: 'Affirm: The nature of the Father is in my heart, and the love of Christ is shed abroad in my heart by the Holy Spirit. I am committed to obeying and keeping the principles of the Lord. Thank you, Lord, for the unimaginable blessings that you have in store for me. Hallelujah!'
