title: Anticipation for the Return of Christ
scripture: John 14:3 KJV
verse: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.
body:
  - The return of Christ is the second coming of Jesus, which He has promised to all believers as seen in John 14:3. This promise gives us great hope and encouragement, knowing that the Lord Jesus will return again to take us home. We are pilgrims on earth, and heaven is our eternal home. A glorious future awaits us, and this should motivate us to live daily in holiness and anticipation.
  - Acts 1:11 KJV says, “Which also said, Ye men of Galilee, why stand ye gazing up into heaven? this same Jesus, which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven.” The phrase in like manner means that Jesus will return visibly and bodily, just as He ascended.
  - '1 Thessalonians 4:16-17 KJV reveals more about this return: the Lord Jesus will descend from heaven with a commanding shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trumpet of God. At the sound of the trumpet, all believers will be gathered. Those who died in faith will rise first, as stated in 1 Corinthians 15:20-23, and then the believers who are still alive will be'
  - caught up to meet the Lord in the air.
  - The return of Christ gives us comfort and hope, knowing we will see our loved ones again if they died in Christ. More importantly, we will be with the Lord forever. As we anticipate His return, we must remain prepared. We prepare by being born again, living in holiness, and sharing the gospel with others.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for the hope we have in you, for your promise to return and take us home to be with you forever. I will remain faithful and strong to the end. I choose to live a godly and purposeful life, prepared for your return. In Jesus' name, Amen.
