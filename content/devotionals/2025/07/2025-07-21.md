title: Anything is Possible
scripture: Mark 9:23 KJV
verse: Jesus said unto him, If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth.
body:
  - Mark 9:23 shows us the response of Jesus to a man who brought his sick son to be healed. Although Jesus, the Healer, was available, the man still doubted whether Jesus could actually intervene. Yet, Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, and today, and forever (Hebrews 13:8 KJV). He is always available and ready to intervene in matters that concern us. But faith is necessary in order to receive from Him.
  - Mark 5:34 KJV says, “And he said unto her, Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace, and be whole of thy plague.” Mark 5:28 KJV also says, “For she said, If I may touch but his clothes, I shall be whole.” Many people were clustered around Jesus during this event. Others touched Him accidentally and received nothing. But this woman with the issue of blood had a different approach. She was intentional and believed that if she could touch Jesus, she would be healed. Her action was faith in motion. And because of her faith, she received her healing and was made whole.
  - All things (excluding nothing) are possible to the one who
  - believes. The woman's action of touching Jesus' garment was an expression of her faith in His divine power and authority. Her faith was rewarded. She received healing, and the deep desire of her heart was granted.
  - The healing, miracle, deliverance, restoration or answer you need is possible if only you believe in God's ability to grant your request. Matthew 19:26 KJV says, “With God all things are possible.” God has no limitations. He is always willing and able to grant your desires. Now, the responsibility rests on your faith. All things are possible to him that believes (Mark 9:23 KJV).
prayer: Thank you, Father, for being faithful and reliable. Thank you for the opportunity to receive what we desire through faith in you. I affirm that I can do all things through Christ that strengthens me. Amen.
