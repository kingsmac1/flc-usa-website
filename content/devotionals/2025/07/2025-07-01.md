title: understanding Stronghold
scripture: 2 Corinthians 10:4 KJV
verse: For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strongholds
body:
  - The word stronghold was used in the Bible in several contexts. It could have either a positive or negative meaning. In simple terms, a stronghold is anything that has a firm grip on you. It is something that holds you strongly and emphasizes how forceful its influence can be.
  - 'A stronghold is usually a barrier. It can be a positive barrier, as the scripture reveals in Psalm 9:9 NIV: The Lord is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. In this context, a stronghold is seen as a fortified place offering safety and protection against enemies. It means the Lord is a defence against your enemies (Nahum 1:7, Psalm 18:2).'
  - However, strongholds can also be negative, and that is our focus. For the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but have divine power to destroy strongholds (2 Corinthians 10:4 ESV). These negative strongholds are barriers engineered by the devil, who is our enemy. They may appear as mental strongholds, which include arguments, ideologies, reasonings, beliefs, and traditions that oppose the knowledge of God. As a believer, any cultural ideology that contradicts God's word is a
  - stronghold.
  - The enemy erects these strongholds to resist the truth of God's word. Other examples include the strongholds of offence, pride, bitterness, and human values that do not agree with the principles of God's word.
  - Strongholds could also be spiritual. These are areas of one's life where Satan has kept someone in bondage. It could be in your finances, marriage, health, or any other area. And ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, be loosed from this bond on the Sabbath day? (Luke 13:16 KJV) This verse shows that the woman's affliction was caused by Satan. He goes about trying to oppose God's word and hinder the blessings of God's people and humanity. In such oppression, Satan has a firm and powerful hold.
  - I pray for your liberation today from every area where the enemy has held you bound, in Jesus name, amen.
