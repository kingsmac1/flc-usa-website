title: The Lord Has Made you a Deliverer
scripture: Obadiah 1:21 NIV
verse: Deliverers will go up on Mount Zion to govern the Mountains of Esau. And the kingdom will be the Lord's.
body:
  - Adeliverer is a person God has raised to rescue others from affliction, danger, troubles and mistakes. A deliverer is an instrument of God's intervention, acting in alignment with His plan to bring deliverance and restoration to people. These individuals are chosen by God, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and are expected to obey His instructions in order to deliver others.
  - According to Acts 10:38 NIV, “how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and power, and how he went around doing good and healing all who were under the power of the devil, because God was with him.” Jesus was a deliverer. God empowered Him with the Holy Spirit, which enabled Him to heal and deliver people from the oppression of the devil, from their past, their sins and their limitations.
  - 1 John 4:17 KJV says, “because as he is, so are we in this world.” Jesus is a deliverer, and God has made us deliverers too in our time. We have received the same empowerment He gave Jesus (the Holy Spirit) so we can function in the same capacity.
  - This helps us understand that we have a divine assignment to deliver others.
  - As a believer, it is important to know that to be effective as a deliverer, you must understand that God has a set time for everything. Your spiritual growth and maturity are essential. God wants you to grow so that you can handle greater levels of responsibility and influence. This growth starts with knowing the scriptures. Study the scriptures and become familiar with the written word of God. The word will reveal your true identity, your rights, and the divine provisions you have in Christ.
  - The process of studying God's word prepares and qualifies you before God. 2 Timothy 2:15 KJV says, “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” The word approved here implies a testing or proving process. Only those who pass this test are deemed fit and approved by God's standard.
  - Isaiah 61:1 KJV says, “The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound.” You have received the divine empowerment of the Holy Spirit, and God expects you to submit yourself to Him and maintain a continuous walk with Him through prayer. As you do this, you will grow in spiritual stature and become increasingly effective in bringing deliverance to your family, your city and your nation.
prayer: I declare, the Spirit of the Lord is upon me. I am no longer limited because the Lord enables me to do all things. I have received the divine empowerment and presence of God. Thank you, Lord, for making me a deliverer and for anointing me with your Spirit and power. I produce good works and I effectively bring hope, deliverance, restoration and your perfect will to the lives of others, in Jesus' name, Amen.
