title: Trust the Process
scripture: Proverbs 3:5-6 NIV
verse: Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your path straight
body:
  - To trust the process means to have faith in what God is doing, that He is purposefully and actively at work in your life, even when things seem uncertain and challenging. In good times and in rough seasons, God is truly involved to ensure that all things work out according to His plans and for your good.
  - Romans 8:28 NIV states, And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose. All things, including the bad and the good, the pleasant and the unpleasant, the joyful and the sorrowful, are intentionally and harmoniously orchestrated to create a cohesive and pleasant outcome for you.
  - There are events in life that may not make sense. You might not be able to comprehend how God will turn them around for your good. You may even find it difficult to feel that God is working out something great. Did you lose your job, your business, or a loved one? Are you struggling to understand how God is harmoniously working things out? This is why you are instructed not to rely on your own understanding (Proverbs 3:5).
  - Proverbs 3:6 AMP says, In all your ways know and acknowledge and recognize Him, and He will make your paths straight and smooth (removing obstacles that block your way). When you recognize God's sovereignty and His involvement in your journey, He will make your way straight by removing unnecessary and unplanned obstacles. Even when faced with unfavorable situations, as long as you acknowledge God in your ways, He will strengthen you and shape you through that process. As it is contained in Romans 5:4 NLT, Endurance develops strength of character, and character strengthens our confident hope of salvation.
prayer: Dear Lord, I am ever grateful for your great plans to make all situations work together for my good. I will always trust you with all my heart and not rely on my own understanding. Thank you because your ways are perfect, and as you guide me through your paths, I am being perfected in Jesus' name, amen.
