title: What’s on your Mind During Prayer?
scripture: Romans 8:6 NLT
verse: So, letting your sinful nature control your mind leads to death. But letting the Spirit control your mind leads” to life and peace
body:
  - It is not uncommon for the mind to wander during prayer. This does not mean something is wrong with you. Rather, it simply means you have not yet gained mastery over your mind. This kind of mastery is not achieved overnight. It involves self-discipline and transformation by the Holy Spirit and the word of God (Romans 12:2).
  - 'During prayer, distractions and irrelevant thoughts may arise. These often come from the worries and cares of this world (Matthew 6:34), and unwanted thoughts planted by the enemy. These are strategies used by the devil to distract you and prevent you from gaining the full benefit of prayer. This is why spiritual warfare includes pulling down imaginations, thoughts, and voices that are not in line with the word of God (2 Corinthians 10:5). There are three types of thoughts that are permitted during prayer:'
  - First, thoughts inspired by the Holy Spirit. These are divine ideas, such as the inspiration to write a book, the remembrance of your dreams, or strategies to solve a particular challenge. In
  - these moments, the Holy Spirit is guiding you to understand spiritual matters and to receive divine insight (John 14:26).
  - Second, thoughts about God and His sovereignty. These are thoughts of reverence, adoration, and worship. For great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods (Psalm 96:4). These thoughts glorify God and are commonly found in the book of Psalms.
  - Third, thoughts of anticipation concerning the impact of your prayer. Here, you allow your mind to believe that God will answer your request. This means you have faith that God will do what you ask. For example, when praying for healing, reject every negative thought and begin to imagine and see yourself healed. Picture yourself doing the things you were previously unable to do because of the sickness (Hebrews 11:6).
prayer: My heart is sanctified. I permit only wholesome thoughts and thoughts inspired by the Holy Spirit. I have the mind of Christ. My mind is focused on God's word, in Jesus' name, amen.
