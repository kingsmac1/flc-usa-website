title: Deliverance from the Negatives
scripture: Colossians 1:13 KJV
verse: Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son
body:
  - Deliverance from the negatives implies freedom from negative and harmful influences, experiences, and thoughts. The phrase emphasizes freedom from spiritual bondage. The domain or kingdom of darkness refers to the realm of sin, negativity, darkness, and the influence of Satan. This deliverance is not by human effort, rather, it is by divine intervention.
  - Colossians 1:13 says, hath delivered us from the power of darkness. This means the work is already done. But why are some believers still afflicted, tormented, and influenced by the devil? The answer is clear. Though God has delivered us, the devil is a rebel and always tries to oppose the work of God. Satan wants to test if you really know all that Christ did for you through His death, burial, and resurrection.
  - Satan is an enemy of God and goes about seeking whom he may devour (1 Peter 5:8). He targets those who are vulnerable to him, those who are ignorant of their inheritance in Christ, and those who cannot resist him. Resist the devil and he will flee
  - from you (James 4:7). This is where the spiritual warfare aspect of deliverance comes in.
  - Matthew 11:12 KJV says, And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force. The devil has always opposed God's kingdom and God's people. It is those who are spiritually violent that can resist the devil and enjoy their full inheritance in Christ. Do not be gentle with the devil. Use the name of Jesus to resist him and he will flee.
  - I pray for you today, that every yoke of affliction by the devil over your life will be broken right now. Receive your total deliverance in Jesus' name, amen.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for delivering me from the kingdom and influence of Satan. I declare that every work of Satan and darkness in my life, family, finances, health, city, and nation is broken now in Jesus' name. I declare I am free. Thank you for delivering me.
