title: Enjoy His Rest
scripture: Hebrews 4:10 KJV
verse: For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.
body:
  - When the Bible talks about rest, there are basically two types. The first is the rest we have in Christ while we are still on earth. It is our present-day reality and one of the benefits of salvation. From our opening verse in Hebrews 4:10 KJV, “For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his,” we see that this is a spiritual rest a believer enters into through faith in Jesus Christ. It is not physical rest but spiritual rest that comes with receiving eternal life.
  - This rest is a foretaste of the eternal rest we will enjoy when we get to heaven. It means we no longer strive to earn salvation through our own works. Rather, we rely on the finished work of Christ on the cross. This rest is similar to God's rest after creation. In Genesis 2:2, God rested on the seventh day, not because He was tired but to set a pattern for man and to symbolize the completion of His work. This rest foreshadows the true rest we find in Christ. It also relates to the Sabbath rest, which was a shadow of the reality fulfilled in Christ, as seen in Colossians 2:16-17.
  - 'The second type of rest is eternal rest, and it takes place when we get to heaven. Revelation 14:13 KJV says, “And I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them.” This eternal rest is the reward for believers who remain faithful to Christ until death. They now enjoy peace and relief from all earthly struggles.'
  - It is important to note that you must receive the invitation to rest in Christ here on earth to qualify for eternal rest. Jesus is still calling you to enjoy His rest. Even though many believers are saved, they are still not enjoying the peace and joy His rest brings. Matthew 11:28 NLT says, “Then Jesus said, 'Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest.'” This is an invitation to all who are burdened physically, emotionally or spiritually. The hands of Jesus are wide open, welcoming you into His warm embrace that brings peace, healing and restoration.
  - Take advantage of the grace in Christ Jesus. Acknowledge and accept God's unearned favour, and allow it to empower your life. Begin to draw from the well of inexhaustible blessings in Christ. There is grace, healing, joy, deliverance and increase. Come boldly to the throne of grace and obtain mercy and favour in times of need. Use the name of Jesus today, and God will answer you, strengthen you and cause you to enjoy all-round peace, Amen.
