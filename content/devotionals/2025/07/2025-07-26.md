date: '2025-07-26'
title: Endeavour to Finish Well
scripture: Galatians 6:9 NLT
verse: So let's not get tired of doing what is good. At just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we do not give up.
body:
  - The scripture above gives us hope to persevere in well doing. It encourages us not to become exhausted or discouraged when doing good or when going through trials. For in God's perfect timing, we will reap a harvest from all our actions. Therefore, do not give up. Do not reduce your commitment to God.
  - Your labour and commitment to God are not in vain, even if you do not see immediate results. Hebrews 6:10 NASB says, God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love which you have shown toward His name, by having served and by still serving the saints. God takes note, and He values all your good works, even when others do not. This includes the many forms of service you render to the body of Christ and to other believers.
  - Many people get discouraged in their labour, either because of trials, fatigue, or even offence. The Lord, however, expects us to be steadfast and endure to the end. Our walk with the Lord is a race. Hebrews 12:1 ESV says, Therefore, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us also lay
  - aside every weight and sin which clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us.
  - Continue putting all necessary effort into your lasting commitment, with patience, perseverance, and discipline, knowing that at the end, you will receive a crown from the Lord. Philippians 3:14 NLT clearly says, I press on to reach the end of the race and receive the heavenly prize for which God, through Christ Jesus, is calling us.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for my labour of love in your name and for your assurance that my service is not in vain. I thank you for the great reward that awaits me when I meet you face to face. Strengthen me, Lord, that I may remain faithful and endure to the end. In Jesus name, amen.
