title: Deliverance into your Inheritance
scripture: Proverbs 11:9 KJV
verse: Through knowledge shall the just be delivered
body:
  - There are two aspects of deliverance. They are deliverance from and deliverance into. This means deliverance from the negatives (satanic influence) and deliverance into your inheritance in Christ. Through knowledge shall the just be delivered (Proverbs 11:9 KJV). Deliverance is complete when both aspects are in place.
  - 'In Matthew 12:43-45, when the devil and his influence are cast out, the evil spirit leaves. But if he does not find a suitable place to rest or a new captive, he will return to check for an opportunity to come back. The devil will return if the light of God is absent. God''s word is light. Through the knowledge of God''s word, you will be ushered into a realm of light that darkness cannot resist. John 1:5 buttresses this: And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.'
  - Salvation is a complete package, and it consists of several blessings both in our current lives and in eternity. These blessings cover the physical and the spiritual. My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge (Hosea 4:6 KJV). This lack of knowledge leads to moral and spiritual destruction. The devil,
  - who is the destroyer, looks for every opportunity to stop people from receiving the knowledge of God's word that will help them enjoy their inheritance.
  - God's word is light (Psalm 119:105). It brings illumination and drives out darkness. This means light delivers from darkness. It means light reveals. The knowledge of God's word will reveal your inheritance to you and deliver you into it.
  - Thank you, Lord, for your word. Your word is truth and light.
prayer: Your word illuminates every area of my life. It gives me divine direction, expels the influence of darkness, and delivers me into my inheritance in Christ. In Jesus name, amen.
