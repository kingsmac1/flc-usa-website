date: '2025-07-17'
title: Fight A Good Fight of Faith
scripture: 1 Timothy 6:12 KJV
verse: Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses
body:
  - The fight of faith means to actively use your faith in God to overcome challenges and to stand firm against difficult circumstances. It is using your faith as a weapon by unleashing it to combat obstacles and situations that seem to oppose God's will for your life. It conveys a determined standpoint of trust and dependence on God even when confronted with adversity. At this point your faith is anchored on God's word concerning your situation. You are holding on to God's word until what His word says comes to pass.
  - A biblical example is Abraham. Romans 4:20-21 KJV says He staggered not at the promise of God through unbelief, but was strong in faith, giving glory to God, and being fully persuaded that, what he had promised, he was able also to perform. In Genesis 15:6, Abraham was childless. God told him to change his name from Abram, meaning assumed father, to Abraham, which means father of many. He believed God's promise of making him a father of many despite his age and Sarah's old age. He held on to the word of God. He had so much confidence in God that He would definitely accomplish His promise. He refused to stagger because of his contrary circumstances and
  - was not perturbed even when it seemed the blessings of God were delayed. His faith in God kept growing stronger until God's word came to pass. This meant that his faith had prevailed. To fight a good fight of faith means that your faith has prevailed over the unpleasant situation.
  - Jeremiah 29:11 NLT says For I know the plans I have for you, says the Lord. They are plans for good and not for disaster, to give you a future and a hope. God's plans for you are good and not for disaster. Disasters are unpleasant circumstances and they are rebellious in nature because they tend to oppose what God has said in His word concerning you. If you are in a quagmire or any unpleasant situation, or your current experience is the opposite of what God has planned for you, do not run away. Rather, fight a good fight of faith by standing firm on God's word concerning the matter.
  - According to Ephesians 6:16 KJV, Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. There are various weapons of spiritual warfare. Above all means most importantly and overall. Your faith will shield you and quench all the fiery darts of the enemy. These fiery darts are the unpleasant and rebellious circumstances around you. Now, unleash your weapon of faith against those circumstances just like trained dogs are unleashed to attack the enemy.
  - To fight the fight of faith effectively, you need to increase your knowledge of God's word because faith comes by hearing the word of God (Romans 10:17) and faith speaks. Vocalize your faith (Romans 10:8). Declare faith filled words over that situation. Declare what God has said concerning you.
prayer: I declare, I have great faith. My faith is alive unto God. My faith prevails at all times. God's word in my heart and in my mouth produces the desired result. I will continue to grow in the knowledge of God's word and I refuse to stagger at the promises of God through doubt. Rather, I am always thankful and giving glory to God. I thank you, Father, for backing up my words with the irresistible power of your Spirit in Jesus' name, Amen.
