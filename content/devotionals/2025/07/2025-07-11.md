title: Practice Being Filled with the Spirit Always
scripture: Ephesians 5:18 KJV
verse: And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit
body:
  - Practicing being filled with the Spirit simply means to consistently live in a state where you are full of the Holy Spirit. It means actively engaging in practices that nurture a healthy relationship with the Holy Spirit and always seeking to be guided by His will at every point. It is choosing to be full of the Spirit and not full of wine.
  - As clearly contained in Ephesians 5:18 KJV, And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit. The phrase but be filled with the Spirit contrasts the negative influence of alcohol with the positive influence of the Holy Spirit. Being filled with the Spirit refers to living a life controlled and regulated by the Holy Spirit. It involves consistent submission to His guidance and leadings. When you are filled with the Spirit, it will result in you bearing the fruit of the Spirit and walking in Christ-like character. Being full of the Spirit is foundational for living an effective Christian life.
  - Galatians 5:16 KJV says, This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. To walk in the Spirit means to live a continuous lifestyle that is controlled by the Holy
  - Spirit, just as alcohol controls those who are drunk. This walk is a consistent reliance on and submission to the Holy Spirit for guidance. It is equivalent to practicing being filled with the Spirit.
  - There are several ways to practice being filled with the Spirit, as revealed in scripture. Acts 2:4 KJV says, And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance. When you are filled with the Spirit, you will speak in tongues. Speaking in tongues is also a way to remain full of the Spirit. It is a principle to keep your spirit charged. Praying with the Spirit refers to praying in tongues, a spiritual gift that is beneficial for personal edification (1 Corinthians 14:4).
  - 'Other ways of being filled with the Spirit are revealed in Ephesians 5:19-20 KJV: Speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord; giving thanks always for all things unto God and the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. Psalms are sacred songs, especially from the book of Psalms. Hymns are songs of praise directed to God, often reflecting biblical truths and the work of Christ. Spiritual songs are led by the Holy Spirit, potentially expressing joy or other emotions, and may be more spontaneous and contemporary.'
  - 'Praying with the spirit means praying in tongues, while praying with understanding means praying with your mind. Likewise, singing with the spirit refers to spirit-led worship, and singing with understanding involves worshipping with intellectual comprehension. 1 Corinthians 14:15 KJV corroborates this: What is it then? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also: I will sing with the spirit, and I will sing with'
  - the understanding also.
  - Engage in these spiritual activities always, as they will help you to stay full of the Spirit.
