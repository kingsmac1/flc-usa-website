title: The Life of Diligence
scripture: Proverbs 22:29 KJV
verse: Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men”.
body:
  - Diligence is an admirable virtue that means paying close attention and showing competence in your duties. To be diligent involves being intentional. You have to be deliberate about it. In your service to God, are you diligently executing all He has planned for you?
  - 'As a believer, being diligent is a quality that is expected of you in everything you do, including your workplace. Joseph worked diligently for his master even as a slave in Egypt. Jacob served Laban diligently, caring for his flock, and his master testified that indeed God had blessed him because of Jacob. There are three areas of life where you are expected to exhibit diligence: your thoughts, words, and actions.'
  - Joshua 1:8-9 KJV says This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth, but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein, for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success. Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage, be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed, for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever
  - thou goest. The instruction here is that God's word should not depart from your heart and that you should meditate on it. For this to happen, you have to be intentional. You must ensure that the word of God does not cease from your mouth. Even when you are tempted to say things that are not in line with scripture, you must pause and ensure your words comply.
  - The next area is diligence in your thoughts. Meditation falls under this category. Proverbs 4:23 NKJV says Keep your heart with all diligence, for out of it spring the issues of life. The heart refers to the center of your being, including the mind, will, and emotions. The phrase with all diligence emphasizes the importance and consistency needed to guard your thoughts. This is a continuous effort, like a watchman guarding a city. Many thoughts will flood your heart daily, but you must deliberately filter them. Those who are lazy about regulating their thoughts will see a different outcome in life.
  - You must also be diligent in your actions. Proverbs 4:27 KJV says Turn not to the right hand nor to the left, remove thy foot from evil. In our present day, there are many enticing actions and opportunities seeking our attention, but God is saying to you, in the midst of all these, ensure you diligently stay away from evil ways.
  - There is a reward for anyone who shows diligence in their thoughts, words, and actions. Such people will be blessed, honored, and will not remain average in life. Proverbs 22:29 KJV says Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your word that has brought illumination to me today. I affirm that I will be diligent in all my ways. I avoid mediocrity and mundanity. I will be devoted and excellent in all I do. I will not be average in life. My services will always be of value and needed by kings because I will be diligent in all I do. Lord, I affirm that I will be diligent in my commitment and walk with you, in Jesus' name, Amen.
