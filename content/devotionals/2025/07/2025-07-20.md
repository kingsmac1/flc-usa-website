title: Walking in Godly Wisdom
scripture: James 3:13 AMP
verse: Who among you is wise and intelligent? Let him by his good conduct show his good deeds with the gentleness and humility of true wisdom.
body:
  - There is a clear difference between worldly wisdom and godly wisdom. Worldly wisdom is earthly, mundane and ends in vanity, while godly wisdom originates from God through His word and the Holy Spirit. Worldly wisdom is often driven by selfish ambition and envy. It lacks godly standards and leads to practices that do not please God. Godly wisdom, however, is known for humility and a genuine desire to serve others. It produces good deeds and peace, and it originates from the fear of the Lord.
  - 'Proverbs 9:10 KJV states, “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding.” Any form of wisdom that does not acknowledge or reverence the Lord is worldly wisdom. Godly wisdom is not merely intellectual. It aligns with God''s will and is rooted in a reverent fear of Him.'
  - James 3:13 reveals the physical evidence of wisdom. It manifests in one's conduct and behavior. These actions are done in humility. As a believer, are your deeds and actions done
  - in humility? If your answer is no, or not always, then you need to grow in wisdom. Thankfully, it is easy to do so with God's help.
  - Wisdom is not only having knowledge, but the ability to apply that knowledge in a godly manner. James 1:5 AMP says, “If any of you lacks wisdom (to guide him through a decision or circumstance), he is to ask of our generous and benevolent God, who gives to everyone generously and without rebuke or blame, and it will be given to him.” Pray to the Lord for wisdom, and He will grant it to you without criticizing you.
  - 2 Timothy 3:15 NIV says, “And how from infancy you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.” The Scriptures can make you wise. Create a consistent study plan for yourself and be committed to it. As you grow in the knowledge of God's word, you will see a noticeable difference in how you operate and the choices you make.
prayer: Thank you Father, for you give wisdom generously to all who ask. I ask today that you grant me divine wisdom to help me act in ways that please you and to always deal wisely in all situations. Thank you, Lord, that as I study and hear your word, I am becoming wiser than I used to be, in Jesus' name, Amen.
