title: Divine Timing
scripture: Ecclesiastes 3:1 NIV
verse: There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens
body:
  - God has fixed and appointed times for everything. Every event has its own appointed time, as planned and ordained by God. God has His calendar. He purposefully designed everything, including events and people, to occur and manifest as He has scheduled them. Therefore, nothing happens by chance.
  - The sovereignty and omniscience of God make Him operate beyond human understanding. His timing is flawless and purposeful. It usually requires our faith in Him and our patience. That is why we often say, God's time is the best. God is in control of all events, and His plan is definitely for your good and for the glory of His name. As Romans 8:28 KJV says, And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
  - The Lord wants you to be convinced and assured that all things will work out well. This confidence will strengthen your faith and help you wait patiently on Him, even when you are faced with trials. Waiting on the Lord helps your spiritual growth and
  - maturity. Be assured that the testing of your faith (through experience) produces endurance, leading to spiritual maturity and inner peace. Let endurance have its perfect result and do a thorough work, so that you may be perfect and completely developed in your faith, lacking in nothing (James 1:3-4 AMP).
  - Wait on the Lord patiently and be courageous. He has made all things beautiful in its time. Trust the Lord's perfect timing, for His plans will unfold at the appointed time.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your perfect plans for me. You have made all things beautiful. I am convinced that all things in life are contributing and cooperating for my good. I am strengthened in my faith and I will always be patient to follow your timing. I thank you for the harmonious orchestration of all events of life for my good and your glory. In Jesus name, amen.
