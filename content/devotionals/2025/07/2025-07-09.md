---
date: '2025-07-09'
title: A Life of Ever Increasing Glory
scripture: Haggai 2:9 KJV
verse: 'The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former, saith the LORD of hosts: and in this place will I give peace, saith the LORD of hosts'
body:
  - Haggai 2:9 was a prophecy about the latter house (temple), which was going to have more spiritual significance and glory. This was fulfilled in Jesus because He is the temple who brought greater glory. This greater glory was made available by the Holy Spirit. Thanks be to God, because our body is now the temple of God, and the Holy Spirit lives in us. His presence makes our lives glorious.
  - Colossians 1:27 KJV says, To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles; which is Christ in you, the hope of glory. Christ living in a believer is the hope of glory. This glory is beyond human imagination. Christ in you refers to the presence of the Holy Spirit in you. Because He lives in you, He orders your steps and directs your path.
  - Proverbs 4:18 KJV says, But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day. The journey of your life is one of ever-increasing glory, ever
  - increasing radiance, and ever-increasing illumination. Things cannot get worse; they can only get better and better. Glory to God! Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path (Psalm 119:105 KJV).
  - God's word is light to everyone. The more of His word you have, the more glorious your life becomes. As you grow in the knowledge of God, your path shines brighter, and ever- increasing glory will be evident in your life in Jesus' name, amen.
prayer: I affirm that God's word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path. I will never walk in darkness because I have the light of life. I am on the path of the just that shines brighter and brighter unto the day of Christ's appearance. I will live to the glory of God, amen.
furtherStudies: Job 11:17 KJV, Isaiah 58:8 KJV, Isaiah 60:1-3 KJV
