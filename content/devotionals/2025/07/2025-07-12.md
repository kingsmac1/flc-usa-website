date: '2025-07-12'
title: You have the Indwelling Presence of God
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:16 KJV
verse: Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?
body:
  - 'The indwelling presence refers to the presence of the Holy Spirit residing within a believer. This indwelling presence of God is the fulfillment of God''s promise to be intimately involved and to dwell in the lives of believers, providing sanctification, empowerment and divine guidance. John 14:16-17 KJV says, And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you forever; even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.'
  - 'In Romans 8:9 KJV, Paul explained this further: But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his. Here, Paul makes it clear that the indwelling Spirit is a defining characteristic of being a believer. 1 Corinthians 3:16 KJV says, Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you? This implies that the moment you received salvation, God made your body His home. The Holy Spirit now lives in you, not temporarily but forever. As a'
  - 'believer, you do not have to wonder if the Holy Spirit dwells in you. He lives in you, and He will never leave. Acts 10:38 KJV expresses How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him. The Holy Ghost is that indwelling presence. He enabled Jesus to do good, heal the sick and perform miracles. What is even more amazing is that you have the same indwelling presence, and He enables you to do great things and be a blessing to your world.'
  - '1 John 4:4 KJV states: Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. The phrase greater is He that is in me means the power of God residing in you is greater than any force in the world, including Satan, his agents and all systems of this world. The indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit gives you the strength and assurance to overcome any adversity that may come your way. This is a message of encouragement and reassurance. You are not alone. You carry God inside of you. You are never disadvantaged. Be of good cheer and remain conscious of the superiority and power of God''s Spirit that dwells in you. He makes you an overcomer.'
prayer: Thank you, Father, for the indwelling presence of your Spirit in me. I thank you for the superiority of your presence in me over the forces and challenges of this world. I declare, I am an overcomer and a victor in this world. Glory to God.
