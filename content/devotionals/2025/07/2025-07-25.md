title: Dealing Wisely in the Affairs of Life
scripture: Job 28:28 NLT
verse: 'And this is what he says to all humanity: The fear of the Lord is true wisdom; to forsake evil is real understanding.'
body:
  - The affairs of life are vast and often unpredictable. Human understanding cannot fully grasp them because it is limited and can fail. Proverbs 3:5 reminds us of this. However, to deal wisely in all aspects of life as Jesus did, one must learn to depend completely on God.
  - God created the earth, all its components, and the systems within it. He alone holds the fine print of life. Therefore, to handle life wisely and adapt to its demands, we must use His recommended approach, as shown in Job 28:28.
  - King Solomon understood this truth. That was why he asked God for wisdom. God said to Solomon, Since this is your heart's desire and you have not asked for wealth, possessions or honour, nor for the death of your enemies, and since you have not asked for a long life but for wisdom and knowledge to govern my people over whom I have made you king, therefore wisdom and knowledge will be given you (2 Chronicles 1:11-12 NIV).
  - Solomon asked for wisdom, and God gave him both wisdom
  - and additional blessings he did not request. As contained in 1 Kings 4:34, So, men of all nations came to listen to Solomon's wisdom, sent by all the kings of the world who had heard of his wisdom.
  - 'Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore, get wisdom: and with all thy getting, get understanding (Proverbs 4:7 KJV). Wisdom is supreme. It involves discernment, seeing beyond the surface, and handling life with insight. True wisdom begins with the fear of the Lord. The scriptures remain the basic source of this fear and wisdom. As stated in Psalm 119:98 NLT, Your commands make me wiser than my enemies, for they are my constant guide.'
  - As you study God's word, you will become wiser, enjoy constant guidance, and be equipped to handle the affairs of life with divine insight.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you because your word makes me wiser. I thank you because your word gives me constant guidance in all the affairs of life. I will never walk in confusion, and I will continue to grow in the knowledge of your word.
