title: The Battle is the Lord’s
scripture: Isaiah 59:19 KJV
verse: So shall they fear the name of the LORD from the west, and his glory from the rising of the sun. When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the LORD shall lift up a standard against him
body:
  - 'Our opening Bible verse reveals God''s intervention over the attacks of the enemy against His people, those who bear the seal of Christ. The phrase lift up a standard is similar to what we find in Isaiah 5:26 NKJV: He will lift up a banner to the nations from afar, and will whistle to them from the end of the earth; surely they shall come with speed, swiftly. Just as the Lord lifted a banner and the nations assembled, He also lifts up a standard by assembling His army to oppose your enemies and frustrate their efforts.'
  - 'The Lord''s army does not always refer to physical armies. It may come as a flood, a blast of God''s breath, confusion in the enemy''s camp, or even make them turn on themselves, just like we see in 2 Chronicles 20:22, Psalm 149:6-9, and Joshua 6:16-20. These are divine strategies God uses to protect His people. I pray for you today: may the Lord open your eyes to see in the spiritual realm, to behold the divine strategy and heavenly armies fighting on your behalf.'
  - Exodus 14:14 KJV says The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace . Are you trapped in your own Red Sea like the Israelites were, when the Egyptians pursued them? Are you distressed, oppressed, or being attacked by the enemy in any way? I bring you good news today. There is hope for you. The Lord will fight your battles.
  - 'David understood this truth, which is why he prayed in Psalm 35:1 NIV: Contend, O LORD, with those who contend with me; fight against those who fight against me. Learn to pray this way when faced with enemy resistance, and the Lord will fight for you and give you victory.'
