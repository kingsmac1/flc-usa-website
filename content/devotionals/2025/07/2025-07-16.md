title: Wage War with Prophecy
scripture: 1 Timothy 1:18 KJV
verse: This charge I commit unto thee, son Timothy, according to the prophecies which went before on thee, that thou by them mightest war a good warfare.
body:
  - Prophecies are divine messages that reveal the mind of God concerning a person or a situation. 1 Corinthians 14:3 KJV says, “But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort.” Edification means to strengthen, encourage or motivate. Exhortation implies urging or charging someone forward. Comfort means to provide reassurance and peace, especially during pressure, conflict or adversity. These attributes are necessary for anyone facing spiritual battles.
  - Prophecy is not limited to what is spoken through the mouth of a prophet. It includes every divine revelation God has given you. It could be a prophetic word you received, a dream, a word of wisdom, or a scripture that was revealed to you about a particular matter.
  - 1 Timothy 1:18 ESV says, “This charge I entrust to you, Timothy, my child, in accordance with the prophecies previously made about you, that by them you may wage the good warfare.” During Timothy's ordination, prophetic words were spoken over
  - him. Paul was encouraging him to hold on to those words and fight with them. When life gets tough, when challenges come and things seem contrary, hold on to the message God gave you. Remind God of His word concerning your life. Speak it over your situation.
  - For doing this, you will be strengthened, encouraged, and motivated. 2 Peter 1:19 NIV says, “We also have the prophetic message as something completely reliable, and you will do well to pay attention to it, as to a light shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.” God's word is completely reliable. Do not doubt it. Hold on to it until you see victory. Prophecy is a weapon. Wield it, declare it, and let it shape your outcome.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your word is true and reliable. I hold on to your word at all times. I wage war with the prophecies spoken over me, and by them, I prevail. Thank you, Father, for you have spoken glorious things concerning me, and I testify that they are my reality in Jesus' name, Amen.
