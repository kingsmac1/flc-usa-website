title: Say No to Pride
scripture: Proverbs 29:23 KJV
verse: 'A man''s pride shall bring him low: but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit.'
body:
  - Pride is not just a personal flaw, it is something God detests. But he giveth more grace. Wherefore he saith, God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble (James 4:6 KJV). The proud are those who despise God's wisdom and authority. They elevate their ideologies above the knowledge of God's word. Such people choose to say, think, or do what they feel like, instead of submitting to what God's word says.
  - John 3:30 NIV says He must become greater; I must become less. In everything about you, let God's word take the lead. From the above scripture, you decrease by submitting the things you say or do to God, by allowing His word to prevail over your actions. God promotes the humble, but He demotes and turns away from the proud.
  - Humility is not self-deprecation. It means you are putting God above yourself. You are always willing to submit to God's will, according to His word and by the help of His Spirit. God blesses such people who live this way. The reward of humility (that is, having a realistic view of one's importance) and the reverent, worshipful fear of the LORD is riches, honour, and life (Proverbs 22:4 AMP). Let your humility be practical. It should not be limited to your heart but also expressed outwardly; not for showmanship or eye service, but as your way of life. Let your life be an example of a true believer, as written in 1 Timothy 4:12. Be someone who truly represents Christ. Jesus never had any trace of pride in Him. Therefore, be humble, so you can reflect the character of Christ in all that you do.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank you for your word today. It purges my heart of every deposit of selfishness and pride. May your word always prevail over all my thoughts, words, and actions. I will always reflect the character of Christ. I walk in true humility, in Jesus' name, amen.
