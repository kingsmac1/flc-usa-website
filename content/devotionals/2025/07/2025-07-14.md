date: '2025-07-14'
title: What is the Extent of your Vision?
scripture: Genesis 13:14-15 NIV
verse: The Lord said to Abram after Lot had parted from him, Look around from where you are, to the north and south, to the east and west. All the land that you see I will give to you and your offspring forever.
body:
  - The scripture from our opening verse is a call to look beyond your current circumstances. It is not just about looking with your optical eyes. Rather, it is seeing from your heart. The human sight range is limited, but through your heart you can see far more than your eyes can. There is no limit to what your mind's eye can see. When the Bible uses the word see, it could refer to any of these three things. First, to see with the optical eyes, which is a part of your body. Secondly, it could mean to have a spiritual vision by the enablement of the Holy Spirit. It also means to see with the eyes of the spirit, which takes place in the spirit realm. Thirdly, it could also mean to see with the eye of your mind. This happens in the soulish realm.
  - In Genesis 13:14-15, when God told Abraham as far as your eye can see, He was referring to seeing with the mind's eye, his imagination. Abraham had a mental picture that captured the whole world, and God gave him all he saw with his mind. He visualized the whole world. What can you see? What can you
  - imagine? What can you picture in your mind? What can you visualize? There is no limit to what you can see, and God is ready to grant you what you see, as He did for Abraham. Are you seeing fear, lack, sickness, limitations, or losses? Or do you see wholeness, healing, victory, success, and a great future? It matters what you see. That is why Proverbs 4:23 says Guard your heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. You need to protect your heart and ensure you only visualize great and positive things.
  - The extent of your vision is the boundary of your blessings. The extent to which you will experience the blessings of God is dependent on what you see. No matter the impartations you receive, if you do not see it with your heart, you cannot have it. Your mind has both creative and destructive abilities. You can begin now to create the kind of future you want, regardless of your current situation. Ecclesiastes 3:11 NKJV says Also He has put eternity in their hearts, except that no one can find out the work that God does from beginning to end. There is no limitation to the abilities God has placed in your heart.
  - Mark 9:23 NIV says If you can? said Jesus. Everything is possible for one who believes. Believing takes place in the heart. That means God has placed endless possibilities in your heart. The word everything signifies an unconfined and unlimited scope of possibility to the one who believes. As Abram saw beyond his current circumstances, look beyond your own challenges and begin to visualize and constructively create imaginations of your next phase of life, filled with greatness, joy, and fulfillment. As you do, God will bring them to fruition.
prayer: Thank you Lord for revealing to me how great you made my mind and for the limitless possibilities you have placed in my heart. I declare today, I will continue to guard my heart diligently. Out of my heart springs joy, greatness, healing, success and all forms of positive and godly thought, in Jesus' name, Amen.
