date: '2025-07-03'
title: Be Knowledgeable
scripture: John 4:24 KJV
verse: 'God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth'
body:
  - The word truth as seen in our opening scripture means sincerity and understanding as revealed in the scriptures. God does not want you to be ignorant. You need to know the scriptures in order to have a basic understanding of God. He also reveals Himself to you through the scriptures.
  - 'John 4:22 KJV says, Ye worship ye know not what. Jesus was speaking to the Samaritan woman, telling her that she did not know what or who she was worshipping. This means she had an incomplete understanding of God. Many people have missed out on God''s blessings, while others are frustrated in their Christian walk because of incomplete understanding, which is also called ignorance. As detailed in Hosea 4:6 KJV, My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge.'
  - Many people are too busy to study the scriptures for themselves or even go to church to be taught and discipled in the Lord. Such people are the ones who reject knowledge (Hosea 4:6). God wants you to be knowledgeable in His ways. He wants you to be wise, and wisdom is obtained through knowledge and
  - understanding (Proverbs 24:3-4).
  - 'You are where you are in life because of the level of knowledge and revelation you currently have about God. God will reveal Himself and manifest through you based on the revelation you receive. Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore, get wisdom: and with all thy getting, get understanding (Proverbs 4:7 KJV).'
  - Acquiring knowledge and applying that knowledge in your daily life makes you wise. Finally, I encourage you today to develop a hunger for the knowledge of God's word, for all you need in life is in the word. God bless you.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for the revelation of your word to me today. Grant me the appetite and discipline to study your word always. As I study your word, I will increase in wisdom, knowledge, and understanding, and I will also be changed to reflect your glory. In Jesus name, amen.
