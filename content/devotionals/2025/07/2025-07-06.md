date: '2025-07-06'
title: You are a Nation Builder
scripture: Jeremiah 29:11 NIV
verse: For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the LORD, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future
body:
  - God has great plans for every individual or group of people. His plan is to bring you peace and wholeness in every area of your life. It is not a plan to keep you disadvantaged. God wants every one of His creations to walk in the plans He has for humanity.
  - However, not everyone has experienced this. God wants to execute these great plans in your life and through you. God expects you to be part of nation building. I classify a nation as a group of two people. Adam and Eve formed a nation. Abraham formed a nation. You are God's agent of change, His instrument for nation building. Jeremiah 1:10 NIV says See, today, I appoint you over nations and kingdoms to uproot and tear down, to destroy and overthrow, to build and to plant. God has appointed you over your family, your neighborhood, your city, and your country.
  - 'As a nation builder, you uproot every evil and ungodly thing that exists. You also tear down and demolish every high thing that is not approved by God. This includes intercession as you wage war against satanic forces of darkness that afflict people with things that do not reflect God''s will. These powers of darkness manipulate the minds of people so they live in ungodliness and reject the way of Christ. This is clearly evident in Ephesians 6:12 KJV: For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.'
  - As a nation builder, in the process of uprooting and demolishing what is not planted by God, you are also called to build and to plant. You do this by declaring words of blessing over the nation and its people. You counter ungodly belief systems and doctrines, and renew people's ways of thinking. You help them embrace the truth of God's word by teaching it. As God's word grows in the lives of people, it will prevail; So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed (Acts 19:20 KJV).
  - You can begin the process of nation building today. Start with your family. Pray for them and begin to teach them God's word. Finally, Righteousness exalteth a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people (Proverbs 14:34 KJV). As righteousness prevails, you will experience the blessings of God. Amen.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for making me a nation builder. Through your Spirit, I will continue to intercede for my people and teach them your ways. I declare that righteousness will prevail in my nation, and may your blessings rain on us. In Jesus name, amen.
furtherStudies: 1 John 5:19 KJV, 2 Corinthians 4:4 KJV, Romans 12:2 KJV
