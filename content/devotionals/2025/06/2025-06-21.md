title: Guard Your Heart
scripture: Proverbs 4:23 NLT
verse: Guard your heart above all else, for it determines the course of your life
body:
  - Your heart is so precious. Guard it well because your entire life depends on it. Your heart is your inner being. It is very delicate, yet very important. If the heart is not protected, it becomes vulnerable to corruption.
  - Jeremiah 17:9 ASV says, The heart is deceitful above all things, and it is exceedingly corrupt; who can know it? The scripture above emphasizes the nature of the human heart. Because of the fall of man in Eden, the human heart became naturally drawn to moral corruption and sinfulness. This state of the heart is incurable except by God's intervention, and no human can fully understand its complexity.
  - God's response to the corrupt nature of the heart is to give the Holy Spirit and a new heart to those who believe in Him. Ezekiel 36:26-27 ESV says, And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you. And I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules. The Holy Spirit will help you follow God's will and instructions.
  - 'In Romans 12:2 NLT, we are directed to obey a key instruction: Do not copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. One major way to guard your heart is to change the way you think. Regulate what flows in and out of your thoughts. The word guard in our opening Bible verse is a military term. It means to carefully protect your heart and thoughts, so that no unpermitted idea gains access.'
  - 'These unpermitted thoughts are like contraband. They are thoughts that do not align with God''s perfect will. For example, thoughts of fear, suicide, failure, lust, greed, offense, or revenge do not agree with God''s Word. Therefore, reject them. When such thoughts try to impose themselves on your mind, begin to declare to yourself: I have the mind of Christ. There is no room for fear, revenge, or failure. Say this often and those thoughts will lose their grip.'
  - Remember, negative thoughts are projected by the devil. Refuse to let them enter your heart. By doing so, you are guarding your heart from corrupting influences. Then, begin to focus on God's Word, and you will begin to experience lasting change.
prayer: God's Word will not depart from my mouth. I meditate on it daily. It brings great change in my life. I am immune to ungodly thoughts. I have the mind of Christ, in Jesus' name, amen.
