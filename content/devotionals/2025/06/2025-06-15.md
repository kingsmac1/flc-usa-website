title: 'The Heart: The Seat of Wisdom'
scripture: Proverbs 2:10 NIV
verse: For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul
body:
  - t he biological definition of the heart is the muscular organ that pumps blood in the body. However, this is quite different from the scriptural definition. When the Bible says the heart, it refers to the seat or center of human will, emotions, and thoughts. The heart is the seat of wisdom.
  - From the Hebrew definition, the heart refers to the inner being, and from our opening scripture, wisdom enters the heart, resides there, and influences one's thought process, words, choices, and actions. The quality of your life depends on how much wisdom you allow to enter your heart. The condition of your heart is based on the level of wisdom that is present. God's word is the source of wisdom (2 Timothy 3:15 NLT). How much of God's word do you have in your heart?
  - Matthew 15:19-20 KJV says, For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornication, thefts, false witness, blasphemies. These are things that defile a man... King Solomon wrote in Proverbs 4:23 NLT, Guard your heart above all else, for it determines the course of your life. It is now your
  - 'responsibility to protect your heart because it will determine the progression of your life: either in wisdom or foolishness, life or death.'
  - I admonish you today to open your heart to God's word, for it will grant you divine wisdom to walk in the ways of God and deal wisely in all the affairs of life. I pray for you; may the Lord grant you a new heart that is receptive to His word. May He give you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in your knowledge of Him and so enlighten the eyes of your understanding. In Jesus' name, amen.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for the spirit of truth and wisdom that you have given me. I thank you for giving me a heart that delights in your word and your precepts. Cause me to continue to grow in wisdom and understanding. In Jesus' name, amen.
