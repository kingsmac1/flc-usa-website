title: The Peace of God
scripture: Isaiah 26:3 KJV
verse: Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee
body:
  - Peace simply means wholeness. It is not merely the absence of troubles or challenges in life, but the stillness that comes from God's presence, even in the midst of chaos. Peace is multifaceted and includes inner calmness and harmonious relationships with others and with God (Romans 12:18 KJV).
  - 2 Corinthians 5:19 NLT says, For God was in Christ, reconciling the world to himself. The redemptive work of Christ restored our relationship with God, and that is why we have peace with God through our faith in Jesus Christ (Romans 5:1).
  - John 14:27 NLT says, I am leaving you with a gift - peace of mind and heart. And the peace I give is a gift the world cannot give. So don't be troubled or afraid. Here, the peace the world gives is temporary, and it is different from the peace of God, which is referred to as perfect peace. The man or woman who will experience this perfect peace is the one whose thoughts are firmly fixed on God. Fixing one's thoughts on God requires mental and spiritual discipline, where you do not allow your mind to drift away from God's word. It is a clear sign that you
  - trust in God.
  - Philippians 4:6-7 KJV says, Be careful for nothing; but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus. This refers to the divine peace that comes from God when you pray. The above scripture does not say that things will change immediately or that your prayers will be instantly answered. Rather, it reveals that God can also grant you peace after praying. This means you can experience peace even in the midst of challenges, and people will begin to wonder how and why you are so peaceful, unshaken, and unbothered. Hallelujah.
prayer: I declare I experience the peace of God that surpasses all human understanding always. I decree peace in the midst of whatever challenges I face. I declare peace be in my body, my finances, my family, my career, in my nation, and on my mind, in Jesus' name, amen.
