title: The Illumination of God’s Word
scripture: Psalm 119:105 KJV
verse: Thy word is a lamp to my feet, and a light unto my path
body:
  - The Scriptures are God's words that were written down. They are the divine revelation of God. Just as a driver who travels at night needs light from the car's headlamp to see and navigate in darkness, every believer needs God's Word to light up their path and guide them through life. Anyone who lives without God's Word is merely existing and may not be able to fulfill their purpose on earth. God's Word is necessary for divine direction, especially in today's world filled with spiritual darkness.
  - From our opening Bible verse, the word 'feet' refers to a person's conduct or way of life. This emphasizes that God's Word gives divine guidance for every step you take. Beyond guidance, light also helps you see clearly so you do not stumble. God's Word ensures that you are protected from danger, risks, confusion, and the hidden attacks of life.
  - Ephesians 5:13 CSB says, Everything exposed by the light is made visible. In Scripture, light represents truth and God's presence. Since God's Word is light, it reveals the truth about all
  - things and brings the reality of God's presence into your life. When light comes on, darkness disappears and what was hidden becomes visible.
  - Darkness represents Satan and his works. When you introduce God's Word into your life, it pushes out all the baggage and influences of Satan.
  - In all your pursuits in life, God's Word will bring clarity and direction that helps you overcome the complexities of life. John 8:12 KJV says, He that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life. Follow Jesus today by following His Word.
prayer: I declare that I have the light of life. I will never walk in darkness. I will never walk in confusion. I will never walk in obscurity. I will never be deceived because God's Word reveals the truth to me. The hunger for the Word of God is increasing in me, and I will give myself to it always, in Jesus' name. Amen.
