title: Understanding Divine Order in Relationships
scripture: Titus 2:4-6 KJV
verse: That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children, to be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed. Young men likewise exhort to be sober minded
body:
  - Divine order in relationships is the structured arrangement established by the sovereignty of God. It reflects God's wisdom and purpose for human relationships. This helps us understand the essence of relationships, family, and moral duty.
  - Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands as you do to the Lord (Ephesians 5:22 NIV) and Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her (Ephesians 5:25 NIV). In marriage, God's divine order is that the man is the head of the home. He is expected to love his wife as Christ loved the church, and the woman is to submit herself to her husband.
  - 'In a marriage relationship, God expects parents to raise their children in godly ways, as we see in Proverbs 22:6 KJV: Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old, he will not depart from it. This helps children to develop good morals and'
  - eventually raise their own children in God's way. This is the definition of godly parenting. It also includes the responsibility of the man to provide for his household (1 Timothy 5:8) and the woman being supportive. Children should also honour their parents (Ephesians 6:2-3 NIV).
  - In Leviticus 18:6-18, God defines sexual relationships with close relatives as forbidden. No matter how much you love a family member, God's divine order does not permit marriage or intimacy with a relative. Also, Leviticus 18:22-24 reveals that God detests sexual relationships between a fellow man, a fellow woman, or with animals. These are defilements of one's self and are outside of God's plan for human relationships.
  - I pray for you, that you may always recognise God's sovereignty and purpose for creation, and live according to God's will. In Jesus' name, amen.
