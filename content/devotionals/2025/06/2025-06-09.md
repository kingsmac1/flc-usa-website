title: How to Receive God’s Word
scripture: John 6:63 KJV
verse: The words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life
body:
  - This means that God's words are invisible forces and power. If anyone receives His words, they will begin to work in that person's life and produce exactly what the word says.
  - In Mark 4:9, “And he said unto them, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear”, Jesus wants us to hear His words. There is a special kind of hearing He is referring to. It is not the kind of attention or listening you give when hearing a newscaster, a commentator, or music. Though the ears are for hearing, He wants a deeper kind of listening, one that involves your mind and your heart, in order to gain spiritual understanding from God's words.
  - To hear God's word is to receive it with faith, base your life on it, and allow it to influence your decisions, attitude, and actions. God's word is filled with His power to produce exactly what it talks about, if you allow it and receive it wholeheartedly with faith.
  - When you receive God's word wholeheartedly with faith, it
  - strengthens the faith you already have, renews your mind, helps you recognize God's voice, helps you walk in obedience, and makes you not just a hearer but also a doer (James 1:25). The next time you listen to or study the scriptures, receive them wholeheartedly with faith. The word will produce in you and for you everything it says, and it will make you fruitful unto God.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your word. Your word is spirit and life. I declare that I will always receive your word with faith and meekness of heart. Your word will always produce in me what it talks about and make me fruitful unto you. I am a doer of the word. Hallelujah!
