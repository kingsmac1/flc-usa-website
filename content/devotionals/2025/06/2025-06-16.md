title: How to Handle Depression and Sorow
scripture: Psalm 42:11 NET
verse: Why are you depressed, O my soul? Why are you upset? Hope in God! For I will again give thanks to my God for His saving intervention
body:
  - 'The King James version of Psalm 42:11 says, Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.'
  - The psalmist experienced several trials, feelings of abandonment, depression, sorrow, and being downcast. All these affect mental and emotional health. They are all classified as emotional turmoil.
  - Faith is not the denial of facts. As believers, we have faith in God, and we should not deny that many people are affected by emotional turmoil. We should not shy away from this. It is important to deal with it from the standpoint of God's word, which is the best approach. There are motivational teachings and therapists that have helped many over the years, and that is good. However, the principles of God's word are ever sure and are the most reliable approach, even for dealing with the spiritual aspects of depression and sorrow.
  - 'In Matthew 26:38 NJV, Jesus was overwhelmed with sorrow at the point of death. Job was faced with great loss (Job 3:26) and many more. Here are the ways they handled the situation:'
  - '(1) Prayer and supplication: Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your request be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your heart and your mind in Christ Jesus (Philippians 4:6-7 ESV). The victory of Jesus at the Cross was initiated at the garden of Gethsemane when He was sorrowed in prayer (Matthew 26:38-39). That prayer gave Him strength. He did not give up. Rather, He went on to do the will of the Father. Prayer, even when broken, gives strength. Sorrow and depression will heal quicker through prayer and will help you come out strong.'
  - '(2) Trust in God''s word: No matter how painful or distressed you are, just trust God''s word that all things, including your current situation, will work out for your good (Romans 8:28).'
  - '(3) Seek support from Christian community: The Christian community or the local church will be a great support to you. The church is for the edification of God''s people (Ephesians 4:12). Galatians 6:2 KJV says, Bear ye one another''s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ. You will receive a lot of resources from the Christian community to encourage you and help you navigate out victoriously.'
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you, for you are my ever-present help in time of need. I thank you for you are my firm foundation, and I will not be shaken regardless of whatever I face in life. I am and will be victorious at all times. In Jesus' name, amen.
