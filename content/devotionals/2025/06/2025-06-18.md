title: engaging Actively with Scriptures
scripture: Hebrews 4:12 CSB
verse: For the word of God is living and effective and sharper than any double-edged sword, penetrating as far as the separation of soul and spirit, joints and marrow. It is able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart
body:
  - 'The Bible is more than just information. Engaging actively with scriptures is a purposeful way of interacting with God''s word. It is a practice that goes beyond mere reading. It involves allowing the word to cause a change in your life as it molds your thoughts, words, actions, and overall perspective. The following are ways to engage actively with scriptures:'
  - '1. Daily Bible Study: Reading the scripture differs from studying it. Reading is the first level and involves familiarizing yourself with the scriptures and their specific teachings. Bible study, on the other hand, requires in-depth analysis of the text, deeper research, and the use of commentaries and other study materials (2 Timothy 2:15 KJV).'
  - '2. Meditation: Meditation, according to the scriptures, is an intentional reflection on God''s word. This gives room for the word to deeply penetrate both the mind and heart. This is where true change begins as the scriptures begin to shape'
  - your thoughts (Romans 12:2 KJV).
  - '3. Daily Application: This is the intentional practice of living out the word you study and hear. It involves ensuring that your thoughts, words, and actions are in line with the scriptures. But be doers of the word and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves (James 1:22 CSB).'
  - '4. Community Involvement: You can engage more actively with the scriptures by joining a community. This allows for mutual growth and encouragement, especially during challenging times. A local church or assembly is a good example. There, you may find accountability partners who will help you remain focused and committed to your spiritual goals. Not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another and all the more as you see the day approaching (Hebrews 10:25 NIV).'
prayer: I declare that I will continue to devote myself to your word. I will meditate on the scriptures always and observe to do all that is written in them. I will diligently study to show myself approved, rightly dividing the word of truth. Your word will grow in my life and prevail just as it did in Ephesus, in Jesus' name, amen.
