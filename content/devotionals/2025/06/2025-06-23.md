title: The Path of the Just
scripture: Proverbs 4:18 KJV
verse: But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day
body:
  - The just is the man who is born again, whom the Bible calls the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus (2 Corinthians 5:21). These are those who are in right standing with God through faith and obedience. The path of the just refers to the way of life a person chooses to follow in terms of moral, spiritual, and purposeful direction. It is different from the path of the unjust, which is void of God's blessing and assurance. For the LORD watches over the path of the godly, but the path of the wicked leads to destruction (Psalm 1:6 NLT).
  - The path of the just will always have God in it. God has a predestined path for your life, and it is a path of victory, greatness, success, and glory. Those who accept and follow God's direction will experience a glorious life not only in this world, but also in the world to come. As a believer, God expects your obedience to His will, and when you follow it, He will lead you on that path.
  - Psalm 23:3 KJV says, He restoreth my soul. He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake. The Lord leads
  - only those who are willing to follow. Proverbs 14:12 NLT says, There is a path before each person that seems right, but it ends in death. Matthew 7:13 NIV adds, Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. The path that leads to death is wide and crowded, but the path that leads to life is narrow and requires obedience.
  - Anyone who is obedient to God enjoys the benefit of the path of life, which shines brighter and brighter until the perfect day, when we shall see the Lord (1 John 3:2). Even if you are saved, you must still walk on the path that God has designed for you so that you will not be left behind on the day of His appearing.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for saving me and for setting a path for me to walk in. Continue to lead me on the path of righteousness so that I will be found worthy on the day of your appearing. Forgive me and redirect me in any way I might have deviated from your ways, in Jesus' name, amen.
