title: Be Concerned about God
scripture: Matthew 6:32 NLT
verse: These things dominate the thoughts of unbelievers, but your heavenly Father already knows all your needs
body:
  - Matthew 6:33 GNT says, “Instead, be concerned above everything else with the kingdom of God and with what he requires of you, and he will provide you with all these other things.” What are these other things? These are the things people desire to live comfortably on earth. Unbelievers and ignorant people struggle and spend most of their lives trying to acquire these other things, without knowing that God will provide them when they begin to be concerned about His Kingdom and what He requires of them.
  - God created you, and He also knew that you would need certain things at various stages of your life, so He made provision for them. God does not want you anxious and burdened because of the things you need. That is why He is saying to you, “Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest” (Matthew 11:28 NIV).
  - 'God knew that you would need a great family, home, job, material things, peace of mind, healing, miracles, and much more. And He gives you a solution, which is: be more concerned about His Kingdom and how to please Him. For when you do,'
  - He will give you rest by providing everything you need. Christ is your sufficiency, for in Him, you will have all you need.
prayer: Dear Lord, I will continue to press on to seek You and all that concerns Your Kingdom. I will not allow myself to be carried away by the cares of this world, because You care and provide for all my needs, in Jesus' name, amen.
