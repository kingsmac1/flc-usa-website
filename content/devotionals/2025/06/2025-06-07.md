title: Firm Foundation
scripture: Matthew 7:24 KJV
verse: Therefore, whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock
body:
  - 'J esus Christ is the firm foundation. This reminds me of an old hymn from the Graham family: “On Christ the solid rock I stand, all other ground is sinking sand.” Indeed, all other ground sinks because they have no foundation, but in Christ, we have a firm foundation and we remain unshakeable in Him.'
  - Jesus is the Word of God made flesh (John 1:14). Since Jesus is the firm foundation and He is also the Word of God, that means the Word of God is the firm foundation. Matthew 7:24 reveals to us that acting on the Word of God builds your life on a solid rock. When you start acting on the Word of God, your life becomes firm. But the one who hears the Word and does not take action is a person with a shaky foundation and such a person is likened to be foolish.
  - To build on a solid foundation means to be a doer of the Word. Practicing obedience to God's Word builds a firm foundation and enables you to withstand the pressures and storms of life.
prayer: I am a doer of the Word of God, not a hearer only. The Word of Christ dwells in me richly, and when the enemy rises like a flood, the Word of God in my spirit will rise against it. God's Word is always in me.
