title: Godly Self-Assessment
scripture: 2 Corinthians 13:5 GW
verse: Examine yourselves to see whether you are still in the Christian faith. Test yourselves! Don't you recognize that you are people in whom Jesus Christ lives? Could it be that you're failing the test?
body:
  - Self-assessment is the same as self-examination or self- evaluation. It means, as a Christian, to evaluate yourself to see if you are living in faith as you profess. Scrutinize your conscience to see if you are genuinely living for Christ. Test yourself - your motives, your ambitions, your way of life - to see if they are Christlike. After the self-assessment, genuinely answer yourself if you really need to make amends.
  - 'A common question I ask myself is: In my way of life, my walk with God, in my dealings with people, and in the transactions of my heart, if the trumpet sounds now, will I be raptured? I pose this question to you. The possibility of your answer being “No” means you have failed the test, and it is a sobering warning. You should genuinely repent and start displaying a life that is fully changed by the presence of Christ.'
  - The call for self-assessment goes beyond doctrinal accuracy or religious purity. It shows how deep-rooted your faith is. As the Bible reveals in the parable of the seeds that were sown on rocky ground, they could not withstand pressure, troubles, or
  - persecution and they died because they had no root (Matthew 13:18-23).
  - 'Finally, I will leave you with a key point: no matter what you discover from your self-evaluation, devote yourself to God''s word, for it will change you (2 Corinthians 3:18 KJV), and totally submit yourself to the Holy Spirit (James 4:7 KJV). These two keys will help you.'
prayer: Dear Lord, help me to remain focused on you as I run this race so that I may not be a castaway before you. Cause me to be sober minded. I will continue to devote myself to your word and submit to the leading and changing power of your Spirit, in Jesus' name, amen.
