title: He Makes you Prosper
scripture: Jeremiah 29:11 NIV
verse: For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the LORD, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future
body:
  - The word “prosperity” comes from “prosper”, which means to be happy, to have a successful journey, to be favorable, to be blessed, and to have continued success. God wants you to prosper. The Beatitudes talk about being blessed (see Matthew 5). Prosperity is being favored and flourishing.
  - It is sad that many have limited prosperity to wealth and material possessions. Those things are not necessarily proof of prosperity. Beyond money and material things, you can prosper in your health, in your academics, in your mind, in your domestic relationships, in your walk with God, and in many other areas of life.
  - What good is it if you prosper financially and materially, but do not prosper in your health, your mind, and your walk with God? Focusing only on material things as a sign of prosperity is a sign of carnality and reveals the state of your heart as one that loves money. And remember, the love of money is the root of all evil (1
  - Timothy 6:10).
  - God wants you to experience all round prosperity in your life.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for blessing me with all things that pertain to life and godliness. I flourish in every area of my life. I am abundantly supplied with all good things of life. In all my getting, I put You first and I will never be covetous.
