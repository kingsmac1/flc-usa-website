title: Too Blessed to be Cursed
scripture: Genesis 12:3 NIV
verse: I will bless those who bless you, and whoever curses you I will curse; and all people on earth will be blessed through you
body:
  - Genesis 22:18 NIV says, And through your offspring all nations on earth will be blessed, because you have obeyed me. You are blessed because you are an offspring of Abraham through your faith in Christ Jesus. You were made to enjoy and walk in the abundance of God's blessings.
  - The blessings God gave Abraham were so great that they were designed to last even for future generations. That means these blessings are inexhaustible and beyond measure. There is no curse strong enough to corrupt or neutralize the blessings of God upon your life. Because of these blessings, whatever you lay your hands to do becomes blessed. When you say God bless you, people and situations receive that blessing. This was the experience of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and it should be your daily experience as well.
  - If, by any chance, you are seeing signs of curses and are beginning to wonder whether you are truly blessed, the answer is yes, you are. But you have a role to play. Live a holy life and
  - take hold of your possession by speaking words of blessings over your life. Keep declaring, and you will begin to see a turnaround.
prayer: I am too blessed to be cursed. The blessings of God abound in my life and are visible for all to see. I terminate everything that is inconsistent with God's blessings in my life, in Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: Genesis 26:18-26 KJV, Genesis 18:18 KJV, Galatians 3:16 KJV
readingPlan: 1 Chr 4-5
