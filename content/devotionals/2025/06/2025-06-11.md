title: Nurturing the indwelling Presence
scripture: 1 Corinthians 6:19 AMP
verse: Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God?
body:
  - God dwelling in you through His Spirit is the indwelling presence. This is the promise of the Father which has been fulfilled in the life of every believer. God wants an intimate relationship with you. Earthly relationships need to be nurtured. Likewise, our relationship with the indwelling presence must be nurtured.
  - Nurturing the indwelling presence means making available the right atmosphere that will nourish the presence of God in your life. The Holy Spirit desires to flourish in your life if you permit Him. The full definition of your life and fulfilment of your purpose depends on how much you nurture the indwelling presence.
  - It is important to understand that the Holy Spirit has a distinct personality. He can be pleased or displeased. It is interesting to know that whatever you do that nurtures His presence makes Him pleased (Acts 15:28 NLT), and whatever steps you take that do not nurture His presence grieve and displease Him (Ephesians 4:30-32 KJV).
  - 'Here are a few ways to nurture the indwelling presence of God: (1) Holiness – The Holy Spirit requires you to live a holy and sanctified life. As a believer being the temple of the Holy Spirit, He needs you to live a worthy life that pleases the Lord. Live a godly life and produce fruits of righteousness (Romans 8:9).'
  - (2) Consistent Life of Prayer – Prayer is communication with God, and it leads to intimacy with Him. As communication is vital in marriage, so also is communication with God through prayer. Prayer sharpens your spiritual sensitivity, especially when praying in the Spirit. Praying in the Spirit edifies and strengthens the believer's spirit (1 Corinthians 14:2).
  - (3) Studying God's Word – God's Word transforms your mind and thought process, allowing you to approach life from God's perspective and understand His precepts. This will foster your relationship with the indwelling presence of God (Romans 12:2).
  - (4) Total Submission to the Holy Spirit – God is always speaking to us through His Spirit. He wants to lead you in the great path He has planned for you. He needs you to do His will, not yours. Self is an enemy of the Spirit, always wanting to satisfy your desires rather than submitting to God's desires, leadings, and promptings. He must increase, but I must decrease (John 3:30 KJV).
  - (5) Christian Service – Learn to serve others with your gifts. Your gifts are to glorify God. Serving with your gifts and abilities is a tangible expression of your devotion to God, and it helps mature the presence of the Holy Spirit in your life (1 Peter 4:10- 11).
prayer: I declare the word of Christ dwells in me richly. It changes my thoughts and my approach to life. The ministry of the Holy Spirit blossoms in my life. I will always submit to the Spirit and His leadership. I declare I shall serve others with my gifts to the glory of your name. In Jesus' name, amen. FURTHER
