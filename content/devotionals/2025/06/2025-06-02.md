title: Storing Treasures in Heaven
scripture: Matthew 6:20 NLT
verse: Store your treasures in heaven, where moths and rust cannot destroy, and thieves do not break in and steal
body:
  - Generally, treasures are valuable items, but in the context of this Bible verse, treasures refer to the activities you perform on earth that have valuable significance to God. He has a reward for you because of those activities. As you serve the Lord and render services to Him, do them wholeheartedly and do not expect rewards from anyone on earth, because your reward awaits you in heaven.
  - Hebrews 6:10 NKJV says “For God is not unjust to forget your work and labor of love which you have shown toward His name, in that you have ministered to the saints, and do minister.” God is not unfaithful to forget all the things you do for His sake on earth. That is why they are compiled as treasures for you in heaven.
  - There is such a thing as prayer banks. Prayer is an act of worship, and as you pray, they are stored up as prayer deposits. These prayers can produce results for you on earth. Just as you save money in the bank, you can also store up prayers in the prayer bank. So, keep praying and making deposits as
  - treasures in heaven.
  - Your giving for the furtherance of the gospel and your soul winning activities are also treasures. These are valuable to God and He will definitely reward you for them. All that you do for the Lord and the body of Christ is valuable to God. Therefore, continue to store up treasures for yourself in heaven, where no moth can destroy them.
prayer: Thank you, heavenly Father, for the privilege to render services to you. I am grateful that you are not unjust to forsake all my services to your name. I will never grow weary. Rather, I will always do more to the glory and praise of your name, in Jesus' name, amen.
