title: Healthy Spiritual Eyes
scripture: Matthew 6:22 NIV
verse: The eye is the lamp of the body. If your eyes are healthy, your whole body will be full of light
body:
  - To have a healthy spiritual vision means having the ability to see the purposes of God clearly, to see God clearly, and to see things from His point of view. God sees things differently, and it is only through the scriptures and His Spirit that you will be able to have healthy spiritual eyes.
  - 'Someone''s spiritual eyes can be obscured by self-gratification: self-interest and self-desires (Romans 13:14 NIV). The devil is also an expert in obscuring the spiritual eyes of people. 2 Corinthians 4:4 says “Satan, who is the god of this world, has blinded the minds of those who do not believe…”'
  - Proverbs 14:14 KJV says “A backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways…” A backslider does not see anything wrong in what he or she is doing because their spiritual eyes are obscured. Serving the Lord wholeheartedly is a great way to restore your sight. It will help your desires and gaze to be focused on God, and His illumination will cause you to begin to see through God's lenses.
  - I pray for you today that the eyes of your understanding be enlightened by the power of God's Spirit, and may any veil that has hindered you from seeing, doing, and becoming all that God
  - has planned for you be broken, in Jesus' name, amen.
prayer: I arise, I shine, for my light has come. The light of God illuminates my heart, causing me to see from God's standpoint. The eyes of my understanding are enlightened to realize and accomplish God's purpose for my life, in Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: Matthew 6:22-23 KJV, Ephesians 1:17-19 ESV, Acts 26:18 KJV
