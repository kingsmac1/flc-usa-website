title: Endure to the End
scripture: Matthew 10:16 KJV
verse: 'Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves'
body:
  - Matthew 10:5 tells us that Jesus sent his disciples to preach the good news of the kingdom of God. He also warned them about the persecution they would face. In Matthew 10:16, Jesus said they were being sent as sheep in the midst of wolves. Wolves are always ready to devour sheep, which means persecution was certain.
  - Times of persecution are difficult. They come with pressure, trouble, distress, or adversity. This persecution arises because you believe in and are identified with Christ Jesus and his kingdom. “If you are weak in a crisis, you are weak indeed” (Proverbs 24:10 GNT). Do not grow weary, do not go underground, and do not stop preaching. Do not stop attending church when persecution against God's people arises. Be strong in the Lord and stand firm.
  - As conveyed in James 1:2 NLT, “Dear brothers and sisters, when troubles of any kind come your way, consider it an opportunity for great joy”. And “Blessed is the one who perseveres under trial because, having stood the test, that person will receive the crown of life that the Lord has promised
  - to those who love him” (James 1:12 NIV). The person who endures persecution to the end has a greater reward than any earthly blessing. The Lord himself will reward such a person with a crown of life.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for your hedge of protection upon my life. I fear no evil and my heart will never grow weary. I will maintain my faith in you always. I will serve you and make your name known till the end of time, in Jesus' name, amen.
