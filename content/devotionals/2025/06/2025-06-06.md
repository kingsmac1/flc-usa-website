title: The Heirs of Blessings
scripture: Galatians 3:29 KJV
verse: And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise
body:
  - If you belong to Christ, then you are a seed of Abraham, not by blood but according to the promise. Anyone who is saved belongs to Christ. The question is, what promise?
  - Romans 4:13 CSB says, “For the promise to Abraham or to his descendants that he would inherit the world was not through the law, but through the righteousness that comes by faith.” Through your faith in Jesus Christ, you have been made righteous and officially a descendant of Abraham, and you are now entitled to inherit all the good things God promised him.
  - Genesis 22:2 KJV says, “And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing.” Genesis 27:28 KJV also declares, “Therefore God give thee of the dew of heaven, and the fatness of the earth, and plenty of corn and wine.”
  - These are blessings for every believer. There are so many blessings laid up for you. You are too blessed to be cursed. As long as you continue to walk in righteousness and boldly declare the blessings and inheritance you have in Christ, you
  - will have the full experience of all that belongs to you, because you are an heir of blessings.
prayer: I am an heir of blessing. I am productive and I multiply on every side. I enjoy the dews of heaven and I eat the good of the land. I own and I will always be exceedingly fruitful, in Jesus’ name, amen!
furtherStudies: Genesis 15:5 KJV, Genesis 17:16 KJV, Genesis 27:28-29 KJV
