title: Godly Parenting
scripture: Proverbs 22:6 AMP
verse: Train up a child in the way he should go, teaching him to seek God's wisdom and will for his abilities and talents, even when he is old, he will not depart from it
body:
  - Training up a child involves an intentional process of guardianship or parenting. It is an act of consistent commitment to the moral and spiritual upbringing and development of a child in the path that is appropriate for the child. Each child is unique. It is important that parents or guardians understand their unique gifts, personality, and calling.
  - Parenting consists of long term committed and consistent training which begins in the early stage of the child's life. This involves imparting foundational teachings of wisdom and righteousness. It is easier at this stage because children are open and easily influenced. This is vital because whatever impact or seed you sow will be the fruits they will bear when they grow up.
  - Parents are primary key players in a child's life, as they are responsible for inculcating discipline and God's instruction in a child. Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger by the way you treat them. Rather, bring them up with the discipline and instruction that comes from the Lord (Ephesians 6:4 NLT). As
  - parents, it is important not to provoke a child or push them too hard, which may lead to anger. Rather, they should patiently nurture the child in a loving manner, easily forgiving of new mistakes. Endeavour to correct the child in love.
  - Parents should also be of good influence and be knowledgeable. Proverbs 4:3-4 KJV says, For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother. He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words; keep my commandments, and live. This reveals a personal transfer of wisdom, instruction, and discipleship from parents to children. A child can only embrace wisdom if the parents have wisdom to offer.
  - In today's fast-paced and innovative world, parents should endeavour to embrace self-development. This will enhance their parenting skills, improve communication, and increase resilience.
prayer: Lord, continue to grant me guidance, grace, and wisdom to be a good parent and guide to children in our society. I declare that I will love children, be patient, nurturing, and influence them positively and in the ways of the Lord. In Jesus' name, amen.
