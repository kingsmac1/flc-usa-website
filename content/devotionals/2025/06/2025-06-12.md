title: You have the Seal of Christ
scripture: Deuteronomy 30:19 KJV
verse: '...that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live.'
body:
  - The seal of Christ is God's mark on a believer. It symbolizes God's ownership, authenticity, and protection on His people. Seals are used to secure documents, letters, and packages to ensure that their contents remain untouched and authentic.
  - '1 Corinthians 6:20 KJV says “For ye are bought with a price: therefore, glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God''s. As similarly contained in Revelation 5:9 NIV, “And they sang a new song, saying, You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, because you were slain, and with your blood you purchased for God persons from every tribe and language and people and nation”. In the ancient world, to be bought meant being purchased as a slave or as property, and a seal was often placed on them as a brand. As a believer, you were purchased with the precious blood of God''s only Son, Jesus. You have been branded by God. You are God''s brand, and you have His seal upon you.'
  - You have been redeemed from sin and death through the blood of Jesus Christ. Sin and death are consequences that Satan inflicts on people who belong to him. In fact, all of creation was bound to sin and death because of Adam's sin (Romans 5:17). But God, in His mercy, sent Jesus to die for humanity. The blood of Jesus was the price God paid to free you from sin and death and to claim you as His own. As a believer, you are God's own. You bear the seal of Christ.
  - 'Ephesians 1:13 KJV says “In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that Holy Spirit of promise. This seal implies that, as a believer, you have been set apart for God. You have been separated from the domain of Satan and his influences (Colossians 1:13). You are protected by God''s sovereign authority as His precious possession.'
  - The Holy Spirit God gave you when He purchased you with the blood of His Son is a guarantee of the full inheritance to come. This signifies the certainty of all God's promises and blessings, which include the future resurrection, glorification, and fulfilment of His redemptive plan (Ephesians 1:14 KJV).
prayer: Thank you, Father, for making me a valuable possession of Yours. Thank you for the Holy Spirit that serves as Your seal upon my life. I am divinely protected, cared for, loved, and guaranteed of the promises which are to come. In Jesus' name, amen.
