title: Striving for Mastery
scripture: 1 Corinthians 9:25 KJV
verse: And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we, an incorruptible
body:
  - Striving for mastery refers to diligent and lawful efforts required to excel or win during a competition. The phrase every man that striveth for mastery in Greek is agonizomenos, which is where the English word agonise comes from. It refers to deep intensity, fervent and heartfelt effort, such as in prayer.
  - 'In general, it points to great commitment, devotion, and dedication to achieve a given task. Apostle Paul is saying that anyone who wants to become a master or a winner must put in great effort. This great commitment involves being temperate in all things: fulfilling all (not just some) necessary conditions.'
  - 'Being temperate in all things means exercising self-denial and self-control. In ancient Greco-Roman culture, athletics was a popular activity, and the winner was usually honoured with a floral crown. Before the game, contestants had to adhere to strict training routines. This is what being temperate means: being disciplined. They were subject to specific diets, exercise, and mental preparedness. Apostle Paul uses athletic discipline to illustrate the discipline required in our Christian journey,'
  - emphasising the importance of self-control and perseverance.
  - 'Hebrews 12:1 KJV says “Let us run with endurance the race that is set before us”. The heavenly race is set before us, and we should run it well so we can win an incorruptible crown when we get to Heaven. Therefore, we must be disciplined in all things. Self is an enemy of this race: self-preservation, self- glorification, self-reward, and so on. Keep your focus on Jesus (Hebrews 12:2 KJV). Exercise discipline, perseverance, and dedication in your relationship with God.'
