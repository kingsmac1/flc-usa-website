date: '2025-06-10'
title: The Holy Spirit Dwells in you
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:16 KJV
verse: Do you not know that you are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?
body:
  - Ezekiel 37:14 KJV says, “I will put my spirit in you, and you shall live, and I will place you in your own land. Then you shall know that I, the LORD, have spoken it and performed it, says the LORD.” Also, Ezekiel 36:27 NIV says, “I will put my Spirit within you and cause you to walk in my statutes, and you will keep my judgments and do them.” This was God's promise to release His Spirit (the Holy Spirit) to dwell in His people. This promise was fulfilled in the new covenant, which began after the resurrection of Jesus (Acts 2:1-13).
  - The Holy Spirit dwelling in a believer is known as the indwelling presence. This means He has come to abide in you and make your body His abode. That was why Apostle Paul reminded believers that their bodies are the temple of God (1 Corinthians 3:16 NKJV). The indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit in the life of a believer reveals intimacy between man and God. It brings divine empowerment, guidance, assurance, and the fulfilment of all God wants to do in and through a believer.
  - The indwelling presence serves as a seal on every believer. This is called the seal of Christ, and it guarantees our
  - 'inheritance in Christ. This inheritance includes multitudes of blessings: physical, spiritual, and eternal. Ephesians 1:13-14 AMP says, “In Him, you also, when you heard the word of truth, the good news of your salvation, and as a result believed in Him, were stamped with the seal of the promised Holy Spirit (the one promised by Christ) as owned and protected by God. The Spirit is the guarantee (the first installment, the pledge, a foretaste) of our inheritance until the redemption of God''s own purchased possession, to the praise of His glory.”'
  - The Holy Spirit you have received came to dwell in you forever, and He will not leave or forsake you (John 14:16). He expects you to nurture the relationship so you can benefit from His ministry in your life.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for the fulfilment of your promise by pouring out your Spirit into my life. Thank you for the amazing blessings the Holy Spirit has brought into my life. As I continue to pray and study your word, I am being transformed and growing in my relationship with you. Amen!
