title: The Salvation Package
scripture: Romans 10:9 KJV
verse: That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved
body:
  - To be saved means to be delivered from eternal condemnation and to receive the gift of eternal life. All of this happens when you say the prayer of salvation (Romans 10:9). The gift of eternal life is found in Christ Jesus. When you receive Christ into your heart, you also receive eternal life. Because you are in Christ, there is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:1).
  - Salvation is a total package. Instead of chasing after other things that can distract you, Jesus advised us to seek Him and all other things will be added. “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you” (Matthew 6:33). To seek means to desire, to search, to press on. In the first place, above everything else, desire the kingdom of God and every other thing will be yours. “Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness” (Proverbs 8:18 KJV).
  - The salvation package contains all you need to live a successful Christian life on earth and to prepare for eternity. It includes
  - forgiveness and freedom from sin, justification, spiritual authority, divine health and healing, deliverance, protection, assurance of the future, grace, preservation, spiritual blessings and all other great things in Christ (2 Peter 1:3).
prayer: Thank you, Father, for your divine power has given me everything that I require for a godly life. I will continue to desire your kingdom and your righteousness. I refuse to walk in the flesh, but I will always walk in the Spirit, in Jesus' name, amen.
