title: Be Gentle
scripture: Philippians 4:5 KJV
verse: Let your moderation be known unto all men.The Lord is at hand
body:
  - The word moderation as used in our opening Bible verse means gentleness. It means having a patient and kind attitude even when faced with unfavorable situations. It means not being harsh. Gentleness is a combination of humility, being considerate, showing tenderness, understanding, and empathy toward others. This does not mean you are weak or a coward.
  - 'Gentleness is a virtue and one of the fruits of the recreated human spirit. As a believer, this is one of the fruits you should bear in your daily life. Always remember that by their fruits you will know them (Matthew 7:16). Christ, our perfect role model, was described as gentle, as seen in 2 Corinthians 10:1 KJV: Now I Paul myself beseech you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ, who in presence am base among you, but being absent am bold toward you.'
  - Proverbs 15:1 NIV says, A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. In a world where everyone is quick to defend themselves and claim their rights, gentleness is a
  - deliberate choice to respond with patience, kindness, and grace.
  - Gentleness is a work of the Holy Spirit, and as you study the Word of God and yield yourself to the Holy Spirit, you will be able to bear the fruit of gentleness at all times and in all situations.
prayer: I am a new creation in Christ Jesus. The old life and its nature are gone. I now have Christ in me, and He fills me with the Holy Spirit. Through the Spirit, I bear fruits of meekness, gentleness, patience, and kindness. I reflect the character of Christ always and at all times, in Jesus' name, amen.
