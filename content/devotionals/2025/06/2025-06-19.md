title: Growing in Discernment
scripture: 1 Kings 3:9 NKJV
verse: Therefore, give to your servant an understanding heart to judge your people, that I may discern between good and evil. For who is able to judge this great people of yours?
body:
  - One of the reasons for the fall of man in the Garden of Eden was the lack of discernment. Eve was unable to distinguish between right and wrong. She could not discern the will of God from the deception of Satan. From our opening scripture, Solomon understood the importance of discernment. That was why he prayed to God for an understanding heart to judge rightly and to be able to discern between good and evil.
  - 'Discernment is the ability to distinguish between right and wrong, good and evil, truth and falsehood. It is the ability to make sound choices and judgments in line with God''s will. Discernment is also a spiritual gift (1 Corinthians 12:10). This tells us that it goes beyond human intelligence and requires the guidance of the Holy Spirit to make the right choices. In these last days, it is important to grow in discernment so we are not swayed by false teachings and doctrines. The following are ways to grow in discernment:'
  - '1. Prayer: If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you (James 1:5 NIV). Prayer creates room for the Holy Spirit to help you discern God''s will. It helps you identify deception and make sound judgments. Trying to develop discernment without prayer leads only to futility and self-deception.'
  - '2. The Word of God: The Holy Spirit uses God''s word to reveal spiritual truths and shape your perspective. This helps you choose God''s path in the midst of many options. Sanctify them by the truth; your word is truth (John 17:17 NIV).'
  - '3. Rely on the Holy Spirit: But you have an unction from the Holy One, and you know all things (1 John 2:20 KJV). The Holy Spirit reveals truth to you and enables you to discern it in the midst of deception and confusion. In a time when the enemy is actively spreading lies, do not rely on natural sense. Stir up the anointing of the Holy Spirit and allow Him to guide you (2 Timothy 1:6).'
  - '4. Godly Counsel: Seeking godly counsel helps you gain clarity in complex and confusing situations. Many times, when you are unsure of God''s will or you need confirmation, it is wise to seek counsel from other believers, especially those who are spiritually mature and ahead of you in the faith (Proverbs 11:14).'
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for the spirit of wisdom and revelation that you have bestowed upon me. I thank you because I have the light of life. I have a sound mind and I am guided by your Spirit to walk in wisdom and grow in discernment, in Jesus' name, amen.
