date: '2025-10-06'
title: Learn to Forgive
scripture: Matthew 6:14–15 NLT
verse: If you forgive those who sin against you, your heavenly Father will forgive you. But if you refuse to forgive others, your Father will not forgive your sins.
body:
  - Forgiveness is not just an act; it is a divine expression of God's heart. As believers, we are called to reflect His character and that includes the grace to release those who have hurt us. It does not mean the pain did not happen or that it does not matter. It means we are choosing to be like our Father in heaven who forgave us while we were yet sinners. Your ability to forgive even when it hurts is a powerful measure of spiritual maturity.
  - In Matthew 18, Jesus paints a striking picture of a servant who was forgiven an unimaginable debt only to turn around and withhold mercy from another who owed him far less. His refusal to extend the same grace led to severe consequences. This parable reminds us that unforgiveness is a heavy burden that not only traps others, but also binds us. If we who have received much mercy from God are unwilling to forgive, we hinder our own relationship with Him.
  - Forgiveness is not about keeping score. When Peter asked Jesus how many times we must forgive, expecting perhaps seven to be sufficient, Jesus responded with seventy times; seven not a literal number, but a command to forgive without limit. God does not cap His forgiveness toward us and neither should we toward others. Forgiveness should flow from a heart that has been touched and shaped by mercy.
  - Many people think forgiveness is for the benefit of the other person but in truth, it sets you free. It opens your heart to healing and allows you to move forward unburdened. Bitterness clogs spiritual growth and leaves little room for the Spirit to move freely in your life. When you choose to forgive, you are walking in obedience, humility and love which are three core pillars of a Christ-like life.
  - Let forgiveness become your way of life. This does not mean being naïve or lacking wise boundaries. It means releasing resentment and entrusting justice to God. As you do, His peace will guard your heart, His joy will be restored in your soul, and His presence will remain evident in your walk.
prayer: I affirm that I will always walk in forgiveness. I release every offense and every burden I have held onto. I will not allow bitterness or pain to define my heart. Just as I have been forgiven through Christ, I extend forgiveness to others. I am free, whole and full of God's love. His grace empowers me to live above offense and to love as He loves.
