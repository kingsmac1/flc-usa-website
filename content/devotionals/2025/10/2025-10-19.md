date: '2025-10-19'
title: Prepare For Divine Visitation and deliverance
scripture: Daniel 9:21 NIV
verse: While I was still in prayer, Gabriel, the man I had seen in the earlier vision, came to me in swift flight about the time of the evening sacrifice.
body:
  - Staying consistent in your prayer watch opens the door to divine encounters. In Daniel 9:21, we see that while Daniel was still in prayer, the angel Gabriel came in swift flight. Daniel was not chasing supernatural experiences; he was faithfully fulfilling his intercessory assignment. This visitation was not a coincidence. It was the result of Daniel's disciplined life of prayer, rooted in devotion and covenant. God moves in response to sustained spiritual pursuit, not casual requests. When you remain on your watch, heaven takes notice.
  - A similar example is found in Luke. Zechariah, a priest, received an angelic visitation during his service. Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense (Luke 1:11 NIV). His consistency in the temple, even in old age and disappointment, became the place of divine interruption. God shows up where He finds faithfulness. Consistency attracts clarity, and intercession becomes the birthing ground for revelation, instruction, and visitation.
  - The purpose of divine visitation is not merely encouragement, it is empowerment and assignment. God is not only coming to visit you; He is preparing to raise you as a deliverer. As evident in Obadiah 1:21 KJV, And saviors shall come up on mount Zion to judge the mount of Esau; and the kingdom shall be the Lord's. God raises men and women from the place of intercession and commissions them as saviors, instruments through whom He brings freedom, healing, and judgment upon darkness. You are being trained for spiritual warfare, refined to become a vessel for others' deliverance.
  - 'Your altar is not just a personal devotion corner. It is your training ground, your spiritual base, and your commissioning platform. It is in this regard that Leviticus 6:13 KJV says, The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out. Like John the Baptist, you are called to be a burning and a shining light. He was a burning and a shining light (John 5:35 KJV). But note the order: you must burn before you shine. Burn with passion, purity, prayer, and perseverance. That fire brings supernatural clarity, empowerment, and manifestation in due season.'
prayer: Divine visitation is my portion. God is training me in the secret place to be a deliverer for my generation. I burn consistently at the altar of prayer, and I will shine with divine power. My altar will never lack fire. My spirit will never lack oil. I will not grow weary. God is raising me from Mount Zion for His purpose.
