date: '2025-10-09'
title: Halloween Symbolisms are ungodly
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7 KJV
verse: For God hath not given us the spirit of fear but of power and of love and of a sound mind.
body:
  - In today's culture, especially during Halloween, fear and death are, oftentimes, celebrated as entertainment. But as Christians, we are called to reflect the nature of God and fear is not part of what He has given us. Instead, He offers us power, love, and a sound, secure mind.
  - The spirit of fear does not come from God; it is a tool used by the enemy to keep people in bondage. Horror movies, gruesome costumes, and death-centered decorations may seem like fun to the world but they subtly reinforce the darkness Christ came to defeat. The Bible says clearly in 2 Timothy 1:7 that God has not given us a spirit of fear but of power, love and a sound mind. Celebrating fear contradicts the peace and authority we have been given as children of God.
  - Jesus did not come so we could flirt with darkness; He came to bring abundant life and to destroy the works of the devil. As evident in John 10:10 KJV, The thief cometh not but to steal and to kill and to destroy; I am come that they might have life and have it more abundantly. Fear steals our peace, and death
  - outside of Christ leads to despair. But through Jesus, we have hope, life and victory. As believers, our focus should always be on the life-giving message of the gospel, not the culture's obsession with fear.
  - 'Hebrews 2:14–15 explains the power of what Jesus accomplished: That through death, he might destroy him that had the power of death that is the devil and deliver them who, through fear of death, were all their lifetime subject to bondage. Christ''s sacrifice not only defeated Satan but also set us free from the fear of dying. This fear is what the Halloween culture often elevates, but we have been delivered from it. Why would we celebrate what Christ came to conquer?'
  - 'In a world that glamorizes fear, believers are called to shine a different light: the light of truth. We are not meant to blend in with the culture but to stand apart, pointing others to the freedom found in Jesus. Death and darkness have no hold on us and fear should not shape our decisions. As God''s people, let us walk in confidence, love and clarity, rejecting anything that pulls us away from the light of Christ.'
prayer: I do not walk in fear because God has given me power, love and a sound mind. I reject any celebration of fear or death, and I choose to walk in the victory and freedom that Christ provides.
