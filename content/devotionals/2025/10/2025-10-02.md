title: Your Identity in Christ
scripture: Ephesians 2:6 KJV
verse: And hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus.
body:
  - When you got saved, you were not just forgiven; you were born anew with a new identity and repositioned from where you used to be to a place called Christ. You stepped into a new identity that heaven recognizes and hell fears. Scripture tells us that God wants the eyes of your understanding opened, so you fully grasp the hope, inheritance and power available to you as His child (Ephesians 1:18-19). You are not just walking on earth; you are spiritually seated with Christ in heavenly places, a position of authority and dominion.
  - This new status means you are no longer subject to spiritual defeat. Ephesians 2:6 reveals that God raised you up with Christ and seated you with Him far above any demonic power, fear or stronghold. If Jesus is enthroned above all and you are seated with Him, then, you operate from victory not for it. You are empowered to walk in boldness, overcome temptation, and influence your world for God's kingdom. Dominion is your birthright.
  - Your strength is not self-made; it comes from the indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit. 1 John 4:4 reminds us that greater is He that is in you than he that is in the world. You do not face life alone. God lives in you, equipping you to handle challenges and resist the enemy. That power enables you to live a life marked by peace, righteousness and spiritual effectiveness.
  - Being in Christ means you no longer live under limitation. Philippians 4:13 KJV says, I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me. You have access to divine strength, supernatural wisdom and endless grace. You are not defined by your past or your struggles. Your identity is found in the one who overcame death and sin. You have overcome the world through your faith (1 John 5:4-5). This is your present reality.
  - As a child of God, your life is meant to reflect His authority and goodness. You carry the name, power and image of Christ. Heaven sees you as victorious. The more you grow in the knowledge of your identity, the more you will operate in confidence, walk in your spiritual inheritance, and fulfill your calling. You are no longer a victim of circumstance; you are a vessel of glory and a bearer of light.
prayer: I am seated with Christ in a place of authority. I live from a position of victory not defeat. God's Spirit within me empowers me every day. I am not who the world says I am; I am who God says I am. My life reflects the glory and dominion of Christ.
