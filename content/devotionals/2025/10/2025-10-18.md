date: '2025-10-18'
title: When You Watch, You See
scripture: Habakkuk 2:1 KJV
verse: I will stand upon my watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say unto me.
body:
  - 'Some believers struggle to get feedback from God. They ask how to hear the voice of God or see in the realm of the spirit. Any believer who commits to a life of dedicated prayer will find spiritual senses begin to awaken. This is the posture Habakkuk took, choosing not just to pray but to position himself to hear and perceive what God was saying. I will stand upon my watch is not casual praying, it is determined waiting with expectation. He did not just listen, he said, I will watch to see what He will say (Habakkuk 2:1 KJV). This highlights a powerful truth: God communicates through visions, not just words.'
  - As you stay faithful in prayer, God opens your eyes to the unseen. Scripture shows He reveals His thoughts through dreams, visions, and spiritual impressions. This is not limited to prophets or church leaders. Joel 2:28 NKJV says, I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions. These revelations are available to every believer who seeks God with diligence and humility.
  - God enables us to see in different ways. Some experience visions while awake, others receive dreams at night, and many notice inner pictures or impressions during prayer. These spiritual insights help you understand what God is doing, sense the enemy's strategies, or receive direction for life and others. Numbers 12:6 KJV clearly says, If there be a prophet among you, I the Lord will make myself known unto him in a vision, and will speak unto him in a dream. This shows God's willingness to reveal Himself when we draw near.
  - Your spiritual watchpost is not just a place of prayer; it is a place of revelation. Like the sons of Issachar who understood the times, you too can walk strongly in discernment and clarity (1 Chronicles 12:32 KJV). As Elisha perceived what was spoken in secret, your life can be marked by divine insight when you remain spiritually alert. The key is consistency. Stay on your tower. Keep your heart burning. From that place, your spiritual eyes will be opened.
prayer: My spirit is alert and sensitive to God's voice. I receive divine visions and revelations from the Lord. I am not spiritually blind. I see with the eyes of the Spirit. As I remain in prayer, heaven downloads strategies, warnings, and insight. God trusts me with His secrets because I seek Him faithfully. Glory to God.
furtherStudies: Habakkuk 2:1–3 KJV, Joel 2:28–29 NKJV, Acts 2:17 NIV
