title: Halloween is not for Christains
scripture: Ephesians 5:11 NKJV
verse: And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather reprove them.
body:
  - Halloween is widely viewed as a fun and harmless celebration filled with costumes, candy and spooky decorations. However, its origins trace back to the ancient festival of Samhain where people believed the boundary between the living and the dead was blurred, leading to occult rituals and communication with spirits. These practices are not merely historical or cultural artifacts; they reflect spiritual darkness that Scripture warns us to avoid. As believers, God calls us to live distinctly, rejecting anything that associates us with darkness and instead, walking as children of light.
  - The Bible is very clear on this point. Ephesians 5:11 urges us not to have fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness but rather to reprove them. The imagery associated with Halloween (witches, ghosts, and demons) is not just playful fantasy; these symbols represent real spiritual forces opposed to God's holiness and purity. In 1 John 1:5, we are reminded that God is light and that in Him, there is no darkness at all. When we engage in customs that glorify evil, fear or death, we risk stepping away from God's light and inviting confusion or spiritual harm. Scripture also warns against adopting pagan or occult practices. Jeremiah 10:2 commands us not to learn the way of the heathen, and Deuteronomy 18:10-12 explicitly forbids divination sorcery and contacting the dead, calling these detestable to the Lord. Choosing to participate in such activities, even indirectly through cultural celebrations, opens doors to deception and compromises our witness. Romans 13:12 exhorts us to cast off the works of darkness and put on the armor of light. This is a call to live intentionally for Christ and to reflect His holiness in every area of life.
  - As you consider the season, reflect on your heart and choices. Are there traditions or celebrations that might be pulling you closer to darkness instead of light? This is an opportunity to intentionally choose the path of holiness; to live boldly as a child of light. Instead of Halloween's dark customs, seek ways to shine Christ's light through acts of kindness, prayer and celebrations that honor God. Your life can be a powerful testimony in a dark world, pointing others toward the hope and purity found only in Jesus. Remember, the night is passing and the day is at hand. Put on the armor of light and walk boldly in God's truth.
prayer: I declare that I am a child of light. I reject all works of darkness and stand firm in the armor of God. I will walk boldly in the truth and holiness of Christ, shining His light in every area of my life. My light shines brightly. I cannot be dimmed.
