title: Why Attending Church MATTERS
scripture: Ephesians 4:11–12 KJV
verse: And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ.
body:
  - God did not leave His people alone without guidance or structure. One of the clearest expressions of His design for spiritual growth is found in the Church, not just as a concept, but as a real, physical community where His people gather to be taught, trained, and sent.
  - In Ephesians 4, Paul explains that God gave specific ministry offices, apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers, for the purpose of building up the Church. These roles exist to equip believers, not just entertain or inspire. Church leaders are called to prepare God's people for spiritual maturity, service, and unity.
  - The phrase, for the perfecting of the saints, speaks of a process of equipping. The original Greek word used here, katartismos, implies being fully outfitted or made ready. It is like preparing a soldier with the right armor, tools, and strategy. In the same way, God uses the Church to shape, sharpen, and strengthen
  - believers so they are ready to do what He has called them to do. That calling is what Paul describes as “the work of the ministry.” Ministry is not reserved for pastors or church staff. Every believer has a role in serving others, whether it is preaching the gospel, helping those in need, praying for the sick, discipleship, mentoring the next generation, or offering hospitality. The local church is the environment where these gifts are discovered, nurtured, and activated. Consequently, It is a training ground, not a performance stage.
  - As each member begins to serve, the body of Christ is built. The Church grows not just in number, but in depth and maturity. Everyone contributes to the health and strength of the whole. That is why attending church in person matters, it is not just about receiving, it is also about participating.
  - Though online church can be a helpful tool, it can never replace the physical in-person experience of worship, accountability, discipleship, and community. The laying on of hands, the shared prayer, and the face-to-face encouragement are all part of how God strengthens His people through fellowship.
  - Real spiritual development does not happen in isolation. It happens when believers are planted in the house of the Lord, consistently learning, serving, and growing together.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the gift of the Church. I am grateful for the leaders You have placed to guide, teach, and equip me. Help me not to stay on the sidelines, but to step into my role in the body of Christ. Teach me to serve faithfully, grow continually, and remain committed to gathering with Your people. May I be and remain committed to gathering with Your people. May I be used to build up others as I grow in You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
