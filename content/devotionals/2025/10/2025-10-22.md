title: The Power of Intercession
scripture: 1 Samuel 12:23 KJV
verse: 'Moreover as for me, God forbid that I should sin against theLORD in ceasing to pray for you: but I will teach you the good and the right way.'
body:
  - Intercession is not optional; it is a divine mandate. When Samuel declared that ceasing to pray for the people would be a sin against the Lord, he revealed the weight and responsibility that comes with standing in the gap for others. Intercession is more than asking God for help, it is spiritual intervention on behalf of someone else's destiny. It is warfare. It is love. It is obedience.
  - 'But here is a soul-searching question: Who needs your prayers right now that you have been neglecting to intercede for? Your spouse, family, friend, pastor, community, or nation?'
  - Intercession shifts our focus from self-centered prayers to heaven-centered priorities. Just like Abraham in Genesis 18:23–32, we are called to stand before God and negotiate for mercy. Like Moses in Exodus 32:11–14, we are called to cry out for wayward people, believing God can still redeem and restore.
  - 'However, when believers fail in intercession, they are likened to blind watchmen, as clearly described in Isaiah 56:10 KJV: His'
  - 'watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark; sleeping, lying down, loving to slumber. This image is confronting. It describes someone who should be alert and praying but is instead sleeping through the battle.'
  - 'Let this be a wake-up call. Intercession is not about being gifted in prayer; it is about being obedient to the call. Ask yourself: Have you allowed spiritual slumber or personal busyness to silence your intercessory voice?'
  - God is calling you today to be the one who stands in the gap, not for applause, but for impact. You may never be seen by others, but heaven sees you clearly. And when you intercede, chains break, destinies align, and the kingdom advances.
prayer: Lord, awaken my heart to carry others in prayer. Give me Your burden for the lost, the hurting, and the bound. Help me to intercede with persistence, compassion, and faith. May I never grow weary in standing in the gap. In Jesus' name, Amen.
