title: Sound the Alarm
scripture: Ezekiel 33:6 KJV
verse: But if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet, and the people be not warned; if the sword come, and take any person from among them, he is taken away in his iniquity; but his blood will I require at the watchman's hand.
body:
  - 'God does not take spiritual negligence lightly. In Ezekiel 33:6–9 KJV, He clearly defines the role and weight of responsibility placed on a watchman: But if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet, and the people be not warned... his blood will I require at the watchman''s hand.'
  - This metaphor of the watchman refers to those whom God has positioned to see ahead, intercessors, spiritual leaders, and every believer entrusted with the truth. The sword in this context is symbolic of coming judgment, calamity, or spiritual peril. It is not enough to see or know danger is coming, the call is to act, to lift your voice, to warn, to intercede, and to stand in the gap.
  - 'The Lord reiterates in verse 7: So, thou, O son of man, I have set thee a watchman unto the house of Israel; therefore, thou shalt hear the word at my mouth, and warn them from me. God''s watchmen are not to speak their own words but deliver divine instruction faithfully. This mirrors the responsibility Paul spoke of in Acts 20:26–27 KJV: Wherefore I take you to record this day, that I am pure from the blood of all men. For I have not shunned to declare unto you all the counsel of God.'
  - Paul understood the weight of his calling and the clarity of being guiltless before God because he did not hold back the truth, even when it was hard to preach. The same standard applies to all believers. The gospel is not merely good news, it is also a warning, a lifeline to those perishing in sin.
  - 'God goes further in Ezekiel 33:8 KJV: When I say unto the wicked, O wicked man, thou shalt surely die; if thou dost not speak to warn the wicked from his way... his blood will I require at thine hand. This reveals that silence in the face of sin is not neutrality, it is complicity. If we see someone headed toward destruction and say nothing, we are no different from the negligent watchman. But there is hope and relief in verse 9: Nevertheless, if thou warn the wicked of his way to turn from it... thou hast delivered thy soul.'
  - Faithful obedience, even when ignored or rejected, frees you from guilt. You are responsible for the warning, not the response. This frees you from the pressure of results, but never from the duty of obedience. Are you sounding the alarm where God has posted you? Whether it is in your family, community, workplace, or ministry, God expects us to warn, pray, and speak when danger is ahead. Silence can cost souls.
prayer: Lord, awaken in me the urgency of a true watchman. Grant me boldness to speak when it is easier to be silent. Keep my heart pure so I can see clearly, and my lips ready to declare Your truth. Help me not to fear man but to obey You fully. In Jesus' name, Amen.
