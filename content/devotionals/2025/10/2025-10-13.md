title: Growing in Grace
scripture: 2 Peter 3:18 KJV
verse: But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and forever. Amen.
body:
  - The Christian journey is one of continual growth, a process by which God shapes us to become more like Christ. From the moment we receive salvation, we are invited into a lifelong transformation that depends not on our strength but on God's abundant grace. Scripture encourages us to grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ (2 Peter 3:18 KJV), reminding us that spiritual maturity comes as we deepen our understanding of God and allow His character to be reflected in our lives.
  - God's grace is not only the foundation of our salvation but also the power that sustains our spiritual growth. As we immerse ourselves in God's Word, grace is multiplied in our lives. This is affirmed in 2 Peter 1:2–3 KJV, where it says, Grace and peace be multiplied unto you through the knowledge of God, and of Jesus our Lord. God has generously provided everything we need to live godly lives and to progress spiritually, but it is up to us to embrace and walk in that grace daily.
  - 'Often, growth is hindered by complacency or neglect of spiritual disciplines like prayer, reading the Bible, worship, and obedience. Hosea''s warning in Hosea 4:6 KJV highlights the danger: My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Hearing the Word alone is not enough; we must engage with it, apply it, and let it transform us. As we do, we transition from spiritual infancy to maturity, bearing fruit in every aspect of life.'
  - Growing in grace is not a race but a steady, faithful walk with God. We may face setbacks or seasons of waiting, but God is patient and steadfast. Each step forward, no matter how small, brings us closer to the fullness of Christ's likeness. Trust in His timing, rely on His strength, and allow His grace to work powerfully in your life day by day, knowing that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion.
  - Declare today that you are growing daily in the grace and knowledge of Jesus. Believe that God's Word is transforming you and that the Holy Spirit is producing fruit in your life. Affirm that you walk in spiritual strength, aligned with God's purpose and will. With every moment, God's grace empowers you to live a life that honors Him and reflects His glory.
prayer: I declare that I am growing in the grace and knowledge of Jesus Christ every day. God's Word builds me up and strengthens me always. I walk in spiritual wisdom, guided by the Holy Spirit. I receive God's abundant grace to live a godly life and reflect the glory of God. By the power of the Holy Spirit, I am maturing in faith and fulfilling God's purpose for my life in Jesus' name, Amen.
