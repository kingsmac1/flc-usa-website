date: '2025-10-17'
title: Choose to be a Light in a Dark World
scripture: Matthew 5:14 KJV
verse: Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hid.
body:
  - When the world embraces darkness, we are called to shine even brighter. As followers of Jesus, we are not meant to blend in, we are meant to stand out. In every season, including times like Halloween, we have the opportunity to reflect the light of Christ.
  - Darkness is not just physical, it is spiritual. In today's culture, fear, death, and moral compromise are often celebrated and normalized. But God has not called us to participate in darkness, He has called us to disrupt it. Matthew 5:14 KJV says, Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hid. That means our presence in the world should be unmistakable. When others choose fear, we choose faith. When others glorify death, we speak life. When the culture blends in with darkness, we stand out with the hope and truth of Christ.
  - Being a light is not always easy, but it is powerful. The Apostle Paul encouraged us in Philippians 2:15 KJV to live as blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world. The darker the culture gets, the
  - more obvious the light becomes. Whether it is how we speak, what we celebrate, or how we raise our children, our lives should point people to Jesus. Even small acts of faithfulness can shine brightly in a dark place.
  - 'It is tempting to compromise to fit in, especially when something is popular or said to be just for fun. This is why Romans 12:2 ESV says, And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your minds. This reminds us to choose differently. We are not here to mimic cultures; we are here to mirror Christ. That means rejecting what contradicts God''s Word and embracing what is pure, true, and holy. Even if the world does not understand our decision to stand apart, God honors those who choose Him over popularity.'
  - Let this season be more than a rejection of darkness; let it be a declaration of light. Teach your children what it means to walk in holiness. Use the time to show the love of Christ to neighbors. Choose joy over fear, truth over tradition, and conviction over compromise. We were never called to hide our light; we were called to shine it.
prayer: I arise, I shine for my light has come and the glory of the Lord is risen upon me. I am a city set upon the hill that cannot be hidden. I am a light in the darkness. I stand for truth and reflect Jesus everywhere I go. I will not blend in with the world. I will shine for God's glory.
furtherStudies: Philippians 2:15 KJV, Romans 12:2 ESV, Isaiah 60:1-4 KJV
