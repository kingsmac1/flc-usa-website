date: '2025-10-15'
title: Know God For Yourself
scripture: Daniel 11:32 KJV
verse: 'And such as do wickedly against the covenant shall he corrupt by flatteries: but the people that do know their God shall be strong, and do exploits.'
body:
  - One of the greatest weapons in the hands of the enemy is spiritual ignorance. According to Hosea 4:6 KJV, My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge... This verse speaks directly to believers (those called by God) who are still being defeated not because of a lack of power, but because of a lack of understanding. To walk in dominion, we must pursue revelation. Dominion was given to man in Genesis, but it is sustained through knowing God and aligning with His will. Knowledge of God's Word equips you to resist deception, grow in authority, and live out your kingdom assignment.
  - 'Psalm 82:5-7 KJV paints a striking picture: They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness... I have said, Ye are gods... But ye shall die like men... God calls His people “gods” because they are made in His image and given authority, yet their ignorance causes them to live like ordinary men: defeated and directionless. This scripture is a warning that when you do not know your identity in God, you become vulnerable to darkness and disorder. Knowledge is not optional for dominion; it is foundational.'
  - The more you know God, the more strength and spiritual authority you walk in. This is why Daniel 11:32 KJV declares, ...the people that do know their God shall be strong, and do exploits. Knowing God personally builds spiritual stamina and positions you to do great and mighty works. You begin to move from merely surviving to thriving, functioning in boldness, faith, and impact. This knowledge is not just head knowledge but revealed truth gained through fervent prayer, obedience, and fellowship with the Holy Spirit.
  - 'Jesus defined eternal life not as a distant future in heaven, but as the reality of intimately knowing God. John 17:3 KJV says, And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent. To know God for yourself is to experience true life. It is not enough to rely on others'' revelations or teachings; you must cultivate your own walk with God. This is where dominion begins: in the secret place of knowledge and intimacy with the Lord.'
prayer: I grow daily in the knowledge of God and His Word. I am strong, full of faith, and empowered to do exploits. Ignorance has no place in me; I walk in divine revelation. I am not ordinary; I am crowned with glory and authority. My relationship with God is personal, intimate, and life-giving.
