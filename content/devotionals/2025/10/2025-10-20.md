title: You Are a Burning and a Shining Light
scripture: John 5:35 KJV
verse: 'He was a burning and a shining light: and ye were willing for a season to rejoice in his light.'
body:
  - Before a believer can shine, they must first burn. Jesus called John the Baptist a burning and a shining light. Burning symbolizes fervent devotion, prayer, and consecration. Shining represents visible impact and influence. The parable of the ten virgins shows the same order. Only those with oil in their lamps could keep their fire burning and meet the Bridegroom. Without burning oil in prayer and consecration, there can be no sustained shining.
  - Tend the fire of devotion daily. The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out. (Leviticus 6:12–13 KJV). This picture of the believer's altar shows that your heart is the altar and your life is the priest. The fire represents your prayer life, your worship, and your hunger for God. When prayer dies, the fire goes out. In Romans 12:11 AMP, Paul urges us to Be aglow and burning with the Spirit, serving the Lord. God is looking for believers who will burn with sustained passion, not temporary excitement. Fuel your fire daily through the Word and communion with God.
  - Intercession shows that your altar is burning. It is more than praying for your needs. It is standing in the gap for others, for
  - cities, and for nations. Abraham interceded for Sodom and Moses pleaded for Israel's mercy. This kind of prayer is incense before the throne. As Isaiah 62:6–7 NLT declares, Let them give the Lord no rest until He completes His work. True intercessors burn in secret and shake things in the spirit.
  - When your altar is ablaze, your life becomes a beacon. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hid (Matthew 5:14 KJV). As you burn in prayer, your life will shine with divine authority, clarity, and purpose. Others will see and glorify God in you. Like John the Baptist, people will rejoice in your light because it points them to Jesus. Keep burning. Keep faithful. Tend the altar, and let your life be a burning and a shining light.
prayer: I am a burning and a shining light for Jesus Christ. I burn with unquenchable fire on the altar of prayer, and my life shines with divine influence. My fire will never go out. Daily, I fuel my spirit through the Word, worship, and intimacy with the Holy Spirit.
