title: how to Retain your Miracle
scripture: Luke 17:17-19 NLT
verse: Jesus asked, Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine? Has no one returned to give praise to God except this foreigner? Then he said to him, Rise and go, your faith has made you well.
body:
  - The story of Jesus healing ten lepers, and only one returning to thank Him, highlights the importance of thanksgiving. Jesus' response to the one who returned was unique. He made him well. Being made well means a state of restoration and perfection. It is one thing to receive a miracle, it is another to retain and walk in the fullness of it.
  - For example, imagine a blind man who begins to see partially during a church service. That is already a miracle. He should not stay quiet. He should rejoice and boldly come out to share his testimony. This glorifies God and can lead to the perfection of that miracle, bringing full restoration to his sight. Also, by testifying, he secures and retains his miracle.
  - In Mark 5:8-20, Jesus healed the mad man of Gadarenes and then told him to go home and tell everyone what the Lord had done for him and how merciful God had been. The man obeyed and began to tell people about Jesus. When God saves or heals you, He wants you to share your testimony so that others can believe and be saved too.
  - Finally, when God heals you or gives you a miracle, He expects you to have a change of heart. You should begin to serve Him, grow in your commitment, and not return to your old ways of life or sin. This is how to retain your miracle and walk in its fullness.
