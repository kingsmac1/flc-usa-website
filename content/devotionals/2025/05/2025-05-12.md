date: '2025-05-12'
title: Make Right Decisions
scripture: Deuteronomy 30:19 KJV
verse: '...that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live.'
body:
  - Choices are not merely about physical life and death. They also encompass spiritual and eternal effects. It is important to consider our choices before making a decision, because each one might lead to life or death.
  - Do not be compelled to make the wrong decisions, regardless of the pressure you may face. This is especially true for those in leadership or positions of authority, because the effects of their decisions can either build or destroy lives.
  - 'Matthew 27:24 KJV says, “When Pilate saw that he could prevail nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this just person: see ye to it.” Pilate knew Jesus was innocent, but the Jews threatened to accuse him of rebelling against Caesar (John 19:12). Even though he washed his hands, he was still guilty. That act alone was not enough. He should have stood his ground to ensure Jesus was exonerated. Do not make excuses. Be responsible for your decisions.'
  - Herod beheaded John the Baptist because of Herodias' influence, even though he knew it was wrong (Mark 6:14-29). King Ahab accepted his wife's schemes, Jezebel's manipulation, to murder Naboth and take his property (1 Kings 21). Many others in authority failed to oppose injustice and to stand for what was right. Make up your mind to be bold enough to make the right decisions, no matter what happens.
prayer: Father, I thank You for strengthening my faith through Your word. You give me wisdom, and I will always make the right decisions. I will stand for the truth and for justice. I will defend and speak for the voiceless in my society, in Jesus' name. Amen!
furtherStudies: Daniel 3:16-28 KJV, 2 Samuel 11:14-26 KJV, Esther 3:5-15 KJV
