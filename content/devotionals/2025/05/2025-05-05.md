title: God’s Word is Forever Sure
scripture: Matthew 24:35 KJV
verse: Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.
body:
  - Our opening verse emphasizes the temporary nature of all creation, but God's word remains unchanging and eternal. God values His word even above His name. Psalm 138:2 KJV says, “For thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name.”
  - 2 Corinthians 1:20 KJV says, “For all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us.” This shows God's faithfulness and unchanging nature. You can hold on to His word. He is faithful to the end and able to do all He has promised you. No matter the pressure you face, do not lose faith in His word. Even if everything else fails, His word is forever sure.
  - Romans 4:20 KJV tells us, “He staggered not at the promise of God through unbelief; but was strong in faith, giving glory to God.” Abraham's confidence was in God's word, and it came from God's word. You too can put your confidence in God's word. This confidence is called faith. As your faith in God's word
  - remains firm, His word will prevail over whatever situation you may face. God's word is truly reliable. Trust it and make it the foundation of your life.
  - Lord, I live by your word and I will remain steadfast in faith. As I hear and study your word, my faith is strengthened. I will not be shaken by contrary situations, and I thank you because your word prevails in my life, in Jesus' name. Amen.
prayer: Lord, I live by your word and I will remain steadfast in faith. As I hear and study your word, my faith is strengthened. I will not be shaken by contrary situations, and I thank you because your word prevails in my life, in Jesus' name. Amen.
