title: Test in the crucible of Adversity
scripture: Matthew 26:35 NKJV
verse: Peter said to Him, “Even if I have to die with You, I will not deny You!” And so said all the disciples”.
body:
  - Peter's strong declaration of loyalty, where he insisted that he would never abandon Jesus, stands in sharp contrast to his eventual denial. This fulfilled Jesus' prophecy in Matthew 26:34. When Peter faced opposition, he denied Jesus. Not only Peter, but all the disciples scattered in fear, despite their earlier declarations that they would rather die than deny Him.
  - It is easy to declare that you are a Christian, but when challenges arise, will you be able to maintain your declaration and stand firm in faith? Will you stay strong in Jesus? Will your faith remain steady when tested in the crucible of adversity? Is your faith strong enough to remain firm under the trials of life and persecution?
  - Romans 8:37 KJV says, “Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.” In the face of opposition, do not stagger at God's promises concerning you. Rather, be strong in faith like Abraham. Anchor your faith in God and in His word. When you do, you will stand strong and emerge
  - victorious.
  - Adversities and challenges are tests of life and they help to develop your character. Do not be discouraged by them. James 1:2-3 says, “Consider it pure joy, my brothers and sisters, whenever you face trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith produces perseverance.”
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for my light afflictions which are temporary. They are producing for me a glory that far outweighs them. I thank you because I have the victory. I remain strong in faith and my faith prevails, in Jesus' name. Amen.
