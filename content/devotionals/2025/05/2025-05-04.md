title: Learn to Serve
scripture: Matthew 23:11 NIV
verse: The greatest among you will be your servant.
body:
  - J ohn 13:14-15 KJV says, “If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.”
  - The word example in the context of this scripture is from the Greek word hypodeigma, which means a model or pattern to be followed. Jesus shows us a model of leadership that involves serving others. He was humble and selfless.
  - In the Christian context, leadership is servant leadership. Jesus served His disciples, and this is worthy of emulation. 1 John 3:16 NKJV says, “By this we know love, because He laid down His life for us. And we also ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.” You lay your life down for others by serving them. This comes from sacrificial love and humility.
  - There are great rewards for serving others, and also for rendering services to God. 1 Peter 4:10 NIV says, “As good servant managers of God's grace in its various forms, serve one another with the gift each of you has received.” Whenever you
  - serve others and God, do so with a faithful heart. Do it willingly and in any capacity you can, as though you are working for God and not for man (Colossians 3:23).
  - Finally, do not always wait for others to serve you. Rather, take up responsibilities that will benefit others. Look out for ways you can be useful in your local assembly, family, and community.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for such a privilege we have to serve. Give me a humble heart and the right motive to always serve you and others. Help me, Lord, to always serve with joy. I thank you for the great reward that awaits me in Heaven, in Jesus' name.
