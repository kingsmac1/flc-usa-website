title: Be Persistent in Prayer
scripture: Matthew 22:14 KJV
verse: For many are called, but few are chosen.
body:
  - This is a general invitation to salvation extended to all humanity. Ephesians 2:10 NIV says, “For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do.” God had a plan for you before you were born. This plan is His purpose for your life, and it can only be accomplished in His own way and in Christ Jesus.
  - God knows that you cannot fulfil His plans for you without Christ Jesus. And now, He has given you Christ so you can be saved and do all that He planned for you to do. This is known as Christian service.
  - 'From Matthew 22:14 KJV, there is a distinction between being called and being chosen. Being called is a general call for salvation, and it is vital that you recognize this call and respond to it. Being chosen implies that God selected you for a specific ministerial role. This is also referred to as ministerial calling or the fivefold ministry, as we see in Ephesians 4:11-12 KJV: “And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the'
  - edifying of the body of Christ.”
  - The chosen are elected by God to equip the called in order to help them discover and fulfil their purpose in Christ and for Christian service. Being chosen by God is not about favoritism. Rather, God expects you to answer this call with faith, obedience, and faithfulness.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your precious gift of salvation. I thank you for calling me with a holy calling by your purpose and grace. I will always set my focus on you as you guide me in your preordained pathway, in Jesus' name, amen.
