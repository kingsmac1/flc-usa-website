date: '2025-05-09'
title: You are Acceptable to God
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17 KJV
verse: 'Therefore, if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new”.'
body:
  - It is important to know how God sees you. The way God sees you is your true identity. The scripture shows us what was in God's mind when He formed and fashioned you for His glory and purpose. Many people suffer from low self- esteem, depression, and a sense of rejection because they lack knowledge of who they are in Christ (Hosea 4:6).
  - 2 Corinthians 5:17 AMP says, “Therefore if anyone is in Christ [that is, grafted in, joined to Him by faith in Him as Savior], he is a new creature [reborn and renewed by the Holy Spirit]; the old things [the previous moral and spiritual condition] have passed away. Behold, new things have come [because spiritual awakening brings a new life].”
  - The moment you said the prayer of salvation, you became a brand new person. God sees you as new. Sometimes, the devil may try to bring back thoughts of your past in order to make you feel guilty over things that the blood of Jesus has already washed away. The devil is the accuser of God's people (Revelation 12:10). He goes about projecting thoughts of guilt,
  - fear, condemnation, and rejection.
  - Romans 8:1 AMP says, “Therefore there is now no condemnation [no guilty verdict, no punishment] for those who are in Christ Jesus [who believe in Him as personal Lord and Savior].” Condemnation and rejection are now over. You are now acceptable to God. Similarly, Romans 5:17 KJV says, “Much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ.” As a believer, you have received the gift of righteousness from God. By this gift, you are acceptable to Him.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You for Your grace and my acceptance in Christ. Thank You for choosing me and for making me a royal priesthood. I thank You because the old is passed and the new has come. I walk in the newness of life to the glory of Your name. Amen.
