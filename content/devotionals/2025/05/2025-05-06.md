title: Hospitality is an Art
scripture: Hebrews 13:2 NIV
verse: Do not forget to show hospitality to strangers, for by so doing some people have shown hospitality to angels without knowing it.
body:
  - Hospitality literally means to love strangers, and to generously care for guests or those you do not know. In Jesus' teaching in the gospel of Luke 10:25-37, He instructs us to love our neighbours. Leviticus 19:34 also emphasizes the importance of caring for foreigners and the marginalized.
  - 'Romans 13:8 KJV says, “Owe no man anything, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.” You are called to love not only family and friends, but strangers as well. This act of loving strangers is what is called hospitality. It is an art. You must be skillful about it, and it involves knowing the best way to care for people. A hungry stranger needs food. Offering such a person a ride is not the help they need. Providing a meal and refreshment is the appropriate response. That is how hospitality works; you meet the need with care and wisdom.'
  - Hospitality is seen throughout the Bible. God provided manna for the Israelites in the wilderness. Abraham cared for strangers. The Shunammite woman offered food and shelter to Elisha. Jesus fed the multitude.
  - Do you show hospitality to guests? And what kind of hospitality do you offer them? Jesus once visited the home of Mary and Martha. Mary gave Jesus the attention He needed, while Martha was more concerned about cooking. Though both made efforts to receive Jesus, what He needed at that moment was their attention.
  - Today, Jesus still longs for hospitality from you. Give Him the attention He deserves by listening to His word and worshipping Him.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your love that is shed abroad in my heart by your Spirit. It moves me to love and care for others. I will be consistent and skillful in hospitality to others, in Jesus' name. Amen.
