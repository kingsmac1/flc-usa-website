date: '2025-05-26'
title: Resist Ungodly Values
scripture: Romans 12:2 KJV
verse: 'And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God'
body:
  - The scripture above warns believers against embracing the patterns and lifestyle of this secular world and present age. This world has its system, and it comprises practices that are contrary to the will and purposes of God. The devil is the god of the system of this world and he has set up deceptions in order to cause people to derail from God's will. No wonder 2 Corinthians 4:4 KJV says, “In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.”
  - There are several societal norms and traditions that conflict with Christian teachings and they cause a lot of moral decay. Practices like polygamy, cohabitation, gay marriage, and many more are against God's will. God warns us to resist and reject societal standards that do not align with His will.
  - This world's system is very enticing but, in all things, we have to use the principles of God's Word and rely on the Holy Spirit to reject them. God's Word will help you discern rightly in the midst of the cunning craftiness of the devil and this world. It will renew
  - your thought process as we see in Ephesians 4:23-24 KJV, “And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; and that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.”
prayer: In the name of Jesus, I refuse to be conformed to the system of this world, rather I will continue to renew my mind through God's Word. As I study and hear God's Word, I am being transformed and aligned to the character of Jesus Christ, amen.
furtherStudies: 1 John 2:15-17 KJV, Colossians 3:10 KJV, 1 Peter 1:14 KJV
readingPlan: 1 Kgs 2
author: Apostle Chuks
