title: Overcoming Temptations
scripture: Luke 4:13 GNT
verse: After the devil had finished tempting Jesus in every possible way, the devil left him until another time
body:
  - 'After the baptism of Jesus, He was led into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. The tempter, being the devil, tried Jesus in three major areas, and all the temptations we face also fall into these three categories: the lust of the eyes, the pride of life, and the lust of the flesh. 1 Samuel 2:9 KJV says, “…for by strength shall no man prevail.” This simply means human wisdom and strength are not enough to confront the tempter. Human strategies are limited.'
  - Matthew 6:13 KJV says, “And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.” Jesus was showing us the importance of depending on divine guidance and strength. Trust in the Lord with all your heart and not in your own understanding (Proverbs 3:5). When Adam and Eve were tempted in the garden of Eden, they failed. But Jesus, being the second Adam, was also tempted and He overcame.
  - Adam ate freely from everything in the garden, and perhaps because fasting was not part of his life, he lacked spiritual strength and revelation. Jesus, however, fasted and prayed, and was well positioned when the devil came to tempt Him.
  - Fasting and prayer are vital resources that will quicken your spirit, discipline your flesh, and prepare you for the day of trial.
  - 1 Peter 5:8 KJV says, “Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour.” The enemy is always searching for vulnerable people to tempt and attack. Make fasting and prayer a consistent part of your life, as they will help you overcome temptation.
prayer: I declare that I will always watch and pray. I will not be vulnerable to the strategies and attacks of the enemy. I am strengthened by Your Spirit and as I resist the devil, he flees from me. Lord, continue to lead me not into temptation and deliver me from all evil, in Jesus' name. Amen.
