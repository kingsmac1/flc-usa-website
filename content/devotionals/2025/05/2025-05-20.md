title: understanding Spiritual warfare (2)
scripture: Matthew 28:18
verse: Then Jesus came to them and said, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me
body:
  - Beyond the armour of God, there are important areas of knowledge that every believer must understand in order to be victorious in spiritual warfare. These resources serve as forms of training. Without them, it is like a soldier who is well armed but untrained in certain military tactics.
  - 'These knowledge areas include:'
  - '(1) The Finished Works of Christ: This refers to what Christ has already accomplished. When you engage in spiritual warfare, you are not trying to defeat the devil all over again. You are standing on the victory that Jesus already secured over Satan. You are standing on an existing protocol, and that protocol is Jesus. Colossians 2:15 AMP says, “When He had disarmed the rulers and authorities (those supernatural forces of evil operating against us), He made a public example of them (exhibiting them as captives in His triumphal procession), having triumphed over them through the cross.”'
  - '(2) The Believer''s Authority: Luke 10:19 AMP says, “Listen'
  - carefully! I have given you authority that you now possess to tread on serpents and scorpions, and the ability to exercise authority over all the power of the enemy; and nothing will in any way harm you.” When you gave your life to Christ, you received the authority to use the name of Jesus. This authority is activated as you put your faith to work through prayer, obedience to God's word, and the leadings of the Holy Spirit.
  - '(3) Watch and Pray: Ephesians 6:18 KJV says, “Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit…” This emphasizes the importance of being guided by the Holy Spirit in your prayers because praying in the flesh will not profit you much. Romans 8:26 supports this truth. You must always be spiritually sensitive and alert.'
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for being my firm foundation. The authority is in Your name. I exercise that authority over every onslaught of the enemy. Thank You for making me more than a conqueror. Regardless of the plans of the enemy, I am victorious in You, in Jesus name. Amen.
