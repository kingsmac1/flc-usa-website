title: understanding Spiritual warfare (1)
scripture: 2 Corinthians 10:3
verse: For though we walk in the flesh as mortal men, we are not carrying on spiritual warfare according to the flesh and using the weapons of man
body:
  - Although we are in a physical body and living in a three- dimensional world, the battles we face on earth are not to be fought with physical weapons or human strategies. Attempting to fight spiritual battles with human effort leads to futility.
  - Understanding spiritual warfare begins with acknowledging the existence of spiritual battles and equipping yourself with the necessary armour to resist the forces of darkness and live a victorious life in Christ. These essential armours are referred to as the whole armour of God. You need to put on all the armour without leaving any part out. This is what gives you full protection against the weapons the enemy may use against you.
  - 'Ephesians 6:10-18 outlines these armours:'
  - '· The belt of truth: This means grounding yourself in the truth of God''s word. · The breastplate of righteousness: This represents living a holy life, as it protects your heart and soul. · The gospel of peace: This refers to the readiness of a believer to share the good news of salvation. It helps you stand firm in spiritual warfare. · The shield of faith: Your faith is used to quench the flaming arrows the enemy throws at you. It is a symbol of your trust in God. · The helmet of salvation: Just as a helmet protects the head, the helmet of salvation protects your mind from anything the enemy may throw at you. It gives you assurance of your salvation. · The sword of the Spirit: This is the word of God. With God''s word, you cut off anything projected by the enemy. KJV, "You have not chosen me, but I have chosen you and ordained you, that ye should bring forth fruit...”'
  - All these armours work together. As you engage in spiritual warfare, ensure you have every part of the armour in place, and exercise your authority in Christ. God will make you victorious always.
prayer: Iprecious Father, I thank You because our weapons of warfare are not ordinary but mighty through You. I thank You for the power of attorney in the name of Jesus. Whenever I mention the name of Jesus, the adversary and situations must conform. I thank You, Lord, for the victory I have in You. Amen.
