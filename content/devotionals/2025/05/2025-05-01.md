title: The Lord Reigns
scripture: Psalm 97:1 NKJV
verse: The Lord reigns, let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be glad.
body:
  - The Lord reigns” is a recurring phrase in the Bible, especially in the book of Psalm. It emphasizes the sovereignty of God Almighty over all creation. It reveals God's ultimate authority and control over the universe. "The Lord is king! He is robed in majesty. Indeed, the Lord is robed in majesty and armed with strength. The world stands firm and cannot be shaken" (Psalm 93:1 NLT).
  - 'God''s authority is in no way comparable to that of earthly rulers. Someone might ask, “The Lord reigns, but what has that got to do with me?” The answer is, it has a lot to do with you. Start by allowing the Lord to reign in your life. The word “Lord” means master. God is your Maker and your Master. You allow Him to reign in your life by acknowledging Him for who He is and accepting His Lordship. This is done in two stages: by believing and by declaring that Jesus is Lord.'
  - Romans 10:9 AMP says, "Because if you acknowledge and confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord (recognizing His power, authority, and majesty as God), and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved." As your
  - Lord and Maker, you are subject to Him. God made you for His glory (Revelation 4:11). Permit His Lordship to extend into every area of your life by allowing Him to be glorified in your thoughts, words, and actions.
  - You may ask, “How can I allow God to be Lord over my thoughts?” This is simple. Ensure that you do not permit thoughts that are unpleasant to God to run through your mind. Philippians 4:8 gives us the guide. Whenever ungodly thoughts show up, declare to yourself, "I have the mind of Christ."
  - You can apply this same principle to regulate your words and actions. By doing this, you are allowing Him to reign over your life.
