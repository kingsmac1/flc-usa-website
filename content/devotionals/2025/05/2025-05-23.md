title: Resistance in the Evil Day
scripture: Ephesians 6:13 KJV
verse: Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand
body:
  - In Mark 14:38, Jesus commanded His disciples to watch and pray in order not to enter into temptation. Temptation could be enticements to sin, tests, or trials. These trials are attacks against your faith, the things you believe in Christ, and against God's provisions and inheritance for you in Christ. The evil day is the day the enemy unleashes these attacks on you.
  - 'The attack itself is not the main issue, because we already know that we are in an ongoing spiritual warfare. The real question is: are you able to resist the attacks or will you be vulnerable? This is what Apostle Paul was emphasizing to us: to put on the whole armour of God in order to develop resistance when the evil day shows up (Ephesians 6:13).'
  - From Mark 14:38, the scripture emphasizes that being spiritually vigilant and prayerful are protective measures against falling into the snares and attacks of the enemy. In addition to the protective power of prayer, Ephesians 6:13-18 outlines the spiritual armours that enable a believer to develop resistance against the enemy and the evil day. Ephesians 6:16
  - KJV says, “Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.”
  - The shield Paul referenced was the Roman shield, and it was large enough to protect the entire body of a soldier. This Roman shield provided an overall resistance to the enemy's attacks during warfare. Faith in God and in His word serves as a shield that provides all-round protection and nullifies the enemy's attacks, rendering them ineffective.
  - Strengthen your faith by giving more attention to God's word. Faith comes by hearing God's word (Romans 10:17).
prayer: 'I declare: my faith is alive and active. I submit myself to God''s word, and it strengthens my faith. My faith is firm. I am vigilant and prayerful. My walls of defense are well protected. I am immune to Satan''s attacks in Jesus'' name, amen.'
