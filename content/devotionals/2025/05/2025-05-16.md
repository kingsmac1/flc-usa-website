date: '2025-05-16'
title: the Lord Needs Your Attention
scripture: Mark 1:35 KJV
verse: And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, he went out and departed into a solitary place, and there he prayed
body:
  - Do not get too busy doing the work of the Lord that you no longer have time for the Lord of the work. This popular quote reminds us of the need to balance our Christian service with our personal relationship with God. Your Christian service includes all your commitments and acts of service to God, while your relationship with Him is about making time to truly be with the Lord.
  - Many times, believers get caught up in being active in service and committed to ministry work, yet they begin to neglect the quiet time they should spend in fellowship with God. But God longs for fellowship with you. He needs your attention. Even though your labour in the Lord is not in vain (1 Corinthians 15:58), the Lord who owns the work desires much more from you than just service.
  - When you give God your attention, you become more effective in your work for Him. From Mark 1:35, we learn how Jesus Himself gave special attention to God. We can easily get so involved in ministry that we begin to neglect prayer, worship,
  - Bible study, and quiet time. If this continues, you will burn out. Your strength is renewed in the place of fellowship.
  - Giving God the attention He needs is essential if you want to be effective in Christian living and ministry. To achieve this, you must rearrange your daily schedule to include time for God. Create a specific time for Him, remove distractions, and spend quality time in fellowship. Study the Bible and meditate on His word.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for your faithfulness in calling me into fellowship with Jesus Christ. I declare that I will prioritise you above everything else. Daily, as I study your word, I will continue to grow stronger and stronger in Jesus' name. Amen.
