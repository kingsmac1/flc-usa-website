title: Firm Trust in God
scripture: Matthew 26:39 NLT
verse: ...Yet I want your will to be done, not mine...
body:
  - Our opening scripture is the ultimate expression of submission to the will of the Father. It also points out that believers should have firm trust in God's plan, regardless of how they feel. It takes prayer and obedience to have firm trust in the Lord.
  - 'When you have firm trust in God''s plan, you will not be moved when you face diverse troubles. Isaiah 43:2 KJV says, “When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee: when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned; neither shall the flame kindle upon thee.” Here, God assures you of His commitment to be with you. The Bible never said troubles will not rise against you, but God assures you of His presence and expects you to trust Him.'
  - When you go through challenging moments, you should know for sure that all the things you are faced with will work together for your good. Romans 8:28 KJV says, “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to His purpose.” God has said that all things will work for your good, and that settles it. Even if the
  - pressure increases, tell yourself, “In all these things I am facing, I am more than a conqueror, for God is with me. This condition is temporary. I am coming out of this with testimonies.”
  - 'Job 13:15 KJV says, “Though He slay me, yet will I trust in Him: but I will maintain mine own ways before Him.” Job was expressing his trust in God, even in the face of suffering and possible death. Remain strong and unshaken in your trust in God, and everything will work for your good. He will not leave you nor forsake you.'
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You for Your love and providence towards me. You are too faithful and ever merciful. Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I shall fear no evil and I shall not be perturbed, for You are with me. I will always trust You. Amen. FURTHER
