title: Declare the Lord’s Favor
scripture: Psalm 90:17 NIV
verse: May the favor of the Lord our God rest on us; establish the work of our hands for us. Yes, establish the work of our hands.
body:
  - 'The King James Version renders the above verse as: “And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us…” This scripture is a prayer for God''s favor and blessing. The favor of the Lord is closely linked to His presence and approval. From the same verse, “establish the work of our hands for us” is a request for God to grant permanence and success in one''s life.'
  - As we see, the phrase “establish the work of our hands” was repeated. This shows that without God's intervention, human effort is limited and cannot achieve lasting success. Do not rely on your intelligence, wealth, or connections. All these amount to nothing without the favor of God. Even King Solomon, the wisest and richest king with all his influence and connections, acknowledged his dependence on God. Proverbs 3:5 says, “Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.”
  - Have you tried all you could and it didn't amount to anything? Are you at a crossroad in life where it seems you don't know
  - 'what to do next? Has all you tried failed and everyone given up on you? Jesus is the only one worthy of your trust. Simply declare the Lord''s favor upon yourself: “May the favor of the LORD my God rest upon me and establish the work of my hands” in Jesus'' name.'
  - Today, I pray for you specially. May the hand of God be mighty upon your life. May His face shine upon you. May He be gracious to you. May the Lord establish and settle you, in Jesus' name, amen.
prayer: I declare the Lord favors me. I have been accepted by God. The Lord establishes me. The Lord promotes me. The Lord enlarges my coast. The glory of the Lord is upon me. The Lord makes my life beautiful at all times. My trust is always in God. Hallelujah!
