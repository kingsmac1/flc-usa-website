title: obedience is Better than Sacrifice
scripture: 1 Samuel 15:22 NLT
verse: ...Obedience is better than sacrifice, and submission is better than offering the fat of rams.
body:
  - t here are situations where people knowingly disobey God for one reason or the other, and then later return to sow seeds. One common example is what some describe as “robbing Peter to give Paul”. That is, someone steals or engages in wrongdoing just to give to the house of God. Such acts are not obedience but disobedience followed by sacrifice, and they are not accepted by God. These kinds of offerings do not show love for God.
  - Obedience is an act of submission to God's will and purpose. When you obey, you are yielding to a higher authority. To submit to God means to honour Him. Your obedience is proof that you love Him. It comes from the heart, and God desires those who worship Him with sincerity and not mere show. He seeks worshippers who obey from the heart, not those who only offer outward sacrifices like the Pharisees, whom Jesus called hypocrites.
  - Your sacrifice will only be acceptable to God if your obedience has already been tested and proven. Cain's offering was
  - rejected by God because of his disobedience. He did not bring the right kind of offering as instructed (see Genesis 4:1-7). Disobedience is simply refusing to do what God says, and Samuel compared it to idolatry in 1 Samuel 15:23. Idolatry means allowing something or someone to take the place of God in your heart. It is also linked to seeking help from evil spirits. That may sound extreme, but it does not change the truth of scripture. Satan, the father of rebellion, was the first to disobey God. Anyone who follows his path is counted among the disobedient (Ephesians 5:6-7 KJV, Colossians 3:6 KJV).
  - As a child of God, maintain your true identity by being obedient to His word. Obedience honours God and keeps you in His will.
prayer: I am a child of God and I have the nature of God in me. Flesh and self-will will not rule over me. Nothing will ever take the place of God in my life. I am an obedient child of God. Hallelujah!
