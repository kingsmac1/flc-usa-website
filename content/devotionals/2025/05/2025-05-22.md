title: How to Obtain God’s favour
scripture: Genesis 39:21 KJV
verse: But the Lord was with Joseph, and showed him mercy, and gave him favor in the sight of the keeper of the prison
body:
  - Until God favors you, man will not favor you. It is a futile effort to seek the favor of man because you might be humiliated and faced with compromising options. This is not to say that you should be prideful or arrogant. The key point here is that you should rather focus on finding favor with God, who in turn will cause men to favor you (Proverbs 21:1 AMP).
  - God's favor can be described as a clear proof that a person has God's approval. This approval brings evident beauty and blessings into your life. Favor is not hidden; it is obvious and reflects outwardly too.
  - Daniel 1:9 ESV says, “And God gave Daniel favor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.” The favor Daniel received is similar to Joseph's experience. It is important to note that this kind of favor is not just human kindness but is divinely inspired. It is not dependent on your circumstances; rather, it is usually a divine intervention.
  - Jesus and Samuel obtained favor from God, according to Luke 2:52 and 1 Samuel 2:26. There was something common with all these people; they all feared the Lord, and this made them find favor with man.
  - Psalm 147:11 AMP says, “The Lord favors those who fear and worship Him (with awe inspired reverence and obedience), those who wait for His mercy and loving kindness.” The fear of the Lord does not mean to be afraid of God. Instead, it means to have a reverential respect for Him and to honor Him. Anyone who fears the Lord will definitely obtain favor from Him.
prayer: The Spirit of the fear of the Lord is at work in me. It makes me to reverence the Lord in all my ways. I produce fruits of righteousness, and I will live my life to please You, Lord. Amen.
