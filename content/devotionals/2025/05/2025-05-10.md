title: Keep Watch and Pray
scripture: Matthew 26:41 KJV
verse: 'Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak”.'
body:
  - The phrase watch and pray emphasizes the importance of being vigilant and prayerful as a believer. This is a command to remain spiritually alert and awake. The combination of watching and praying forms a defense system against the strategies and traps of the enemy (Psalm 59:3).
  - When you watch and pray, you will receive strength and grace to help you resist temptation. This is the grace to overcome the tests and trials of life. God wants you to stand firm against the evil day (Ephesians 6:13).
  - Watching and praying will strengthen your faith so that you do not fail in the day of adversity. Myles Munroe described prayer as man giving God permission to interfere in the affairs of the earth. By watching and praying, you are giving God the access and permission to intervene in your life.
  - 'In Matthew 26:41, you will notice that Jesus called and prompted His disciples to watch and pray the first, second, and third time. Yet they still could not keep watch, and then the enemy struck. One thing is certain: if the disciples had listened'
  - to Jesus and remained vigilant and prayerful, they would have withstood and overcome the devil. They would not have scattered after Jesus was arrested, and Peter would not have denied Jesus.
  - Today, God keeps calling your attention to pray and remain alert. But when you keep ignoring the call to prayer, the enemy eventually strikes. Be vigilant and pray.
prayer: Thank You Lord for always answering me when I pray. I thank You for the divine promptings of Your Spirit. As I pray, I am being strengthened in my spirit to withstand and overcome the snares of the enemy. I will always devote myself to prayer and be watchful and thankful. In Jesus name, amen.
