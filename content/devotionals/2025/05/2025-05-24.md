title: The Power of Testimonies
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:8 KJV
verse: 'Be not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God'
body:
  - 'Romans 1:16 KJV says, “For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to everyone that believeth.” The gospel is the good news of the life, death, and resurrection of Jesus and all He has done for you. The word testimony means a solemn declaration or attestation to the truth of God''s goodness. Do not be ashamed to declare who God is and what He has done.'
  - When you testify of God's goodness, not just to you but to humanity, God's power is made available to turn people's hearts and lead them to salvation. This simply means testimonies lead people to Christ.
  - Testifying of God's goodness is an act of thanksgiving that reflects a heart of gratitude. Doing this brings wholeness and perfection to your life, just as seen in the story of the ten lepers. Only one returned to testify, and his healing was made complete (Luke 17:17-19).
  - Revelation 12:11 KJV says, “And they overcame him by the
  - blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony.” This reveals the believer's victory over Satan through the blood of Jesus and their declaration of the finished works of Christ.
  - Satan does not want you to testify of your rightful inheritance in Christ because he knows how powerful it is. In all you do, do not stop declaring the good news of God and your realities as a new creation.
prayer: 'I testify: I am a new creation in Christ Jesus. I am the seed of Abraham and a partaker of the Abrahamic blessings. I am blessed beyond measure. I walk in victory and dominion. I am favored. I am wonderfully and fearfully made. Hallelujah!'
