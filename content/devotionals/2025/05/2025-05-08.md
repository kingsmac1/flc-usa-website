title: Response to the Distress of the Soul
scripture: Psalm 6:3 CEV
verse: And I am in deep distress. How long will it be?
body:
  - 'Psalm 31:9 KJV says “Have mercy upon me, O LORD, for I am in trouble: mine eye is consumed with grief, yea, my soul and my belly”. David, in this psalm, was in great distress. This was a state of severe unrest. The psalmist acknowledged the human condition and the need for God''s intervention.'
  - From David's prayer and language (“mine eye,” “my soul,” and “my belly”), we can see that the distress he experienced was total. It affected every part of his being. The soul represents the inner self or life force, while the body represents the physical aspect. His words show that the impact of the distress was not just spiritual or emotional, but physical as well.
  - 'In Matthew 26:38 KJV, “Then saith he unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful, even unto death: tarry ye here, and watch with me,” Jesus expressed a similar emotional distress. It was a deep turmoil of the soul, just like David''s experience. Have you ever felt such unease, distress, or trouble in your soul, where you could not even figure out what it was?'
  - 'Jesus gave us the answer in Matthew 26:38-39: “tarry ye here and watch...” Whenever you experience such distress, go and pray. Be spiritually alert and vigilant. That burden could be a spiritual weight that needs immediate attention. Praying immediately could avert a danger or bring peace to the situation. Do not stop until the burden lifts.'
prayer: I am spiritually sensitive. I am fervent in prayer. Whenever I pray, things get settled in sensitive quarters in the realm of the spirit. I thank You, Lord, for Your Spirit helps us by making intercession whenever the need arises. In Jesus' name, Amen.
