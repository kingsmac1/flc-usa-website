date: '2025-05-13'
title: how to Receive God’s Word
scripture: Mark 4:20 KJV
verse: And these are they which are sown on good ground; such as hear the word, and receive it, and bring forth fruit, some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some an hundred.
body:
  - 'J ohn 6:63 KJV says, “It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.” God''s word is not merely informative or directional. It carries power and life. If anyone receives His word, it begins a work in their life and produces the result of what it speaks.'
  - The word of God is living and active (Hebrews 4:12). It is not static. It is powerful and dynamic. God's word has a catalytic effect on anyone who receives it rightly. “Such as hear the word and receive it,” as seen in our opening verse, refers to those who hear the word with their hearts and receive it with faith. Not those who analyse it with mere sense knowledge. That kind of analysis creates doubt and stands in the way of faith. Receive God's word with your whole heart like a child (Matthew 18:3), and position yourself to receive more.
  - 'As you hear God''s word, your faith grows and helps you to receive even more. Faith is a vital element that makes the word of God produce what it says. “For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not'
  - profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it” (Hebrews 4:2 KJV).
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You for the power in Your word. I thank You because Your word is alive and active, always working in me. It brings life, shapes me, and guides me. My heart is always open to receive Your word with faith, in Jesus name. Amen!
furtherStudies: Psalm 19:7-8 KJV, 1 Thessalonians 2:13 KJV, 2 Timothy 3:16-17 KJV
