title: Dwelling in the Secret Place
scripture: Psalm 91:1 KJV
verse: He that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty
body:
  - From our opening verse, it emphasizes the importance of dwelling in the secret place. It did not say he that visits the secret place but he that lives there. Continual habitation in the secret place is what matters. What is the secret place? It is not a physical location but fellowshipping and having an intimate relationship with God.
  - 'Think of it this way: in your home, there is usually a private room or chamber which is not meant for visitors. God does not need you to be a visitor; He needs you to become a resident in the secret place. That is why the scripture says, “Come boldly to the throne of grace, that you may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need” (Hebrews 4:16). This shows the need to continuously fellowship with God through prayer and the Word. God sent Jonah to the Assyrian city of Nineveh, but Jonah tried to run from his assignment.'
  - The verse says, “shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty” (Psalm 91:1). The shadow of the Almighty signifies the glory and presence of God. Anyone who dwells in the secret place will
  - experience God's glory and presence. The weight of God's glory you will manifest is dependent on your commitment to the secret place.
  - God's presence will protect you from the dangers and challenges of life (Psalm 91:3-16 NIV). James 4:8 ESV says, “Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double- minded.” As you spend more time with God, He will draw near to you by revealing Himself and His glory. This will purge you of all ungodliness, enabling the fruits of the recreated human spirit to take root and grow in your life. You will begin to see life from an eternal perspective.
prayer: Thank you, LORD, for bringing me into fellowship with your dear Son, Jesus Christ. Thank you for granting me access to your throne of grace. Help me, LORD, not to neglect my responsibilities in the secret place. I will continue to totally surrender to your Spirit and will for my life in Jesus' name, amen.
