date: '2025-01-18'
title: Jesus Loves Little Children
scripture: Matthew 19:13-14 (NLT)
verse: One day some parents brought their children to Jesus so He could lay His hands on them and pray for them. But the disciples scolded the parents for bothering Him. But Jesus said, ‘Let the children come to me. Don’t stop them! For the Kingdom of Heaven belongs to those who are like these children.
body:
  - There are a few important lessons in this scripture. First, Jesus rebuked His disciples for trying to stop parents from bringing their children to Him. This shows that Jesus loves children and wants them to come to Him, indicating His deep interest in their lives. Unfortunately, some parents today do not prioritize taking their children to church. From the scripture, we see that these parents left their homes and made an effort to bring their children to Jesus for prayer and blessings. If they had stayed home, their children would not have received the blessing from Jesus. Parents should make it a point to bring their children to church, as they will be blessed for being in God’s presence.
  - 'If you truly love your children, you will train them in the right way. Proverbs 22:6 (KJV) says, “Train up a child in the way he should go: And when he is old, he will not depart from it.” A child’s behavior reflects their upbringing, and it is the responsibility of parents to guide them. When they grow up,'
  - they will continue to follow the path you have set for them. Bringing your children to church is part of this training, as it helps them learn godly principles that will shape their lives. This not only saves parents from future challenges but also leads to children who appreciate their upbringing and grow into godly adults.
  - 1 Samuel 1:27-28 (NLT) says, “I asked the Lord to give me this boy, and He has granted my request. Now I am giving him to the Lord, and he will belong to the Lord his whole life.” Children are gifts from God. Love them as God loves them, dedicate them to God, take them to His house, and teach them how to serve Him, just like Hannah did with her son.
  - The opening scripture also teaches us that the Kingdom of Heaven belongs to those who are like children. This refers to the purity of mind and heart. Children are innocent, and God expects us to cultivate a pure heart as well. As adults, we must strive to maintain a clean and sincere heart, as there are blessings attached to it. “God blesses those whose hearts are pure, for they will see God.” – Matthew 5:8 (NLT)
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for making me a custodian of children. I love and care for them. I pray that all children will grow up in the fear of the Lord, that they will always love God, and that they will delight in His ways, in Jesus' name, Amen!
