title: God Will Always Give you a Sign
scripture: 1 John 2:20 (AMP
verse: But you have an anointing from the Holy One (you have been set apart, specially gifted and prepared by the Holy Spirit), and all of you know (the truth because He teaches us, illuminates our minds, and guards us from error).
body:
  - God never leaves us in the dark. He provides clues, often in the form of signs. These signs can be tangible or intangible, but they are usually distinct. Your ability to recognize these signs depends on your sensitivity to the Holy Spirit and how familiar you are with God’s Word. This is important because many of these signs have been experienced by people in the Bible, and they manifest in similar ways in our lives today.
  - 'In Genesis 18:2 (KJV), it says, “And he lift up his eyes and looked, and, lo, three men stood by him: and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the tent door, and bowed himself toward the ground.” Here, Abraham encountered three strangers and perceived they were not ordinary men. No one told him, but he had an inner witness and treated them well. It was later revealed that one of them was the Lord Himself. God never leaves us clueless.'
  - God is always calling our attention to the signs He gives us. Sometimes, He allows certain things to happen so we can focus on what He is trying to communicate. These signs can be tangible – something physical – or intangible, like an impression or heaviness in your heart. Have you ever felt an inner prompting and weren’t sure what it meant? If so, begin to pray in the Spirit when it happens again. As you pray, God will enlighten your mind and help you understand what He is communicating.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for the gift of the Holy Spirit, who guides, teaches, and reveals all things to me. I have received an anointing from the Holy One, and He teaches me all things. I will never walk in darkness or confusion. Thank You, Lord, for enlightening my heart, in Jesus’ name, Amen!
