title: Helpers of God
scripture: Psalms 46:1 (NLT)
verse: God is our refuge and strength, always ready to help in times of trouble.
body:
  - The Bible says in Isaiah 41:13 (ESV), “For I, the Lord your God, hold your right hand; it is I who say to you, ‘Fear not, I am the one who helps you.” God is your helper, not the other way around. God never asked you to help Him; He is always willing and ready to help you, if you will allow Him. What He expects from you is willingness and obedience.
  - 'Many believers feel that God is delaying in responding to their needs, that time is running out, or that His promises are taking too long to come to pass. This leads them to put themselves under unnecessary pressure, trying to find other ways to achieve similar results. Let’s consider the case of Abraham and Sarah: God promised to make Abraham a father of many nations, but Sarah felt she was too old to conceive. So, she suggested to Abraham that he go in with Hagar, her maidservant. She thought she was helping God to raise a descendant who would inherit Abraham’s blessings (Genesis 16:1-13). Hagar gave birth to Ishmael, but little did Sarah know that her attempt to help God would create a generation of enemies against the future heirs of Abraham – God’s people today. We can see this in the ongoing conflict between Israel and its enemies in the Middle East, a consequence of Sarah’s attempt to assist God in fulfilling His promise.'
  - Another example is King Saul’s unlawful sacrifice in 1 Samuel 13. Israel was about to face the Philistines in battle, and the law required the priest to perform a sacrifice before they could engage in combat. They waited for seven days, but the priest had not yet arrived. In his impatience, King Saul decided to perform the sacrifice himself. After Saul’s action, Samuel the priest arrived. In 1 Samuel 13:13, Samuel said to Saul, “You have done foolishly. You have not kept the commandment of the Lord your God, which He commanded you. For now the Lord would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.” The result of Saul’s impatience was the loss of his throne.
  - Many people today compromise in the name of “helping God” to get things done more quickly. Some enter wrong marriages, businesses, investments, or move into unsuitable neighborhoods because they feel pressured by family, friends, or their own perception of time running out. But it's essential to stay calm and composed in the midst of troubles. Isaiah 41:10 (ESV) says, “Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.” God is your ever-present help in times of need; He upholds and strengthens you at all times.
prayer: I take advantage of the Holy Spirit, who helps me at all times. I am strengthened and courageous in the face of every situation. I stand firm, unshaken by pressures, competition, or challenges. I soar higher and higher above all obstacles, in Jesus' name, Amen!
