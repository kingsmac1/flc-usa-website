title: New Year Goals
scripture: Habakkuk 2:2-3 (KJV)
verse: 'And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it. For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, ait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry.'
body:
  - Vision is the ability to think about or plan the future with imagination or wisdom. It involves having a mental picture of what you want to accomplish. This mental picture is called imagination. Visions can come in the form of ideas. What plans do you have for the new year? What ideas do you want to accomplish? Do you have things you want to achieve this year? Are there things the Lord has brought to your heart? Do you have dreams? If your answer is YES, then make it plain by writing them down.
  - Writing down your visions will help you stay focused. You will not be easily distracted by other things. Proverbs 4:27 (KJV) says, “Turn not to the right hand nor to the left....” Maintain your focus by writing down your visions. Another important step after writing down your vision is to review them periodically. This will keep your inspiration fresh in your mind.2 Chronicles 15:7 (NIV) says, “But as for you, be strong and do not give up, for your work will be rewarded.” An individual who has written goals remains strong in the midst of opposition. Even if there are
  - several failed attempts, such a person remains courageous and perseveres. And God rewards such persons in due season for all their efforts. Write down your goals for 2025, make them plain, and run with them.
prayer: Lord, continue to make me a visionary and a focused person. You give me divine and innovative ideas. I am a goal-getter. I will accomplish all my goals for this new year because I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me. In Jesus' name, Amen!
