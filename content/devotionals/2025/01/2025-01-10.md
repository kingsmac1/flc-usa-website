title: The Word of God Purifies your Heart
scripture: John 15:3 (KJV)
verse: Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.
body:
  - Have you been struggling with negative thoughts, feelings, or emotions? The solution is the Word of God. Many times, people find themselves in situations where certain negative thoughts flood their minds so much that they end up controlling their actions. It could be thoughts of trauma — war, abusive relationships, grief, accidents, hurt, worry, fear of death, fear of the unknown, anxiety, etc. The solution is to fill your mind with God’s Word. Study the Word diligently, and it will address the situation.
  - Give the Word of God unrestricted access to dwell in your heart at all times. David the psalmist said, “Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You” (Psalms 119:11, NKJV). He made God’s Word permanent in his heart; he treasured and stored it there. The reason was that God’s Word purifies the heart, allowing only thoughts that please God, which then lead to actions in line with His will. This is one of the keys to living a life free from sin.
  - The Word of God has a healing and purifying power (Proverbs 4:22, ESV). You can take a daily dose of it, and it will cleanse
  - and purify your heart, and even your entire being. When a sick person goes to the hospital, the doctor prescribes medication and advises adherence to the dosage at specific intervals. God's Word works in the same way. Don’t skip the doses. Be consistent with it, and before long, it will cleanse your thoughts and free you from any negative or controlling influences.
prayer: I declare that as I study the Word of God, it cleanses me from every negative thought and emotion. I trust in the Lord at all times, and I experience perfect peace of mind. My mind is anointed, and only thoughts that come from God enter my heart, in Jesus' name, Amen!
