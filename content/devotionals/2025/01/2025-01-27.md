title: You Are Full of Abilities
scripture: Philippians 4:13 (KJV)
verse: I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.
body:
  - When you received the Holy Spirit, one of the gifts He brought into your life is divine strength. This means that the strength of God is now at work in you, enabling you to do all things. With this strength, there is no such thing as impossibility when you approach any task because of God's power within you.
  - When you become aware of this truth, your response to any assignment will be, “Consider it done!” The strength of God in you makes you bold and courageous in the face of challenges. It empowers you to speak with confidence like Joshua and Caleb, who said, “For they are bread for us” (Numbers 14:9). These men remained unshaken even when they saw the giants in Canaan. Similarly, being aware of God's strength in you will make you audacious like David when he confronted Goliath (1 Samuel 17:46).
  - 'You are not alone; the Almighty God lives in you. If God could empower Joshua, Caleb, and David, He can do even more with you. Declare to yourself: "I have divine strength. I am full of abilities. I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."'
  - Speak to the challenges and obstacles before you today, and they will crumble, because you are full of God's abilities!
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for filling me with Your Spirit. I am full of strength, might, and abilities. There is no impossibility with me because my ability and strength come from You. I declare that every challenge troubling me falls flat right now, in Jesus’ name, Amen!
