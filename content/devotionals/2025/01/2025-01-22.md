title: Don’t Miss God
scripture: 1 Chronicles 12:32 (KJV)
verse: And of the children of Issachar, which were men that had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do; the heads of them were two hundred; and all their brethren were at their commandment.
body:
  - God never leaves us in the dark. A person who misses God's visitation is often careless and lacks wisdom. This is a call for spiritual sensitivity and alertness. Those who are spiritually alert prepare through the Holy Spirit for moments of divine visitation. Spiritually sensitive people can discern what God has in store for them at each stage of their lives. For example, believers who are spiritually alert understand that the rapture is imminent and prepare for it by living righteously and holy, so they are not caught off guard.
  - 'Elisha asked for a double portion of Elijah’s spirit, and Elijah responded, “Thou hast asked a hard thing: nevertheless, if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so” (2 Kings 2:10, KJV). Elisha had been spiritually sensitive and knew that Elijah was about to be taken to heaven. He was prepared and took the right actions by following his master closely.'
  - In Matthew 25:1-13, the parable of the five foolish virgins carefully illustrates the gross consequences of insensitivity and
  - unpreparedness. These virgins missed out and were called “foolish” because they were not ready. I pray that the Lord enlightens your spiritual senses, grants you grace, and strengthens you to take the right actions at the right time. May you not miss the timing of your divine visitation in Jesus' name. Amen!
