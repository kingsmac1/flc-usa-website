date: '2025-01-21'
title: Every Child is Your Child
scripture: Psalm 127:3 (KJV)
verse: 'Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: And the fruit of the womb is his reward.'
body:
  - Children are gifts from God. They are precious and should be cherished and cared for. Not caring for children demonstrates a lack of appreciation for them. Jesus Christ is a gift to humanity, but while He was on earth, many mistreated Him. The Pharisees and others did not recognize the value of this divine gift. Similarly, those who mistreat children are no different from the Pharisees who mistreated Jesus.
  - Here are reasons why you should treat every child as if they were your own. Children learn from examples; therefore, be a positive role model for them to follow. If you do not become a positive influence, they might grow up to negatively impact others, including your own children.
  - Provide for underprivileged children. Matthew 18:6 (KJV) states, “But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.” Children are innocent; make them happy and do not harm them. By providing for less privileged children, you bring joy to
  - their lives, and God will reward you richly for such acts.
  - Do not abuse or mistreat other people’s children. Such actions can lead to lasting effects of offense, bitterness, and trauma, which may then be passed on to other children. The children we see today will grow up to become the leaders of tomorrow. The quality of leadership we hope for depends on how we nurture them now. A great society is built on raising children without hurtful experiences. Nation-building includes godly child- rearing.Treat other people’s children as you would your own. Every child is your child, and you should care for them as such. Contact us for more information on how you can be involved in our project to support underprivileged children. God bless you.
prayer: The love of God is poured into my heart by the Holy Spirit. I love every child as my own and treat them with kindness. I will be a positive influence and teach them the ways of God. I will care for, protect, and provide for them in Jesus' name, Amen!
furtherStudies: Matthew 18:8, Proverbs 22:6, Ephesians 6:1
