title: Use Your Time Wisely
scripture: Psalm 90:12 (KJV)
verse: So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
body:
  - When the Bible speaks of restoration in Joel 2:25 (KJV), which says, “And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpillar, and the palmerworm, my great army which I sent among you,” it focuses on the restoration of time. Years, months, weeks, days, hours, and seconds are all measurements of time. God’s promise is about restoring lost time, and after that comes prosperity and abundance (Joel 2:26, KJV). As you begin to redeem your time, God will cause you to walk in prosperity and abundance. Do not wander aimlessly.
  - 'Don’t waste your time on things that are irrelevant or fail to elevate you. Psalm 119:60 (ERV) says, “Without wasting any time, I hurried back to obey your commands.” We will give an account to God concerning how we spent our time on earth. When you appear before God, He will ask if you accomplished what He sent you to do within the time He allocated for you. John 9:4 (KJV) says, “I must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work.” Begin to take seriously all that God has called you to do,'
  - because the clock is ticking fast.
  - The difference between life and death is time. Those who have passed away often wished they had more time. Similarly, the difference between seed time and harvest is time. Knowing the right time to plant and the right time to harvest is essential, as there is a time for everything. An individual with a good time management plan will be successful because they have no room for time wastage. As Solomon wisely said in Proverbs 21:5 (NLT), “Good planning and hard work lead to prosperity, but hasty shortcuts lead to poverty.” Time is a precious resource, so manage it wisely.
prayer: Dear Lord, teach me to number my days and accomplish all that You have designed for me. I use my time wisely, doing the right things at the right time and achieving the right results. Lord, restore all my wasted years and efforts, in Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: James 4:13-14, John 9:4 (KJV), Ephesians 5:15-17
