date: '2025-01-12'
title: Pride is Dangerous
scripture: Proverbs 16:18 (KJV)
verse: Pride goeth before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.
body:
  - Pride and disobedience often go hand in hand. Pride occurs when you elevate your ideas or opinions above the principles of God’s Word. You must consciously examine your thoughts and words to ensure they align with God’s teachings. Keep a diligent watch over your heart to allow only thoughts and ideas that are consistent with God’s Word.
  - God hates pride, as we see in Proverbs 8:13 (NLT) - “All who fear the Lord will hate evil. Therefore, I hate pride and arrogance, corruption and perverse speech.” Our lives should be pleasing to God. Therefore, let’s learn to avoid pride by aligning our thoughts with God’s Word. “For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.” - Hebrews 4:12 (KJV)
  - Lot’s separation from Abraham serves as an example of how pride crept into his heart, leading him to make a serious mistake. Pride causes people to act foolishly. If Lot had been wise, he would have returned to his uncle to apologize instead of ending up living in a cave. Living in a cave is a sign of the
  - poverty he eventually faced. I’ve heard people say things like, "I’d rather die than apologize" or "I’d rather be broke than say I’m sorry." Perhaps Lot felt this way, even after losing everything.
  - 'The prodigal son, on the other hand, returned to his father, repented, and his identity and inheritance were restored (Luke 15:21-22). Genesis 19:17 (KJV) says: “And it came to pass, when they had brought them forth abroad, that he said, Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed.” Who knows? If Lot had obeyed and gone to the mountain, he might have seen the land of Canaan and decided to reconcile with his uncle. If you’ve lost everything like the prodigal son or Lot, don’t let pride stop you from experiencing restoration. Repent, make peace with God or whoever you need to reconcile with, and start afresh. I pray that God will make all things new and beautiful for you again, rewrite your story, and give you fresh hope and a renewed destiny.'
prayer: I rebuke every trace of pride and arrogance in my heart. I reject lofty thoughts, and from today, God’s Word will guide my thoughts and actions. I am filled with meekness, humility, peace, forgiveness, love, and mercy, in Jesus' name, Amen!
