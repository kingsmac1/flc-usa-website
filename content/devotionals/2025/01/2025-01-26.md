title: We Are Able Ministers
scripture: 2 Corinthians 3:6 (KJV)
verse: 'Who also hath made us able ministers of the New Testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.'
body:
  - Anew creation in Christ has numerous benefits. The opening scripture says, "He has made us able ministers." We are not in need of abilities; He has already empowered us. This means we have been strengthened, equipped, and thoroughly furnished with all the resources we need to be competent and efficient in executing God's plans.
  - Apostle Paul was conscious of the fact that God had made him an able minister. No wonder he said in Philippians 4:13 (KJV), "I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me." Like Paul, be aware of God’s abilities at work in you. Being conscious of God's blessings brings them into reality.
  - You are not in need of external help, strength, or support. Your Helper, Strengthener, and Supporter lives in you. The Holy Spirit is your ever-present help in times of need (Psalm 46:1). Take advantage of His presence within you. He is not far away, but resides in you, ready to be stirred up so He can manifest and show up for you.
  - It's vital to know how to stir up the Holy Spirit within you. Samson knew how to tap into divine strength whenever he was confronted by an enemy. As believers, praying in the Spirit is one of the easiest ways to stir up the Spirit of God in us. 2 Timothy 1:6 (KJV) says, “Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands." Pray in the Spirit and put Him to work.
prayer: Dear Heavenly Father, I praise Your name for Your great power at work in me. Thank You for making me an able minister of the New Covenant. I will never be helpless because You are always strengthening me. Thank You for being my sufficiency and competence. In Jesus’ name, Amen!
