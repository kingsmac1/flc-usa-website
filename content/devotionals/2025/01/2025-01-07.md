title: Christian Service in the Local Church
scripture: Hebrews 6:10 (NLT)
verse: For God is not unjust. He will not forget how hard you have worked for Him and how you have shown your love to Him by caring for other believers, as you still do.
body:
  - Many times, when Christians say they are going to church, some don’t fully understand the purpose. Concerning service, we go to church to render service to the body of Christ, but many go to church expecting others to serve them. They arrive to find everything set – the sanctuary tidy, chairs well-arranged, bathrooms cleaned, the choir organized, and the sound system perfect – without seeing the importance of being involved in these efforts.
  - As we see in our opening verse, “For God is not unjust. He will not forget how hard you have worked for Him and how you have shown your love to Him by caring for other believers, as you still do” (Hebrews 6:10, NLT). One way to demonstrate our love for the Lord is by caring for other believers. Many people work hard before church services to ensure that when others arrive, they are well cared for. They do this by joining various departments in the church, attending rehearsals, participating in regular evangelism and outreach, and looking for ways to be actively involved in the smooth running of the church, so others feel
  - welcomed when they come. Finally, Mark 10:45 (NLT) says, “For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve others and to give His life as a ransom for many.” Jesus, our perfect example, served, and you too can make yourself available to serve in your local church, regardless of your busy schedule. God sees all the good works and services you render in the church. He will not forget them and will reward you for your labors. Make the decision today to be actively involved in your church and join a department. God bless you.
prayer: Thank You, precious Father, for giving me the opportunity to serve in Your house and among my brethren. I will always serve with a heart full of love, passion, and excitement. I will remain faithful and committed in my service to You. Thank You, Lord, for You are my great reward, in Jesus' name, Amen!
