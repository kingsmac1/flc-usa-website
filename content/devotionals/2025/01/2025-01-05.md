title: We Are God’s Project Managers
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:8-9 (KJV)
verse: 'Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labor. For we are laborers together with God: ye are God''s husbandry, ye are God''s building.'
body:
  - As the topic today suggests, we are indeed God’s project managers. God's project is to save mankind, and we are laboring with Him to achieve this. This means He is relying on us as His project managers to carry out His plan on earth in a timely manner. Amazingly, we will be rewarded for our labor when we get to heaven. This has been God's plan for humanity since before the foundation of the world.
  - We should be filled with passion for being part of what pleases God. God's desire is to save souls and deliver people from eternal condemnation. 1 Timothy 2:4 (KJV) says, “Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.” God wants all people to receive salvation and come to the full knowledge of the truth. This truth will build your faith and enable you to enjoy your inheritance in Christ Jesus.
  - Before we were born, God predestined that we would carry out His purpose on the earth (Jeremiah 1:5, KJV). That’s why He
  - left us on earth after we gave our lives to Him. God has equipped us with the necessary tools for this task, namely, the Holy Spirit and His Word. There are many lessons to learn from the scriptures, and God expects us to study the Bible for ourselves in order to discover the things that will help us become effective in executing His project during our time.
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for making me a co-laborer with You in saving lives and bringing people into their inheritance in Christ. Today, I affirm that I am an effective soul winner, and through me, many will come to know and serve You in spirit and in truth, in Jesus' name, Amen!
