title: Evaluate Yourself
scripture: Lamentations 3:40 (NLT)
verse: Instead, let us test and examine our ways. Let us turn back to the Lord.
body:
  - Self-evaluation is the process of reflecting on one’s strengths and areas that need improvement. Because of the teachings in 2 Corinthians 12 and Joel 3:10, I prefer not to use the word "weaknesses," but rather refer to them as "delicate" or "not too strong areas." Self-evaluation gives you the chance to critique your behavior and performance constructively. Doing this helps you better understand your own actions and identify areas that require growth.
  - Psalm 119:59 (NLT) says, “I pondered the direction of my life, and I turned to follow your laws.” Take time periodically to reflect on the direction of your life. Examine your efforts and results. Identify what affects you positively or negatively. Determine which factors will help you become a better person and draw you closer to your goals and to the version of yourself that God desires. It’s time to discard what slows you down and replace it with things that will bring you closer to your dreams.
  - Psalm 139:23-24 (ERV) says, “God, examine me and know my mind. Test me and know all my worries. Make sure that I am not going the wrong way. Lead me on the path that has always been
  - right.” As we enter a new year, it is important to evaluate yourself. Reflect on your spiritual life, finances, business, career, academics, and relationships. These areas are crucial, and you should make plans to improve in each one. The benefits of self-evaluation are numerous.
prayer: Dear Father, thank you for your word that has brought light into my heart. Search my heart and cleanse me of thoughts and desires that are not of you. I will always reflect on your word. As I evaluate myself, I will make decisions that lead to excellence and righteousness.
