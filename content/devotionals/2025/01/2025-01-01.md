date: '2025-01-01'
title: New Year, New Beginning
scripture: Isaiah 43:19 (KJV)
verse: Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.
body:
  - Congratulations and welcome to a new year! As we step into 2025, we should be grateful to God for bringing us into this new year. It’s understandable that you might have goals from the previous year that you couldn't accomplish, but don’t carry that burden and disappointment into the new year. Start with a heart of thanksgiving and recognize that God has given you an opportunity for a fresh start.
  - You can embark on something new for yourself. A new page has been opened, a brand new chapter awaits you. No two years are exactly the same; while they may seem similar, each year is distinct. Don’t let the limitations of the past year become a stronghold. Don’t assume that the same obstacles from last year will appear in this new year. Approach the year with optimism and a heart full of possibilities. Embrace new opportunities with an open mind.
  - Our opening scripture, Isaiah 43:19 (KJV), says, “Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.”
  - In this context, the term "wilderness" refers to an unpleasant situation. This verse is meant to give you hope. In this new year, God is telling you that He will transform your difficult situations into favorable ones.
  - He will bring victory out of your troubles and turn your mourning into joy. Be encouraged, for God has many wonderful things in store for you this year.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for this new year and the many great things that await me throughout the year. I am starting 2025 with a heart full of gratitude. Thank You, Lord, for making this year beautiful, great, and a testament to Your goodness. In Jesus' name, Amen!
