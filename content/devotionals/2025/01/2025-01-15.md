date: '2025-01-15'
title: The Lord Will Cause You to Laugh
scripture: Genesis 21:6 (NLT)
verse: And Sarah declared, ‘God has brought me laughter. All who hearabout this will laugh with me.
body:
  - God’s word is “Yes” and “Amen”! He is faithful to the end and able to fulfill all His promises to you. No matter how long you’ve been waiting on God, rest assured that He will show up for you with amazing surprises that will cause you to laugh.
  - Sarah was 90 years old when she gave birth to Isaac. This was an incredible miracle because it seemed unimaginable for a woman of her age to conceive. The Bible says in Genesis 21:6- 7 (NLT), “And Sarah declared, ‘God has brought me laughter. All who hear about this will laugh with me.’ ‘Who would have said to Abraham that Sarah would nurse a baby? Yet I have given Abraham a son in his old age!’” My prayer for you is that the Lord will grant you the miracle you desire. He will cause you to laugh, and all who hear of your miracle will laugh and rejoice with you, in Jesus' name, Amen!
  - Psalm 30:5 (KJV) says, “…Weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.” During times of hardship, it may feel as though the pain lasts forever, like someone who weeps
  - through the night. But there is hope – joy will come in the morning. This joy will make you forget the sorrow you endured.
  - The Lord is ushering you into the morning of your life, where you will experience unending joy, peace, laughter, and happiness, in Jesus' name, Amen!
  - 'I also love what King David said: “When the Lord restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dreamed. Our mouths were filled with laughter, our tongues with songs of joy. Then it was said among the nations, ‘The Lord has done great things for them.” (Psalms 126:1-2, NIV).'
prayer: My joy is restored, my peace is restored, my health is restored, my fortune is restored, and all that concerns me is restored. I have victory on every side, new miracles, and new testimonies. I am full of joy, happiness, excitement, and glory. The Lord is causing me to laugh, and I will continue to laugh and rejoice evermore, in Jesus' name, Amen!
furtherStudies: Job 8:20-22 (MSG), Job 8:21 (NIV), Psalms 126:4 (NIV)
readingPlan: Gen 34-35; Mat 11
