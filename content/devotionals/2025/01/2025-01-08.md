title: Don’t Miss Church Service
scripture: Hebrews 10:25 (NLT)
verse: And let us not neglect our meeting together, as some people do, but encourage one another, especially now that the day of His return is drawing near.
body:
  - In the early church, Apostle Paul noticed that some people had developed the habit of missing church gatherings. While he understood that certain situations might arise unexpectedly, some had become accustomed to skipping church regularly. He admonished them to stop such behavior. In today's world, many people also fall into the habit of missing church services, and when questioned, they often respond with, "Church is in the heart." This is a deception from the devil, and we must be cautious not to be misled by such reasoning.
  - 'Matthew 18:20 (ESV) shows us one of the key benefits of attending church services: “For where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them.” Jesus promises His presence, even in small gatherings. Be assured that when you go to church, God’s presence will bless and impact your life.'
  - 'Psalm 50:5 (ESV) further reminds us: “Gather to me my faithful ones, who made a covenant with me by sacrifice!” Isn’t it wonderful that God calls you faithful? He delights when we'
  - gather in His name, and attending church services is one of these important gatherings.
prayer: I am a persistent doer of the Word, and I act promptly on God’s instructions. I will not forsake attending church. I am committed to being consistent in church services and bringing others to the Lord, helping them grow and become involved, in Jesus’ name, Amen!
