title: Just Believe
scripture: Genesis 15:6 (KJV)
verse: And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness.
body:
  - In the New Testament, the new creation is justified by faith in God and not by works. So how is it that in the Old Testament, Abraham was the only person who was justified and declared righteous because of his faith in God? Genesis 15:6 (KJV) says, “And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness.”
  - 'This implies that there was something very unique about Abraham. He was called a friend of God and the father of faith. Abraham secured blessings for himself and his descendants, of which you are a part. He pleased the Lord through his faith, and God rewarded him, as we see in Hebrews 11:6 (KJV) – “But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.”'
  - “Even when there was no reason for hope, Abraham kept hoping...” (Romans 4:18, NLT). No matter the stage of life you are in right now – whether your health is deteriorating or your situation seems hopeless – God wants you to keep believing in Him. He will heal you, bring you out victoriously, and grant you the miracle you desire. Amen!
