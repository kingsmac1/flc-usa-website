title: Exercise Self-Control
scripture: Ephesians 4:25-27 NLT
verse: And don’t sin by letting anger control you. Don’t let the sun go down while you are still angry, for anger gives a foothold to the devil.
body:
  - In his epistle, Apostle Paul warns God’s people about the consequences of anger. "Don’t sin by letting anger control you" – this means that failing to exercise self-control is a sin against God. Self-control is the ability to manage your emotions, impulses, and behaviors. It enables you to think through and evaluate your actions rather than reacting impulsively.
  - 'Self-control is a commendable virtue. It is part of the inner beauty that Peter refers to in 1 Peter 3:4 (NLT): “You should clothe yourselves instead with the beauty that comes from within, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is so precious to God.” True beauty is the beauty within, and it doesn’t fade. Are you slow to anger? Do you exercise self- control when situations push you to the edge?'
  - At times, people say, “I have reached my self-control elastic limit, so I had to explode.” As a believer filled with the Spirit, God has given you the ability to stay calm under pressure. You should not "explode" because you are not explosive.
  - Explosions cause harm and are dangerous. This implies that losing control often leads to harmful consequences. Let your actions and responses bring glory to God and reflect His image (Matthew 5:16).
prayer: I am a new person in Christ; the old nature of anger, offense, and unforgiveness is gone. Through the Spirit of God, I subdue fleshly reactions and control my emotions. Anger has no dominion over me, in Jesus’ name. Amen!
