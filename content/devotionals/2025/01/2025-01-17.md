title: The Cure for Spiritual Comatose
scripture: Luke 18:1 (KJV)
verse: And He spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint.
body:
  - Jesus told His disciples that people are meant to always pray and not faint. The opposite of prayer is not merely prayerlessness, but fainting or slumbering. When people stop praying, they grow weak. The word "faint" here can be likened to spiritual comatose, which many refer to as a coma.
  - 'In medical terms, comatose is a state of unconsciousness where a person is unable to wake up, often due to illness or injury. In a similar way, many Christians lose their spiritual awareness and perception because they lack prayer. We are created to pray; prayer sustains us and gives us life. Just as fish need water to survive, Christians need consistent prayer to maintain a healthy spiritual life. Paul illustrates this in Colossians 4:2 (KJV): “Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving.”'
  - Those experiencing spiritual comatose often struggle with fear. Philippians 4:6 (NKJV) says, “Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.” Consistent prayer
  - drives away fear, anxiety, and depression. A steady prayer life helps to maintain both spiritual and physical well-being. “But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word.” – Acts 6:4 (KJV)
  - Consistency in prayer and study of the Word provides the nourishment needed for a healthy spiritual life and a strong walk with the Lord.
prayer: I am spiritually healthy, sound, and alive. I give myself continually to prayer and the study of God’s Word. I am fervent in prayer at all times, and my prayers prevail over every circumstance. The Word of God is working in me, producing what it speaks about, in Jesus’ name, Amen!
