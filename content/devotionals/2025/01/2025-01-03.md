title: What Can You See?
scripture: Genesis 13:15 (KJV)
verse: for all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed for ever.
body:
  - Even if Abraham was standing on a hill, he could only see a small part of the land. So how did God give him all the land? The answer is that Abraham saw beyond his physical eyes. He saw with his heart, through the eyes of his imagination. With his imagination, he was able to envision the entire land.
  - Your imagination is very important. It has creative power. No matter where you are or what situation you find yourself in, always see yourself beyond your present circumstances. Stop seeing failure, disappointment, lack, or impossibility. Just See yourself rising from that sickbed, victorious and successful. See yourself becoming great, influential, and thriving.
  - 'Jeremiah 1:12 (KJV) – “Then said the LORD unto me, Thou hast well seen: for I will hasten my word to perform it.”'
  - From the scripture above, we see that God acknowledged the fact that Jeremiah had seen correctly. I believe God wouldn’t have hastened to fulfill His word if Jeremiah hadn’t seen properly. God wants you to see clearly; He wants you to imagine the right things because they will eventually manifest in the
  - physical world. That’s why He encourages you to guard your heart diligently, because from your heart spring forth the things you will see in your life (Proverbs 4:23 KJV).
  - God is always working to bring to pass what you see in your heart. Therefore, ensure you see things the right way.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank you for the gift of sight you have given me. I declare that my heart is anointed to see only the things You want me to see. I do not accommodate negative thoughts or imaginations. I bring forth good treasures from my heart, in Jesus' name. Amen.
