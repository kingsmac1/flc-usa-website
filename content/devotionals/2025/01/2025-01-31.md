title: Be at Peace with Everybody
scripture: Romans 12:18 NLT
verse: Do all that you can to live in peace with everyone.
body:
  - Be a person of peace. Let others know you for your peaceful nature and strive to live in harmony with everyone. Some people enjoy disputes and arguments; they feel compelled to always express their opinions or disagreements. But this is not always necessary. Practice discipline by choosing to overlook offenses and focus your energy on more meaningful pursuits.
  - 'Romans 12:18 (NLT) reminds us: "Do all that you can to live in peace with everyone." You have a responsibility to ensure that peace prevails, even if others refuse to comply. Strive to maintain peace as much as possible. When you encounter conflicting parties, make efforts to reconcile them because Matthew 5:9 says, “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.”'
  - 2 Timothy 2:24 (ISV) instructs, “A servant of the Lord must not argue. Instead, he must be kind to everyone, teachable, and willing to endure wrong.” Avoid arguments and calmly express your thoughts and opinions. This is essential for righteous living, and by doing so, you exemplify the qualities of a true
  - believer (Romans 12:9-21). I pray for you today, may the Lord fill your heart with His Spirit, guide you to make wise decisions in all areas of your life, and help you to live in peace with everyone, in Jesus’ name. Amen!
prayer: The peace of God fills my heart always. I live in peace with everyone. I avoid arguments and conflicts, and I act wisely with all people and in all situations.
