date: '2025-01-19'
title: One More Attempt
scripture: 2 Kings 5:14 (ERV)
verse: So Naaman did what the man of God said. He went down and dipped himself in the Jordan River seven times, and he became pure and clean. His skin became soft like the skin of a baby.
body:
  - One more attempt reflects perseverance and determination. It is a sign of strong faith, and strong faith leads to the desired outcome. It means pressing on in the face of unfavorable or difficult circumstances, regardless of previous failures. Never give up until you achieve your goal. It implies persistence. In Genesis 18:23-32, we see Abraham continuously negotiating with God as He was set to destroy Sodom and Gomorrah. Abraham didn’t give up; he kept pressing on until he reached an agreement with God, which resulted in the preservation of Lot and his family.
  - In Luke 18:4-5 (TPT), it says, “He ignored her plea for quite some time, but she kept asking. Eventually, he said to himself, ‘This widow keeps annoying me, demanding her rights, and I’m tired of listening to her. Even though I’m not a religious man, and I don’t care about the opinions of others, I’ll get her off my back by answering her claims for justice and I’ll rule in her favor. Then she’ll leave me alone.’” This scripture teaches the impact of persistence. The judge grew weary of the widow’s continuous requests for justice. Imagine the widow waking up each
  - morning, determined to present her request again, telling herself, “One more attempt might bring the change I need. Even if I’m turned down today, I will try again tomorrow.” This is the attitude of every person who has achieved greatness and victory in life.
  - In 1 Kings 18:43-44 (NKJV), it says, “…And said to his servant, ‘Go up now, look toward the sea.’ So he went up and looked, and said, ‘There is nothing.’ And seven times he said, ‘Go again.’ Then it came to pass the seventh time, that he said, ‘There is a cloud, as small as a man’s hand, rising out of the sea!’…” A severe drought had caused a famine that lasted for three years, and Elijah was praying for rain. There was no sign of rain as God had promised, but Elijah did not give up. He resolved to pray again, one more time, until the seventh attempt, and then the rain came. Perhaps you’ve been praying and making healing declarations over your body, but you’ve given up because you haven’t seen any changes. Remember, winners don’t quit. Continue from where you left off. Press on and press harder. Keep praying and making faith declarations for your healing. You might be 99% toward your breakthrough, and one more attempt could be the key to your miracle.
prayer: My faith is alive. I am resolute and persistent. I am a winner, and I always triumph. I do not give up, no matter what challenges I face. My faith prevails every time, in Jesus’ name, Amen!
