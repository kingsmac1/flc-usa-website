title: Benefits of Tithing
scripture: Genesis 14:20 (KJV)
verse: And blessed be the most high God, which hath delivered thine enemies into thy hand. And he gave him tithes of all.
body:
  - It was Abram who gave Melchizedek a tithe of all (i.e., the spoil). Who is Melchizedek? Melchizedek is a type of Christ. Like Jesus, Melchizedek is both a king and a priest; he is referred to as "king of peace" and "king of righteousness" (Hebrews 7:2, KJV). Abraham, the father of God’s chosen people, performed this act, which symbolizes the practice his descendants would follow in the future.
  - 'This custom of paying a tithe, or a tenth part, has long been the practice of God''s people. According to Genesis 28:22 (KJV), “And this stone, which I have set for a pillar, shall be God''s house: and of all that thou shalt give me I will surely give the tenth unto thee.” This principle is not restricted to the Old Testament because Abraham began a lineage of godly people, a lineage that is not just biological but spiritual – the new creation (Galatians 3:29, KJV). If tithing were merely an Old Testament law, why do we still claim to be the seed of Abraham? If we belong to Christ, we are automatically Abraham’s descendants, which is a New Testament truth. It is important to follow this principle for the preservation of blessings. This practice existed before the law of Moses.'
  - 'Genesis 14:18-20 (KJV) says, “And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God. And he blessed him, and said, Blessed be Abram of the most high God, possessor of heaven and earth: and blessed be the most high God, which hath delivered thine enemies into thy hand. And he gave him tithes of all.” In this passage, we see that Melchizedek provided food and wine and blessed Abram before Abram gave his tithe. This teaches us to follow the same principle of giving our tithes whenever God provides for and blesses us. Be a doer of the Word, not just a hearer. God is always looking for faithfulness, even when no one is watching (Luke 16:10). There are more blessings for those who are faithful in the small things.'
