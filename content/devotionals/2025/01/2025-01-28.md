title: True Worship
scripture: John 4:24 KJV)
verse: 'God is a spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.'
body:
  - True worship is a reverent devotion and allegiance to God in spirit and in truth. There are different kinds of worship, but what makes it true worship is that it is performed in spirit and truth.
  - Firstly, we need to understand that God is a spirit, and He lives in the spiritual realm, not the physical realm. To offer acceptable worship to God, it must come from your spirit. Worship is a sacrifice to God, and it must be a spiritual act, not a carnal one. In this context, "carnal" refers to something sensual or fleshly. In Genesis 4:3-6, we see that God rejected Cain's offering. They must have brought their offerings because God had revealed to them the importance of a sacrifice. The simple reason Cain’s offering was rejected is that he didn’t follow the requirements of acceptable worship, which must be done in spirit and truth. If Cain had truly known the truth and understood the sovereignty of God, he would have given his best. Cain’s carnality led to his rejected sacrifice, and his jealousy, which caused him to kill his brother, further reveals his lack of spiritual understanding. Just because worship songs and devotion appeal to your senses does not mean they are acceptable to God.
  - 'Secondly, true worship must be rendered in truth. God is truth (John 17:17), and true worship must align with God''s Word. It must come from a sincere heart, from a contrite spirit. Psalms 51:17 (KJV) says, “The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.” True worship must also be offered without anger and in faith (1 Timothy 2:8).'

I will worship You with lifted hands and offer the sacrifice of my lips. My mouth will sing praises to Your name, and my whole being will worship You. Amen!
