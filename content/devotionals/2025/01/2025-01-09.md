title: Regulate the Transactions of your Heart
scripture: Proverbs 4:23 (KJV)
verse: Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.
body:
  - To regulate means to be in control, while transactions refer to activities. Therefore, be in control of the activities of your heart. It is your responsibility to monitor what goes in and out of your heart because the transactions of your heart will reflect in your life. It will soon become obvious what you have been feeding your heart. It’s similar to eating healthy meals which nourish your body.
  - 'I love how the Good News Translation puts Proverbs 4:23: “Be careful how you think; your life is shaped by your thoughts.” Wow! God is warning us to be mindful of our thoughts because our lives will be shaped by them. We must carefully regulate what we allow into our minds. Say to yourself, “Never again will I allow garbage into my heart.” Garbage refers to anything that is worthless, inferior, or vile.'
  - Who you are and where you are today are the results of your thoughts from yesterday. If you expect a better future, start now by filling your heart with the right things. Make up your mind to think on what is good. Replace those negative thoughts and
  - imaginations with the Word of God. Don’t entertain thoughts of fear, lack, disease, failure, disappointment, or any other negative or unclean thoughts.
  - 'Philippians 4:8 (KJV) says: “Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.”'
prayer: My mind is anointed to conceive thoughts inspired by the Holy Spirit. I receive positive, faith-filled thoughts. I have a sound mind, and I diligently guard my heart at all times, in Jesus' name, Amen!
furtherStudies: Proverbs 4:23 (GNT), Matthew 12:35 (KJV), Philippians 4:8 (KJV)
