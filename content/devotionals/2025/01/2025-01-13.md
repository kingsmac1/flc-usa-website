title: Dangers of Disobedience
scripture: Genesis 19:17-20 (NLT)
verse: When they were safely out of the city, one of the angels ordered, 'Run for your lives! Don’t look back or stop anywhere in the valley! Escape to the mountains, or you will be swept away!' 'Oh no, my Lord!' Lot begged. 'You have been so gracious to me and saved my life, and you have shown such great kindness. But I cannot go to the mountains. Disaster would catch up to me there, and I would soon die. See, there is a small village nearby. Please let me go there instead; don’t you see how small it is? Then my life will be saved.
body:
  - Disobedience is altering an instruction. This, oftentimes, originates from hesitation, which is clearly similar to unwillingness. Someone who disregards a given instruction and chooses to act their own way is demonstrating disobedience. Many believers try to have their own way with God, preferring to follow their desires rather than God's instructions. But God’s word is meant to be followed, not negotiated. The moment you start questioning whether to do what God has instructed, alternative options arise.
  - The devil often uses this strategy to deceive God’s people. The key is to obey God’s instructions without hesitation. When we look for alternatives to God’s directives, we neither please Him nor achieve the results He desires, even if our actions seem logical in the physical world. In 1 Samuel 15:18 (NLT), the Lord sent Saul on a mission saying, “Go and completely destroy the
  - sinners, the Amalekites, until they are all dead.” But Saul acted according to what he thought was right, disobeying God's command. As a result, God rejected him, and he lost his throne.
  - Psalm 62:11 (KJV) says, “God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this.” Lot hesitated to follow the angels’ instructions, his wife disobeyed by looking back and became a pillar of salt, and as seen in the opening verse, Lot negotiated with the angels. He thought he was being wise by suggesting going to a nearby village. However, after moving to the village, he feared its inhabitants and ended up living in a cave. This led to further consequences – his daughters got him drunk, and he committed incest with them (Genesis 19:36, NLT).
  - '“But Samuel replied, ‘What is more pleasing to the Lord: your burnt offerings and sacrifices or your obedience to His voice? Listen! Obedience is better than sacrifice, and submission is better than offering the fat of rams.” - 1 Samuel 15:22 (NLT). Ultimately, obedience is better than sacrifice. Act on God’s word promptly; don’t hesitate, and don’t alter His instructions.'
prayer: I am a persistent doer of God’s word. I act swiftly on His instructions in every situation. I will never be disobedient to heavenly directives. God’s word is working in me, producing results in every area of my life, family, finances, and all that concerns me in Jesus' name, Amen!
