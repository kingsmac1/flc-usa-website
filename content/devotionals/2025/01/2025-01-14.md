title: Let him that Steal, Steal No More
scripture: Ephesians 4:28 (KJV)
verse: 'Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labor, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.'
body:
  - Stealing is simply taking possession of what does not rightfully belong to you. It doesn't always involve force; it can also happen through deception. Stealing isn’t limited to money, cars, or material possessions; it can also involve intangible things, such as stealing someone’s business ideas without permission or hijacking their vision.
  - Depriving others of their benefits or rightful compensation is also considered stealing. Taking another person’s spouse is a form of theft, and many other acts fall under this category. Stealing stems from covetousness and selfishness. In Genesis 12:17 (NLT), it says, “But the Lord sent terrible plagues upon Pharaoh and his household because of Sarai, Abram’s wife.” Although Abraham lied to Pharaoh, he did so out of fear that Pharaoh would take his wife by killing him first. The consequence of this was that terrible plagues afflicted Egypt.
  - We also see in Genesis 20:18 (NLT), “For the Lord had caused all the women to be infertile because of what happened with
  - Abraham’s wife, Sarah.” This was a recurring event. When you steal from others, you often inherit their problems. Abimelech took Abraham’s wife, and the result was that all the women in the land became barren. Perhaps the challenges you face are a result of material or immaterial things you’ve forcefully or deceitfully taken from others.
  - 'Psalm 62:10 (KJV) says, “Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: If riches increase, set not your heart upon them.” Similarly, Exodus 20:17 (NLT) says, “You must not covet your neighbor’s house. You must not covet your neighbor’s wife, male or female servant, ox or donkey, or anything else that belongs to your neighbor.” Dreaming big is admirable, but not at the expense of others. There is a difference between having big aspirations and being greedy. Be content with what you have, and it will save you from much trouble.'
