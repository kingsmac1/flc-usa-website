title: As He is, So Are You in the World
scripture: 1 John 4:17 ESV
verse: By this is love perfected with us, so that we may have confidence for the Day of Judgment, because as he is, so also are we in this world.
body:
  - Genesis 1:26 says, “Then God said, ‘Let us make man in our image, after our likeness.’” He gave man dominion over all things. God created animals after their own kind (Genesis 1:24). Cattle were created after cattle, birds after birds, and so on. The implication is that animals were made in the image and likeness of their kind. But God created you in His own image and likeness.
  - You are a unique being, specially crafted by God to be like Him. He gave you His Spirit so you can function like Him. He wants you to represent Him here on earth. Take action and respond to situations the way He would respond because His Spirit is in you.
  - What would Jesus do if He were here? Jesus wouldn’t allow sickness to ravage His body. Whenever He showed up, He exercised dominion over every situation around Him – over the wind, the land, the water, sickness, and even death. From the opening scripture, "As He is, so are you in this world." Never allow sickness to afflict you or those around you. Use the name
  - of Jesus to take control over your finances, your city, and everything around you. In doing so, you are representing Him well.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for making me in your image and likeness. I exercise dominion over the situations around me. I represent the fullness of your kingdom here on earth, in Jesus’ name. Amen!
