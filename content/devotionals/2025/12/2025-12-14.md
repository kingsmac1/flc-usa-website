title: You Are Anointed
scripture: Acts 10:38 (KJV)
verse: 'How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him.'
body:
  - The anointing is not something distant, rare, or reserved for a few people. As a believer, you carry the same Holy Spirit who empowered Jesus during His earthly ministry. Acts 10:38 (KJV) shows clearly that Jesus was anointed with the Holy Spirit and with power, and through that anointing, He healed, delivered, and transformed lives. When you received the Holy Spirit, you received that same divine empowerment. This means you are not ordinary; you carry God's ability within you.
  - 'Many believers spend years praying for “more anointing,” not realizing that the fullness of the Holy Spirit already lives within them. The anointing is not a substance you receive in doses; it is the presence and power of the Holy Spirit at work in you. Once the Spirit comes to dwell in you, the anointing comes with Him. 1 John 2:27 (NKJV) affirms this truth: “But the anointing which you have received from Him abides in you…” The anointing is not visiting you occasionally; it abides, remains, and continues to function in you.'
  - Understanding this gives you boldness. You don't have to wait for a certain feeling or atmosphere before stepping out in faith. The Holy Spirit within you equips you to pray effectively, minister to others, overcome challenges, and walk in spiritual authority. The anointing is the empowerment that enables you to live out God's purpose confidently, knowing that you are not operating in your own strength.
  - This awareness also helps you value your relationship with the Holy Spirit. Instead of chasing after external experiences, you learn to cultivate fellowship with Him daily. The more you yield to Him, the more His power flows naturally through you. The anointing is not something you try to activate; it is something you allow to operate by living in obedience, faith, and sensitivity to the Spirit's leading. God has already equipped you with everything you need to fulfill His will. You are anointed, not some day, not partially, but fully, because the Holy Spirit lives in you. Walk with confidence, serve with boldness, and speak with authority, knowing you carry God's power everywhere you go.
prayer: Lord, thank You for anointing me with Your Holy Spirit. I walk in confidence, knowing Your power works in me and through me. I'm anointed. I'm full of the Holy Spirit. I'm divinely empowered, in Jesus' name, Amen.
