title: An Outburst of Prosperity
scripture: Deuteronomy 8:18 (KJV)
verse: 'But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth…'
body:
  - Prosperity is God's idea, not man's invention. The word prosperity comes from a concept that means to advance, to flourish, and to succeed with God's help. When the Bible says that the Lord gives power to get wealth (Deuteronomy 8:18), it emphasizes that genuine prosperity begins with Him. It is not luck, chance, or human strength. It is the hand of God causing your life to move forward. Whenever the Lord is involved in a person's life, something begins to rise, improve, and flourish in ways natural effort alone cannot produce.
  - Prosperity is often misunderstood as only financial gain, but Scripture paints a fuller picture. True prosperity includes peace, wisdom, progress, and fruitfulness in every area of life. Joseph prospered in Potiphar's house, not because he had abundance at first, but because God was with him and caused everything he did to succeed (Genesis 39:2–3). That is prosperity; God touching your life in a way that produces visible results. When His presence rests on your work, your steps, and your decisions, things begin to grow and increase naturally.
  - The Bible repeatedly shows that the Lord delights in His people
  - doing well. When Isaac planted in a time of famine, the Lord caused him to increase beyond expectation (Genesis 26:12). His environment did not determine his outcome; God did. This is the nature of divine prosperity. It is not controlled by economic conditions, backgrounds, or limitations. When God decides to lift you, no situation around you can hinder the result. Prosperity becomes your portion because His hand is upon you.
  - 'An outburst of prosperity happens when God breathes on your efforts and multiplies the work of your hands. It may begin quietly, but suddenly, you notice fresh ideas, new strength, open opportunities, and unusual progress. Prosperity is the Lord turning little into plenty, slow into steady, and delay into fruitfulness. It is God moving you forward one step at a time until your life reflects His goodness. Nothing is too small for Him to enlarge, and nothing is too hard for Him to accomplish. The Lord takes pleasure in your progress, and He will cause you to flourish in ways you never imagined. I pray for you today: may He increase your greatness and cause you to prosper on every side. May the Lord cause you to experience material and financial prosperity: prosperity in your health, in your spirit, soul and body in Jesus name.'
