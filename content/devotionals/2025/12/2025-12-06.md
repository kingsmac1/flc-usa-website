title: Reaping the Fruits of your Lips
scripture: Proverbs 18:21 (KJV)
verse: 'Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.'
body:
  - Words are powerful forces. Proverbs 18:21 (KJV) empathizes that our tongue carries the potential to produce life or death. This means your speech is never neutral; every word you speak plants a seed. Some seeds bring strength, peace, and blessing; others produce discouragement, confusion, or regret. God has given you the ability to shape the world around you through what you choose to say.
  - Because of this, God's word teaches us to be purposeful with our words. Jesus said in Mark 11:23 (KJV) that a person “shall have whatsoever he saith.” Your words reveal what you truly believe, and they also reinforce it. When you speak fear, fear grows. When you speak faith, faith grows. When complaints flow freely, discouragement follows. But when you speak with hope and confidence in God, you sow seeds that produce a harvest of strength and victory.
  - Your words also affect your inner life. They influence your thoughts, your emotions, and even your expectations. That's
  - 'why Psalm 19:14 (KJV) expresses a heartfelt prayer: “Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight…” When your heart is filled with God''s truth, your mouth naturally follows. Your speech becomes a well of encouragement, wisdom, and peace without effort or pretense.'
  - Speaking life doesn't mean ignoring reality; it means choosing a higher perspective. It means allowing God's word to shape your response instead of letting fear control your voice. When you declare gratitude instead of complaint, assurance instead of panic, and truth instead of doubt, you create an atmosphere where God's peace and strength can flourish. Little by little, you begin to see the fruit of your words taking root in your daily life.
  - Every day, God gives you the gift of choice. What will you plant with your words? Speak what strengthens your spirit. Speak what lifts others. Speak what reflects God's goodness. As you consistently sow words filled with grace and truth, you'll harvest a life filled with growth, blessing, and peace.
prayer: Father, thank You for the power You have given my words. I thank you for your word is truth. Therefore, I speak life, hope, and truth in every situation. My words are not ordinary but my words build, encourage, and bless. I declare that the fruit of my lips will always reflect Your goodness and bring increase into my life in Jesus' name, Amen.
