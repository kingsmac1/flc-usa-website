date: '2025-12-20'
title: Prepare for the Increase
scripture: Isaiah 54:2 KJV
verse: 'Enlarge the place of thy tent, and let them stretch forth the curtains of thine habitations: spare not, lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.'
body:
  - Increase is coming. God is preparing to lift you, expand you, and usher you into greater heights. But increase is never accidental; it is always preceded by preparation. Before God pours out more, He stirs your heart to make room for what He is about to release. Proverbs 23:18 (KJV) says, “For surely there is an end; and thine expectation shall not be cut off”. Your expectation positions you for manifestation.
  - There are seasons for everything, and you are approaching a season of harvest, progress, and enlargement. Don't wait until the breakthrough arrives before preparing for it. Faith gets ready ahead of time. Just as a farmer prepares the soil before the rain, you prepare your life before the increase. The Bible says, “Prepare thy work without, and make it fit for thyself in the field; and afterwards build thine house” (Proverbs 24:27, KJV). Preparation is a sign of faith and wisdom.
  - When opportunity meets a prepared person, success is inevitable. But when opportunity meets someone unprepared, increase can be missed, mishandled, or wasted. God does not bless confusion; He blesses readiness. He promotes those who structure their lives, sharpen their skills, strengthen their character, and make room for more. The Lord is ready to do His part; now, He calls you to prepare for what is coming.
  - God is expanding your borders. He is stretching your capacity, enlarging your influence, and positioning you for more. But you must align your mindset with His plan. Stop thinking small. Stop planning small. Stop expecting little. Scripture says in Matthew 9:29 (KJV), “According to your faith be it unto you. If you expect increase, prepare for increase. If you expect promotion, act like it. If you expect favor, position your life to receive it.
  - This is your season to lengthen your cords and strengthen your stakes. God is ready to increase you. Now, prepare your life for what He is bringing.
prayer: 'Father, I thank You for the season of increase ahead of me. I declare: the Holy Spirit prepares my mind, my plans, and everything about my life for what You are releasing. Enlarge my capacity, and guide my steps so I can fully receive and handle the blessings You have prepared. In Jesus'' name, Amen.'
