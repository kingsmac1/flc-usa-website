date: '2025-12-16'
title: The Lord Gives You Stability
scripture: Psalm 40:2 (AMP)
verse: He brought me up out of a horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my steps.
body:
  - 'God understands the moments when life feels heavy, confusing, or overwhelming. Psalm 40:2 (AMP) portrays God as one who not only sees your distress but actively lifts you out of it. This reflects the assurance found in Psalm 34:17 (NIV): “The righteous cry out, and the Lord hears them; He delivers them from all their troubles.” No petition you make to God is ever ignored. His rescue is intentional, loving, and perfectly timed.'
  - 'But God''s involvement in your life doesn''t end with pulling you out of difficulty. After He rescues, He establishes. Scripture says He “set my feet upon a rock,” meaning He gives you firm footing where instability once reigned. Psalm 18:2 (NKJV) says: “The Lord is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer… my stronghold.” When everything around you feels shaky, God Himself becomes your steady place.'
  - 'Beyond stability, God gives you direction. Psalm 40:2 (AMP) continues, saying He is “steadying my footsteps and establishing my path.” This aligns beautifully with Proverbs 3:6 (NKJV): “In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct'
  - your paths.” God's guidance is not vague or distant; it is personal and precise. He doesn't simply lift you up; He leads you forward, ensuring your steps are guided in His wisdom.
  - This divine stability produces confidence. When you know God is the One securing your steps, you no longer walk in fear or uncertainty. Even when challenges arise again, you are not easily shaken because you stand on the unchanging foundation of His faithfulness. Stability from God is more than emotional calm; it is spiritual grounding that transforms how you move through life.
  - Ultimately, Psalm 40:2 (AMP) says that true stability comes from God alone. He rescues, He restores, and He redirects. With Him, sinking places lose their power, and previously uncertain steps gain purpose. He is the steady Rock beneath your feet and the faithful Guide before you. In Him, you move from distress to stability, and from uncertainty to a firmly established path. Glory to God!
prayer: Lord, thank You for lifting me out of every pit and placing my feet on Your solid foundation. You steady my steps and direct my path. I declare that my life is anchored in Your strength, guided by Your wisdom, and secured by Your faithfulness. I will never be down in life and I remain unstable and established in you in Jesus' name, Amen.
