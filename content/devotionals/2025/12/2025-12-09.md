title: The Blessing That Makes You Rich
scripture: Proverbs 10:22 (KJV)
verse: The blessing of the LORD, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it.
body:
  - When the Bible speaks of “the blessing of the Lord,” it is describing something far deeper than financial increase or material comfort. God's blessing is His divine empowerment on a person's life; His hand resting upon you, His favor speaking for you, and His goodness shaping your path. When God blesses a person, that blessing produces peace, progress, stability, and fulfillment. It goes beyond what effort alone could achieve. This is why Scripture declares that it is the Lord's blessing that makes a person rich, not merely human skill or hard work (Proverbs 10:22).
  - 'Throughout Scripture, those who walked under God''s blessing carried a unique fragrance of divine help. Abraham is a clear example. God told him, “I will bless thee… and thou shalt be a blessing” (Genesis 12:2). Notice the order: God blessed him first, and the results followed. Prosperity, protection, increase, and honor were products of the blessing, not the cause. The blessing is the root; riches, influence, and progress are the fruits. When God''s blessing rests upon you, ordinary moments produce extraordinary results. The blessing also has the power to lift a person regardless of their starting point. Isaac experienced this during a severe famine. While others'
  - struggled, the Bible says the Lord blessed him, and that blessing produced undeniable increase (Genesis 26:12). His environment did not decide his outcome; the blessing did. This is why believers must never fear external conditions. When God's blessing is on your life, He provides wisdom, ideas, strength, and favor that lift you above every limitation. The blessing works anywhere, and it works at any time.
  - 'God''s blessing brings a kind of richness no sorrow can touch. It includes joy, peace of mind, sound relationships, good health, spiritual growth, and steady progress. Many people chase wealth but lack rest. Others accumulate possessions but have no lasting fulfillment. The blessing of the Lord gives richness that strengthens your life rather than draining it. It adds peace, not pressure. It brings stability, not fear. When God blesses you, everything you touch carries His signature. As you move forward, remember this: true richness comes from God, not from stress, struggle, or self-dependence. When you honor Him, walk with Him, and obey His voice, His blessing settles upon you and shapes your life in ways you could never produce by your own ability. Expect His blessing to rest on you today and always.'
