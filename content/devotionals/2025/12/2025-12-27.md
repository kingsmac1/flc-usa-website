title: Walking in the Path of Righteousness
scripture: Romans 5:17 KJV
verse: For if by one man's offence death reigned by one; much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ.
body:
  - 'When you received Christ, you received more than forgiveness; you received a brand-new nature: the nature of righteousness. You are not trying to become righteous; you are the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. The Bible says, “Therefore, if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature” (2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV). A new creature carries a new identity, a new mind, and a new way of living. Righteousness became part of who you are the moment you believed.'
  - David wrote, “He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake” (Psalm 23:3, KJV). This shows that God Himself guides you to live a righteous life. Righteousness is not lived by human effort alone; the Spirit within you enables you to do the will of God with joy. What once felt difficult becomes possible because your heart has been transformed by the life of Christ.
  - Righteousness is not a reward; it is a nature. Just as a newborn baby cannot become “more human,” you cannot become “more righteous.” The child only needs to grow and develop, and so do
  - you. You already possess the fullness of righteousness, but God wants you to grow in expressing it. Scripture says, “As He is, so are we in this world” (1 John 4:17, KJV). You reflect His nature, so your lifestyle should show it.
  - 'Because you are righteous, God expects your life to produce fruits of righteousness: actions, choices, habits, and attitudes that honor Him. This means refusing paths, habits, and environments that draw your life away from what pleases Him. The Bible warns, “Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners” (1 Corinthians 15:33, KJV). Choose associations that encourage holiness, strengthen your walk, and inspire you to live a life pleasing to God. Surround yourself with influences that nourish your spiritual growth and support the righteous nature already within you. You are righteous; now, walk boldly in that righteousness and let it be seen through the life you live.'
prayer: Father, thank You for making me righteous in Christ. I will always walk in the path of righteousness daily. I'm enabled by you to live a life that pleases You and reflects Your nature. My actions, habits, and decisions will glorify you always In Jesus' name, Amen.
