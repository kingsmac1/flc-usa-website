title: Enjoy the Ministry of Angels
scripture: Psalm 91:11 (KJV)
verse: For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
body:
  - 'As a believer, you are an heir of salvation. You have a unique privilege: the ministry of angels. Angels are described in Scripture as ministering spirits sent to attend to the needs of God''s children. To minister means to serve, help, or render assistance. Because we belong to God, angels are assigned to us to provide guidance, protection, and help in times of need. This is not just a comforting thought; it is a spiritual reality for those who are saved.'
  - Psalm 91:11 (KJV) says, “For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.” This verse reveals that God Himself commands angels to watch over His children. Their service includes guarding us from danger, directing our steps, and even delivering us from situations we cannot handle on our own. Knowing this encourages you to walk in faith, trusting that God's heavenly messengers are attentive to His instructions concerning your lives.
  - Angels are active throughout Scripture. Daniel was ministered to by angels in moments of distress, and the disciples were strengthened by angelic presence in the book of Acts. Hebrews
  - '1:14 (KJV) explains their role clearly: “Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?” Their ministry is directly tied to our salvation and position in God''s family. Whenever you face a need (spiritual, physical, or emotional), you can confidently call on God, and His angels will respond to minister to you.'
  - Being aware of angelic ministry changes how you approach life. You are not alone in your challenges; you are attended to by powerful spiritual beings under God's authority. This knowledge brings peace, boldness, and confidence in prayer. You can take advantage of this divine service by making your request to God and He will mobilize his angels to attend to you.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the ministry of angels in my life. I am always conscious of Your heavenly messengers to guide, protect, and assist me in all situations. I walk with the confidence of your presence. I am never alone and never helpless, for the angels of the Lord are always attending to me and making life beautiful for me. Hallelujah!
