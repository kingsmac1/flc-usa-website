title: Guard Your Mouth
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:3 KJV
verse: 'Talk no more so exceedingly proudly; let not arrogance come out of your mouth: for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him, actions are weighed.'
body:
  - 'The way we speak often reveals what is happening inside our hearts. Jesus explains this clearly: “For out of the abundance of the heart, the mouth speaketh” (Matthew 12:34, KJV). Arrogant speech is never random; it shows that pride has begun to influence one''s thoughts and attitudes. That is why God warns us not to let proud or harsh words leave our mouths. He listens to our words, but He also examines the intentions behind them.'
  - As believers, our speech should express the character and gentleness of Christ. The Apostle Paul said in Colossians 4:6 (KJV), “Let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt”. Jesus also calls us “the salt of the earth” in Matthew 5:13, KJV). Salt brings out flavor and adds strength to whatever it touches. In the same way, our words should add value wherever we speak, bringing clarity, peace, and encouragement. When grace guides our speech, we build up others rather than tear them down.
  - The scriptures repeatedly warns us about the consequences of pride. “Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall” (Proverbs 16:18, KJV). The Bible also says, “The bows of the mighty men are broken” in 1 Samuel 2:4, KJV, showing that even strong and capable people collapse when pride controls their decisions. Pride closes the door to wisdom and makes a person ignore warning signs. It leads to mistakes, broken relationships, and unnecessary struggles.
  - Humility, however, opens the heart to God's help. James 4:6, KJV says, “God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble”. A humble heart speaks gently, listens carefully, and responds with understanding. Meekness is not weakness; it is our strength submitted to God. When our hearts are yielded to Him, our words naturally become kinder and wiser. That is why David prayed, “Set a watch, O LORD, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips” (Psalm 141:3, KJV). When God guides our speech, our words bring healing and honor Him. Make up your mind to say only wholesome words that reverences God.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for the cleaning effect of Your word. From this day onwards, my speeches will be filled with grace, and truth. My words will always bring life, peace, and glory to Your name. In Jesus' name, Amen.
