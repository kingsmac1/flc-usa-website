date: '2025-12-12'
title: The Lord Multiplies Your Seed
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:21 (KJV)
verse: And the LORD blessed Hannah, and she conceived, and bare three sons and two daughters. And Hannah did dedicate Elkanah her son… and the LORD visited her.
body:
  - 'Giving to God is more than just a duty; it''s planting a seed that carries life. Every time you give your resources, your time, or your talents to God, you are sowing a seed that will produce fruit. Hannah''s story in 1 Samuel 2:21 shows this clearly: her dedication and faithfulness brought God''s blessing, and Samuel was born. Your seed may seem small, but God takes it and multiplies it beyond what you can imagine.'
  - God doesn't just multiply what you give; He also multiplies the impact of it. Luke 6:38 says, “Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom.” When you sow into God's work, it is an act of faith, and God responds by creating results that go beyond what your eyes can see. Even the smallest seed, when placed in His hands, grows into something mighty.
  - Giving to God is also partnering with Him. Malachi 3:10 shows that He takes your faith-filled actions and turns them into fruit. You may not see the harvest immediately, but God's timing is perfect. Every seed you plant in obedience will produce something worthwhile, whether it's in your finances, your health, your relationships, or your spiritual life.
  - Seeds sown into God's work don't just bring material gain; they bring lasting impact. Your faithfulness can produce peace, favor, joy, and even influence in ways you never expected. Hannah's dedication changed her life, but it also shaped the future of Israel. That's how powerful a seed in God's hand can be.
  - Walk in faith and keep planting. No seed is too small, and no act of obedience is wasted. Trust God to multiply your giving, and watch as it turns into a harvest that blesses you, others, and glorifies His name.
prayer: Father, thank You for always multiplying every seed I sow for You. I also thank you for always providing seed for me to sow. I declare that my givings bear fruit in abundance, brings honor to Your name, blesses my life, and makes impact in the kingdom. I thank you for you are bringing increase beyond what I can imagine. In Jesus' name, Amen.
