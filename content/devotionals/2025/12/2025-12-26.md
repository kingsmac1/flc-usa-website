title: You are a Life-Giver
scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:45 NKJV
verse: The first man Adam became a living being. The last Adam became a life-giving spirit.
body:
  - 'As a child of God, you carry something powerful inside you: the ability to give life. From the memory verse, the Bible explains that the first Adam was a living soul, limited and natural, but Jesus, the second and final Adam, became a life-giving Spirit. He didn''t just receive life; He produced it, released it, and imparted it everywhere He went. The same Holy Spirit who empowered Jesus to pour life into people now lives in you. That means you can bring strength, hope, healing, encouragement, and transformation into any situation.'
  - 'Jesus Himself confirmed this truth in John 14:12 (KJV): “He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do…” Jesus wasn''t exaggerating; He meant it. If the Spirit who made Him a life- giver is now in you, then, you are equipped to do the same works He did on earth. You can speak life into someone''s health, emotions, mind, and circumstances. You can carry God''s presence into places that feel empty or broken.'
  - Think of a real-life moment when someone around you felt discouraged; maybe a friend struggling, a colleague overwhelmed, or even a family member facing sickness or confusion. You didn't need a microphone or spotlight to help. A simple prayer, a Spirit-led word of encouragement, or even your peaceful presence began to lift them. That wasn't just kindness; it was the life-giving Spirit flowing through you. You were releasing God's life into their situation, even if you didn't realize it at the time.
  - The life you carry is contagious. Everywhere Jesus went, things changed; storms calmed, sick bodies recovered, hopeless people found courage again. You are called to walk in the same way. When you enter a room, light enters with you. When you pray, power is released. When you speak God's Word, life flows. You are not ordinary; you are a vessel of the Holy Spirit, and His life is meant to flow through you. So, from today hence forth, make up your mind to give life; speak hope where there is fear and bring peace where there is chaos. Heal the sick, cast out devils, and encourage those who feel broken. You are a life- giver because the life-giving Spirit lives in you. Let the divine life flow freely through you and watch situations begin to change.
prayer: Lord, thank You for making me a life-giver. By the enablement of the Holy Spirit, I give life to situations. I bring healing and hope. My light shine so brightly and your glory is revealed through me always in Jesus' name. Amen!.
