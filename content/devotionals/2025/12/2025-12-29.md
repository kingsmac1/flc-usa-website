date: '2025-12-29'
title: Christ is Your Advantage
scripture: Colossians 1:27 KJV
verse: Christ in you, the hope of glory.
body:
  - You have an advantage in life. It is a divine advantage. Christ Himself is your advantage. You are not ordinary, and you were not saved to live like everyone else. The moment you received Jesus, something supernatural happened. The Holy Spirit came to dwell in you, and with Him came the life, wisdom, and nature of God. “Greater is He that is in you, than he that is in the world” (1 John 4:4 KJV). This means you carry something (Someone) that sets you apart everywhere you go.
  - Because the Holy Spirit lives in you, your story cannot follow the patterns of those who do not have Him. He brings divine insight, divine strength, and divine ability. He empowers you to rise above limitations that others face. Scripture says, “According as His divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and godliness” (2 Peter 1:3 KJV). You are not trying to get an advantage; you already have one through the life of God that works in you.
  - Your case is different. Even in challenging seasons, the presence of the Holy Spirit in you guarantees victory. You cannot be disadvantaged because God causes all things to
  - work in your favor. When people say things are down, your testimony remains different. When others see impossibilities, you see opportunities. “If God be for us, who can be against us?” (Romans 8:31, KJV). Christ in you positions you above defeat, above fear, and above the limitations of this world.
  - 'You are not a believer for nothing. You have divine help. You walk with divine wisdom. You function with divine strength. The Holy Spirit in you makes your path brighter, your decisions clearer, and your life victorious. Always remember: Christ is not merely with you; He is in you, and that reality makes all the difference.'
prayer: Father, thank You for the advantage I have in Christ. I declare that the greater One lives in me. I walk in wisdom, strength, favor, and victory. I can never be disadvantaged, for Christ in me is my hope of glory. Thank you for your continuous guidance, for strengthening my spirit, and for letting Your light shine through my life daily. In Jesus' name, Amen.
