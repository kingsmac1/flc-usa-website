title: Positioned for a Lack-free Life
scripture: Psalm 23:1 KJV
verse: The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.
body:
  - You are positioned by God for a lack-free life. When the Bible says, “The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want,” it is not a poetic expression; it is a divine guarantee. A shepherd provides, guides, and protects. When God calls Himself your Shepherd, He announces that you will never lack what you truly need. He leads you into sufficiency, not scarcity.
  - David continues, “He maketh me to lie down in green pastures” (Psalm 23:2, KJV). Sheep do not lie down unless they are fully fed and completely safe. The imagery of lying down in green pastures speaks of rest, abundance, satisfaction, and overflowing supply. It means God has already gone ahead of you to provide everything required for your journey. You are not designed to struggle through life; you are called to live out of divine provision.
  - The Lord is your source and your supplier. He is not depending on the economy, people, or circumstances to meet your needs. Scripture says, “My God shall supply all your need according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus” (Philippians 4:19, KJV).
  - This supply is not limited; it is drawn from His “riches in glory,” meaning endless spiritual and earthly resources are available to you through Christ.
  - 'All you ever need for the good life has already been made available in Him. The Bible declares, “The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger: but they that seek the LORD shall not want any good thing” (Psalm 34:10, KJV). Lack is not your covenant. Scarcity is not your identity. You are a child of the King, and your inheritance is abundance.'
  - Therefore, stop the lack mentality. Consciously reposition your thoughts from shortage to supply. You walk with the One who owns all. Every day, declare, “The Lord is my Shepherd. I cannot lack.” Christ has positioned you to live above fear, above shortage, and above worldly limits. Your case is different because your Shepherd is divine.
prayer: Father, thank You for positioning me for a lack-free, abundant life. You are my Shepherd and my source. I reject every mentality of lack and embrace the reality of Your supply. I walk in provision, peace, and supernatural abundance. In Jesus' name, Amen!
