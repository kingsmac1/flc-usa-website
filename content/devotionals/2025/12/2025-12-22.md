date: '2025-12-22'
title: Fulfillment is Coming
scripture: Habakkuk 2:3
verse: For the vision is yet for an appointed time… though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry.
body:
  - Fulfillment is coming. Everything God has spoken concerning you is approaching its appointed moment. You have waited, prayed, believed and, at times, wondered when the promise would finally manifest. But God is not slow. He works by timing, alignment, and divine preparation. Thessalonians 5:24 (KJV) says, “Faithful is He that calleth you, who also will do it”. The God who spoke is the God who performs.
  - Sometimes, the waiting season feels long. You hold on to prophecy, yet it looks like nothing is moving. You hear testimonies from others and wonder when yours will come. But waiting is never wasted with God. He uses it to build strength, maturity, and capacity for what He is about to release. The psalmist said, “Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God” (Psalm 87:3, KJV). What God has spoken about you is glorious, and glorious things require preparation.
  - 'There is a kingdom principle found in Ecclesiastes: “If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth” (Ecclesiastes 11:3, KJV). Your cloud has been filling. Every prayer, every seed, every act of obedience, every moment you held your peace, every tear you cried in faith has all been adding weight to your cloud. Now, it is saturated, and it is about to empty itself over your life.'
  - God is shifting things for your sake. Delays are breaking. Doors are opening. Favor is being released. What looked impossible will unfold with divine ease. The waiting season is coming to an end, and the season of fulfillment is beginning. This is your time to rejoice and thank God ahead of the manifestation. Anticipate the rain because it will fall.
prayer: Father, thank You for the season of fulfillment that is coming into my life. Strengthen my faith as I wait. I receive every word You have spoken, and I position myself for manifestation. I thank You that every cloud of blessing will empty itself in Your perfect timing. In Jesus' name, Amen.
