title: Show Gratitude
scripture: 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (KJV)
verse: 'In everything, give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.'
body:
  - Gratitude is a powerful attitude that transforms how we see life. It shifts our focus from what we lack to what we already have, and it opens our hearts to God's goodness. Showing gratitude is more than saying “thank you”; it is a posture of the heart that acknowledges God as the source of every blessing. When we live thankfully, we keep our faith strong and our spirits joyful, no matter what challenges we face.
  - 'A thankful heart pleases God. In Scripture, we see that Jesus Himself gave thanks often: before feeding the multitudes, before raising Lazarus, and even before going to the cross. Gratitude strengthens our trust in God''s faithfulness, reminding us that He is always working for our good. When we give thanks in all things, we declare that God is greater than our circumstances. The psalmist wrote, “O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth forever” (Psalm 107:1).'
  - 'Gratitude also changes our perspective. It helps us see the hidden blessings in ordinary moments: the gift of life, the love of family, the kindness of others, and the peace that comes from knowing Christ. When we stop to thank God daily, worry begins'
  - to fade, and contentment grows. Thankfulness is like a light that dispels the darkness of complaint and negativity.
  - Furthermore, expressing gratitude blesses others. A simple “thank you” can lift someone's spirit and remind them that their efforts matter. Gratitude builds stronger relationships and fosters a spirit of encouragement in our homes, workplaces, and communities. The Bible says, “And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him” (Colossians 3:17). Living this way brings honor to God and joy to those around us.
  - 'So, let gratitude be your daily language. Thank God for what He has done, what He is doing, and what He will do. Thank people for their kindness and love. A grateful heart attracts blessings and keeps you connected to the source of all good things: your Heavenly Father.'
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for filling My heart with gratitude. I thank you for and in every situation because you are faithful and good. I live with a thankful spirit, and His joy and peace overflow in my life daily.
