title: Your Rights in Christ
scripture: Romans 8:17 KJV
verse: And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ.
body:
  - As a Christian, you are not ordinary. The day you received Christ, you stepped into a new kingdom with rights, privileges, and spiritual authority. You are not begging God for favors; you are walking in an inheritance that already belongs to you. Scripture says, “Therefore, if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature” (2 Corinthians 5:17, KJV). As a new creation, you function from a different realm, with heavenly rights that govern your life on earth.
  - One of your greatest rights is access to God's presence. You do not come as a stranger or a servant, but as a son. The Word declares, “Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace” (Hebrews 4:16, KJV). You have the right to pray confidently, to receive mercy, to obtain help, and to stand in God's presence without fear or guilt. Bold access is not arrogance; it is your covenant privilege as a child of God.
  - You also have the right to live in victory. Christ did not save you so you could struggle through life defeated and helpless. The Bible says, “Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him that loved us” (Romans 8:37, KJV). Victory is not something you seek; it is something you walk in. Through Christ, you have the right to win spiritually, mentally,
  - emotionally, and physically. You overcome not by your strength, but by the authority of the One who lives inside you.
  - You have the right to divine provision, divine health, divine wisdom, and divine protection. These are not wishes; they are divine truths established by the finished work of Christ. You have the right to speak God's Word, enforce your authority, and walk in the freedom Jesus paid for. Never let life, fear, or circumstances talk you out of what Christ has given you. You are an heir of God; start living with that consciousness.
prayer: Father, thank You for the rights and privileges I have in Christ. I walk in boldness, victory, wisdom, and divine authority. I refuse fear, lack, and defeat. I live as your child in Your Kingdom, fully aware of my inheritance. In Jesus' name, Amen.
