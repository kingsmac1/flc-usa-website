title: Heirs of Salvation
scripture: Romans 8:17 (KJV)
verse: And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.
body:
  - As a believer, you are more than saved; you are an heir of salvation. Being an heir means you have been given a rightful share in all that belongs to God through Christ. It is not earned by your effort, but granted by grace. Your new identity as a child of God gives you access to His love, guidance, and blessings. Knowing this truth changes the way you view life because you are no longer a stranger to God's family; you are part of His household, with all the rights and privileges of a son or daughter.
  - Romans 8:17 (KJV) tells us, “And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.” This verse shows that being an heir is both a privilege and a responsibility. It means sharing in Christ's glory and trusting God through life's challenges. Even in difficulties, your identity in Him remains secure. Your inheritance is guaranteed, and your life has purpose because it is anchored in God's eternal plan.
  - 'Galatians 4:7 (KJV) adds clarity: “Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.” This reminds us that salvation does more than forgive; it gives you a family, a new position, and a lasting hope. As an heir, you are called to live confidently, knowing that God''s provision and protection are always available. Your life reflects the inheritance you hold, not in worldly riches, but in eternal blessings and spiritual security.'
  - Understanding your identity as an heir transforms your perspective. You are not defined by circumstances, setbacks, or past mistakes. Instead, your focus is on God's word, your relationship with Him, and the eternal reward that awaits you. Living with this awareness encourages faith, patience, and gratitude, even in the face of challenges.
prayer: Lord, thank You for making me an heir of Your salvation. I walk confidently in my identity as Your child, trusting Your guidance and provision. I'm strengthened and my steps are firm You. I walk in super abundance, victory, success and greatness every day of my life.
