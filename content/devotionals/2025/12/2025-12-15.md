date: '2025-12-15'
title: You are Never Confused
scripture: 1 Corinthians 14:33 (KJV)
verse: For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace…
body:
  - Confusion is not in the nature of God you received as new creation. Confusion is not your identity. You are not meant to feel lost, overwhelmed, or mentally scattered. You have a sound mind. Confusion doesn't come from God, and because the Spirit lives in you, divine understanding and peace are your right. The Holy Spirit is your inner guide and teacher. He brings understanding, not uncertainty. Even when life feels complicated, you are never without direction. God has equipped you with wisdom from within.
  - 1 Corinthians 14:33 (KJV) says, “For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace…” That means anytime confusion tries to cloud your mind, you can reject it confidently. God's nature is peace, and since you belong to Him, peace is your inheritance. Think about moments when you didn't know what to do; yet, somehow, clarity came. That wasn't luck; it was God working in you.
  - John 16:13 (NKJV), says “However, when He, the Spirit of truth, has come, He will guide you into all truth…” The Holy Spirit guides you step by step. You may not always see the full picture, but He gives you the right thought, the right direction, or the right
  - understanding at the right time.
  - Maybe you've experienced this personally. Perhaps you were faced with an important decision, whether about school, work, a relationship, or a financial choice, and you felt stuck, unsure of what to do. After praying or simply quieting your mind, a sense of direction began to form. Maybe God brought a Scripture to your heart, or you suddenly felt peace about one option and uneasiness about another. That gentle clarity was the Holy Spirit guiding you. What felt confusing at first became clear because you are not left to figure out life on your own.
  - So, walk with confidence. When your mind feels pressured, slow down and invite the Holy Spirit to give you clarity. You are not designed to live confused. You have the wisdom of God in you. Even when things seem uncertain, you are not lost; you are guided. Your mind is guarded by God's peace and your steps are directed by His Spirit.
prayer: Holy Spirit, thank You for guiding me into all truth. I reject every form of confusion and obscurity and embrace Your peace and guidance. My mind is steady, my mind is sound, my decisions are guided by the Holy Spirit, and I walk in confidence because You lead me every day. Amen.
