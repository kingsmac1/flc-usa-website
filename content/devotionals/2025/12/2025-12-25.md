title: Jesus is the Reason For the Season
scripture: Luke 2:11 (KJV)
verse: For unto you is born this day in the city of David, a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.
body:
  - 'During Christmas season, it''s easy to get caught up in the activities: decorations, gifts, gatherings, and celebrations. These things are enjoyable, but they are not the heart of Christmas. Christmas is about Jesus. Everything else is secondary. The entire season exists because a Savior was born. Without Him, there would be no celebration, no hope, no redemption. So, as you move through this season, remind yourself that Jesus is the reason for it all.'
  - The Bible says in Matthew 1:21 (NKJV), “And she will bring forth a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins.” That is the true story of Christmas; God stepped into the world in human form to save humanity. The world may focus on the lights, the food, the trees, and the gifts, but you must keep your heart focused on the One who came to give you eternal life. Jesus didn't come to add another holiday to the calendar; He came to restore your relationship with God.
  - 'John 3:16 (KJV) tells you why this matters: “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son…” Christmas is God''s love in action. It''s His announcement to you that you are valued, chosen, and deeply loved. This season isn''t about what you can buy or how beautifully you decorate; it''s about recognizing the greatest gift ever given. Jesus is the centerpiece of Christmas, and your celebration becomes richer when He remains at the center. You may remember moments when the season felt overwhelming; planning events, shopping for gifts, or trying to meet expectations. Maybe you felt pressure to make everything perfect. But when you pause and refocus on Jesus, everything shifts. Peace replaces stress. gratitude replaces pressure. Instead of rushing, you find yourself celebrating with meaning. When Jesus becomes your focus, the season becomes deeper, calmer, and more joyful.'
  - 'So, celebrate Jesus. Celebrate His birth, His love, His sacrifice, and His presence in your life. Enjoy the gatherings, the food, and the gifts, but let your heart remain anchored in the true reason for the season. This Christmas, let your worship be louder than the festivities. Let your gratitude shine brighter than the lights. Remember: Jesus is the reason for the season.'
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for sending your son, Jesus, into the world to save me and the entire humanity. As I celebrate this season, my heart stays focused on You. I will walk with this consciousness and let everything I do reflect gratitude for Your love. You are the reason for the season, and I honor You today and always. Amen.
