title: How to Find Favour with God and Man
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:26 (KJV)
verse: And the child Samuel grew on, and was in favor both with the Lord, and also with men.
body:
  - Finding favor with God and with people is a profound blessing, but Scripture shows that it begins with our relationship with God. To find favor means to receive approval, delight, kindness, and divine help. When God is pleased with a person, His favor rests upon them, guiding their steps and opening doors that no human effort could achieve. Favor is not luck; it is the visible evidence of God's hand at work in a believer's life.
  - One of the key principles for walking in God's favor is the law of honor. Honor means valuing God above all else; His Word, His instructions, His presence, and His ways. When you honor God in your decisions, your lifestyle, and your daily choices, His favor naturally flows toward you. God Himself said, “Them that honour me I will honour” (1 Samuel 2:30, KJV). Honor aligns your life with God's heart, and where alignment exists, His favor follows.
  - Many people spend their energy trying to win the approval of others, hoping that human favor will lift them. While relationships matter, Scripture teaches us not to chase human
  - validation. True favor with people is a by-product, not a pursuit. When God favors you, people are moved (sometimes, unexpectedly) to support, assist, and bless you. Divine favor influences human hearts in ways you could never orchestrate on your own.
  - At the same time, honoring God does not mean dishonoring people. Honor extends to how we treat others; our humility, integrity, respect, and kindness. However, the honor we show to people must never come at the cost of displeasing God. When we value God first and value people rightly, we position ourselves to walk in balanced, abundant favor both vertically (with God) and horizontally (with people). This harmony is what Samuel, David, Daniel, and even Jesus demonstrated throughout their lives.
  - As you walk into this season, let your heart be anchored in honor. Seek to please God above all else, treat people with grace, and trust the Lord to surround you with favor as with a shield. When your life reflects honor, God's favor becomes your testimony.
prayer: Lord, I thank You for Your grace and mercy. I'm divinely enabled to walk in honor toward You and in love toward people. Your favor rests upon my life in a powerful and undeniable way. I declare that I will experience uncommon favor with God and with man in this season, in Jesus' name. Amen.
