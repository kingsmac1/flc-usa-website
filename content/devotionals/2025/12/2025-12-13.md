title: Value Prophetic Pronouncement
scripture: 1 Samuel 1:17 (KJV)
verse: 'Then Eli answered and said, Go in peace: and the God of Israel grant thee thy petition that thou hast asked of him.'
body:
  - In the Bible, God often used the words of His servants to bring comfort, direction, and change into people's lives. Hannah's story shows this clearly. She entered the house of God with a heavy heart, but everything shifted when Eli spoke a prophetic blessing over her. That simple declaration, “Go in peace”, became the turning point God used to respond to her prayers. Later in 1 Samuel 2:20–21, the blessing grew even further, proving that prophetic pronouncements carry weight when spoken under God's leading.
  - Today, many believers hear a man of God say, “God bless you,” yet treat it like a casual greeting or a polite expression. When it is viewed that way, the spiritual value behind those words is lost. But when you receive those same words with honor and with faith, believing that God truly intends to release something into your life, things begin to shift. Doors open, help comes, and situations turn in your favor. What you treat as ordinary cannot help you, but what you receive with reverence has the power to lift you.
  - God often chooses to use His servants as channels through which He speaks. This is not a new idea; it is seen throughout Scripture. 2 Chronicles 20:20 teaches that believing in God strengthens your foundation, and believing His prophets brings progress. When a prophetic declaration is released, God is presenting an opportunity for something new to happen. If you dismiss or ignore the words spoken over you, you may miss something God intended for your good. But when you treasure those words, you make room for God to bring them to pass.
  - 'Honoring prophetic pronouncements is not about elevating a human being above God. It is recognizing that God uses people to carry His message. Over and over in Scripture, those who treated prophetic words with respect saw God''s hand move powerfully in their lives. Whether it was Hannah, the Shunammite woman, Peter, or many others, the pattern is the same: they valued the word, and the blessing followed.'
  - As you continue in your journey of faith, keep your heart open to what God speaks through His servants. Treat every genuine prophetic pronouncement with seriousness, humility, and expectation. When you do, you position yourself to experience what God has spoken, and your life reflects His goodness in ways you could never produce on your own.
