title: God is your Provider
scripture: Genesis 22:14 KJV
verse: 'And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovah Jireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of the LORD it shall be seen.'
body:
  - Over the years, I have heard people refer to the person who caters to the needs of the family as the breadwinner. That always sounded funny to me because it stems from ignorance. God is your provider. The moment you realize this, your trust will be in God, and He will always provide for you using several means and people.
  - From Genesis 22:14, we see Abraham use the word Jehovah Jireh, which means “God will provide.” This shows that he trusted God. You too can trust God to provide for you regardless of the pressure, even if it is at the last moment. God is your Father. He is your provider and sustainer, and it is His responsibility to supply all your needs.
  - Philippians 4:19 AMP says, “And my God will supply (fill until full) your every need according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.” Are you in need of anything today? Simply ask God, and He will be delighted to provide for you and meet your needs. Ephesians 3:20 KJV tells us, “Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us.”
prayer: Thank you, Heavenly Father, for always meeting my needs and granting my desires. Thank you for providing all that I need that pertains to life and godliness. Unto you be glory and praise forever, in Jesus' name, Amen.
