title: Pray for the Unsaved
scripture: John 17:3 KJV
verse: And this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.
body:
  - In Genesis 19:24, Sodom and Gomorrah were destroyed because of their iniquity. The inhabitants of these cities did not know God, and they wallowed in sin. Lot lived there, but he was unable to win them to the Lord. When destruction came, he was affected because he lost his wife, became displaced, and ended up in a cave, losing all his possessions.
  - God does not want anyone to have the same experience. Rather, He wants us to intercede for the unsaved in our neighborhoods, cities, and nations. Pray for them that they may receive eternal life and “know the only true God, and Jesus Christ” (John 17:3 KJV). God is not willing that any sinner should perish; instead, He wants them to repent (2 Peter 3:9).
  - Many unbelievers desire to know God and accept Jesus, but the enemy has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts (John 12:40). This is one of the strategies the devil uses to stop them from accepting the truth of the gospel. Without prayer, you can preach to them from morning to night without making an impact. It is in the place of prayer that you table them before God for Him to open their hearts and deliver them from the bondage of the
  - enemy. Take this time to pray for a family member, friend, or anyone you know who is yet to be saved.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for Your love for everyone and Your desire for all to be saved. I pray for the unsaved that they may be loosed from the bondage of Satan. Grant them a new heart so they can receive eternal life and live to serve You all the days of their lives. In Jesus' name, Amen.
