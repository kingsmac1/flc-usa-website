title: Have you Found Jesus?
scripture: Matthew 2:12 KJV
verse: And being warned of God in a dream that they should not return to Herod, they departed into their own country another way.
body:
  - m atthew 2:1-12 reveals how the Magi, also known as wise men, searched for Jesus when He was born. When they found Jesus, they worshiped Him and presented gifts to Him. As they were about to depart, God warned them in a dream not to return to Herod, and they obeyed the Lord. The key emphasis here is yielding to the Lord.
  - What has happened to you since you got saved? Have you been yielding to the Lord since you found Him? When the wise men found Jesus, they took a different direction. Finding Jesus might mean that your life could take a new direction. Your desires and aspirations might need to align with His purpose for your life.
  - 'In the book of Acts, “As he journeyed, he came near Damascus: and suddenly there shined round about him a light from heaven” – Acts 9:3 KJV. Saul, who was on a mission to persecute Christians, encountered Jesus, and the Lord told him, “Arise, and go into the city, and it shall be told thee what thou must do” – Acts 9:6 KJV. Saul''s life and assignment took a new direction for the purpose of the Master.'
  - Finding Jesus requires being open to His word and leadership. In what ways has Jesus affected the direction of your life? Jesus is the Lord of your life, and the word "Lord" means Master. As your Master, cultivate the attitude of yielding to Him, His purposes, and His directions.
prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You for being the Master of my life. My heart and my whole being will always yield to Your leadership and direction. I will trust You at all times. In Jesus' name, amen.
