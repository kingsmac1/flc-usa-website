title: True Worship
scripture: John 4:23 AMP
verse: But a time is coming and is already here when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit (from the heart, the inner self) and in truth; for the Father seeks such people to be His worshipers.
body:
  - j ohn 4:24 explains that God is a Spirit, and He created man to have a spirit, soul, and body. The spirit of man enables him to communicate with God. Your spirit was designed to interact with God, and true worship must come from the spirit - not from the flesh (body) or the soul. This means worship must be spiritual.
  - Many have thought that the "spirit" in John 4:24 refers to the Holy Spirit. However, it refers to the human spirit. This is the part of man's being where he bears the image of God and where the Holy Spirit dwells ("Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities..." – Romans 8:26 KJV).
  - It is vital to understand that worship in spirit is different from mere physical rituals, sacraments, or routines such as postures, washings, or recitations. Worshiping God in truth means doing so with revelation and understanding of who He is. God is eternal and sovereign. He is the Almighty, deserving of all glory and honor.
  - Understanding His majesty and glory transforms how we
  - 'worship him. His word is truth ("Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth." – John 17:17 KJV). As you study the Word of God, you will gain deeper insight into who God is. This understanding will help you worship Him in spirit and in truth.'
prayer: Thank You, Father, for giving me an understanding of who You are and the kind of worship You expect from me. I will always worship You in spirit and in truth. As I continue to grow in Your Word, I will become more effective in offering You the worship You deserve. In Jesus' name, amen.
