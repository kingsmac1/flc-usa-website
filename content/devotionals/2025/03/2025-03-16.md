title: Intimacy with God
scripture: Psalm 84:2 AMP
verse: My soul (my life, my inner self) longs for and greatly desires the courts of the LORD; My heart and my flesh sing for joy to the living God.
body:
  - Intimacy is a deeper form of friendship. It is not a casual or surface-level relationship. God desires us to have an intimate relationship with Him. Have you ever noticed moments during your prayer time when, just as you are about to end, you feel prompted to continue? At such times, do not stop. Continue the fellowship because God is enjoying the connection with you.
  - Rick Warren once said, "There is nothing, absolutely nothing, more important than developing friendship with God." This highlights the essence of intimacy with God. David understood this deeply. His passion for intimacy with God was his priority. That is why he said in Psalm 84:2 AMP, "My soul (my life, my inner self) longs for and greatly desires the courts of the LORD."
  - In 1 Samuel 13:14, God called David a man after His own heart. David knew how to please God, and you can become a person after God's own heart too. Develop intimacy with Him, and He will be pleased with you. This deep connection will prompt God to manifest Himself in your life in special ways.
  - John 14:23 NIV says, "Jesus replied, 'Anyone who loves me will obey my teaching. My Father will love them, and we will come to them and make our home with them.'” When you build intimacy with God, He will dwell with you, and your life will reflect His presence.
prayer: Father, thank You, for in You I live, move, and have my being. Strengthen me to know You and the power of Your resurrection. My desire for You will continually grow. In Jesus' name, Amen.
