date: '2025-03-20'
title: Renewal of Strength
scripture: Isaiah 40:31 KJV
verse: But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.
body:
  - a man or woman who has not waited upon the Lord does not know (by experience) what divine strength is. Renewal of strength comes from God and not from motivational speeches. While waiting upon the Lord, you will mount up with wings as an eagle. There are different kinds of birds, but an eagle is used here because it is special. It is a high flier and has the ability to soar above the turbulence of the wind.
  - 'To wait upon the Lord is to hope and trust in Him. It requires your faith, patience, humility, and endurance regardless of what comes your way, and it also requires acting on the Word of God (James 1:22). As you wait on the Lord, you will have to encourage yourself with His Word. Psalm 27:14 says, “Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: Wait, I say, on the LORD.”'
  - When you wait on the Lord, you will receive strength in your finances, body, mind, and spirit, as well as in all aspects of your life. Suddenly, you will have strength to overcome the things that once limited you. Renewal of strength comes with the ability
  - to withstand and resist the adversary and the pressures of life. In Genesis 49:24, God strengthened Joseph's arms. His arms were made agile against the arrows and pressures of his enemies. He remained strong and did not have to fight back.
  - If you want to receive strength and fly above your dissatisfaction and troubles, then you must wait upon the Lord. Psalm 18:32 KJV says, “It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.”
prayer: Thank You, Father, for giving me the opportunity to renew my strength through waiting on You. Help me to wait upon You the right way. Equip me with strength, guide me rightly, and enlarge my capacity. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Psalm 18:32 KJV, Psalm 28:7 KJV, Micah 7:7 KJV, Habakkuk 3:19 KJV
