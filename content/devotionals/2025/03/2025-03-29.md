date: '2025-03-29'
title: Expect Persecution
scripture: Matthew 5:11 NKJV
verse: Blessed are you when they revile and persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely for My sake.
body:
  - As a believer in Jesus Christ, you should expect persecution. The persecution is not meant to tear you down because you are never alone. God is with you, even in the midst of the persecution. Rejoice, because the Bible calls you “blessed” when people revile and persecute you for the sake of the kingdom of God and your faith in Him (Matthew 5:11).
  - Jesus performed diverse kinds of miracles because He had compassion on the people afflicted by the devil. Yet, the Pharisees accused Him of blasphemy, disrespecting religious values, forming friendships with sinners, and using satanic powers to cast out demons (Matthew 9:34). They did not have sufficient evidence against Jesus, but they politically charged Him to death.
  - 'In the days to come, there will be an uprising against the body of Christ and those who believe in the name of our God. There will be governmental policies designed to persecute Christians. But I say to you: rejoice and be glad, for blessed are you. Matthew 5:12 says it is an honor to be persecuted for the Lord: “Rejoice'
  - and be glad, because great is your reward in heaven.”
  - Similarly, Philippians 1:29 KJV reminds us, “For unto you it is given in the behalf of Christ, not only to believe on Him, but also to suffer for His sake.” You are not only a believer but also called to partake in His sufferings. Do not turn back, do not hide, and do not stop declaring your belief in Jesus.
prayer: My belief in Jesus will always remain firm, even in the midst of persecutions. I am courageous and full of strength as I face diverse trials. I thank You, Lord, because You are with me, and greater is He that is in me than he that is in the world. Hallelujah!
