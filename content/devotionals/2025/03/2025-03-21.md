title: How to Wait upon the Lord
scripture: Isaiah 40:31 KJV
verse: But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.
body:
  - 'Do you desire a positive change in your relationship with God, your health, family, ministry, career, or any aspect of your life? If your answer is yes, then the solution is to wait upon the Lord. God knows that a time will come in your life when you will be dissatisfied with your current state and need a change. Your current situation might be discouraging, but Psalm 27:14 KJV says, “Wait on the LORD: be strong, and let thine heart take courage; yea, wait thou on the LORD.”'
  - 'Many people desire to wait on the Lord, but they are unsure of how to go about it. Here are ways to wait upon the Lord:'
  - Firstly, trust in the Lord. This is the same as having faith in God. God wants you to come to the place where your trust and confidence are in Him. He needs your absolute dependence. Hebrews 11:8-10 TLB tells us that Abraham trusted God.
  - Secondly, pray and wait. Lamentations 3:25 KJV says, “The LORD is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.” Pray and wait. Keep praying and keep waiting
  - until you see the change you desire.
  - Thirdly, study the Bible always. Studying the Bible will help you maintain focus, strengthen your faith (Romans 10:17), and become transformed (2 Corinthians 3:18).
  - Finally, fasting. Fasting positions you to receive from God, and there are some situations that will not change except through fasting. Matthew 17:21 says this clearly. I will teach more about fasting soon.
prayer: My strength is renewed daily as I wait upon the Lord. I develop the ability to fly above the turbulence and pressures of life. Hallelujah!
