date: '2025-03-05'
title: Be Diligent
scripture: Proverbs 22:29 KJV
verse: Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.
body:
  - t o be diligent means to show care and meticulous attention in your work or duties. It involves putting in your best effort in any task or assignment given to you. Even if you realize your effort is not good enough, strive to acquire new skills to improve your performance.
  - Diligence applies not only to satisfying your earthly master or boss but also to serving God, your ultimate Master. As you show diligence in your physical responsibilities, also be diligent in your prayer life and dealings with the Lord. Do not settle for less or remain at the baseline.
  - I once heard the story of a young boy whose performance in school was poor. He scored 40%, and when questioned by his parents, he replied that others also scored the same. This is not a good place to be - encouraging yourself with poor performance just because others did the same.
  - From our opening verse, we learn that the services of a diligent person will be sought after by kings, presidents and royalties, and not by commoners. When you are diligent, great people will
  - seek you out. Proverbs 21:5 says, "The plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance and advantage, but everyone who acts in haste comes surely to poverty." This means the diligent person will have abundance, wealth, and profit, while one who does things carelessly will end in poverty. Make up your mind to be diligent today.
prayer: I have an excellent spirit within me, enabling me to be diligent, unique, and to produce works of excellence. I refuse to settle for less. Kings and great men seek my services always. I will never be average. In Jesus' name, amen.
