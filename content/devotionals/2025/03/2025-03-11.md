date: '2025-03-11'
title: Glorify God in your Body
scripture: 1 Corinthians 6:19 NIV
verse: Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own.
body:
  - a s a believer, your body is God's dwelling place through the Holy Spirit. You are not your own; your body belongs to God. For this reason, you cannot treat your body however you want.
  - 'Think of it this way: if your boss gave you $500 USD for a specific task, you would not use the money for something unrelated. Similarly, your body, which belongs to God, must be used only in ways that glorify Him. "You were bought with a price (you were actually purchased with the precious blood of Jesus and made His own). So then honor and glorify God with your body" (1 Corinthians 6:20 AMP).'
  - Honor God by using your body in ways that please Him. Avoid actions that dishonor your body, such as sexual immorality, drunkenness, smoking, and gluttony. These activities do not glorify God and can destroy your body. "Flee from sexual immorality. All other sins a person commits are outside the body, but whoever sins sexually sins against their own body" (1 Corinthians 6:18 AV).
  - It is important to refrain from things that defile your body. Some believers engage in inappropriate conversations, visit ungodly places, or participate in evil actions. Even consuming worldly and ungodly content, whether by watching, listening, or reading, misuses your body. These actions do not glorify God.
  - If you did not know this before now, God has given you an opportunity to repent. "And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth all men everywhere to repent" (Acts 17:30 KJV). Since your body has been bought at a price, glorify God in everything you do with your body.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for making me a temple where you dwell. I am grateful for your Lordship over my life. I commit to glorifying you in my body at all times. Through your Spirit, I will mortify the deeds of my body and subject it to your will. In Jesus' name, amen. FURTHER
