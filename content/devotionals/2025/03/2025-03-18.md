title: Developing Intimacy with God
scripture: John 14:23 AMPL
verse: Jesus answered, "If anyone (really) loves Me, he will keep My word (teaching), and My Father will love him, and We will come to him and make Our dwelling place with him.
body:
  - 'From John 14:23 AMPL, we need to pay attention to two key clauses: “My Father will love him” and “make Our dwelling place with him.” John 3:16 reveals to us that God loves us, and 1 Corinthians 6:19 also reveals that God dwells in us. Jesus'' statement in our opening verse shows there is a deeper level of love called intimacy and a higher degree of His manifestation in the life of anyone who keeps God''s word. This is because there are believers who do not keep God''s word, but that does not mean they are not loved by God or that He does not dwell in them.'
  - God wants to fully express Himself and manifest Himself mightily in a believer's life. The only way this can take place is when you develop an intimate relationship with Him, and this begins when you have a strong desire to know God more. This desire will stir you to start seeking God. Anyone who seeks the Lord will find Him (Matthew 7:7).
  - Seeking the Lord involves anything you do that helps you develop an intimate relationship with Him. Your relationship with
  - God is as close as you choose it to be. Intimacy is not accidental; it is your personal choice, and you must decide to be intentional about it. Spending quality time studying God's word, praying, fasting, fellowshipping with other believers, and yielding to the Holy Spirit will help you develop intimacy with God. Decide today to seek Him, and He will reveal Himself to you.
prayer: Dear Father, I want to know You intimately. Reveal Yourself to me. Stir up in me an unquenchable hunger for the things that concern You. Guide me to walk in Your preordained pathway for my life. In Jesus' name, Amen.
