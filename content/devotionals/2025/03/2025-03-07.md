title: Be Kind to Others
scripture: 1 Samuel 20:14 NIV
verse: But show me unfailing kindness like the LORD's kindness as long as I live, so that I may not be killed.
body:
  - k indness is an attitude marked by acts of generosity, compassion, consideration, and rendering help to others without expecting praise or reward in return. From 1 Samuel 20:14, God's kindness towards us is unfailing. He also expects us to show an unfailing kindness to others.
  - Acts of kindness are not only in actions but also in words. "Anxiety in a man's heart weighs him down, but a good word makes him glad." – Proverbs 12:25. When you say kind words to people, it uplifts them, makes them glad, and gives them hope. Simple kind words like "You look beautiful," "I love you," "Thank you," and "God bless you" can go a long way to cheer someone up. Supporting others, offering gifts, and performing acts of service are other ways to show kindness.
  - 2 Timothy 2:24 AM says “And the Lord's servant must not be quarrelsome but must be kind to everyone, able to teach, not resentful.” Be kind to everyone. Do not limit your kindness to only family members, friends, or well-wishers. Kindness is a generous act, so extend it even to those who have not treated
  - you well. This act of kindness can win their hearts for the Lord.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for your nature in me. Thank you for your love shed abroad in my heart by your Spirit. I will love everyone and show kindness to others through my words and actions. In Jesus' name, amen.
