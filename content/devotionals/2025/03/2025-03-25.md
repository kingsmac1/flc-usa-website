title: Have you Tried Fasting?
scripture: Matthew 17:21
verse: Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.
body:
  - From Matthew 17:21, we find a rule to engage stubborn situations and forces. Fasting is a spiritual exercise and plays a significant role in your walk with God. It invites heavenly interference in earthly matters. You do not necessarily need to fast to have an angelic visitation or interference, but certain difficult situations can only be handled by angels of higher ranks like the archangels. To provoke certain changes, you need to include fasting in your prayers.
  - In Daniel 10:12-13, the angel sent to deliver answers to Daniel's prayers was intercepted, but because Daniel continued fasting, God had to send Angel Michael to deliver his answers. Angel Michael is a higher rank of angel, an archangel. Daniel was dealing with stubborn forces of darkness. Has your healing or deliverance been delayed for a long time, regardless of your prayers? Try fasting! Add fasting to your prayers and do not stop until you receive your miracle.
  - “Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.” – Ephesians 6:13 KJV. There is definitely an evil day that awaits every believer. The evil day is when the devil shows up to tempt you. It comes with a lot of opposition and pressures.
  - The temptation of Jesus by Satan was His evil day, and Jesus withstood and defeated Satan because He fasted (Matthew 4:1-11). Finally, it is better to add fasting to your prayers in order not to meet the evil day unprepared.
prayer: I declare that I am strengthened in my inner man by the Holy Spirit. I will always keep watch and pray. Thank you, Lord, for always causing me to triumph over every temptation and challenge that shows up. I give myself to fasting and prayers always. Hallelujah!
