date: '2025-03-27'
title: You Are Valuable to God
scripture: Matthew 6:26 NIV
verse: Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?
body:
  - 'You are of great value in God''s eyes. It does not matter what is happening around you right now. In all things, God still values you. You are not worthless. To enjoy the great benefits God has for you, it is important to see yourself differently: see yourself the way God sees you. You are valuable to Him.'
  - '1 John 4:10 says, “This is love: not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as an atoning sacrifice for our sins.” The punishment for sin is death, but because you are valuable to God, He sent His only Son to die in your place.'
  - Not only did Jesus die for you, but God also filled you with the Holy Spirit. This is the very essence of God Himself (Ephesians 1:13). You are worth the blood of Jesus and the indwelling presence of God's Spirit. After you got saved and were filled with the Holy Spirit, God made you His ambassador (2 Corinthians 5:20). He believes in you and has chosen you to represent Him on earth by giving you the ministry of reconciliation, to win more people to His Kingdom.
  - Finally, Jesus is coming back for His church, and you are part of His church (1 Corinthians 15:51-52). Rejoice because you are valuable to God.
prayer: Thank You, Father, for loving me with an everlasting love. I am thankful because You value me so much that You sent Jesus to die for me. Thank You for saving me, filling me with Your Spirit, and making me Your ambassador. I will go about telling everyone of Your love for humanity. In Jesus' name, Amen!
