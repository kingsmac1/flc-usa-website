title: Don't Be Like a Hypocrite
scripture: Matthew 6:1 ESV
verse: Beware of practicing your righteousness before other people in order to be seen by them, for then you will have no reward from your Father who is in heaven.
body:
  - The Strong's Concordance defines a hypocrite as an actor under an assumed character, a stage actor. A hypocrite is a person who pretends to possess attributes they do not genuinely have. Such individuals say or do things to gain approval and praise from people, but their intentions are different. Their actions, words, and beliefs contradict what is in their hearts.
  - In Matthew 23:13-33, you will find Jesus challenging the Pharisees for their hypocrisy. Instead of acknowledging the truth and repenting, they schemed against Jesus, leading to His crucifixion. The Pharisees viewed the law from a legalistic perspective, but Jesus taught them that what matters is the motive of the heart and not outward displays of sanctimonious behavior.
  - In your walk with the Lord, why do you do the things you do? Why do you pray, fast, give, or practice generosity? Proverbs 16:2 GNT says, "You may think everything you do is right, but the Lord judges your motives." Similarly, Proverbs 30:12 KJV states, "There is a generation that are pure in their own eyes,
  - and yet is not washed from their filthiness.
  - As you serve the Lord, always examine your heart to ensure that all you do for Him is out of love, not for selfish purposes or the applause of people.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You for Your word that has come to me today. Help me to serve You with a perfect heart and to worship You in spirit and in truth. Enable me to examine my motives in my dealings with others too. In Jesus' name, Amen.
