title: Earthly Permission for Heavenly Interference
scripture: Matthew 18:18 KJV
verse: 'Verily I say unto you, whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.'
body:
  - n othing just happens on earth. Before anything manifests here, it has already been approved in the spiritual realm. The spiritual realm operates either through the Kingdom of Heaven or the kingdom of darkness. For God to intervene in earthly matters, man must give Him permission because He has made man the custodian of the earth (Genesis 1:26). This permission is called prayer.
  - 'When we pray, we give God the authority to intervene. The New Living Translation renders Matthew 18:18 as: "I tell you the truth, whatever you forbid on earth will be forbidden in heaven, and whatever you permit on earth will be permitted in heaven." From this scripture, we understand that it is man''s responsibility to grant permission for divine intervention.'
  - 'As a believer, you have the power to forbid negative circumstances and permit God''s will in your life through prayer. God watches over the earth, sees what you are going through, and waits for your permission to intervene. Jesus highlighted this truth in Luke 13:16 KJV, saying: "And ought not this'
  - woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?" This woman was afflicted because Satan had bound her, but when she encountered Jesus, He loosed her from the bondage, and she was healed.
  - 'As a believer, you can use the name of Jesus to loose yourself or anyone else from any form of bondage or affliction. Mark 3:27 KJV explains: "No man can enter into a strong man''s house and spoil his goods, except he will first bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house." This teaches us that to address negative situations, you must first bind the devil behind them.'
  - When you bind or forbid Satan in prayer, God receives the permission to act on earth concerning your situation. As you loose or permit God's will, He brings about the desired change. Use the name of Jesus today to effect the change you desire.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for the power of attorney in the name of Jesus. As I pray and decree using the name of Jesus, I grant you the permission to intervene in my situation. Thank you for the resulting change and for making my joy full. In Jesus' name,
