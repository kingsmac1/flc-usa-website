title: Overcoming Temptation
scripture: Romans 8:37 KJV
verse: Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him that loved us.
body:
  - 'Temptations are different from testing. Testing is to prove your faith, while temptations are meant to entice you to disobey God''s will. The sources of temptation are the world, the flesh, and the devil. They usually show up with various kinds of pressure. But one thing is certain: God is faithful, and He will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you can bear, and He will always provide a way of escape that you may endure it.'
  - Everyone faces temptation at various points in life – even Jesus was tempted, yet He overcame the tempter and his temptations. The devil often tempts us with deceptive schemes, so subtle that we might not even realize what is happening. That is why Proverbs 3:5 says, “Trust in the Lord with all thine heart, and lean not unto thine own understanding.”
  - 'God is the One who strengthens you at all times to overcome the tempter (Satan) and his temptations, for we are overcomers in this world (1 John 5:4). One way to overcome temptation is through prayer: “Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.” (Matthew 6:13) Jesus'' prayer reveals the effective way of dealing with temptation. Secondly, rely on the Holy Spirit and'
  - declare God's word to yourself. Declaring God's word strengthens you, and it is one of the ways to resist the devil.
  - James 4:7 says, “Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” No matter the pressures and temptations that come against you, be rest assured and say to yourself, “In all these things, I am more than a conqueror through Jesus Christ.”
prayer: I have been made more than a conqueror in this world. No matter the pressures and enticements of the devil, I will not give in. Greater is He that is in me than the temptations in this world. Thank You, Lord, for You will always deliver me from all temptation, in Jesus' name. Amen.
