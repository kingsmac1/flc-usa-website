  - Some people may fall sick due to complications, mistakes, or carelessness. For example, someone who has been careless with their health and is fond of smoking could develop lung cancer.   - On the other hand, some illnesses are caused by a direct affliction from the devil, with no known cause. For example, a person might wake up with ear pain, and even after medical consultations, the pain persists, leading to deafness despite a healthy lifestyle.
