title: Use the Name of Jesus to Effect Change
scripture: John 14:13-14 ESV
verse: Whatever you ask in my name, this I will do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son. If you ask me anything in my name, I will do it.
body:
  - You can use the name of Jesus to effect the change you desire. Jesus Christ gave us a blank cheque for unlimited withdrawal. The word “whatever” in the opening scripture refers to all things, excluding nothing. Psalm 24:1 says, “The earth is the Lord's and all that dwell therein.” The Lord owns the earth and everything in it, and He is ready to give you all things you request in the name of Jesus, as long as it aligns with His purpose for your life. This is because He will not give you anything that will lead to destruction.
  - Psalm 2:8 KJV says, “Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.” God wants you to ask Him for whatever you need. What area of your life do you need a change? Is it your health, marriage, finances, career, or city? Just make your request to God, and He will grant it to you.
  - 'John 16:24 KJV says, “Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.” Are you worrying and complaining? Have you asked the Lord?'
  - Jesus is saying, “Until now, you have asked me nothing.” Go ahead and make your request to God using the name of Jesus. He will grant your requests so that your joy will be full and that He may be glorified.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for giving us the name of Jesus as a key to effect changes and for always being faithful in answering our prayers. Thank you for the joy that is made full whenever we make requests to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
