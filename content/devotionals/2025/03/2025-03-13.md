title: May Your Kingdom Come!
scripture: Luke 11:2 KJV
verse: And he said unto them, When ye pray, say, Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, as in heaven, so in earth.
body:
  - m ay your kingdom come" is part of the Lord's Prayer. The Lord's Prayer is a model of prayer that Jesus taught His disciples and all future believers. The disciples observed how Jesus prayed and asked Him to teach them how to pray.
  - 'To understand the phrase "May your kingdom come," it is essential to know a bit about kingdoms. There are two kingdoms: the Kingdom of God, also known as the Kingdom of Light, and the kingdom of Satan, also known as the Kingdom of Darkness (Matthew 12:26 KJV).'
  - From the beginning, Satan has rebelled against God, leading to a conflict between the Kingdom of God and the kingdom of Satan. "And from the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force" (Matthew 11:12 KJV).
  - Jesus came to establish God's Kingdom and His government on earth. Before leaving the earth, He gave us the assignment
  - to share the message of God's Kingdom. Those who accept Jesus into their hearts gain access to the Kingdom of God (John 14:12, John 14:36, Romans 10:9 KJV).
  - 'However, Satan continues to fight against God''s Kingdom and His purposes for the earth. This is why Jesus taught us to pray, "May your kingdom come and your will be done on earth." Praying for God''s Kingdom to come invites His rule into the hearts of men and women. It paves the way for His ultimate will (that all men be saved) to be accomplished on earth. As we anticipate Christ''s return, let us pray fervently in this manner: "Even so, come, Lord Jesus" (Revelation 22:20 KJV).'
prayer: Our Father, all honor and praise be to You. Let Your Kingdom come and Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Let Your work prosper on earth, and I pray for the unsaved. May they be freed from the bondage of the devil and come to the knowledge of Your Word and be saved. In Jesus' name, amen.
