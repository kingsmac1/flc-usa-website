title: You Have Self-Control
scripture: Romans 6:14 KJV
verse: 'For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.'
body:
  - d ominion means exercising control or having power over a particular thing or situation. Sin should not control you; rather, you should exercise dominion over all types of sin. Today's article focuses on self-control, a vital behavioral pattern that God desires in your life. God wants you to be in control of yourself - your thoughts, feelings, emotions, words, and actions. The ability to be in control of yourself is called self-control.
  - From Romans 6:14 KJV, we learn that it is possible to live a sin- free life while walking on this earth. The key to living a sin-free life is self-control. The Holy Spirit enables you to exercise self- control (Galatians 5:22-23 KJV).
  - The Holy Spirit gives you the ability to exercise restraint and discipline when faced with conflicting desires and pressures. Conflicting desires may include the lust of the flesh or fame, which do not glorify God. Pressures may come from peers, family, or society. For example, there may be pressure to prove a point, respond to online trolls, or lash out when offended.
  - However, through the Holy Spirit, you can choose not to respond negatively. You can remain calm in traffic, avoid yelling when provoked, and maintain discipline in other challenging situations. As the Bible says, "But if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live" (Romans 8:13 KJV). It is only through the Spirit of God that you can subdue and discipline your impulses, desires, and deeds of the flesh.
  - In addition, devote yourself wholly to God's Word so that your mind will be renewed and strengthened to live according to His will.
prayer: The Holy Spirit enables me to subdue the desires and actions of the flesh. I have dominion over sin and its corrupting influence. Through the Spirit, the old nature is crucified. I will continue to give myself to the Word of God. In Jesus' name, Amen.
