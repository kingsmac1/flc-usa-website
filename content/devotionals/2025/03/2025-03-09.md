title: Don’t Reject the Name of Jesus
scripture: Acts 4:12 NRSV
verse: There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among mortals by which we must be saved.
body:
  - a cts 4:12 clearly states that no one else can provide salvation other than Jesus. The careful choice of words in this scripture highlights a few important truths. The phrase "under heaven" refers to the earth, and the word "mortals" signifies human beings who are alive. Apostle Peter emphasizes that there is no other name on earth, given to humanity, by which we can be saved. The acceptance and use of the name of Jesus are for people on earth.
  - He did not say "beneath the earth." Beneath the earth refers to those who are dead. That is why Peter boldly declared that the only name that can save those who are alive is the name of Jesus. When a person dies, they cannot accept Jesus or use His name. This is why people who die without accepting Jesus cannot receive a second chance to believe in Him or use His name. They face eternal separation because they rejected Him while alive (Luke 16:24-29).
  - '2 Corinthians 6:2 ESV says: "For he says, ''In a favorable time I listened to you, and in a day of salvation I have helped'
  - you.'Behold, now is the favorable time; behold, now is the day of salvation." Indeed, now is the favorable time to accept Jesus and His name. This is the perfect opportunity to accept Him, live by His name, and allow Him to guide your life before it is too late.
