title: Where is your Heart?
scripture: Matthew 6:21 KJV
verse: For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.
body:
  - w here is your treasure? Where are your valuables? And what are your valuables? Storing your valuables in the wrong place has the ability to control your heart and lead it to the wrong place. The things you treasure the most have a way of attaching your heart to them.
  - Matthew 6:21 NLT says, “Wherever your treasure is, there the desires of your heart will also be.” The things you treasure the most can become like a god to you. Many times, people unknowingly practice idolatry in their hearts. It could be material possessions, your spouse, your job, or self-love. These things become so valuable to you that they take the first place in your heart, instead of God.
  - Consider how much time people spend on their phones or social media, always curious to check their devices. For some, it becomes the last thing they look at before sleeping and the first thing they check upon waking, even before their morning devotion.
  - Let your heart focus on things that have eternal value. Colossians 3:2 AMP says, “Set your mind and keep focused
  - habitually on the things above (the heavenly things), not on things that are on the earth (which have only temporal value).” The earthly things that consume your time and energy are temporary. You will leave them all behind when your time on earth ends.
  - Set your heart right by seeking the Lord your God (1 Chronicles 22:19).
prayer: Dear Lord, my mind will always be stayed on You. I will meditate on Your Word and do all it says. I set my heart on things above and will not entangle myself with things that are unprofitable to You and Your agenda. In Jesus' name, Amen!
