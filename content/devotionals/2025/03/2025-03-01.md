title: Flee from Every Appearance of Evil
scripture: 1 Thessalonians 5:22 KJV
verse: Abstain from every form of evil
body:
  - f rom the opening Bible verse, the word abstain refers to staying away or not getting close. It also means to flee. Apostle Paul was addressing the Christian community in Thessalonica, which is present-day Greece, instructing them to stay away from anything that appears to be evil. Don't get close to anything that looks like evil; stay away from anything ungodly, immoral, or unscriptural.
  - 'There is a Persian proverb that says, “Don''t play with the tail of a lion.” This means certain situations are very dangerous. Abstain from every form of evil because it is very dangerous. “And she caught him by his garment, saying, Lie with me: and he left his garment in her hand, and fled, and got him out” – Genesis 39:12 KJV.'
  - This refers to Joseph. When faced with the seduction of Potiphar's wife, he quickly ran away. Joseph didn't have to find out if it was truly an attempt because it was the appearance of evil. He fled. He didn't walk away but ran. This reveals no tolerance for evil. Don't play with or tolerate anything that appears to be evil. This includes communication, association, relationships, neighborhood, thoughts, and anything else that
  - appears like evil. “Run away from the evil that people long for. Try hard to do what is right. Have faith, love, and peace. Do these things together with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart” – 2 Timothy 2:22 NIRV.
  - 'Reuben, as a young man, accommodated evil, and it cost him dearly. “And it came to pass, when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father''s concubine: and Israel heard it” – Genesis 35:22 KJV. Although the consequences of his sin were not immediate, they were severe. As the oldest son, he was supposed to receive a double portion of the family inheritance and a place of leadership among his people. However, his blessings were given to someone else because he defiled his father''s couch by lying with his father''s concubine.'
  - The consequences of evil often plague us long after the sin has been committed. We may think we can escape unnoticed, only to discover later that the sin has been quietly breeding serious consequences. Finally, flee from sin as you would from the face of a serpent, for if you come too near it, it will bite you.
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for the cleansing effect of your Word. Forgive me for any form of sin I have committed because I didn't flee from it. Thank you for filling my heart with your Word so I may not sin against you. Your Word will always guide my thoughts and actions. In Jesus' name, amen.
