title: Love your Enemies
scripture: Luke 6:28, Matthew 5:44 KJV
verse: Bless those who curse you, pray for those who mistreat you.
body:
  - 'The command to love our enemies is profound. Our opening scripture makes it clear that those who hate, curse, and persecute us are regarded as our enemies. There are two categories of enemies: physical and spiritual.'
  - Physical enemies are individuals who hate or mistreat you without using spiritual forces against you, as seen in Matthew 5:44 and Luke 6:28. Joseph's brothers hated him, but they did not employ divination or enchantments against him. When dealing with physical enemies, approach them with caution, but still love them.
  - Spiritual enemies, on the other hand, are those who antagonize and fight against you using dark spiritual forces, such as witchcraft, divination, or enchantments. These are your true adversaries. It is important to note that some people may act under the influence of such satanic forces, opposing you unknowingly. Such cases are spiritual warfare, and they require a different approach (Ephesians 6:10-18). You can love these individuals by praying for their salvation but avoid sharing your secrets or forming close relationships with them.
  - In Matthew 5:44, Jesus was specifically referring to physical enemies. He teaches us not to retaliate when they do us harm. Instead, we should love and pray for them. By doing so, we overcome evil with good. Loving your enemies and treating them kindly is evidence that Jesus is guiding your life.
  - Do not say you cannot love your enemies because of the wrongs they have done to you. "The love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Spirit" (Romans 5:5). Depend on the Holy Spirit to help you love and treat your enemies well. Loving your enemies can bring them to the Lord and even inspire them to love you in return. Love conquers all, including people and challenging situations (1 Corinthians 13:7).
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your love and for giving me the nature of love. Your love in me enables me to love everyone, regardless of what they may have done to me. I will continue to treat my enemies rightly and pray for them. I ask that you touch their hearts and bring them to salvation so they can know Jesus. In His name, amen.
