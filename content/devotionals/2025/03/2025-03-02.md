date: '2025-03-02'
title: He Lives in You
scripture: 1 Corinthians 6:19 NIV
verse: Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own.
body:
  - w hen you got saved, God came to make His home in your heart. You have become God's residence. God lives in you now through the Holy Spirit. “Don't you realize that all of you together are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God lives in you?” – 1 Corinthians 3:16 NLT. God wants you to come to the realization and be conscious that you are His dwelling place.
  - 'Since He lives in you, it is important to permit Him to regulate your life because He is the Lord of your life. He is trustworthy, and He will guide you in the path you should go: the path of life, victory, and success. The presence of God in your life will be your inner light and guide. He will bear witness of Himself to you. He will grant you a good sense of judgment and discernment.'
  - Because He lives in you, you are in a place of advantage. Hallelujah! “To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory” – Colossians 1:27 KJV.
  - Because He lives in you, you have hope - hope for joy, peace, victory, and wholeness. This hope is not just for this present world but also for a glorious future. We have a future; we have a home where we will spend eternity with the Lord, and that place is Heaven. Christ in you is your hope of glory.
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for choosing to make Your home in me. I thank You for the glorious hope You have brought into my life. I will forever trust and submit to the leadership of Your Spirit in me. In Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: 1 Corinthians 3:16-20 KJV, 2 Corinthians 6:16 KJV, John 14:17
