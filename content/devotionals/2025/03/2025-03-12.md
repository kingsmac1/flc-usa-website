title: Perfection in Christ
scripture: Matthew 5:48 KJV
verse: Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.
body:
  - 't he Amplified version of Matthew 5:48 renders this verse in clearer terms: "You, therefore, will be perfect (growing into spiritual maturity both in mind and character, actively integrating godly values into your daily life), as your heavenly Father is perfect."'
  - God is perfect, and He desires us to grow into perfection as He is perfect. While we are not there yet, He calls us to work towards it. Perfection is a process that requires our active involvement.
  - 'The perfect believer is one who has grown into maturity in Christ. "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; for the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ" (Ephesians 4:11-13 KJV).'
  - We are all works in progress. As Paul said, "I don't mean to say that I have already achieved these things or that I have already
  - reached perfection. But I press on to possess that perfection for which Christ Jesus first possessed me" (Philippians 3:12 NLT).
  - 'The question often arises: how can a believer become perfect? Here are key ways: 1. Holiness: Separate yourself from the world and its sinful values. Let your desire be to please the Lord rather than to satisfy the desires of the flesh. 2. Growing into Maturity: God expects us to "press" forward. This means putting in full effort into activities that enhance our development in Christ and our character. 3. Walking in Love: "The love of God is shed abroad in our hearts" (Romans 5:5 KJV). This love motivates us to obey God''s Word and spend more time with Him. As we do this, the desires of the flesh begin to fade, and He reveals Himself to us more deeply. This increases our desire to grow into His perfection.'
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank you for your perfect nature in me. It causes me to desire and press towards becoming a perfect reflection of Christ. As I continue to know you, I am continuously transformed into the fullness of Christ. In Jesus' name, amen.
