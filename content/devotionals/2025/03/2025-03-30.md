date: '2025-03-30'
title: Jesus is Coming Again
scripture: John 14:3 KJV
verse: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you unto Myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.
body:
  - Oour Lord Jesus is coming again. He left us with a message to share with those who have not heard and to remind other believers of His return to take us home. Therefore, let us be watchful and prudent to avoid erring. Ephesians 5:15 KJV says, “See then that ye walk circumspectly, not as fools, but as wise.”
  - 1 Peter 1:17 NLT reminds us, “And remember that the heavenly Father to whom you pray has no favorites. He will judge or reward you according to what you do. So you must live in reverent fear of Him during your time here as temporary residents.”
  - The phrase “temporary residents” refers to sojourners. Sojourners are people who have temporary residence; they do not consider a place their permanent habitation. We should always remember that we are sojourners on earth, and our ultimate destination is Heaven. When we get to Heaven, God will reward us according to how we lived our lives and our works on earth. Therefore, let us walk circumspectly by living according to the Spirit and forsaking all works of
  - unrighteousness (1 Corinthians 6:9-10).
  - As you live on this earth, strive to please God above all else. Even as you endeavor to make your family or others happy, remember that pleasing God is what matters most. You will be accountable to Him.
prayer: Thank You, Father, because there is no condemnation for me as I am in Christ Jesus. I submit to You and walk in the Spirit always, not after the flesh. Through Your Spirit, I mortify the desires and deeds of the flesh. I walk circumspectly as I await Your return. Hallelujah!
