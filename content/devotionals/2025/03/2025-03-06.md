title: Practice Forgiveness
scripture: Hebrews 8:12 NIV
verse: For I will forgive their wickedness and will remember their sins no more.
body:
  - t he New Oxford American Dictionary defines forgiveness as "the action or process of forgiving or being forgiven." I have heard people say things like, "I will forgive but I will not forget." The downside of forgiving and not forgetting is that whenever you remember what happened, it tends to reopen old wounds. The scar might remain, but you are still in bondage. God has the ability to make you whole. Why not pray to God to help you totally forgive, forget, and let go? When this happens, you will realize the scars are gone, and you will be free.
  - Forgiveness requires letting go of grudges and bitterness. It is more spiritual than physical. When you forgive, you experience a sense of peace. This peace comes from making the right decision to forgive. As Philippians 4:7 KJV says, "And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”
  - In Genesis 45:17-20, Joseph was rejected, enslaved, falsely accused, and imprisoned. His brothers hurt him deeply and caused him great suffering. Many in his position would have
  - sought revenge, but Joseph graciously forgave his brothers, loved them, and repaid their evil with good. Joseph's actions reveal how God forgives us.
  - Forgiveness is a virtue and a fruit of the recreated human spirit. Emulate Joseph by practicing forgiveness. Do not wait for people to apologize before you forgive them. Forgive ahead of time so nothing holds you back. Some people may not know they hurt you, while others might refuse to apologize. Regardless, forgiveness liberates your soul. As detailed in Colossians 3:13 NIV, "Bear with each other and forgive one another if any of you has a grievance against someone. Forgive as the Lord forgave you."
prayer: Thank you, Father, for your nature of forgiveness at work in me. I will always bear with others and forgive their shortcomings. I will love others and see them from your point of view. I will be gracious and kind to others as I have obtained grace and kindness from you. In Jesus' name, amen.
