title: Refuse to Worry
scripture: Matthew 6:27 NLT
verse: Can all your worries add a single moment to your life?
body:
  - t he word worry comes from the Greek word merimnao, which means to be anxious, concerned, or bothered. Worry often stems from a lack of peace and trust in the Lord. It arises when we try to figure out solutions on our own and doubt the possibility of God's Word coming to pass in our lives (Proverbs 3:5).
  - Anyone who puts their full trust in the Lord will experience peace. Worry, on the other hand, adds nothing to your life. As Matthew 6:27 reminds us, "Can all your worries add a single moment to your life?"
  - People worry about many things today, but worrying will never solve problems. Proverbs 23:4 NLT says, "Don't wear yourself out trying to get rich. Be wise enough to know when to quit." This applies not only to riches but to all aspects of life. Worrying will only wear you out.
  - In engineering, the term "wear" refers to gradual deterioration or damage caused by stress. Similarly, worry can wear you down over time. When you face challenges and begin to feel overwhelmed, be wise and shift your focus from the situation to
  - God's Word.
  - 'Isaiah 26:3 KJV says, "Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee." Focus on God''s promises, and He will give you peace that replaces worry. You will find the burdens lifted, and the anxiety will fade away.'
prayer: I refuse to be anxious about anything. I cast all my cares upon the Lord, for He cares for me. My mind will always stay on the Word of God, and I experience perfect peace. In Jesus' name, Amen.
