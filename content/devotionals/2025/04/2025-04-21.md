date: '2025-04-21'
title: Your Labor for the Lord is Not in Vain
scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:58 NIV
verse: Always give yourselves fully to the work of the Lord, because you know that your labor in the Lord is not in vain.
body:
  - Apostle Paul, in 1 Corinthians 15:58, was encouraging the brethren to labor for the Lord. To labor for the Lord means to put in effort, commitment, and hard work to advance God's kingdom. This includes giving your time, ideas, resources, and dedication through evangelism, intercession, volunteering, missions, and other ways of serving in God's work.
  - Laboring for the Lord can be demanding, but it is meaningful and rewarding. Sometimes, the earthly results may not seem to match the effort put in, but the labor is never in vain. Many years ago, when I started evangelism, I went from door to door and street to street, preaching the gospel and inviting people to church. Most times, I started in the morning and continued until evening. On Sunday mornings, out of the many I invited, only a few would show up. Yet, I did not allow myself to be discouraged.
  - From 1 Corinthians 15:58, the phrase "always abounding" means to go beyond the minimum and exceed expectations in
  - our service to the Lord. Instead of growing weary, we should do more for the Lord. Hebrews 6:10 NLT says, "God is not unjust. He will not forget how hard you have worked for him and the love you have shown him..."
  - As you continue to labor for the Lord, do it passionately, wholeheartedly, and with a perfect heart. There is a great reward waiting for you in heaven (Matthew 5:12).
prayer: Heavenly Father, I thank you for making me a co-laborer with You. I thank You because no labor of love for You is in vain. As I continue to strive for the furtherance of the gospel, I will do all with a perfect heart. I remain steadfast and will do more for Your kingdom, in Jesus' name. Amen!
