title: What Does God Require From You?
scripture: Hosea 6:6 KJV
verse: For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.
body:
  - In Hosea 6:6, the word "mercy" is better understood as "love" (dutiful love). This implies love for God and love for mankind. In Luke 10:27, "He answered, 'You must love the LORD with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength and with all your mind; and love your neighbor as yourself.'”
  - 'The focus from our opening scripture is this: "I desire mercy and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings." God desires and craves our love. He has an appetite that can only be satisfied by our love for Him. Beyond loving others, He expects us to prove our love for Him through obedience to His Word and His will.'
  - In 1 Samuel 15:22 KJV, "And Samuel said, Hath the LORD as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the LORD? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams." Obedience is an act of submission to the will of God. It is a recognition of His sovereignty. This shows that you truly honor God.
  - God is saying to you today that He wants you to honor Him by obeying His Word and following the leading of His Spirit. He desires to guide you on the right path to the glorious destination He has planned for you. You can know God through the scriptures, prayer, and yielding to His promptings. As you do so, He will reveal Himself and the riches of His glory to you. Hallelujah!
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for the inspiration and blessing of Your Word. From today, I yield myself to Your will. I will obey all You want me to do. I am strengthened in my inner man by Your Spirit to delight in doing what pleases You, in Jesus' name. Amen!
