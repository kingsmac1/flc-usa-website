title: The Day of Reckoning
scripture: Luke 12:47 KJV
verse: And that servant, which knew his Lord's will, and prepared not himself, neither did according to his will, shall be beaten with many stripes.
body:
  - The day of reckoning is also known as the judgment day. It is the day when everyone will give an account to God of how they lived their lives on earth. It will be a day of reward for those who lived rightly before the Lord and a day of condemnation for those who rejected Jesus and continued in iniquity.
  - Sin and iniquity are often used interchangeably to describe lawlessness as defined by God. However, iniquity is a premeditated choice to continue in sin without repentance. There are many who believe that a believer can continue to walk in sin and still be raptured and spend eternity with God. That is false. Any believer who deliberately continues to live in sin without repentance is a worker of iniquity.
  - 'Several believers have abused the teaching of grace. Romans 6:1 KJV says, "What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?" The judgment day is coming, and everyone will give an account to God of how they used the grace given to them after being born again. One thing is certain: God'
  - will reward all believers who faithfully served Him, but He will also say, "Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity," to those who continued in sin and those who rejected Christ. They will be sent to the place of weeping and gnashing of teeth (Matthew 7:18-23).
  - Prepare for that day of reckoning by forsaking every way of life that is displeasing to God.
