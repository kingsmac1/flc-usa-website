title: The Price of Following Christ
scripture: Matthew 8:20-21 CSB
verse: A scribe approached him and said, 'Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go.' Jesus told him,'Foxes have dens, and birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay his head.
body:
  - f rom our opening Bible verse, the phrase "the Son of Man has no place to lay His head" does not necessarily mean that Jesus was poor or homeless. Jesus made that statement to correct the scribe's misunderstanding of what it means to be a follower of Christ. The scribe had the wrong motivation, expecting earthly grandeur and wealth. Jesus was informing him that following Him comes at a cost.
  - Following Christ is not always easy or comfortable. It usually comes with a price and requires sacrifice. It might cost you friendships, habits, and lifestyle. Although the price is high, the reward is much greater.
  - When you got saved, Jesus became the Lord of your life. He became your Master, and you are to submit to Him wholeheartedly. Song of Solomon 7:10 ALT says, "I am my lover's, and he claims me as his own." Jesus claims you as His own, and you are to live for Him. Galatians 2:20 ESV says, "I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. And the life I now live in the flesh I live by
  - faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.”
  - There are great benefits in paying the price to follow Christ. You received eternal life when you said the prayer of salvation (Romans 10:9, John 6:47). Salvation is God's gift to you. It includes forgiveness of sins, freedom from sin, divine health, authority over demons, deliverance, victory, protection, prosperity, divine provision, and all spiritual blessings. Those who are saved are entitled to all that comes with salvation.
  - Above all, you will spend eternity with God and receive a reward for your service to Him.
