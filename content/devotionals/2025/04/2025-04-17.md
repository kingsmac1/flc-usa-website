title: You Are Not Different from your thoughts
scripture: Matthew 12:34 ESV
verse: For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh.
body:
  - Matthew 12:35 ESV says, "A good person out of his good treasure brings forth good, and the evil person out of his evil treasure brings forth evil." Thoughts are conceived first before they are eventually expressed through words. Your words reveal what you have in mind and what you are truly made of.
  - Until you speak, others might not know what is in your heart. Although, sometimes, nonverbal communication such as body language can give others an idea of your thoughts. These reflect who you are and your personality. No wonder some people struggle with bad habits and negative responses such as harsh words and unpleasant behaviors.
  - When your heart is occupied with negative thoughts, it will produce negative words and actions. An example is when someone says, "Something is eating me up." If such thoughts are not controlled, they can have a corrosive effect on you.
  - Control your thoughts and regulate what you permit into your heart. As detailed in Proverbs 4:23 GNT, "Be careful how you
  - think; your life is shaped by your thoughts."
  - You can begin today by replacing the wrong things in your heart with positive things. Replace negative movies, news, articles, skits, and thoughts with God's word. Whenever negative thoughts try to rise within you, boldly say to yourself, "I have the mind of Christ" (1 Corinthians 2:16). "Therefore, negative thought, I command you to cease from my heart in Jesus' name."
  - After a while, you will notice your thoughts taking a new shape. You will experience a peaceful and positive life filled with pleasant words and actions.
prayer: I declare that I have the mind of Christ. I think the thoughts of Christ, and my mind is renewed daily. God's word will not depart from my mouth, and I will meditate on it at all times. God's word dwells in me richly, shaping me into the full stature of Christ. Amen.
