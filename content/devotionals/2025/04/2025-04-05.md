title: The Gate to the Kingdom of Heaven
scripture: John 14:6 KJV
verse: 'Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no one comes to the Father except through me.'
body:
  - J ohn 17:3 NKJV says, "And this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent." Indeed, Christ is the way to eternal life. He is the only way to the Father. When someone gets saved, there is a path the person needs to walk in order to spend eternity with God, and it is called the gate to the Kingdom of Heaven.
  - Matthew 7:13-14 NIV says, "Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life, and only a few find it." Jesus calls the gate that leads to the Kingdom of Heaven narrow. He did not mean that it is difficult to be a Christian, but that the only path that leads to eternal life with God is through Him. There is a requirement for that, and only a few choose to meet it.
  - To walk through the narrow gate, one has to be saved and begin living according to the ways of the Lord. It may not be popular or easy, but it is the right way. It requires living in holiness and
  - submission to the Lord.
  - Psalms 118:20 NKJV says, "This is the gate of the Lord, through which the righteous shall enter." Glory to God for providing us a way to be with Him in eternity.
