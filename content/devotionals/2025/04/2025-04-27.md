title: The Power of Choice
scripture: Deuteronomy 30:19 KJV
verse: 'I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live.'
body:
  - We are free moral agents. The word "free" implies the privilege to enjoy certain rights. The word "moral" refers to right conduct, while "agent" describes someone who exercises the power to make a choice (Genesis 2:17). From our opening scripture, life and death are placed before humans, yet God desires that you choose life. He does not impose it on you, but there will surely be consequences for whatever choice you make.
  - The choices we make are either acts of obedience or disobedience to God. Through the scriptures, we understand what pleases God and the path He wants us to follow. However, when we choose not to obey Him, it is disobedience.
  - 'In Matthew 7:13-30, Jesus highlights examples of the choices we need to make: the narrow gate or the broad road, the good tree or the bad tree, true or false discipleship, and the firm or weak foundation. The choice is yours to make.'
  - Before making any choice, always remember that your decisions will determine your destiny and destination.
prayer: Dear Lord, guide me on the path of life that You have destined for me. Continue to lead me to make the right choices that will bring glory to Your name. In Jesus' name, amen.
