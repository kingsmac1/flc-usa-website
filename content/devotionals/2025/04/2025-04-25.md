title: Stop Running Away from your Assignment
scripture: John 18:37 ESV
verse: I was born and for this purpose I have come into the world, to bear witness to the truth...
body:
  - Ephesians 2:10 NIV says, "For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God has prepared in advance for us to do". Your works are considered "good works" only when they are acceptable to God. Being acceptable means they meet God's specifications, which are based on what He has predestined for you.
  - In Ephesians 1:11, "In Him we have obtained an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of Him who works all things." Every individual has been predestined by God for a purpose. This purpose is your assignment on earth. Your works will only be classified as good when you begin to fulfill this assignment.
  - God sent Jonah to the Assyrian city of Nineveh, but Jonah tried to run from his assignment. Nineveh was known as a violent and cruel nation. However, when Jonah chose to fulfill his assignment, God used him to bring about a great revival. The entire city repented and turned to God. Who knows how many people, destinies, cities, and nations will be saved from
  - darkness when you stop running from your assignment?
  - In John 18:37 ESV, "You were born to bear witness to the truth." This does not only mean preaching but also reflecting Christ through your life. By doing so, you will testify that Jesus Christ is Lord, and many will be saved from poverty, sin, disease, and affliction because of you.
prayer: I am the light of the world, a city set on a hill that cannot be hidden. I will not be hidden from bearing witness to the Lordship of Jesus. I will fulfill my purpose and ministry in Christ Jesus. Amen!
