title: True Productivity
scripture: Colossians 1:10 KJV
verse: That ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God.
body:
  - There are times when some people keep moving in circles, thinking they are making progress, without realizing they are returning to where they started. In life, real progress and fruitfulness can only be found in Christ. The more time you spend with God's word and His presence, the more you will experience what true productivity is.
  - 1 Timothy 4:15 KJV says, "Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all." This is not about showing off or publicly displaying your productivity. Rather, it means that when you wholly give yourself to God's word, you will make significant achievements and progress in life.
  - In your Christian walk, distractions may arise, and you might feel pressured at times, but you must give yourself completely to God's word. That is where you will find inspiration and guidance to navigate your way through. Worries, conflicts, and excessive busyness can keep Christians from studying God's word deeply and growing from it (Matthew 13:22). Being too busy with work or other things can shift priorities, making it
  - difficult to live a truly productive life.
  - True productivity is a life that continually produces real value to God and to yourself.
prayer: I am a tree planted by the rivers of water; I bear fruit in every season. I will continually give myself wholly to God's word, and my life bears witness unto God.
furtherStudies: Colossians 1:6-11 KJV, Ephesians 2:10 KJV, John 15:8-16 KJV
