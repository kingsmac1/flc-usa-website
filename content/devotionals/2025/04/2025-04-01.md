title: Qualities of True Worship
scripture: John 4:23 KJV
verse: 'But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth: for the Father seeketh such to worship him.'
body:
  - The opening Bible verse teaches us some qualities of true worship during the dialogue between Jesus and the Samaritan woman at the well. Worship must be spiritual. It has to come from your spirit. True worship should not be carnal. Just because the choir sang well, the voices were great, and you felt good does not mean it was done in the spirit. Worship proceeds from your spirit and not from the flesh.
  - Secondly, worship must be in truth. God's word is the truth. Worship has to be based on the truth revealed about God in the Scriptures. This means you need to understand who God is through the Scriptures to offer Him true worship (John 8:31- 32).
  - In Matthew 2:11, the Magi worshipped and honored God with their gifts. Worshiping and honoring God for who He is requires your willingness to give Him what is valuable to you, such as your resources and time.
  - Worship should be heartfelt. Psalm 63:4-5 TPT says, "I will
  - worship you passionately and with all my heart. My arms will wave to you like banners of praise." Not only should worship be heartfelt, but it also involves lifting holy hands to the Lord (Psalm 141:2), singing, and making melodies with the fruit of your lips (Hebrews 13:15). If you notice, the last few qualities of worship involve your body. That is why you should be intentional about what you do with your body. Romans 12:1 KJV says, "I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.”
  - Finally, let your life be an instrument of worship unto God by doing good works and reflecting Christ in all you do. Matthew 5:16 says, "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for making me a light that shines brightly to all men, reflecting Your glory through my good works. I will worship You with all my heart, time, and resources. I will live for You and always present my body as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable unto You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
