date: '2025-04-29'
title: Be Identified with Christ
scripture: Luke 9:26 KJV
verse: For whosoever shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him shall the Son of Man be ashamed, when He shall come in His own glory.
body:
  - J esus, in Luke 9:26 KJV, was speaking about being identified with Him. Some people, although saved, do not want to be associated with Jesus or His words. They hesitate to speak boldly for God, even at their workplace, for fear of losing friends. Nicodemus and Joseph of Arimathea secretly followed Jesus because they did not want the Jews to see them with Him, except for their support during His burial.
  - Many believers today cannot even quote scriptures during conversations because they feel it is old-fashioned. The phrase "if anyone is ashamed of me and my words" in Luke 9:26 highlights the importance of living boldly for Christ and not being ashamed of God and His words, regardless of persecution and societal pressure.
  - It is essential to prioritize your relationship with God over worldly acceptance. Apostle Paul, who once disliked and persecuted early Christians, came to realize this truth and became bold for Christ, no matter the cost. Romans 1:16 KJV says, "For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God unto salvation for everyone who believes.”
  - Make up your mind to stand for Christ against all odds, like Daniel, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. It is an honorable thing to be identified with the King of the universe.
prayer: Thank You, Father, for the privilege to serve You and to be called Your own. I thank You, Lord, for in You I live, in You I move, and in You I have my being. I will not be ashamed to be identified with You, and I will boldly declare Your name and word to everyone around me, in Jesus' name. Amen!
