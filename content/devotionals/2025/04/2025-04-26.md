title: Reasons Some Prayer Requests Are Not Answered
scripture: Isaiah 65:24 NIV
verse: Before they call, I will answer; while they are still speaking, I will hear.
body:
  - Mark 11:24 NIV says, "Therefore I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.” This is Jesus giving us a blank check to make demands on anything we desire. He also guarantees that we will receive what we ask for. However, this is not the case for everyone, as some people do not consistently receive answers to their prayers. God is not partial or inconsistent, and our experiences do not invalidate the word of God.
  - There are several reasons for unanswered prayers. First, many ask amiss. Such requests are inconsistent with God's will. God knows that granting such prayers may not benefit you and could even lead to destruction (1 Samuel 8:4-22).
  - Secondly, there is a time for everything (Ecclesiastes 3:1). In God's calendar, there is an appointed time for Him to manifest what you have been asking for. When that time comes, your answers will appear, and it will be to the glory of God (Exodus 23:29). Often, when there is a delay in receiving answers, God may be teaching you persistence while building patience in you.
  - God expects you to ask and receive in faith (Matthew 21:22).
  - Finally, the condition of your heart is crucial. If you harbor unforgiveness, God will not answer you (Mark 11:25).
prayer: Thank You, Father, for You always hear me when I pray. Through the scriptures and Your Spirit, Your will is made known to me. I do not ask amiss. I will always ask in faith and with a pure heart. Amen!
