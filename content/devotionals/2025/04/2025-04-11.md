title: God’s Word in your Heart
scripture: Psalm 119:11 AMP
verse: Your word I have treasured and stored in my heart, that I may not sin against you.
body:
  - In our opening Bible verse, we see that when God's Word is treasured and stored in a person's heart, it enables them to abstain from sin. Psalm 37:31 says, "The law of his God is in his heart; his steps do not falter." It matters what dwells in your heart and what you feed your heart with. The moment you begin to ponder on God's Word at all times, it will guide your steps, keep you from erring against Him, and help you make the right choices in your daily life.
  - Isaiah 59:19 KJV says, "When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him." God raises a standard, a banner, or an armed resistance against the opposition of the enemy.
  - The Lord does this in several ways. One way is by stirring up stored scriptures in your heart to counter any situation. For instance, when faced with opposition, the Lord may stir up 1 John 4:4 in your heart, which says, "Greater is He that is in you, than he that is in the world." At that moment, God expects you to proclaim the scripture He has placed in your heart, for that is the banner against such opposition.
  - God expects you to store up scriptures in your heart through meditation so He can draw from that resource to raise banners for you in times of trouble. That is why Colossians 3:16 says, "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly." Also, God's Word in you provides a firm foundation and resistance against the challenges of life.
