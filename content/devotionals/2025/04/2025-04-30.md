date: '2025-04-30'
title: Walking in Dominion
scripture: Luke 10:19 KJV
verse: Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall by any means hurt you.
body:
  - All victories over Satan and his agents are obtained through the power derived from Jesus Christ. Colossians 2:15 NIV says, "And having disarmed the powers and authorities, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them by the cross." Jesus defeated Satan and his cohorts, and every believer in Christ benefits from these victories.
  - 'In Luke 10:19, we have the authority to exercise dominion over the enemy. However, many believers are still affected by the devil today. To walk in dominion, here are a few things you should do:'
  - '1. Pray: Make your request and use the name of Jesus to resist the devil from interfering in your life. Philippians 2:10 NIV says, "That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth." 2. Act in faith: Do not allow fear to grip you. Maintain your composure and trust in what God''s word says concerning that situation. Mark 9:23 NIV says, "Everything is possible for one who believes." 3. Do not be ignorant: The enemy will always test how much of God''s word you know. Jesus was able to overcome Satan''s temptation because He knew the Scriptures. Colossians 3:16 KJV says, "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly." Also, Daniel 11:32 KJV says, "But they that know their God shall be strong and do exploits." The word "exploits" refers to walking in dominion. Know God''s word for yourself. 4. Walk in obedience: The devil is the accuser of the brethren. He knows when you do not comply with God''s words and instructions. You cannot expect to walk in dominion when you live in immorality and sin. Romans 6:16 KJV says, "Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey; whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?" Purge yourself of all ungodliness, and you will experience a great manifestation of dominion in your life.'
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for giving me the authority in Your name. I will continue to exercise dominion and ensure Your purpose is accomplished in my world. In the name of Jesus, I will always bring every negative situation and all things to the obedience of Christ. Amen!
