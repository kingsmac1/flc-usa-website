title: The Blessings of the Lord Make You Rich
scripture: Proverbs 10:22 NKJV
verse: The blessing of the LORD, it makes one rich, and He adds no sorrow to it.
body:
  - t he blessing of the Lord is what makes you rich, not your struggles or the so-called hustle. Your life will take an entirely different shape when you understand this truth. True riches come from the Lord. The word "rich" as used in the scripture above means to be prosperous, to have in abundance, and to lack nothing. To be prosperous goes beyond money and material possessions. It is an all-inclusive package that involves having good health, peace of mind, spiritual growth, and financial increase. God wants you to prosper in every area of your life.
  - One of the ways to recognize the blessing is that it adds no sorrow. It does not come with affliction or regrets. Many have thought that their hard work will make them rich, but that is not true. Hard work is good, but all it can do is make you have money. However, having money alone is not the true definition of riches; it is just one aspect.
  - Proverbs 10:22 GNT says, "It is the LORD's blessing that makes you wealthy. Hard work can make you no richer." When the blessing of the Lord is upon your life, it causes you to
  - prosper. Every little effort of yours produces great increase. No wonder many struggle and work so hard, yet their results do not measure up to their efforts.
  - Deuteronomy 8:18 KJV says, "But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God; for it is He that giveth thee power to get wealth." There is a power and a grace to make wealth, and it comes from God. May God bless you on every side.
prayer: I am the seed of Abraham. I am blessed beyond measure. No curse is permitted to work in my life. I will enjoy Your blessings every day, and there shall be no sorrow in my life. In Jesus' name, Amen!
