title: You Mean So Much to God
scripture: John 3:16 KJV
verse: For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
body:
  - God loves the world because you are in it. He sent His Son to die for you, all because you mean so much to Him. In Genesis 1:27, God created you in His own image and likeness. He crafted you specially, just like Himself. This proves how much you mean to Him.
  - God has given His angels charge over you to guide and protect you because He values you greatly (Psalm 91:11-12). Not only do His angels watch over you, but He Himself watches over you (Psalm 12:5-8). In all His sovereignty and majesty, He still looks after you because you are His precious child.
  - In John 16:23, God wants your joy to be full. That is why He listens to you and answers your prayers. He has also promised to take care of you so that you will have no reason to worry about anything (Isaiah 51:12).
  - Indeed, you mean so much to God. In all that you do, always be conscious of this truth.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for Your great love and provision for me. Thank You for revealing how valuable I am to You. I will always worship You and live for Your glory.
furtherStudies: 1 Peter 5:7 KJV, John 14:14 KJV, Psalm 40:28 KJV
