title: The Peace that Emanates from God
scripture: Acts 10:36 NLT
verse: This is the message of Good News for the people of Israel, that there is peace with God through Jesus Christ, who is Lord of all.
body:
  - The message of Good News is the gospel of Christ. Romans 1:16 KJV says, "For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God unto salvation to everyone who believes." The power of God that leads people to salvation is in the gospel.
  - When you got saved, you also received justification. This justification brought you peace with God. Romans 5:1 KJV says, "Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ." Peace with God means being reconciled with Him.
  - Another peace that emanates from God is the peace of God. This is the inner calmness and tranquility that comes from a personal relationship with Him. The Holy Spirit brings this peace to your heart, and it is beyond human understanding (Philippians 4:6-7).
  - There is also peace as a sign of God's approval. Due to your relationship with Him, when you feel this peace, it can be a sign that God approves of your actions. No wonder people become restless when they are about to make a wrong decision. Focusing on God's Word and trusting Him will help you experience perfect peace. Isaiah 26:3 NLT says, "You will keep in perfect peace all who trust in you, all whose thoughts are fixed on you.”
  - Finally, since we have peace with God, we should restore peace between God and others. This is called reconciliation, and God has made us ministers of reconciliation. Preaching the gospel to others is the way to accomplish this (2 Corinthians 5:18).
prayer: Dear Lord, I thank You, for I have been justified by faith, and I have peace with You. I refuse to be anxious about anything. As I focus my mind on You, I experience perfect peace. Amen.
furtherStudies: Psalms 29:11 KJV, Romans 15:13 KJV
