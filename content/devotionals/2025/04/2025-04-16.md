title: You Can Stop that Storm
scripture: Mark 11:24 KJV
verse: For verily I say unto you, that whosoever shall say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea, and shall not doubt in his heart, but shall believe that those things which he saith shall come to pass; he shall have whatsoever he saith.
body:
  - Storms are unfavorable situations in life. They are circumstances that pose a threat to you. Storms are obstacles, and if you do not stop them, they might overwhelm you.
  - Not long ago, I saw a write-up that said, "Some storms are from God." I was dissatisfied with that statement. From the definition of storms, they all have negative attributes and are not good. James 1:17 NIV says, "Every good and perfect gift is from above." Storms are not good. However, what God does is convert storms to work for your good, as seen in Genesis 50:20, "That which the devil intended for evil, God has turned it around for good." At times, He might permit a storm to get your attention, refine you, and make you better, but He is not the initiator.
  - Since storms represent turbulence and adversity, God wants you to know that you can stop them. A storm could be in your
  - marriage, health, finances, job, neighborhood, or even your nation.
  - In Mark 4:37-39, the storm became life-threatening, and the disciples were afraid. But Jesus said unto the sea, "Peace, be still." The wind ceased, and there was a great calm. Jesus rebuked the source of the storm, and it stopped.
  - God has given you a great tool to stop any challenge you are going through. That tool is the name of Jesus. Use the name of Jesus to stop any unfavorable situation in your life. Command the storm to cease, and it will cease. You will experience peace again.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for the power in the name of Jesus. I decree in the name of Jesus, Satan, take your hands off my life, my home, my family, my health, and everything that concerns me. My life experiences perfect and complete peace. In Jesus' name, Amen!
