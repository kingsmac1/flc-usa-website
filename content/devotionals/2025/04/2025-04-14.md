date: '2025-04-14'
title: What Kind of Soil Are You?
scripture: Matthew 13:23 NLT
verse: The seed that fell on good soil represents those who truly hear and understand God's word and produce a harvest of thirty, sixty, or even a hundred times as much as had been planted.
body:
  - The different types of soil where the sower sowed his seed represent the different responses people have to the word of God. When God's word is shared with people, their individual response is based on the readiness of their hearts. The sower is the preacher, the seed is the word, and the soil is the human heart (Matthew 13:18-28).
  - 'Some have hardened hearts. A hardened heart causes disobedience to God''s word, which leads to unproductivity. That is why Hebrews 3:15 NIV says, "As has just been said: ''Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts as you did in the rebellion.''" Others have shallow hearts, meaning they are not well-rooted in God''s word, and when problems and persecution arise, they fall away easily. Some do not allow the seed of God''s word to produce results in their lives because of the worries of life and the lure of wealth. These distractions hinder the productivity of the word.'
  - Matthew 7:24-27 says, "Anyone who listens to my teaching and follows it is wise, like a person who builds a house on solid
  - rock." Spending more time with God through prayer and meditation on His word will help you develop deep roots. This will make your heart good soil, capable of producing a bountiful harvest (Matthew 13:23).
prayer: Thank you, Father, for your word builds me up, establishes me, and makes me unshakable. The pressures and distractions of this world will not take me away from your presence. Your word will not depart from my mouth, and I will meditate on it always, in Jesus' name. Amen.
furtherStudies: Matthew 7:24-28 TPT, 2 Timothy 2:19, Joshua 1:8-9 KJV
