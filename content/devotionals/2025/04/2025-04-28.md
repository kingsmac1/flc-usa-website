date: '2025-04-28'
title: You Have Authority Over Demons
scripture: Luke 10:19 NKJV
verse: Behold, I give you the authority to trample on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall by any means hurt you.
body:
  - God has given every believer authority over demons. You should not allow demons to take control of your life. They come to steal, kill, and destroy (John 10:10), but by default, the new creation is above demons, and their activities should not harm you.
  - You are seated with Christ at the right hand of God, far above all principality, power, might, dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world but also in that which is to come (Ephesians 1:21 KJV). This is your identity in Christ. Today, you can put an end to Satan's oppression in your life.
  - God has given us a special tool to enforce dominion over the works of the devil, and that tool is the name of Jesus. When you use the name of Jesus, no devil or power can resist it. Philippians 2:10 KJV says, "That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth.”
  - Exercise your authority by commanding the devil to take his hands off your faith and life in the name of Jesus. God bless you.
prayer: Thank You, Father, for the authority You have given me to use the name of Jesus. Thank You because I am seated with Christ, far above all principality, power, might, dominion, and every name that is named. I declare that I am immune to every work of the devil, in Jesus' name. Amen!
furtherStudies: Matthew 28:18 KJV, Ephesians 1:20-22 NKJV, Philippians 2:10 KJV
readingPlan: 1 Sa 1-2; Luk 19
