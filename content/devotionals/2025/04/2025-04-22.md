title: He Has Great Plans for You
scripture: Matthew 6:33 CEV
verse: But more than anything else, put God's work first and do what he wants. Then the other things will be yours as well.
body:
  - God has great plans for you. He wants you to prosper and dwell in peace (Jeremiah 29:11). God's thoughts for you are good, and they will come to fulfillment in Christ Jesus. However, seeking Him is necessary for the manifestation of these great plans. Matthew 6:33 says, "But seek ye first the kingdom of God and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you." All of God's plans for you will become a reality when you seek Him first.
  - You might be wondering, "If God's plans for me are great, then why am I still facing this situation? Why have I not seen those great plans yet?" You will start experiencing them when you seek Him. As detailed in Matthew 7:7 NASB, "Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you." Press on to seek Him, and you will find Him. God's love is unlimited. This means His love has no boundaries; it is eternal and unending. "I have loved you with an everlasting love" (Jeremiah 31:3 KJV). His love is everlasting.
  - 2 Peter 1:3 NIV says, "His divine power has given us everything
  - we need for a godly life through our knowledge of him who called us by his own glory and goodness." God's divine power is not trying to give us everything; rather, it "has given" us everything. God has fulfilled His part, and the responsibility is now yours. If you truly want to experience His plans, you must seek God and His kingdom. As you seek Him, you will discover how to possess all that He has made available for you and fulfill your purpose in Christ.
prayer: I delight myself in the Lord always. I put God first and above all other things. I am blessed with all the good things of life and everything I need to live uprightly. Hallelujah!
