title: A Worry-Free Life
scripture: Luke 12:22 NIV
verse: Therefore, I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear.
body:
  - Someone might ask, "Is it possible to live a worry-free life?" The answer is yes. Jesus instructs us to live without worry. It has never been heard that worrying has any benefits; rather, it is harmful to the spirit, soul, and body.
  - The effect of worry on the human spirit is fear. Fear makes you lose confidence and trust in God. A person cannot receive anything from God when fear dwells in them. It makes an individual vulnerable to the fiery missiles of the adversary. Ephesians 6:16 NLT says, "In addition to all these, hold up the shield of faith to stop the fiery arrows of the devil.”
  - 'Worrying leads to anxiety, and when prolonged, it might result in depression. This affects the neurochemistry of the brain and impacts a person''s overall performance. God wants you to be at your best. He never worries about anything, and He made you in His image and likeness (Genesis 1:27). Represent God well by exhibiting His nature in you: a worry-free life.'
  - I understand that there are times when certain pressures might
  - 'arise. God knew such days would come, and His solution is found in Philippians 4:6 GNT: "Don''t worry about anything, but in all your prayers ask God for what you need, always asking him with a thankful heart." Always take your matters and concerns to God in prayer with thanksgiving, and He will grant your requests.'
prayer: I refuse to worry about anything. I cast all my cares upon the Lord, for He cares for me. In all things, I bring my requests and supplications to God, and He daily loads me with great benefits. In Jesus' name, Amen!
