title: Be Persistent in Prayer
scripture: Isaiah 26:3 (KJV)
verse: 'Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.'
body:
  - l uke 18:1-8 talks about a widow who repeatedly made requests to a judge for justice after he rejected her during her first attempt. Because of her persistence, the judge agreed to grant her request to avoid being bothered further. Our Lord Jesus recommends such an approach in our prayer life. Be persistent until your request is granted.
  - 'Matthew 7:7 says, "Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you." To ask means to make a request. If after making a request, the answer is not given to you, then move to the next level, which is to seek. Seeking involves putting in more effort to find or search for what you are looking for. Let''s say you prayed and did not receive the answers you desired, then you begin to seek or search the scriptures to know what the Bible says about the subject, pray at night, and so forth.'
  - If you do not find answers after asking and seeking, do not give up. Then, knock. Knocking involves force and intensified effort. You knock by including fasting, increasing your prayer time, sowing seeds, increasing Bible study, and repeating these
  - activities. These actions increase the force field, and eventually, the closed doors will open. You will have a testimony. Do not give up.
prayer: Thank you, Father, for You always hear me and always want my joy to be full. I am persistent in prayer. I am strengthened in my inner man by Your Spirit to continue making my requests with thanksgiving. My faith is alive in You, and it produces the right results. In Jesus' name, Amen!
