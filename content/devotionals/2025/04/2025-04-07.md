title: The Name of the Lord is a Strong Tower
scripture: Proverbs 18:10 KJV
verse: The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous runs into it and is safe.
body:
  - The phrase "the name of the LORD" refers to the name of Jesus. The righteous run into it and are safe. This simply means that in Jesus, there is safety. Whoever comes into Christ is safe. Safe from what? Safe from all the evil in this world. To come into Christ is to be born again. The man who is born in Christ has a different experience from the world.
  - 'Psalm 91:2-4 KJV says, "I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust. Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence." There is nowhere else that is safe except in Christ Jesus.'
  - 'Acts 4:12 KJV says, "Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved." There is no other place where you can find safety except in Christ Jesus. That is why Jesus is calling on you today. "Come to me, all of you who are tired from carrying heavy loads, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28 NIV).'
  - Are you tired of carrying the heavy burdens of insecurity, disease, financial difficulties, inflation, natural disasters, and uncertainty about the future? Come to Jesus. His arms are wide open for you. In Him, you will find rest, peace, and safety. If you are not in Christ yet, or if you want to make peace with God, kindly see the back pages of this devotional or reach out to us through the contact information provided. God bless you.
prayer: Dear Heavenly Father, I thank You for the name of Jesus, which is my strong tower. In You, I have peace, joy, love, rest, and safety from evil and the corrupting influences of this world. I will always walk in Your protection and safety. In Jesus' name, Amen!
