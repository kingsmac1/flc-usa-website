title: The Blessings of the Lord Make you Blessed
scripture: Proverbs 10:22 CSB
verse: The blessing of the LORD enriches, and he adds no painful effort to it.
body:
  - To be blessed is to be approved by God. It means to be favored by the Lord. The blessings of the Lord work better for those who truly understand them. True blessings come from God, and they cause others to favor you. Since blessings come from God, those who acknowledge Him in all their ways experience His blessings.
  - The Brenton Septuagint Translation of Proverbs 10:22 says, "The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the righteous; it enriches him, and grief of heart shall not be added to it." Abraham was a righteous man who acknowledged God. He not only experienced the blessings of God, but he was also a blessed man and became the father of a blessed generation (Genesis 24:1).
  - A believer is the seed of Abraham. "And through your offspring, all nations on earth will be blessed..." (Genesis 22:18 NIV). Many are blessed but unaware of their blessings, while others repeatedly experience negative situations. That does not mean you are cursed. You must begin and continue acknowledging
  - 'God in all your ways and consciously declare: "I AM BLESSED, AND I EXPERIENCE GOD''S BLESSINGS EVERY DAY!”'
prayer: I am blessed, and I experience the blessings of God always. Through me, others will be blessed. Everything I have been experiencing that is inconsistent with the blessings of God is terminated now, in Jesus' name. Amen!
furtherStudies: Acts 3:25 KJV, Genesis 12:3 KJV
readingPlan: Jdg 10-11; Luk 14
author: Apostle Chuks
