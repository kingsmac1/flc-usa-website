title: Don't Be Deceived
scripture: Matthew 24:25 NLT
verse: See, I have warned you about this ahead of time.
body:
  - In Matthew 7:15, Jesus warns us about false prophets and teachers. He said, "Beware of false prophets who disguise themselves as harmless sheep but are really vicious wolves." They appear harmless, but their true intention is to destroy the body of Christ.
  - Jesus warned us about this ahead of time, telling us about the emergence of false prophets and teachers in the last days, and we are now in those days. In Matthew 7:16, Jesus tells us how to identify them and be careful, saying, "By their fruits, you will recognize them." There are key differences between true prophets and teachers and false ones.
  - Firstly, their source of information is very important. False prophets may sound accurate, but their source is either divination or human wisdom. They rely on carnal interpretations of the scriptures. "For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life" (Jeremiah 28:16, Jeremiah 14:14).
  - Secondly, consider their motivation. True prophets and teachers are loyal to God above everything else, while false
  - ones are driven by self-interest, personal gain, and a desire for fame. 1 Kings 22:13-14 shows the difference in motivation.
  - The focal point of true prophets is Jesus. They want Christ to be glorified, and their words align with God's will. Lamentations 3:37 reminds us that only God's words will be fulfilled. However, the fulfillment of a prophecy does not always mean it is from God.
  - Finally, discernment is needed in these last days. You must know the scriptures for yourself. Through the Word of God and the Holy Spirit, you can identify falsehood and avoid deception.
