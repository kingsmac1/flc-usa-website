title: Share the Good News
scripture: Romans 1:16 KJV
verse: For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God unto salvation to everyone that believeth.
body:
  - 't he gospel is good news: the glad tidings about Jesus Christ (Mark 1:1). Good news is meant to be proclaimed. When the Lord does something great for you, He expects you to share it with others. Sharing the good news will encourage them in their walk with God and cause others to believe in Jesus.'
  - In Mark 5:19-20, after Jesus healed the madman of Gerasenes, the man became sane and requested to follow Jesus. However, the Lord's response was, "Go home to your family and tell them everything the Lord has done for you and how merciful He has been." The man then went into the towns to proclaim the great things Jesus had done for him. Through his testimony, the Gentiles came to know God, making him the first missionary to the Gentiles.
  - Sharing the good news of the Lord is testifying of His goodness. Revelation 12:11 KJV says, "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony." Sharing the good news is one of the ways to retain your miracles. Be
  - bold to testify about what God has done for you.
  - Mark 5:27-29 shows that the woman with the issue of blood heard about Jesus, acted on her faith, and received her miracle. She probably heard the good news of what Jesus had done for others. When you share the good news with others, you not only make them believe in God, but you also prepare them for a supernatural experience. The good news, which is the gospel, carries the power of God and has the ability to change lives (Romans 1:16). Go and share the goodness of God with others.
prayer: Heavenly Father, I thank you for your grace, mercy, faithfulness, kindness, and goodness in my life. I will not keep quiet anymore. I will go about sharing the good news of your goodness. I thank you for the many lives that will be saved and transformed by the testimony of your goodness. In Jesus' name, Amen!
