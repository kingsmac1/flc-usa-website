title: Be Productive
scripture: Matthew 13:12 NLT
verse: To those who listen to my teaching, more understanding will be given, and they will have an abundance of knowledge.
body:
  - 'In Matthew 13:18-23, we find the parable of the sower, who sowed seeds on four different types of soil: the footpath, the rocky soil, the thorns, and the good soil. The first three types of soil had unproductivity as a common denominator. The fertile and productive soil was outstanding because it yielded results.'
  - From the parable, the different kinds of soil represent the different kinds of people who receive the word, while the sower represents the preacher. The seed that fell on the fertile soil was productive because, "The seed that fell on the good soil represents those who truly hear and understand God's word..." (Matthew 13:23 NLT). In line with this parable, the fundamental key to productivity is continuous listening and studying of God's word, which will grant you more understanding and an abundance of knowledge (Matthew 13:12).
  - From the beginning of the world, God commanded man to be productive (Genesis 1:28). This does not only apply to increasing in material things but also to allowing the word of God to produce its fruits in your life. As evident in John 15:16 KJV, "You have not chosen me, but I have chosen you and
  - ordained you, that ye should bring forth fruit...”
  - God has ordained you to be fruitful. Your role is to give attention to God's word, and you will be productive like the fertile soil.
prayer: I declare that I will always be productive and bear fruits unto the glory of God. In all I do, I will continue to grow in the knowledge and understanding of God's word. In Jesus' name, Amen!
