title: Be Liberal
scripture: Proverbs 11:24–25 (NLT)
verse: Give freely and become more wealthy; be stingy and lose everything. The generous will prosper; those who refresh others will themselves be refreshed.
body:
  - To be liberal is to give generously. In God's kingdom, generosity is not merely an act of kindness; it is a spiritual principle that governs increase. God's economy does not function by hoarding or excessive caution. While wisdom and stewardship matter, Scripture makes it clear that abundance is purely connected to a generous heart, and not to withholding.
  - Proverbs 11 reveals a powerful contrast. One person releases freely and experiences increase, while another holds back and ends up lacking. This shows that true prosperity flows through open hands. God invites you to reflect His nature through generosity and to trust Him as the One who faithfully supplies every need.
  - In the New Testament, Jesus taught this same principle, showing that giving activates a divine cycle of return. What is released in faith in God's Word and this principle does not disappear; it returns multiplied and overflowing. In the kingdom of God, giving is never subtraction. It is seed. Every seed sown carries the potential for increase and harvest.
  - The word stingy speaks of withholding. It describes the tendency to restrain generosity while waiting for perfect conditions. Some pause to analyze their resources, monitor their accounts, or calculate future outcomes before deciding to give. While this approach seems logical, it is not how faith operates. Faith responds to God's Word, not to visible conditions.
  - God wants you to trust Him completely. Proverbs 3:5 urges us to trust in the Lord with all our heart rather than rely on human reasoning. Generosity requires confidence that God remains your source, regardless of what you see. Obedience to His Word always leads to life, provision, and peace. The Apostle Paul reinforces this truth by explaining that the measure of the harvest is connected to the measure of the seed sown (2 Corinthians 9:6). Generous sowing produces generous results. This principle never fails because it is established by God Himself.
  - Choose to give generously. Become a vessel through which God blesses others, and you will always experience continual refreshment, increase, and divine supply in your own life.
prayer: Father, thank You for teaching me the principle of increase through Your Word. I choose generosity as a lifestyle. As I give freely, I walk in increase, supply, and continual refreshment. My life reflects Your generous nature. In Jesus' name, Amen.
