title: God Is At Work In You
scripture: Philippians 2:13 (KJV)
verse: For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of His good pleasure.
body:
  - 'One of the greatest revelations a believer can have is this: God is at work in you. Notice that the Scripture does not say He works for you only, or around you; it says He works in you. His activity is internal, constant, and purposeful. As Philippians 2:13 KJV says, He works “both to will and to do of His good pleasure,” meaning, He shapes your desires and empowers your actions to fulfill His perfect will.'
  - Have you ever paused to consider which GOD is working in you? It is the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, the God of supernatural provision, the God of Jacob, the God who transforms destinies. The God who was with Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, guiding them to record His life and teachings. The God who parted the Red Sea, shut the mouths of lions, raised the dead, and created the universe by His Word. That same God is at work in you right now!
  - His work in you is not delayed. It is active, dynamic, and intentional. He is shaping your heart, aligning your will with His, strengthening your resolve, and preparing you for glorious testimonies. You are not alone, nor are you trying to fix life by
  - your own strength. God Himself is working in you, enabling you to walk in His purposes.
  - What situation are you facing today? A financial challenge, a health concern, family pressure, emotional battles, delayed answers, or an uncertain future? God does not fold His hands watching. He is working in your heart, in your circumstances, in your steps, and in your favor. You are His workmanship (Ephesians 2:10), and He never abandons His work.
  - 'Because God is at work in you, the outcome is guaranteed. It will end in victory. Your part is to believe, surrender, and speak His Word (Hebrews 11:1). Boldly declare: God is working in me both to will and to do of His good pleasure. I am willing, empowered, and enabled to walk in His will. God is active in me, shaping my desires and strengthening my steps. I am His workmanship, and my story is of victory & triumph.'
prayer: Father, thank You for You are working in me. Thank You for shaping my will, strengthening my heart, and guiding my steps. I trust Your work in my life, and I declare that every situation is turning for my good, and I live out the divine life to its fullest in Jesus' name, Amen.
