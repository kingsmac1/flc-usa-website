title: 'God Loves You: Trust Him With Your Life'
scripture: Proverbs 3:5 (KJV)
verse: Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.
body:
  - Trusting God with your life begins with believing that His Word is trustworthy. God's Word is highly dependable, infallible, consistent, and unchanging. You can safely anchor your entire life on it. Come to that settled place in your heart where you say, “As long as God's Word has said it, I will trust it.” This kind of trust removes fear and uncertainty.
  - God never makes promises He cannot keep. 2 Corinthians 1:20 (KJV) says, “For all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us.” Every word God has spoken has His integrity. When you trust Him, you are trusting a faithful God whose words never fail, regardless of circumstances or delays.
  - God delights in those who trust Him wholeheartedly, without conditions, without backup plans, and without divided loyalty; those who trust him like a child. Trusting God like a child means resting fully in His care, even when you do not understand the process; while being double-minded means being unsure, constantly wavering, or keeping alternatives in case God does
  - not come through. But God needs your complete trust.
  - Abraham is a clear example of this kind of trust. God instructed him to leave his home country without revealing the destination. Abraham followed God without hesitation, even when obstacles and delays arose. He did not question God's Words. The Bible says, “He staggered not at the promise of God through unbelief; but was strong in faith, giving glory to God” (Romans 4:20 (KJV). His trust was steady, and God honored it.
  - God is still speaking today, through the scriptures and through His Spirit. What He has spoken to you is enough to stand on. Hold tightly to His Word, refuse doubt, and trust Him with your life. When your trust is settled, your heart remains at peace, and your faith stays strong, knowing that God will do exactly what He says.
prayer: Father, I know you love me. I choose to trust You with my life. Your Word is dependable and sure. I reject doubt and double- mindedness. I trust You completely, without reservation. I hold on to what You have spoken, and what you have written, and I rest in Your faithfulness. My life is secure in You in Jesus' name, Amen.
