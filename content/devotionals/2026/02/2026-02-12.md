title: Firm Foundation In Christian life
scripture: Proverbs 22:6 (KJV)
verse: 'Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.'
body:
  - The Christian life is built, and not stumbled into. God's intention from the beginning has been the establishment of lives that are grounded and stable. And a firm foundation is essential in this process because everything that follows is shaped by what is first laid.
  - The word “foundation” means establishing a basic platform for Christian growth and development. In Proverbs 22:6, many have limited this instruction to child upbringing, but it goes beyond that. It also applies to the discipleship of believers. This is very crucial in the establishment of believers in their walk with God. It is not enough to say the prayer of salvation; God expects that new converts & believers are nurtured and given a good foundation for their Christian growth. Lack of this is the reason many who were once saved return to worldly values.
  - 'The phrase “train up a child” as used in our opening verse involves a deliberate and intentional process of teaching and guiding. In Bible days, education was the responsibility of the family, particularly the parents. The Hebrew word chanak also means to dedicate or initiate, suggesting a formal commitment to moral and spiritual development. This is why Deuteronomy 6:6–7, KJV says, “these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: and thou shalt teach them diligently”. Parents here also imply those who brought others the message of salvation.'
  - A new believer should be pointed “in the way he should go.” He/she should not go any way they like, because “there is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of destruction” (Proverbs 14:12, KJV). This speaks of direction and a defined path. It implies understanding the new believer's spiritual growth, gifts, and calling.
  - 'The phrase “And when he is old” speaks of long-term impact. In biblical understanding, age reflects maturity. What is sown early produces results later. Early training becomes strength in later seasons. Paul affirmed this when he wrote in 2 Timothy 3:15, KJV: “the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation”. This gives believers a firm foundation to thrive in their journey of faith, so they will not depart from it. It will reflect lasting adherence to what has been instilled and the enduring power of Godly instruction.'
prayer: Father, I thank You for the foundation You are establishing in my life. I receive intentional training, sound instruction, and wise direction. I am established, and strengthened to continue in the way You have set for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
