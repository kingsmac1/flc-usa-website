title: Develop Spiritual Sensitivity
scripture: Proverbs 3:5 (KJV)
verse: Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.
body:
  - Develop your spiritual sensitivity. Physical sensitivity is important, but relying primarily on your senses can limit your faith because you begin to trust what you see, hear, or feel more than what the Spirit reveals. Leaning on your own understanding is limited to what the senses can perceive, but being spiritually sensitive allows you to discern God's guidance beyond natural perception. As detailed in Proverbs 3:5 (KJV), “Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.”
  - Being carnally minded brings limitation. “For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace” (Romans 8:6 KJV). A life dominated by the senses struggles to walk in faith, faces uncertainty in decision-making, and experiences spiritual stagnation. Spiritual sensitivity, however, brings life, peace, and clarity. It positions you to respond accurately to God's guidance.
  - Ephesians 5:18 (KJV) Says, “And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit.”. Being filled with the Spirit sharpens your sensitivity to God's voice, helping you recognize His promptings in your thoughts, emotions, and
  - circumstances. Spiritual sensitivity is cultivated intentionally through consistent spiritual practices that attune your heart and mind to God.
  - You can develop spiritual sensitivity by engaging in prayer and meditation on God's Word, spending time daily listening to God, and seeking His guidance. Reading and studying the Word allows God's truths to renew your mind and attune your spirit. Psalm 119:105 (KJV) says, “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” Obedience to those small promptings from the Spirit strengthens trust and increases awareness, while fasting subjects your body to respond to the Holy Spirit rather than your natural desires. Quiet reflection and removing distractions also help you cultivate stillness, enabling you to be more sensitive and hear God more clearly.
  - As your spiritual sensitivity grows, your choices, relationships, and outward responses begins to reflect the life of God. It's vital to know that Spiritual sensitivity is not a gift; it is developed intentionally as you yield yourself to God's perfect will.
prayer: Father, I thank You for the gift of Your Spirit within me. I choose to develop spiritual sensitivity and hear Your voice clearly. I reject carnal thinking and trust in Your guidance. I'm Strengthened to respond promptly to Your promptings, and let my life reflect life, peace, and Your perfect will. In Jesus' name, amen!
