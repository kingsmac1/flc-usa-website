title: The Prevailing Word
scripture: Acts 19:20 (KJV)
verse: So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed.
body:
  - The prevailing Word means that the Word of God has the ability to prevail. God's Word is inherently powerful and transformative, capable of changing lives and societies. It is not ordinary words; it carries divine life and authority. This is why you should have confidence in its ability to prevail against traditional, spiritual, and physical opposition. Wherever the Word of God is given free course, it dominates circumstances and produces undeniable results.
  - The New Living Translation of Acts 19:20 says, “So the message about the Lord spread widely and had a powerful effect.” This means that as the Word of God grows in your life, it will have a powerful effect in your life. Growth precedes effect. When the Word increases in you, its influence becomes evident in your thoughts, decisions, and experiences. This also means God's Word can have a powerful effect on any individual, group of people, family, nation, or any situation.
  - 'Just a quick question: have you given God''s Word permission to grow in you? Have you ever thought that as you give room for God''s Word to grow in you, it will prevail over any situation you are faced with? It surely does. Amen! The Word only prevails where it is welcomed, believed, and acted upon. When you consciously make space for the Word through study, meditation, and confession, it rises to take control of your life.'
  - Jeremiah 23:29 says, “Is not My word like fire,” declares the LORD, “and like a hammer that smashes a rock?” Rocks and mountains signify obstacles and the challenges of life. The phrase “like a hammer that smashes” means that God's Word has the ability to smash (break into pieces) every challenge in life. No obstacle is too hard or too stubborn for the Word. That's the prevailing effect of the Word.
  - Some people can pray for five hours daily and don't give attention to learning and studying the Word. Many times, the answer you need is in the Word, and with the Word, you are able to pray rightly. The Word gives direction to prayer and produces lasting results.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for the prevailing power of Your Word. I give Your Word permission to grow mightily in me. As it grows, it prevails over every obstacle, challenge, and opposition in my life. I declare that the Word of God is working powerfully in me, producing results and victories daily. In Jesus' name, Amen.
