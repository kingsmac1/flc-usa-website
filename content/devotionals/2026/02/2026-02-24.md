title: The Incorruptible Life
scripture: 1 Peter 1:23 (KJV)
verse: Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.
body:
  - 'Salvation ushered you into something extraordinary. When you received Christ, you received eternal life: the very life and nature of God. This life is not symbolic, emotional, or temporary. It is divine, real, and eternal. The new birth introduced you into a realm where life is governed by incorruption.'
  - The apostle Peter describes this life as coming from an incorruptible seed. This seed represents the divine nature imparted to every believer. Unlike natural life, which is subject to decay, limitation, and death, this life is imperishable. It does not deteriorate, weaken, or expire. It carries the same quality of life that flows from God Himself.
  - This new birth is made possible through the living and enduring Word of God. The Word is alive, active, and eternal. It is not confined to time or circumstance. When the Word was received into your heart, it produced regeneration; an entirely new creation. The same Word that gave birth to this life continues to
  - sustain, energize, and preserve it.
  - Because the Word abides forever, the life it produces also abides forever. This incorruptible life within you is the source of strength, growth, and spiritual vitality. It defines your identity, shapes your consciousness, and empowers you to live above corruption and decay. You do not struggle to maintain this life; it maintains you.
  - This revelation establishes confidence and boldness in the Christian walk. What God has planted in you cannot be destroyed. The seed is divine, the life is eternal, and the source is the living Word. You carry within you a life that triumphs over time, weakness, and death.
prayer: I acknowledge the incorruptible life at work in me. I am born of the living and enduring Word, and I carry the divine nature. This life in me is eternal, powerful, and victorious. I live from this life, I grow by this life, and I manifest its reality daily. I walk in strength, confidence, and divine vitality in Jesus' name, Amen.
