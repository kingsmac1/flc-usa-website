title: Endued With Miracle- Working Abilities
scripture: Acts 1:8 (KJV
verse: 'But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me …'
body:
  - Miracles are supernatural manifestations of God. They are events that surpass human understanding and abilities. The source of every miracle is God Himself, and the remarkable truth is that this God lives in you. He dwells within you not only to guide, comfort, and empower you but also to express His power through you.
  - When you encounter someone who is sick, oppressed, or in need, God desires to demonstrate His compassion and glory. Often, He chooses to do it through you. Through your obedience and faith, His miraculous power flows, touching lives, and bringing healing. You are not limited by human strength, for the divine enablement in you surpasses all natural limitations.
  - 'Acts 1:8 highlights this divine empowerment. The Greek word dunamis translated as “power” refers to miraculous ability and dynamic strength: the capacity to effect change. This means that when you step into situations of need, God''s power in you can transform circumstances. Where sickness resides, healing can flow. Where oppression reigns, the enemy can be bound in the name of Jesus. You are God''s instrument, equipped and'
  - trusted to perform miracles, for He has given you everything necessary to be effective.
  - 'Luke 10:19 KJV reinforces this authority: “Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you”. Through the Holy Spirit, you walk with supernatural boldness, overcoming spiritual opposition, and demonstrating God''s glory.'
  - The miracle-working abilities of God are resident in you. Yield to His Spirit, act in faith, and allow Him to perform His wonders through your life. Step boldly into every situation, knowing that God has equipped you with power, authority, and divine enablement to bring heaven's reality to earth.
prayer: Father, I thank You for your miracle-working power at work in me. I walk in Your authority, bringing healing, deliverance, and wherever I go. I am Your instrument, enabled by the Holy Spirit to manifest Your wonders and glorify Your name. In Jesus' name, Amen.
