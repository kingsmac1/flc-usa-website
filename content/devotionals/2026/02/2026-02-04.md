title: Carrier of His Presence
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:16 (NKJV)
verse: Do you not know that you are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?
body:
  - You are a carrier of God's presence. You live with God in you, allowing His presence to naturally flow through your everyday life. Being a carrier of God's presence is not about being “holy enough.” When the Spirit of God lives in you, you carry His love, peace, and guidance wherever you go, even in the small and ordinary moments. Your smile, your words, and your actions all become channels through which people can experience God, even if they do not realize it.
  - 'Jesus promised us a Helper who would remain with us forever: “And I will pray the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may abide with you forever” (John 14:16 NKJV). This Helper, the Holy Spirit, does not remain passive within us. He moves, guides, comforts, and empowers us daily.'
  - Carrying God's presence requires awareness. Paul said, “Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption” (Ephesians 4:30 NKJV). This reminds us that our words, attitudes, and actions matter. The more we yield to Him, the more naturally His love and power flow through us. People notice when God's presence is real in someone.
  - They sense peace, hope, and encouragement simply by being around them.
  - This is both a privilege and a responsibility. Jesus said, “You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden” (Matthew 5:14 NKJV). Wherever you go, at work, at home, in school, or even online, you have the opportunity to shine His light. You do not need to do something dramatic to make an impact. Simple acts of love and kindness can carry God's presence powerfully.
  - The more time you spend with Him, the more naturally His presence flows through you. When this happens, your life becomes a place where others can encounter God, often without even realizing it. Glory to God.
prayer: Thank You, Lord, for Your precious Holy Spirit who lives in me. I carry Your presence wherever I go. I affirm that everything about my existence reflects You, bringing hope, peace, and love to everyone around me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
