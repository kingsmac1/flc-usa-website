title: Filled With Divine Power
scripture: Acts 1:8 (GNT)
verse: But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be my witnesses…
body:
  - 'The Bible says in 2 Timothy 1:7 (GNT): “For the Spirit that God has given us does not make us timid; instead, His Spirit fills us with power, love, and self-control”. The Spirit imparted to us by God is not one that produces fear, weakness, or hesitation. He is the Spirit of power. This power is divine enablement: the supernatural ability of God made active within the believer. It is not limited to physical strength or emotional confidence; it is spiritual authority and capacity to function effectively in God''s purpose.'
  - The Christian life was designed to be lived from a position of power. God never intended His children to struggle through life relying on human ability. Through the Holy Spirit, divine power is deposited within our human spirit, enabling us to live boldly. This power works in harmony with love and self-control, producing a balanced and disciplined life.
  - When you received the baptism of the Holy Spirit, you were endued with this power. This was not a symbolic experience but a real impartation of divine ability. Acts 1:8 reveals that the coming of the Holy Spirit resulted to empowerment for effective
  - witness. The Spirit energizes you from within, causing your words to carry the same power of God and your actions to produce results.
  - This power is not reserved for special moments; it is present now. It strengthens the inner man, eliminates fear, and produces confidence rooted in God's ability rather than human limitation. As you consciously yield to the Holy Spirit, His power finds expression through your words, decisions, and daily conduct. Living with this awareness transforms how you approach life, you can now face Challenges with confidence, The Holy Spirit within you is the guarantee of a powerful, effective, and victorious life.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for the Holy Spirit who lives in me. I am filled with divine power, love, and soundness of mind. Fear has no place in my life. I function with spiritual authority and confidence, energized by the power of the Spirit within me. I speak, act, and live from this divine enablement, fulfilling Your purpose effectively, in Jesus' name. Amen. FURTHER
