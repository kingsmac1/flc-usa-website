title: Designed To Reign
scripture: Romans 5:17 (KJV)
verse: …much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ.
body:
  - Reigning is the language of kings. It speaks of authority, dominion, and influence. You were not designed to struggle through life helplessly; you were designed to reign. This ability to reign is anchored in the vicarious work of Christ. You are not striving to earn authority; you are standing on His finished work. Through Him, grace and righteousness have been lavishly supplied, empowering you to rule in life.
  - 'The reign described in Romans 5:17 is not postponed to the future; it is a present reality. Revelation 5:10 declares, “And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth.” While there is a future physical reign with Christ, God''s intention is that you exercise kingdom authority here and now. You are a king and a priest, called to represent God and to enforce His will on the earth.'
  - Reigning in life means living above circumstances, not under them. Sin, sickness, defeat, fear, and failure were never meant to dominate you. Through Christ, you have received authority to rule over these things. God did not create you to be trapped by pain, oppression, or limitation. His confidence in you is rooted in
  - 'what He has deposited within you: His life, His righteousness, and His power.'
  - You reign by revealing God's glory through your life and by manifesting the dominion mandate He gave from the beginning. Your words, actions, and choices are expressions of your kingly authority. When challenges arise, you respond from a position of victory, not desperation. You enforce what Christ has already accomplished.
  - God takes pleasure in seeing His sons and daughters walk in authority. Make Him proud by exercising your dominion over every negative condition. Refuse to be passive. Stand firm in grace, conscious of your righteousness, and deliberate in your authority. As you reign in life, God is glorified, and His kingdom is made visible through you.
prayer: Father, I thank You for the abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness I have received through Jesus Christ. I reign in life, exercising authority over sin, sickness, fear, and failure. I walk in dominion, revealing Your glory and enforcing Your will on the earth. In Jesus' name, Amen.
