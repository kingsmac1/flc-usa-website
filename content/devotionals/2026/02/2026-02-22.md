title: The Authority of God’s Word
scripture: 2 Timothy 3:16
verse: All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness
body:
  - 'God''s Word has great authority above everything else because it comes directly from Him. The Scriptures are not human ideas; they are God-breathed and divinely given. Every sentence has a purpose: to teach, correct, and train the believer in righteous living. When God''s Word speaks, it speaks with power, precision, and finality, equipping you fully for every good work He has prepared for you.'
  - 'The authority and infallibility of the Word originate from divine inspiration. This inspiration is one infused with the breath of God, similar to how God breathed life into Adam in Genesis 2:7; the same breath that made Adam a living soul. Though the scriptures are written by men, they originated from God Himself. The Holy Spirit guided every writer, ensuring accuracy: “For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost” (2 Peter 1:21, KJV). This divine source guarantees that God''s Word can be trusted completely in every situation and season of life.'
  - 'Jesus clearly affirmed the authority of Scripture throughout His ministry. He quoted it, lived by it, and fulfilled it: “Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law.” (Matthew 5:18, KJV). By honoring Scripture, Jesus established it as enduring and unchanging. If the Son of God submitted Himself to the authority of the Word, then, we all are called to do the same.'
  - 'In the life of the Church, God''s Word remains central. The early believers devoted themselves to teachings grounded in Scripture, and their spiritual growth continued to flow from that commitment: “And they continued steadfastly in the apostles'' doctrine” (Acts 2:42, KJV). A church that stands strong is one that stands on the Word.'
  - 'God''s Word is also the ultimate standard of truth. In these last days where people are coming up with various opinions and shifting of values, the scripture is truth, and it provides us a clear direction: “Thy word is truth” (John 17:17, KJV). It sanctifies, guides, and anchors the believer in God''s will. Moving on, rest in the sufficiency of God''s Word. It contains everything needed for salvation, wisdom, and Godly living. Trust its authority, submit to its guidance, and allow it to shape every area of your life.'
prayer: Father, I thank You for the authority of Your Word. I submit my heart, mind, and life to your word. Your Word instructs me, corrects me, and equips me for every good work. I live by Your word today, and I will always trust it above every other thing in Jesus' name, Amen.
