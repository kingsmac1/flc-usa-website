title: The Effulgence of God’s Glory
scripture: 2 Corinthians 3:18 (NKJV)
verse: But we all, with unveiled face, beholding as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, just as by the Spirit of the Lord.
body:
  - The word “effulgence” means brilliant radiance, splendor, or a shining forth of light. It often describes dazzling brightness, like the sun or stars, and can also refer to a radiant inner glow; like the presence of God shining through someone. God made you to reflect His glory. In essence, you are the effulgence of God's glory. God's Spirit lives in you, and His light shines through your life. Wherever we go, the radiance of His presence can be seen, felt, and experienced by others.
  - As God's glory dwells in us, it changes the way people encounter life. In Paul's epistle in 2 Corinthians 3:18, NKJV, He said that we are being transformed “from glory to glory”. This means the more we abide in Him, the more His brilliance shows through our character, words, and actions. The world may notice our kindness, hope, and joy; and even if they don't name it, God's presence is radiating through us.
  - 'Jesus exemplified perfect effulgence as detailed in John 1:14, KJV: “And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.” He carried God''s glory in every word and action. Today, His Spirit empowers us to carry that same divine'
  - radiance. Wherever we go (home, work, school, or city), our lives can reflect God's splendor and touch the hearts of others.
  - 'Paul writes in Philippians 2: 14-15, NKJV: “Do all things without complaining and disputing, that you may become blameless and harmless, children of God without fault in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world.” When we yield ourselves to God, His brilliance flows freely through us, making us living testimonies of His glory. Being the effulgence of God''s glory is a privilege and also a responsibility. Our lives become mirrors reflecting His divine light. Let''s continue to shine His glory in our world.'
prayer: Father, I thank You that Your Spirit tabernacles in me. I affirm that I am the effulgence of Your glory, and Your light radiates through my life. All my words, actions, and thoughts reflect You and brings hope, love, and joy to others. In Jesus' name, Amen.
