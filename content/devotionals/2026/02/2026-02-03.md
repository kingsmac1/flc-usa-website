title: Bearing Fruits of Life
scripture: Ephesians 5:8 (NIV)
verse: For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light.
body:
  - Before you were saved, darkness described your state. Now, light defines your nature. This is a spiritual reality. You do not merely reflect light; you are light in the Lord. This kind of light is not something you activate occasionally. It is who you are now, and your life is required to reflect this reality consistently.
  - Because you are light, your life produces fruits that correspond with light. Ephesians 5:9–10 (NIV) makes this clear, “For the fruit of the light consists in all goodness, righteousness and truth.” These fruits are not forced behaviors. They are the natural expression of your new life in Christ. Where light is present, darkness cannot dominate.
  - In Ephesians, the Apostle Paul contrasts the works of darkness with the fruit of the light. Darkness thrives in secrecy, confusion, and corruption, but light reveals, orders, and gives life. This truth traces back to Genesis 1:3, where God spoke light into existence, bringing form and beauty out of chaos. Jesus later declared, “I am the light of the world” in John 8:12, revealing that following Him results in spiritual illumination and life. When
  - you received Christ, you did not merely receive illumination and life; you became an illumination and a life giver.
  - The word “consists” as used in Ephesians 5:9 is very important. It shows that goodness, righteousness, and truth are not optional traits or occasional outcomes. They basically form the substance of genuine Christian living. These qualities are the visible proof that Christ's light is active in you. As Jesus taught, identity is revealed by fruit, not claims.
  - 'Your light is meant to be seen and experienced by others. Let it be evident in your character, choices, and interactions. As you walk consciously as a child of light, your life naturally aligns with what pleases the Lord and influences your world for good. Shine on, bear fruit without strain, and live as who you truly are: light in the Lord.'
prayer: Father, I thank You for making me a light. I affirm that I am light in the Lord. I live as a child of light, bearing fruits of goodness, righteousness, and truth. Darkness has no hold on me. I shine to Your glory, in Jesus' name, Amen.
