title: Divine Metamorphosis
scripture: 2 Corinthians 3:18 (NKJV)
verse: But we all, with unveiled face, beholding as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, just as by the Spirit of the Lord.
body:
  - Transformation, or metamorphosis describes the ongoing, Spirit-powered change in every believer. From the moment we receive Christ, God's purpose for our lives is to shape us into the image of His Son. Romans 8:29, NKJV says, “For whom He foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son”. The new self, renewed in knowledge after the image of its Creator, grows continually, guided by the Spirit (Colossians 3:10). This work is certain because it is grounded in God's Word, not in how we feel each day.
  - The process never stands still. Proverbs 4:18, NKJV says, “The path of the righteous is like the first light of dawn, shining brighter and brighter until the full light of day”. From the moment of first faith to our final breath, God's glory in us escalates, radiating with increasing brilliance. Philippians 1:6 reassures us that the Lord will complete the work He has begun, and nothing can halt His purpose.
  - Because Christ has removed the veil, we enjoy open, personal fellowship with God. No longer do we approach Him through
  - intermediaries or shadowed understanding. With unveiled faces, gazing upon the Lord in His Word and in worship, we mirror His glory. This reflection is not static; it is a steady, Spirit- driven metamorphosis that transforms our hearts, minds, and lives into Christlikeness.
  - The brightness of our spiritual reflection intensifies with every step of our fellowship with the Lord. The glory we mirror is sourced entirely in the Spirit, and the transformation is assured. God's ultimate purpose (to display His glory fully in and through His people) is unfolding, moment by moment. Every believer participates in this divine metamorphosis, a life-long journey of renewal, radiance, and triumph in the Spirit. Hallelujah!
prayer: Lord, I thank You for your Spirit that is transforming me daily. I behold Your glory, allowing Your Word to renew my mind, heart, and life. I am being shaped into the image of Your Son, growing brighter and stronger in Your likeness. I walk in Your power, manifesting Your glory in all I do. In Jesus' name, Amen.
