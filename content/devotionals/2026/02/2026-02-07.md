title: Develop Strong desires For the Word
scripture: 1 Peter 2:2 (KJV)
verse: As newborn babies, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby.
body:
  - Spiritual growth begins with desire. Just as a newborn instinctively craves milk, you are called to cultivate a deep appetite for God's Word. Peter's instruction is not a suggestion; it is a command. He calls you to desire; to crave, long for, and hunger after the Word. This desire is deliberate and intentional, not casual or occasional.
  - The Word of God is described as pure spiritual milk. “Pure” means uncontaminated, free from worldly mixtures, human opinions, or false doctrine. God's Word is complete, clean, and trustworthy. “Milk” emphasizes nourishment. While milk is associated with infancy, it is essential at every stage of life. In the same way, the Word remains your source of life, strength, and growth no matter how mature you become.
  - Appetite grows by exposure. The more you feed on the Word, the stronger your hunger becomes. On the other hand, neglect dulls desire. When the Word is absent, spiritual sensitivity weakens. You cannot grow healthy without consistent intake. Growth does not happen by determination alone; God has
  - ordained a means, and that means is His Word.
  - 'Just as natural milk provides the basic nutrients that support development and immunity, Scripture supplies what your spirit needs to thrive. God''s Word brings truth that sanctifies: “Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth” (John 17:17). It also provides strength for lasting growth. In this regard, Acts 20:32 establishes: “And now, brethren, I commend you to God, and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up.” The Word cleanses, builds, corrects, and establishes you.'
  - When the Word of God becomes your daily nourishment, growth becomes inevitable. Your thinking is renewed, your faith is strengthened, and your life begins to align with God's will. Remove the Word, and growth stops. Restore the Word, and life flows again. Make up your mind to develop a strong desire for God's Word. Create time for it. Meditate on it. Feed on it daily. As you do, you will grow steadily, mature spiritually, and walk confidently in all God has prepared for you.
prayer: Dear Lord, stir up in my heart a strong hunger for Your Word. To desire it daily, feed on it consistently, and grow by it continually. Your Word nourishes me, strengthens me, and builds me into all You have designed me to be. I grow steadily and fruitfully by Your truth. In Jesus' name, Amen.
