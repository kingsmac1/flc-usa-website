date: '2026-02-13'
title: Free From Sickness
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:16 (KJV)
verse: Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?
body:
  - 'Your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. GOD lives in you in the person of the Holy Spirit. The Lord was ministering to me some time ago; He gave me an instance of the house where the president of a nation lives. He asked me: can the president live in a house that has roof leakage, or a clogged bathroom? If such happens, such will be fixed or replaced. You are the house of God. He lives in you. He can''t be comfortable living in a house that is faulty.'
  - This fault implies sickness, diseases, and infirmity. What He does is fix the fault by healing your body or replacing whatever went bad in your body. Nothing is too small or too large for Him to restore. Every part of your body is important to Him because it is the dwelling place of His Spirit. He desires that you walk in health, vitality, and strength, fully free from the burdens of illness.
  - This truth is a life-changer. Come to that place where you become fully conscious that God lives in you and He ensures that everything in your body works perfectly. He quickens and invigorates your body. Romans 8:11 (KJV) says, “But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he
  - that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal body by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.”
  - Meditate on this divine reality. Speak it over your body. Declare in faith that every organ, every system, and every cell is operating in harmony and strength. Health is not just the absence of sickness; it is the fullness of God's life flowing in you. Confess it daily, knowing that your body responds to the Spirit of God who lives in you.
  - Faith-filled declarations over your body change your atmosphere. When you believe that God's presence heals, restores, and invigorates, your health aligns with His words. Every weakness is strengthened, every tiredness renewed, and every sickness rendered powerless. You are not merely surviving; you are thriving because the Spirit of God lives in you.
prayer: Father, I thank You that my body is Your temple. I declare that I am free from sickness, disease, and infirmity. Every part of my body works in perfect harmony, quickened and invigorated by Your Spirit. I receive complete restoration and health today. I walk in strength, vitality, and life, because You live in me. Amen.
