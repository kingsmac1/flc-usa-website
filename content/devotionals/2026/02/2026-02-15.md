title: Give No Place To the Devil
scripture: Ephesians 4:27 (NKJV)
verse: ...Nor give place to the devil.
body:
  - John 10:10 (NKJV) says, “The thief does not come except to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly.” This verse clearly reveals the intent behind every work of the adversary. His agenda has no good outcome, only loss and ruin. Life flourishes where his access is denied. Vigilance is therefore essential, because destruction often begins with tolerance rather than open resistance.
  - The enemy rarely advances in obvious ways. He moves gradually, taking advantage of small openings that appear insignificant at first. A thought left unchecked, an attitude excused, or a response driven by emotion can become an entry point. Space that is granted, even briefly, never remains limited. What is allowed to stay eventually seeks control.
  - Give the devil no room because if you give a room, he will eventually take over the entire house. It could be in your marriage, your career, your health or ministry. For instance, in your marriage, it could be the anger and irritation you have been having towards your spouse. That's the first step of the enemy creeping into your home. Not very long, you both will stop
  - 'praying together; next is argument, abuse, separation, and even divorce. All of these will arise because you gave the devil a room: the permission into your home.'
  - 2 Timothy 2:26 (NKJV) says, “And that they may come to their senses and escape the snare of the devil, having been taken captive by him to do his will.” This passage highlights the importance of awareness and discernment. Not giving any place to the devil is maintained by recognizing early signs of entrapment. Sensitivity to the Holy Spirit enables quick detection; then, use the name of Jesus to resist and overturn every attempt to gain control.
  - Your Daily living calls for deliberate guarding of your heart, thoughts, and actions to the principles of God's Word. This keeps every door secured. When no room is granted, you will experience peace.
prayer: Father, I thank You for the authority You have given me in Christ. I declare that my heart, home, and assignments are protected. I refuse every form of compromise and deception. In the name of Jesus, I stand firm and exercise wisdom in all I do. My life remains secure, fruitful, and filled with peace. Amen.
