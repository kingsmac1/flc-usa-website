title: the Life Of Obedience
scripture: Romans 5:19 (NIV)
verse: Just as through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners, so also through the obedience of the one man the many will be made righteous.
body:
  - Every believer is called into a life of obedience; not as a burden to achieve, but as a nature to live from. Obedience is not foreign to us; it is the very life we inherited in Christ. Through His redemptive work, Jesus broke the power of the enemy that once held humanity captive and restrained us from living as God intended. The days of striving in our own strength to obey are over. In Christ, obedience flows from freedom, not fear.
  - The obedience of Christ was intentional and complete, and its purpose is threefold. First, He obeyed as our example. John 6:38, NIV says, “For I have come down from heaven not to do my will but to do the will of him who sent me”. Jesus showed us what true obedience looks like; wholehearted surrender to the Father's will. His life reveals that obedience is not selective but joyful alignment with God's purposes.
  - Second, Christ obeyed as our guarantee. By His perfect obedience, He fulfilled all righteousness on our behalf. Philippians 2:8, NIV says, “He humbled himself by becoming obedient to death; even death on a cross.” In His death, Jesus
  - demonstrated that obedience means readiness to obey to the uttermost. He bore the guilt of our disobedience and offered Himself as a substitutionary sacrifice, opening the way for us to live free from condemnation.
  - Third, Christ obeyed as our leader. The disobedience of Adam, with all its devastating effects, was put away and replaced by the obedience of Christ. Legally, we are justified; declared righteous before God. Vitally, we are united with Christ in His death and resurrection. The Bible says, “We were therefore buried with him through baptism into death… that we too may live a new life” (Romans 6:4, NIV). The life we now live is His life within us.
  - This means obedience is no longer an external demand but an internal reality. As Christ lives in us, His obedient life is expressed through us. We are dead to sin and alive to God, empowered to walk in joyful submission. Obedience is the fruit of our union with Christ and the pathway into the fullness of life God has called us to.
prayer: Father, I thank You that in Christ I have received a new nature; one marked by obedience. I declare that I am dead to sin and alive to God. The life of Christ flows through me, empowering me to walk in obedience joyfully and completely. I walk today in freedom, righteousness, and alignment with Your will, in Jesus' name. Amen.
