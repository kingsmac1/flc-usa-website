title: The God Class
scripture: Genesis 1:27 (KJV)
verse: So God created man in His own image, in the image of God created He him.
body:
  - 'Every system in the natural world operates by class. Class determines identity, capacity, and function. In the spirit realm, God also established a class system; but unlike human systems, it is not based on effort, achievement, or status. It is rooted in origin. From the beginning, humanity was created in the image and likeness of God. This positioned man in God''s class (His species), with the capacity to relate to Him, represent Him, and function in His authority. Scripture affirms this reality: “I have said, Ye are Gods; and all of you are children of the Most High” (Psalm 82:6, KJV).'
  - The God Class is revealed through the principle of shared nature. In biological order, class determines association and interaction. Humans do not fellowship with animals because fellowship flows from shared nature. Chickens do not relate with dogs, and fish do not commune with birds. In the same way, God fellowships with man because man was created with God- consciousness and divine capacity.
  - Another defining purpose of the God Class is dominion. God did not create man merely to exist; He created man to rule.
  - 'Dominion is not ambition; it is inheritance. Our authority flows from our identity in Christ, not our effort. When God declared, “Let them have dominion,” He was expressing the natural outcome of His image within man: “Let us make man in our image… and let them have dominion” (Genesis 1:26, KJV).'
  - 'Through Christ, our place in the GOD Class is fully restored. Sin affected expression, but it never cancelled God''s original intention. In Christ, believers receive the life and nature of God again. We are not just forgiven; we are recreated, restored to sonship, and empowered to live from divine nature. That''s what 2 Peter 1:4, KJV reveals: “That you might be partakers of the divine nature.”'
  - 'The GOD Class also includes a process of growth, discipline, and maturity. Challenges do not disqualify us; they refine us. His Grace ensures our progress at all times. God''s ultimate goal is not public recognition, but inward transformation, until Christ is fully revealed through us: “To be conformed to the image of His Son” (Romans 8:29, KJV).'
prayer: 'I affirm that I am created in the image and likeness of God. I belong to the GOD Class: The God''s species. I walk in fellowship with the Father, operate in divine authority, and manifest God''s life on the earth. I think from the standpoint of my identity, and live as God''s representative. In Jesus'' name, Amen.'
