title: Living For God
scripture: Philippians 1:21 (NKJV)
verse: For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain.
body:
  - 'Living for God is beyond moral behavior or religious activity; it is a surrendered life to Christ. It means you don''t live for yourself any more. Living for yourself simply means living a selfish life: making decisions based on comfort, desire, or convenience without considering God''s will. Selfish living disregards God''s heart and purpose. True Christian living flows from relationship with God, not obligation. We do not live for God to earn His love; we live for Him because we already have it and desire to please Him.'
  - 'Paul urges us: “I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service” (Romans 12:1, NKJV). Living for God involves daily choices to yield our thoughts, desires, and actions to Him. It is a continual offering, shaped by gratitude for His mercy rather than fear of punishment. Each decision to align with His will is a step away from self-centered living and toward Christ-centered living.'
  - 'Jesus made it clear that following Him requires wholehearted commitment: “If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow Me” (Luke 9:23, NKJV). Denying oneself is not self-hatred, but a willing'
  - surrender of self-rule. It is choosing God's desires over personal comfort & His plans over personal preference. This will change your priorities and decisions, ensuring that every action honors Him rather than selfish impulses.
  - Living for God also reshapes our impact in the world. Paul explains, “For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand that we should walk in them” (Ephesians 2:10, NKJV). Doing the will of God becomes a joyful expression of love when we understand that God has designed meaningful works for us.
  - Ultimately, living for God is love in action. When you surrender daily, your live becomes a testimony of His grace, The world is transformed not by self-centered people, but by those who prioritize God above all. Choose daily to live for Him, not yourself, and let every thought, word, and action glorify your Creator.
prayer: Dear LORD, I am fully determined to stop living for myself and live entirely for You. I surrender my desires, plans, and actions to Your will. Let my life honor You, reflect Christ, and fulfill Your purpose. I declare that from this day forward, I live to please You, Lord. In Jesus' name, Amen.
