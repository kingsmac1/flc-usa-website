date: '2026-02-27'
title: Think Big
scripture: Proverbs 23:7 (KJV)
verse: For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he.
body:
  - Your life moves in the direction of your thoughts. You are not separated from what occupies your mind; your thoughts shape your actions, decisions, and outcome in life. When you think small, you live small. When you think big, your life expands in purpose, capacity, and impact. Your mind is powerful.
  - Many people continually dwell on failure, sickness, poverty, loss, and fear. These thoughts drain their strength, weaken their faith, and limit their progress. Yet the same mental energy used to replay negativity can be redirected toward something better, and more productive. Your mind was designed to host visions and possibilities.
  - 'Proverbs 4:23, KJV instructs you clearly: “Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.” Your heart represents the core of your being: your thoughts, intentions, emotions, and will. Guarding it is not optional; it is a continuous responsibility. Like a watchman protecting a city, you must stay alert about what you allow into your heart.'
  - Your heart determines the quality of your life. Just as a spring supplies water that affects everything around it, your inner thoughts influence every outward experience. What you repeatedly think on eventually shows up in how you live. This is why you must deliberately reject negative, limiting, and low-life thoughts. Do not allow yesterday's painful experiences to imprison your future.
  - 'Replace unproductive thoughts with God''s Word. Begin to think big: about growth, purpose, impact, blessings, and progress. Elevate your mindset to be attuned with God''s vision for your life. Philippians 4:8 (KJV) provides us a clear framework: “Whatsoever things are true, honest, just, pure, lovely, of good report… think on these things.” These thoughts will nourish your faith and position you for forward movement'
  - Thinking big is not pride; it is alignment with divine possibilities. As you guard your heart and intentionally feed your mind with the right thought, your life will reflect strength, and excellence. Your thinking sets the ceiling; lift it and watch your life rise accordingly. Glory to God!
prayer: I will always guard my heart diligently. I fill my mind with God's Word, faith-filled, and visionary thoughts. I think big, greatness, and walk in the fullness of what You have prepared for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Proverbs 4:23; Romans 12:2; Philippians 4:8
