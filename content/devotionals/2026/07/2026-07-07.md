title: Christ Is Your Hiding Place
scripture: Psalm 32:7
verse: For thou art my hiding place; thou shalt preserve me from trouble…
body:
  - There are moments in life when your heart becomes tired from pressure, uncertainty, and spiritual battles. Sometimes, situations rise suddenly and leave you searching for peace and stability. In those moments, many people run toward distractions or human comfort, but the believer has a refuge that never fails.
  - Christ is your hiding place. A hiding place is a place of safety. It is where a person runs when danger appears. Throughout Scripture, God continually revealed Himself as a refuge for His people. David understood this deeply. He faced betrayal, opposition, and difficult seasons, yet he continually returned to God because he knew true safety was found in His presence.
  - There are battles that cannot be handled through human strength alone. There are moments when your mind feels overwhelmed and your emotions feel unsettled. Yet, even during those seasons, the presence of God can become a place of inward rest. Sometimes, God may not remove the storm immediately, but He preserves you while you walk through it.
  - You may have experienced seasons where the pressure should have broken you completely; yet, somehow, you remained standing. Your strength may have felt weak, but God continued
  - sustaining you quietly. That is what happens when Christ becomes your hiding place.
  - Many people withdraw from God during difficult seasons when they actually need Him the most. But the secret place is where strength is renewed again. It is where fear begins to lose its grip. It is where the heart becomes steady after being troubled for so long.
  - The enemy often attacks the mind heavily during painful seasons. Thoughts of fear, discouragement, and hopelessness may try to settle into the heart. But when you remain close to God, His presence becomes a covering over your inner life. There is peace that comes from knowing you are not carrying life's burdens alone. You must learn to run toward God instead of away from Him when life becomes difficult. Stay near Him in prayer. Stay near Him in His Word. Stay near Him even when emotions feel unstable. Christ remains your refuge. He remains your covering, and He knows how to preserve those who stay close to Him.
prayer: I declare that Christ is my hiding place and my refuge. I will not be overcome by fear, discouragement, or the pressures of life because God is preserving me continually. My heart finds rest in the presence of God, and my spirit remains secure under His covering. The Lord is strengthening me inwardly and sustaining me always. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
