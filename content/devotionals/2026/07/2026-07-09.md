title: God Sees All You Do For Him
scripture: 2 Chronicles 27:3
verse: He built the high gate of the house of the Lord…
body:
  - 'When many people read about Jotham, they often focus on the fact that he became king. But Scripture also takes time to mention something else: his work for the house of God. The Bible specifically records that he built the high gate of the house of the Lord. That detail matters because it reveals something important about God''s nature.'
  - God notices what is done for Him. Nothing you do sincerely for God escapes His attention. Many times, people feel unnoticed when serving quietly. Some labor faithfully in prayer, giving, service, encouragement, ministry, or support behind the scenes while receiving little recognition from people. But heaven keeps record differently from men.
  - God sees the hidden sacrifices. He sees the consistency. He sees the effort others overlook. Sometimes, the enemy discourages believers by making them feel insignificant. He whispers that their contribution does not matter because it is not public or celebrated openly. But throughout Scripture, God continually showed that He notices the hearts of those who serve Him sincerely.
  - 'Jesus noticed the widow who gave two small mites while others overlooked her. God remembered Cornelius because of his prayers and giving. Even a cup of cold water given in His name was not forgotten before Him. This should cause you to reflect personally: What are you doing for God? Not someday. Not when life becomes easier. Not when everything is perfect. Now. Every believer has been given something that can honor God. Your time, your gifts, your resources, your service, your encouragement, your prayers, your obedience, and your willingness all matter before heaven.'
  - You may not stand on a public platform, but your life can still build something for God's kingdom. Some build through prayer. Some through giving. Some through serving faithfully. Others through strengthening people quietly behind the scenes. God sees every sincere labor done from a heart of love toward Him. Never become weary in doing good for the Lord. What is done for God carries eternal value even when human recognition is absent. And often, the things done quietly before God are the things heaven values deeply.
prayer: I declare that my life will be useful for the kingdom of God. I will not become discouraged, weary, or distracted in serving the Lord. God sees every sacrifice, every act of obedience, every prayer, and every labor I do for His glory. My service to God is not in vain. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
