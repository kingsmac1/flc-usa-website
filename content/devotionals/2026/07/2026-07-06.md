body:
  - Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven - Matthew 5:16, KJV
  - Never think your life is insignificant or without purpose. God created every believer to influence the world around them and reveal His goodness to others. No matter where you are today, you have the ability to make a difference through the life of Christ in you.
  - Many people assume impact only comes through large platforms, public recognition, or great achievements. But often, the greatest changes begin through simple acts of obedience, kindness, encouragement, faith, and love. One word spoken at the right time can strengthen a discouraged heart. One act of compassion can restore hope to someone who feels forgotten.
  - Jesus told us to let our light shine before men. This means you should not hide the life and nature of Christ within you. The word “light” comes from the Greek word phōs, which refers to illumination, brightness, or that which makes things visible. God placed His light within you so others can see His love, truth, and grace through your life.
  - Everywhere Jesus went, He brought transformation. He lifted the broken, comforted the hurting, healed the sick, and gave hope to the hopeless. As His followers, we are called to carry that same spirit of love and compassion into our world.
  - You may not realize how much your attitude, prayers, generosity, encouragement, and example affect people around you. There are individuals whose lives can change simply because you chose to obey God and reflect His character. Never underestimate the impact of a yielded life.
  - Sometimes, the world around us could be dark, difficult, or discouraging. But light becomes most noticeable in darkness. God positioned you in your family, workplace, school, church, and community for a reason. Through you, someone can encounter peace, wisdom, healing, or encouragement.
  - Do not wait until everything is perfect before deciding to serve God wholeheartedly. Start where you are. Use what God has already placed in your hands. The Holy Spirit can use your gifts, your voice, your resources, and your compassion to touch lives in remarkable ways. Choose every day to be intentional about reflecting Christ. As you do, your life will become a testimony of God's grace and a blessing to many.
prayer: Father, thank You for making my life a vessel of light and blessing. Help me to influence others with Your love, wisdom, and compassion. Teach me to recognize opportunities to encourage, uplift, and minister hope to those around me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
