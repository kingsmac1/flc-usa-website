title: Speak Life
scripture: Proverbs 18:21 KJV
verse: 'Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof'
body:
  - Words are powerful. Through words, hearts are encouraged, lives are shaped, and destinies are influenced. God designed speech to carry impact, which is why Scripture repeatedly emphasizes the importance of what comes out of our mouths. Many people underestimate the effect their words have on their lives and on those around them.
  - The phrase “speak life” reflects the truth that our words can release hope, healing, strength, encouragement, and faith. The word “life” in this context comes from the Hebrew word chayyiym, which refers to life, vitality, renewal, and active living. God desires that our speech produce life and not destruction.
  - Throughout the Bible, we see God using words to create, bless, strengthen, and transform. In Genesis, God spoke light into darkness. Jesus spoke peace to storms, healing to the sick, and hope to the broken. His words carried authority because they were aligned with truth and faith.
  - Many times, people speak based only on what they feel or see in the moment. When challenges arise, fear and negativity often dominate conversations. But believers are called to speak differently. Our words should reflect faith in God and confidence in His promises. This does not mean pretending difficulties do not exist. Rather, it means refusing to let hopelessness, bitterness, or fear shape our confession continually. There is enough darkness in the world already. God wants His children to release words that strengthen and uplift others.
  - A person who constantly speaks defeat eventually weakens their own faith. But words filled with faith and truth strengthen the heart and renew the mind. This is why it is important to guard your speech carefully. Speak peace over your family. Speak hope over your future. Speak strength in times of weakness. Speak God's promises over your life.
  - Your words also affect those around you. One encouraging conversation can restore someone who feels weary or forgotten. Kind words can heal wounded hearts and remind people that God has not abandoned them. Choose today to speak words that build, heal, encourage, and bring life.
prayer: Father, help me to guard my words carefully and speak in alignment with Your truth. Let my speech bring encouragement, peace, hope, and strength to others. Teach me to speak faith instead of fear and life instead of negativity. May my words reflect the wisdom and love of Christ daily. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
