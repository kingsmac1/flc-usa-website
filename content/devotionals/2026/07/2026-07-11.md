title: Maintain A Tender Heart Before God
scripture: Matthew 5:8 KJV
verse: 'Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God'
body:
  - One of the most valuable things you can preserve is a tender heart before God. A tender heart remains humble, teachable, sensitive, and responsive to the Holy Spirit. It does not resist God's dealings or become hardened through disappointment and pain.
  - The condition of your heart shapes spiritual perception. Long before outward actions appear, inward conditions are already developing quietly within. This is why you must pay attention to what is happening inside you, not only around you.
  - A hardened heart rarely develops overnight. It often grows gradually through repeated hurt, pride, unresolved offense, disappointment, or continual resistance to conviction. Many people continue functioning outwardly while inwardly becoming spiritually dry. Worship no longer moves them deeply. Prayer becomes routine. Compassion weakens. Spiritual sensitivity slowly fades.
  - 'Ezekiel 36:26 (KJV) says: “A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.” A heart of flesh represents softness toward God. It responds when corrected. It remains open to healing and transformation. It does not hide behind pride or constantly defend its wounds. The enemy understands this, which is why he tries to harden people through painful experiences and unresolved bitterness.'
  - Sometimes, wounded people quietly withdraw from fellowship and spiritual relationships. Isolation may feel safer, but it often creates greater vulnerability. God never intended for you to carry hidden pain alone. Healing begins when your heart stays open before Him.There are moments when the Holy Spirit gently reveals areas that need healing, forgiveness, or surrender. Those moments should not be ignored. A tender heart allows God to continue shaping your character, strengthening your faith, and deepening your spiritual growth.
  - Remain quick to forgive and humble enough to receive correction. Refuse to nurture hidden resentment. Protect your spiritual sensitivity carefully because a tender heart can still hear God clearly even in difficult seasons. When your heart stays tender before God, peace flows more freely. Discernment remains sharp. Joy becomes steady again. Grace continues working deeply within.
prayer: Father, keep my heart soft and yielded before You. Remove every trace of bitterness, pride, and hidden hurt from within me. Teach me to remain humble, sensitive, and responsive to the Holy Spirit. I walk closely with You and grow continually in spiritual strength in Jesus' Name. Amen. FURTHER
