title: This Is The Day Of Salvation
scripture: 2 Corinthians 6:2
verse: Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.
body:
  - One of the greatest mistakes people make is assuming they have unlimited time to respond to God. Many intend to seek Him later, surrender later, repent later, or take their spiritual life seriously later. But Scripture continually reminds us that tomorrow is never guaranteed.
  - Paul wrote in the above opening scripture, “Now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.” Salvation is not something to postpone. God's invitation is extended today. The heart can become hardened when a person continually ignores God's voice. Conviction that is resisted repeatedly can slowly turn into spiritual indifference. This is why Hebrews 3:15 says, “Today, if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.”
  - Jesus came so humanity could be reconciled to God. Through His death and resurrection, forgiveness, mercy, and eternal life became available to all who believe in Him. Salvation is not earned through human effort or personal goodness. It is received through faith in Christ. Many people spend years searching for peace, fulfillment, and purpose in temporary things, yet the soul was created for fellowship with God. Nothing in this world can replace the life found in Christ.
  - The enemy often whispers that there is still plenty of time, but no one knows how long they have on earth. Life moves quickly. Eternity is too serious to treat casually. This is the day to return to God wholeheartedly. This is the day to surrender fully to Christ. This is the day to stop delaying obedience and respond to His call.
  - God is still saving. He is still restoring. He is still calling hearts back to Himself. His mercy remains available today. Jesus said in John 6:37, “Him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.” No matter the past, no matter the failures, His grace is sufficient for the one who turns to Him sincerely.
  - The opportunity to respond to God is a gift. Do not ignore it. If you are not saved yet, I would like to invite you to make Jesus the lord of your life so you can find peace with God. Kindly say the prayer of salvation written out for you at the final page of this devotional.
prayer: Father, thank You for the gift of salvation through Jesus Christ. Thank You because Your mercy is still reaching hearts today. My heart remains responsive to Your voice and fully surrendered to Your will. My life belongs to You, and my heart will continually walk in obedience and faith. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
