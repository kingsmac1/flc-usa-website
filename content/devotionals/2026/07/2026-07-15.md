title: Put On Christ
scripture: Romans 13:14
verse: But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh…
body:
  - When you gave your life to Christ, God did not simply call you to stop certain behaviors. He called you into transformation. Scripture says to “put on the Lord Jesus Christ.” This means your life should gradually begin to reflect His nature, His character, and His way of living.
  - Every day, you are presented with opportunities to reveal either the flesh or Christ. When offense comes, what comes out of you? When pressure rises, how do you respond? When people misunderstand you, does your attitude still reflect Jesus? These moments reveal what is truly being formed within the heart.
  - The flesh naturally wants revenge, pride, bitterness, and self- exaltation. But the nature of Christ produces humility, patience, wisdom, and love. This is why spiritual growth is not only about outward activities. It is about allowing the character of Christ to become visible in your daily life.
  - Many people desire the power of God while resisting the process of transformation. Yet true maturity is not only seen in spiritual gifts or public ministry. It is seen in conduct. It is seen in speech. It is seen in the ability to remain yielded to God even when emotions are rising strongly within you.
  - Putting on Christ requires intentional surrender. Your old nature will constantly try to rise. Anger may try to control your words. Pride may try to shape your reactions. Fleshly desires may try to pull your heart away from God. But the Holy Spirit works within you daily, helping you walk differently.
  - This is why fellowship with God matters deeply. The more time you spend with Christ, the more His nature begins affecting your thoughts, desires, and responses. Things that once felt normal no longer sit comfortably within your spirit because Christ is being formed within you.
  - People should encounter something different when they encounter you. Not because you are pretending to appear spiritual, but because the life of Jesus is becoming visible through your conduct and character. God did not call you to resemble the world around you. He called you to reveal His Son.
prayer: I declare that I am putting on the nature of Christ daily. My words, thoughts, attitudes, and conduct will reflect Jesus. I will not be ruled by pride, anger, bitterness, or fleshly desires. The Holy Spirit is transforming my life and teaching me to walk in righteousness, humility, and love. Christ is being revealed through me continually, and my life will honor God in every season. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
