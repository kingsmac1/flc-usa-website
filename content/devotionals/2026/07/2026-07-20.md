body:
  - That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man - Ephesians 3:16 KJV
  - One of the greatest works God does in the life of a believer happens inwardly. Many people focus only on external results, but God is deeply concerned about the condition and strength of your inner man. He knows that when you are strong within, you can stand firm through pressure, uncertainty, delays, and opposition without losing your peace or confidence.
  - In Paul's prayer for the Church, he asked that believers would be strengthened with might by the Holy Spirit in their inner man. This means God does not expect you to rely only on human strength or emotions to navigate life. He has given you the Holy Spirit to strengthen, sustain, and empower you daily.
  - There are seasons when life feels overwhelming. Sometimes, challenges come unexpectedly, and people begin to feel tired mentally, emotionally, or spiritually. But the beautiful thing about walking with God is that your strength is not dependent on circumstances. The Spirit of God within you renews you continually.
  - The more you fellowship with the Lord through prayer, worship, and meditation on the Word, the stronger your spirit becomes. Fear begins to lose its hold. Anxiety fades. Confidence rises within you because you become more conscious of God's presence and power at work in your life.
  - Paul also prayed that Christ would dwell in our hearts by faith and that we would be rooted and grounded in love. Many believers struggle because they have not fully settled in the love of God. When a person truly understands that they are loved by the Father, insecurity, condemnation, and striving begin to disappear. God's love gives stability to the soul.
  - The love of Christ is deeper and greater than human understanding. It heals wounded hearts, restores broken confidence, and reminds us that we are never abandoned. Even in difficult moments, His presence remains constant.
  - God's desire is not merely for you to survive spiritually but to be filled with all His fullness. He wants your life overflowing with His peace, wisdom, joy, strength, and grace. This is why you must never speak weakness over yourself. The Holy Spirit lives in you, and He is able to strengthen you from within every single day.
prayer: Father, strengthen me with might by Your Spirit in my inner man. Let Christ dwell richly in my heart by faith. Root and ground me deeply in love so I may understand the greatness of Christ's love. Fill me with all Your fullness and let Your presence overflow through my life daily. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
