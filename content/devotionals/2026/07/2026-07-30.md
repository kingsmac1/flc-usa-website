title: Christ Is Your Fountain
scripture: 1 Corinthians 3:11
verse: For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.
body:
  - Afoundation is the base upon which something is built and supported. It determines the strength, stability, and endurance of a structure. No building can stand properly if the foundation is weak. In the same way, the foundation of a person's life determines how they will stand through the pressures, trials, and uncertainties of life.
  - Scripture reveals that Jesus Christ is the only true foundation that cannot fail. Everything built outside of Him is unstable and temporary. Careers, possessions, human approval, emotions, and worldly success may change with time, but Christ remains unshaken forever.
  - 'Jesus explained this in Matthew 7:24–25 when He spoke about the wise man who built his house upon a rock: “And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock.” The storms still came, but the house remained standing because its foundation was secure.'
  - 'Christ is that solid rock. He is often called the Rock of Ages because He is eternal, dependable, and unchanging. Isaiah 26:4 says, “Trust ye in the Lord for ever: for in the Lord Jehovah is everlasting strength.” The phrase everlasting strength carries the picture of an eternal rock that cannot collapse. You are also described as a spiritual house being built upon Christ. 1 Peter 2:5 says, “Ye also, as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house.” A life established on Christ becomes steady even in difficult seasons because its foundation is rooted in truth.'
  - Many people appear strong outwardly until pressure exposes what their lives were truly built upon. Storms reveal foundations. Difficult seasons reveal whether trust has been placed in God or merely in temporary things.
  - Building on Christ means allowing His Word to shape decisions, values, desires, and direction. It means remaining rooted in Him through prayer, obedience, and fellowship with the Holy Spirit. A life built on Christ may still face storms, but it will not collapse. The foundation remains secure because Christ Himself remains faithful.
prayer: Father, thank You because Christ is the firm foundation of my life. Thank You because my heart remains established upon truth that cannot be shaken. My life is rooted in Your Word, and my confidence remains secure in You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
