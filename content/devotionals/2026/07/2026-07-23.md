title: Set Your Mind On Things Above
scripture: Colossians 3:2
verse: Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth.
body:
  - 'The Amplified Bible puts Colossians 3:2 this way: “Set your mind and keep focused habitually on the things above [the heavenly things], not on things that are on the earth [which have only temporal value].”'
  - The word “set” is important. It suggests intention. Your mind will not remain focused on the things of God by accident. There are too many things competing for your attention every day. If you are not careful, you can spend most of your time thinking about what you want to acquire, what people think about you, or how your life compares with someone else's.
  - The “things above” are the things that matter in God's Kingdom. They are the realities of your new life in Christ and the priorities that carry eternal value. Setting your mind above does not mean ignoring your responsibilities on earth. You still have a family to care for. You may have a career to develop or goals you want to accomplish. The difference is that none of these things becomes the center of your life.
  - 'Observe the expression of Jesus in Matthew 6:33 - “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you” Notice the order: seek first the Kingdom. There is nothing wrong with having possessions, pursuing success, or enjoying the good things God provides. The danger comes when those things begin to possess your heart. Something temporary should never become more important to you than something eternal.'
  - This is worth thinking about because your mind can become occupied without you realizing it. You can spend hours worrying about money and only a few minutes thinking about God's Word. You can know everything happening on social media while becoming increasingly unfamiliar with what God is saying to you through Scripture.
  - Take control of where your mind lives. Let Christ occupy your thoughts. Allow God's Word to influence the way you see your future. When your heart is settled on what matters to God, earthly things begin to find their proper place. You can enjoy what God gives you without allowing it to define you. Your life is bigger than what you own or what you accomplish here. Your life is in Christ, and that is where your heart should remain.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for the life I have in Christ. My heart is set on Your Kingdom, and my mind is continually renewed by Your Word. I live each day conscious of what has eternal value. The attractions of this world will not control my heart because Christ is my greatest treasure. I confidently pursue Your purposes, knowing that everything I need is supplied as I put Your Kingdom first. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
