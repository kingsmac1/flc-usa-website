title: Heal The Sick
scripture: Mark 16:17–18, KJV
verse: And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils… they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover
body:
  - Healing has always been part of God's desire for His people. Throughout the ministry of Jesus, we repeatedly see Him moved with compassion toward the sick, the weak, and the broken. He healed diseases, restored strength, opened blind eyes, and brought hope to those society had given up on. Jesus did not merely talk about healing; He demonstrated the love and power of God through it.
  - Before He ascended, the Lord commissioned believers to heal the sick in His Name. This instruction was not given only to a select group of people; it was given to those who believe. The word “heal” comes from the Greek word iaomai, which means to cure, restore, or make whole. God's healing power does more than address symptoms; it brings restoration and wholeness.
  - Many believers desire to see God move through them but often feel unqualified or uncertain. Yet, healing is not based on human ability or personal perfection. It flows from the power of the Holy Spirit and the authority in the Name of Jesus. God simply looks for yielded hearts willing to believe and act on His Word.
  - When Jesus sent out His disciples, He instructed them to preach the Kingdom and heal the sick. The early Church continued in this ministry because they understood that healing reveals God's compassion and points people to Christ. Even today, God still desires to touch lives through His people.
  - When you meet sick people, pray for them. Speak words of faith and life. Be sensitive to the leading of the Holy Spirit. Sometimes, healing may be instantaneous, and at other times, it may be progressive; but always remain confident in God's power and love.
  - Compassion is important in ministering healing. Jesus was often moved with compassion before miracles occurred. People are not projects or opportunities for display; they are souls deeply loved by God. Healing ministry should always reflect the heart and love of Christ. Do not allow fear or doubt silence your faith. The same Jesus who healed in the Scriptures is alive today, and His power has not changed. As you yield yourself to Him, He can work through your hands, your prayers, and your words to bring healing and hope to others.
prayer: Father, thank You for the healing power in the Name of Jesus. Teach me to walk in faith, compassion, and sensitivity to the Holy Spirit. Let Your healing power flow through me to bring restoration, strength, and hope to others. I declare that sickness, pain, and oppression bow to the authority of Jesus Christ. Amen.
