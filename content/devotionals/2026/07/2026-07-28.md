title: Be Virtuous
scripture: Proverbs 31:10
verse: Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.
body:
  - In a world where people are often valued or recognized by appearance, status, or popularity, God still places great value on character. Virtue may not always receive attention publicly, but it matters deeply to God.
  - A virtuous person is someone who chooses to live with integrity, purity, wisdom, and reverence for God. This kind of life is not built only through outward appearance or public image. It is formed quietly through daily choices, private decisions, and a heart that genuinely desires to honor God.
  - 'Many people work hard to look good outwardly while neglecting what is happening within. But Scripture reminds us that character is far more valuable than temporary beauty or public admiration. Proverbs 31:30 says, “Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised.”'
  - Virtue becomes evident in everyday life. It affects the way a person speaks, handles relationships, responds to temptation, and treats others. A virtuous person understands that who they are in private matters just as much as who they appear to be publicly.
  - Living virtuously also requires conviction. There will always be pressure to compromise, fit in, or lower godly standards. But believers are called to live differently. Romans 12:2 says, “Be not conformed to this world.” God desires His people to reflect His nature through their conduct and decisions.
  - Joseph is a powerful example of this. When temptation came privately in Potiphar's house, he chose to honor God rather than satisfy temporary desire. His integrity remained strong even when nobody else would have known.
  - Virtue is not about becoming perfect or acting superior to others. It is about allowing God to shape the heart daily. As believers grow in fellowship with Him, their lives begin reflecting greater wisdom, self-control, humility, and sincerity. True virtue carries a beauty that outward appearance alone can never produce. It brings stability to life and leaves a lasting testimony before both God and people. God still honors those who choose to walk uprightly before Him.
prayer: Father, thank You for continually shaping my heart through Your Word and by Your Spirit. Thank You because my life is being formed in integrity and godly character daily. My decisions, words, and conduct will reflect wisdom and reverence for You. Compromise will not control my life, and my heart will remain established in truth. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
