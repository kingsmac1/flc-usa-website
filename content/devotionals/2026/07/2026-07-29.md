date: '2026-07-29'
title: Call Light Out Of Darkness
scripture: 2 Corinthians 4:6 KJV
verse: For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ
body:
  - One of the first things God did in creation was speak light into darkness. In Genesis, the earth was without form, empty, and covered with darkness, yet God did not focus on the darkness; He spoke light. His Word changed the condition of creation and brought order, beauty, and life where confusion once existed.
  - This reveals an important spiritual principle for every believer. We are called to speak in agreement with God's Word and not merely according to circumstances. The same God who called light out of darkness now shines His light in our hearts through Jesus Christ.
  - The word “light” comes from the Greek word phōs, meaning illumination, brightness, or that which reveals and gives life. God's light drives out confusion, fear, deception, and hopelessness. Wherever His light appears, darkness loses its power.
  - There are moments in life when situations may appear uncertain or overwhelming. Some people respond by speaking fear, negativity, and defeat continually. But believers are called to respond differently. We speak faith. We declare truth. We call forth what aligns with God's promises. This does not mean denying reality; it means refusing to allow darkness have the final word. God has given you His Word so you can release life, hope, and truth into situations around you. Your words carry influence, and faith-filled speech creates room for God's power to work.
  - When Jesus faced storms, sickness, and impossible situations, He spoke with authority. He understood the power of words spoken in faith. Likewise, believers should learn to speak light over their families, health, future, purpose, and circumstances.
  - Perhaps there are areas of your life where things seem unclear right now. Instead of surrendering to discouragement and fear, begin declaring God's promises over those situations. The light of God's Word has the power to transform what appears dark and hopeless. Remember, the light within you is greater than the darkness around you. Christ lives in you, and through Him, you carry divine illumination everywhere you go.
prayer: Father, thank You for shining Your light into my heart through Jesus Christ. I refuse to walk in fear, confusion, or discouragement. Your Word lights my path and brings clarity, hope, and life to every area of my life. Help me to speak faith continually and reflect Your light to the world around me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
