title: Spread The Good News
scripture: Mark 16:15
verse: Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.
body:
  - The word gospel simply means "good news." It is the joyful announcement that God sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to save sinners. Through His death on the cross and His resurrection, forgiveness of sins, reconciliation with God, and the gift of eternal life are available to everyone who believes in Him (1 Corinthians 15:1–4). This is the good news you have been entrusted to share.
  - People hear bad news every day. They hear about violence, sickness, broken families, uncertainty, and disappointment. In the middle of all this, God has given you a message that brings hope. The gospel tells people that their past can be forgiven, their lives can be transformed, and they can have peace with God through Jesus Christ
  - Sharing the gospel is not reserved for pastors, evangelists, or missionaries. Jesus gave this commission to everyone who believes in Him. You may share Christ during a conversation with a friend, encourage a colleague with a Scripture, invite someone to church, or tell another person how Jesus has changed your life. God often uses ordinary conversations to accomplish extraordinary things.
  - Do not allow fear to keep you silent. You are not responsible for changing anyone's heart. Your responsibility is to faithfully share Christ. The Holy Spirit is the One who convicts, draws people to Jesus, and brings new life.
prayer: Lord, thank You for saving me through Jesus Christ and entrusting me with the gospel. I declare that my heart is filled with compassion for those who do not know You. Open doors for me to speak about Christ with wisdom and love. Thank You because the Holy Spirit works through the message of the gospel, bringing people into the saving knowledge of Christ. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
