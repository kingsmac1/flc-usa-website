title: Pray For The Unsaved
scripture: 1 Timothy 2:1
verse: I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men.
body:
  - Every person you meet will spend eternity somewhere. Some know Christ personally, while others have never experienced His saving grace. Before they hear the gospel from your lips, they should be carried to God in prayer.
  - Prayer prepares the heart for the message of Christ. It is God who opens blind eyes, softens hardened hearts, and draws people to His Son. Paul wrote, “For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour; Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth” (1 Timothy 2:3–4). God's desire is that people come to know Him.
  - Think about the people God has placed around you. It may be a family member who has drifted from the Lord. It may be a friend who has never given their life to Christ. It may be a colleague who appears successful on the outside but is searching for peace within. Do not give up on them. No heart is beyond God's reach.
  - Your prayers matter. As you call their names before the Lord, ask Him to reveal Himself to them. Pray that every barrier keeping them from Christ will be removed. Ask the Holy Spirit to create opportunities for them to hear the gospel and to respond with faith. Keep praying even when you do not see immediate results. Saul of Tarsus seemed like the last person who would become a follower of Jesus. He persecuted the church and imprisoned believers. One encounter with the risen Christ changed everything, and the man who opposed the gospel became the apostle Paul (Acts 9:1–22).
  - Never decide that someone is too far from God. His grace reaches farther than you can see, and His power is able to transform the hardest heart. Let your prayers become part of God's work in bringing people into His kingdom.
prayer: Lord, thank You because Your desire is for people to come to the knowledge of Jesus Christ. Thank You for Your mercy that continues to reach those who are far from You. I lift before You those who do not know Christ. Let their hearts be opened to the gospel, and let every obstacle to their salvation be removed. Thank You for drawing many into Your kingdom and for allowing my life to be used for Your glory. In Jesus' Name, Amen
