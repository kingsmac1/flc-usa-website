title: Avoid Mediocrity
scripture: Colossians 3:23
verse: And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men.
body:
  - God did not create you to live carelessly or to settle for less than what His grace enables you to become. Every assignment He places before you deserves diligence because it is ultimately done for Him. Whether anyone notices your effort or not, the Lord sees the attitude of your heart.
  - Excellence begins long before public recognition. It is developed in the quiet moments when you choose to prepare well, finish what you started, and remain faithful in responsibilities that seem small. Those decisions shape your character and prepare you for greater opportunities.
  - Daniel understood this principle. The Bible says, “An excellent spirit was in him” (Daniel 6:3). He served with such faithfulness that even those who opposed him could not find fault with his work. His excellence reflected a life that honored God.
  - Do not become comfortable with average when God has given you the capacity to grow. Learn new skills. Improve what you already know. Welcome correction, and remain teachable. Every step you take to develop what God has entrusted to you becomes an expression of gratitude to Him. Ask yourself today, Am I giving God my best, or only what is convenient? Let your answer be seen in the way you work, the way you serve, the way you represent Christ, in your academics, business, and in your daily affairs. When your desire is to honor the Lord, excellence becomes a way of life rather than an occasional effort.
prayer: Lord, thank You for every opportunity You have given me to serve You. Thank You because Your grace enables me to work with diligence and faithfulness. I will honor You by giving my best in every assignment You place before me. My life will reflect excellence, and everything I do will bring glory to Your name. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
