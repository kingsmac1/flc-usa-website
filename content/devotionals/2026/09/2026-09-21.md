title: Redeemed By Christ’s Precious Blood
scripture: 1 Peter 1:18–19
verse: For as much as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold... But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot.
body:
  - Redemption is one of the greatest gifts God has given you. The word “redeem” means to buy back, to set free by paying a price, or to deliver from bondage. That is exactly what Jesus did for you. He paid the price that you could never pay so that you could belong to Him.
  - Peter reminds us that our redemption was not purchased with silver or gold. Those things have value in this world, but they could never remove sin or reconcile anyone to God. Only the precious blood of Jesus Christ was sufficient. His sacrifice was complete, perfect, and accepted by the Father once and for all.
  - When Jesus shed His blood on the cross, He did more than forgive your sins. He brought you into a new relationship with God. You are no longer separated from Him. You have been welcomed into His family and given the privilege of calling Him Father.
  - Do not allow guilt or the memory of past failures to define your life. The blood of Christ speaks a better word over you. It declares that you have been forgiven, cleansed, and accepted through Him. Your identity is no longer determined by who you were before you came to Christ. It is determined by what Christ accomplished for you.
  - This redemption also calls you to live like Christ while He walked the face of the earth. You were purchased at a great price. Therefore, let your conduct reflect the One who gave His life for you. Every day is an opportunity to honor the Savior whose blood secured your freedom.
  - Whenever you remember the cross, remember your worth in God's eyes. God gave His greatest treasure to bring you back to Him. That is how deeply you are loved.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for redeeming me through the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Thank You because I have been forgiven, accepted, and brought into fellowship with You. I will live each day with gratitude for all that Christ has accomplished on my behalf. My life belongs to You, and I will honor You in all that I do. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
