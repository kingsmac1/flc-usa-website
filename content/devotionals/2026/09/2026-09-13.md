date: '2026-09-13'
title: How Far Can Your Eyes See?
scripture: Genesis 13:14–15
verse: Lift up now thine eyes, and look from the place where thou art... for all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it.
body:
  - After Lot separated from Abraham, God gave Abraham an unusual instruction. He did not tell him to start walking. He first told him to look. God said, “Lift up now thine eyes.” Before Abraham could possess the land, he had to see it.
  - Your vision will often determine the direction of your life. If you can see only your present limitations, you will struggle to believe for anything beyond them. But when you begin to see life through God's Word, your expectations changes. You stop focusing on what seems impossible and start trusting God for whom nothing is impossible.
  - This is why the enemy often attacks your vision before he attacks anything else. If he can convince you that your situation will never change, he can weaken your faith without ever touching your circumstances. But God's Word continually lifts your eyes beyond what is visible. He reminds you that His plans are greater than your fears and that His power is greater than every obstacle.
  - Ask yourself today, “what have I been looking at”? Have you been looking at closed doors, unanswered prayers, and discouraging reports? Or have you been looking at what God has spoken? Faith grows when your heart is filled with His Word instead of your circumstances.
  - Do not allow today's reality to become the boundary of tomorrow's possibilities. God is able to do far more than you can presently imagine. Keep your eyes on Him. Let His Word enlarge your vision, strengthen your expectation, and give you the courage to keep moving forward. The future God has prepared for you is often seen by faith before it is experienced in reality.
  - A planted tree is not constantly uprooted by changing weather. In the same way, a believer rooted in God does not collapse every time circumstances shift. They become spiritually strong, fruitful, and difficult to shake. This is why consistency with God matters so deeply. Stay planted in His Word. Stay planted in prayer. Stay planted in fellowship with Him. Because people who stay connected to God continue growing even in seasons that should have broken them.
prayer: Lord, thank You for opening my eyes to see beyond my present circumstances. My heart is fixed on Your Word, and my faith is anchored in what You have spoken. I will not be limited by fear or by what I see around me. Thank You for leading me into all that You have prepared for my life and for giving me the confidence to follow wherever You lead. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
