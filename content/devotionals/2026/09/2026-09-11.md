title: He Makes All Things Beautiful
scripture: Ecclesiastes 3:11
verse: He hath made everything beautiful in his time...
body:
  - There are times when you look at your life and wonder why certain things are taking so long. You have prayed about them. You have done what you know to do, but what you expected has not happened. Waiting can become challenging when you cannot see what God is doing.
  - Ecclesiastes 3:11 says, “He hath made everything beautiful in his time.” There is something comforting about those words because they remind you that God works with purpose and timing. The word “beautiful” in this verse carries the idea of something being fitting or appropriate. It speaks of something coming into its proper place. What looks unfinished today does not mean God has finished working. Sometimes, you are looking at one part of a story while God already sees the whole picture.
  - Joseph's life is a good example. God had shown him something about his future, but what followed did not look anything like what he had seen. He was sold by his brothers and later found himself in prison after being falsely accused. For a while, his circumstances seemed to be moving in the opposite direction. But God had not forgotten Joseph. At the right time, Pharaoh sent for him. Joseph walked out of prison and eventually became a ruler in Egypt (Genesis 41:39–41). What looked like a forgotten season became part of a much bigger story. You may be looking at some areas of your own life and asking, Lord, when will this make sense? You do not have to force an answer. Keep walking with God. Give Him room to work in ways you cannot presently see. Do not measure your life by someone else's timing either. Their season is not your season. What God is doing with you has its own course.
  - There are things you may understand only when you look back. One day you may realize why a certain door did not open when you wanted it to. You may even thank God for some of the delays you once found unappealing. For now, trust Him. God knows where your life is going, and He knows the proper time for what He has planned for you. He makes all things beautiful in His time.
prayer: Thank You Father for the beautiful work You are doing in my life. I rejoice because my times are in Your hands, and Your wisdom is directing my path. I look forward to each new season with joyful expectation. My heart is at peace because I know You are working everything according to Your purpose. I see goodness ahead, and I am confident that my life will continually reveal the beauty of Your perfect timing. In Jesus' Name, Amen. FURTHER
