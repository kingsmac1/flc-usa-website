title: Grow In Grace
scripture: 2 Corinthians 6:1
verse: We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.
body:
  - GGod's grace is not only what brings you into His family; it also enables you to grow into the person He has called you to become. Every day, His grace is at work, shaping your heart, strengthening your faith, and helping you walk in obedience.
  - From the opening verse above, Paul's appeal was to urge you not to receive God's grace in vain. His concern was not whether you had received grace, but whether you are allowing that grace to accomplish its work in your life.
  - 'Consider Peter''s encouragement in 2 Peter 3:18: “But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ”. Growth is evidence that grace is active. A growing believer becomes more patient, more forgiving, and more eager to please the Lord. God''s grace does not leave you where it found you.'
  - As you follow Christ, keep responding to what He is doing in your life. When He corrects you through His Word, receive it gladly. When He gives you an opportunity to serve, embrace it wholeheartedly. When He stretches your faith, trust Him. Every response of obedience allows His grace to shape you further. Do not become satisfied with yesterday's walk with God. Keep growing. Keep learning. Keep walking closely with Him. There is always more of His wisdom to discover, more of His love to experience, and more of His likeness to reflect. God's grace is still at work in you. Let it continue its beautiful work until Christ is seen more clearly through your life.
prayer: Father, thank You for the grace You have lavished into my life. Thank You because Your grace continues to strengthen me and shape me each day. My heart remains open to Your work, and I will continue to grow as I walk with You. Let my life reflect Christ more clearly so that everything I do brings honor to Your name. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
