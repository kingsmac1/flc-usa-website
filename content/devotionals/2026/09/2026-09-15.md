title: Let Christ Be Formed In You
scripture: Galatians 4:19
verse: My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you.
body:
  - When Paul wrote these words to the believers in Galatia, he was not questioning whether they had received Christ. They were already believers. His concern was that Christ would be fully formed in their lives. He longed to see their thoughts, their attitudes, and their conduct reflect the One they had believed.
  - The Christian life is more than attending church or knowing Scripture. It is allowing Jesus to shape you from the inside out. The longer you walk with Him, the more your life should resemble His. You begin to respond differently to people. You learn to forgive more readily. Pride gives way to humility. Selfish ambition loses its grip as your desire to please Christ grows stronger.
  - This work does not happen overnight. Just as a child grows over time, spiritual maturity is also a process. The Holy Spirit patiently works within you through God's Word, prayer, and the circumstances of everyday life. Every lesson He teaches and every correction He brings is shaping you into the image of Christ.
  - There will be moments when God exposes attitudes that do not
  - reflect His heart. Do not resist His work. Welcome it. A loving Father corrects His children because He desires to see Christ revealed in them.
  - 'Ask yourself this question today: Can people see more of Christ in my life than they did a year ago? That is the direction of healthy spiritual growth. God''s desire is not merely that you know about Christ but that His life becomes visible through you.'
  - This is why fellowship with God matters deeply. The more time you spend with Christ, the more His nature begins affecting your thoughts, desires, and responses. Things that once felt normal no longer sit comfortably within your spirit because Christ is being formed within you.
  - As you continue to walk with the Lord, allow Him to shape every part of your life. The world does not need a better version of you. It needs to see Christ in you.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the work You are doing in my life through Your Spirit. Day by day, Christ is being revealed in me. My thoughts, my words, and my actions will reflect His heart. Continue to shape me according to Your will until my life becomes a clear testimony of Jesus Christ. In Jesus' Name, Amen. season. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
