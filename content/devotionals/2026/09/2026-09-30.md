title: You Are Precious In God’S Eyes
scripture: Isaiah 43:4
verse: Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee...
body:
  - Have you ever noticed how easily people measure their worth by what they have achieved or by the way others treat them? A promotion can make someone feel valuable, while rejection can leave them wondering if they matter at all.
  - Long before you accomplished anything, God knew you. Before anyone spoke words of acceptance or rejection over your life, He had already set His love upon you. Through the prophet Isaiah, He said, “Thou wast precious in my sight... and I have loved thee.” Those words reveal the Father's heart. He does not see you as forgotten, overlooked, or insignificant. He sees you as someone deeply loved.
  - The cross settles every question about your value. God did not send an angel to redeem you. He gave His only begotten Son. The blood of Jesus is the price of your redemption. If heaven considered you worthy of such a sacrifice, never allow the opinions of people to convince you that you are worthless.
  - There will be days when you feel unnoticed. There may be moments when others fail to appreciate you. Do not build your identity on changing circumstances. Stand on what God has spoken. His Word remains the same when feelings change and when circumstances are uncertain.
  - Walk through today with quiet confidence. You are not trying to earn God's love; rather, you are living from it. Let that assurance influence the way you think, the way you speak, and the way you treat others. A heart that knows it is loved finds it easier to extend love, forgive freely, and live with confidence.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for loving me and calling me Your own through Jesus Christ. Thank You because my life has great value in Your sight. My heart is established in Your love, and I will walk with confidence each day, knowing that I belong to You. Let the life of Christ be seen in me, In Jesus' Name, Amen.
