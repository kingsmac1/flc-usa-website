title: The True Meaning Of Love
scripture: 1 John 3:16
verse: 'Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.'
body:
  - Love is one of the most misunderstood words today. People often measure love by feelings, words, or how someone makes them feel. Love is revealed by what you are willing to do for someone else's good, even when it costs you something.
  - Jesus showed us what love looks like. He gave Himself for us. He forgave those who rejected Him. He welcomed those who were overlooked. He served people who could never repay Him. His love was seen in His actions long before it was heard in His words.
  - That same love should be evident in your daily life. Love is choosing to forgive instead of keeping a record of wrongs. It is taking time to listen when someone needs encouragement. It is being present for a friend who is going through a challenging season. It is speaking with gentleness when anger would be the easier response. Sometimes, love means giving quietly without expecting recognition. At other times, it means telling someone the truth because you genuinely care about their relationship with God.
  - Jesus said, “By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another” (John 13:35). People may never read a Bible, but they will notice the way you treat them. Your kindness, your patience, and your willingness to forgive may become the very thing that opens their heart to Christ.
  - 'Before you ask whether people feel loved by you, ask yourself this question: Do my words, my attitude, and my actions make it easier for others to see Jesus? That is the kind of love God desires to produce in your life.'
prayer: Lord, thank You for showing me what genuine love looks like through Jesus Christ. Thank You because Your love is shaping my heart every day. My words will bring encouragement, my actions will reflect Your compassion, and my life will point others to Christ. Let everyone who encounters me experience the love that You have poured into my heart. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
