title: Who is your Role Model?
scripture: 1 Corinthians 11:1
verse: Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ.
body:
  - If you ask people today, “Who is your role model?” it is common to hear the name of a celebrity, successful entrepreneur, athlete, or popular personality. There is nothing wrong with appreciating someone's achievements or learning from what they do well. The concern begins when admiration becomes imitation without considering whether that person's life reflects the values of Christ.
  - A role model is simply someone whose life or behavior you desire to imitate. That means the person you continually look up to can gradually influence the person you become. For you as a Christian, Christ must remain your highest example. 1 Corinthians 11:1 says, “Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ”. Paul was comfortable asking others to follow his example because he had already decided who he was following. His standard was Jesus.
  - To imitate Christ means allowing His life to become the pattern for yours. Watch how Jesus treated people who could offer Him nothing in return. Notice how He responded when He was misunderstood. Look at His devotion to the Father and the way He handled temptation. His life gives you a pattern you can actually follow.
  - This becomes very practical. Before responding when someone offends you, consider how Christ would respond. Before adopting something because it has become popular, ask whether it agrees with the life you have received in Him. You do not have to follow every trend simply because everyone around you has accepted it.
  - People will influence you, so be intentional about whose example you admire. Learn from people who inspire you to become better, but never allow human admiration to replace your devotion to Christ. Jesus is more than someone you admire from a distance. He lives in you by His Spirit, making it possible for His character to find expression through your life. Let Christ remain your standard. The more you know Him, the clearer the pattern becomes.
prayer: Lord Jesus, You are the pattern for my life. I delight in following Your example and allowing Your Word to shape the way I live. My heart is set on becoming more like You each day. I will not be carried away by influences that draw me away from Your ways. Your life is being expressed through me, and I confidently represent You wherever I go. Amen.
