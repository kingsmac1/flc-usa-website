title: Don’t Take God’s Grace For Granted
scripture: 2 Corinthians 6:1
verse: We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.
body:
  - Every grace God makes available for you is for a purpose. He does not bless your life so that His grace remains unused. He pours it into you so that it can be expressed through the way you live, serve, and honor Him.
  - From our opening scripture, Paul's words are both an encouragement and a challenge. It is possible to receive God's grace and fail to respond to it. A person may know the Scriptures but never live by them. Another may recognize God's call but continually postpone obedience. Someone else may receive gifts from the Lord but allow fear, comfort, or distractions to keep those gifts hidden.
  - God's grace invites a response. If God has opened your eyes to His Word, keep growing in it. If He has entrusted you with a gift, use it faithfully. If He has given you an opportunity to serve, embrace it with gratitude. Every act of obedience allows His grace to accomplish its work in your life.
  - 'Apostle Paul had an understanding of this, that was why he could say, “By the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain” (1 Corinthians 15:10). God''s grace shaped his life because he responded to it wholeheartedly. So, pause and reflect: “How am I responding to'
  - the grace God has given me?”
  - Do not waste today's grace by postponing obedience. What God has placed in your hands is too valuable to ignore. Let your life - your words, your decisions, and your service - be a testimony of His grace at work. One day, you will be grateful you did not let it go unused.
prayer: Father, thank You for the abundant grace You have given me through Christ. I acknowledge that Your grace is active in my life, producing growth and fruit. I choose not to waste it. Help me to walk in obedience and faithfulness so that everything You have entrusted to me brings glory to Your name. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
