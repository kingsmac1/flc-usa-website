title: Put On Christ
scripture: Romans 13:14
verse: But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.
body:
  - Every morning, you choose what to wear before leaving your home. In the same way, God's Word tells you to “put on the Lord Jesus Christ.” This is not about putting on a religious appearance. It is about allowing the life of Christ to be seen in the way you think, speak, and respond to the people around you.
  - To “put on Christ” means to embrace His way of living. When you are offended, ask yourself, How would Christ respond? When you are faced with a difficult decision, ask, What would honor Him? When someone needs compassion, become the hands through which Christ expresses His love.
  - This is not something you accomplish through human effort alone. The Holy Spirit works within you, producing the character of Christ as you yield to Him. The more time you spend in God's Word, the more your mind is renewed. The more you walk with the Lord, the more His nature becomes evident in your daily life.
  - People may never ask what church you attend or what books you read, but they will notice your patience when others lose their temper. They will remember your honesty when cutting corners would have been easier. They will see your kindness when everyone else chooses indifference. Those moments become opportunities for Christ to be revealed through you.
  - 'Before you leave your home each day, make this your desire: "Lord, let people see Jesus in me today." There is no greater testimony than a life that reflects the One who has transformed it.'
prayer: Lord, thank You because the life of Christ is at work within me. Thank You for Your Spirit who is continually shaping me into the likeness of Christ. Today, I choose to put on Christ. My thoughts, my words, and my actions will reflect His heart. Let everyone I meet see His love, His grace, and His wisdom through my life. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
