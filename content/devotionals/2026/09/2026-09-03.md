title: Be Filled With Divine Wisdom
scripture: James 1:5
verse: If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.
body:
  - Every day presents you with decisions that shape your future. Some choices are simple, while others carry lasting consequences. In those moments, you need more than intelligence or experience. You need the wisdom that comes from God.
  - Divine wisdom is the ability to see situations from God's perspective and respond in a way that pleases Him. It keeps you from making decisions based only on emotions, pressure, or appearances. It enables you to recognize the right path, even when several options seem appealing.
  - Solomon understood the value of divine wisdom. When God invited him to ask for anything, he did not ask for wealth, long life, or victory over his enemies. He asked for “an understanding heart” to lead God's people (1 Kings 3:9). His request pleased the Lord because he desired wisdom above personal gain.
  - The same invitation is extended to you. James writes, “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God.” God is not reluctant to guide His children. He delights in giving wisdom to those who seek Him with a sincere heart.
  - As you spend time in God's Word, the Holy Spirit renews your thinking and teaches you how to live wisely. Your decisions become shaped by Scripture instead of the opinions of the world. You begin to value what God values, and your life reflects His understanding.
  - Do not rush ahead of the Lord. Seek His wisdom before making important decisions. A moment spent listening to His voice can spare you from years of unnecessary regret. His wisdom brings clarity when you feel uncertain and gives confidence when the way forward seems unclear. Walk with the assurance that God will guide every life that is yielded to Him.
prayer: Lord, thank You because You are the source of all wisdom. Thank You for giving me understanding as I seek You. My mind is being renewed by Your Word and my decisions will reflect Your wisdom. I will not be directed by fear or confusion because Your Spirit leads me in the way I should go. Thank You for ordering my steps each day. In Jesus’ Name, Amen!
