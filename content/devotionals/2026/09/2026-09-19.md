title: Prepare For Eternity
scripture: Hebrews 9:27
verse: And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment.
body:
  - Life passes more quickly than we expect. One day blends into the next, and before long, another year has gone by. It is easy to become occupied with work, paying bills, caring for family, and pursuing personal goals while giving little thought to eternity. Yet, one day, every one of us will stand before God.
  - 'Preparing for eternity begins with Jesus Christ. He said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me” (John 14:6). A relationship with Him is not just preparation for life after death. It changes the way you live today.'
  - When eternity remains in view, your priorities begin to change. You become less concerned about impressing people and more concerned about pleasing God. You value time with Him because you know that your relationship with Christ is the one thing that will last forever.
  - Jesus asked, “For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul?” (Mark 8:36). It is possible to achieve everything the world celebrates and still miss what matters most. No success is worth drifting away from Christ.
  - None of us knows how much time we have. That is why today is so important. If the Lord has been speaking to your heart, respond to Him. If there is an area where you have delayed obedience, surrender it to Him now. Do not wait for a more convenient time. Every day is another opportunity to walk closely with the One who gave His life for you.
  - Live with eternity in your heart. Let every decision build your relationship with Christ, because the greatest joy is not found in what you accomplish on earth but in knowing that one day, you will stand before Him with confidence.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the gift of eternal life through Jesus Christ. Thank You because my future is secure in You. My heart will remain fixed on You, and my life will honor You each day. I am divinely enabled to make choices that please You. Thank You for leading me until the day I see You face to face. In Jesus' Name, Amen..
