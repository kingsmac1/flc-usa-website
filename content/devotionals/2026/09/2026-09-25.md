title: The Fullness Of Christ
scripture: John 1:16
verse: And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace.
body:
  - 'John makes an extraordinary statement: “Of his fulness have all we received.” He does not say that you have received a small portion of Christ or that you are waiting for Him to become more present in your life. Through your union with Him, you have been brought into the abundance of His life.'
  - The word “fullness” speaks of completeness, abundance, and the totality of all that Christ is. Everything necessary for your new life is found in Him. You do not have to search elsewhere for what only Christ can supply. In Him, you have forgiveness, acceptance, peace with God, and the privilege of living by the power of the Holy Spirit.
  - This does not mean you know everything or have reached spiritual maturity. It means your growth comes from the One who already possesses all fullness. Just as a branch receives its life from the vine, your strength, wisdom, and fruitfulness flow from your relationship with Christ.
  - Paul expressed the same thought when he wrote, “For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily. And ye are complete in him” (Colossians 2:9–10). Your completeness is not based on your achievements, your experiences, or the approval of others. It is found in Christ alone.
  - Whenever you feel inadequate, remind yourself where your life comes from. When you need wisdom, look to Christ. When you need strength, depend on Christ. When you need grace to face another day, draw from the inexhaustible riches that are already yours in Him.
  - Do not live as though you are spiritually empty. Live with the confidence that Christ is enough. Every day, draw from His fullness through His Word, through prayer, and through fellowship with Him. The more you know Him, the more His life is expressed through yours.
prayer: Lord, thank You because all that I need is found in Christ. Thank You for making me complete in Him and for filling my life with Your grace. I will continually draw from His fullness, and His life will be seen in me each day. Thank You because Your Spirit is at work within me, helping me grow into the likeness of Christ. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
