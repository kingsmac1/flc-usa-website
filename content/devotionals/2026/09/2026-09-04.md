title: Be Resourceful
scripture: Exodus 4:2
verse: And he said, What is that in thine hand? And he said, A rod.
body:
  - 'When God called Moses to deliver the Israelites from Egypt, Moses focused on what he lacked. He questioned his ability to speak and wondered whether anyone would listen to him. God answered with a simple question: “What is that in thine hand?”'
  - Moses saw an ordinary shepherd's rod. God saw an instrument that would become part of one of the greatest deliverances recorded in Scripture. The rod did not become significant because of what it was. It became significant because it was placed in God's hands.
  - It is easy to believe that you could do more if you had more money, more education, better connections, or greater opportunities. While those things have value, they are not where God usually begins. He often starts with what you already have.
  - Perhaps He has given you a skill that can help someone. You may have knowledge that can solve a problem at work. You may have the ability to encourage, teach, organize, or create. What seems ordinary to you may become a blessing in God's hands when you are willing to use it. Being resourceful means looking beyond limitations and recognizing opportunities. It means asking, "How can what God has given me serve His purpose today?" A resourceful person does not wait for perfect conditions before taking action. They faithfully use what is available and trust God with the outcome.
  - 'Do not underestimate what God has entrusted to you. Place your time, your abilities, and your opportunities before Him. As you do, He can multiply what you offer and use it in ways you never imagined. The question God asked Moses is still worth asking today: "What is in your hand?"'
prayer: Precious father, thank You for everything You have entrusted to me. Thank You because nothing You have given me is without purpose. I will use my gifts faithfully and make the most of every opportunity You place before me. Thank you for granting me wisdom to recognize what is already in my hands, and let my life become a blessing to others and bring glory to Your name. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
