title: Exercise Your Faith
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:7
verse: For we walk by faith, not by sight.
body:
  - Faith is like a muscle. It becomes stronger when it is exercised. God has given every believer the capacity to trust Him, but that faith grows as you choose to act on His Word rather thank your sensory perception.
  - It is one thing to say you believe God. It is another to keep trusting Him when the answer has not yet appeared. Every opportunity to act on His Word, to forgive someone who has maltreated you, give generously, or step into a new assignment is an opportunity to exercise your faith.
  - Peter experienced this when Jesus called him to walk on the water. As long as Peter remained in the boat, he would never have known what it was like to walk where no one else had walked. Faith required him to take a step. It was not the strength of Peter's feet that held him up; it was the power of Christ's word, “Come” (Matthew 14:29).
  - The same is true for you. There will be moments when God asks you to trust Him beyond what feels comfortable. His Word may lead you in a direction that does not make sense to everyone around you. Those moments are not meant to weaken your faith. They are opportunities to discover that God is faithful.
  - Do not wait until you feel fearless before you obey. Faith often moves while questions still remain. Every step of obedience gives you another reason to trust the Lord more. Look back over your life and remember the times God came through for you. Those testimonies are reminders that the God who helped you yesterday has not changed today. Let them strengthen your confidence as you face whatever lies ahead.
  - Keep exercising your faith. The more you trust the Lord, the more you will discover that He is worthy of your trust.
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for the gift of faith and for every opportunity to trust You. Thank You because Your Word is sure, and You are always faithful. I choose to act on what You have spoken, knowing that You will guide my steps. My confidence rests in You, and my faith will continue to grow as I walk with Christ each day. In Jesus' Name, Amen
