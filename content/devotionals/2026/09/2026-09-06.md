title: Divine Turbocharge
scripture: Jude 1:20
verse: But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost.
body:
  - Have you ever started praying when you felt completely worn out, only to realize some time later that you did not feel the same way anymore? Nothing around you may have changed, but something inside you did. You came into prayer tired and came out strengthened.
  - 'Jude gives us an insight into what is happening: “Building up yourselves... praying in the Holy Ghost.” There is an inward strengthening that takes place when you give yourself to prayer in the Spirit. Your mind may not understand every utterance, but your spirit is not inactive. Paul said, “For if I pray in an unknown tongue, my spirit prayeth” (1 Corinthians 14:14).'
  - Think about a car climbing a steep hill. The engine suddenly needs more power than it needed on a level road. A turbocharger forces more air into the engine, allowing it to produce greater power. The hill is still there; what has changed is the power available to climb it.
  - That picture helps explain what I mean by divine turbocharge. There will be seasons when the demands on your life increase. You may have responsibilities that require more patience than usual. You may be carrying an assignment that stretches you beyond what you have done before. At such times, relying only on natural energy can leave you exhausted. Paul prayed that believers would be “strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man” (Ephesians 3:16). God has made provision for inward strength. So, develop the habit of praying in the Spirit beyond a hurried few minutes. There are times to stay there. Pray until your attention moves away from the pressure and your heart becomes conscious of God again. Pray until the fear that felt so loud begins to lose its influence over your thoughts. You may walk into that place of prayer feeling depleted and walk out ready to face what is ahead.
  - The Holy Spirit is not an occasional source of emergency help. He lives in you. His strength is available in the middle of an ordinary Monday just as much as during an intense church service. When you feel that life is demanding more than you naturally have to give, don't immediately conclude that you cannot handle it. There is strength in your inner man. Get spiritually turbocharged!
prayer: Thank You, Holy Spirit, for Your mighty work within me. Divine strength is at work in my inner man. I am refreshed and energized for every assignment before me. As I give myself to prayer in the Spirit, I rise above weariness and move forward with renewed strength. I am equipped for the demands of today, and I accomplish what God has entrusted to me with joy. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
