title: Lessons From Amaziah
scripture: 2 Chronicles 25:2
verse: And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, but not with a perfect heart.
body:
  - Your walk with God is shaped as much by the condition of your heart as it is by your actions. People often see what you do, but God always sees why you do it. That was true of King Amaziah. Scripture says he “did that which was right in the sight of the Lord,” yet it also says, “not with a perfect heart.” He obeyed God, but his devotion was divided. His outward actions appeared right, while his heart was not fully surrendered to the Lord.
  - As you grow in your relationship with God, keep your heart centered on Him. Do not allow your walk with Christ become just a routine. Read His Word because you want to know Him. Pray because you enjoy His presence. Let your obedience flow from a heart that loves Him.
  - Amaziah's life also teaches you the value of trusting God when obedience seems costly. Before going to battle, he hired soldiers from Israel. God instructed him not to use them because the Lord was not with Israel (2 Chronicles 25:7–8). Amaziah had already spent a large amount of money to hire them, so he asked, “But what shall we do for the hundred talents...?” The prophet replied, “The Lord is able to give thee much more than this” (2 Chronicles 25:9). Those words should encourage you today. You never lose by obeying God. What you place in His hands is never beyond His ability to restore. His provision is greater than anything you could lose by following Him.
  - After God gave Amaziah victory, he made a choice that changed the direction of his life. He brought back the idols of the nation he had defeated and worshipped them (2 Chronicles 25:14). It is difficult to understand why someone who had witnessed God's power would turn to powerless idols, but the human heart can slowly drift when it no longer remains fixed on the Lord.
  - Keep your heart fixed on God. Success should even deepen your fellowship with God not to distract you. Every blessing should increase your gratitude. Every victory should remind you that the Lord is your strength. Walk with Him wholeheartedly. A heart fully devoted to God will always find joy in following Him.
prayer: Lord, thank You for knowing my heart and stirring My desire to love You wholeheartedly and to honor You in every area of my life. I choose to trust You even when obedience requires sacrifice because I know Your ways are always good. Thank You for keeping my heart fixed on You and for leading me in the path of life. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
