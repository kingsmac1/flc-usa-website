title: Mind Your Business
scripture: 1 Thessalonians 4:11
verse: And that ye study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands...
body:
  - Have you noticed how easy it is to become interested in someone else's life while your own responsibilities wait for your attention? Conversations about other people can consume hours, yet leave your own work unfinished.
  - 'From the opening bible verse, Paul encourages us: “Study to be quiet, and to do your own business.” This was not to make you become isolated or indifferent to others. It was an invitation to live with focus. God has given you an assignment, and it deserves your attention.'
  - When your eyes are fixed on what everyone else is doing, it becomes difficult to recognize what God is doing in your own life. Comparison steals contentment. Curiosity becomes distraction. Before long, your energy is invested in matters that were never yours to carry.
  - Jesus addressed this when Peter became concerned about John's future. Peter asked, “Lord, and what shall this man do?” Jesus answered, “What is that to thee? follow thou me” (John 21:21–22). In other words, Keep your attention on Me. I know how to lead each of My children.
  - That is a wise counsel for you today. Celebrate what God is doing in the lives of others, but do not abandon your own race. Develop the gifts He has placed within you. Be faithful in your responsibilities. Give your best to the work before you today.
  - There is great freedom in leaving other people's assignments in God's hands. You do not have to understand every decision they make or compare your progress with theirs. The Lord knows how to lead His people. Your responsibility is to walk faithfully where He has placed you. A focused life is often a fruitful life.
prayer: Father, thank You for the work You have entrusted to me. My heart will remain focused on Your direction for my life. I refuse every distraction that pulls my attention away from Your purpose. Teach me to be faithful in the responsibilities You have given me, and let my life produce fruit that brings honor to Jesus Christ. Amen.
