title: Reigning In Life Through Christ
scripture: Romans 5:17
verse: For if by one man's offence death reigned by one; much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ.
body:
  - God did not save you merely so that you could endure life until you get to heaven. Through Jesus Christ, He has made you to live with victory, confidence, and the assurance that sin, fear, and the powers of darkness no longer have dominion over you.
  - Romans 5:17 says that those who have received “abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life.” Notice that Paul does not say you will reign someday. He says you shall reign in life. This is your present inheritance in Christ.
  - To reign means to exercise authority, to rule, or to prevail. It does not mean that life will be free from challenges. It means that your circumstances no longer determine your identity or control your walk with God. Because Christ has overcome the world, you can face every situation with the confidence that He is with you and His grace is sufficient.
  - This authority does not come from your own strength. It flows from your relationship with Christ. He conquered sin through His death, defeated death by His resurrection, and now shares His victory with everyone who belongs to Him. You are not fighting for victory; you are living from the victory Christ has already won. When discouragement comes, remember that Christ is your hope. When temptation comes, remember that sin is no longer your master. When opposition comes, remember that the One who lives in you is greater than the one who is in the world (1 John 4:4).
  - Walk each day with this confidence. You are a child of God. You have received His righteousness as a gift. His Spirit lives within you, and His grace is at work in your life. Let that confidence shape the way you pray, the way you speak, and the way you face every challenge. Christ has made you more than a survivor. In Him, you are called to reign.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for the abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness I have received through Jesus Christ. Thank You because Christ has won the victory, and my life is hidden in Him. I will walk with confidence add exercise the authority You have given me. Thank You for leading me in triumph and revealing the life of Christ through me each day. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
