title: 'God’s Word: The Regulator'
scripture: Psalm 119:133
verse: 'Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.'
body:
  - Life can change very quickly. One phone call can alter your mood. A disappointing report can affect how you see tomorrow. When things are going well, you feel confident; when something goes wrong, fear tries to take over. If you are not careful, you can spend your life allowing circumstances to regulate how you think and respond.
  - A regulator keeps something within the proper range regardless of changing conditions around it. That is a helpful picture of what God's Word should do in your life. Your circumstances may change, but the Word gives you a steady standard from which to live. Instead of allowing every situation to determine your response, you bring your response under the influence of what God has said.
  - This does not mean ignoring reality. If something needs your attention, deal with it. If you receive information that requires action, respond wisely. But do not hand your emotions over to every report that comes your way. There is a difference between acknowledging what is happening and allowing it to control you. When pressure rises, return to the Word and allow it to regulate your thoughts.
  - The psalmist said, “Order my steps in thy word.” That is a
  - powerful way to live. Before fear tells you what to expect, find out what the Word says. Before anger determines how you respond to someone, allow Scripture to govern your conduct. The more God's Word fills your heart, the less easily circumstances can push you from one emotional extreme to another.
  - 'Make this your practice: let the Word regulate you. When circumstances speak loudly, do not immediately surrender your peace to them. Bring your mind back under the authority of Scripture. Let God''s Word determine what you believe about the situation and how you move forward. Circumstances will continue to change, but a life regulated by the Word can remain steady through them all.'
prayer: God's Word governs my life. My thoughts and responses come under its influence every day. I remain steady when circumstances change because my confidence rests in what God has spoken. His Word gives direction to my decisions and keeps my heart established. I live from the Word, and no circumstance has the power to dictate the course of my life. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
