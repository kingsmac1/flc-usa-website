title: Sealed By The Holy Spirit
scripture: Ephesians 1:13 (KJV)
verse: 'In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise.'
body:
  - When you believed the gospel and received Christ, God did more than forgive your sins. He gave you the Holy Spirit and placed His seal upon your life. Scripture says that after you believed, you were “sealed with that holy Spirit of promise.” That seal says something about who you now belong to.
  - In the ancient world, a seal was used to mark ownership and authenticate something as genuine. In the same way, the Holy Spirit is God's mark upon you. Your identity is no longer rooted in your past or in what others have called you. You belong to God.
  - Paul continues in Ephesians 1:14 by describing the Holy Spirit as “the earnest of our inheritance.” An earnest is a pledge or guarantee that more is to come. The Spirit living in you is God's assurance that what He began in salvation will reach its appointed completion. You have already received a foretaste of the inheritance that awaits you.
  - This truth should give you confidence. You do not walk through life as someone trying to convince God to accept you. In Christ, you have been received, and the Holy Spirit bears witness to the new relationship you now have with God. Ephesians 4:30 says, “Grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.” Because the Spirit dwells in you, your life should reflect the One whose seal you carry. Your words and choices should agree with your new identity.
  - Being sealed by the Holy Spirit does not imply you become careless about how you live. It should produce the opposite. When you know that you belong to God, you want your life to honor Him. You become more conscious of His presence and more willing to follow His leading.
  - So, when doubt tries to make you question whether you belong to God, remember what Scripture says. You heard the gospel, you believed in Christ, and God gave you His Spirit. You are sealed by the Holy Spirit. You belong to God.
prayer: Father, thank You for giving me the Holy Spirit. I declare that I belong to You and carry Your seal upon my life. My identity is established in Christ, and I walk conscious of Your Spirit within me. My life honors You, and I confidently look forward to the inheritance You have prepared for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
