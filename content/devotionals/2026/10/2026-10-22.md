date: '2026-10-22'
title: Be Strong And Very Courageous
scripture: Isaiah 41:10 (KJV)
verse: 'Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee.'
body:
  - There are moments in life when you don't have an option other than to be courageous. You may be facing a decision that feels bigger than you, a responsibility you do not feel ready for, or a season where fear keeps trying to speak louder than faith. In those moments, God does not tell you to pretend that the challenge is small. He tells you to be strong because He is with you.
  - 'Joshua received this instruction when he was about to lead Israel after Moses. God said, “Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.” - Joshua 1:9 (KJV). Joshua''s courage was not based on confidence in himself. It was rooted in the presence of God.'
  - The same truth applies to you. Courage is not the absence of fear; it is choosing to obey God even when fear is present. You may feel uncertain, but you do not have to allow uncertainty to control your steps. God's presence gives you a reason to move forward.
  - 'Deuteronomy 31:6 says, “Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee.” God did not promise that there would be no opposition. He promised that His presence would remain with His people.'
  - Sometimes, courage means taking the next step when you would rather retreat. At other times, it means standing firm when pressure is telling you to compromise. You become stronger when you stop measuring the situation only by your ability and begin measuring it by God who is with you.
  - Do not allow fear to make decisions for you. Do not let discouragement convince you that you are alone. God is present, and He is able to strengthen you for what He has called you to do. Be strong. Be courageous. Move forward with the confidence that the One who sent you will go with you.
prayer: Father, thank You that You are with me. I declare that I am strong and courageous because Your presence goes with me. Fear will not control my decisions, and discouragement will not stop my progress. I move forward in faith, confident that You strengthen me and help me. I will stand firm and obey Your Word. In Jesus' name, Amen.
