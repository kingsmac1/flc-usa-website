title: Don’t Give Up
scripture: Hebrews 10:35
verse: Cast not away therefore your confidence, which hath great recompence of reward.
body:
  - Faith is often tested, not at the beginning of a journey, but somewhere along the way. It is easy to begin with enthusiasm when your heart is full of expectation. The greater challenge comes when time passes and what you have believed God for remains unseen. At such moments, the temptation is not always to stop praying; sometimes, it is simply to stop expecting.
  - Scripture says, “Cast not away therefore your confidence.” There is something deliberate about those words. Confidence can be surrendered. You can allow disappointment to slowly weaken what you once believed, until you continue with the language of faith but no longer carry its expectation in your heart. Guard against this. If your confidence was founded upon God's Word, the passing of time has not made His Word less dependable.
  - 'Consider the Syrophoenician woman who came to Jesus pleading for her daughter. At first, “he answered her not a word” (Matthew 15:23). She was not discouraged by His silence. Even when the conversation seemed to offer little reason for hope, she remained before Him. At last, Jesus said, “O woman, great is thy faith: be it unto thee even as thou wilt” (Matthew 15:28). What appeared to be silence did not become the end of her story.'
  - There will be seasons when faith must stand without the encouragement of visible progress. This is where perseverance becomes precious. Do not continually dig up the seed of God's Word to see whether it is growing. Let it remain planted in your heart. Continue in faith. The farmer does not abandon his field because the harvest did not appear the morning after he planted. He understands that there is a season between sowing and reaping.
  - Perhaps you have waited longer than you expected. Do not let the length of the wait determine the strength of your confidence. God has not become less faithful with the passing of time. Keep your heart anchored in His Word and your expectation alive. You have come too far with God to allow discouragement to have the final word. Hold your faith. Your season of rejoicing will come.
prayer: My heart is steadfast, and my confidence in God remains strong. I hold firmly to His Word with joyful expectation, knowing that He is faithful. Every day strengthens my assurance that what God has spoken will stand. I look ahead with hope, confident that my faith will bear fruit and my mouth will be filled with thanksgiving. I will see the goodness of the Lord and rejoice in His faithfulness. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
