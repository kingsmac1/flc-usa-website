date: '2026-10-25'
title: Christ Is Your Radiance
scripture: Colossians 1:13 (KJV)
verse: Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son.
body:
  - Salvation is more than having your sins forgiven. In Christ, you were rescued from the dominion of darkness and brought into a new kingdom. You were not merely given a better life; you were delivered from an old authority and brought under the lordship of Jesus.
  - Before Christ, sin held mankind in bondage, and darkness exercised its influence through deception and spiritual death. But Jesus came as the answer to that condition. His death and resurrection broke the claim of darkness over everyone who believes in Him. Salvation means rescue. It means deliverance. It means that through Christ, you have been brought out from under the authority that once held you.
  - 'This is why the name of Jesus is so important. Acts 4:12 says, “Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.” Your deliverance is not based on human effort, religious activity, or your ability to defeat darkness by yourself. It rests upon what Jesus has already accomplished.'
  - Christ did not save you and leave you halfway between two kingdoms. Colossians 1:13 says you have been “translated into His kingdom”. Your position has changed. The devil is no master. Darkness no longer has the rulership over your life. You now belong to Christ.
  - This does not imply you will never face temptation or spiritual opposition. It means you face them from a new position. You are no longer fighting to become free; you stand in the freedom Christ has given you. The enemy may attempt to influence your thoughts or intimidate you, but he does not have the authority to reclaim what Christ has redeemed.
  - Learn to live conscious of what salvation has done for you. You have been rescued. You have been brought into the kingdom of God's Son. Let the truth of Christ's victory shape how you pray and how you face spiritual opposition. Jesus is not simply the One who will deliver you someday. Through His finished work, He has already brought you out of darkness and into His kingdom of light.
prayer: Father, thank You for delivering me through Jesus Christ. I declare that I have been rescued from the authority of darkness and brought into the kingdom of Your Son. I belong to Christ, and darkness has no dominion over me. I walk in the freedom and victory Jesus has secured for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
