title: Whose Report Will You Believe?
scripture: Isaiah 53:1
verse: Who hath believed our report? and to whom is the arm of the LORD revealed?
body:
  - Imagine sitting across from your doctor and hearing a report you never expected. The test result is not good, and suddenly your mind begins racing ahead. What happens now? How serious is this? What will my life look like from here? A medical report can describe what has been found in your body, but as a believer, you do not have to allow that report to become the final conclusion over your life.
  - 'Isaiah asks a question: “Who hath believed our report?” Faith does not require you to pretend the doctor''s report does not exist. If treatment is needed, receive appropriate care. Take wise steps concerning your health. But there is a difference between acknowledging a medical diagnosis and allowing it to become greater in your heart than what God has said. The doctor tells you what has been detected; God''s Word tells you where to place your confidence.'
  - Isaiah 53:5 says concerning Christ, “He was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities... and with his stripes we are healed.” When a report tries to fill your heart with fear, return to what Christ has accomplished. You can discuss your condition responsibly without making sickness the language of your future. Let the Word of God shape your
  - expectation while you walk through whatever medical care may be necessary.
  - There may be moments when symptoms feel more convincing than Scripture because you can physically feel what is happening. This is where faith becomes personal. You keep your heart anchored in God while taking the practical steps before you. Most importantly, you refuse to surrender your hope. Your diagnosis may have a name, but the name of Jesus remains above every name.
  - So, whose report will you believe? When fear presents its conclusions, let God's Word answer. When your mind begins imagining the worst-case scenarios, bring your thoughts back to God's word. You can respect your doctor's expertise without making a medical report the final authority over your expectation. Stand in faith, receive the care you need, and keep your confidence firmly in God.
prayer: I believe the report of the Lord concerning my life. My heart is filled with faith, and I look to Jesus with confident expectation. I am filled with wisdom for every decision concerning my health. Fear has no authority over my heart. I rejoice in the life of Christ within me, and I confidently expect the goodness of God to be evident in my body and in every area of my life. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
