title: The Path Of Life
scripture: Psalm 16:11 (AMP)
verse: You will show me the path of life; In Your presence is fullness of joy; In Your right hand there are pleasures forevermore.
body:
  - Life comes with many decisions, questions, and uncertainties. At different stages, you may wonder which direction to take, what opportunities to pursue, or what choices will lead you where you are meant to be. The good news is that you were never expected to figure out the path of life by yourself.
  - The psalmist says, “You will show me the path of life.” There is a path for your life, and God knows it. He is the Author of life, so He understands its design better than anyone else. You may have plans, ideas, and expectations, but God sees what you cannot see. He knows where every road leads and what is necessary for the journey ahead.
  - The path of life can be understood as God's way for you to live and walk through life. It includes His wisdom, His direction, His purpose, and the choices that agree with His will. It is like a blueprint. A builder does not simply begin putting materials together and hope the building turns out correctly; he follows the design. In the same way, life works best when you allow God to show you His pattern rather than trying to create your own.
  - You cannot discover this path by yourself only through human
  - reasoning. Intelligence, experience, and advice are valuable, but they have limits. There will be moments when everything looks right outwardly, yet God is leading you another way. This is why you need His Word and the guidance of the Holy Spirit.
  - 'Notice also where this path leads: “In Your presence is fullness of joy.” God''s direction will never lead you away from Him. The path of life is a path of fellowship with God. As you walk with Him, you find a joy that circumstances cannot manufacture and a satisfaction that achievements alone cannot provide.'
  - Do not put yourself under the pressure of having everything figured out. Your responsibility is to stay close to the One who knows the way. Seek Him, listen to His Word, follow His leading, and obey the direction He gives you. You do not have to invent the blueprint for your life. God already knows the path. Let Him show you the way.
prayer: Father, thank You because You know the path of life. I refuse to depend on my understanding alone. Show me Your way and guide my decisions. I declare that I will follow Your Word, listen to Your Spirit, and walk in the path You have prepared for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
