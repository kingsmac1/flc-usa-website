title: You Are The Righteousness Of God
scripture: Romans 5:17 (NIV)
verse: For if, by the trespass of the one man, death reigned through that one man, how much more will those who receive God's abundant provision of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man, Jesus Christ!
body:
  - You did not become righteous before God because of your effort or because you managed to avoid every failure. Righteousness is a gift you receive through Jesus Christ. Adam's disobedience brought sin and death into the human story. Through him, humanity came under the rule of sin, and death marked that broken condition. But God did not leave you trapped under Adam's fall. He gave you a new representative in Christ.
  - The scripture above says that those who receive the abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness will reign in life through Jesus Christ. Notice that righteousness is called a gift. You do not earn a gift; you receive it. God gives you right standing with Himself because of what Christ has done, not because of personal merit.
  - At the cross, Jesus bore your sin, and through faith in Him, you are accepted before God. Second Corinthians 5:21 KJV says, “For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him”. Your confidence is not in your ability to make yourself acceptable. Your confidence is in Christ. Understanding your righteousness in Christ gives you a new foundation for holy living. You do not obey God in order to become accepted; you obey because you have been accepted in Christ. Your conduct can grow out of the identity you have received instead of becoming an attempt to earn God's approval.
  - When you sin, do not run from God as though your standing with Him depends on your perfection. Come back to Him and receive His correction. Condemnation tells you to hide, but righteousness gives you confidence to come before your Father and keep walking in His grace. You are not trying to become righteous by your own effort. In Christ, righteousness has been given to you. Receive this truth and learn to live from it.
prayer: Father, thank You for the gift of righteousness through Jesus Christ. I refuse to build my confidence on my works or performance. I declare that in Christ, I am accepted before You, and I will live from the grace You have given me. Help me to walk in a way that reflects who I am in Christ daily. In Jesus' name, Amen.
