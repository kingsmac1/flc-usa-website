title: The Portion Of Your Inheritance
scripture: Psalm 16:5 (KJV)
verse: 'The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot.'
body:
  - 'There are many things people look to for security in life: money, property, position, relationships, or influence. These things may be valuable, but none of them can take the place of God. David understood this when he said, “The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance.” His greatest treasure was not what he possessed; it was the God to whom he belonged.'
  - When Israel entered the Promised Land, the tribes received territories as their inheritance. The Levites, however, were told, “I am thy part and thine inheritance” (Numbers 18:20). Their portion was not measured primarily by land or possessions. God Himself was their inheritance. David took hold of this truth personally and declared that the Lord was his portion. His security was rooted in God's presence, faithfulness, and provision.
  - If the Lord is your portion, your life is not defined by what you do not have. You may still have desires, goals, and things you are trusting God for, but your satisfaction cannot rest entirely on them. Possessions can be lost, opportunities can change, and people can disappoint you, but God remains faithful.
  - David also said, “The LORD is the portion of my cup.” The cup can represent the portion assigned to you in life. Some seasons bring joy and increase, while others involve waiting, stretching, or circumstances you may not fully understand. Through every season, you can trust the One who holds your life. Then David adds, “Thou maintainest my lot.” God does not merely give you an inheritance and leave you to preserve it by yourself. He watches over what concerns you. Your future does not depend only on your strength, wisdom, or ability. The God who gave you your portion is also able to sustain you through every season.
  - 'Do not measure your life only by what is in your hands. Your greatest inheritance is not land, money, influence, or possessions. Your greatest inheritance is God Himself. In Him, you have a source that does not run dry, a refuge that does not fail, and a treasure that cannot be taken from you. Let your heart rest in this truth today: the Lord is your portion, and He will maintain your lot.'
prayer: Father, thank You for being my portion and my inheritance. I declare that my security and satisfaction are found in You. I trust You with my life, my future, and everything You have placed in my hands. Maintain my lot and help me remain content and confident in You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
