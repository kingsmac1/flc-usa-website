date: '2026-10-30'
title: The Lord Will Not Forsake You
scripture: Isaiah 41:10 (KJV)
verse: 'Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.'
body:
  - To forsake means to abandon someone, to leave them behind, or to withdraw your support from them. This is exactly what God says He will not do to you. People may walk away and circumstances may change, but the faithfulness of God is not as fragile as human commitment.
  - 'Isaiah 41 speaks to God''s people in the face of uncertainty and powerful nations around them. God did not tell them that there was nothing to be concerned about. Instead, He gave them a reason not to be controlled by fear: “I am with thee.” Their confidence was not supposed to come from the absence of trouble, but from the presence of God.'
  - 'Notice how personally God speaks: “I am thy God.” Then He promises, “I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee.” You are not expected to hold yourself together by determination alone. When your strength feels insufficient, God remains your help. When you cannot see how things will work out, His hand is still able to uphold you.'
  - This was consistent with the way God had dealt with His people throughout their history. He had led them through the wilderness and remained faithful even when they wandered. His correction did not mean He had abandoned them. His desire was always to bring them back to Himself.
  - 'Hebrews 13:5 carries the same assurance: “I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.” There may be seasons when you do not understand what God is doing, but His silence is not evidence that He has left you. A delayed answer does not mean you have been forgotten.'
  - 'So, when fear tells you that you are on your own, answer it with the Word of God. The Lord is still with you. His help has not disappeared because your situation became difficult. You can keep moving forward knowing that the One who called you has not walked away. You may not always understand the season, but you can remain confident of this: the Lord will not forsake you.'
prayer: Father, thank You for Your unfailing presence. I declare that I am never abandoned or without help. You are with me, and Your hand upholds me. I refuse to be ruled by fear. I move forward confidently, knowing that You remain faithful to me in every season. In Jesus' name, Amen.
