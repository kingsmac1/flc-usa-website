date: '2026-10-26'
title: Don’t Make Impulsive Promises
scripture: Ecclesiastes 5:2 (KJV)
verse: Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thine heart be hasty to utter any thing before God.
body:
  - Not every decision needs an immediate answer. Sometimes, the wisest thing you can do is slow down and give yourself time to think. Pressure can make you feel as though you must respond quickly, but haste often produces commitments you later regret. What feels urgent today may become much clearer after prayer and proper rest.
  - Scripture warns you against being rash with your words. A promise made in a moment can create an obligation that remains long after the emotion has passed. This is especially important when you are angry, hungry, or exhausted. In those moments, your judgment can be affected, and what feels right now may look very different when you are calm again.
  - 'Proverbs 14:29 KJV says, “He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.” Anger can push you to make a decision simply to prove a point. Tiredness can make you impatient, while hunger or physical discomfort can make you more reactive than usual. This does not mean you can never make a good decision in those conditions, but you should recognize when your present state may be influencing your judgment. You do not lose anything by giving yourself time. Waiting can protect you from unnecessary commitments. Before you say yes, consider what the decision will require from you. Ask yourself whether you have prayed about it and whether your heart is settled. Important decisions deserve more than an emotional response.'
  - The same applies to promises you make to God. Do not speak carelessly because you are moved by the moment. God values sincerity. It is better to make a thoughtful commitment and keep it than to make an impressive promise that you cannot fulfill.
  - When you feel pressured to decide immediately, learn to say, “Let me think and pray about it.” That simple response can save you from avoidable mistakes. Wisdom does not always rush. Sometimes, wisdom tells you to wait until your emotions settle and your thinking becomes clearer. Do not allow a temporary feeling to create a lasting obligation. Slow down, seek God, and make your decisions from a settled heart.
prayer: Father, thank You for the wisdom You have given me through Your Word and Spirit. I declare that I am not ruled by impulse, pressure, or emotion. I make sound decisions with a settled heart and a clear mind. My words are thoughtful, my commitments are sincere, and I walk in wisdom before I speak or act. I am led by Your Spirit, and I choose patience over haste. In Jesus' name, Amen.
