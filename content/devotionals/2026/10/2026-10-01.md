title: Abounding In Every Good Work
scripture: 2 Corinthians 9:8
verse: And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work.
body:
  - 'There is a beautiful confidence in this verse: “God is able.” Your ability to do good is not limited to what you can accomplish through personal strength or what you presently have in your hands. God is able to supply the grace you need so that your life does not become empty or unproductive. He wants you to have enough to fulfill your responsibilities and still have something to contribute wherever He gives you an opportunity.'
  - A good work is anything you do that reflects the heart and purpose of God. It may be helping someone through a difficult season or using your ability to serve effectively. It can be supporting the work of the gospel or quietly meeting a need that nobody else noticed. Not every good work will be seen publicly, and it does not need to be. Its value comes from the good it accomplishes and the God it honors.
  - Paul does not say that you will occasionally participate in good works. He says you may “abound to every good work.” To abound means there is an overflow. God can bring you to a place where doing good becomes part of the way you live. You begin to notice opportunities instead of always waiting for someone to ask. You become willing to use what you have rather than focusing on what you do not have.
  - Do not underestimate what God can accomplish through your availability. Sometimes, you may feel that what you have to offer is too small to matter. Start with what is already in your hands. Give your time where it can make a difference. Use the ability God has given you. When your heart is willing, His grace can make your contribution reach further than you imagined.
  - Expect your life to be productive for God. There are people you can help and good works waiting for your participation. God is able to give you the wisdom and resources necessary to accomplish them. You are blessed not only to have enough, but to abound in every good work.
prayer: God's grace abounds toward me, and I have sufficiency for every good work He places before me. My life is productive and useful in His hands. I recognize opportunities to make a difference and respond with a willing heart. Through me, lives are blessed and the purposes of God are advanced. I abound in every good work and bring glory to Jesus Christ. Amen.
