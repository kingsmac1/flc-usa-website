title: Christ Is Your New Address
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)
verse: 'Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.'
body:
  - When you came to Christ, you didn't simply adopt a new belief. Scripture says that you are now “in Christ.” That phrase speaks of union, identity, and spiritual position. Christ is not a physical location, yet the Bible presents Him as the sphere in which your new life is found. In that sense, Christ has become your new address.
  - 2 Corinthians 5:17 does not only say that Christ is in you. It says, “if any man be in Christ.” You have been brought into a new spiritual reality. Your past no longer has the final word over who you are. You are no longer defined by the life you lived before salvation. In Christ, you have received a new identity.
  - Think about what an address tells you. It tells where someone belongs and where they can be found. Spiritually, you belong in Christ. If someone were to ask, “Where are you?” there is a biblical sense in which you can answer, “I am in Christ.” If they ask where you live, you can say, “My life is in Christ.”
  - 'Paul writes in Colossians 3:3, “For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God.” Your true life is now connected to Him. You are not trying to build your identity from past failures, people''s opinions, or old labels. Your life is hidden with Christ in God. Living conscious of this changes how you see yourself. When condemnation reminds you of who you used to be, remember your address. When fear tells you that you are exposed and alone, remember where your life is found. When the past tries to reclaim your identity, answer it with the truth: I am in Christ.'
  - Your old address was Adam, sin, and separation from God. Your new address is Christ, where you have received new life and acceptance before God. Do not keep introducing yourself by where you came from. Learn to live from where God has placed you. Christ is your new address. Hallelujah!
prayer: Father, thank You for placing me in Christ. I declare that my life is found in Him, and my identity is established in what He has accomplished for me. The past does not define me. I am a new creation, accepted by God, and secure in Christ. I live every day conscious of my position in Him. In Jesus' name, Amen.
