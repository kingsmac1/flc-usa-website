title: Fight The Good Fight Of Faith
scripture: 1 Timothy 6:12
verse: Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called...
body:
  - Your Faith is not for you to remain quiet when circumstances rise in opposition to God's Word. Paul called it a fight, which means there are times when you must take a definite stand concerning what you believe. The fight of faith is not a physical struggle against people. It is refusing to allow circumstances that contradict God's Word concerning you to determine what you believe.
  - Think of someone who has a trained guard dog. When danger appears, the owner does not wrestle the intruder while keeping the dog locked away. The dog is released against the threat. In a similar sense, there are situations where you need to unleash your faith. You have spent time hearing the Word and building your confidence in God; when opposition comes, that faith should be put to work.
  - How do you unleash your faith? You take what God has said and deliberately apply it to the situation before you. You pray from the standpoint of His Word. Your words remain consistent with what you believe, and your actions follow your conviction. You refuse to sit quietly and allow fear to grip you. Faith rises and says, “This circumstance will not have the final say over my life. God's Word will”. Paul did not say, “Watch the good fight of faith.” He said, “Fight.” There are things you should not accommodate when they stand contrary to what God has spoken concerning you. This does not mean living anxiously or trying to force outcomes. It means becoming spiritually determined. You know what God has said, and you refuse to surrender your confidence because something presently looks different.
  - Whatever is standing before you, do not fold your hands and conclude that nothing can be done. Your faith has a purpose. Put it to work. Stand on God's Word concerning that situation and remain there until you see the result. Unleash your faith against everything that challenges God's Word concerning you, and fight the good fight of faith.
prayer: My faith is alive and active. I refuse to surrender to anything that contradicts God's Word concerning my life. I stand firmly on what He has spoken and exercise my faith. I lay hold of everything God has made available to me in Christ, and I remain victorious through faith. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
