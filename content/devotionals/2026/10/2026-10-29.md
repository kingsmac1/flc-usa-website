title: Redeemed From The Curse
scripture: Galatians 3:13 (KJV)
verse: 'Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree.'
body:
  - To redeem means to release someone by paying the required price. That is what Christ did for you. The law revealed God's standard, but it also exposed human sin. Scripture says, “Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them” (Galatians 3:10). No one could establish righteousness before God by keeping the law perfectly.
  - Jesus stepped into that hopeless condition for you. He lived without sin, yet He willingly took the place of those who were guilty. When He hung on the cross, He bore the judgment that sin deserved. As the NLT version of the scripture puts it, “Christ has rescued us from the curse pronounced by the law.” Your rescue was accomplished through His sacrifice, not through your ability to make yourself worthy.
  - The cross was not evidence that Jesus had become sinful. He remained the spotless Son of God. He stood in your place and carried what belonged to you. Isaiah had foretold that the Servant would be wounded for our transgressions, and 2 Corinthians 5:21 says that “the One who knew no sin was made sin for us, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God”.
  - This means you no longer stand before God as someone trying to escape condemnation by personal effort. In Christ, the penalty has been borne. You have been brought into right standing with God and given access to the blessing promised through faith.
  - Being redeemed from the curse of the law does not mean that you will never face hardship in this present world. It means the law's sentence of condemnation is no longer hanging over you. Christ has dealt with the guilt that separated you from God and has brought you into the blessing of belonging to Him.
  - So, do not live as though the price still needs to be paid. Jesus paid it. Receive what He accomplished and stand confidently in the grace of God.
prayer: Father, thank You for redeeming me through Jesus Christ. I declare that Christ has borne the curse and paid the price for my freedom. I stand before You accepted and righteous in Christ. Condemnation will not rule my heart. I live in the grace and blessing You have freely given me through faith in Jesus. Amen.
