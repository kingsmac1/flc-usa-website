title: Be Renewed In The Character Of Your Mind
scripture: Ephesians 4:23 (KJV)
verse: And be renewed in the spirit of your mind.
body:
  - When you received Christ, you became new in Him, but your mind still needs to learn how to agree with the new life you have received. Old ways of thinking do not disappear simply because you are born again. Some thoughts were formed through past experiences, wrong teaching, fear, or habits developed over many years. This is why Scripture tells you to be renewed in the spirit of your mind.
  - 'The renewal of the mind goes more than learning new information. God wants His Word to affect the very character of your thinking: how you interpret situations, how you see yourself, and how you respond to life. Your thoughts should increasingly reflect what God says rather than what your past has taught you.'
  - Romans 12:2 says, “Be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind.” Transformation begins when your thinking comes into agreement with truth (God's Word). If you continually think according to fear, insecurity, or unbelief, those thoughts will influence how you live. But when God's Word begins to govern your mind, your responses begin to change.
  - For example, you may have spent years thinking that you are not good enough or that nothing good can happen through you. Then you discover what Scripture says about who you are in Christ. Renewal happens when you stop allowing the old thought to remain unchallenged and begin accepting God's truth as the standard.
  - This requires consistency. You cannot feed your mind with everything and expect it to naturally think like God's Word. Pay attention to what you repeatedly allow into your thoughts. When a thought rises that contradicts Scripture, do not entertain it simply because it feels familiar. Replace it with truth.
  - Renewal of your mind also affects the way you respond to people. A renewed mind does not have to react the way it once did. You can learn to respond with wisdom where you once responded emotionally. Your mind can be trained by the Word until godly thinking becomes increasingly natural to you. Do not settle for old thinking in a new life. You are in Christ; now, allow His Word to reshape the character of your mind.
prayer: Father, thank You for the new life I have in Christ. I declare that my mind is being renewed by Your Word. My thoughts agree with Your Word, and old patterns no longer govern the way I see myself or respond to life. I think according to Your Word and walk in the transformation it produces. In Jesus' name, Amen.
