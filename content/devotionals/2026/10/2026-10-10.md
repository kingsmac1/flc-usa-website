title: How To Experience Divine Protection
scripture: Psalm 27:5 (KJV)
verse: 'For in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me; he shall set me up upon a rock.'
body:
  - Divine protection is part of the believer's inheritance in Christ. Through salvation, you have been brought into relationship with God and made a member of His household. You now belong to Him. Therefore, you can look to Him as your refuge, keeper, and defender.
  - However, possessing an inheritance and consciously walking in its benefits are not always the same thing. Salvation establishes your relationship with God; fellowship teaches you to live daily in the reality of that relationship. This is why the secret place is so important.
  - Psalm 27:5 gives us the key. David says, “In the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion.” David did not say trouble would never come. His confidence was that when trouble came, God would hide and preserve him. Where is this place of safety? David continues, “In the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me.” The secret is the secret place.
  - 'Psalm 91:1–2 reveals the same principle: “He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.” Notice the word “dwelleth.” The secret place is not somewhere you visit only when danger appears. It describes a life of communion, trust, prayer, and dependence upon God.'
  - Prayer, the Word, worship, and fellowship with the Holy Spirit keep your heart conscious of God as your refuge. You are not trying to earn His protection through these things. Christ has already brought you into relationship with God. Rather, through fellowship, you learn to trust Him, hear His direction, obey His leading, and remain conscious of His presence.
  - Psalm 91 says the one who dwells in the secret place shall “abide under the shadow of the Almighty.” A shadow speaks of nearness. There is security in living close to God and making Him your habitation. Divine protection does not mean you will never encounter trouble. Psalm 27 speaks of “the time of trouble.” The assurance is that in trouble, you have a refuge.
  - So, do not wait for trouble before seeking God. You belong to Him through Christ; now, cultivate daily fellowship with Him. Salvation gives you your place in God; the secret place teaches you to live from that place.
prayer: Father, thank You for making me Yours through Christ. Thank You for being my refuge and protector. Teach me to dwell in Your presence, trust Your Word, and follow Your leading daily. I make You my habitation and rest under Your covering. In Jesus' name, Amen.
