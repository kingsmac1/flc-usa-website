title: Your Words Are Messengers
scripture: Psalm 107:20
verse: He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.
body:
  - Words travel. Once they leave your mouth, they carry the message you have given them. Psalm 107:20 says, “He sent his word, and healed them.” Notice the expression “He sent his word.” The Word was sent with an assignment, and it accomplished what God intended. This gives us a powerful way to think about our own speech. Words are like messengers; they carry something from the person who sends them and communicate what has been placed within them.
  - 'Creation itself shows us the authority of God''s spoken Word. Genesis 1 repeatedly records, “And God said,” followed by the appearance of what He had spoken. “And God said, Let there be light: and there was light” (Genesis 1:3). God spoke into darkness and light appeared. He did not describe the darkness or spend time discussing how empty the earth looked. He released His Word, and His Word accomplished His intention.'
  - As someone created in God's image, you should become more conscious of what you continually say. Proverbs 18:21 says, “Death and life are in the power of the tongue.” Your words should not become messengers carrying fear into your future. Be careful about repeatedly saying, “Nothing ever works for me,” “I will never make it,” or “This situation will only get worse.” You may say such things casually, but words repeated over time can shape your thinking and influence the direction of your actions.
  - Give your words better assignments. Let them carry God's Word concerning your life. Speak healing instead of continually magnifying sickness. Speak progress rather than announcing defeat before you have even started. When circumstances look unfavorable, you do not have to lie about what is happening; you can acknowledge the situation without giving it authority over your speech. Let what God has said become the language that governs your mouth.
  - Before you speak, remember that you are sending something out. Ask yourself, What assignment am I giving my words? Do not mobilize your own words against the future you are believing God for. Fill your heart with Scripture and send forth words that agree with it. Your words are messengers; give them the right message to carry.
prayer: My mouth is filled with words of faith and life. I speak in agreement with God's Word concerning my life and future. My words carry healing and encouragement, and they give expression to what God has placed within my heart. I refuse to use my own mouth against myself. I speak with wisdom and faith, and my words accomplish good purposes. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
