title: Let No Arrogancy Proceed From Your Mouth
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:3 (KJV)
verse: 'Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.'
body:
  - 'Arrogance often reveals itself first in the way a person speaks. It can appear in boasting, belittling others, speaking as though you know everything, or constantly drawing attention to yourself. Hannah''s words in 1 Samuel 2:3 are a strong warning: “Let not arrogancy come out of your mouth.” God hears not only what you say, but He also knows the heart from which your words come.'
  - You may be gifted, successful, knowledgeable, or respected, but none of these gives you a reason to speak proudly. Everything you have is ultimately received from God. When you remember that, humility becomes easier. You can be confident without being arrogant, and you can speak with conviction without making others feel small.
  - Colossians 4:6 says, “Let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt.” Salt gives savour; it does not destroy the meal. In the same way, your words should add grace to a conversation rather than leave people wounded by pride. Meekness does not mean weakness. It means strength that is under control.
  - Pride in speech can also make correction difficult. When you always need to prove that you are right, you stop listening. When you constantly compare yourself with others, you begin to measure your worth by superiority. God does not want you to live above people; He wants you to walk humbly before Him.
  - Hannah also reminds us that “the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.” God sees beyond impressive words. He knows motives and the condition of the heart. This should make you careful about what comes out of your mouth.
  - Let your words reflect the grace God has shown you. Speak confidently, but remain humble. Celebrate what God is doing in your life without using it to diminish someone else. If you have achieved something, give Him the glory. Your speech says much about what is happening within you. Let humility be heard in your words.
prayer: Father, thank You for teaching me to walk in humility. I declare that my words are gracious, measured, and free from pride. I speak with divine wisdom and respect, and I give You the glory for every good thing in my life. My heart remains teachable, and my speech reflects Your grace. In Jesus' name, Amen.
