title: He Gives You Divine Counsel
scripture: Psalm 16:7 (AMP)
verse: I will bless the LORD who has counseled me; Indeed, my heart (mind) instructs me in the night.
body:
  - There are moments in life when you simply are not sure of what to do. You may have options before you, people giving different opinions, and your own thoughts pulling you in several directions. In moments like these, you need more than human advice. You need divine counsel.
  - David said, “I will bless the LORD who has counseled me.” He recognized that God was not silent concerning his life. Again and again, when he faced danger, uncertainty, or important decisions, he sought the Lord for direction. He did not assume that experience alone was enough. He wanted to know what God was saying.
  - God still counsels us today. He guides you through His Word and by the Holy Spirit. His counsel will never contradict what He has already revealed in Scripture. His Word gives light to your path, corrects wrong thinking, and helps you recognize what agrees with His will.
  - Sometimes, God's counsel comes by bringing a scripture strongly to your heart. At other times, the Holy Spirit may give you an inner caution, a sense of restraint, or a clear conviction about the direction you should take. This is why fellowship with God is so important. The more familiar you become with His Word and His ways, the easier it becomes to recognize His leading.
  - David also said, “Indeed, my heart instructs me in the night.” There are times when everything becomes quiet and God brings understanding to your heart. An answer you could not see earlier may become clearer. A decision you were rushing into may suddenly require reconsideration. God can use those quiet moments to settle your heart and direct your steps.
  - You do not have to make every decision by trial and error. You have access to the wisdom of the One who sees what you cannot see. Before making important choices, learn to ask, “Lord, what would You have me do?” Then give Him room to guide you. Human wisdom has limits, but God sees the whole picture. His counsel can keep you from unnecessary mistakes and lead you into the path He has prepared for you. Make it a habit to seek His counsel before you move.
prayer: Father, thank You because You are my Counselor. I choose not to depend on my understanding. I thank you for your guidance through Your Word and by Your Spirit. Your wisdom works in me for every decision and I will always recognize Your direction clearly. I declare that I will not be confused, because You will instruct me and lead me in the way I should go. In Jesus' name, Amen.
