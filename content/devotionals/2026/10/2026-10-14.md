title: The Abiding Presence
scripture: John 14:16 (KJV)
verse: And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever.
body:
  - One of the greatest realities of your life in Christ is that you are not alone and will never be alone. God did not save you only to visit you occasionally. He came to make His home in you. Through the Holy Spirit, His presence is not temporary, seasonal, or dependent on circumstances. He abides with you.
  - To abide means to remain, stay, continue, and dwell. Jesus said concerning the Holy Spirit, “that he may abide with you for ever.” This means the presence of God in your life is not based on whether you feel Him at a particular moment. Feelings may change, circumstances may change, and your environment may change, but His presence remains.
  - There may be moments when you strongly sense the presence of God during prayer, worship, or fellowship. There may also be moments when you do not feel anything unusual. Yet the absence of a feeling does not mean the absence of God. You must learn to live with the consciousness that He is always with you.
  - Jesus said, “I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.” - Hebrews 13:5 (KJV). This gives you confidence wherever you go. You can walk into difficult situations knowing that you are not entering them alone. You can face uncertainty knowing that the One who knows the end from the beginning is with you. You can pray with confidence because God is not far from you.
  - The abiding presence also means that fellowship with God is available throughout your day. Prayer does not have to end when you leave your prayer room. You can speak with Him while driving, working, studying, walking, or handling the responsibilities of life. His presence turns everyday moments into opportunities for fellowship.
  - 'David understood the value of God''s presence when he wrote, “Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy.” - Psalm 16:11 (KJV). The presence of God brings direction, strength, peace, and joy. When you become conscious of His presence, fear begins to lose its grip because you recognize who is with you. The abiding presence of God should also influence how you live. Your conversations, decisions, relationships, and actions should reflect an awareness that you carry His presence.'
prayer: Father, thank You for Your abiding presence in my life. Thank You that I am never alone. From today onwards, I become increasingly conscious of Your presence throughout my day. Teach me to fellowship with You continually and to walk with confidence, knowing that You are always with me. Everything about me will reflect the reality of Your presence in me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
