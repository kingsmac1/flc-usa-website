title: Don’t Observe Lying Vanities
scripture: Jonah 2:8
verse: They that observe lying vanities forsake their own mercy.
body:
  - 'Jonah spoke these words from the belly of the fish, surrounded by conditions that gave him little reason for hope. Everything around him could have convinced him that his life was over, but in prayer, he came to an important realization: “They that observe lying vanities forsake their own mercy.” A lying vanity is something empty or deceptive that presents itself as worthy of your confidence. To “observe” it is to give it your attention until it begins to influence what you believe.'
  - Something can look convincing and still tell you the wrong story about your future. A present limitation may suggest that you will always be limited. A closed door may try to convince you that there is no other way forward. What you are facing may be real, but the conclusion it is suggesting does not have to become your conclusion. Faith allows God’s Word to interpret your circumstances instead of allowing circumstances to interpret God for you.
  - 'Jonah had another choice to make. He could keep looking at where he was, or he could turn his attention toward God. He said, “When my soul fainted within me, I remembered the LORD: and my prayer came in unto thee” (Jonah 2:7). That is where his attention shifted. The fish had not disappeared when he prayed those words, but Jonah was no longer allowing his surroundings to determine what he expected from God.'
  - Be careful what you keep observing. If you stare at impossibility long enough, it can become larger in your thinking than the God you believe in. Do not keep studying everything that appears to be working against you. Give the Word greater attention. Let Scripture correct the conclusions that fear and discouragement are trying to form in your mind.
  - You do not have to accept every picture circumstances place before you. Look beyond what is temporary and keep your heart fixed on what God has said. Jonah prayed from inside the fish, and he eventually came out of it. Do not allow a temporary condition to convince you that it has become your permanent reality. Refuse lying vanities and keep your faith in God.
prayer: My attention is fixed on God and His Word. I refuse every conclusion that contradicts what He has spoken concerning me. My present circumstances do not define my future. I see from the standpoint of faith and remain confident in God’s goodness. My heart is established, my expectation remains strong, and I experience the mercy and goodness of the Lord in my life. In Jesus’ Name, Amen.
