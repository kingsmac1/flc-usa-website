title: Be Fruitful To God
scripture: John 15:8
verse: Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples.
body:
  - God desires your life in Christ to be fruitful. Jesus did not describe the Christian life as one in which you receive from God but have nothing flowing through you to others. He said that the Father is glorified when you “bear much fruit.” Fruit is the visible expression of the life within a tree. In the same way, the life of Christ within you should increasingly find expression in the way you live and in what your life contributes to His kingdom.
  - Fruitfulness takes different forms. A soul brought to Christ through your witness is fruit. The character of Christ becoming evident in you is fruit. Your service in God’s house can also produce fruit as lives are helped through what you contribute. Jesus said in John 15:16, “I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit should remain.” God has placed something within you that can become useful in His hands.
  - Scripture also speaks seriously about unfruitfulness. Jesus said of the unfruitful branch in John 15:2, “Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away”. In the parable of the barren fig tree, the owner came looking for fruit and found none (Luke 13:6–9). These passages are not given to make you live afraid of God. They remind you that your life was meant to produce something. In fact, the fig tree was given additional time and attention so that it could become fruitful. God is interested in your growth and wants to see your life flourish.
  - Perhaps you feel that you have not accomplished much for God. Do not condemn yourself; begin where you are. You do not need a platform before you can become fruitful. There is someone you can encourage and someone who needs to hear the gospel. There is work in God’s kingdom to which you can contribute. Ask the Lord to show you where your life can make a difference, and be willing to respond.
  - Jesus said in John 15:5, “He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit.” Fruitfulness begins with remaining connected to Christ. Stay in His Word and allow His life to work through you. Your life can count for God. Be fruitful where He has planted you, and let the influence of your life continue long after you have passed through a place.
prayer: My life is fruitful unto God. The life of Christ in me finds expression through everything I do, and I am useful in the advancement of His kingdom. Through my life, people are strengthened and drawn to Christ. I continually grow and produce fruit that remains, bringing glory to my heavenly Father. In Jesus’ Name, Amen.
