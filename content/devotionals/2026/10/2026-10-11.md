title: Saturate Your Cloud
scripture: Ecclesiastes 11:3
verse: If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth...
body:
  - Have you ever prayed about something for so long that you started wondering whether you should still be praying about it? You began with great expectation, but time passed and nothing seemed to change. That can be a difficult place, because when you cannot see results, it is easy to assume that your prayers are making no difference.
  - 'Ecclesiastes 11:3 gives us a beautiful picture: “If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth.” Rain does not begin with what you see falling from the sky. Long before the first drop reaches the ground, moisture has been gathering in the clouds. Eventually, the cloud becomes full and releases what it has been carrying.'
  - Prayer can sometimes feel like that. You may have been praying rightly concerning a particular matter and still have nothing visible to show for it. Do not be too quick to conclude that your prayers are not working. Keep praying in faith. There are things being settled beyond what your natural eyes can presently see.
  - Jesus said in Luke 11:9, “Ask, and it shall be given you”. There are times when you stay with a matter in prayer because you believe God concerning it. You are not trying to convince God to help you. Your persistence comes from the confidence that He hears you. Each time you return to prayer, you are refusing to allow what you see to become greater than what you believe.
  - Maybe your cloud is closer to being full than you realize. Do not walk away because you have waited longer than expected. Keep your heart in faith and remain thankful. The cloud that is full of rain will eventually empty itself. Stay in prayer. Saturate your cloud. Expect the rain.
prayer: My prayers are effective, and I pray with confidence because God hears me. My heart remains full of expectation concerning everything I have committed to Him. I see the answers I have prayed for becoming evident in my life. My cloud is full of rain, and I rejoice in the manifestation of God's goodness. In Jesus' Name, Amen. FURTHER
