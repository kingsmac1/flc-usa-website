date: '2026-10-12'
title: Seek God Before Trouble Comes
scripture: Psalm 27:4 (KJV)
verse: One thing have I desired of the LORD, that will I seek after; that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD, and to enquire in his temple.
body:
  - 'It is easy to seek God when experiencing challenging situations. When pressure rises or something goes wrong, we often become more serious about prayer. There is nothing wrong with calling on God in trouble. But David shows us a better way: do not wait until trouble comes before you seek God.'
  - David's desire was to dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of his life. He wanted God's presence in peaceful and challenging seasons alike. For David, seeking God was not an emergency response; it was a way of life. That daily fellowship gave him stability when trouble came.
  - When David faced enemies, pressure, and uncertainty, he was not trying to discover God in the middle of crisis. He already knew Him. He had spent time in His presence, learned to trust Him, and built confidence in His faithfulness. So, when difficult moments came, David had somewhere to draw strength from. His secret place prepared him for the battle.
  - This is why your daily walk with God is very important. Do not
  - wait until you are confused before asking for direction. Seek His wisdom before making decisions. Do not wait until fear comes before opening His Word. Let His truth already be in your heart. Do not wait until danger appears before making God your refuge. Walk with Him every day.
  - Sometimes, God protects you by delivering you from trouble, but at other times, He protects you by guiding you away from it. His Word gives wisdom, and the Holy Spirit can lead you to pause, reconsider, or change direction. A simple act of obedience today can spare you unnecessary pain tomorrow.
  - The secret place prepares you for what you cannot yet see. Prayer strengthens your heart, the Word renews your mind, and fellowship with the Holy Spirit helps you recognize His leading. Make seeking God your lifestyle, not just your emergency plan. When trouble comes, let it find you already walking with God.
prayer: Father, I will seek You every day, not only when I need your intervention. Teach me to value Your presence, to trust Your Word, and follow Your direction at all times. I declare that I will walk with You daily and make You my refuge in every season. In Jesus' name, Amen.
