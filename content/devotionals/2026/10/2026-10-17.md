title: When The Enemy Comes IN Like A Flood
scripture: Isaiah 59:19
verse: When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him.
body:
  - 'There are times when challenges do not seem to come one issue at a time. Before you have finished dealing with one situation, something else appears. Pressure comes from unexpected directions, and you begin to wonder why so much seems to be happening at once. That picture helps us understand the language Isaiah uses: “When the enemy shall come in like a flood.”'
  - 'A flood can be frightening because of the force with which it moves. It attempts to overwhelm whatever stands in its path. In this passage, the picture is of an enemy advancing with such intensity that, from a human standpoint, resistance may appear impossible. But the verse does not end with the flood: “The Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him.” Heaven has an answer for what appears overwhelming.'
  - A standard in ancient times could refer to a banner raised where people could see it and rally around it. when the enemy advances, God does not abandon you to face the pressure alone. His Spirit rises against the opposition. What came against you with force encounters a greater force. What intended to overwhelm you meets divine resistance.
  - Therefore, when several things seem to go wrong at once, do not immediately conclude that you are losing the battle. Do not measure God's presence by the intensity of what is happening around you. The flood may be real, but it is not greater than the Spirit of God. There are moments when what looks like an overwhelming attack becomes the setting in which you discover just how mighty God's power is on your behalf.
  - Perhaps you are facing your own flood right now. Lift your eyes above what is rushing toward you and remember who stands with you. You are not at the mercy of the opposition. The Spirit of the Lord is your divine resistance. When the enemy rises, God raises a standard that the enemy cannot overcome.
prayer: The Spirit of the Lord is mighty within me, and no opposition can overwhelm my life. When pressure rises, divine power rises greater. I stand secure in Christ and face every situation with confidence. Every scheme of the enemy is defeated, and I move forward under the victorious banner of Jesus Christ. I will not be overwhelmed because the power of God is at work in me. Amen.
