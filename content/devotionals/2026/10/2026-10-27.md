date: '2026-10-27'
title: God’s Recipe For Success
scripture: Joshua 1:8 (KJV)
verse: This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night… for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.
body:
  - 'God never left you without direction for living well. From our opening scripture, Joshua was stepping into an enormous responsibility when God gave him a clear instruction: keep the Word in your mouth, meditate on it continually, and live according to what it says. God connected Joshua''s progress to his relationship with the Word.'
  - Biblical success is more than money, recognition, or reaching personal goals. You can accomplish many things and still miss God's direction for your life. Good success means moving forward in a way that agrees with God's will. It is reaching where He wants you to go without losing what matters along the way.
  - Notice that God told Joshua to meditate on His Word. Meditation means giving the Word your attention until it begins to shape your thinking. You read it, think about what it means, allow it to influence the choices you make and proclaim it. When God's Word becomes familiar to your heart, it becomes easier to recognize when something does not agree with His wisdom.
  - Psalm 1:2 speaks about the person whose “delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.” The next verse describes that person as a tree planted by rivers of water, bringing forth fruit in season. Fruitfulness begins with being rooted in the right place.
  - God's instruction to Joshua also included obedience. Knowing Scripture without putting it into practice will not produce the result God intended. The Word must move from your Bible into your daily decisions. Sometimes, success will require saying no to something attractive because it does not agree with God's way. At other times, obedience may require patience when you would rather move quickly.
  - 'You do not have to invent your own formula for success. God has already given you a pattern: let His Word fill your heart, govern your thinking, and direct your actions. As you remain rooted in Scripture, you gain wisdom for the road ahead. Let the Word become more than something you read occasionally. Build your life around it. God''s Word is His recipe for good success.'
prayer: Father, thank You for giving me Your Word as my guide. I declare that Your truth governs my thinking and directs my choices. I meditate on Your Word and walk in obedience to what You reveal. My steps are ordered by Your wisdom, and I prosper in the purpose You have prepared for me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
