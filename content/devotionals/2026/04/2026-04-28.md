title: The Call To Holy Living
scripture: 1 Peter 1:15
verse: But as He who called you is holy, you also be holy in all your conduct
body:
  - Holiness is defined as being set apart for a special, divine purpose and the state of absolute moral purity or sanctity. It describes God's complete separation from all that is not God, including sin, and implies a calling for believers to live dedicated to God rather than worldly things.
  - Holiness is the very expression of God's nature in the life of a believer. When God calls you to holiness, He is not placing a demand on you to become something you are not; rather, He is calling you to live out who you already are in Christ. This understanding changes everything. Holiness is not a struggle for acceptance, but a response to the life of God within you.
  - Many people see holiness as restriction, but true holiness is transformation. It begins in your spirit when you are born again and continues as your mind is renewed by the Word of God. Your desires begin to shift, your values are elevated, and your life starts to reflect God's character. What once seemed difficult becomes a natural outflow of your new nature.
  - The Bible says, “For this is the will of God, even your sanctification…” (1 Thessalonians 4:3). This means holy living is God's desire for every believer. Yet, it is not achieved through human effort alone. Holiness is cultivated through fellowship with God, yielding to the Holy Spirit, and making daily choices that align with His Word. Every decision you make becomes an opportunity to express His nature.
  - 'To live holy, you have to be deliberate, and It requires that you guard your heart, be mindful of your words, and choose actions that honor God. It involves refusing to compromise, even in subtle ways, and standing firm in your identity as one who is set apart. This is not about perfection, but about direction: a consistent pursuit of God and His righteousness.'
  - 'Holiness also positions you for deeper fellowship with God. The more you align your life with Him, the more clearly you experience His presence, guidance, and peace. Your spiritual sensitivity increases, and you become more aware of His leading in every area of your life. In this way, holiness is not just about what you avoid; it is about what you gain: intimacy with God and alignment with His purpose.'
  - Choose daily to walk in holiness. Remind yourself of who you are in Christ, and let that identity shape your lifestyle. As you do, you will not only please God but also experience the fullness of life He has prepared for you.
prayer: I am set apart unto God, and I walk in the reality of my holy nature. My life reflects God's righteousness, and my choices align with God's Word. I yield to the Holy Spirit daily, and I walk in purity, grow in wisdom, and grace. I live to please God in all I do, in Jesus' name, Amen!
