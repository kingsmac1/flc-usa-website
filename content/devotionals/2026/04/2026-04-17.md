title: the two sides of god
scripture: 1 Chronicles 13:10
verse: And the anger of the Lord was kindled against Uzzah, and he struck him down because he put his hand to the ark; so he died there before God.
body:
  - 'Consider the account in our theme scripture: “As the Ark of God was being transported, the oxen pulling the cart stumbled. Instinctively, Uzzah reached out his hand to steady the Ark so it would not fall. Immediately, the Lord struck him, and he died beside the Ark”. To many readers, this moment may seem harsh, especially since Uzzah appeared to act out of concern. Yet this event reveals an important truth about God''s character: He is a loving God and just God.'
  - Long before this incident, God had given clear instructions about how the Ark of the Covenant was to be handled. According to the Law, only the Levites were permitted to move it, and it had to be carried on their shoulders using poles placed through the rings on its sides (Numbers 7:9). No one was allowed to touch the Ark directly because it symbolized the holy presence of God among His people.
  - However, when David arranged for the Ark to be brought to Jerusalem, those instructions were not followed. Instead of carrying it as commanded, they placed it on a cart, copying the method previously used by the Philistines (1 Samuel 6). While the celebration surrounding the move was enthusiastic, the process itself ignored God's clear directions. When Uzzah reached out to steady the Ark, his intention may have seemed honorable, but his action still broke a direct instruction from God. This moment reminds us that good intentions cannot replace obedience to God.
  - 'Yet the incident does not end with judgment. After Uzzah''s death, the Ark was taken to the house of Obed-edom, where it remained for three months. During that time, the Lord blessed his entire household (1 Chronicles 13:14). This contrast reveals an important aspect of God''s character: He is both perfectly just and abundantly loving. Many people develop a one-sided understanding of God. Some focus only on His blessings and overlook His judgement. Others emphasize judgment so strongly that they forget His blessings. The truth is that both qualities exist together in perfect balance.'
  - God's blessings are real, but they come with responsibility. He calls you to live with integrity, fairness, honesty, and obedience. When you approach Him with reverence and follow His Word carefully, you experience the fullness of His presence and the richness of His blessings.
