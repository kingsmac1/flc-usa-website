title: Practicing The Presence of god
scripture: 1 John 5:14
verse: This is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us
body:
  - 'Experiencing God''s presence is basically one of the most transformative principles of the Christian life. But what does it truly mean to practice His presence? Practicing the presence of God is not about seeking emotional highs or waiting for extraordinary experiences; it is a conscious, continual awareness that God is with you in every moment. It is a lifestyle where every thought, word, and action acknowledge His presence in your life. In the Old Testament, God''s presence was often experienced in specific locations: the tabernacle, the temple, or upon holy mountains. People recognized Him in those sacred settings, yet His presence was largely situational, reserved for certain times and places.'
  - In the New Testament, however, the Holy Spirit dwells in you, making God's presence constant and personal at all times. Jesus said in John 14:16–17 that the Spirit would live in us, guide us, and empower us. Practicing His presence, therefore, means intentionally cultivating awareness of the Holy Spirit in your daily life. It means inviting Him into your work, your rest, your relationships, and even your challenges. It is living with the understanding that you are never alone, that every situation is under God's control, and that He is continually at work on your behalf. When you practice the presence of God, your spirit becomes sensitive to His guidance. Like Moses, who communed with God on the mountain and interceded for the people, your daily communion allows God's wisdom, power, and love to flow through you. Every ordinary moment becomes a spiritual encounter, and every decision is informed by His Spirit.
  - Therefore, practicing the presence of God is an intentional pursuit. It is a response of your human spirit to the indwelling Spirit of God. As you live in this higher realm of awareness, your life becomes a testimony of His glory. It is not enough to acknowledge God occasionally; God is inviting you to walk continually in His presence, letting Him shape every aspect of your life.
prayer: Father, I practice Your presence every day. I acknowledge that You are with me in every situation, guiding, strengthening, and comforting me. I remain aware of Your presence, allowing Your Spirit to lead, empower, and fill me with peace, joy, and courage. I walk in Your presence, and my life reflects Your glory in Jesus' name, Amen.
