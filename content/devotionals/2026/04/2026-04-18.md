title: True leadership
scripture: Proverbs 16:12
verse: But lead with integrity, and in the wisdom of our Lord and Saviour, Jesus Christ. To Him be all honor both now and forever. Amen
body:
  - The Bible tells us that leadership is more than a position or title. It's about how you influence others through obedience to God. Don't rely on authority alone. God wants you to lead in ways that inspire trust, in a way that shape character, and leave a lasting impact. You might ask, “How can I become this kind of leader?” The Word of God shows us exactly how.
  - Proverbs 16:12 says, “It is an abomination to kings to commit wickedness, for a throne is established by righteousness.” Notice that success in leadership is tied to righteousness, not skill, connections, or intelligence. Just like David (chosen at a young age) wasn't trained as a soldier and Joseph wasn't trained as an economist, yet they exhibited great and true leadership. Leadership that pleases God grows when your heart is aligned with His standards.
  - The wisdom that transforms leadership is God-centered. When you yield to God, pray for guidance, and allow His Word to direct your decisions, your ability to lead multiplies. This growth may show in your home, your workplace, or your community, in ways that people notice naturally.
  - 'Everyday leadership can be simple but powerful: it could be a parent guiding his children with patience, a team leader modeling fairness and honesty, or a political leader ensuring equality and honesty. When God guides your actions, your influence multiplies quietly but effectively, creating impact beyond what you can measure.'
  - That's when you see real change. Your relationships flourish, you will notice that your decisions will bear more fruits, and your character will inspire others. This is why Scripture instructs us to “Train up a child in the way he should go; and when he is old, he will not depart from it” (Proverbs 22:6). To train up is to lead and the way to Go is the way of righteousness. Leadership that honors God leaves a legacy that cannot be hidden. Hallelujah!
prayer: Father, thank You for teaching me the principles of true leadership. I submit my actions, decisions, and influence to You. Let my leadership honor You, impact others positively, and leave a lasting legacy of integrity and Godly wisdom, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
