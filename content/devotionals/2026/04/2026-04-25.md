title: Faith that moves mountains
scripture: Mark 11:22
verse: And Jesus answered and said unto them, “Have faith in God
body:
  - 'Someone once asked, “How can I see God''s word come to pass in my life?” The answer is simple: exercise faith. True faith is not wishful thinking; it is trust in God''s Word and His power. In the book of Acts, we see believers stepping out in faith, trusting God to move in impossible situations. Their confidence wasn''t in their own ability, but in the faithfulness of God.'
  - The Bible says in Hebrews 11:1, “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” Faith strengthens your spirit and aligns your heart with God's will. The more you act on God's Word, the more your confidence in Him grows, and the bolder your steps of obedience become.
  - Think of David. As a young shepherd, he faced the giant Goliath with only a sling and a stone. He could have been paralyzed by fear, but his faith in God's power gave him courage. Against all odds, he proclaimed, “The battle is the Lord's” and defeated the giant. Faith greatly empowered him to act beyond human understanding.
  - The same faith that David walked in is available to you today. Mark 11:22 implores us to continually trust God. As we speak God's Word, pray with expectancy, and step forward in obedience, our faith grows. Small acts of faith today prepare us for greater victories tomorrow.
  - When you act in faith, even in uncertain circumstances, God works through you to accomplish the impossible. The Mountains of fear, doubt, sickness, financial lack, delay and others begin to move. That faith is of God, and it produces miraculous results.
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for the gift of faith. I trust Your Word and step boldly in obedience, knowing that You are faithful. I am strengthened in my spirit and I walk by faith and not by sight. May my life be a proof of Your power as I move forward, seeing mountains of impossibility removed, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
