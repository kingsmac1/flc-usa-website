title: be persistent in prayer
scripture: Luke 18:1 (NLT)
verse: One day Jesus told his disciples a story to show that they should always pray and never give up.
body:
  - 'Jesus once told His disciples a story to teach them something very important about prayer: always pray and never give up. He knew that people often become discouraged when they pray for something and don''t see an immediate answer. Waiting can be difficult, and sometimes, it feels like our prayers are not being heard. But Jesus wanted His followers to understand that persistence in prayer matters.'
  - Being persistent means refusing to quit. It means continuing to trust God even when things take longer than expected. Prayer is not just about asking once and then giving up when nothing seems to happen. Instead, it is about coming to God with faith again and again, knowing that He hears us and cares about our needs.
  - 'The Bible also encourages this kind of perseverance in Galatians 6:9: “Let us not grow weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.” Sometimes, the answer to our prayers requires patience. God works in His perfect timing, and what may feel like a delay to us could actually be part of His plan.'
  - 'Jesus explained persistence in prayer in a very practical way in Matthew 7:7–8: “Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.” Notice the progression in these words: ask, seek, and knock. First, we ask. This is when we bring our request to God in prayer. Then we seek, which means we pursue God with greater focus and trust. Finally, we knock, which shows determination and persistence.'
  - Each step reflects a greater level of faith and commitment. Jesus was teaching that when answers do not come immediately, we should not stop praying. Instead, we should continue to seek God and trust Him until the door opens.
  - Persistent prayer does more than bring answers; it also strengthens our faith and deepens our relationship with God. It reminds us that He is always listening and that He cares about every detail of our lives. So, when you pray, don't give up too quickly. Keep asking, keep seeking, and keep knocking. Trust that God is working, even when you cannot yet see the results.
