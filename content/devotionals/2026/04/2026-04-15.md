title: the anointing and The anointer
scripture: 1 John 2:27
verse: But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you…
body:
  - 'The anointing and the Anointer are not the same. The anointing is the divine enablement, the grace, the empowerment to function in God''s purpose. The Anointer, however, is the Holy Spirit Himself: the Person who imparts that power. Many believers seek the anointing as though it were a substance or spiritual energy, yet the true source of all empowerment is the Holy Spirit.'
  - In Scripture, oil was used as a symbol of consecration and divine appointment. Kings, priests, and prophets were anointed to signify that they were set apart for God's service. But in the New Covenant, the anointing is not merely external; it is the indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit. He does not just visit; He abides. The anointing flows from relationship with the Anointer.
  - The Holy Spirit is the source of power. He teaches, guides, strengthens, and empowers believers to fulfill their calling. Without Him, ministry becomes effort. With Him, service becomes grace-enabled. God calls, Jesus authorizes, and the Holy Spirit empowers. This divine order ensures that what we do is sustained by heaven's strength and not human ability.
  - A common mistake is to pursue manifestations rather than intimacy. When believers focus only on the anointing (seeking power, gifts, or outward results), they may neglect fellowship with the Anointer. Power without relationship leads to emptiness. But when you cultivate communion with the Holy Spirit, the anointing flows naturally. The Person must always be greater than the gift.
  - The anointing is not something you manufacture; it is something you receive. It is not achieved by striving but accessed through surrender and faith. As you walk in step with the Spirit, His power operates through you. He equips you to teach, to serve, to heal, and to influence according to God's will.
  - 'The difference is profound: the anointing is what He gives; the Anointer is who He is. One is the expression of His power; the other is the source of it. If you have the Anointer, you will never lack the anointing necessary for your assignment. Therefore, seek not merely to be anointed; seek to know the Anointer. Fellowship with Him daily. Honor His presence. Yield to His guidance. As you do, His power will rest upon you, and His purpose will be fulfilled through you.'
prayer: Holy Spirit, I honor You as the Anointer in my life. Teach me to value Your presence above manifestations. Thank you for your spirit empowers me to fulfill your purpose with grace and boldness, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
