title: striving for mastery
scripture: 1 Corinthians 9:25
verse: Everyone who competes in the games exercises self-control in all things. They do it to receive a perishable crown, but we an imperishable one.
body:
  - 'To strive for mastery means to fully engage in the race God has set before you. It''s a deliberate struggle, a focused effort, and a disciplined pursuit of excellence. The Greek word for “strive” (agonizomenos) implies endurance, perseverance, and intentional exertion: like an athlete training for a championship.'
  - In today's world, striving for mastery may look very real in your everyday life. It could be resisting the urge to procrastinate and choosing to complete your work with excellence. It may be staying disciplined in your finances, avoiding impulsive spending, or investing wisely in your future. It could be saying no to distractions (social media, unhealthy habits, or toxic relationships) so that you can grow in your purpose and spiritual life.
  - 2 Timothy 2:5 says, “anyone competes as an athlete, they do not receive the prize unless they compete according to the rules.” “Compete according to the rule” implies self-control. Self-control is central to mastery. True success is not about
  - what you achieve by chance but about the choices you consistently make. Discipline is an intentional focus. It's choosing long-term growth over short-term comfort. For example, a student may wake up early to study instead of scrolling on their phone, or an employee may stay late to finish a project with excellence. These small acts of discipline train you for bigger victories in life.
  - The crown God offers is eternal, not temporary. Unlike worldly recognition, which fades, the reward for faithful, disciplined effort in God's kingdom is everlasting. But striving must be done according to God's ways. Your efforts must align with His Word, and in integrity. Every choice, every sacrifice, and every act of persistence matters.
  - So, today, commit to running your life like an athlete running toward the prize. Control your thoughts, manage your habits, and persevere through challenges. Let discipline and focus shape your journey.
prayer: Father, thank You for calling me to a life of mastery. I will discipline my thoughts, habits, and actions, to persevere through challenges, and to pursue excellence according to Your Word. Strengthen me to run my race with purpose and faithfulness, so that I may receive the incorruptible crown You have promised. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
