title: God’s design for marriage
scripture: Genesis 2:24 (KJV)
verse: 'Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.'
body:
  - Marriage begins with God. Long before culture shaped traditions, before society created formal ceremonies, God Himself established marriage. From the very beginning, it was never meant to be merely a social arrangement or a convenience. Marriage is a sacred union, carefully designed by God to reflect His wisdom, love, and purpose for human relationships.
  - The word “cleave” carries deep meaning. It means to cling, adhere firmly, and become closely joined. It is a picture of intimacy, loyalty, and lasting commitment. Marriage is not intended to be casual, unstable, or temporary. When a husband and wife cleave to each other, they are committing to walk together through life's joys and challenges, sharing purpose, dreams, responsibilities, and faith.
  - 'The phrase “one flesh” goes far beyond physical intimacy. It points to complete unity: emotional, spiritual, relational, and practical. Marriage is meant to be a partnership where two individuals come together as one. Husbands and wives are called to share their lives fully: hopes, burdens, challenges, successes, and spiritual growth. They are no longer separate'
  - entities but a united team, moving together in God's plan.
  - 'When couples understand marriage from God''s perspective, challenges are no longer reasons to separate but opportunities to grow stronger together. Just as the body seeks healing when injured, marriage is designed to move toward restoration and unity, not division and resentment. Difficult seasons can become moments of greater intimacy and love when God is at the center. Jesus confirmed this divine design in Matthew 19:6: “What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.” Healthy marriages are built when both spouses continually return to God''s original plan, allowing His Word to guide their decisions, conversations, and hearts.'
  - Marriage is not a legal contract or social expectation; it is a covenant. It thrives when both partners cling to each other and cling to God. When a couple follows His design, they experience the strength, joy, and purpose God intended from the very beginning.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the gift of marriage and Your perfect design for it. Help me to cleave to my spouse with love, patience, and commitment. We will honor You in every aspect of our relationship, face challenges with grace, and grow together as one flesh. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
