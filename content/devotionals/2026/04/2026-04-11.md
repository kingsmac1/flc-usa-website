title: Increase In Grace
scripture: John 14:12 KJV
verse: Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do; because I go unto my Father.
body:
  - Many believers search for a “double portion anointing,” imagining it to be an extra measure of God's power that enables them to accomplish more. Yet, this idea often comes from a misunderstanding of Scripture. When Elisha asked Elijah for a double portion (2 Kings 2:9), he wasn't asking for a second Holy Spirit. He was asking for an increased grace to continue and expand the prophetic ministry. It was about responsibility and continuation, not duplication of the Spirit.
  - The “law of the double portion” in Deuteronomy 21:17 refers to inheritance rights, where the eldest son received twice the material share. That principle concerns family inheritance, not spiritual empowerment. The anointing is the manifested presence and power of the Holy Spirit in a believer's life. There is no “double Holy Spirit.” There is one Spirit, given fully.
  - Jesus clarified this truth. In John 3:34, we are told that the Spirit is given without measure. That means there is no partial supply from God's side. The limitation is never in His giving; it is in our growth, yieldedness, and understanding. Furthermore, John
  - '1:16 declares, “Of His fullness have all we received, and grace for grace.” This is continuous supply: grace upon grace, increase upon increase.'
  - If your desire is to accomplish more for the Kingdom, the answer is not chasing a “double portion.” The answer is growth. Grow in grace. Grow in revelation. Grow in faithfulness. As you mature in the Word and in obedience, the capacity of the anointing in you becomes more evident and more effective.
  - Jesus already settled the matter in John 14:12. Every believer carries the divine enablement to do the works of Christ (and even greater works) because He has gone to the Father and sent the Spirit. The key is not receiving something new, but walking boldly and yielding to what you already have.
  - Stop searching for what you think is missing. Embrace the fullness already given. As you grow in grace, your impact increases, your influence expands, and your effectiveness multiplies. The anointing you have is complete, sufficient, and powerful enough to change your world.
