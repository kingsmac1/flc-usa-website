title: god’s plan for the family
scripture: Genesis 2:24
verse: 'Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.'
body:
  - In the days of Middle Eastern royalty, accumulating wives and concubines was common. Kings and rulers believed that multiple marriages would bring them influence, wealth, and security. Yet, this was never God's ideal for family life. David, a man after God's own heart, experienced the consequences firsthand. Though his many marriages gave him power and influence, they also brought strife, jealousy, rivalry, and even murder into his household. Polygamy, while culturally accepted, disrupted the harmony of his family and caused pain that echoed for generations.
  - 'Today, some people still try to water down God''s Word and justify embarking on polygamy. They seek cultural acceptance instead of divine alignment. God''s plan is simple and perfect: one man and one wife. If polygamy were His design, God would have formed more than one wife for Adam. The unity and intimacy intended for marriage can only thrive under the one- flesh principle, where husband and wife commit fully to each other under God.'
  - The first recorded case of polygamy was in Genesis 4:19 when Lamech, a descendant of Cain, took two wives. Lamech's
  - actions highlight a departure from God's original design for family. In the city of Nod, Cain established a civilization that operated apart from God's guidance. It is no surprise that Lamech continued a lifestyle outside God's intended pattern. When humans reject God's plan, they often experience confusion, conflict, and a breakdown of family values.
  - God's design for marriage is not restrictive; it is protective. It ensures stability, peace, and mutual support within the home. When families adhere to His blueprint (one man, one wife), they reflect His love and wisdom in society. This is why Christians are called to honor God's plan, preserve their households, and guard their marriages against cultural compromises.
  - Stay committed to God's plan. Walk in His Word. Protect your family. Avoid the pitfalls of imitation and the desire to conform to the world's standards. One man, one wife, united in faith and love, is the foundation of a Godly home. Departing from this plan invites unnecessary strife and sorrow.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your plan for my family. I will follow Your Word and preserve the unity, love, and peace of my home. I refuse to be swayed by worldly standards. The Lord rules and reigns in my home In Jesus' Name, Amen.
