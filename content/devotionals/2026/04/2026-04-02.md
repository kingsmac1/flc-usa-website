title: Building a healthy marriage
scripture: Malachi 2:15– 16 (KJV)
verse: Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth… therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously.
body:
  - Marriage is one of God's most beautiful designs. It was created not only for companionship, but also to reflect unity, faithfulness, and God's glory. A healthy marriage does not happen by accident; it is built intentionally when both husband and wife honor God and remain committed to one another.
  - In Malachi, God gives a serious warning about dealing “treacherously” with the spouse of one's youth. This refers to betrayal, unfaithfulness, or breaking the sacred covenant of marriage. Marriage is beyond a contract between two people; it is a covenant made before God. Because of this, God desires faithfulness, loyalty, and long-term commitment between husband and wife.
  - 'From the very beginning, God established marriage as a union where two people become one. Genesis 2:24 reminds us: “Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.”'
  - When two people marry, they become one flesh. This means
  - that the relationship should be treated with care, love, and respect. Just as we would not intentionally harm our own body, we should not do things that harm our spouse or damage the marriage.
  - Every marriage will surely face challenges. Disagreements, misunderstandings, and hardships will come. But God calls couples to work through those moments with grace, patience, forgiveness, and prayer. Instead of giving up when difficulties arise, couples should seek God's wisdom and strength to rebuild and restore their relationship.
  - A healthy marriage grows when both partners remember their vows, nurture their love, and keep God at the center of their home. When marriage is built on faithfulness and commitment, it becomes a powerful testimony of God's love and a blessing for future generations.
  - Ask yourself these questions and be honest to your answers - Am I honoring my spouse with my words, actions, and attitudes? Is God truly at the center of our marriage? What is one way I can strengthen my marriage today?
prayer: Heavenly Father, thank You for the gift of marriage. Teach me to honor and to treat my spouse with love, respect, and faithfulness. Teach us to forgive, to communicate with grace, and to keep You at the center of our relationship. Amen!
