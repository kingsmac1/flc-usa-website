title: don’t be a wanderer
scripture: Genesis 4:16
verse: And Cain went out from the presence of the Lord, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.
body:
  - Adam had two sons, Cain and Abel. Due to jealousy and anger, Cain killed his brother. Because of this sin, God judged him and declared that he would become “a fugitive and a wanderer on the earth” (Genesis 4:12). Afterward, the Bible says “Cain went out from the presence of the Lord and settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.”
  - Interestingly, the word “Nod” means “wandering,” “exile,” or “fugitive.” It described exactly what Cain's life had become. Adam and Eve had already been driven out of the Garden, but Cain moved even farther away. This shows what happens when someone continues moving away from God instead of toward Him. Cain's wandering wasn't just about location; it was about relationship. The real tragedy was that he left the presence of the Lord. From that point on, his life became one of separation from God; a civilization and lifestyle without God.
  - 'In many ways, this still speaks to you today. A person can be busy, successful, and moving through life, yet still feel lost on the inside. Without God''s presence and guidance, life can begin to feel like wandering: moving but not really knowing where'
  - you're going. That's why the knowledge of God's Word is so important. Hosea 4:6 says, “My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge”. Without God's word and the Holy Spirit guiding you, it's easy to drift through life making decisions based only on feelings, circumstances, or personal desires.
  - But God never intended for you to live like a wanderer. His Word brings light and direction. The psalmist said, “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path” (Psalm 119:105). When God's Word guides your life, you're no longer wandering; you're walking with purpose.
  - So, don't drift away from God like Cain did. Abide in Him. Let His Word shape your thinking and guide your steps. When you walk with God, your life gains direction and fulfillment. Don't be a wanderer. Walk with God.
prayer: Dear Father, thank you for the light of your Word that guides my life. I will not drift away from your presence. Your Word directs my steps and gives me wisdom for every decision. I walk in your word and live by it, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
