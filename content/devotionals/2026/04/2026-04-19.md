title: Ways Of Thanksgiving
scripture: Psalm 92:1–2
verse: 'It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O Most High: To show forth thy loving kindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night.'
body:
  - Giving thanks to God goes beyond a polite gesture; it's a posture of the heart. Psalm 92:1–2 reminds us that “it is truly good and right to praise Him and acknowledge His faithfulness”. Sometimes, though, a simple “thank you” doesn't feel like enough. When we stop and reflect on all He has done, our hearts naturally overflow with gratitude. Thankfully, the Bible shows us that thanksgiving can be expressed in four practical ways.
  - First, remember what God has done. Think back on His blessings, the prayers He has answered, and the times He has protected or guided you. “Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits” (Psalm 103:2). Remembering these things fills our hearts with joy and strengthens our trust in His unfailing love.
  - Second, tell others about His goodness. Sharing your experiences of God's faithfulness is powerful; it encourages others and glorifies Him. “Give thanks unto the Lord, call upon his name, make known his deeds among the people” (1 Chronicles 16:8). As we share how God has worked in our lives, we give hope to someone who may be waiting for their own miracle. Third, show God's glory through your life. Our actions, attitudes, and choices can reflect His goodness to those around us. Jesus said, “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven” (Matthew 5:16). When we live in love, and let the light of God shine through us, others will want to thank God because of us, and our gratitude becomes visible in everyday life.
  - Finally, offer gifts of yourself, your time, and your resources. Serving God and others is a tangible way to say “thank you”. Romans 12:1 says, “Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service”. Giving back in service shows that our gratitude isn't just words; it's a lifestyle.
  - True thanksgiving touches every part of life. It's remembering God's goodness, sharing it with others, reflecting His glory, and giving our lives in service. When our hearts are genuinely grateful, it shows not just in what we say, but in everything we do.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your faithfulness and all the blessings You avail in my life. I will remember Your goodness, I will share Your works with others, reflect Your glory in my actions, and dedicate my life to serving You. Gratitude will always be the natural response of my heart every day, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
