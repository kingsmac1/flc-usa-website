title: the power of appreciation
scripture: 1 Thessalonians 5:18
verse: Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you
body:
  - Appreciation is the recognition and acknowledgment of the value, effort, or kindness of someone or something. Spiritually, it is expressing thankfulness to God and to people for the blessings, guidance, and support in our lives.
  - Gratitude opens the door to joy and strengthens relationships. The Bible repeatedly emphasizes the importance of gratitude, not only to God but also toward others. Colossians 3:15 says, “Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts… and be thankful.” Gratitude aligns our hearts with God, cultivates humility, and fosters peace.
  - Appreciation is more than a simple courtesy; it is an act of spiritual kindness. Many wait for monumental moments to express gratitude, thinking only grand gestures matter. Yet Scripture shows that even small expressions of thankfulness are meaningful in God's eyes and impactful in human relationships.
  - A few years ago, I had a coach who guided me during a critical career transition. Even after paying for his service and completing the sessions, we maintained a cordial relationship.
  - 'Years later, the Lord impressed on me to send him a message simply to show appreciation. When I did, he replied immediately, expressing how much my words meant to him. That moment reminded me: appreciation does not require extravagance. Saying “thank you” for small acts (someone opening a door, a meal shared, guidance offered) can profoundly bless others and open doors for God''s favor.'
  - Gratitude also protects our hearts. Neglecting thankfulness leaves a vacuum that anxiety, depression, and despair can fill. A lack of appreciation blinds us from seeing God's blessings and to the good in the people around us. Even thoughts of hopelessness or self-harm can arise when gratitude is absent. Practicing appreciation shifts our focus to seeing the positive sides of life.
  - Take a moment today to look around. See the blessings God has placed in your life. Acknowledge the people who have helped, guided, or loved you. Speak a word of thanks; it is both spiritual obedience and a source of joy.
prayer: Lord, I thank You for every blessing and for the people You have placed in my life. My heart is full of appreciation and I see Your goodness in every circumstance. Thank you, Lord, for you load me daily with benefits in the Name of Jesus. Amen.
