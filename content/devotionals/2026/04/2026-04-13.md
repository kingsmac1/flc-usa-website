title: serve god wholeheartedly
scripture: John 4:23 KJV
verse: 'But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth: for the Father seeketh such to worship him.'
body:
  - Jesus' words reveal the true nature of worship; it is spiritual, sincere, and established in the truth of God's Word. Worship is beyond an outward routine or religious performance; it is a heartfelt response to God that flows from a transformed spirit. The Gospel is not just a formality; it is a living reality that changes the heart and shapes the life. True worship engages the whole person (spirit, soul, and body) and aligns us with the truth found in Christ.
  - In the Old Testament, after the Assyrians captured Israel, the Assyrians resettled the land with a mixture of people who later became known as the Samaritans. These settlers attempted to worship God while still clinging to their pagan customs. Their worship was divided. They sought to appease God rather than truly honor Him, treating Him as one option among many.
  - This divided devotion is still common today. Many claim to believe in God but are unwilling to surrender their habits, attitudes, or values that contradict His Word. Yet God cannot simply be added to our existing priorities. He does not compete for first place; He requires it. True worship requires placing God above all else and allowing His Word to shape our thoughts,
  - decisions, and lifestyle.
  - To serve God wholeheartedly is to give Him undivided devotion. It is to love Him sincerely, and honor Him not only with words but with actions. When we worship God in spirit and in truth, our lives become living offerings, reflecting His power and grace.
  - From today henceforth, serve God with your whole heart. Let your worship be genuine, let your obedience be complete, and your devotion firm.
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your word to me today. I will always worship You in spirit and in truth. Remove every divided loyalty from my heart. Help me to serve You wholeheartedly, placing You first in every area of my life. Let Your Word shape my thoughts, my actions, and my desires. May my life be true worship before You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
