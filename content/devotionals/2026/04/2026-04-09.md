title: Grace To recover all
scripture: 1 Samuel 30:18
verse: And David recovered all…
body:
  - In 1 Samuel 30, David returned to Ziklag to find the city burned and everything taken. It was a devastating moment. The men who once celebrated him now wept and even spoke of stoning him. But the Bible says, “David encouraged himself in the Lord his God.”
  - 'Before recovery came strengthening. Before restoration came revelation. David inquired of the Lord, and God gave him a powerful assurance: “Pursue: for thou shalt surely overtake them, and without fail recover all” (1 Samuel 30:8). Notice the certainty: surely overtake and without fail recover all. This was not a possibility; it was a guarantee backed by divine instruction.'
  - 2 Peter 3:18 says, “But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and for ever. Amen.” We are admonished to grow in grace. Grace multiplies through the knowledge of revelation. When you grow in grace, you step into greater dimensions of restoration. The same grace that sustained you last season is able to restore all to you at a multiplied level.
  - Sometimes, loss can make you feel discouraged. But when you turn to the Lord in intimate knowledge (what the Greek calls
  - epignosis), your strength rises and your capacity expands. Recovery is not only about getting things back; it is about coming back stronger, wiser, and increased.
  - 'The Bible declares in Joel 2:25, “I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten.” This means God''s restoration is thorough, abundant and undeniable. When grace is multiplied in your life: Your strength is renewed, your influence increases, your opportunities are restored, and your testimony becomes visible to all.'
  - Like David, you will recover all. Do not stay in yesterday's grace. There is multiplied grace available for restoration. Seek the Lord. Meditate on His Word. Let the revelation of knowledge increase in you, and pursue your objectives boldly. A glorious outcome is assured. Hallelujah!
prayer: Dear Father, thank You because You are the God of complete restoration. I receive multiplied grace to pursue, overtake, and recover all that belongs to me. I grow in grace and in the knowledge of revelation. Nothing shall be missing, nothing broken, nothing lacking in my life. My restoration is evident and undeniable, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
