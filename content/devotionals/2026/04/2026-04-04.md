title: confidence in the anointing
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7 KJV
verse: For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
body:
  - Fear, timidity, and cowardice do not come from God. The holy Spirit you received is not weak, he is not hesitant, or unsure. The Holy Spirit, the Anointing within you, is the Spirit of power, love, and a sound mind. That means boldness is your inheritance.
  - Confidence in the anointing is the deep assurance that the Holy Spirit living in you is genuine, powerful, and sufficient. You are not trying to get an anointing; you have already received Him. As 1 John 2:27 reminds us, “the anointing abides in you and teaches you”. This means you can trust His guidance, rely on His wisdom, and stand firm in spiritual understanding.
  - When you are confident in the anointing, you stop shrinking back. You stop doubting whether God can use you. Then You begin to act in faith because you know God is with you and in you. Acts 1:8 declares, “You shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you.” That power is not theoretical; it is practical, and it is a present reality.
  - This confidence also produces peace. Even when Challenges arise, you are not shaken. Romans 8:28 assures us that “in all
  - things God works for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose.” Because the anointing is in you, you can persevere with courage, knowing God is actively working on your behalf. Walk into every situation aware that you carry divine enablement. You are not alone, and you are not inadequate.
  - 'Declare boldly today: I do not walk in fear. I walk in power, love, and a sound mind. The anointing in me is real and sufficient. I am bold because God''s Spirit lives in me.'
prayer: Father, thank You for the Holy Spirit who lives in me. I reject fear and timidity. I embrace the power, love, and soundness of mind You have given me. I'm strengthened by the Holy Ghost; I am confident in the anointing; and I walk boldly in faith every day. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
