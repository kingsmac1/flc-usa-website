title: Growing in the anointing
scripture: 1 John 2:27
verse: But the anointing which you have received of Him abides in you… and just as it has taught you, you shall abide in Him
body:
  - The anointing is not something to chase; it is the Holy Spirit dwelling in you. The moment you received Him, you were anointed. Growth in the anointing is not about gaining more of the Spirit; it is about learning to recognize, trust, and flow in the power already within you. It is a process of increasing sensitivity, maturity, and yielding to His leading.
  - Jesus said, “Abide in Me, and I in you” (John 15:4). Abiding is the foundation of growth. Like a branch depends on the vine to bear fruit, you cannot operate fully in the anointing if you are disconnected from Christ. Reading, meditating, and applying God's Word sharpens your spiritual perception. Colossians 3:16 encourages us to let the Word dwell in us richly, because the Word and the Spirit work together, guiding, teaching, and empowering us.
  - Secondly, Prayer builds your inner strength and activates the anointing. Jude 1:20 tells us to “build ourselves up by praying in the Holy Spirit.” Spirit-led prayer sharpens your discernment, strengthens your faith, and deepens your relationship with God.
  - Learn to yield to the Holy Spirit. This unlocks the flow of the anointing. Acts 5:32 says, “the Spirit is given to those who obey
  - God”. Every act of obedience increases your capacity to function in His power. Neglecting His guidance dulls sensitivity; while faithful response amplifies it.
  - Consecration preserves effectiveness. This is why 2 Timothy 2:21 teaches that “a cleansed vessel becomes fit for the Master's use”. Holiness doesn't produce the anointing, but it ensures God's Spirit can move unhindered. Finally, exercise your gifts. Romans 12:6 urges us to use our abilities according to God's grace. Step out in faith, serve, minister, and pray boldly. The more you act in alignment with God's calling, the more the anointing strengthens and flows through you. Growth in the anointing is not by might, but by His Spirit. As you abide, yield, and exercise your gifts of the spirit, you are assured of lasting impacts.
prayer: Father, thank You for the anointing within me. Continue to teach me to abide, yield , and grow in sensitivity to Your Spirit. Your anointing in me is strengthened daily, empowering me to fulfill my divine assignment. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
