title: you are anointed
scripture: 1 John 2:27
verse: But the anointing which you have received of Him abides in you… as His anointing teaches you about all things and is truth… and just as it has taught you, you shall abide in Him.
body:
  - The anointing is not a feeling. It is not a symbol. It is the empowering presence of the Holy Spirit living inside every believer. When you received the Holy Spirit, you received the anointing. You were set apart, empowered, and equipped to fulfill God's purpose for your life.
  - 'Too often, believers limit the anointing to symbols: oil, fire, or a dove. While these are biblical representations, they are not the substance. The anointing is the Holy Spirit Himself. Acts 10:38 tells us that God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, not with oil. The power came from the Spirit. This means you don''t need to run everywhere searching for someone to pour oil on your head as though the anointing is external. If you are born again and have received the Holy Spirit, the anointing already abides in you. What you need is not a new anointing, but growth, understanding, and sensitivity to the One who already lives within you.'
  - Oil has its place. It can serve as a symbolic act of consecration or ordination. But it is only a representation. Without the Holy Spirit, oil is just oil. The true anointing is not a ceremony; it is a
  - Person. The Spirit of God is the One who empowers, teaches, guides, and transforms..
  - 'Jesus said in Luke 4:18: “The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He has anointed Me…” His ministry flowed from the Spirit''s empowerment, to preach good news, heal the brokenhearted, bring deliverance, and set captives free. The same Spirit now lives in you and he has anointed you too.'
  - 'So, stop searching outside for what is already inside. Abide in Him. Grow in Him. Yield to Him. The anointing in you will teach you, strengthen you, and empower you to fulfill your divine assignment. Boldly declare today: I am full of the Holy Spirit. I am anointed. I am empowered to fulfill God''s purpose for my life.'
prayer: Father, thank You for the gift of the Holy Spirit who lives in me. I declare I am growing in the awareness of the anointing within me. Teach me and guide me in your ways and to walk in my purpose in you. I yield to Your Spirit and declare that I am anointed for Your purpose. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
