title: Faith in god’s timing
scripture: Luke 18:1 (AMP)
verse: Now Jesus was telling the disciples a parable to make the point that at all times they ought to pray and not give up and lose heart.
body:
  - 'If you carefully observe the last part of our theme scripture, “not give up and lose heart”, Jesus understood something about human nature: we easily become discouraged when things don''t happen as quickly as we expect. When prayers seem unanswered or when the answers appear delayed, it''s easy to feel like time is running out.'
  - Many believers have faced moments like this. You pray, you believe, and yet the situation doesn't change immediately. During those times, faith in God's timing becomes very important. Faith in God's timing means trusting that God has a perfect plan and that everything in our lives happens according to His wisdom. Even when we don't understand the delay, we can trust that God sees the bigger picture. His timing may not match our expectations, but it is always right.
  - The Bible reminds us in Ecclesiastes 3:1, “To everything, there is a season, and a time for every purpose under heaven.” This means life moves in seasons. Just as nature has its proper times (planting, growing, and harvesting), God also has appointed times for the things that happen in our lives.
  - Sometimes, what feels like a delay is actually preparation. God may be shaping our character, strengthening our faith, or even arranging circumstances that we cannot yet see.
  - 'Another reminder is 2 Peter 3:8: “With the Lord a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day.” God is not limited by human time. What seems slow to us is never late in God''s plan. Faith requires surrender. It means letting go of our own timelines and trusting God''s wisdom instead. When we do this, we find peace even while waiting.'
  - 'Romans 8:28 gives us a strong assurance: “And we know that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose.” No matter what you are going through right now, God is able to turn it around for your good. So, don''t allow discouragement to take over your heart. Keep trusting Him. As long as you love God, walk in His purpose, and continue doing what is right, you can be confident that He is working behind the scenes. In His perfect time, everything will come together for your good.'
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for helping me trust Your timing. I refuse to become discouraged or lose heart. I believe that You are working all things together for my good. As I walk in love, faith, and obedience, Your perfect plan for my life unfolds in Jesus' Name. Amen.
