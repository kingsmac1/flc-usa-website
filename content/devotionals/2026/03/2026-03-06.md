date: '2026-03-06'
title: Freedom From Fear
scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7 (AMP)
verse: For God did not give us a spirit of timidity or cowardice or fear, but [He has given us a spirit] of power and of love and of sound judgment and personal discipline…
body:
  - The Spirit of God at work in you does not produce fear. He does not make you timid, inferior, shy, hesitant, or uncertain. Fear is not a product of the divine life. What God has given you is power for boldness, love, confidence, and sound judgment that produces well-balanced and disciplined mind. These are the hallmarks of a life governed by the Spirit.
  - Many people struggle with fear of the unknown, afraid of what they cannot define. Often, they fear what does not even exist. Fear thrives where faith in God is absent. It feeds on imagination rather than the truth of God's Word. Where faith in God's presence and power is active, fear has no place to remain.
  - Throughout Scripture, fear is revealed as a response that contradicts confidence in God. The Israelites allowed fear to dominate them when they doubted God's Word concerning the Promised Land (Numbers 13–14). Their fear magnified opposition and minimized God's ability. Fear, in this sense, operates as a tool of the enemy, opposing the peace and assurance God provides.
  - 'The spirit of fear also describes timidity and cowardice, traits inconsistent with the bold life expected of believers. God continually reassures His people of His presence and strength. Psalm 27 declares a victorious confession: “The Lord is my light and my salvation - nobody. Whom shall I be afraid of? Nobody.” Even when opposition rises, it cannot prevail. Those who come against the one upheld by God stumble and fail.'
  - 'Isaiah 41:10 reinforces this assurance: “God strengthens, helps, and upholds His own”. Fear has no power over you because its source has been defeated. You live from a place of confidence, knowing that God''s presence guarantees your victory. Live conscious of the Holy Spirit in you. Fear has been vanquished.'
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You that You are my light and my salvation. I fear no one and nothing. You are the strength of my life, and I live in confidence and peace. Fear has no place in my heart. I walk in boldness, sound judgment, and divine assurance, upheld by Your mighty hand, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
furtherStudies: Isaiah 41:10 KJV; Romans 8:15 KJV; Joshua 1:9
