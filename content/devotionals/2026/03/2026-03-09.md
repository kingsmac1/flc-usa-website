date: '2026-03-09'
title: Partakers Of The Divine Nature
scripture: 2 Peter 1:4 (NKJV)
verse: By which have been given to us exceedingly great and precious promises, that through these you may be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.
body:
  - Have you ever paused to really think about what God has made you? From the memory verse, it says we are partakers of the divine nature. That means God has shared His life with us. Through Christ, we are invited into a supernatural reality where His Spirit lives in us, shaping who we are from the inside out. We have become carriers of God's nature (His love, holiness, and power) within our human lives.
  - Peter clearly reminds us that this gift comes through God's “exceedingly great and precious promises” (2 Peter 1:4, NKJV). Every promise of God carries divine life and not only that, all of God's promises have been fulfilled in Christ (2 Corinthians 1:20). As you believe and live by His Word, you escape the world's corruption and limitations. The more we lean into His truth, the more His nature becomes evident in your life.
  - In John 1:16, NKJV, Jesus modeled this divine life perfectly. “And of His fullness we have all received, and grace for grace” What flowed from Jesus now flows into us. Hallelujah! Because Christ lives in us, we are not bound to live powerless or defeated lives anymore. We carry His nature, which empowers us to love
  - beyond ourselves, forgive freely, and walk in righteousness.
  - Partakers of the divine nature also means our identity has changed. 2 Corinthians 5:17, NKJV says, “If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new”. God didn't just improve us; He recreated us. This is amazing! His life in us produces new desires, new attitudes, and a new way of living that reflects Him to the world.
  - So, today, live with awareness of who you are. You are not trying to become spiritual; you already carry God's nature within you. As you walk in faith, His life shines through yours. You are a living expression of His divine nature on the earth. Amen!
prayer: Father, I thank You for making me a partaker of Your divine nature. Your life is at work in me, shaping everything about me for your glory. I declare that I walk in the fullness of Your power, love, and grace. The Spirit of the Lord is upon me and in me; the Almighty God has breathed life into me; as a result, I carry God in me wherever I go. In Jesus' name, Amen.
