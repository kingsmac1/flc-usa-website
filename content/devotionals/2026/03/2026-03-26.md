title: Stirring Up The Holy Ghost
scripture: Acts 1:8 (KJV)
verse: But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you...
body:
  - When you received the Holy Ghost, you also received power. However, this power initially exists in the form of potential energy. Potential energy is the stored energy that an object has because of its position or configuration, giving it the potential to do work. Think of it as energy "waiting" to be released.
  - 'The manifestation of it depends on whether you stir up the Holy Ghost within you. It is not a second experience, but a second procedure: the activation of what you already possess. Just as a procedure in technology involves deliberate steps to accomplish a task, stirring up the Spirit requires intentional spiritual engagement. The Holy Ghost does not need to come again; He is already in you. The responsibility is to activate His power.'
  - This activation comes through spiritual discipline. Studying the Word strengthens your faith. Prayer ignites the Spirit. Fasting subdues the flesh. Consecration positions you for divine use. Impartation can further stir the gift within you. These are not religious routines; they are spiritual procedures that convert potential power into manifested power.
  - Jesus demonstrated this reality. Acts 10:38 says that “God anointed Him with the Holy Ghost and with power, and He went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed of the devil”. The Holy Ghost was stirred, and the power was evident.
  - Paul reminded Timothy to “stir up the gift of God” (2 Timothy 1:6–7), emphasizing that the Spirit given to us is not one of “fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind”. The power of the Spirit does not fade, but it can remain dormant if neglected.
  - The Holy Ghost in you is a dynamic force. As you engage Him through prayer, the Word, and consecration, the potential becomes manifested, and the power becomes evident in your life. Hallelujah!
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for the gift of the Holy Ghost and the power within me. I stir up that power in me and I declare I am full of the Holy Ghost. Let Your Spirit be manifested in me in a significant way for Your glory. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
