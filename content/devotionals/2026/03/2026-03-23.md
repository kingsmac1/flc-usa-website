date: '2026-03-23'
title: Refuse To Be Trapped
scripture: Colossians 2:20 (KJV)
verse: Wherefore if ye be dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as though living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances?
body:
  - 'If you are dead with Christ, you are no longer subject to the elementary principles of this world. Yet many believers still live as though they are bound by systems Christ has already freed them from. Colossians 2:20 asks a powerful question: If you died with Christ, why live as though you still belong to the old order?'
  - Galatians 4:3 explains that before Christ died for us, we were “enslaved under the basic principles of the world.” Verse 9 continues, “But now that you know God… how is it that you are turning back to those weak and worthless principles? Do you wish to be enslaved by them all over again?” The tragedy is not ignorance alone, but returning to bondage after receiving freedom.
  - Hosea 4:6 declares, “My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.” Destruction does not always come because of the devil's strength, but because of the believer's ignorance. When knowledge is rejected or not properly taught, people remain vulnerable. What you do not know, you cannot enforce. What you do not understand, you cannot walk in.
  - As a new creation, you are not under curses, foundational bondage, or ancestral chains. However, we are in warfare. The enemy contests what he has legally lost. That is why Scripture instructs us to put on the whole armor of God. Ignorance leaves a believer exposed, but revelation equips him to walk in victory.
  - Why then do some believers experience setbacks similar to unbelievers? Often, it is not because Christ failed, but because they have not renewed their minds or exercised their authority in Christ. Freedom must be known, believed, and enforced. You cannot live above what you do not understand.
  - Refuse to be trapped by what Christ has already defeated. You are not subject to the rudiments of this world. You are in Christ; dead to the old system and alive unto God. Walk in knowledge. Stand in authority. Enforce your victory. Hallelujah!
prayer: Father, I refuse to live in ignorance or return to bondage. I am dead with Christ and alive unto You. I reject every weak and worthless principle of this world. I walk in revelation, authority, and freedom. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
