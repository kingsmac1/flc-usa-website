title: Resist The Devil
scripture: James 4:7 (AMP)
verse: So, submit to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.
body:
  - Satan is rebellious. He knows that Jesus has already blotted out every ordinance that stood against you, yet he still attempts to accuse, intimidate, and afflict; trying to enforce charges that Christ has already canceled. But at that point, you must stand firm on the finished work of Christ. Jesus said, “It is finished.” The transaction is complete. The debt has been paid.
  - The enemy's strategy is persistence. He will show up. He will attempt to suggest that you are still under bondage, still under a curse, still bound by foundations or generational altars that Christ has already dealt with. This is a mind- bending strategy. If he can penetrate your mind, he can trap you. But if you stand firm, he cannot prevail.
  - Ephesians 6:16 says, “Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.” The shield of faith is your unwavering confidence in what Christ accomplished. Faith does not debate the devil; it
  - enforces victory.
  - 'First Peter 5:8–9 reveals to us that the devil prowls like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour. Notice: he roars, but he is not the Lion. He searches for the unstable, the ignorant, the fearful. The Scripture says, “Stand firm against him, and be strong in your faith.” Resistance is firmness in truth.'
  - 'If unfavorable conditions arise, do not panic or assume bondage. Take the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God (Ephesians 6:17), and cut down every lie. Declare what Christ has done. Enforce your freedom. Refuse to entertain accusations that the cross has already silenced. Be assured: the devil will flee when resisted. It may not be instant, but persistence in truth forces retreat. When he flees, you begin to fully enjoy what Christ secured for you. Hallelujah!'
prayer: Father, I submit to Your authority and stand firm in the finished work of Christ. Every accusation, every lie, and every fiery dart of the enemy is quenched by my faith. I resist the devil, and he flees from me. I enforce my victory in Jesus' Name. Amen.
