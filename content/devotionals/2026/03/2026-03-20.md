title: Live By The Word
scripture: 1 John 1:9 (KJV)
verse: If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.
body:
  - God's Word is truth, whether your feelings agree with it or not. When Scripture says that if you confess your sins, He forgives you, that settles it. The instant you confess, forgiveness is granted.
  - Many Christians struggle because they allow their feelings to govern them. Imagine confessing a sin to God today and receiving forgiveness immediately. Then two hours later, feelings of guilt and inferiority begin to surface. For the next two days, you feel condemned and conclude that perhaps God did not forgive you after all. What happened? The Word said you were forgiven, but your feelings said otherwise.
  - 'Later, while hearing the Word preached, light dawns in your heart. You realize you were forgiven the moment you confessed. Your feelings had been lying to you. At that moment, you make a decision: “God said if I confess, He forgives. I confessed. Therefore, I am forgiven.” You reaffirm the forgiveness you already received. You choose the Word over your emotions.'
  - This is what it means to live by the Word and not by feelings. Feelings fluctuate but the Word remains. Faith stands on what God said, not on what the senses report. Living by the Word also means deciding that God's word is greater than your emotions, greater than your circumstances, and greater than your present experience. The Word is final authority.
  - Refuse to be governed by how you feel. Stand on what God has said. Reaffirm what you have received. Walk by faith, and not by your senses.
prayer: Father, I live by Your Word and not by my feelings. Your word is true, and I stand on it. My emotions are subject to Your Word. And your word is working in my spirit, soul and body. I take advantage of every blessing and provision You have given me in your word. I walk by faith and not by sight. In Jesus' Name, Amen!
