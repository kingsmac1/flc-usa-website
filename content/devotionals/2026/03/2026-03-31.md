title: You Are Anointed
scripture: 1 John 2:27 (KJV)
verse: But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you…
body:
  - Anything that burdens your body and anything that oppresses your body is a yoke. There is not a single thing in this life that can burden your physical body or oppress you physically that the anointing cannot free you from! This is good news, especially when you understand that oppression is a word often used in the Bible to describe the effects of sickness and disease.
  - 'Acts 10:38 says, “How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him”. According to this scripture, sickness and disease fall under the category of oppression. To be oppressed means to be subjected to harsh control or domination. Sickness tries to dominate a person''s life by determining how they function, limiting their activities, and influencing their daily choices. It seeks to control the body and restrict freedom. Yet the Word of God makes it clear that the anointing has the power to liberate from such oppression.'
  - Isaiah 10:27 declares, “…and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.” It does not suggest possibility or uncertainty. It does not say the yoke could be destroyed. It firmly
  - states that the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing. The word “shall” expresses confidence and divine certainty. It assures us that no burden or bondage can ultimately prevail where the anointing is present. The anointing of the Holy Spirit within you is God's provision for breaking every chain, lifting every burden, and removing every heavy load.
  - The same Spirit who anointed Jesus now lives in you. The anointing within you is greater than any force that tries to attach itself to your body or your life. Stand firm in this truth, because you shall know the truth and the truth shall make you free.
  - 'Boldly declare it: I am anointed of the Holy Ghost, and this anointing breaks every yoke of bondage, sickness, and affliction. It restores my strength. Amen! Walk in the confidence that you are anointed and that God''s power is actively at work in you.'
prayer: Father, thank You that I am anointed by Your Spirit. The same power that worked in Jesus is at work in me. Every burden is lifted and every yoke is destroyed because of the anointing. I walk in freedom, strength, and victory today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
