title: Endued With Divine Authority
scripture: Luke 10:19 NLT
verse: Look, I have given you authority over all the power of the enemy, and you can walk among snakes and scorpions and crush them. Nothing will injure you.
body:
  - 'When the Lord Jesus said, “I have given you authority,” He was acknowledging that the enemy has power, but emphasizing that the authority God has given us is far greater. This authority is complete and covers all the power of the enemy, giving believers the ability to walk in victory and overcome every scheme of darkness. The Amplified Bible explains this further: “Listen carefully: I have given you authority to tread on serpents and scorpions, over all the power of the enemy (Satan); and nothing will harm you” (Luke 10:19 AMP).'
  - Many believers are unaware of this authority, which is why they experience needless affliction. The power has already been given, and God has also provided the ability to exercise it effectively. When Jesus spoke of treading on serpents and scorpions, this can be understood literally, as in protection from dangerous creatures (Mark 16:18), or figuratively, as representing the devil, his principalities, powers, and all his emissaries. These forces are cunning, harmful, and destructive, yet Christ has power over all of them and has communicated this authority to His followers.
  - 'This is very clear: nothing shall, by any means, harm you. This'
  - includes natural dangers, malicious people, and even spiritual forces of darkness. Nothing can touch your soul, threaten your eternal welfare, or hinder the work of God prospering in your hands. Believers who understand and exercise this authority walk boldly, fearlessly, and victoriously, knowing that their life, ministry, and purpose are fully protected and empowered. Hallelujah!
prayer: Father, I thank You for the divine authority You have given me over all the power of the enemy. I walk boldly in this authority, treading on every serpent and scorpion in my life. Nothing can harm my soul, my spirit, or the work of Your hands. I exercise the authority You have freely given me, and I walk in victory and divine protection, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
