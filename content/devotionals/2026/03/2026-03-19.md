title: Keeping The Love Aflame
scripture: Revelation 2:4 (KJV)
verse: Nevertheless, I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.
body:
  - 'Love can be present, yet not burning. Jesus rebuked the church at Ephesus not for lack of works, discipline, or doctrine, but because they had left their first love. They were active, but their affection had cooled. This reveals a sobering truth: it is possible to serve God and yet lose the fire of love.'
  - The love of God was shed abroad in your heart when you were born again (Romans 5:5). That love is divine and enduring. However, its intensity depends on how you steward it. Fire must be maintained. If unattended, even a strong flame can diminish.
  - Keeping the love aflame requires intentional fellowship. Time in the Word keeps your heart aligned with God's heart. Prayer keeps intimacy alive. Worship rekindles affection. Love grows where communion is consistent.
  - 'Guard your heart from the things that quench love: offense, bitterness, pride, and distraction. The cares of this world can slowly cool spiritual passion if left unchecked. Refuse to allow familiarity to replace reverence. Love also remains aflame when it is expressed. Share the Gospel. Serve others. Forgive quickly. Walk in obedience. Love completely intensifies when it'
  - is practiced.
  - Paul exhorted Timothy to “stir up the gift of God” (2 Timothy 1:6). In the same way, you must stir up your love for God daily. Reflect on what Christ has done for you. Remember the cross and meditate on His mercy because gratitude fuels love.
  - Do not allow your love to become mechanical or routine. Let it burn brightly. Let it be evident. A heart set ablaze with love for Christ will walk in power, purity, and purpose. Glory to God!
prayer: Father, I refuse to let my love grow cold. Rekindle my passion for You daily. As I pray, study, and worship, let my love burn brighter. I guard my heart against anything that would quench this fire. My love for You remains strong and fervent. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Revelation 2:1–5; Romans 5:5; 2 Timothy 1:6
