title: Stir Up The Love
scripture: Romans 5:5 (KJV)
verse: And hope maketh not ashamed; because the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us.
body:
  - When you were born again, the love of God was poured into your heart by the Holy Ghost. This was not temporary. The ability to walk in divine love was implanted in you. You have the capacity to forgive. You have the capacity to be patient, kind, and gracious. That love does not evaporate, fade, or disappear. Once God gives it, it is yours.
  - Yet many believers show little evidence of this love in their daily lives. Not because it is absent, but because it is dormant. The same way the gifts of God can be impacted to you through the laying on of hands and remain inactive if not stirred up (2 Timothy 1:6), the love of God within you can lie unused. What is given by God must often be activated by you.
  - 'This clearly proves an important truth: a Christian can receive something from God and still fail to walk in its manifestation. The presence of love in your heart does not automatically guarantee the expression of love in your actions. Stirring it up is your responsibility.'
  - Every born-again child of God has the ability to activate the love
  - within at any time. Once God gives you the capacity to love, what you do with it is up to you. God desires that you walk in love, but He will not force it to operate in you. Love is a choice. Activation is intentional.
  - You control whether love remains dormant or becomes active in your life. If you can stir up fear, anger, or offense, you can also stir up love. Choose to forgive. Choose to overlook wrongs. Choose to respond with grace. The love of Christ is already in you. Release it and walk in it. Hallelujah!
prayer: Father, I thank You that Your love has been shed abroad in my heart by the Holy Ghost. I refuse to let that love remain dormant. I choose to walk in forgiveness, patience, and kindness. I stir up divine love within me and let it flow freely through my life. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
