date: '2026-03-12'
title: Walk In Love
scripture: 1 John 4:16
verse: God is love, and whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them.
body:
  - God is love; not merely loving, but love itself. To know God is to express love. It is impossible to claim intimacy with Him while struggling to love people. Love is the clearest evidence of divine life at work in a person. When love is absent, something vital is missing in one's understanding and expression of God.
  - Many today struggle with discrimination, prejudice, and division, loving only those who look like them, think like them, or belong to the same class or background. Such attitudes do not reflect the nature of God. The life you received in Christ carries His nature, and that nature produces definite fruits. Love is not optional; it is the primary fruit of the recreated human spirit.
  - Jesus taught that identity is revealed by fruit. Just as a tree is known by what it produces, those who truly know God are recognized by the love they express. Love is not mere emotion or preference; it is a deliberate expression of God's nature through words, actions, and attitudes.
  - 'Loving others is no longer difficult because the source of love is already within you. Romans 5:5 declares: “And hope does not'
  - disappoint us, because God's love has been poured out into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us.” God's love has been poured out (lavishly) into our hearts by the Holy Spirit. This means you do not love from emptiness or effort. You love from abundance. There is more than enough love in you to forgive, to endure, and to give repeatedly.
  - From Matthew 18:21-22, When Jesus spoke of forgiving “seventy times seven,” He did so with confidence in the Holy Spirit who would dwell in believers. Forgiveness, patience, and kindness are possible because divine love supplies them continually. Hatred, bitterness, envy, strife, and unforgiveness are inconsistent with the life you carry.
  - 'Choose to walk in love daily. Let love govern your responses, shape your relationships, and define your conduct. As you yield to the Holy Spirit, love flows freely and completely through you: pure, unconditional and divine love.'
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for Your love poured richly into my heart by the Holy Spirit. I walk in love toward all, without prejudice, bitterness, or unforgiveness. My words and actions reflect Your nature. I forgive freely, respond kindly, and live as an expression of Your love. Love governs my life, and through me, your love is made visible, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
