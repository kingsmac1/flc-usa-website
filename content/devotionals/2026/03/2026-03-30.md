date: '2026-03-30'
title: Use Your Time Wisely
scripture: Psalm 90:12
verse: So, teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom
body:
  - 'Time is one of the most precious resources God has given you. Money lost can be regained. Opportunities missed may return. But time, once spent, cannot be reclaimed: except by divine mercy when God grants a kairos moment, a divinely appointed season of restoration and opportunity.'
  - The Psalmist prayed, “Teach us to number our days.” Why? Because wisdom begins with the awareness that life is limited. When you understand that your days are numbered, you become intentional. You stop drifting and start living purposefully. The Bible says, “See then that you walk circumspectly, not as fools but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil” (Ephesians 5:15–16). To redeem the time means to make the most of every opportunity.
  - King Solomon, the wisest man who lived before Jesus Christ, understood this truth later in life. Though God blessed him with extraordinary wisdom, he allowed distractions and misplaced priorities to consume valuable years. In reflection, he wrote, “Remember now your Creator in the days of your youth, before the difficult days come” (Ecclesiastes 12:1). He realized that seasons pass quickly, and neglected time cannot be recovered by human effort alone.
  - 'Time is a sacred trust. God expects you to be a faithful steward of every second and every minute. What you do daily determines who you become permanently. Jesus declared, “I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work” (John 9:4). There is a “day” season of opportunity: strength to act, capacity to build, and grace to grow. When that season closes, unused moments cannot be reclaimed.'
  - Work while it is day. Pray while it is day. Build while it is day. Learn while it is day. Refuse procrastination. Refuse distractions that drain your destiny. Ask the Lord to align your schedule with your purpose. Let your time reflect your calling. Let your priorities mirror eternity. Time is valuable. Use it wisely.
prayer: Dear Lord, teach me to number my days and to steward my time with wisdom. I refuse distraction and delay. I redeem the time and maximize every opportunity You give me. My days are purposeful, productive, and aligned with Your will, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
