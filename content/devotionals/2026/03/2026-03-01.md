title: Agents Of Peace
scripture: Matthew 5:9 (KJV)
verse: 'Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the sons of God.'
body:
  - Corinthians 5:19 (KJV) says “To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation.” God's plan for the world is peace, and He accomplished it through Christ. In Jesus, God reconciled the world to Himself, refusing to hold humanity's sins against them. This divine act of reconciliation reveals God's heart; He desired restoration, settlement, and peace between Himself and man. Then, having completed the work in Christ, He entrusted that same message to us.
  - The word of reconciliation is the Gospel; the good news that peace has been made available through Christ. Reconciliation means settlement, harmony, and restored relationship. Through the Gospel, hostility is removed and fellowship is restored. This message is not just an information; it is a divine invitation. God has charged us with carrying this message to the world.
  - As believers, we are not spectators; we are participants. We are carriers of peace. When we proclaim the Gospel, peace is announced, offered, and received. Through our words and conduct, men encounter God's love and grace. This is why
  - Jesus called peacemakers sons of God; they reflect His nature and mission.
  - '2 Corinthians 5:18 makes this responsibility unmistakable: God has given us the ministry of reconciliation. This is a task, a stewardship, and a calling. Every believer is entrusted with inviting others into the peace God has already provided. Evangelism and discipleship are not optional extras; they are central to the Christian life.'
  - Do not allow busyness, comfort, or complacency to silence your witness. Your life should not push people away from Christ; it should draw them toward Him. Refuse to be a casual or bench- warming Christian. Make yourself available. Proclaim the Gospel. Live out the message. Let your words and actions consistently point others to the peace found in Christ. You are God's agent of peace in your world. You were sent to reconcile, restore, and reveal His love.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for reconciling me to Yourself through Christ. I receive the ministry and word of reconciliation with joy and responsibility. I am an agent of peace, carrying the Gospel boldly and living it clearly. Through my life and words, many are drawn to Christ and experience Your peace. I make myself available for Your purpose, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
