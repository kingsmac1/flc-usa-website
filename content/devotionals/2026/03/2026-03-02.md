date: '2026-03-02'
title: Be Accountable For Your Body
scripture: Romans 12:1 (KJV)
verse: I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
body:
  - One of the most profound ways of worshipping God is to glorify Him with your body. Your body is not truly yours; you are only a custodian. Every habit, every choice, and every action reflects how you honor God. Doing what pleases Him with your body is an act of worship. 1 Corinthians 10:31 says, “Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God.” Recognizing that God lives in you should create a lifestyle of reverence toward the temple He inhabits.
  - 'Don''t destroy your body. Habits such as smoking, alcoholism, gluttony, and other unhealthy practices dishonor God. Many neglect their health and even die young or in sickness because of careless choices. Your body is a gift from God, and it deserves protection and care. 1 Corinthians 9:27 says, “But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway.” Taking care of your body ensures that it functions optimally and fulfills God''s purpose for you.'
  - Don't defile God's temple with ungodly conduct. Guard your
  - 'thoughts, your habits, and the things you allow into your life. Impure actions and ungodly behaviors weaken the temple God dwells in. 2 Timothy 2:22 (KJV) says, “Flee also youthful lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.” Maintaining holiness in body and mind strengthens your walk with God and honors His Spirit within.'
  - 'Speak life over your body. Words carry power; they can either build or destroy. Declare health, strength, and vitality over yourself daily. “Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof” (Proverbs 18:21). Care for your body with proper rest, nutrition, and exercise, and refuse to allow sickness or weakness a foothold. Your diligence in stewarding your temple brings glory to God and sustains your spiritual and physical life.'
prayer: Father, thank You for the gift of my body. I declare that I will care for it, honor it, and glorify You in all that I do with my body. I reject every habit, thought, or action that dishonors Your temple. I speak life, health, and vitality over my body, and I present it to You as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable. Amen.
