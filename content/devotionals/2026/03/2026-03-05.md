title: Focus On Intimacy With Christ, Not Results
scripture: Job 28:28
verse: And unto man He said, Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.
body:
  - Wisdom is only effective when it is expressed through obedience. Knowing what is right without acting on it produces no lasting fruit. God's wisdom is not given only to improve outcomes or enhance success; it is given to cultivate intimacy with Him and guide our daily walk.
  - Solomon's life offers a clear lesson. At the start of his reign, he recognized his dependence on God and humbly asked for wisdom. This pleased the Lord, and Solomon received extraordinary insight. However, even with such divine wisdom, he began making choices that conflicted with God's Word. Through alliances and compromises, his heart slowly drifted from wholehearted devotion.
  - This shows that wisdom alone does not guarantee intimacy with God. Wisdom must be maintained through fellowship. Solomon's decline did not begin with rebellion but with misplaced priorities. He began to value influence, convenience, visibility and results above obedience and relationship with God.
  - God entrusted Solomon with great possibilities, but those possibilities came with responsibility. While Solomon became famous for his achievements, his inward devotion weakened. He focused on what he could build and produce, yet neglected the condition of his heart. Consequently, the wisdom that impressed nations could not preserve his personal walk with God.
  - Later in life, Solomon reflected and concluded that life pursued apart from God is empty. Achievements, pleasure, and recognition fade when they are not established in fellowship with the Lord. True fulfillment is found only in a living relationship with God, where intimacy precedes results.
  - God is more interested in who you are becoming than your results and achievements. When intimacy with Him is your priority, results follow in their proper place and time. Let the Holy Spirit guide your decisions and shape your desires.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for the fellowship I have with You. Your Word governs my heart and actions. I choose knowing you more over outward success and to do your will over my personal gain in Jesus' Name. Amen.
