title: Apart From God
scripture: Proverbs 14:14
verse: 'The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways: and a good man shall be satisfied from himself.'
body:
  - 'Being apart from God simply means the exemption of God. This is a civilization without God: not getting God involved. It is doing things outside God''s governing principles, the ways of life He has established (Proverbs 3:5-6). Living apart from God means going about life in your own ways: the way of the senses, the way it feels right, the way that brings comfort, or putting yourself first so far that it brings temporary happiness (Isaiah 55:8-9).'
  - 'Proverbs 1:32 warns, “For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.” People can reach a point where they are filled with their own ways instead of the ways of God. Without realizing it, they are on the road to Babel: the ways of Babel, carried away by the vanity of their minds. At times, they may even appear successful, not knowing that this path will ultimately lead to destruction (Psalm 1:1-6).'
  - Many act as if God's involvement is optional. They say, “Every time and everything, God… let me enjoy myself for once, it wouldn't hurt.” “Who has God helped? God apart, church apart,
  - 'let''s be real and not talk faith.” A Biblical example is Cain, the originator of this civilization and way of life without God. He tried to exist with his Creator but on his own terms: “Let me do it my own way” (Genesis 4:1-8). “The self” (self-righteousness, self- grandeur, self-worth) is the enemy of God. It leads to pride, and pride is the downfall of men: Just as Proverbs 16:18 rightly says, “Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.”'
  - No wonder John 3:30 says, “He must increase, but I must decrease.” When we live apart from God, the 'self' increases and God decreases. True life and lasting success come only in dependence on Him (Matthew 6:33; Psalm 37:4-5).
prayer: Blessed Father, I refuse to live apart from You. I submit my ways, my desires, and my plans to Your guidance. Let Your principles govern my life, and may I always walk under Your covering. I decrease so that You may increase in me. Hallelujah!
