title: Oneness With Christ
scripture: Galatians 2:20 (NKJV)
verse: I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me.
body:
  - Our oneness with Christ is a spiritual reality established by God Himself. When we placed our faith in Jesus, we were not just forgiven; we were united with Him. Scripture teaches that we are joined to the Lord as one spirit, meaning, His life now animates ours. This inseparable union forms the foundation of our identity, authority, and purpose. Christianity is not about striving to become something new, but about living from what has already been made true in Christ. Glory to God!
  - 'Jesus described this oneness using the image of a vine and its branches in John 15:4, NKJV: “Abide in Me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in Me”. Fruitfulness occurs naturally from connection, not effort. It''s interesting to know that some people tend to disconnect with their minds because they either don''t believe or doubt this reality. When we remain conscious of our oneness with Him, His life produces love, obedience, and power through us. Disconnection weakens, but abiding strengthens and sustains.'
  - 'The apostle Paul further explains that this oneness means shared life and shared victory: “But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit with Him” (1 Corinthians 6:17, NKJV). This oneness means Christ''s righteousness covers us, His Spirit dwells within us, and His strength empowers us daily. We are not facing life alone; Christ faces it in us and with us. Christ overcame every situation he faced and because He is facing life with us, victory is 100% guaranteed.'
  - Understanding our inseparable oneness transforms how we see ourselves. We no longer define our identity by past failures, emotions, or circumstances. Instead, we see ourselves hidden in Christ, secure and accepted. His victory over sin and death becomes our victory, giving us confidence to live boldly and faithfully.
  - Finally, this truth calls us to live intentionally. Because Christ lives in us, our words, actions, and choices matter. We represent Him wherever we go. As we yield daily to His indwelling presence, our lives become living testimonies of His power and love. Oneness with Christ is not only our position; it is our daily experience.
prayer: Father, I thank You for making me inseparable with Christ. As Christ is, so am I here on earth. I declare that His life lives through me, His strength sustains me, and His victory defines me always. I abide in Christ daily, walking in faith, obedience, and love. In Jesus' name, Amen.
