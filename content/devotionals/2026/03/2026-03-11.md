title: He Sent His Word
scripture: Psalm 107:20 (KJV)
verse: He sent His word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.
body:
  - 'God''s response to human need has always been His Word. Psalm 107:20 reveals a profound truth: God sent His Word, and healing followed. He did not send silver or gold, nor did He send methods or materials; He sent His Word. The Word was the messenger, and the result was healing and deliverance. This shows us that the Word of God carries divine life, power, and effect.'
  - Everything God does is initiated and accomplished through His Word. Healing, protection, restoration, provision, direction, and deliverance are wrapped up in what He has spoken. When the Word is released, it goes on assignment. It performs, it restores, and it brings men out of destruction. This means that whatever the need is (whether in the body, the mind, relationships, or circumstances), the Word is the answer.
  - 'Isaiah 55:11 (KJV) says, “So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void…”.This reveals that God''s Word never fails in its mission. Once released, it accomplishes exactly what God intends and prospers in the purpose for which it was sent. Situations do not restrict it.'
  - Human experiences do not cancel it. God's Word stands superior to every opposing situation.
  - 'Negative experiences do not invalidate God''s Word. The truth remains unchanged regardless of what is seen or felt. Victory is found in cleaving to the Word: studying it, meditating on it, and giving it full authority in your life. Neglect of the Word leaves one vulnerable.'
  - When you speak, you are actually sending your words on an assignment. As you declare God's Word over your life and situations, it carries the same power and produces the same effect. The Word in your mouth becomes a living force, releasing healing and restoration. All you will ever need is already contained in God's Word. Receive it, speak it, and watch it prevail.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for Your Word sent to me. Your Word brings healing, peace, and restoration into my life. I hold fast to what You have spoken, knowing it never fails. I speak Your Word boldly over my body, my life, my family, my nation and my circumstances, and I see it produce results in Jesus' Name. Amen! FURTHER
