title: Christ is Your Deliverance
scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17 (AMP)
verse: Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creature; the old things have passed away. Behold, new things have come”.
body:
  - 'When you are in Christ, deliverance is not something you are trying to obtain; it is your present reality. You are not the same person you used to be. The old moral and spiritual condition has passed away. A new life has begun. Deliverance is not merely an experience; it is a transformation. Colossians 2:14 KJV declares that Christ was “blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us… nailing it to his cross.” The “handwriting” refers to a bond: a legal certificate of debt that demanded payment and penalty. It stood against us, condemning and opposing our peace, joy, and freedom. But Christ canceled it.'
  - The New International version (NIV) of the above scripture says He “canceled the charge of our legal indebtedness… he has taken it away, nailing it to the cross.” The Amplified version explains that this certificate of debt consisted of legal demands that were hostile to us. Yet Jesus did not cover it; He removed it completely.
  - 'Ephesians 2:15 reveals how this was done: “by abolishing in His crucified flesh the hostility caused by the Law with its commandments and ordinances”. What condemned us, He satisfied. What stood against us, He took out of the way. The act'
  - of atonement is finished, but its effect remains. This means your deliverance is not pending. The bondage has been broken. The legal claim of the enemy has been canceled. The cross was complete. In Christ, you are free, justified, and restored to peace with God.
  - 'Stop seeking deliverance as though it is outside of you. If you are in Christ, you have been delivered: “who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son” (Colossians 1:13) Walk in this reality. Hallelujah!'
  - Make up your mind completely to rejoice today. Refuse to allow circumstances to dictate your happiness. Let the joy of the Holy Spirit rise in your heart, overflow in your life, and shine through every situation. Remember, the joy of the Lord is your strength, and it is yours to walk in daily.
prayer: Father, I thank You that in Christ, I am a new creation. Every legal claim against my life has been canceled and nailed to the cross. I refuse condemnation and bondage. I walk in the freedom, peace, and deliverance purchased for me through Jesus Christ. Amen.
