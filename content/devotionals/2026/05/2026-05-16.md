date: '2026-05-16'
title: Preach The Gospel
scripture: Mark 16:15
verse: Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.
body:
  - 'The Gospel is the greatest message ever given to mankind: the message of salvation, hope, love, and eternal life through Jesus Christ. And this message has been entrusted to you. You are not just a believer; you are a messenger, a carrier of good news to a world that desperately needs it.'
  - Preaching the Gospel is not limited to a pulpit; it is a lifestyle. It is in your words, your actions, your character, and your love. Every time you speak truth, show kindness, or demonstrate God's grace, you are revealing Christ to others.
  - You don't have to wait until you feel “ready” or “qualified.” God has already equipped you. The same Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead lives in you. That means you have been enabled to impact lives, to bring light into darkness, and to point people toward salvation.
  - There are people around you (at work, in your neighborhood, among your friends) who are searching for something real. You are the answer God has placed in their path. Your testimony, your faith, and your boldness can lead someone to a transformed life in Christ. Do not be silent. Refuse fear, reject intimidation, and step out with confidence. The Gospel is not something to hide; it is something to proclaim. When you share it, heaven rejoices, lives are changed, and God is glorified. Let your light shine. Speak the Word. Live the message. Preach the Gospel everywhere you go.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for entrusting me with the Gospel of Jesus Christ. I declare that I am bold, unashamed, and ready to share Your word with others. My life reflects Your love, and my words carry power and grace. I impact lives, bring hope, and lead others to Christ, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
