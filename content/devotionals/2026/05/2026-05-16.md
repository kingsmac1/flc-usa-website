title: Return To Your First Love
scripture: Revelation 2:4
verse: Nevertheless, I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.
body:
  - It is possible to remain active in spiritual things while the heart slowly drifts away from deep fellowship with God. A person may still attend church, pray occasionally, and continue outward routines, yet inwardly lose the passion and tenderness they once had toward the Lord.
  - Jesus spoke to the church in Ephesus about this very condition. They were disciplined, hardworking, and doctrinally sound, but something important was missing. Their love for Him was no longer what it once had been.
  - Many believers do not drift away suddenly. It often happens gradually through distraction, spiritual neglect, disappointment, busyness, compromise, or prolonged focus on earthly pressures. Over time, prayer becomes routine, worship loses sincerity, and time with God becomes less important. God never intended relationship with Him to become empty religious activity. Christianity is not merely about duty. It is fellowship with the living Christ.
  - The phrase first love speaks of devotion, affection, and sincere pursuit of God. It reflects the hunger and sensitivity that often marks a believer's early walk with Him. There was excitement in prayer, joy in worship, and genuine desire for His presence.
  - Jesus did not speak to condemn the church in Ephesus but to call them back. Revelation 2:5 says, “Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works.” God still calls hearts back to closeness with Him.
  - Many people search for satisfaction in achievements, entertainment, relationships, or possessions while their souls quietly long for deeper communion with God. Nothing in this world can replace intimacy with Him. Returning to your first love begins with turning your attention back to God sincerely. It means making room again for prayer, worship, Scripture, and fellowship with the Holy Spirit. It means valuing His presence more than distractions.
  - God has not withdrawn from His people. He still desires closeness with those who seek Him sincerely. Jeremiah 29:13 says, “And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.” A heart fully turned toward God will never remain empty.
prayer: Father, thank You for continually drawing my heart back to You. Thank You because Your presence still satisfies the deepest needs of my soul. My heart remains tender toward You and responsive to Your voice. Spiritual distraction and coldness will not take root within me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
