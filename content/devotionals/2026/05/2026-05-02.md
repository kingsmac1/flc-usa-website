title: Be modest
scripture: 1 Timothy 2:9 (KJV)
verse: In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety…
body:
  - 'Modesty is more than outward appearance; it is a reflection of the heart. It is a posture of humility, self- respect, and reverence for God. True modesty is not about restriction; it is about expression: expressing a life that is yielded to God and aware of His presence.'
  - In a world that often promotes attention, display, and self- exaltation, God calls you to something deeper. Modesty is choosing to carry yourself in a way that honors Him, whether in your words, your attitude, or your appearance. It reflects a quiet strength and confidence that comes from knowing who you are in Christ.
  - The Bible says, “Whose adorning let it not be that outward adorning… But let it be the hidden man of the heart…” (1 Peter 3:3–4 (KJV). God is more concerned with your inner beauty (your character, your spirit, your heart) than anything external. When your heart is aligned with Him, your outward life naturally reflects it.
  - Modesty also shows in how you speak and how you relate to
  - others. It is seen in gentleness, respect, and self-control. It is not weakness; it is strength under control. It is choosing wisdom over attention and purpose over approval. Being modest does not mean hiding your worth; it means expressing it in a way that honors God. You don't need to compete for attention when you carry divine identity. Your value is already established in Christ.
  - Let your life reflect dignity, grace, and humility. Let your choices point others to God, not to self. Walk in quiet confidence, knowing that who you are in Christ is more than enough. Modesty is not limitation; it is distinction.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for teaching me to live with humility and grace. I declare that my life reflects modesty in my thoughts, words, and actions. My heart is aligned with You, and my outward life honors You. I walk in confidence, dignity, and self- control, expressing my true identity in Christ, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
