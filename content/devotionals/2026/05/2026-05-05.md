date: '2026-05-05'
title: don’t be average
scripture: Hebrews 6:11 (KJV)
verse: And we desire that every one of you do show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope unto the end.
body:
  - 'There''s something about you that was never meant to blend in. Deep down, you know it: you were created for more than just getting by, more than just doing what''s expected, more than settling into a life of routine and comfort.'
  - Average is easy. It doesn't require much; just enough effort to stay where you are. But the life God designed for you calls for something greater. It calls for intention, for growth, for a mindset that refuses to stay the same. Excellence begins with how you think. When you start seeing your life as meaningful and purposeful, your actions begin to change. You stop doing things halfway. You become more focused, more disciplined, and more aware that what you carry deserves to be expressed fully.
  - The Bible says in Proverbs 22:29 (KJV), “Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings…” That kind of elevation doesn't come randomly; it comes from consistency and commitment. It comes from choosing to give your best, even when no one is watching. This doesn't mean you won't have off days or moments to rest. It simply means you don't stay there. You keep growing. You keep showing up. You
  - keep improving.
  - Colossians 3:23 (KJV) says, “Whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord…” When you approach life this way, everything changes. Your work becomes meaningful. Your effort becomes intentional. Your results begin to reflect a higher standard.
  - You don't need to compete with anyone else. Your assignment is to become the best version of who God created you to be. And that requires you to move beyond average thinking and average effort. So, raise your standard. Be consistent and stay committed to growth.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for placing purpose inside me. I choose to rise above average thinking and living. I commit to growth, discipline, and excellence in all I do. I will remain focused and consistent, giving my best at all times. My life reflects purpose, progress, and Your grace, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
