title: Joint Heirs With Christ
scripture: Romans 8:17 (KJV)
verse: And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ…
body:
  - Your identity in Christ is far greater than many realize. You are not just saved; you are a joint heir with Christ. This means you share in everything that belongs to Him. His victory is your victory. His authority is your authority. His inheritance is your inheritance.
  - Being a joint heir means you are not on the outside looking in; you are fully included. God didn't just bring you close; He brought you into His family with full rights and privileges. You are not trying to qualify for blessings; you already belong, and everything that comes with that relationship is yours.
  - The Bible says in 2 Peter 1:4 (KJV), “Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises…” These promises are not unreachable; they are part of your inheritance in Christ. Healing, peace, provision, righteousness, victory and many more are already made available to you.
  - Sometimes, people live below what God has provided because they are not aware of who they are. But when you understand that you are a joint heir with Christ, your mindset changes. You stop thinking like someone who is lacking and start living like
  - someone who has been richly supplied. This doesn't mean life won't have challenges, but it means you face them from a place of victory, not defeat. You don't beg God for what He has already given; you receive it, walk in it, and manifest it.
  - The Scripture also says, “The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God” (Romans 8:16, KJV). The Holy Spirit constantly reminds you of your identity and your inheritance. As you stay connected to Him, your confidence grows, and your life begins to reflect what is already yours in Christ. You are a child of God. You are an heir. You are a joint heir with Christ. Live like it. Speak like it. Walk in it.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for making me a joint heir with Christ. I declare that I walk in the fullness of my inheritance. I am not lacking; I am richly supplied in Christ. I live with confidence, authority, and joy, knowing who I am and what belongs to me. I manifest Your blessings and promises in my life daily, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
