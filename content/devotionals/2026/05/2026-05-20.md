date: '2026-05-20'
title: Step Out For Soul Winning
scripture: Proverbs 11:30
verse: He who wins souls is wise.
body:
  - Soul winning is the heartbeat of God. It is His deepest desire that no one should perish, but that all should come to the knowledge of the truth. And you are His vessel to make that happen. You are His outstretched hand, His voice, His light in a world searching for hope.
  - It takes a step of faith to reach out. Sometimes, fear may whisper, “What if they reject you?” But remember, you are not representing yourself; you are representing Christ. You are simply sharing the greatest gift ever given. The results are in God's hands; your role is obedience.
  - 'Every person you encounter is valuable to God. That coworker, that neighbor, that stranger: each one is a soul that matters deeply to Him. When you step out to share the Gospel, you are participating in something eternal. Lives are transformed, destinies are changed, and heaven rejoices.'
  - You don't need perfect words or deep theology. Just share from your heart. Speak about Jesus. Tell them what He has done for you. Let your life be a living testimony of His grace and love. The Holy Spirit will guide you, give you the right words, and touch the
  - hearts of those who hear.
  - Don't wait for a “perfect moment.” Create the moment. Be intentional. Be bold. Carry the urgency of heaven in your spirit. One conversation can lead to a lifetime of transformation for someone else. Step out today. Share the Gospel. Win souls. This is wisdom. This is purpose. This is impact that echoes into eternity.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for the privilege of winning souls. I declare that I am bold, fearless, and led by Your Spirit. I step out with confidence to share the Gospel, knowing that lives are changed through me. My words carry power, my life reflects Your love, and I bring many into Your Kingdom. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
