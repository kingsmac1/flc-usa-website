title: divinely enabled
scripture: Philippians 4:13
verse: I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.
body:
  - 'Have you ever faced something that made you feel like, “I don''t think I can do this”? Maybe it felt too big, too new, or beyond your ability. The truth is this: you''re right on your own. But you were never meant to do life on your own ability. One of the reasons people struggle is because they try to run life or do things by their own ability. You didn''t create yourself, and you can''t handle it alone without divine enablement. God has not just called you; He has also equipped you.'
  - Being divinely enabled means you possess God's strength on the inside of you. It's not about how skilled, experienced, or confident you feel. It's about who is with you and who is working through you. When God is involved, what seems impossible becomes possible.
  - There will be moments when you feel stretched or unsure. That's normal. But instead of backing away, lean in. That uncomfortable place is often where God shows up the strongest. He gives you ideas you didn't have before, strength you didn't know you had, and peace in situations that should overwhelm you.
  - Stop talking yourself out of what God has already placed inside you. You don't need to have everything figured out before you start. Take the step. Trust Him. As you move, you'll realize you're not doing it alone; He's right there, helping you every step of the way. You are stronger than you think; not because of you, but because of Him.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for being my strength. Even when I feel unsure, I know I'm not alone. My faith is alive and I rely on Your power in me. I declare that I am capable, not because of my own ability, but because You are working through me. I move forward with confidence and peace, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
