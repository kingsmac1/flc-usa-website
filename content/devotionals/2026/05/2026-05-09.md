title: God’s Formula For Success
scripture: Psalm 119:97
verse: O how I love thy law! it is my meditation all the day.
body:
  - Joshua 1:8 says, “This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night… for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.” God's Word reveals a simple yet powerful formula for success, and it centers on meditation. This kind of meditation isn't empty thinking; it is the intentional, continuous focusing of your mind on God's Word until it becomes your reality. It involves reading, declaring, and deeply pondering the Scriptures until they shape your thoughts and actions.
  - Psalms 1:2 says, “But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.” We see that the successful man delights in the law of the Lord and meditates on it day and night. This means his focus is steady and his passion is genuine. Likewise, in Psalm 119:97, the psalmist expresses a deep love for God's Word, showing that meditation flows naturally from affection. When you love the Word, you won't struggle to stay with it.
  - In Joshua 1:8, God gave a direct instruction to Joshua at a critical moment. He was about to lead God's people into their inheritance, yet the key to his success wasn't human wisdom or strategy; it was a firm attention to the Word. God told him to keep the Word in his mouth and meditate on it continually. The result would be prosperity and good success. So, if you really want to have good success and experience prosperity, you can't overemphasize the importance of meditation. You might feel you are successful where you are without meditation, but it's important to know that there is a higher level of success God has planned for you.
  - 'Meditation produces revelation, and revelation produces a positive change. As you meditate, your thinking changes. As your thinking changes, your words align with God''s word. And when your words are right, your actions follow in the direction of success. This is God''s system: spiritual, yet practical and dependable.'
  - 'It doesn''t matter the challenges around you today. The Word of God has the power to reposition your life. As you keep it before you, speak it boldly, and think on it consistently. You will walk in divine wisdom and make progress in every area of your life. Make God''s Word your daily focus and your constant meditation. Let it fill your heart and guide your decisions. As you do, you will experience the undeniable results of God''s recipe: lasting success, stability, and victory.'
prayer: Dear Father, I thank you for your Word, which is the foundation of my success. I delight in it and meditate on it day and night. I walk in prosperity and good success continually, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
