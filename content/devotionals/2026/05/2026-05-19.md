title: Speak Life - words That Build
scripture: Proverbs 18:21
verse: 'Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof'
body:
  - Have you ever spoken in haste and later wished you could take your words back? Every word you speak carries power. You can either build up or tear down, encourage or discourage, bless or curse. God wants you to choose life-giving words that reflect His Spirit, rather than impulsive words that bring harm. Speaking life is a conscious, Spirit-led practice, not a natural habit of the human tongue.
  - When you speak, your words reveal the condition of your heart. If you are anxious, angry, or impatient, your words often follow suit. But when your heart is aligned with God and your spirit is sensitive to the Holy Spirit, your speech becomes a channel of encouragement, wisdom, and grace. Speaking life means choosing words that inspire, uplift, and reflect God's love in every situation.
  - 'The Bible calls you to think before you speak. James 1:19-20 says, “Let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath.” Before you respond, pause. Ask yourself: Will my words build up or tear down? Will they reflect God''s love or my own frustration? Allow the Holy Spirit to guide your speech. Sometimes, silence is stronger than words, and a careful pause'
  - can prevent unnecessary conflict or regret.
  - When you consistently speak life, you create an environment of peace, trust, and encouragement around you. Your words will carry God's wisdom, strengthen others, and reflect your own spiritual maturity. Even in difficult conversations or stressful situations, life-giving words calm, restore, and inspire. People notice when your speech consistently aligns with God's Spirit, and your influence becomes a testimony of His presence in your life.
  - Let Your tongue be a tool for blessing, a channel for encouragement, and a reflection of God's love in a world that often speaks negativity. Make it your habit to speak with discernment, patience, and faith.
prayer: I speak words that bring life, hope, and encouragement. The Holy Spirit guides my heart and my tongue, and every word I speak reflects God's love and wisdom. I am slow to anger, quick to listen, and intentional in blessing others. My speech flows with grace, truth, and divine purpose in Jesus' name, amen.
furtherStudies: Proverbs 18:21; James 1:19-20; Ephesians 4:29
