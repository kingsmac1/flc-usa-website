date: '2026-05-26'
title: You Are A Light
scripture: Isaiah 60:1
verse: Arise, shine; for your light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee.
body:
  - There is a light in you that the world cannot ignore. It is not natural, neither is it dependent on your circumstances; it is the very light of God deposited in your spirit. When God called you, He filled you with His glory, His presence, and His power.
  - This means you are not meant to live in obscurity, confusion, or defeat. No matter how dark things may seem around you, darkness has no authority over light. The moment light appears, darkness disappears. That is the power you carry. You are God's answer to situations that look impossible.
  - Sometimes, people wait for the “right moment” before they step out. But God is saying, “Arise now.” Shine now. You don't need to have everything figured out before you begin. The light within you will guide your steps as you move forward in faith.
  - Refuse to dim your light because of fear, comparison, or past mistakes. Your light is unique. Your assignment is specific. The same God who placed that light in you is committed to ensuring it shines brighter and brighter.
  - Stay focused in the Word, because the Word fuels your light. As you meditate on it, speak it, and live it, your path becomes clearer, your confidence stronger, and your impact greater. You are not trying to find the light; you are the light. So, arise, shine, and let the world see the glory of God revealed through you.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for making a light. I declare that I will not hide or shrink back. I arise and shine boldly, knowing that Your glory is revealed through my life. My path is illuminated, my purpose is clear, and I walk in victory every day
furtherStudies: Matthew 5:14–16; John 8:12; Proverbs 4:18
