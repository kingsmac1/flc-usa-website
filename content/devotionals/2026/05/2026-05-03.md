title: called into fellowship
scripture: 1 Corinthians 1:9 (KJV)
verse: God is faithful, by whom ye were called unto the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
body:
  - 'c hristianity is more than a belief system; it is a relationship. You have been called into fellowship, into a living, daily connection with Jesus Christ. This is one of the greatest privileges of the new creation: not just to know about God, but to truly know Him.'
  - Fellowship means sharing, communion, and partnership. It means God desires your attention, your time, and your heart. He wants you to walk with Him, talk with Him, and experience His presence in every part of your life. The Bible says, “Truly, our fellowship is with the Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ” (1 John 1:3, KJV). This means you are never alone. God is not distant or unreachable; He is present with you, ready to guide, comfort, and strengthen you.
  - Sometimes, we can get caught up in doing things for God and forget to spend time with God. But fellowship is where strength is renewed, direction is received, and peace is restored. It's in those quiet moments (when you pray, when you study the Word, when you listen) that your spirit is refreshed.
  - Fellowship is also where transformation happens. As you spend time with Him, you begin to think like Him, speak like Him, and live like Him. His nature begins to reflect in you more clearly. The Scripture says, “Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you” (James 4:8, KJV). God is always ready; you just need to respond.
  - Don't reduce your walk with God to just a routine. Make it personal and enjoy the relationship you've been called into. You are called into fellowship; live in it daily.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for calling me into fellowship with You. I walk closely with You every day. My heart is open, my spirit is receptive, and I enjoy Your presence continually. I grow stronger, wiser, and more aligned with You as I spend time in fellowship. My life reflects Your nature and Your love, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
