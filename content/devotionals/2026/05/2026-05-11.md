title: Immovable In Christ
scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:58 (KJV)
verse: Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye steadfast, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord…
body:
  - Life will test your stability. There will be moments of pressure, uncertainty, and opposition that try to shake your faith and shift your focus. But in Christ, you have been given a firm foundation; one that cannot be moved. To be immovable doesn't mean you won't face challenges; it means you refuse to be shaken by them. Your confidence is not in circumstances, people, or outcomes; it is grounded in Christ. When everything around you seems unstable, you remain steady because your anchor is secure.
  - Jesus said, “Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock” (Matthew 7:24, ESV). When your life is built on His Word, storms may come, but they will not destroy you. You stand, not because of your strength, but because of your foundation.
  - Being immovable requires consistency. It means choosing faith over fear, conviction over feelings, and obedience over convenience. It means holding on to God's promises even when you don't see immediate results. There will be voices that try to discourage you, situations that try to wear you down, and
  - delays that test your patience. But don't move. Don't give up. Don't compromise. Stay anchored in what God has said.
  - 'The more you stay grounded in Christ, the stronger you become. Your faith becomes stronger. Your perspective will change. You begin to respond differently: not out of panic, but out of peace and confidence. You are not fragile; you are fortified. You are not easily shaken; you are established in Christ. Stand firm. Stay steady. Be immovable.'
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for being my firm foundation. I declare that I am steadfast and immovable in Christ. No challenge, pressure, or opposition will shake my faith. I stand strong in Your Word, confident in Your promises, and anchored in Your Word. I remain consistent, focused, and unshaken, in Jesus' Name. Amen. FURTHER
