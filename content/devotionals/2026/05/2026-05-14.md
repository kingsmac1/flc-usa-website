title: One Spirit With Christ
scripture: 1 Corinthians 6:17 (KJV)
verse: But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit.
body:
  - There is a divine union that took place the moment you received Christ; a union so real and so powerful that the Bible describes it as becoming one spirit with Him. This is not symbolic; it is a spiritual reality that defines your identity.
  - You are not separated from Christ; you are joined with Him. His life is now your life. His nature is now in you. This means you don't relate to God from a distance; you relate to Him from within. You possess His presence everywhere you go.
  - 'Colossians 1:27 (KJV) says, “...Christ in you, the hope of glory”. This is the mystery and the beauty of the Gospel: Christ lives in you. You are not trying to reach Him; He is already in you, working through you.'
  - Being one spirit with Christ means you are never alone, never powerless, and never without help. His wisdom is available to you. His strength is at work in you. His peace guards your heart. Everything you need flows from this union.
  - “For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all
  - of one…” (Hebrews 2:11, KJV). You share in His life, His righteousness, and His victory. What belongs to Him has been made available to you because you are joined to Him. This changes how you see yourself. You are not just human; you are divinely connected. You are not trying to become something; you are already one with Him. Therefore, you can think like Him, speak like Him, and live like Him. Glory to God!
  - Let this awareness shape your daily life. When you face challenges, remember you are not facing them alone. When you make decisions, remember His wisdom is in you. When you speak, remember His power backs your words. You are one spirit with Christ; live in that consciousness.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for my union with Christ. I declare that I am one spirit with Him. His life flows in me, His wisdom is working in me, and His power works through me. I am never alone, never limited, and never without help. I live daily in the consciousness of this divine union, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
