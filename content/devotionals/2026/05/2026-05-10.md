title: Heir Of Salvation
scripture: Hebrews 1:14
verse: Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?
body:
  - Did you know that you are not just saved but you are an heir? Salvation is not only about being rescued; it is about being brought into an inheritance. Through Christ, you have been given a place in God's family, with full rights, privileges, and access to everything that belongs to Him.
  - An heir doesn't beg for what already belongs to them; they walk in it. You have inherited righteousness, peace, joy, authority, and victory. This means you are not trying to earn God's blessings; you are living from them. Your life is meant to reflect the abundance and grace that comes from being a child of God.
  - 'The Bible tells us that even angels are sent to minister for you. Think about that: heaven is actively working on your behalf. You are backed by divine resources, and positioned for a life of purpose and impact.'
  - Sometimes, the enemy may try to make you feel unworthy or disconnected from God's promises. But your inheritance is not based on your feelings; it is secured in Christ. You are accepted, chosen, and fully qualified to partake in all that God has prepared for you.
  - Walk with the consciousness of who you are. You are not ordinary; you are royalty. You carry divine authority. You have access to heaven's supply. Refuse to live small when you've been called into something so great. Let the Word of God continually remind you of your identity. As you grow in this knowledge, you will live boldly, pray confidently, and manifest the realities of your inheritance every day.
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for making me an heir of salvation. I declare that I walk in the fullness of my inheritance in Christ. I am not lacking, I am not limited; I am richly supplied, divinely empowered, and fully equipped for every good work. I live in victory, authority, and joy, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
