title: all you need is in christ
scripture: Colossians 2:10 (KJV)
verse: And ye are complete in him, which is the head of all principality and power.
body:
  - 'There is a powerful truth every believer must come to understand: everything you need for life and Godliness is already found in Christ. You are not incomplete, lacking, or trying to become whole; you are already complete in Him.'
  - The world often tells you that you need more (more resources, more recognition, more validation) to feel fulfilled. But God's Word reveals something different. Your completeness is not based on what you have externally, but on who you are connected to internally.
  - 'The Bible says, “According as his divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and Godliness…” (2 Peter 1:3 (KJV). Note that it says “has given”, not “will give”. This means God has already made provision for everything you need: wisdom, strength, peace, direction, and victory. Sometimes, the challenge is not availability but awareness. When you don''t realize what you have, you may live as though you lack. But as you grow in the knowledge of Christ, your eyes begin to open to the riches already within you.'
  - Christ in you is your source. That means you don't have to run from place to place searching for answers; you can look inward, guided by the Holy Spirit, and find direction. His presence in you is your assurance that you are never without help. This doesn't mean you won't grow or increase; it means you grow from a place of fullness, not emptiness. You move forward with confidence, knowing you are already equipped.
  - So, stop seeing yourself as lacking. Stop speaking insufficiency. Begin to align your thoughts and words with what God has said about you. You are complete in Him. All you need is in Christ. Praise the Lord!
prayer: Dear Father, I thank You for making me complete in Christ. I declare that I lack nothing because everything I need has been made available to me in Christ. I walk in wisdom, strength, and confidence, knowing that Your power is at work in me. I live from a place of fullness and victory every day, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
