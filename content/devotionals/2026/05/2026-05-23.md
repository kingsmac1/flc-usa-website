title: The Reality Of Christ
scripture: Colossians 2:9–10
verse: For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily. And ye are complete in him, which is the head of all principality and power
body:
  - 'Many Christians still struggle with a sense of lack in their spiritual lives because they haven''t fully understood what it means to be complete in Christ. As a result, they look outward for things to make them feel closer to God: special experiences, physical tokens, or human validations. But the truth is that everything you need for life and godliness has already been made available to you in Christ.'
  - When you were born again, you didn't receive a partial salvation or a limited portion of God's presence. You received Christ Himself. The fullness of God now dwells in you by the Holy Spirit. This means your relationship with God is not dependent on external things but on the living reality of Christ within you.
  - 'Some believers unknowingly fall into the trap of thinking they must add something to their faith to make it effective: certain rituals, repeated formulas, or symbolic acts. But the Gospel reveals something far greater: Christ has already accomplished everything necessary for your salvation and victory. Your role is to believe, receive, and walk in that truth.'
  - The Christian life is therefore not about striving to obtain what God has already given. It is about growing in the knowledge of who you are in Christ and expressing that life daily. As you stay on God's Word, your confidence grows, and your faith becomes stronger because it is built on the finished work of Christ.
  - You don't need to search elsewhere for power, approval, or spiritual completeness. Christ is your wisdom, your righteousness, your strength, and your peace. In Him, you have everything. Therefore, live with this consciousness every day. Refuse the lie that you are lacking or incomplete. Walk boldly in the reality that Christ lives in you, and His life in you is more than enough.
prayer: Dear Father, thank you for making me complete in Christ. I walk daily in the consciousness of your life within me, knowing that I lack nothing because Christ is my sufficiency. My faith rests firmly on your Word, and I grow continually in the knowledge of who I am in Christ. Amen.
