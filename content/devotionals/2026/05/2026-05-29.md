title: Preparation Through The Word
scripture: Revelation 19:7 (KJV)
verse: …for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready.
body:
  - 'God is always ready, but the question is, are we? Scripture shows something very striking: the bride made herself ready. That means preparation is not something we wait for; it''s something we participate in. And one of the greatest tools God has given us for this preparation is His Word.'
  - Preparation through the Word is not a religious routine; it is a living experience. The Word of God has the power to shape you from within. It doesn't just tell you what is right; it makes you right as you receive and respond to it.
  - Many times, people are expecting sudden change - one moment everything is different, everything is perfect. But real spiritual preparation doesn't usually happen that way. It is built daily. It happens as you stay with the Word, meditate on it, and allow it to correct and guide you.
  - 'The Word works like a mirror. When you look into it, you begin to see yourself the way God sees you. You begin to notice attitudes, thoughts, and patterns that need to change. But it doesn''t stop at revealing; it also empowers you to become what it says. That''s the beauty of God''s Word: it doesn''t just expose; it also transforms.'
  - There is also a responsibility on your part. God has made His Word available, but you must give yourself to it. You must choose to sit with it, meditate on it, and act on it. Transformation happens when the Word moves from being something you read to something you live. As you consistently stay in the Word, something begins to happen within you. Your desires begin to align with God's will. Your thinking becomes clearer. Your spirit becomes more sensitive. You begin to reflect Christ naturally, not by struggle anymore, but by growth.
  - Preparation is not about trying to be perfect overnight. It is about daily alignment. Every time you respond to the Word, you are becoming ready. Every time you obey, you are being refined. Over time, the Word quietly but powerfully prepares you for what God has ahead.
prayer: The Word of God is working in me. It is cleansing, shaping, and perfecting my life. I walk in alignment with God's will, and I am being prepared daily for His purpose. My heart is open, my spirit is responsive, and my life reflects Christ.
