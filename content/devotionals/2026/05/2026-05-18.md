date: '2026-05-18'
title: Show yourself approved
scripture: 2 Timothy 2:15
verse: Study to show yourself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
body:
  - Growth in your walk with God doesn't happen by chance; it happens by intention. God calls you to study, not just to know more, but to become more. Studying the Word is how you build a strong, confident, and unshakable faith.
  - To be “approved” means to be tested and proven. It's not about impressing people; it's about being grounded in truth before God. When you take time to study the Scriptures, you are allowing God to shape your thinking, to refine your understanding and, at the same time, strengthen your spiritual foundation.
  - Many believers rely only on what they hear from others, but God desires a personal relationship with you through His Word. When you study, you begin to see things for yourself. You gain understanding. You grow in discernment. You become confident in what you believe and why you believe it.
  - The Bible says, “All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness” (2 Timothy 3:16). That means the Word trains you. It corrects you, guides you, and equips you to
  - live right.
  - Studying requires discipline, but it produces strength. It may not always feel easy to sit down, focus, and dig deeper, but the reward is worth it. Over time, the Word begins to dwell richly in you. It shapes your responses, influences your decisions, and gives you confidence in every situation. When challenges come, you won't be shaken because you are rooted. When questions arise, you won't be confused because you understand the truth. You become a workman who is prepared, equipped, and ready.
  - Don't settle for surface-level understanding. Go deeper. Take time to study, reflect, and grow. You are being built into someone who can stand firm, speak truth, and live with confidence. Show yourself approved, not by striving, but by staying committed to the Word.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for Your Word that teaches and transforms me. I commit to studying and growing in truth. Open my understanding and help me to rightly divide Your Word. I declare that I am grounded, confident, and approved in You. I walk in wisdom and live according to Your word, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
