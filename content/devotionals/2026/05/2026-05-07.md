title: God Is With You
scripture: Matthew 1:23
verse: Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Emmanuel… God is with us.
body:
  - 'There''s a powerful confidence that comes from the simple realization: God is with you. This isn''t just a comforting statement; it is a divine reality that changes how you see everything around you.'
  - 'From the prophetic words in Isaiah to their fulfillment in Jesus Christ, the message has always been clear: God desires to be present with His people. The name Immanuel reveals this truth in its fullness. God didn''t remain distant; He came near. In Jesus, we see the living expression of God''s presence among men.'
  - However, this truth is even more personal today. Through the Holy Spirit, God's presence is not just around you; it is within you. This means wherever you go, God goes. In every decision, every challenge, and every moment, you are accompanied by divine wisdom, strength, and power.
  - Understanding this changes your response to life. When others see obstacles, you see opportunities, because you know you're not alone. When pressure arises, you stand firm, aware that the greater One lives in you. This is the confidence behind the
  - 'words in Romans 8:31: “if God is for us, who can be against us”?'
  - God's presence is your greatest advantage. It steadies your heart in uncertain times and gives you courage in the face of adversity. You don't have to rely solely on your own strength or understanding; you have divine help at work in you and through you.
  - 'Refuse to live as though you are alone or unsupported. Build a daily awareness of His presence. Talk to Him, trust Him, and lean on Him. The more conscious you are of God with you, the more boldly and confidently you will live. No matter what lies ahead, hold on to this truth: God is with you: now and always. That assurance is your strength, your peace, and your unshakable victory.'
prayer: Dear Father, I thank you for your abiding presence in my life. I am never alone, for you are with me and in me. I walk in confidence, strength, and boldness, knowing that you are my help and my advantage. No challenge can overcome me, because you are in me and for me, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
