title: The Deep Things Of God
scripture: Proverbs 25:2
verse: 'It is the glory of God to conceal a thing: but the honour of kings is to search out a matter'
body:
  - God does not dwell in the shallow; He dwells in the deep. His wisdom, plans, and mysteries are hidden from casual observers and revealed to those who truly seek Him. To encounter the fullness of God, you must enter the secret place, spend time in His presence, and meditate on His Word. When you do, you begin to see His glory and understand His ways in ways that are hidden from others.
  - “The honour of kings is to search out a matter.” Those who take the time to explore God's heart and discover His truths gain honor, not for themselves, but because they uncover revelations that others do not yet know. Seeking God's depth brings insight, discernment, and spiritual strength that shape your life and equip you for His purposes.
  - 'Have you searched the heart of God? Have you sought His wisdom and guidance beyond what is visible? Those who fear the Lord and pursue Him wholeheartedly receive a special reward: “The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant” (Psalm 25:14). God entrusts His hidden truths to those who honor Him, and He reveals His plans to those who seek Him with reverence.'
  - 'Exploring the deep things of God requires your patience, prayer, and a listening heart. It is in these moments of communion that you discover His wisdom, strength, and direction. The rewards are eternal: understanding for life, and the joy of seeing God''s glory unfold. Those who search God''s depths walk with confidence, power, and a Spirit-filled perspective that sets them apart.'
prayer: Father, I will seek the deep things of Your Spirit. Keep drawing me into the secret place and revealing Your wisdom, and showing me Your plans. I recognize your guidance in every step. I treasure your Word, I walk in understanding and insight, and my life reflects your glory, purpose, and divine revelation in Jesus' name. Amen!
