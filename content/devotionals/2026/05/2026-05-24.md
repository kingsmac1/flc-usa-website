date: '2026-05-24'
title: The Secret Place
scripture: Psalm 31:20
verse: 'Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: Thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues'
body:
  - 'There is a place in God where you are hidden, secure, and at peace: a place the world cannot access or disturb. The Bible calls this the secret place. It is not a physical location, but a spiritual reality: the place of intimate fellowship with God, where His presence surrounds you, speaks to you, and preserves you. Many desire protection, peace, and clarity, yet few learn to dwell in this sacred place of communion with Him.'
  - The secret place is the realm of God's presence where you are separated from fear, confusion, and the pressures of life. It is where your identity is affirmed, your spirit is strengthened, and your heart is aligned with God's will. To be hidden in His presence means to be covered, shielded, and kept by His power. The world may be filled with noise, opinions, and conflict, but in the secret place, there is stillness, assurance, and divine direction.
  - Dwelling in the secret place is not something you visit occasionally; it is a lifestyle you cultivate daily. You dwell there
  - through prayer, worship, meditation on God's Word, and quietness before Him. It requires you to step away from distractions and consciously focus your heart on God. As you consistently spend time with Him, your awareness of His presence grows, and your spirit becomes more sensitive to His voice and guidance.
  - The benefits of the secret place are profound. In His presence, you find protection from the “pride of man” and the “strife of tongues”, meaning you are preserved from harm, criticism, and unnecessary conflict. You receive peace that guards your heart, wisdom for decision making, and strength to face challenges. God refreshes your soul, renews your mind, and equips you to walk confidently in His purpose.
  - When you dwell in the secret place, you are not easily shaken by circumstances. You carry a calm assurance that God is with you, guiding and defending you. It becomes your refuge, your covering, and your place of divine encounter.
prayer: I dwell in the secret place of the Most High, and I remain close to God's presence daily. The LORD draws me near, covers me with His peace, and strengthens me from within. I am hidden in Him, protected from harm, and guided in every step I take. My life is established in His presence, and I walk confidently in His love, His power, and His divine protection in Jesus name, amen.
