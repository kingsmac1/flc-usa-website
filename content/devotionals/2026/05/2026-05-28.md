title: You Are Wonderfully Made
scripture: Psalm 139:14
verse: 'I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well'
body:
  - It is easy to look at yourself through the lens of comparison, doubt, or insecurity. You may sometimes feel inadequate, overlooked, or not enough. But God's Word tells a completely different story about you. You are not an accident, a mistake, or a random creation; you are fearfully and wonderfully made. That means you were crafted with intention, precision, and divine purpose.
  - To be “fearfully made” means you were created with reverence, care, and divine attention. God did not rush your creation; He designed every part of you with purpose. To be “wonderfully made” means you are unique, set apart, and extraordinary in His sight. There is no one else exactly like you, and there never will be. Your identity is rooted not in what others say, but in what God has already declared about you.
  - When you begin to understand this truth, your perspective changes. You stop striving for validation from people and start resting in God's approval. You no longer measure your worth by appearance, achievements, or comparison, but by the fact that the Creator of the universe formed you intentionally. This brings confidence, peace, and a deep sense of purpose. continue to doubt your value? Your soul must come to the place where it “knows right well” that God's works are marvelous, and that includes you. This truth transforms how you think, speak, and live. You begin to walk with boldness, knowing you carry divine value and purpose.
  - You are not here by chance. You are designed for a reason, equipped for a purpose, and loved beyond measure. When you embrace this, you step into the confidence and identity God has always intended for you.
prayer: Father, I thank You that I am fearfully and wonderfully made. I am created by God with divine purpose and value. I see myself through His truth, and every doubt, insecurity, and false belief is removed by His Spirit. I walk in confidence, knowing who I am, and I move boldly in faith, secure in my identity, and aligned with His perfect will for my life in Jesus' Name. Amen!
