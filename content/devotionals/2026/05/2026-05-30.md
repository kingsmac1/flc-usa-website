title: Be Transformed By The Word
scripture: Romans 12:2 (KJV)
verse: 'And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind…'
body:
  - God has called you to a life that reflects Him, but that life is revealed and sustained through the renewal of your mind by His Word. One of the greatest battles in a believer's life is not external; it is internal. It is the battle between old patterns of thinking and the truth of God's Word. Even after receiving salvation, the mind must be trained, and renewed continually.
  - This is where the Word of God becomes essential. The Word doesn't just inform you; it re-programs you. For instance, as you hear, study and meditate on God's word, It replaces fears with faith; it removes confusion and gives you clarity, and every weakness in your life receives strength. As you give attention to the Word, your thinking begins to change, and when your thinking changes, your life follows.
  - Many people want different results, but they hold on to the same mindset. True transformation begins when you allow the Word to challenge what you have always believed and reshape your perspective. Sometimes, the Word will confront you. Sometimes it will correct you. But every time, it is working for your growth.
  - Renewing your mind is a daily commitment. It is not something you do once and move on from. It is a lifestyle. Each day, you choose what influences your thoughts; whether it is the noise of the world or the truth of God's Word. The more you dwell on the Word, the more natural righteousness becomes to you.
  - As your mind is renewed, your responses begin to change. Situations that once overwhelmed you no longer have the same effect. You begin to think like Christ, speak with wisdom, and act with understanding.
  - God is not trying to fix you from the outside; He is changing you from the inside. And the Word is His tool for doing it. Stay with the Word. Let it saturate your heart. Let it guide your decisions. Over time, you will see undeniable growth and stability in your life.
prayer: My mind is renewed by the Word of God. I think in alignment with God's truth, and my life reflects His will. I am not conformed to this world, but I am transformed daily by the power of the Word working in me.
furtherStudies: Colossians 3:16; Joshua 1:8
