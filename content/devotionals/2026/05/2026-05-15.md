title: Practice Being Filled With The Spirit
scripture: Ephesians 5:18
verse: And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit
body:
  - 'Many Christians wonder, “How can I experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit every day?” The answer is simple, yet profound: practice being filled with the Spirit. This is not a one-time event or a distant ideal; it is a daily, intentional choice to surrender your thoughts, words, and actions to God''s guidance. Being filled with the Spirit transforms your inner life, empowers your faith, and enables you to live boldly, joyfully, and victoriously in every circumstance.'
  - 'The early disciples in the book of Acts exemplified this reality: ordinary people, when filled with the Spirit, spoke boldly, prayed fervently, and accomplished extraordinary works for God. Their strength and confidence came not from themselves, but from yielding fully to the Spirit''s power and presence.'
  - The Bible says in 1 Corinthians 14:4, “He that speaketh in an unknown tongue edifieth himself; but he that prophesieth edifieth the church.” Here, we learn that spiritual practices (praying in tongues, worship, and thanksgiving) build up your spirit and align you with God's purposes. The more you intentionally engage with the Spirit, the more your heart and mind are stirred, and the bolder, wiser, and more Spirit-led you become. Being filled with the Spirit is practical and experiential;
  - it shapes your thoughts, words, and deeds to reflect God's power and presence.
  - Think of Peter. Before Pentecost, he was fearful, and uncertain. But when he was filled with the Spirit, he boldly preached to thousands, healed the sick, and proclaimed God's word without hesitation. The Spirit transformed his inner man and empowered him to fulfill his God- given calling.
  - 'The same Holy Spirit is available to you today. Ephesians 5:19–21 shows us how to practice being filled: speak in psalms and hymns, sing spiritual songs, give thanks in every situation, and submit to one another in reverence to God. As you cultivate these habits daily, boldness, joy, and the power of the Holy Spirit will naturally flow from within you.'
prayer: Dear Lord, thank you for the gift of the Holy Spirit. I affirm I am filled with Your Spirit today and every day. I am full of boldness, wisdom, and joy. Your Spirit guides and empowers me to live a life that glorifies You. I surrender fully to Your control and yield to Your spirit, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
