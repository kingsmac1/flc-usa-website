title: The Believer’s Authority In Christ
scripture: Luke 10:19
verse: 'Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.'
body:
  - 'Sometimes, life can make you feel small: overwhelmed by challenges, intimidated by opposition, or unsure of what to do next. But Jesus didn''t design you to live feeling powerless. He made it clear: authority has already been given to you.'
  - Notice, He said, “I give unto you power.” That means it's a gift, not something you struggle to earn. As a believer, you carry delegated authority through your relationship with Christ. You are not trying to gain victory; you are standing in the victory He already secured.
  - 'The word translated “power” also means authority (the legal right to act). Think of it this way: a uniformed officer can direct traffic not because of personal strength, but because authority backs him. In the same way, when you speak in faith, God backs you.'
  - 'This doesn''t mean challenges won''t come. It means they don''t have the final say. When pressure and fear rise, you can stand firm and respond with faith and the authority you have in Christ. Your confidence isn''t in yourself; it''s in who you are in Christ. That''s why James 4:7 says, “Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you”. Notice your part: stay aligned with God and refuse to give the enemy ground. When you do, he has no choice but to retreat. You are not helpless and You are not defenseless. You are a believer walking in Christ''s authority. Let that truth shape how you pray, how you speak, and how you face each day.'
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for the authority I have in Christ. When challenges arise, I will not shrink back in fear. I stand firm in the victory Jesus has given me. I speak with faith and I act with confidence, knowing You are with me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
