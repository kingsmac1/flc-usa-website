title: God’s Design For Marriage
scripture: Mark 10:9
verse: What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.
body:
  - When the Pharisees questioned Jesus about divorce, they hoped to draw Him into a controversy. Jesus refused to argue on their terms. He turned their attention to the beginning, reminding them of God's design for marriage.
  - Quoting Genesis 2:24, Jesus said, “They twain shall be one flesh.” Marriage is far more than a legal agreement or a social institution. It is a covenant in which a husband and wife are joined together by God. Two lives become one, sharing the same journey, the same commitment, and the same purpose.
  - If you are married, remember that your relationship is precious in God's sight. Every marriage will experience seasons of joy and seasons of testing. During those difficult moments, resist the temptation to see your spouse as the enemy. Work through challenges together. Choose forgiveness over resentment, and seek God's wisdom before making decisions that could affect your future.
  - If you are preparing for marriage, build your relationship on God's Word before you build your home together. Character, faith, and commitment will carry a marriage much farther than feelings alone. A strong foundation is laid long before the wedding day. Jesus explained that Moses permitted divorce because of the hardness of people's hearts (Mark 10:5). That permission addressed the reality of human sin; it did not lower God's standard for marriage. Christ lifted their eyes to something higher. He showed them a relationship marked by faithfulness rather than convenience.
  - The world often treats marriage as a contract that can be ended whenever it becomes difficult. Christ presents it as a covenant that deserves to be honored and protected. Some marriages have experienced deep pain because of unfaithfulness, abuse, abandonment, or other painful circumstances. Scripture speaks to these situations with both truth and compassion (Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:15).
  - If that is part of your story, remember that God's grace is greater than your past. He restores broken hearts and gives hope for the future. Whether you are single or married, let your view of marriage be shaped by God's Word. His wisdom brings strength to every relationship that is placed in His hands.
prayer: Lord, thank You for Your wisdom and for the gift of marriage. Thank You because Your love teaches me how to honor the relationships You have entrusted to me. My heart will reflect Your faithfulness, and my life will be guided by Your Word. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
