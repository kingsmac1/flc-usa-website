title: What Is The Quality Of Your Leadership?
scripture: 2 Samuel 23:8
verse: These be the names of the mighty men whom David had...
body:
  - David's mighty men are remembered for their courage and remarkable victories, but their story also says something about David. Great leaders do more than accomplish great things. They help other people become all that God has called them to be.
  - When these men first came to David, they were in distress, in debt, and deeply discouraged (1 Samuel 22:2). They were not celebrated warriors. They were ordinary men looking for hope and direction. David accepted them where they were and led them toward something greater. Over time, they became men of courage whose names were recorded in Scripture.
  - Your leadership should have the same effect on the people around you. Whether you lead your family, a ministry, a business, or a team at work, your influence should help others grow. People should leave your presence with greater confidence in God and a clearer sense of who they can become.
  - David also won the hearts of his men. They trusted him because they knew he cared for them. They followed him through seasons of hardship because his life gave them confidence. Leadership is built long before a title is given. It grows through integrity, humility, and a genuine concern for people. Consider the people God has placed in your care. Are you helping them grow? Are you encouraging them when they struggle? Are you creating opportunities for them to develop the gifts God has placed within them? One day, your greatest achievement may not be what you accomplished personally. It may be the lives that were strengthened because God allowed them to walk with you.
  - Lead in a way that leaves people stronger, wiser, and closer to Christ than when they first met you.
prayer: Lord, thank You for every person You have placed in my life. Thank You for the privilege of influencing others. My leadership will encourage people to grow in their walk with You and to become all that You have created them to be. Let my life be an example that brings honor to You and points others to Christ. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
