title: The Unsearchable Riches In Christ
scripture: Ephesians 3:8
verse: Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ.
body:
  - When you gave your life to Christ, God did far more than forgive your sins. He brought you into a relationship with His Son and gave you an inheritance that can never be exhausted. Paul called it “the unsearchable riches of Christ”.
  - The word unsearchable means beyond human discovery. It describes something so vast and so deep that no one can fully measure it. No matter how long you walk with Christ, there will always be more of His grace, His wisdom, and His love to discover.
  - You can spend so much time asking God for what you think you lack that you overlook what He has already placed within your reach through Christ. Scripture says you have been accepted in the Beloved (Ephesians 1:6). Your sins have been forgiven through His blood (Ephesians 1:7). You have become a child of God (John 1:12), and the Holy Spirit now lives within you (1 Corinthians 6:19). These are not promises you are waiting to receive. They are blessings you already possess because you belong to Christ.
  - Think of someone who inherits a great fortune but never opens the documents that explain what belongs to them. They continue living in lack, not because they have nothing, but because they have never discovered what is theirs. In the same way, you grow into the riches of Christ as you spend time in God's Word. Every time you open the Scriptures, God reveals more of who Christ is and what He has made available to you.
  - Paul prayed that believers would receive “the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him” (Ephesians 1:17). He wanted us to see beyond our circumstances and understand the greatness of what God had already done through Christ.
  - Do not settle for a shallow knowledge of Jesus. Continue to seek Him. Continue to learn from His Word. Continue to walk with Him. The more you know Christ, the more you will appreciate the riches that are already yours. You will never come to the end of His grace. You will never exhaust His love. You will never outgrow your need to know Him more.
prayer: Father, thank You for the riches I have received through Christ. Thank You for opening my eyes to the inheritance that is already mine. Your Word continues to reveal the greatness of Your grace and the depth of Your love. I will keep growing in the knowledge of Christ, and my life will reflect the blessings You have freely given me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
