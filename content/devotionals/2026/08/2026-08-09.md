title: Extinguish Haste From Your Life
scripture: Isaiah 28:16
verse: He that believeth shall not make haste.
body:
  - Haste is more dangerous than many people realize. Haste means acting too quickly without patience, wisdom, or spiritual direction. It is movement driven by pressure, fear, emotion, or desperation instead of peace and discernment. Many painful mistakes begin when people become impatient and move ahead of God.
  - 'The flesh naturally struggles with waiting. When answers seem delayed, the heart can become restless. Thoughts begin racing. Emotions become unsettled. But Scripture says, “Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart” (Psalm 27:14). Waiting develops trust in God and protects you from decisions made through pressure.'
  - Abraham experienced this personally. God promised him a son, but when the promise seemed delayed, impatience entered the situation. Instead of waiting fully on God, Abraham and Sarah tried to help fulfill the promise through human effort, and Ishmael was born. What began through haste later produced sorrow and conflict. Yet God had already spoken clearly that His covenant would come through Isaac.
  - Saul also suffered greatly because of impatience. In 1 Samuel 13, God instructed Saul to wait for Samuel, but pressure from
  - the people made him restless. Instead of remaining obedient, Saul acted impulsively and stepped outside God's instruction. One rushed decision affected his future deeply.
  - This is why haste must be extinguished from your spirit. Not every open door is from God, and not every delay means God has forgotten you. Ecclesiastes 3:11 says, “He hath made every thing beautiful in his time.” God understands timing better than you do. Many times, He is preparing your heart before releasing what you have prayed for.
  - The enemy often works through urgency because hurried emotions weaken discernment. But when your heart remains quiet before God, clarity becomes stronger. Psalm 46:10 says, “Be still, and know that I am God.” Stillness helps you hear God clearly.
  - Do not let pressure push you ahead of God's timing. Stay prayerful. Stay sensitive to His voice. Stay yielded to His process. Because what God builds through peace will never need haste to survive.
prayer: I declare that haste, impatience, and emotional pressure will not control my life. My heart remains steady before God. I will not force doors open through fear or desperation. The Holy Spirit is teaching me patience, discernment, and obedience. I walk in peace, clarity, and confidence as God directs my steps. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
