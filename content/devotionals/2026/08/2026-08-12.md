title: Lessons From Ezra
scripture: Ezra 7:10
verse: For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the Lord, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and judgments.
body:
  - If you desire to live a life that pleases God, Ezra provides a pattern worth following. His influence did not begin with his position as a scribe. It began with the condition of his heart. Before God used him publicly, Ezra devoted himself to God privately.
  - Notice the order found in Ezra 7:10. Ezra first sought the Word of God. He wanted to know God's heart before he attempted to lead others. You cannot grow spiritually if your time in God's Word is occasional. Make it your daily pursuit to know Him through the Scriptures.
  - Ezra then lived out what he learned. He did not treat God's Word as knowledge to be admired. He allowed it to shape his decisions, attitudes, and conduct. As you read the Bible, ask yourself, How can I obey what God is showing me today? The goal of Scripture is not simply to increase your knowledge but to transform your life. Only after seeking God's Word and obeying it did Ezra teach others. This is a reminder that your life is one of your greatest sermons. People are often impacted more by what they see in you than by what they hear from you.
  - Ezra also faced challenges, yet he remained confident because he knew God was with him. He testified, “The hand of the Lord my God was upon me” (Ezra 7:28). The same God who strengthened Ezra will strengthen you as you walk in obedience. You do not have to depend on your own ability. God's grace is sufficient for every assignment He gives you.
  - Let Ezra's example encourage you today. Seek God's Word diligently. Live according to its truth. Then, allow your life to point others to Christ. When your heart is prepared, God will prepare the way before you.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your Word that continues to shape my life. Thank You because Your Spirit teaches me and gives me understanding each day. I will seek Your Word faithfully and walk in obedience to what You reveal. Thank You because Your hand is upon my life, and You will use me to reflect Christ wherever You place me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
