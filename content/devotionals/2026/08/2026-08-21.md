title: The Currency Of Faith
scripture: Hebrews 11:6
verse: But without faith it is impossible to please him...
body:
  - In everyday life, a currency is the means by which you obtain something of value. You cannot walk into a store, admire what you need, and expect to leave with it without the proper currency. In God's kingdom, faith is the means by which you lay hold of what He has made available through Christ.
  - Faith is not a feeling. It is not wishful thinking, and it is not blind optimism. Faith is responding to God because you are convinced that His Word is true. It moves you to act with confidence because you know the One who has spoken cannot fail.
  - Throughout Scripture, people received from God because they believed Him. Noah built the ark before there was any sign of rain. Abraham left his homeland without knowing where God would lead him. The woman with the issue of blood pressed through the crowd because she believed that one touch of Jesus' garment would change her life. Their faith was expressed through obedience.
  - Your circumstances may not always agree with what God has spoken. Keep believing Him. Continue to pray. Continue to
  - obey. Faith grows stronger every time you choose God's Word above what your eyes can see. Jesus often said, “Thy faith hath made thee whole” (Mark 5:34). He was showing that faith opens the heart to receive what God is ready to give. God responds to those who trust Him.
  - Do not allow fear or doubt to replace your confidence in God. Fill your heart with His Word until faith becomes your response to every situation. The more you know Him, the easier it becomes to trust Him. Walk by faith today. Every step of obedience declares that God is faithful, and every act of faith brings glory to His name.
prayer: Lord, thank You for giving me Your Word, which fills my heart with faith. I choose to believe You in every circumstance. My confidence rests in who You are, and I will continue to obey Your voice. Thank You because my faith grows stronger as I walk with You, and my life will reflect Your goodness and power. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
