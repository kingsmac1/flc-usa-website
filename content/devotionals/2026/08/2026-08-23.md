title: The Importance Of Godly Counsel
scripture: Proverbs 12:15
verse: 'The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise.'
body:
  - Godly counsel is wise, biblical direction that helps you see your situation from God's perspective. It comes from people whose lives are submitted to Christ, whose hearts are shaped by His Word, and whose desire is to help you walk in His will rather than simply tell you what you want to hear.
  - Sometimes, you might need counsel to navigate certain season of life. There will be decisions that require more than your own understanding. There might be moments when emotions could cloud your judgment or circumstances make it difficult to see clearly. During those times, God often brings wise people into your life to provide direction, encouragement, and correction.
  - 'Scripture repeatedly highlights the value of seeking counsel. “Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety” (Proverbs 11:14). Proverbs 15:22 says, “Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established”. Seeking advice is not a sign of weakness. It is evidence of humility and a desire to make decisions that honor God.'
  - The value of counsel depends on its source. Not every opinion is worth following. Choose people whose lives reflect Christ. Listen to those who love God's Word, walk in holiness, and are willing to speak honestly, even when the truth is difficult to hear. Advice that contradicts Scripture will never lead you in the right direction.
  - Moses experienced the blessing of godly counsel when his father-in-law, Jethro, saw him carrying more than he could bear. Jethro advised him to appoint capable leaders to share the responsibility, and Moses wisely followed that counsel (Exodus 18:17–24). The burden became lighter, and the people were served more effectively.
  - David also experienced the blessing of godly counsel, although it came through correction. After his sin with Bathsheba, the prophet Nathan confronted him with courage and compassion (2 Samuel 12:1–13). David humbled himself, repented, and found restoration. A true counselor does not simply make you feel comfortable. They help you remain close to God.
  - Be willing to receive counsel with an open heart. Pride resists correction, but humility welcomes it. One conversation with a godly counselor can keep you from years of unnecessary pain and lead you toward God's best for your life.
prayer: Lord, thank You for placing wise people in my life. Thank You for giving me a heart that is willing to learn and grow. I will receive correction with humility and seek counsel that agrees with Your Word. In Jesus’ Name. Amen!
