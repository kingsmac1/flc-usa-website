title: Christ Is Your Safety
scripture: Proverbs 18:10
verse: 'The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.'
body:
  - In a world filled with uncertainty, fear, danger, and instability, many people are searching for security. Some place their trust in money, influence, connections, or human systems, hoping those things will protect them. But earthly security can fail. Only God remains completely dependable.
  - The word safety speaks of protection, preservation, refuge, and security from harm. Scripture continually reveals God as a place of refuge for His people. In Christ, believers find safety for the soul as well as confidence during difficult seasons.
  - Proverbs 18:10 describes the name of the Lord as a strong tower. In ancient times, a strong tower was a place of protection where people fled during danger or attack. It was built to withstand pressure and keep those inside secure. Scripture uses this picture to show the safety found in God.
  - 'Jesus never promised believers a life without challenges, but He continually assured them of His presence through every storm. In John 16:33, He said, “In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.” Christ remains steady even when life feels uncertain.'
  - Many people live constantly overwhelmed by fear because their confidence is rooted mainly in temporary things. Fear grows wherever trust in God weakens. But when the heart becomes established in Christ, peace begins to replace anxiety.
  - Psalm 46:1 says, “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.” Notice Scripture says present help. God is not distant during hardship. He remains near to His people. Christ is also the safety of the soul. Sin destroys, deceives, and separates people from God, but salvation through Christ brings reconciliation and eternal security. In Him, believers are forgiven, preserved, and brought into relationship with the Father.
  - Trusting Christ does not mean living carelessly or ignoring wisdom. It means living with confidence that no matter what happens, God remains faithful and His hand remains upon those who belong to Him. A heart resting in Christ finds peace even during uncertain times because true safety is found in Him.
prayer: Father, thank You because Christ remains my refuge and safety in every season. Thank You because Your presence surrounds and preserves my life daily. Fear, anxiety, and uncertainty will not rule my heart because my confidence is established in You. Thank You for the peace and security found in Christ alone. My heart remains steady because You are faithful through every circumstance. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Proverbs 18:10; Psalm 46:1; John 16:33
