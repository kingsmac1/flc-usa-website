title: Create Time For The Lord Of The Work
scripture: Luke 10:42
verse: 'But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.'
body:
  - Serving God should never replace spending time with God. Martha welcomed Jesus into her home and devoted herself to serving Him. Mary sat at His feet and listened to His teaching (Luke 10:38–42). Martha was not doing something sinful. She was serving the Lord. The problem was that her service had become more important than her fellowship with Him.
  - The same thing can happen to you. You may love the Lord sincerely and be committed to His work. You may be present at every church service. You may faithfully participate in evangelism. You may volunteer whenever help is needed. Your calendar may be full of activities that honor God, yet your personal time with Him begins to disappear.
  - This is a danger for every believer, especially those in ministry. A pastor can become occupied with preparing messages, visiting members, leading meetings, and caring for the church. None of these responsibilities are wrong, but they should never replace personal devotion. The Lord who called you deserves your attention before the work He has entrusted to you.
  - This change is often gradual. A busy schedule begins to dictate your day. Prayer is shortened because there is something else to do. The Bible remains closed because another responsibility demands attention. Days pass, then weeks, and fellowship with God becomes occasional instead of consistent.
  - This is one of the enemy's quiet strategies. If he cannot draw you away from serving Christ, he will try to keep you so occupied that you neglect your relationship with Christ. When fellowship becomes shallow, spiritual strength begins to fade. Afterwards, the person feels drained.
  - Remember the early days of your walk with God. You looked forward to being in His presence. You opened the Scriptures because you wanted to hear His voice. Prayer was a delight because you enjoyed being with Him. Do not lose that desire. Your ministry, your career, and every responsibility in your life should flow from your relationship with Christ. If your work leaves no room for fellowship with Him, it is time to reorder your priorities. The Lord of the work must always remain more important than the work of the Lord.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the privilege of walking with You. My relationship with You will remain the highest priority in my life. My heart will stay on You, and Your Word will continue to shape my daily walk. Thank you for strengthening me as I fulfill every assignment You have entrusted to me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
