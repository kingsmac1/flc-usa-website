title: These Signs Shall Follow You
scripture: Mark 16:17
verse: And these signs shall follow them that believe...
body:
  - When Jesus spoke these words, He was describing what a life surrendered to Him would look like. He did not say signs would follow the gifted, the famous, or the spiritually elite. He simply said, “them that believe.” The supernatural life begins with faith in Christ.
  - Notice that Jesus did not tell believers to chase signs. He said signs would follow them. The focus has never been on miracles themselves but on the Lord who works through His people. Whenever God moves with power, His desire is that people would see Christ and believe the gospel.
  - This was the pattern throughout the book of Acts. As the disciples preached, God confirmed His Word with signs following. The sick were healed, the oppressed were set free, and lives were transformed. These miracles were never meant to glorify men. They revealed that Jesus was alive and still working through those who belonged to Him.
  - The same Lord who empowered the early church has not changed. The Holy Spirit still lives within every believer, giving strength, boldness, wisdom, and grace to fulfill God's purpose. Your confidence should never rest in your own ability but in Christ who lives in you. This also means you must never become intimidated by darkness. Jesus said, “In my name shall they cast out devils.” The authority belongs to Him, and He has shared that authority with you. You do not stand before the enemy as someone trying to gain victory. You stand as someone who is already walking in the victory Christ secured through His death and resurrection.
  - As you continue walking with the Lord, expect your life to reflect His presence. Sometimes, He will work through your words. Sometimes, through your prayers. Sometimes through acts of compassion that open hearts to the gospel. However He chooses to work, remember that your greatest assignment is to reveal Christ.
  - The greatest miracle is still a life transformed by Jesus. When Christ is seen in you, the world witnesses the reality of His kingdom.
prayer: I declare that Christ lives in me, and His power is at work through my life. I will boldly proclaim the gospel without fear or intimidation. The Holy Spirit is strengthening me to represent Jesus faithfully wherever I go. My life will reveal your love and your power. As I obey the Lord, He will confirm His Word and glorify His name through me. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
