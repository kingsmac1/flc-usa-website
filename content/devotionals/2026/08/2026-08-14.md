title: Pride Is Dangerous
scripture: Proverbs 16:18
verse: Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.
body:
  - If God begins to enlarge your influence, guard your heart as carefully as you guard your calling. Success is a blessing, but it also reveals where your confidence truly rests.
  - Pride rarely appears all at once. It grows quietly when you begin to admire what God has done through you more than the God who made it possible. The heart that once depended on Him can slowly become satisfied with its own strength.
  - Nebuchadnezzar reached that point. As he looked over Babylon, he spoke as though the kingdom had been built by his own power and for his own glory (Daniel 4:30). He forgot that everything he possessed had first been placed in his hands by God. His story reminds you that no position, achievement, or ability can take the place of humble dependence on the Lord.
  - As God entrusts more to you, let your gratitude deepen. Keep seeking Him with the same devotion that marked the beginning of your walk with Christ. Continue to welcome His correction, and never lose the desire to learn from Him. A heart that remains teachable is a heart that remains useful.
  - Jesus showed us a completely different way to live. Although all authority belonged to Him, He humbled Himself and became a servant (Philippians 2:5–8). His greatness was revealed through His obedience to the Father.
  - Give God the glory for every open door. Thank Him for every opportunity. Remember that the One who lifted you is also able to sustain you. A humble heart can carry every blessing that God chooses to place upon it.
prayer: Lord, thank You because every good thing in my life comes from You. My heart will remain fixed on You as You continue to guide my steps. I will honor You in every opportunity You give me and remember that You deserve the glory. Thank You for helping me walk in humility so that my life will always reflect Christ. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
