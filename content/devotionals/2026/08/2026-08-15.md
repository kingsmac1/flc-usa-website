title: Provoking Divine Encounters
scripture: 1 Samuel 3:4
verse: 'That the Lord called Samuel: and he answered, Here am I.'
body:
  - AA divine encounter is a moment when God makes His presence, His direction, or His power known in a way that changes the course of your life. One encounter with God can accomplish what years of human effort cannot.
  - Samuel experienced such a moment while serving in the house of the Lord. When God called his name, he responded, “Here am I.” That encounter marked the beginning of his prophetic ministry and changed the direction of his life forever (1 Samuel 3:4–10).
  - As you read through Scripture, you will notice that people who experienced remarkable encounters with God were not spiritually passive. Hannah poured out her heart before the Lord until her prayer was answered (1 Samuel 1:10–20). Daniel sought God faithfully through prayer, even when it placed his life at risk (Daniel 6:10). David yearned for God's presence with all his heart and longed to dwell in His house (Psalm 27:4).
  - Perhaps you have been asking God to intervene in an area of your life. Do not become satisfied with where you are. Let your hunger for God grow deeper. Give Him more of your time than you have before. Pray with greater earnestness. Fast as He
  - leads you. Serve Him with joy. Worship Him wholeheartedly. Honor Him with your resources. A heart that truly longs for God will always find ways to seek Him more.
  - Your pursuit of God is never about earning His love. It is the response of a heart that longs to know Him more deeply. As you seek Him, He shapes you, he prepares you, and opens your eyes to what He is doing.
  - Do not settle for yesterday's experience. Ask the Lord to reveal Himself in a fresh way. He still speaks. He still guides. He still answers those who seek Him with all their hearts.
prayer: Lord, thank You for revealing Yourself to those who seek You. My heart longs to know You more deeply, and I will seek You with greater devotion. Thank You for drawing me into a closer walk with You and for opening my eyes to Your work in my life. I believe that my life will reflect Your power, Your wisdom, and Your presence as I continue to seek You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
