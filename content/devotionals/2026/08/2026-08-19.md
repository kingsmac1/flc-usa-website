title: Submit Yourself To Christ
scripture: John 3:30
verse: He must increase, but I must decrease.
body:
  - One of the greatest decisions you can make each day is to place your life under the lordship of Jesus Christ. Following Him is more than believing in Him. It is allowing Him to shape the way you think, the choices you make, and the direction your life takes.
  - John the Baptist gives you a beautiful example of what true submission looks like. People traveled from many places to hear him preach, and his ministry touched countless lives. When Jesus began His public ministry, John's response revealed the condition of his heart. He was not concerned about keeping the crowds or protecting his reputation. He understood that his assignment was to point people to Christ.
  - That is why he said, “He must increase, but I must decrease.” John knew that his life was never meant to draw attention to himself. His greatest desire was for people to know Jesus. Those words should become the attitude of your own heart. Every day, choose to let Christ have first place. Allow His Word to shape your opinions. Let His word guide your decisions. Surrender your plans to Him and trust His direction, even when it differs from your own.
  - Submitting to Christ does not diminish your life. It brings you into God's best for you. The more you yield to His authority, the more His character is seen in you. Your words begin to reflect His heart. Your actions begin to honor Him. Your life becomes a witness to those around you.
  - God does remarkable things through people who are willing to lay aside personal ambition and seek His glory instead. He is not looking for those who promote themselves. He is looking for those who are willing to exalt Christ.
  - 'As you go through today, ask yourself a simple question: Will this decision make Christ more visible in my life? Let that question guide your thoughts and your actions. When Christ increases in your life, you become the person God created you to be.'
prayer: Thank You, Lord, because You are continually forming the character of Christ in me through the work of the Holy Spirit. My heart remains yielded to You, and my life will reflect Your truth each day. Amen.
