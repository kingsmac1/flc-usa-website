title: Speaking In New Tongues
scripture: Mark 16:17
verse: And these signs shall follow them that believe... they shall speak with new tongues.
body:
  - When Jesus promised that believers would speak with new tongues, He was revealing one of the wonderful ministries of the Holy Spirit. These are not languages learned through study or human ability. They are Spirit given utterances that enable you to communicate with God beyond the limitations of your natural understanding.
  - On the Day of Pentecost, that promise became a reality. The disciples were filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance (Acts 2:4). It was more than a supernatural event. It marked the beginning of a new era where the Holy Spirit would dwell within every believer, empowering them to live for Christ and carry His gospel to the ends of the earth.
  - The same Holy Spirit has not changed. He still fills hearts of every believer that are yielded to Him. He still strengthens us for daily living. He still gives us grace to pray when our words seem inadequate. There are moments when your understanding reaches its limit, but the Holy Spirit never reaches His. He knows the mind of God, and He knows exactly what your spirit needs. The Apostle Paul wrote that the person who speaks in an unknown tongue speaks to God and edifies himself (1 Corinthians 14:2, 4). To edify means to build up, strengthen, or make stronger. Just as your body needs nourishment to remain healthy, your spirit also needs strengthening. The Holy Spirit ministers to your spirit, helping your faith remain vibrant and your heart remain focused on Christ.
  - The purpose of this gift is never to make you feel spiritually superior to others. Every gift of the Spirit points back to Jesus. The Holy Spirit did not come to draw attention to Himself or to us. He came to reveal Christ, produce His character within you, and empower you to become an effective witness wherever God has placed you.
  - As you walk with the Lord, remain open to everything He desires to accomplish in your life. Do not limit God to what your mind can explain. The Holy Spirit delights in filling hearts that are hungry for God. The more room you give Him, the more He shapes your life into the likeness of Christ.
prayer: I declare that I am filled with the Holy Spirit, and the life of God is at work within me. My spirit is strengthened daily, and my faith continues to grow. I remain sensitive to the Holy Spirit. Through the power of the Holy Spirit, I live victoriously and bring glory to God in all I do. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
