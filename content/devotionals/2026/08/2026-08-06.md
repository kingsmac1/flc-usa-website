title: Do Not Receive God’s Grace In Vain
scripture: 2 Corinthians 6:1
verse: We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.
body:
  - God has lavished His grace upon your life for a purpose. His grace saves you, strengthens you, and equips you to become all that He has called you to be. Paul's words remind us that grace should produce a response. It is something to embrace and live by.
  - Receiving God's grace in vain happens when His work in your life produces little or no lasting fruit. God speaks, but His Word is ignored. He opens a door to serve, but fear keeps you from stepping through it. He calls you to grow, but you become comfortable where you are. His grace is available, yet it is left unused.
  - Think about all that God has entrusted to you. He has given you His Spirit. He has placed gifts within you. He has opened your eyes to His Word. He has surrounded you with opportunities to make Christ known. Let His grace move you to action.
  - Paul described himself as God's fellow worker. What a privilege to know that the Lord invites you to participate in His work. He does not need your strength, but He delights in working through
  - a willing heart. Every act of obedience becomes an opportunity for His grace to be seen.
  - Do not settle for simply hearing God's Word. Let it shape your decisions. Let it influence the way you live. Let it inspire you to serve others with joy. A life that responds to God's grace becomes a testimony of what He can accomplish through someone who is fully available to Him. The grace that found you is still working in you. Give it room to accomplish everything God has planned for your life.
prayer: Lord, thank You for the grace You have lavished into my life through Jesus Christ. Thank You because Your grace continues to strengthen and equip me each day. I will respond with willing obedience and make myself available for Your work in Jesus' Name, Amen.
furtherStudies: 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 2:8–10
