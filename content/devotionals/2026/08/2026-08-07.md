title: Don’t Be Too Busy For God
scripture: Luke 10:42
verse: 'But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.'
body:
  - Jesus entered the home of Mary and Martha as a welcomed guest. Martha immediately became occupied with preparing and serving, while Mary chose to sit at His feet and listen to His words (Luke 10:38–42). Both sisters loved Jesus. Martha expressed her love through service. Mary expressed her love by giving Him her full attention.
  - When Martha became overwhelmed, she asked Jesus to tell Mary to help her. Instead, Jesus gently redirected her heart. He was not criticizing her willingness to serve. He was showing her that fellowship with Him should come before activity for Him. Service has great value, but it should always flow from a relationship with Christ.
  - 'Ask yourself an honest question: Does your schedule leave room for God? Your career may demand long hours. Your business may require constant attention. Family responsibilities may fill every part of your day. Even ministry can become so demanding that your time with the Lord gradually disappears. You may speak about God, work for God, and serve God''s people while spending very little time in His presence. The world rewards productivity. Christ values communion.'
  - If your work leaves you too exhausted to pray, too distracted to read the Scriptures, or too occupied to gather with God's people, something needs to change. Your relationship with Christ cannot be sustained on occasional moments squeezed into a busy schedule. He deserves your first attention, not your remaining time.
  - Mary chose what Jesus called "that good part." She understood that before she could serve Him well, she needed to know Him well. Every responsibility in your life will become clearer when your heart is first settled in God's presence. Your work, your decisions, and your relationships all benefit when Christ remains at the center of your life. Do not allow success to replace devotion. Let your relationship with Christ remain the foundation upon which everything else is built.
prayer: Lord, thank You for welcoming me into Your presence. Thank You because You are my greatest treasure. My relationship with You will always come before every responsibility and every pursuit. My heart will remain attentive to Your voice, and my life will be built upon fellowship with You. Thank You for giving me wisdom to order my days in a way that honors You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
