title: Stop Doubting God
scripture: Luke 24:38
verse: And he said unto them, Why are ye troubled? and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?
body:
  - After His resurrection, Jesus stood in the midst of His disciples. Instead of rejoicing, they were troubled. Fear and uncertainty had filled their hearts, making it difficult for them to believe what was happening before their eyes. Jesus looked at them and asked, “Why are ye troubled? and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?”
  - The same question deserves your attention today. What thoughts have occupied your heart? Have they strengthened your faith, or have they quietly nurtured doubt? Doubt grows whenever your attention remains fixed on circumstances instead of God's Word. Faith grows when your heart rests on what God has spoken. Hebrews 11:6 says, “He that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.” Trust is the doorway through which you experience God's faithfulness.
  - When Martha stood before Lazarus' tomb, all she could see was death. Jesus directed her attention beyond the grave and said, “If thou wouldest believe, thou shouldest see the glory of God” (John 11:40). Her situation had not changed, but Christ invited her to believe before the miracle appeared. Faith always
  - looks beyond what is visible. It rests on God's character and His word. Long before circumstances begin to change, God remains faithful. His Word has never failed, and it never will.
  - Let your heart settle on what God has said instead of what fear continues to suggest. Confidence grows as you meditate on His word, and doubt loses its influence when faith fills your heart.
prayer: Lord, thank You because Your Word is true and Your faithfulness never changes. My confidence rests in You and not in my circumstances. I choose to believe every word You have spoken. Fear will not rule my heart because my trust is anchored in You. Thank You for revealing Your faithfulness as I continue to walk by faith. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
