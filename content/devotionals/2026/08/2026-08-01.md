title: Abide In Christ
scripture: John 15:5
verse: 'I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.'
body:
  - The Christian life is not sustained by occasional moments with God. It is sustained by continual fellowship with Him. Jesus used the picture of a vine and its branches to show that spiritual life, strength, and fruitfulness all come from remaining connected to Him.
  - The word abide means to remain, to continue, or to dwell. It describes a relationship that is constant rather than occasional. Just as a branch cannot survive apart from the vine, a believer cannot flourish apart from Christ.
  - Many people try to live the Christian life through determination alone. They rely on their own ability to overcome temptation, maintain peace, or spiritual growth. But Jesus made it clear that lasting fruit comes from abiding in Him, not from human effort.
  - Abiding in Christ means making Him the center of everyday life. It means spending time in His Word, speaking with Him in prayer, and responding to the leading of the Holy Spirit. As this relationship deepens, His character begins to shape ours.
  - A branch does not struggle to produce fruit. It simply remains connected to the vine. In the same way, spiritual growth is the result of staying connected to Christ. As we remain in Him, love becomes more evident, and to yield becomes a natural response to His presence.
  - There will be seasons when life becomes busy or faced with challenges. Those are often the moments when believers are most tempted to neglect fellowship with God. Yet, those are the very times we need Him most. Strength for today and hope for tomorrow are found in Christ.
  - Jesus invites us to remain in Him because He knows that everything needed for a fruitful life is found in His presence. Stay connected to Christ. A life that abides in Him will never lack what is needed to fulfill God's purpose.
prayer: Father, thank You because Christ is the source of my life and strength. Thank You for the privilege of living in constant fellowship with You. My heart remains focused in Your Word, and my life continues to draw strength from Your presence. Thank You because the Holy Spirit continually produces His fruit within me, and my life will bring glory to You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
