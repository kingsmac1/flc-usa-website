title: Correct Others With Love
scripture: Luke 17:3
verse: 'Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.'
body:
  - There will be times when you see a fellow believer making choices that are contrary to God's Word. Remaining silent may seem easier, but genuine love does not ignore sin that is leading someone away from God.
  - In Luke 17:3, Jesus said, “If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.” The word rebuke means to express disapproval of sinful or wrong behavior with the hope of bringing about repentance. Its purpose is not to shame or embarrass someone. It is to help them return to the right path.
  - Before you correct someone, examine your own heart. Ask yourself why you are speaking. Is your desire to restore them, or simply to prove that you are right? Correction offered without love often wounds more than it heals. The apostle Paul wrote, “Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such one in the spirit of meekness” (Galatians 6:1). Notice the goal is restoration. The manner also matters. A gentle spirit opens the door for repentance far more than harsh words ever will.
  - Correction should always leave room for forgiveness. Jesus did not stop at rebuke. He continued by saying, “If he repent, forgive him.” Forgiveness completes what loving correction begins. Without forgiveness, rebuke easily becomes condemnation.
  - If you ever need to correct another believer, let your words reflect the heart of Christ. Speak the truth with humility. Let your concern be for their spiritual growth rather than winning an argument. A loving correction can become the very thing God uses to restore a brother or sister.
prayer: Lord, thank You for Your patience and mercy toward me. Thank You because Your love teaches me how to speak the truth with grace. My words will bring healing and not harm. My heart will remain free from pride, bitterness, and condemnation. Thank You for helping me reflect the compassion of Christ as I encourage others to walk in Your truth. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
