title: The New Wineskin
scripture: Matthew 9:17
verse: 'Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break... but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.'
body:
  - Have you been asking God to do something new in your life? Before He changes what is around you, He often begins by changing what is within you. God's work always starts in the heart before it becomes visible in your circumstances.
  - When Jesus spoke about new wine and new wineskins, His listeners understood the illustration. An old wineskin had become hard over time and could no longer stretch. If fresh wine were poured into it, the wineskin would burst. New wine required a new wineskin.
  - Jesus was using that picture to describe the new life He came to give. You cannot receive all that Christ desires to do while holding on to the old way of living. A heart that has been transformed by God becomes ready for the work of the Holy Spirit.
  - That is why Jesus told Nicodemus, “Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God” (John 3:3). Entering God's kingdom begins with a new birth. It is not about becoming a better version of your old self. It is about receiving a new life from God. Centuries before Christ came, God announced what He would do. “A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you” (Ezekiel 36:26). God knew that outward changes would never be enough. He chose to begin with the heart because everything else flows from it.
  - The moment you placed your faith in Christ, that work began. “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature” (2 Corinthians 5:17). You are no longer defined by who you used to be. God has given you a new heart, and the Holy Spirit now lives within you.
  - Stop measuring your life by your past. Stop allowing yesterday to tell you who you are today. Christ has made you new. Every day is another opportunity to grow, to mature, and to become more like Him. Welcome the work of the Holy Spirit. Allow Him to shape your thoughts, change your desires, and lead your life. As you walk with Him, you will discover that God is preparing you for far more than you imagined.
prayer: Lord, thank You for making me a new creation through Jesus Christ. Thank You because Your Spirit lives within me and continues to renew my life each day. I embrace the new life You have given me. I will not be held back by my past because I belong to Christ. My heart is open to Your work, and my life will reflect the beauty of Jesus. In Jesus' Name. Amen.
