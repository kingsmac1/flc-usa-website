title: The Audacity Of Faith
scripture: Hebrews 11:1
verse: Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.
body:
  - Faith is more than believing that God exists. It is trusting Him so completely that you are willing to act on His Word even when circumstances suggest otherwise. The audacity of faith is the boldness to believe God beyond what your eyes can see, your mind can explain, or your circumstances appear to permit.
  - The word audacity comes from the Latin word audax, meaning bold, daring, or fearless. It describes a confidence that refuses to be intimidated. The audacity of faith is not confidence in yourself; it is confidence in God's character. It is the willingness to expect the impossible because you know that God always keeps His Word.
  - David lived with this kind of faith. While everyone else saw Goliath as an unbeatable giant, David saw an opportunity for God to reveal His power. His confidence did not come from his experience as a soldier but from his knowledge of God's faithfulness. Before the battle began, he declared, “The battle is the Lord's” (1 Samuel 17:47). David's victory was born long before he picked up a stone. It began when he chose to believe God.
  - The woman with the issue of blood demonstrated the same boldness. For twelve years, she had lived with disappointment, yet she refused to accept that her situation would never change. She pressed through the crowd because she believed that one touch of Jesus would make the difference. Her faith moved her to action, and her encounter with Christ changed her life forever.
  - You will face moments when fear tells you to play it safe, when circumstances suggest that nothing will change, and when people expect you to lower your expectations. Do not allow those voices to become louder than God's word. Faith always looks beyond what is visible and fixes its eyes on the One for whom nothing is impossible.
  - Unleash your faith today. Refuse to let fear have the final word. Let your faith rise above your circumstances, and dare to believe that God is still able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that you ask or think.
prayer: I declare that my faith is fixated in the unchanging Word of God. I refuse to allow fear, doubt, or what I see around to grip me. My confidence is in the Lord, and nothing is impossible for Him. I will walk courageously, and trust God completely. My faith is unshakable and I accomplish the impossible as I unleash my faith In Jesus' Name. Amen.
