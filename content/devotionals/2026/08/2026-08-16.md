title: Saturate Your Cloud
scripture: Luke 11:9
verse: Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.
body:
  - Perhaps you have been praying about the same matter for months or even years. You have believed God, stood on His Word, and continued to pray, but the answer still seems far away. At times like this, the temptation is to think that your prayers are accomplishing nothing.
  - Jesus gives you a different perspective. In Luke 11:9, He said, “Ask... seek... knock.” He was encouraging His disciples to persevere in prayer. He did not tell them to pray once and give up if nothing happened. He taught them to keep coming to the Father with confidence.
  - Ecclesiastes 11:3 says, “If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth.” Before rain falls, the cloud must first become full. It does not empty itself after the first drop of water enters it. It continues to receive until it reaches the point where it can no longer hold what has been gathered.
  - Prayer often works in the same way. Every prayer offered in faith is like another drop filling the cloud. You may not see anything changing today, but that does not mean nothing is happening. God hears every prayer. He remembers every promise, and He is working even when His work is hidden from your eyes.
  - Do not become discouraged because you have not seen the answer yet. The cloud may still be filling. Then comes the moment when what has been building quietly begins to overflow. What seemed impossible suddenly happens. What appeared delayed becomes a testimony of God's faithfulness. The rain falls because the cloud has reached its fullness.
  - Keep praying. Keep believing. Keep trusting God's timing. Your persistence is not an attempt to persuade God to care about your situation. It is an expression of your confidence that He is faithful and that His answer will come at the right time. Never abandon your place of prayer because you cannot yet see the rain. The cloud may be fuller than you realize.
prayer: Lord, thank You because You hear every prayer I bring before You. Thank You because not one word spoken in faith is ever forgotten. My confidence remains in Your faithfulness, and I will continue to seek You without growing weary. Thank You because Your timing is perfect, and every Word You have spoken will come to pass. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
