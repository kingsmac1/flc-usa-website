title: The Instrument Of Love
scripture: 1 Corinthians 13:7 (KJV)
verse: Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.
body:
  - The love of God is not merely an emotion you feel; it is a divine force at work in you. When you received Christ, the love of God was poured into your heart by the Holy Spirit. You have therefore been given the capacity to love beyond ordinary human limitations.
  - First Corinthians 13:7 reveals the strength of this love. It bears, believes, hopes, and endures. This does not mean tolerating abuse or approving what is wrong. Rather, God's love gives you the strength to remain gracious when others disappoint you and to refuse bitterness when you have been hurt.
  - Romans 5:5 says, “The love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us.” Notice that Scripture does not say you are trying to obtain this love. It has already been deposited in you. Your responsibility is to allow its expression.
  - Love heals. Many people carry wounds from rejection, betrayal, or painful experiences. Yet the love of God can reach those places and restore the heart. Psalm 147:3 says, “He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.” You do not have to build your life around what hurts you. God's love can heal you until the pain no longer controls your response.
  - Love also refuses revenge. When someone treats you wrongly, your natural reaction may be to retaliate. But the love of God in you gives you a different response. You can forgive without becoming weak. You can speak truth without hatred. You can maintain boundaries without allowing resentment to poison your heart.
  - Become conscious that you are a carrier of God's love. Let people encounter His nature through the way you speak and respond. Your love can bring peace into a tense situation and healing into a wounded relationship. You are not empty of love. The love of God is already in you. Let it find expression through your life.
prayer: Father, thank You for pouring Your love into my heart by the Holy Spirit. I declare that I walk in divine love. Bitterness has no place in me. I forgive freely and respond with grace. God's love governs my words and actions, and through me, others experience His goodness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
