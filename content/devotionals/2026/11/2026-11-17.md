title: The Refreshing Of The Soul
scripture: Psalm 19:7 (NIV)
verse: The law of the LORD is perfect, refreshing the soul. The statutes of the LORD are trustworthy, making wise the simple.
body:
  - 'There are seasons when your soul needs refreshing. You may still be praying and carrying on with your responsibilities, yet inwardly, you feel drained. God has given you a source of renewal that never runs dry: His Word.'
  - Psalm 19:7 says the law of the Lord is perfect, “refreshing the soul.” The Word reaches places that ordinary encouragement cannot reach. It can strengthen you when weariness has settled in and restore spiritual vitality when your heart feels low.
  - Psalm 23:3 says, “He restoreth my soul.” God knows how to restore what has been depleted. He can bring you back to a place of spiritual freshness and renewed desire for Him. Sometimes, you simply need to return to the Word until your heart is nourished again.
  - Psalm 119:25 says, “Quicken thou me according to thy word.” To quicken is to make alive. The same Word that brought you into new life in Christ continues to sustain that life day by day. First Peter 1:23 says that you were born again by the Word of God. That same Word keeps feeding your spirit and renewing your inner man.
  - When sorrow weighs heavily on you, stay with the Scriptures. When your mind feels tired, let the Word settle you. Read slowly enough for what God has said to sink into your heart. Meditate on a verse until it becomes personal to you.
  - A change of environment may give your body rest, but the Word reaches the dept of your soul. It restores your spiritual strength and brings you back into conscious fellowship with God. His Word can revive places within you that have become weary.
  - Make room for the Word every day. Let it wash over your thoughts and awaken fresh desire in your heart. God's Word is living, and as you receive it, your soul is refreshed.
prayer: Father, thank You for the refreshing power of Your Word. I declare that my soul is restored and my inner man is renewed. Your Word revives me and strengthens my heart. I receive fresh spiritual vitality every day, and I remain nourished by the Scriptures. My soul flourishes in Your presence, and I live with renewed strength. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Psalm 23:3; Psalm 119:25, 50; 1 Peter 1:23
