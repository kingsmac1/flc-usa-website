title: Gladness In Your Heart
scripture: Psalm 4:7 (KJV)
verse: Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased.
body:
  - There is a joy that money cannot purchase and favorable circumstances cannot produce. David spoke of a gladness that came directly from God. He compared it with the excitement people experienced when their harvest increased, yet he declared that what God had placed in his heart was greater.
  - This is important for you to know. Your joy does not have to rise and fall with what is happening around you. There is a gladness that comes from within because God Himself is its source. When your confidence is rooted in Him, you can experience joy even while you are waiting for circumstances to change.
  - The world often says, “When I get what I want, then I will be happy.” But Scripture shows you another way. God can put gladness in your heart before the increase comes. You can rejoice before the answer becomes visible because your joy is connected to His presence, not merely to possessions.
  - Jesus said in John 15:11, “These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you.” Notice that He speaks of His joy being in you. This is not ordinary happiness produced by good news. It is divine joy residing within you through your fellowship with Christ.
  - You therefore do not have to wait for everything to become perfect before you rejoice. Refuse to give circumstances control over the condition of your heart. When things are moving slowly, your joy can remain. When others do not understand your confidence, you can still rejoice because you know the One in whom you have believed.
  - Joy is also a strength. When your heart is established in God, discouragement finds less room to settle. You begin to see beyond the present moment and remember that your life is held in His hands. Wake up each day conscious that God has placed gladness within you. Let that joy find expression in your words and in the way you approach life. Your joy is not outside you waiting to happen. In Christ, God has already placed His joy within your heart.
prayer: Father, thank You for putting gladness in my heart. I declare that my joy is rooted in You and is not controlled by circumstances. The joy of the Lord strengthens me. I rejoice in Your goodness and live each day with a heart filled with divine gladness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
