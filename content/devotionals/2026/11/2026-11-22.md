date: '2026-11-22'
title: What Can You See?
scripture: Genesis 13:14–15
verse: And the LORD said unto Abram... Lift up now thine eyes, and look from the place where thou art... For all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it.
body:
  - There is a kind of seeing that does not require your physical eyes. You can close your eyes and still see a future in your heart. You can carry a picture of where your life is going long before there is physical evidence of it. This inner picture matters because what you continually see within can influence what you expect and how you respond to life.
  - 'When God told Abram to lift up his eyes and look, He connected what Abram could see with what he would possess: “For all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it.” God was enlarging Abram''s vision beyond the place where he was standing. The present location was not supposed to become the boundary of what Abram could expect.'
  - What picture have you been carrying about yourself? Perhaps circumstances have been difficult for so long that you have started seeing yourself permanently confined by them. Change that picture through God's Word. See yourself restored. If you are trusting God for healing, let Scripture shape the picture in your heart beyond the present condition. If God has placed a business in your heart, begin to see yourself developing it and doing the work required to establish it. Do not allow failure to become the dominant image of your future.
  - This is not daydreaming or pretending that difficulties do not exist. Faith gives substance to what you see in your heart because that vision is formed by what God has said. Hebrews 11:1 says, “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” Your natural eyes may show you where you are today, while faith allows your heart to embrace what is possible through God.
  - So, look again. Do not keep rehearsing pictures of defeat in your imagination. Let the Word enlarge what you can see concerning your life. See beyond the confinement. See beyond the limitation that has tried to define you. As far as the eyes of faith can see, so will God give you
prayer: My heart is illuminated by the Word of God, and I see my life from the perspective of faith. I see progress where circumstances once suggested limitation. I see myself walking in the fullness of what Christ has made available to me. My future is bright, my Faith is alive, and I move confidently toward everything God has placed in my heart. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
