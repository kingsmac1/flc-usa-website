title: 'The Holy Spirit: Your Guide'
scripture: John 16:13 (KJV)
verse: Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth.
body:
  - The Holy Spirit is your divine Guide. He lives in you to lead you in the knowledge of Christ and to help you walk accurately with God. You are never without direction because the One who knows the mind of God has made His home in you.
  - 'Jesus called Him “the Spirit of truth.” His ministry brings you into a clearer understanding of who Jesus is and what He accomplished through His death and resurrection. John 16:14 says, “He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you.” The Holy Spirit unveils Christ to your heart and makes the realities of the gospel increasingly clear to you.'
  - There are things you may read in Scripture that suddenly become alive in your understanding. A verse you have known for years can open up with fresh clarity. That is part of the ministry of the Holy Spirit. He teaches you and brings the Word into focus until you can walk in its reality.
  - His guidance also reaches into your daily life. Romans 8:14 says, “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.” Being led by the Spirit is part of your life in Christ. You can develop sensitivity to His leading as you fellowship with Him and give attention to the Word. Sometimes, His guidance comes as an inward conviction. At other times, He may bring a particular scripture strongly to your heart. As you respond to His leading, discernment becomes sharper.
  - Hebrews 5:14 speaks of those whose senses are exercised “to discern both good and evil.” Spiritual discernment develops as you practice obedience to the Word and remain attentive to the Holy Spirit. Live every day conscious that you have divine guidance within you. You are not wandering through life trying to find your way. The Spirit of God leads you and gives you understanding. You have a divine Guide, and He knows the way.
prayer: Father, thank You for the Holy Spirit who lives in me. I declare that I am led by the Spirit of God. I recognize your guidance and respond with faith. My understanding of Christ grows continually, and I walk with discernment. I am sensitive to the direction of the Holy Spirit, and I follow His leading with assurance. In Jesus' name, Amen.
