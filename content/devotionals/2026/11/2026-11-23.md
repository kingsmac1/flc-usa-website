title: Be Wise
scripture: Proverbs 4:7 (KJV)
verse: 'Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.'
body:
  - God wants you to be wise. He does not want you living carelessly, repeating the same mistakes, or making decisions without understanding. Wisdom is available to you, and Scripture continually invites you to pursue it.
  - 'The Bible often places two kinds of people side by side: the wise and the foolish. The fool is not simply someone who lacks intelligence. Scripture describes the fool as someone who resists God, ignores correction, or chooses a stubborn path. The wise person responds differently. He seeks God, values His Word, and allows divine instruction to shape his life. Your choices reveal which path you are walking.'
  - Proverbs 9:10 says, “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.” Reverence for God places your heart in the right position to receive from Him. When you honor God, you become more willing to listen. You begin to value what He says above what may simply feel right in the moment.
  - God's Word develops wisdom in you. It helps you see situations from a higher perspective and gives you practical understanding for everyday life. As you spend time in Scripture, your thinking becomes more disciplined. You become better, able to recognize what is worth pursuing and what should be avoided.
  - Wisdom also affects your relationships. It teaches you when to speak and when silence is better. It helps you respond without unnecessary conflict. It gives you the maturity to listen before reaching conclusions. James 1:5 says, “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God.” You never have to pretend that you know what to do. You can ask God. He is generous with wisdom, and He wants to guide you.
  - Choose the path of wisdom deliberately. Stay close to the Word. Remain teachable. Let God shape the way you think until wise choices become a natural part of your life. You were not designed to live foolishly. God wants you to be wise.
prayer: Father, thank You for making wisdom available to me in your word. I declare that I walk in wisdom and remain teachable. My heart honors You, and my choices are shaped by Your Word. I receive understanding for every area of my life, and I make decisions that produce good fruit. In Jesus' name, Amen.
