title: The Shield Of Salvation
scripture: Psalm 18:35 (KJV)
verse: 'Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great.'
body:
  - David looked back over his life and recognized that the Lord had preserved him again and again. His victories were not simply the result of skill or experience. He knew that God had been his protection, and he described that saving power as “the shield of thy salvation.”
  - In this verse, salvation speaks of deliverance and preservation. A shield surrounds and protects the one who carries it. David saw the Lord's saving power in that way. God was not merely present in his life; He was actively preserving him and sustaining him.
  - This same confidence belongs to you in Christ. Psalm 3:3 says, “But thou, O LORD, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.” The Lord Himself is your shield. His presence surrounds you, and His hand upholds you. You can live every day with the assurance that your life is under divine care.
  - The salvation revealed through Jesus Christ is even greater. Through His death and resurrection, Christ delivered you from the dominion of darkness and brought you into the kingdom of God's Son. Colossians 1:13 says that God “hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son.” You have therefore been brought into a place of divine security. Your life is hidden with Christ in God. You belong to a kingdom where Christ reigns, and His saving power is at work in you. Salvation is not merely something you received for eternity; it is a present reality that shapes how you live now.
  - Live with the consciousness that God is your shield and your covering. His saving power surrounds you, and His hand continually upholds you. You are preserved in Christ, secure in His presence, and divinely protected. Your life is hidden with Christ in God, and you walk each day with the assurance that the Lord watches over you. You are kept by the power of God. The shield of His salvation is yours.
prayer: Father, thank You for the shield of Your salvation. I declare that I am divinely preserved and securely kept in Christ. Your hand upholds me, and Your presence surrounds me. I walk in the consciousness of Your protection and saving power. My life is hidden with Christ in God, and I remain secure under Your care. In Jesus' name, Amen.
