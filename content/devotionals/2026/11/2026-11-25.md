title: Don’t Despise Wisdom
scripture: Proverbs 1:7 (KJV)
verse: 'The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.'
body:
  - Wisdom begins with the right attitude toward God. Proverbs 1:7 calls it “the fear of the LORD.” This is a deep reverence for God that makes you value what He says and take His instructions seriously. When you honor God, you also learn to honor the wisdom that comes from Him.
  - The verse goes on to say that fools despise wisdom and instruction. To despise wisdom is to treat sound counsel as though it has little value. It can happen when a person becomes too confident in personal experience or refuses correction simply because it is uncomfortable. Wisdom requires a teachable heart.
  - There will be moments when the instruction you receive may not be what you wanted to hear. Someone may point out an area that needs attention, or the Word may confront a habit you have grown comfortable with. Your response in that moment matters. A wise person pauses long enough to listen and consider what God may be showing them.
  - 'Proverbs 12:15 says, “The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise.” Wisdom is willing to listen. You do not lose strength by receiving correction. In fact, correction can save you from unnecessary mistakes and help you make better choices. God has placed wisdom in His Word, and He can also use godly counsel to help you see what you may have missed. Stay open to instruction. You do not have to know everything before you can move forward; you simply need a heart that is willing to learn.'
  - Never become too successful to receive advice or too experienced to be corrected. Growth continues when you remain teachable. The person who values wisdom will keep gaining understanding. Honor God by honoring His instruction. Receive correction with humility, and let wisdom guide the decisions you make.
prayer: Father, thank You for the wisdom found in Your Word. I declare that my heart is teachable and receptive to instruction. I honor Your counsel and willingly receive correction that helps me grow. I walk in reverence for You, and wisdom governs the choices I make. My understanding increases as I remain attentive to Your Word. In Jesus' name, Amen.
