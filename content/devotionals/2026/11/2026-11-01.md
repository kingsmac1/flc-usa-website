title: Ask In The Name Of Jesus
scripture: John 16:23 (NIV)
verse: In that day you will no longer ask me anything. Very truly I tell you, my Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.
body:
  - Jesus was preparing His disciples for a new order of fellowship with God. Under the old covenant, access to the Most Holy Place was restricted to the high priest. Through the death and resurrection of Jesus, the way into God's presence was opened. You can now approach the Father personally because Christ has brought you near.
  - Hebrews 10:19 says, “Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus.” Think about the privilege you have received. You are welcome in the presence of God. You can speak with your Father freely and present your requests before Him with confidence. Jesus said, “My Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.” Asking in the name of Jesus is more than adding the words “in Jesus' name” at the end of a prayer. His name represents His authority and your standing in Him. You approach the Father on the basis of what Jesus has accomplished for you.
  - You are not presenting your personal merit as the reason God should hear you. Jesus is your great High Priest, and through Him, you have been accepted. His name gives you access. When you pray in His name, you come before the Father conscious of your union with Christ and confident in the relationship He has given you.
  - John 16:24 continues, “Ask, and you will receive, and your joy will be complete.” Jesus wanted His disciples to pray with expectation. Prayer was not intended to be an uncertain religious exercise. It is fellowship with your Father, with the assurance that He hears you. So, pray boldly. Bring your desires before God in agreement with His Word. Expect answers. You are not standing outside hoping to gain entrance; Christ has already opened the way. You have access to the Father, and the name of Jesus is yours to use in prayer. What a glorious privilege!
prayer: Father, thank You for giving me access to Your presence through Jesus Christ. I pray with confidence in the name of Jesus, knowing that I am accepted and heard. I approach You boldly, and my heart is established in the finished work of Christ. I receive answers to prayer with joyful expectation and continually enjoy fellowship with You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
