title: God’s Word Is Perfect
scripture: Psalm 19:7 (KJV)
verse: 'The law of the LORD is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple.'
body:
  - God's Word is perfect because it comes from a perfect God. There is no defect in His character, so there is no defect in what He has spoken. Psalm 19:7 describes His Word as complete and fully dependable. You can build your life on it without wondering whether God will change His mind tomorrow.
  - Proverbs 30:5 says, “Every word of God is pure.” His Word has the quality of the One who spoke it. God sees beyond what you can see, and He knows the end from the beginning. When He speaks, you are receiving wisdom that is not limited by human understanding.
  - Psalm 19 also speaks about “the testimony of the LORD.” Testimony refers to God's own witness concerning who He is and what He has done. It includes what He has revealed about His character and His promises. Psalm 93:5 says, “Thy testimonies are very sure.” You can trust what God has said about Himself because He cannot lie.
  - Titus 1:2 speaks of “God, that cannot lie.” This gives you strong confidence when you read His word. You are not dealing with the uncertainty of human words. God's testimony is dependable because His nature is dependable.Second Timothy 3:16–17 also tells you that Scripture is given by inspiration of God and equips you for the life He has called you to live. The Word corrects your thinking and brings your heart into agreement with God. It gives you a reliable standard when opinions around you are constantly changing.
  - When you face a decision, return to the Word. When your feelings say one thing and Scripture says another, let God's Word have the greater authority in your heart. Feelings can shift, but the Word remains steady. Treat Scripture as something settled. Read it with confidence. Receive it with reverence. Let it shape the way you live. God's Word is perfect, and you can trust it completely.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your perfect Word. I declare that Your Word is my sure foundation. I trust what You have spoken, and my heart rests in Your promises. Your Word governs my thinking and guides my life. I receive it with faith, and I walk confidently in what You have revealed. In Jesus' name, Amen.
