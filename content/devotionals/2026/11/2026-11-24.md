title: Confident Of This Very Thing
scripture: Philippians 1:6 (KJV)
verse: Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ.
body:
  - Confidence in God gives your heart rest. Paul was certain that the work God had begun in the believers would not be abandoned halfway. The same God who started the work would continue shaping their lives until His purpose was fulfilled. You can have that same confidence.
  - God is at work in you. He is developing your character and strengthening your walk with Him. You may look at certain areas of your life and feel that progress is slower than you expected, but God is still working. Growth is often taking place even when you cannot immediately see the full result.
  - Philippians 2:13 says, “For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.” God is actively influencing your desires and helping you respond to what pleases Him. Your spiritual development is not resting on human strength alone. The Holy Spirit is at work within you.
  - Think about how far God has already brought you. There are things you understand now that once confused you. Certain habits may no longer control you the way they once did. Your hunger for God may have increased. These are signs that His work in you is progressing.
  - So, refuse to become discouraged with yourself. Keep yielding to the Word and remain responsive to the Holy Spirit. God knows how to complete what He started. A building under construction may not yet look like the finished design, but the builder knows what he is creating. In the same way, God sees the finished work of grace in your life. He is shaping you according to His purpose.
  - Your responsibility is to remain available to Him. Keep growing. Keep believing. Let your confidence rest in the faithfulness of the One who began the work. God started something beautiful in you, and He is faithfully bringing it to completion.
prayer: I am confident in the work of God within me. The One who began a good work in me is faithfully bringing it to completion. I am growing in grace and becoming more established in the Word. The Holy Spirit is working mightily in me, and I move forward with confidence, knowing that God is faithful to complete what He has started in me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
