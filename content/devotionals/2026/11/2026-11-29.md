title: You Are The Solution The World Is Waiting For
scripture: Romans 8:19 (KJV)
verse: For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.
body:
  - The world is filled with needs waiting for answers. Families need restoration. Communities need direction. People are searching for hope. God has placed His life in you so that through you, His goodness can reach the people around you.
  - Romans 8:19 speaks of creation waiting for the manifestation of the sons of God. Your life in Christ has purpose beyond yourself. God wants His wisdom expressed through you in practical ways. The answer someone is praying for may come through an idea God gives you or through the gift He has placed within you.
  - Jesus said in Matthew 5:14, “Ye are the light of the world.” Light becomes valuable when it shines where it is needed. You may be the person God uses to bring clarity into a confusing situation. The solution He puts in your heart may answer a problem others have struggled with for years.
  - Never underestimate what God has placed inside you. Your gifts have purpose. Your experiences can become useful in helping others. Even lessons learned through difficult seasons can prepare you to strengthen someone who is facing a similar situation. You do not need a large platform before your life can make an impact. Begin where you are. Look around you and notice what needs improvement. Ask God for wisdom about how your abilities can become useful to others. Many great solutions begin when someone sees a need and decides to respond.
  - Joseph was given wisdom that preserved lives during famine. Esther used her position to bring deliverance to her people. They recognized that where God had placed them was connected to what He wanted to accomplish through them. See yourself as someone God can use to bring answers. Stay sensitive to divine ideas and be ready to act when direction comes. Your contribution matters. There is something God has placed in you that your world needs. Give it expression.
prayer: Father, thank You for the purpose You have placed within me. I declare that my life is useful in Your hands. I receive divine wisdom and recognize opportunities to make a difference. Through me, people are strengthened and answers are released. I boldly express the gifts You have given me and fulfill my purpose with confidence. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: Matthew 5:14–16; Romans 8:19; Ephesians 2:10
