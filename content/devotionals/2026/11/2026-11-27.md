title: Live With Dignity
scripture: Philippians 1:27 (KJV)
verse: Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ.
body:
  - Dignity is seen in the way you conduct yourself when no one is trying to impress you and nothing is forcing you to behave well. It comes from knowing who you are in Christ and allowing that identity to influence the way you speak and relate with people.
  - Paul told the believers in Philippi to live in a way that was worthy of the gospel. The word conversation in this verse refers to conduct or manner of life. Your life should agree with the message you profess. If Christ lives in you, His character should become increasingly visible through you.
  - Dignity is not pride. It is a quiet awareness that your life belongs to God. You do not need to lower your standards to gain acceptance or compromise your convictions to fit into an environment. You can remain respectful without losing your values.
  - First Thessalonians 4:12 says, “That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without.” The way you live speaks before you ever explain what you believe. People notice how you handle responsibility. They observe the way you respond when you are treated unfairly. Your conduct can give credibility to the faith you profess. Living with dignity also means valuing yourself correctly. You are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works. You do not have to beg for validation or behave beneath the standard God has shown you. Let your sense of worth come from your relationship with Him.
  - Even in disagreement, dignity should remain visible. You can be firm without becoming disrespectful. You can walk away from what dishonors God without creating unnecessary conflict. Your response should reflect maturity and self-control.
  - Let your life have weight. Let your words be worthy of trust. Let people see consistency between what you believe and how you live. You represent Christ. Live in a way that reflects the honor of the One to whom you belong.
prayer: I am a worthy representative of Christ. I walk in dignity and conduct myself with honor everywhere I go. The character of Christ is expressed through my life, and my words reflect the wisdom of God. I am confident in who I am in Christ and steadfast in godly standards. My conduct inspires respect, my choices glorify God, and my life beautifully represents the gospel. I walk in excellence and honor every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.
