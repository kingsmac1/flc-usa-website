title: Your Words Reveal Your Real Attitude
scripture: Matthew 12:34 (KJV)
verse: For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh.
body:
  - Your words often reveal what is happening beneath the surface. A person may appear calm outwardly, but one conversation can expose frustration that has been growing within. In the same way, words filled with grace can reveal a heart that has been shaped by God's Word.
  - Jesus said that the mouth speaks from the abundance of the heart. Your speech is connected to what you continually allow into your thoughts and emotions. If resentment is being stored within, it will eventually find expression. If your heart is being filled with God's Word, that influence will also begin to show in the way you speak.
  - This is why your words deserve attention. They can reveal attitudes you may not have noticed in yourself. A sarcastic response may point to hidden irritation. Constant criticism may reveal dissatisfaction. Instead of ignoring what your speech reveals, allow the Holy Spirit to show you where your heart needs adjustment.
  - Luke 6:45 says, “A good man out of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth that which is good.” God wants good treasure stored within you. The Word renews your thinking and gives you a healthier way to respond. As your heart becomes established in what God says, your speech begins to reflect that inward work.
  - Guard what you allow to remain in your heart. Forgive quickly so resentment does not settle there. Refuse to rehearse offenses until they shape your attitude. Keep feeding your heart with Scripture, because what fills you will eventually come out of you.
  - Your words also influence the people around you. A gracious response can calm a tense conversation. An encouraging word can strengthen someone who is discouraged. Let your mouth become an instrument through which the character of Christ is expressed. Pay attention to your speech, because it can help you recognize what is happening inside. Let God work in your heart until your words consistently reflect the life He has placed within you.
prayer: Father, thank You for transforming my heart through Your Word. I declare that my speech is gracious and my attitude reflects Christ. My heart is filled with what is good, and my words bring encouragement to those around me. I speak from a heart governed by love and wisdom. In Jesus' name, Amen.
