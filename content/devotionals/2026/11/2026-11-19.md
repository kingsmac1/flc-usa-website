title: The Switch Of Faith
scripture: Hebrews 4:2
verse: 'For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.'
body:
  - Think about a room that has been properly wired for electricity. Power may already be available, but the room can remain dark until the switch is turned on. Turning on the switch does not generate the electricity; it gives expression to the power that is already available. This gives us a useful picture of how faith responds to what God has provided through His Word.
  - Hebrews 4:2 tells us that certain people heard God's Word, but “the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith.” The problem was not that God's Word lacked power. They heard the same message others heard, but their response to it was different. Hearing alone did not bring them into the experience of what God had spoken. Faith was the missing connection.
  - This is why I like to describe faith as a switch. God has already accomplished so much for you in Christ, but you must believe His Word concerning what He has provided. Salvation itself demonstrates this principle. Christ died and rose again long before you heard the gospel, but when you believed and received Him, what He accomplished became a personal reality in your life. You did not create salvation by believing; faith received what grace had already supplied. The same attitude should influence your walk with God. Do not approach His Word wondering whether it has enough power to work for you. Receive it with faith. When you discover what belongs to you in Christ, believe it and live from that understanding. Faith is your positive response to God's Word. It says, “If God has said it, I receive it as mine, and I will live accordingly.”
  - Keep the switch of faith turned on. Refuse to allow disappointment or delay to disconnect your heart from what God has spoken. His Word has not lost its power because circumstances have not changed immediately. Continue responding to Him with confidence. The supply is from God; faith is how you receive and walk in what His grace has made available.
prayer: The Word of God profits me because I receive it with faith. My heart responds readily to everything God has spoken concerning me. I enjoy the realities of my inheritance in Christ and walk surely in what His grace has provided. My faith is active, and my life continually demonstrates the power of God's Word. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
