title: Have A Displeasure For Unrighteousness
scripture: 1 Samuel 11:6 (KJV)
verse: And the Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard those tidings, and his anger was kindled greatly.
body:
  - Not everything that makes you angry is wrong, and not every expression of anger is right. Anger is powerful, which is why it must be governed by God. When it rises from wounded pride or the desire to retaliate, it can quickly become destructive. But there are times when anger is a response to something that genuinely grieves the heart of God.
  - In 1 Samuel 11, Saul heard how the Ammonites threatened the people of Jabesh-gilead with humiliation and cruelty. His anger was kindled greatly, and that anger moved him to act. He did not simply sit with outrage; he responded to the injustice facing his fellow Israelites. In that moment, God used his displeasure to stir action that brought deliverance.
  - You should have a holy displeasure for what God calls unrighteous. Sin should not become so familiar that it no longer troubles you. Injustice should not become so common that you stop caring. When people are mistreated or truth is twisted, something within you should recognize that this is not the way God intends life to be.
  - Yet righteous anger must still remain under the control of the Holy Spirit. Ephesians 4:26 says, “Be ye angry, and sin not.” You can hate what is wrong without becoming hateful. You can confront injustice without becoming cruel. You can speak firmly without allowing bitterness to take root in your heart.
  - The question is not only, “What makes me angry?” but also, “What is my anger producing?” If it only produces revenge or uncontrolled reactions, it is not helping. But if it moves you to pray, speak truth, protect someone being mistreated, or take wise action, then that emotion can be directed toward something constructive.
  - Do not become comfortable with unrighteousness. Ask God to keep your heart tender toward what matters to Him. Let your response reflect His character, not merely the intensity of your feelings.
prayer: Father, I declare that my heart remains sensitive to righteousness. I will not become comfortable with sin or indifferent to injustice. My emotions are submitted to the Holy Spirit. I respond to situations with wisdom and self-control, and I will use my influence to stand for God's righteousness cause. In Jesus' name, Amen.
