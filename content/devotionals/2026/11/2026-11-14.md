title: The Catalyst Of Faith
scripture: Galatians 5:6
verse: For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth anything, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love.
body:
  - Acatalyst causes a reaction to move forward. It does not become the substance being produced, but its presence makes a difference in what happens. Faith also has things that stir it into active expression. You may know the Scriptures and sincerely believe God, but there are times when what you believe must move beyond knowledge and influence the way you respond.
  - Love is one of those catalysts. Galatians 5:6 speaks of “faith which worketh by love.” Faith is not at its best in a heart filled with bitterness toward someone else. You cannot spend your time holding tightly to offenses while expecting your spirit to remain unaffected. Love keeps your heart free to trust God without carrying the unnecessary weight of resentment.
  - Another catalyst is hearing God's Word. Romans 10:17 says, “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” There are moments when one particular Scripture resonates something inside you. You may have been worried about a situation for days, and suddenly, the Word brings clarity to your heart. The situation has not changed, but something has changed inside you. Your confidence increases because you have heard from God through His Word. Prayer also gives your faith room to breathe. Not every prayer needs to be about asking for something. Sometimes, you stay in God's presence until the noise surrounding a situation loses its hold on you. Jude 20 says, “But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost.” You come away from such moments with an inward strength that cannot always be explained by what is happening around you.
  - Pay attention to what keeps your faith alive. Feed yourself with the Word and give yourself to prayer. Keep your heart free from offenses that interfere with love. Faith was given to be lived out, and when the right spiritual influences are present, what you believe begins to show in the way you approach life. Keep the catalysts of faith active in your life.
prayer: My faith is alive and flourishing. God's Word continually strengthens my confidence in Him, and my heart remains full of His love. Through fellowship with the Holy Spirit, I am strengthened from within and ready to act on what I believe. My faith finds expression in my daily life and produces results that glorify Jesus Christ. Amen.
