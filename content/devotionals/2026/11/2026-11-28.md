title: What Is Your Definition Of Success?
scripture: Mark 8:36 (KJV)
verse: For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul?
body:
  - 'Success means different things to different people. For some, it is measured by money, position, influence, or how much they have achieved. The world often celebrates what can be seen, counted, and admired. But Jesus asks a powerful question: what is the value of gaining everything outwardly if your inner life is empty before God?'
  - Worldly success can make a person look accomplished while leaving the heart without peace. You can reach impressive goals and still be far from God's purpose for your life. That is why you must allow Scripture to shape your definition of success.
  - Godly success begins with walking in agreement with God. Joshua 1:8 says that as you meditate on the Word and observe to do what is written in it, you will make your way prosperous and have “good success.” God Himself distinguishes good success from mere achievement.
  - Good success is fulfilling the purpose of God for your life without losing your fellowship with Him along the way. It is becoming what He has called you to be while remaining faithful to His Word. You can prosper in your work and increase in influence, yet your greatest victory is that your life continues to please God.
  - Consider what you are pursuing. If achieving your goal requires you to compromise your convictions, the price is already too high. If success makes you too busy for God or causes you to neglect the people He has entrusted to you, something needs to be reconsidered.
  - Psalm 1 describes the person who delights in God's Word as a tree planted by rivers of water. That person flourishes because the foundation is right. Godly success grows from being rooted in Him. Let your ambitions be shaped by purpose rather than comparison. You were never called to copy someone else's definition of a successful life. Seek God's direction and build according to what He has placed in your heart.
  - Success is not simply reaching the top. True success is becoming who God designed you to be and faithfully completing what He has called you to do.
prayer: Father, thank You for giving my life purpose. I declare that my definition of success is shaped by Your Word. I prosper in what You have called me to do and remain faithful to You. My ambitions are aligned with Your purpose, and my life produces fruit that brings You glory. In Jesus' name, Amen.
