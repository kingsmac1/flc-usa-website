title: God’s Word Makes You Sagacious
scripture: Psalm 19:7 (KJV)
verse: 'The law of the LORD is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple.'
body:
  - God's Word has the power to make you sagacious. To be sagacious is to possess sound judgment and practical wisdom. It is the ability to recognize what is right and respond wisely. Psalm 19:7 says that the testimony of the Lord is sure, “making wise the simple.”
  - The word simple here is not describing someone who lacks intelligence. It speaks of the inexperienced or the person who can easily be influenced. Such a person may accept an idea simply because it sounds convincing or follow a direction without considering where it leads. God's Word develops discernment in you so that you are not easily swayed.
  - Proverbs 1:4 says that wisdom gives “subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.” As you give attention to Scripture, you become more thoughtful in your choices. You learn to recognize what agrees with God's ways and what should be avoided.
  - Wisdom is far more than gathering information. You can know many things and still make poor decisions. Biblical wisdom begins with reverence for God and expresses itself in the way you live. It gives you the understanding to choose what pleases Him even when another option may appear attractive. God's Word also gives you practical skill for everyday life. It trains your judgment when you are dealing with people or making important decisions. The more His Word fills your heart, the less likely you are to be controlled by pressure or popular opinion.
  - 'Psalm 119:98 says, “Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies: for they are ever with me.” There is wisdom available to you that does not depend on age or natural experience. God can make you unusually discerning through His Word. As the Word becomes established in you, wisdom will become increasingly evident in the way you live.'
prayer: Father, thank You for the wisdom of Your Word. I declare that I am discerning and sound in judgment. I recognize the right path and make wise decisions. My mind is trained by the Scriptures, and I am not easily swayed. I walk with understanding and apply Your wisdom faithfully in my daily life. In Jesus' name, Amen.
