date: '2026-11-09'
title: His Death Gave You A Better Life
scripture: John 3:16 (KJV)
verse: For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
body:
  - The death of Jesus was God's great expression of love toward you. Christ gave Himself so that you could receive a life far greater than the one humanity inherited through Adam. Through faith in Him, you have received eternal life; the very life of God.
  - John 3:16 says that whoever believes in Jesus has “everlasting life”. Eternal life is more than living forever; it is the quality of life that comes from God. You have been brought into fellowship with Him and given a new identity in Christ. His death opened the way for you to live as one who belongs to God.
  - 'Jesus also told His disciples, “It is expedient for you that I go away” (John 16:7). His departure would make way for the coming of the Holy Spirit. After His resurrection and ascension, the Spirit came to live in those who believe. Think about that: the life Christ purchased for you includes the indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit.'
  - The cross also brought you into right standing with God. Jesus bore what belonged to humanity so that you could receive what belongs to Him. Second Corinthians 5:21 says that we are made “the righteousness of God in him.” You can therefore approach God with confidence, knowing that your acceptance rests on what Christ accomplished.
  - Your life in Christ should never be viewed as merely an improved version of your old life. You are a new creation. You have been brought into a new relationship with God and given a new spiritual reality. Let this shape the way you see yourself. You are loved by God. You possess eternal life, and the Holy Spirit lives in you. Christ's finished work has given you a life filled with divine purpose and fellowship with the Father. Jesus gave His life so that you could truly live. His death gave you a better life.
prayer: Father, thank You for the life You have given me through Jesus Christ. I declare that I possess eternal life and enjoy fellowship with You. I am righteous in Christ, and the Holy Spirit lives in me. I walk daily in the reality of my new life in Christ. My life reflects the victory and purpose of His finished work. In Jesus' name, Amen.
furtherStudies: John 16:7; 2 Corinthians 5:17, 21; Romans 6:4
