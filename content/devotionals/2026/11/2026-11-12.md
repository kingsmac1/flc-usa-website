title: Priesthood For All Believers
scripture: 1 Peter 2:9 (KJV)
verse: But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light.
body:
  - Under the old covenant, priestly service was closely connected to the tribe of Levi. The priests ministered before God on behalf of the people, offering sacrifices and carrying out sacred duties. But through Jesus Christ, something glorious has taken place. Every believer now has direct access to God and has been brought into a royal priesthood.
  - You are not standing at a distance from God, waiting for someone else to approach Him for you. In Christ, you have been welcomed into His presence. You can pray and worship freely because Jesus has opened the way. Hebrews 13:15 says, “By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name.” Your priesthood finds expression when praise rises from your lips and when your heart remains available to God.
  - Romans 12:1 also speaks of presenting your body as “a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God.” Your life itself becomes an offering. The way you serve and the way you make yourself available for God's purpose matter to Him.
  - A priest also stands in the place of intercession. You can pray for your family and for others God places on your heart. You can carry the gospel to those who need to hear it, knowing that you have been entrusted with the message of reconciliation.
  - Do not become so occupied with life that your priestly calling is pushed aside. Your work and responsibilities matter, but your fellowship with God must remain central. Make room for prayer. Make room for worship. Stay available for what He wants to do through you. You are part of a royal priesthood. Live with that consciousness. You have access to God, and your life can continually bring Him honor.
prayer: Father, thank You for making me a royal priesthood. I declare that I live with the consciousness of my access to You through Christ. My life is available for Your purpose. Praise flows from my lips, and I serve You with a willing heart. I stand in prayer for others and faithfully represent You wherever I go. In Jesus' name, Amen.
