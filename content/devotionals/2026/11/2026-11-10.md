title: Lessons From Solomon
scripture: 1 Kings 3:12 (KJV)
verse: 'Behold, I have done according to thy words: lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart; so that there was none like thee before thee, neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.'
body:
  - 'Solomon asked God for wisdom, and God answered him generously. He became known for extraordinary judgment and understanding. People came from far away to hear him, and his wisdom helped him govern Israel with remarkable insight. Yet Solomon''s life also teaches an important lesson: having wisdom is not the same as consistently living by it.'
  - God gave Solomon a wise and understanding heart, but Solomon still had to choose what to do with what he knew. He could discern what was right, yet later in life, he made choices that weakened his devotion to God. First Kings 11 records that his many foreign wives turned his heart toward other gods. The man who had wisdom for a nation did not always apply that same wisdom to his personal life.
  - That should make you examine more than what you know. You may know the right scripture, understand good counsel, and clearly see what would be best, yet still choose another direction. Wisdom becomes fruitful when it moves from understanding into action.
  - James 1:22 says, “But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only.” Knowledge can warn you, but obedience is what keeps you on the right path. It takes strength of character to act on truth when your feelings, desires, or relationships are pulling you another way.
  - Ask yourself whether you are applying the wisdom God has already given you. Perhaps you are praying for direction about something He has already addressed through His Word. The issue may not be a lack of wisdom; it may be the courage to obey what you already know.
  - Do not admire wisdom from a distance. Practice it in your relationships, your finances, your decisions, and your private life. Let what God teaches you shape the way you actually live. Wisdom is a gift, but applying it is your responsibility.
prayer: Father, thank You for giving me wisdom through Your Word and Spirit. I declare that I do not merely know what is right; I act on it. My choices agree with Your truth, and I have the courage to obey even when obedience is difficult. I walk in wisdom and apply it faithfully to every area of my life. In Jesus' name, Amen.
