title: Learn To Build Your Relationships
scripture: Proverbs 17:17 (KJV)
verse: A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.
body:
  - Relationships are an important part of your life, and the book of Proverbs gives practical wisdom for handling them well. Whether you are relating with family, friends, or people you work with, the quality of those relationships will often reflect the wisdom you bring into them.
  - Good relationships require more than good intentions. You may genuinely care about someone and still need wisdom in the way you communicate with them. There are times when patience is necessary. At other times, wisdom may lead you to remain quiet rather than speak from emotion. Knowing how to respond can protect a relationship from unnecessary wounds.
  - 'Proverbs 15:1 says, “A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.” Your words have great influence on the atmosphere of your relationships. You can disagree with someone without becoming disrespectful. You can address a concern without attacking the person. The wisdom of God helps you handle difficult conversations with maturity.'
  - Consistency also matters. People should not have to wonder which version of you they will meet each day. Love becomes meaningful when it is dependable. Proverbs 17:17 says that a friend loves “at all times.” Genuine relationships grow when people know they can trust your character.
  - There will be occasions when someone disappoints you. Wisdom helps you respond from love rather than allowing one difficult moment to erase everything valuable about the relationship. Where forgiveness is needed, give it freely. Where a conversation is necessary, approach it with the desire to restore understanding.
  - Your relationship with God should influence the way you relate to people. As His Word gains greater influence in your heart, it should become visible in your conduct toward others. Your home should experience the wisdom you profess. The people who work with you should encounter godly character through the way you treat them.
  - Value the relationships God has placed in your life. Build them intentionally and allow His wisdom to guide the way you relate to others.
prayer: Father, thank You for the wisdom to build healthy relationships. I declare that my words minister grace, and my conduct reflects love. I am patient with people and faithful in my relationships. I walk in wisdom toward everyone around me, and my relationships flourish through the Godly character expressed in my life. In Jesus' name, Amen.
