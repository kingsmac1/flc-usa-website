date: '2026-11-13'
title: Reflecting God’s Standards
scripture: Philippians 2:13 (KJV)
verse: For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.
body:
  - Your relationship with God should become visible in the way you live. As you grow in fellowship with Him, His Word begins to shape your values, your decisions, and the way you respond to people. The life of Christ within you is meant to find expression in your daily conduct.
  - 'Philippians 2:13 gives you a powerful assurance: God is at work in you. He produces both the desire and the ability to do what pleases Him. The Holy Spirit works within your heart, helping you develop a genuine desire for what is right and giving you strength to walk in it.'
  - As your mind is renewed by the Word, your standards begin to change. Things you once accepted casually may no longer sit well with you. Your conscience becomes more sensitive, and your decisions begin to reflect a greater awareness of what honors God.
  - 1 Peter 1:15 says, “But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation.” The word conversation here speaks of your conduct or manner of life. God's character becomes the pattern you follow. His Word begins to influence how you speak, how you handle relationships, and how you conduct yourself when no one is watching.
  - A growing relationship with God will always produce evidence. The closer you walk with Him, the more His nature should become visible through you. His love should influence the way you treat others. His truth should govern your decisions. His holiness should shape the standards you choose to live by.
  - You are not trying to earn God's acceptance through good behavior. You already belong to Him in Christ. Your conduct becomes a response to the life He has placed within you. You live differently because you have been made new. Therefore, let your life reveal the One you belong to. Let your choices reflect His Word, and let your daily conduct become an expression of His character.
prayer: Father, thank You for working in me and producing the desire to please You. I declare that my life reflects Your standards. My thoughts agree with Your Word, and my choices honor You. The character of Christ is expressed through me, and I walk in holiness with joy. I yield myself to the Holy Spirit and live in a way that brings glory to Your name. In Jesus' name, Amen.
