title: God Is With You
scripture: 1 John 4:4 (KJV)
verse: 'Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.'
body:
  - One of the greatest assurances you can carry through life is the consciousness that God is with you. You may face situations that look bigger than your strength or circumstances that seem uncertain, but you are never facing them alone. The presence of God with you changes how you approach whatever stands before you.
  - '1 John 4:4 says, “Greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.” God has not simply promised to watch you from a distance. By His Spirit, He lives in you. The One who is with you is greater than every opposition you may encounter. This was the confidence behind David''s words in Psalm 27:1: “The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?” David did not deny that enemies existed. His confidence came from knowing who stood with him.'
  - Fear often grows when you focus only on what is against you. You look at the size of the problem, the strength of the opposition, or what you do not have, and suddenly you feel overwhelmed. Faith turns your attention back to God. It reminds you that His presence is greater than what is threatening you.
  - God being with you does not mean you will never encounter resistance. It means resistance does not have to intimidate you. You can walk into challenging situations knowing that God is your help. You can face uncertainty without surrendering to fear because His presence remains constant.
  - Become conscious of this truth throughout your day. Remember that God is with you. When you face a decision, remember that He is with you. When opposition tries to rise, remind yourself that you carry His presence. The best part of your journey is not that everything will always be easy. It is that God is with you through it all.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your abiding presence in my life. I declare that I am never alone. Greater is He who is in me than anything that comes against me. The Lord is my light, my salvation, and the strength of my life. I refuse fear and move forward with confidence because God is with me. In Jesus' name, Amen.
