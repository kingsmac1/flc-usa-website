title: Express Your Gratitude To God
scripture: 1 Samuel 11:15 (KJV)
verse: And all the people went to Gilgal; and there they made Saul king before the LORD in Gilgal; and there they sacrificed sacrifices of peace offerings before the LORD; and there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.
body:
  - Gratitude should have a visible place in your walk with God. It is not enough to recognize His goodness silently and move on. There are moments when you should deliberately stop, remember what He has done, and express your thanks to Him.
  - When Saul was confirmed as Israel's first king, the people gathered at Gilgal and offered peace offerings before the Lord. These offerings were connected with thanksgiving and fellowship with God. In the middle of a national celebration, they did not forget that God was still the true source of their help.
  - That is worth remembering. Success can easily draw your attention toward the people involved, the opportunity you received, or the result you achieved. Gratitude brings your heart back to God. It reminds you that every good thing in your life has ultimately come through His mercy and provision.
  - 'Psalm 107:1 says, “O give thanks unto the LORD, for he is good: for his mercy endureth forever.” Thanksgiving is not reserved only for the day everything goes exactly as you hoped. You can thank God because His goodness does not change with your circumstances.'
  - The Israelites' offerings were also significant. Although they had insisted on having a human king, their worship at Gilgal acknowledged that God remained above every earthly ruler. Their gratitude recognized His place in their lives. Sadly, that attitude does not always continue. You must guard against the same tendency. It is possible to seek God intensely when you need something and then become less conscious of Him after the answer comes. Do not allow blessings to make you forget the One who blessed you.
  - Make thanksgiving part of your daily life. Thank God for answered prayers, but also thank Him for His presence while you are still waiting. Let gratitude become an act of worship that keeps your heart humble and conscious of His goodness. Never become so familiar with God's blessings that you stop saying, “Thank You, Lord.”
prayer: Father, thank You for Your goodness and faithfulness in my life. I choose to live with a grateful heart. I recognize You as the source of every good thing I enjoy. My blessings will never make me forget You. My heart remains thankful, and my life will continually honor You with praise and gratitude. In Jesus' name, Amen.
