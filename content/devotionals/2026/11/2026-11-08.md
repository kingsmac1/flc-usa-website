title: He Never Forsakes You
scripture: Psalm 9:10 (KJV)
verse: 'And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee: for thou, LORD, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.'
body:
  - There is a confidence that grows in your heart when you truly know God. Psalm 9:10 connects knowing His name with trusting Him. The more you know His character, the more settled your heart becomes in His faithfulness. You discover that God is dependable and that those who seek Him are never abandoned.
  - To seek God is to turn your heart toward Him and desire His presence in your life. It is reaching for Him sincerely and allowing His Word to shape the way you think and live. Deuteronomy 4:29 says, “Thou shalt find him, if thou seek him with all thy heart and with all thy soul.” God welcomes the heart that genuinely desires fellowship with Him.
  - You find Him in His Word and in prayer. Fellowship with other believers also strengthens your walk with Him. Most importantly, Jesus reveals the Father to us. Jesus said in John 14:9, “He that hath seen me hath seen the Father.” Through Christ, you come to know the love of God and enjoy a living relationship with Him.
  - 'Psalm 9:10 gives you a great assurance: “Thou, Lord, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.” God remains with you. His faithfulness is steady, even when you are waiting for an answer or walking through a season you do not fully understand. His presence is constant.'
  - Knowing this brings stability to your heart. You can seek Him with confidence because you are coming to a Father who loves you and delights in fellowship with you. Every time you open His Word or spend time in prayer, you are nurturing a relationship with the One who has promised to remain with you.
  - Let seeking God become a delight rather than something you do only when you need help. Desire His presence because He is worthy of your heart. The more you know Him, the more confidently you will trust Him. He never forsakes those who seek Him.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your unfailing presence in my life. I declare that my heart continually seeks You, and my confidence rests in Your faithfulness. I know Your name and trust You completely. I am never forsaken; Your presence remains with me. I grow daily in fellowship with You and enjoy the security of belonging to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
