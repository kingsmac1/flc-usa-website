title: The Power of Sacrifice
scripture: 1 Samuel 7:9 (KJV)
verse: And Samuel took a sucking lamb, and offered it for a burnt offering wholly unto the LORD…
body:
  - Iwould like you to observe the power of what Samuel did for Israel. In a moment of crisis, he didn't turn to strategy, negotiation, or manpower; he offered a sacrifice. Immediately, Scripture records that “the LORD thundered with a great thunder” against the Philistines (1 Samuel 7:10). Sacrifice provoked divine intervention. It shifted the situation in Israel's favor.
  - Sacrifice, by definition, means giving up something valuable out of reverence, obedience, or devotion to God. In the Bible, sacrifice often stands as a spiritual act that opens the door for God's response. Consider Numbers 16:46–48, where Aaron stood between the living and the dead with incense (an offering) and the plague was stopped. The sacrifice became the bridge between destruction and deliverance.
  - A burnt offering, as described in Leviticus 1:9, is consumed entirely on the altar. It represents complete surrender; nothing withheld. This is the depth of devotion Samuel demonstrated. God responds to such total yielding, because it reflects a heart that places Him above all else.
  - This principle continues into the New Testament. Paul writes in
  - 'Romans 12:1, “Present your bodies a living sacrifice…” This reveals the true essence of sacrifice today: a life wholly offered to God; our will, our time, our desires, our obedience. It is not the object on the altar, but the heart behind the offering.'
  - Gideon demonstrated this when he offered a burnt offering at God's instruction before confronting Midian (Judges 6:26). Sacrifice preceded empowerment. Consecration came before victory. His act of obedience positioned him with divine strength. Sacrifice remains a spiritual force. It breaks resistance and redirects destinies. When you lay something valuable before God (your time, resources, comfort, or will) you position yourself for His move. Make up your mind to live as one wholly surrendered.
prayer: Dear Father, I offer myself completely to You. Let my life be a true and living sacrifice, wholly surrendered, fully yielded, and devoted to Your will. As I walk in obedience and consecration, let Your power work mightily in my life, in Jesus' name. Amen.
