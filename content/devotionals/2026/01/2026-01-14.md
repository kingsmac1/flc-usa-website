date: '2026-01-14'
title: Set Your Goals
scripture: Habakkuk 2:2 (NKJV)
verse: Write the vision and make it plain on tablets, that he may run who reads it.
body:
  - Goal setting simply means defining your desired future with clarity and vision. It is the intentional act of putting structure to your expectations so your life doesn't move in circles. Many people hope for change, but hope alone does not create direction. Hallelujah! When you set goals, you partner with God by giving shape to the desires He places within your spirit. It is a spiritual and practical step toward purposeful living.
  - Goal setting helps in providing focus. Instead of scattering energy on everything, your heart becomes positioned with specific pursuits. James 1:8 (KJV) says, “A double-minded man is unstable in all his ways.” When your goals are clear, you operate with singleness of purpose. You begin to prioritize, plan, and act with intention. Glory to God! This is how excellence is born; not by accident, but through targeted direction.
  - It's important to have your goals set for the year already, but if not, it is still not late to get things straightened out. God is a Redeemer of times and seasons. As you set your goals, you create a roadmap for the year, a picture that guides your prayer life, decisions, and habits. The Bible reminds us in Proverbs 16:3 (ESV), “Commit your work to the Lord, and your plans will be established”. Goal setting is not a worldly idea; it is a kingdom principle backed by grace. Amen!
  - As you visualize your goals, they move from mere thoughts into desires. Desire is powerful. It fuels passion, consistency, and faith. Psalm 37:4 (NKJV) says, “Delight yourself also in the Lord, and He shall give you the desires of your heart.” But if there are no desires, no goals, no expectations, there is nothing for God to grant. Visualization turns your goals into living expectations your spirit can pursue. Hallelujah!
  - Therefore, take time today to write your goals, pray over them, and place them before the Lord. Keep them where your eyes can see them and your heart can meditate on them. As you do, the Holy Spirit will guide, strengthen, and empower you to bring them to fruition. Step into the year with clarity, vision, and faith. Glory to God! Your best is ahead.
prayer: Father, in the name of Jesus, I commit my goals, dreams, and plans for the new year into Your hands. Thank You for You are always guiding my steps, inspiring my desires, and granting me wisdom to pursue my goals diligently. This year, I walk in precision, focus, direction, and divine success. Amen!
