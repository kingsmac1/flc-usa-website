title: Energized On The Inside
scripture: Jude 1:20 (KJV)
verse: But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost.
body:
  - Every believer is called to live from a place of spiritual vitality; a strength that comes from the Holy Spirit working inside. Paul prayed in Ephesians 3:16 that we would be “strengthened with might by His Spirit in the inner man.” This is not human motivation or positive thinking. It is divine energy rising from your spirit, giving you courage, clarity and power, that circumstances cannot shake. God designed the Christian life to be lived from the inside out, drawing strength from His Spirit at work within you.
  - Samson understood this inner empowerment. Scripture says, “I will go out as at other times before, and shake myself” (Judges 16:20). That physical action reflected a spiritual truth; he was stirring the power that God had placed within him. David also walked in this same inner energy. Standing before Goliath, he declared, “The Lord that delivered me out of the paw of the lion… He will deliver me out of the hand of this Philistine” (1 Samuel 17:37). His boldness didn't come from natural ability. It came from the Holy Spirit energizing him on the inside, giving him confidence to do what others feared.
  - In the New Testament, our way of stirring this inner strength is different. Paul told Timothy, “Stir up the gift of God, which is in
  - thee” (2 Timothy 1:6). We don't shake ourselves physically like Samson did. Instead, Scripture shows us how to awaken this spiritual energy by praying in the Spirit. Jude writes, “building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost” (Jude 1:20). The phrase “building up” speaks of charging, like recharging a drained battery. When your phone runs out of power, you connect it to a source. In the same way, when you feel weak, discouraged or overwhelmed, praying in the Spirit reconnects you to divine strength.
  - 'This truth is echoed again in 1 Corinthians 14:4, which says, “He that speaketh in an unknown tongue edifieth himself.” Praying in the Spirit lifts heaviness, dissolves fear and stirs up boldness. It gives fresh energy, sharper discernment and renewed confidence. Challenges that once seemed impossible become manageable because your inner man has been fortified. This is how God wants us to live: continually energized on the inside by the Holy Spirit. Whenever pressure rises or strength feels low, don''t look outward. Charge up your spirit by praying in the Holy Ghost, and rise in the divine might God has placed within you.'
prayer: Father, thank You for strengthening me with might in my inner man. As I pray in the Spirit, I am refreshed, empowered and energized from within. I walk in boldness and victory every day In Jesus' name, amen.
