title: Show Your Family God’s Ways
scripture: Joshua 24:15 KJV
verse: But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.
body:
  - 'Iwould like you to observe Joshua''s firm declaration concerning his home. He didn''t merely speak for himself; he spoke for his household. This reveals a profound truth: your family should not be left without spiritual direction. God designed the home to be the first place where His ways are taught, lived, and demonstrated. What you model to your family becomes the foundation upon which they build their future in God.'
  - There are believers, especially pastors, ministers, and church leaders, who pour themselves into ministry, raising others for God, yet unintentionally neglect their own families. They build the church but lose their children. They train disciples but leave their own homes spiritually empty. This is not God's design. Ministry begins at home. Your first assignment is your family. As Paul admonished, “For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?” (1 Timothy 3:5 KJV).
  - Many times, this neglect happens because of emotions; parents hesitate to discipline, instruct, or firmly guide their
  - 'children. But emotions don''t train leaders. God''s ways must be taught intentionally, consistently, and lovingly. Remember the Lord''s instruction to Abraham: “For I know him, that he will command his children and his household after him, and they shall keep the way of the LORD…” (Genesis 18:19).'
  - Also, do not neglect praying for your family. Prayer establishes, strengthens, and shields them. Pray over their minds, their paths, their associations, and their destinies. Speak God's Word over them. Declare their alignment to God's purpose. When you commit your family to God in prayer, you build spiritual walls that preserve them.
  - Let your life be an example your family can follow. Model devotion, integrity, love, and commitment to God. Teach them the Word. Lead them in prayer. Instruct them in righteousness. Let your home become a sanctuary of God's presence where His ways are known, honored, and lived out daily.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for placing my family under my care. I will lead them in Your ways, instructing them in righteousness and truth. Your spirit will continue to help me to be an example of devotion, wisdom, and love. I commit my household to You, declaring that we walk in Your purpose, strength, and glory, in Jesus' mighty name. Amen.
