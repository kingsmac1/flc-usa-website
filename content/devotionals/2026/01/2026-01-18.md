title: Understanding The Will Of God
scripture: Ephesians 5:17 (KJV)
verse: Be ye not unwise, but understanding what the will of the Lord is.
body:
  - Iwould like you to observe that God expects you to understand His will, not guess it. The will of God is not a mysterious puzzle hidden from His children; it is revealed through His Word and made clear by His Spirit. Many believers struggle unnecessarily, thinking God's will is difficult to discern, but Scripture teaches the opposite. God wants you to know His will so you can walk in it with confidence and boldness.
  - Romans 12:2 explains that you become aligned with God's will through the renewing of your mind. This means exposing your thoughts to the Word until your thinking becomes synchronized with God's thoughts. When your mind is renewed, you no longer interpret life through fear, pressure, or human logic; you interpret it through the lens of Scripture. A renewed mind naturally gravitates toward God's perfect will.
  - Understanding God's will also requires a heart that is yielded. Psalm 25:9 says, “The meek will he guide in judgment.” A proud heart struggles to receive guidance, but a humble and teachable spirit easily recognizes God's direction. God's will is often clear, but our willingness is what determines whether we see it. Sensitivity to the Spirit begins with surrender.
  - God's will is revealed through His Word, confirmed by His Spirit, and established by His peace. Colossians 3:15 says, “Let the peace of God rule in your hearts.” Peace is a divine umpire; it signals approval or disapproval. When your decisions align with God's will, peace follows. When you step outside of His will, peace withdraws. Learn to pay attention to that inward witness.
  - Understanding God's will also means understanding your identity and purpose. Ephesians 2:10 declares that you are created for “good works” prepared beforehand. This means God's will is connected to your assignment. You discover His will as you discover who you are in Him. The more you grow spiritually, the clearer His will becomes.
  - Choose today to pursue God's will with diligence. Make His Word your compass, His Spirit your guide, and His peace your confirmation. You will walk in clarity, wisdom, and assurance as you align with His perfect will.
prayer: Dear Father, thank You for granting me an understanding of Your will. As I renew my mind with Your Word and yield to Your Spirit, I walk in divine precision and your purpose. Your peace guides me, and Your wisdom establishes my steps, in Jesus' name. Amen.
