title: Redeemed From The Curse Of The Law
scripture: Galatians 3:13-14 TPT
verse: …Jesus Christ dissolved the curse from our lives, so that in him, all the blessings of Abraham can be poured out upon Gentiles.
body:
  - To be redeemed from the curse of the law means Jesus stepped into our place and took the full weight of sin's penalty so we could walk free. The curse of the law carried consequences for disobedience, including judgment, sickness, poverty, failure and defeat. But Christ did not partially redeem us. Scripture says He “paid the full price to set us free” and “absorbed the curse completely” (Galatians 3:13 TPT). Nothing was left hanging over us. Nothing remained for the enemy to legally accuse us of. Redemption means the curse has no authority in the life of anyone who belongs to Christ.
  - Under the old covenant, man's failure brought consequences. But under the new covenant, Jesus bore those consequences. When Scripture says He “became a curse in our place” (Galatians 3:13 TPT), it means He took what was ours so we could receive what was His. He exchanged our brokenness for His blessing, our bondage for His freedom, our lack for His abundance and our weakness for His strength. Everything the curse produced, Jesus reversed at the cross. Redemption is not a theory; it is a divine reality.
  - This means sickness is no longer your portion. Poverty is no longer your inheritance. Failure is not your destiny. Jesus has redeemed you from the things that break, limit, weaken and destroy. He redeemed you to prosper, to excel, to walk in health and to experience God's goodness in every area of life. Because the curse has been dissolved, the blessing can now flow freely. God didn't just remove the negative; He replaced it with His best.
  - Not only are we redeemed, we are repositioned. “And if you belong to Christ, then you are now Abraham's child and a true heir of all his blessings” (Galatians 3:29 TPT). This means the same blessings God spoke over Abraham (increase, protection, favor, multiplication and divine guidance) now rest on your life. You are not striving for blessing; you are an heir of blessing. It belongs to you because you belong to Christ.
  - Redemption is your foundation and blessing is your inheritance. Walk in it boldly. Live in it confidently. Jesus paid for nothing to be missing, lacking or broken in your life.
prayer: Father, I thank You that Christ has redeemed me from the curse of the law. I declare that the blessing of Abraham rests on me. I walk in health, provision, protection and success. The curse has no power over my life. I am blessed, empowered and free in Jesus' name. Amen.
