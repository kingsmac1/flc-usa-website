title: A Life Of Service
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:18 (KJV)
verse: And Samuel ministered before the LORD, even as a child, girded with a linen ephod.
body:
  - Samuel's early life shows us the beauty and importance of service. As a boy, he served Eli the priest faithfully by opening the Tabernacle doors, cleaning the furniture, and assisting with sacrifices. Though these tasks may seem ordinary or small, God valued Samuel's faithfulness in them. Serving others is not about prestige or recognition but about honoring God through obedience in every role. When we serve others, we serve God Himself, and every act of service carries dignity and eternal significance.
  - True impact and lasting blessings come through faithful service over time, not quick actions or occasional giving. The example of Elisha serving Elijah, and Joshua services to Moses, shows that consistent service builds character, wisdom, and spiritual maturity. These relationships were shaped by years of humble dedication and resulted in lasting spiritual fruit. The blessings that come from such service don't fade; they create a foundation for God's ongoing work in our lives and communities.
  - Service is more than an action; it's a lifestyle. When we serve with a willing heart, God strengthens us, guides us, and opens
  - doors. The Lord honors those who serve with humility, regardless of how small or routine the tasks may seem. Jesus modeled this perfectly when He washed His disciples' feet and called His followers to serve one another in love. Serving others helps us reflect Christ's character and brings His presence into our everyday lives.
  - Serving blesses both the servant and those served. It builds relationships, strengthens community, and reveals God's power. Every act of service becomes an opportunity for God to work through us. When we serve faithfully, we position ourselves with God's purposes and position ourselves to receive His blessings and guidance.
  - A life committed to service prepares us for God's greater plans. Let us embrace service joyfully and diligently, knowing that in serving others, we honor God and participate in His redemptive work.
prayer: Father, I thank you for the Spirit enables me to serve with joy and faithfulness. He teaches me to see every task as important because serving others is serving You. I'm Strengthened to be faithful in small things and to reflect Your love through my service. In Jesus' name, Amen.
