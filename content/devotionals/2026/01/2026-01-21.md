date: '2026-01-21'
title: Lift Up Your Eyes and SEE
scripture: Genesis 13:14–15 (KJV)
verse: And the LORD said unto Abram, after that Lot was separated from him, Lift up now thine eyes, and look… For all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed for ever.
body:
  - When God told Abram to lift up his eyes, He was calling him to see beyond his present situation. Abram had just separated from Lot, and though the separation could have been discouraging, God used that moment to redirect his vision. The instruction to “look” was not simply physical; it was spiritual. God was activating Abram's imagination so he could picture the future God intended for him. In the same way, God invites us to lift our eyes above the noise of life and envision the future He has already prepared.
  - Renewing your mind is a critical part of this process. Your imagination will either be shaped by God's Word or by the limitations of your past experiences. When you give yourself fully to Scripture, your inner vision becomes aligned with divine reality. You begin to see possibilities instead of obstacles, promise instead of pressure, and destiny instead of delay. It truly matters what you see because your life often moves in the direction of your inner picture.
  - God instructed Abram to look north, south, east, and west, symbolizing unlimited potential. He was teaching Abram that there are no boundaries to what He can give when a believer
  - sees through the eyes of faith. In your own life, God wants you to lift your eyes and see your future with expectation. See your new year filled with progress, your destiny unfolding, and your greatness emerging by His grace. When your vision expands, your capacity to receive increases.
  - 'In biblical times, wealth was often measured by land. The more land a person possessed, the greater their influence and stability. By showing Abram the vastness of the land, God was revealing the magnitude of His promise. In Christ, you too have been granted access to spiritual inheritance: blessing, purpose, and divine direction. But you must see it before you step into it. Vision prepares your heart for manifestation.'
  - Lift your eyes today. Refuse to remain confined by what is visible. Embrace what God shows you through His Word, and walk boldly toward the future He has spoken over your life.
prayer: Lord, I lift my eyes and align my vision with Your Word. I declare my imagination is opened to see the great future You have prepared. I receive clarity, expansion, and divine direction. My eyes are set on Your promises, and I walk in them boldly. In Jesus' name, Amen.
