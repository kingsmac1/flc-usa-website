title: Create Your New Year
scripture: Job 22:28 (KJV)
verse: 'Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways.'
body:
  - At the dawn of a new year, many people step in with uncertainty, saying things like, “Let's see what the year will bring” or “I hope things get better.” But glory to God, the year is not an unpredictable force that shapes itself; rather, you shape your year with the words you speak. Without intentional declaration, life tends to repeat patterns that are neither desirable nor aligned with God's plan. Hallelujah!
  - From the beginning, God models this truth for us. Scripture says, “And God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light” (Genesis 1:3 NKJV). The earth was formless and void, yet God didn't sit back to “see what would happen.” He created outcomes by declaring His will. You are made in His likeness, and the Word says, “As He is, so are we in this world” (1 John 4:17 NKJV). This means you share in His creative ability. Amen!
  - 'God has given you dominion: authority to shape experiences and influence seasons. The Bible affirms, “The heavens are the Lord''s heavens, but the earth he has given to the children of man” (Psalm 115:16 ESV). This is your jurisdiction. The beginning of a new year is not a passive transition but an invitation to command the atmosphere of your destiny.'
  - Hallelujah! It is the moment to speak life, order, increase, peace, and favor into your days.
  - 'Therefore, rise and create your year. Declare boldly: “This year is blessed for me. It is my best year yet. I walk in health, peace, and divine wisdom. No failure or misfortune comes near me. I will walk in the fullness of God''s will.” These are not empty words; they are spiritual seeds that establish divine realities. As you speak, you partner with God to shape a glorious year.'
  - Finally, keep declaring throughout the year. Your words build walls of protection, open doors of grace, and set your steps with divine purpose. Step into this year as a creator, not a spectator. Glory to God. Amen!
prayer: Father, in the name of Jesus, I decree that this year aligns with Your perfect will for my life. I declare increase, favor, health, and divine direction. Every day of this year is blessed for me. I walk in supernatural victory, and everything works together for my good. Amen!
