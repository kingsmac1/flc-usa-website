title: Focus on Your Identity In Christ
scripture: Galatians 2:20 (NIV)
verse: I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me…
body:
  - The fight of faith begins with identity. A believer's greatest strength is knowing who they are in Christ and refusing to let circumstances, emotions, or societal labels redefine them. When Scripture declares that you have been crucified with Christ, it means the old identity, shaped by past failures, limitations, and earthly labels, no longer has authority. Your new life is spiritual, empowered, and rooted in Christ Himself. Faith holds tightly to this truth even when outward conditions seem to reflect the opposite.
  - Identity in Christ is not a feeling; it is a spiritual reality. Ephesians 2:6 tells us that God has already raised us up and seated us with Christ in heavenly places. This means your position is one of victory, authority, and dominion, even if your present situation hasn't caught up yet. Faith does not look at what surrounds you; faith looks at what Christ has done for you. When you know where you are seated, you stop living like someone defeated.
  - 'Circumstances will try to challenge your identity. Life may present contradictions: weakness in the place where God calls you strong, lack in the place where He calls you supplied, or'
  - delay in the place where He calls you victorious. But the fight of faith is not about proving your identity; it is about refusing to surrender it. You hold fast to your rights in Christ regardless of what the natural realm suggests. Your identity is anchored in truth, not trends.
  - This is why it is dangerous to let society describe you. The world defines people by background, appearance, statistics, and limitations. But God defines you by Christ, redeemed, loved, empowered, and seated with Him. When you focus on the wrong voices, your faith becomes weakened. When you focus on who you are in Christ, your faith becomes unshakeable. Your identity determines your expectations, and your expectations shape your manifestations.
  - Fix your eyes on Christ. Let His Word be the mirror that shapes your confidence. When you know who you are in Him, no situation can keep you small, silent, or defeated.
prayer: Lord, my identity is in Christ alone. I refuse every label, lie, and limitation that contradict Your Word. I hold fast to my rights, authority, and inheritance in Christ. I stand confident, victorious, and unshaken. Amen.
