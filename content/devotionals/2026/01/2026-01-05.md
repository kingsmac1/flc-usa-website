---
date: '2026-01-05'
title: Don’t Bother About Your Adversaries
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:10 (KJV)
verse: 'The adversaries of the Lord shall be broken to pieces; out of heaven shall he thunder upon them: the Lord shall judge the ends of the earth.'
body:
  - The word adversary refers to anyone who opposes, resists, or challenges you. In life, we all encounter opposition, but as children of God, the way we deal with adversaries is different. We are purchased by the precious blood of Jesus and are treasured possessions in His sight. This means anyone who fights against you, opposes you, or seeks your downfall is ultimately opposing God Himself. When you face challenges, it is important to remember that the Lord fights for you. The battle is His, and He is faithful to always make you triumphant.
  - God's power is far greater than any opposition. 1 Samuel 2:10 reveals to us that the adversaries of the Lord will be broken. No matter how strong or numerous they seem, those who rise against God's children cannot prevail. You are valuable, chosen, and protected, and your life is under divine authority. When adversaries come, focus on God's power rather than human strength, because victory comes from Him alone.
  - Even when enemies surround you like a camp, you will not be moved. Psalm 35:1–2 (NKJV) declares, “Contend, O Lord, with
  - those who contend with me; fight against those who fight against me. Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise up to help me.” This shows that God intervenes on behalf of His children. Trusting Him positions you to experience His protection and deliverance, even in the midst of conflict.
  - Focusing on your adversaries can lead to fear and distraction, but focusing on God brings peace and confidence. You are secure in Christ, and nothing can separate you from His love or thwart His plans for your life. The Lord honors those who trust Him fully, and He will handle all who oppose you. Remember, your strength is in Him, not in your own abilities.
  - Living in this truth shapes your perspective. Instead of worrying about your oppositions, you can stand firm, confident, and unshaken. God fights for you, and His judgment against your adversaries is certain. Your obedience and faith in Him position you to walk in victory and experience His favor daily. Release all concerns and anxiety about those who oppose you. Let God take the lead in every conflict, knowing that He is your defender. When you trust Him completely, you will walk unshaken, victorious, and secure in His love.
prayer: Father, I thank You for being my shield and protecting me from my adversaries. Thank you for being my light and my salvation. I declare that Your strength surrounds me and Your victory prevails over every situation, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
