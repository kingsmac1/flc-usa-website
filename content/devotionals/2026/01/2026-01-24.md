title: Free From Condemnation
scripture: Romans 8:1–2 (KJV)
verse: There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit. For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.
body:
  - When we receive salvation, we are ushered into a new reality; one where condemnation no longer defines us. Through Christ, the verdict over our lives shifts from “guilty” to “righteous.” This freedom, however, is not a license to indulge the flesh but an invitation to walk in the Spirit. The believer who chooses the Spirit's leading experiences the fullness of this liberty, living beyond the reach of guilt, shame, and fear. God's desire is not to condemn His children but to lead them into the abundant life Christ purchased.
  - 'Yet even as believers, when we yield to fleshly desires, something within us responds: our conscience. God placed within every person an inner witness that alerts us when our steps drift from His will. This is not condemnation but conviction; an act of love that draws us back to alignment with God. Conviction prompts repentance, restores fellowship, and helps us grow. The conscience, when trained by the Word and sensitive to the Spirit, becomes a precious guide in our walk with God.'
  - 'Alongside this, we face a spiritual adversary: the devil, the “accuser of the brethren.” He seeks to attach guilt to forgiven sins, replaying past failures to weaken our confidence in God. But Scripture instructs us to resist him. When we stand firm in the truth of our forgiveness and righteousness in Christ, his accusations lose their power. The believer''s victory is not in arguing with the enemy but in affirming what God has already declared.'
  - 'However, there is a sober warning: choosing to habitually and deliberately live after the flesh leads to spiritual decay. Scripture calls this iniquity: willful, unrepentant sin. God is merciful, but He is also just. A lifestyle that continually rejects His authority ultimately leads to separation from Him. The believer must therefore guard their walk, choosing daily to yield to the Spirit rather than to the cravings of the flesh.'
  - In Christ, you are free from condemnation, but that freedom flourishes when you walk in step with the Spirit, embrace God's correction, reject the enemy's accusations, and live righteously with a sincere heart. This is the path that leads to confidence, holiness, and eternal fellowship with God.
