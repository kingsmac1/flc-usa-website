title: Regulated By God’s Love
scripture: John 3:16 (NKJV)
verse: For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.
body:
  - God's love, as seen in John 3:16, comes from the Greek word agape, which means love that is wholehearted, constant, and unconditional. It is the kind of love that gives without holding back, the kind that keeps loving even when the other person doesn't deserve it. Human love, on the other hand, is often based on feelings; it changes with situations and emotions. But glory to God, His love never fluctuates. God's love is steady, pure, and dependable. Hallelujah!
  - Human love thinks and measures. It says, “I love you because…” or “I love you when…” But God's love simply says, “I love you.” 1 Corinthians 13:8 (KJV) highlights that “Love never fails.” Human love gets tired, gets offended, and sometimes, gives up. But God's love remains strong even when we fall short. This is why agape is the highest kind of love; it reaches beyond feelings and touches the heart.
  - Many people say, “I will forgive, but I can't forget.” But that voice is not agape speaking; that is human love still carrying wounds. God's love does not keep a record of wrongs. When you walk in agape, you forgive from your heart and trust God to heal the
  - old memories. If you still struggle in this area, simply pray, “Lord, remove the hurt and heal the old memories.” He is faithful. His love can soften any heart. Hallelujah!
  - Romans 5:5 NKJV says - “The love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy Spirit.” Notice the phrase 'poured out', not sprinkled. When you received the Holy Spirit, God filled your heart with more than enough love. Glory to God! You don't have to beg for love or struggle to love others. The love you need is already inside you; overflowing, abundant, and available for every situation.
  - Therefore, let your life be regulated by God's love. Let agape guide your reactions, conversations, relationships, and daily choices. When you respond with patience, kindness, mercy, and compassion, you reflect the heart of Jesus. And as you walk in this love, your life will shine brighter, your heart will stay lighter, and your relationships will become healthier. What a blessing to live in the flow of God's love. Glory to God, amen!
prayer: Father, thank You for pouring Your love into my heart. I live by Your love every day. The Holy Spirit helps me forgive easily, care deeply, and respond with patience and kindness. Your love guides my thoughts, my actions, and my relationships in Jesus' name, Amen! FURTHER
