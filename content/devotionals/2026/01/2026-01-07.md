title: Get Your Priorities Right
scripture: Matthew 6:33 (NKJV)
verse: But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you.
body:
  - Getting your priorities right simply means putting first things first. It means deciding what truly matters and giving your time, attention, and energy to those things. Life can easily pull you in many directions, but thank be unto God, you don't have to live scattered or overwhelmed. When your priorities are clear, your days become more meaningful, and your choices become easier. Hallelujah!
  - One important step in living wisely is knowing what should carry the most weight in your life. Psalm 90:12 (NKJV) says, “Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom.” This reminds us that life is precious, and we must use it well. When you choose what truly helps you grow (spiritually, mentally, and emotionally), you begin to live with purpose rather than pressure. Amen!
  - Getting your priorities right also brings peace. Many people feel stressed not because they have too much to do but because they are trying to do the wrong things first. As the Bible tells us, “Let all things be done decently and in order” (1 Corinthians 14:40 KJV). Order brings calmness. When your day starts with the things that feed your spirit and support your well-being
  - (prayer, the Word, healthy habits, meaningful relationships), you will notice a deep sense of steadiness.
  - Having clear priorities improves how you use your time. Instead of rushing or constantly feeling behind, you begin to move with a steady rhythm. You know what to focus on and what can wait. You stop trying to please everyone, and you start doing what truly matters for your growth. Hallelujah! Priorities help you make choices that support your goals and your peace of mind.
  - As you continue into this year, take a moment to look at where your time and attention are going. Put God first, take care of your purpose and health, and let everything else fall into its proper place. When your priorities are right, life becomes lighter, clearer, and more joyful. Glory to God. Amen!
prayer: Thank you, Lord, for your word I have received. Thank you for the Holy Spirit who sets my heart in the right place. The holy spirit teaches me to choose what matters and to use my time wisely. I receive grace to stay focused, peaceful, and steady every day. Amen!
