title: Sing Praises to God
scripture: Psalm 92:1 (NKJV)
verse: It is good to give thanks to the Lord, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High.
body:
  - 'Praise is the believer''s joyful response to who God is and all He has done. When Hannah received her long- awaited son, she didn''t simply celebrate the blessing; she celebrated the God who gave it. Her words in 1 Samuel 2:1 (NIV) declare, “My heart rejoices in the Lord.” Her focus wasn''t on the gift but on the Giver. That is the heart of true praise: lifting our eyes above circumstances to honor the One who holds every moment in His hands.'
  - 'Mary also demonstrated this heart of worship. When God chose her to carry Jesus, she responded with a song: “My soul glorifies the Lord, and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior” (Luke 1:46–47, CSB). Even though she faced uncertainty, her praise flowed freely. Like Mary, we can choose to praise God not only after answers come, but right in the middle of unknowns. Praise strengthens faith. Praise steadies the heart. Praise invites God''s peace into our journey.'
  - Scripture calls us into this lifestyle of worship. Psalm 34:1 (NIV) says, “I will bless the Lord at all times; his praise will always be on my lips.” “At all times” means both in blessing and in hardship. Praise lifts us above worry and reminds us of God's
  - unchanging character. When we sing praises, our perspective shifts; we begin to see God's faithfulness instead of fear, His provision instead of pressure, His presence instead of problems.
  - Singing praises to God also keeps our hearts tender and grateful. Every good thing we experience, big or small, comes from His generous hand. When we intentionally give thanks and exalt Him, we stay spiritually awake and sensitive to His goodness. Praise is not just a moment of worship; it becomes a lifestyle of honoring God in all things.
  - Today, let your heart rise in worship. Sing to the Lord because He is faithful. Sing because He is good. Sing because His love never fails. When your praise goes up, faith grows strong and hope becomes alive. God is worthy yesterday, today, and forever.
prayer: Father, you are great, I lift my voice in praise. You are good, faithful, and worthy of all honor. Stir my heart to worship You daily and to recognize all you do in every area of my life. My soul blesses Your holy name in Jesus' name, amen.
