title: Requesting The Right Thing
scripture: Romans 12:2 (KJV)
verse: 'And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect will of God.'
body:
  - Knowing God's perfect will is essential for every believer. It is not enough to simply pray or make requests; your requests must align with what God wants. This is why you must know the Word and remain spiritually sensitive, so that you can discern His perfect will rather than be moved by emotions, pressures, or carnal desires.
  - 'Many believers ask for things that God never intended for them. They pray from impulse, comparison, fear, or desire, instead of alignment. God wants His children to desire the right things: the things that fit His purpose, His timeline, His wisdom. There is a difference between asking God for what you want and asking God for what He wants. His choices are always superior because He sees the end from the beginning (Isaiah 46:10).'
  - Israel illustrates this truth vividly. They wanted a king, and they had reasons. Samuel's sons were unfit to lead. The tribes struggled to unite. They believed a king would solve political and military problems. And most importantly, they wanted to be like other nations. But this was exactly the issue. Their request arose from comparison, pressure, and carnal observation and not from God's perfect will.
  - God had already foreseen the possibility of Israel requesting a king (Deuteronomy 17:14–20), so the idea itself was not sinful. The problem was the motive; they were rejecting God as their leader (1 Samuel 8:7). They wanted human strength in place of divine leadership. They wanted a visible monarchy instead of a relationship. Their request was permitted, but it was not God's perfect desire.
  - This is why believers must be careful in prayer. Ask for what God wants, and not merely what seems reasonable, popular, or comforting. His will always leads to life, peace, and long-term blessing. When you request the right things, God is pleased because it's according to his purpose.
prayer: 'Dear Father, I thank you for you are at work in me to desire Your perfect will above all else. Align my heart with Your purposes and purify my motives. Teach me to request the right things: those that agree with Your wisdom, Your timing, and Your plan for my life. I submit my desires to You, and I trust Your choices, in Jesus'' name. Amen.'
