title: Follow God’s Directives
scripture: 1 Samuel 7:2–3
verse: 'If ye do return unto the LORD with all your hearts, then put away the strange gods… and prepare your hearts unto the LORD, and serve him only: and he will deliver you…'
body:
  - 'For twenty long years, Israel mourned. The nation was overwhelmed with sorrow, defeat, and spiritual dryness. The Ark, the symbol of God''s presence, was set aside, almost forgotten, like an unwanted box in an attic. To the people, it appeared as though the Lord had abandoned them. But the problem was not God''s absence; it was their disobedience. Samuel, now a mature prophet, called the nation to action: If you are truly sorry, then, obey God and do what He already told you.'
  - 'Many believers today fall into the same trap. They complain about the challenges they face, speak constantly of their frustrations, and even cry in prayer, yet refuse to obey the instructions God has already given them. Complaints do not replace obedience, and emotions do not substitute for action. Samuel''s message to Israel was simple: if you want change, then follow God''s directives.'
  - Israel had been crying for deliverance, but deliverance required obedience. God had not abandoned them; they had abandoned
  - His ways. This is often where many struggle. They want God's intervention without aligning themselves with His instructions. But God's guidance unfolds as you walk in His previous commands. As Scripture says,“ To obey is better than sacrifice” (1 Samuel 15:22).
  - 'Do you ever feel as if God is silent? As if He has withdrawn from your situation? Before concluding that heaven is closed, ask yourself: Have I obeyed the last instruction God gave me? God rarely gives new direction to those who ignore earlier guidance. He leads step by step, and each act of obedience unlocks the next step.'
  - The moment Israel followed Samuel's instructions (putting away idols and preparing their hearts), God moved swiftly on their behalf. Their victory began with obedience, not emotion. God's directives are not suggestions; they are pathways to deliverance, restoration, and transformation. When you obey fully, you position yourself for victory. Choose today to follow God's instructions wholeheartedly. Obey promptly, willingly, and joyfully. In obedience, you'll find clarity, strength, and divine intervention.
prayer: Dear Father, I have made up my mind to follow Your directives with a willing and obedient heart. I refuse to ignore Your guidance. As I obey Your Word, your power, wisdom, and direction will be evident in my life, in Jesus' name. Amen.
