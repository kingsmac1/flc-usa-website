date: '2026-01-23'
title: All of God’s Promises Are Fulfilled in Christ
scripture: 2 Corinthians 1:20 (NLT)
verse: For all of God's promises have been fulfilled in Christ with a resounding 'Yes!' And through Christ, our 'Amen' ascends to God for his glory.
body:
  - 'The Word of God makes a bold and liberating declaration: every promise God ever made finds its fulfillment in Christ. This means that the blessings, covenants, provisions, healings, and assurances scattered throughout Scripture are not distant possibilities; you already have access to them in Jesus. God is not preparing to fulfill His promises; He has fulfilled them. Your role is no longer to wait anxiously but to receive confidently.'
  - Before Christ, God's promises pointed forward to a Savior who would secure them. After Christ's death and resurrection, those promises shifted from anticipation to manifestation. Many believers struggle because they still position themselves as if God needs to be convinced to act. But Scripture reveals that Christ is God's “Yes.” If the Father did not withhold His Son, He is not withholding His blessings. In Christ, everything necessary for life and godliness has already been given.
  - This truth renews how we pray. Instead of pleading for what seems unavailable, we declare “Amen” to what God has already affirmed. “Amen” is not a religious conclusion; it is
  - agreement. It is the believer's response of faith that seals what Jesus has already accomplished. Healing? Yes, in Christ. Peace? Yes, in Christ. Provision, purpose, restoration, strength, and victory? All “Yes”, through Him. Faith does not create the promise; it receives the promise already finished.
  - Understanding this shifts the believer from a mindset of lack to a mindset of inheritance. You are not chasing what God is reluctant to give; you are stepping into what Christ has already secured. This is why renewing your mind is essential. If your thoughts remain stuck in an Old Testament pattern of waiting, you will miss the New Testament reality of receiving. Christ is God's confirmation that nothing He promised will fail.
  - Walk with confidence today. You are not waiting for God's “Yes”; you are standing on it. Let your “Amen” rise with boldness, knowing it aligns with a promise already fulfilled.
prayer: Father, I thank You that every promise is fulfilled in Christ. I stop waiting for what You have already provided. I say “Amen” to Your truth and receive every blessing You have secured for me in Jesus' name. Amen.
