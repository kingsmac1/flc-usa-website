title: Refuse Profanity
scripture: 1 Samuel 2:12 KJV
verse: Now the sons of Eli were sons of Belial; they knew not the LORD.
body:
  - 'Iwant you to observe carefully the character and conduct of Eli''s sons, because the Spirit of God draws our attention to something very important: their profanity. The Word describes them as “sons of Belial,” meaning corrupt, worthless, spiritually depraved. Why? Because they handled sacred things without reverence. Their behavior toward the offerings, the people, and the house of God revealed hearts that had no fear of the Lord (1 Samuel 2:12 17).'
  - The Scriptures says, “Wherefore the sin of the young men was very great before the Lord; for men abhorred the offering of the Lord” (1 Samuel 2:17). Their irreverence made others despise sacred things. Profanity is not merely foul speech; it is treating what is holy as common, casual, or insignificant.
  - Furthermore, 1 Samuel 2:22–25 KJV says, “Now Eli was very old, and heard all that his sons did… Nay, my sons; for it is no good report… if one man sin against the Lord, who shall entreat for him?” Their conduct showed disregard for God's presence, God's people, and God's house. This is why the Lord judged them. This was not about ignorance; it was about contempt.
  - As believers, we are called to have sacredness for sacred
  - things. Your conduct in God's house matters. The way you handle church responsibilities, church finances, ministry assignments, and the resources of the Kingdom matters. Even ministers must be careful not to treat holy things lightly or misuse the resources entrusted to them. Profanity is a seed of destruction; reverence is a posture that honors God.
  - Moreover, be careful not to mock the things of God. Some use the church for jokes, comedy, caricatures, or entertainment. But the Lord warns, “…them that honour me I will honour, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed” (1 Samuel 2:30 KJV). Esteem the Lord. Speak with reverence. Conduct yourself with dignity. Treat His house, His people, and His calling with honor.
prayer: Dear Father, I refuse profanity and irreverence. I honor what You honor. I treat Your presence, Your Word, Your house, and Your resources with deep reverence. My conduct will always reflect Your glory, and I walk in integrity, uprightness, and fear of the Lord, in Jesus' name. Amen.
